Bible PAL — Wave 1a Authoring (Traditional lane)
Source: Full-Canon Production Gate (PR #86, merged to master 2026-07-17).
Use this prompt to open the authoring phase. Phase 1 (research/audit/gating) is closed.

STEP ZERO
Run `git pull` before anything else. The corpus grows between sessions, and story-ID
assignment must be computed against current master, not a stale tree.

OBJECTIVE
Author the highest-quality Traditional Bible PAL stories possible. Do not optimize for
speed. Optimize for faithfulness, literary quality, speakability, and replay value.

EDITORIAL PHILOSOPHY
Facts are surprisingly emotional when they're remembered faithfully. Prefer faithful
narrative over dramatic embellishment. Earn emotion from the biblical events themselves
rather than from invented dialogue, internal thoughts, or cinematic additions. When forced
to choose, choose faithfulness.

CONTEXT
Phase 1 is complete and merged to master: the full-canon research, gap audit, adversarial
verification, and production gating are done. The production gate is authoritative and
already approved. Do NOT reopen canon research, gap analysis, journey gating, or the gate
decision itself — treat candidates CG04, CG05, and CG06 as settled and approved. Your job
is craft: author the prose.

READ FIRST
- docs/editorial/FULL_CANON_PRODUCTION_GATE.md   (the decision; context only, don't re-litigate)
- assets/stories/canon_production_gate.json      (records CG04, CG05, CG06 — scope, cautions, rationale)
- docs/STORY_NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md            (primary craft reference — narration prose voice)
- docs/REFLECTION_VOICE.md                       (reflection voice — each story ships a reflection)
- docs/SPEC.md and docs/INVARIANTS.md            (product law + non-negotiables)
- docs/BIBLE_TRANSLATION_COMPLIANCE.md           (WEB default; public-domain translations only)
The style guide and narration guide are your authoritative references. If you want
calibration examples, select recent stories from the current corpus yourself; do not
imitate any single named batch.

SCOPE
Wave 1a is the TRADITIONAL lane only: WEB short + KJV short + a reflection for each of the
three stories. The gate marks these "both" lanes, but the kid-lane versions are a later
wave — do not author them now.

CG04/CG05/CG06 are planning labels, not corpus IDs. Determine the next-available
Traditional story IDs from the repository, propose them, and pause if there is any
ambiguity or conflict. Do not renumber existing stories.

SCRIPTURE BOUNDARY
Do not import events, dialogue, theology, or later narrative from outside the specified
passage unless already contained within the project's established Traditional narration
doctrine. Stay inside the declared Scripture anchor. (E.g. Peter's Confession must not
drift into "Get behind me, Satan"; John 1's prologue must not bleed into Andrew bringing
Peter.)

   - Do NOT confuse with existing story 1417 (John 1:40-42, Andrew brings Peter) —

   - Keep distinct from existing story 1448 (Cave of Adullam, 1 Samuel 22) — a different

RULES
- Follow all existing editorial doctrine, Scripture-boundary enforcement, narration style,
  reflection voice, and quality gates.
- WEB is the default translation; KJV is the second variant. Public-domain only.
- Set createdByModel to the actual authoring model. Traditional stories are gated by an
  allowlist in test/core/story_engine_compliance_test.dart — currently gpt-4.1,
  claude-opus-4-6, claude-opus-4-7, claude-opus-4-8, openai_chatgpt. Models outside that
  set (e.g. claude-fable-5, claude-sonnet-5) will FAIL CI unless the allowlist is amended
  first. Recommended authoring model: Claude Opus 4.8 at high effort.
- PAUSE after drafting for review and approval. Do NOT generate audio, create or modify
  Journey Beats or arc JSON, update manifests, or commit anything until approved.

DELIVERABLE
For each story: the WEB short + KJV short text files, the reflection, a short
proposed-metadata block (mood, theme tags, suggested narrator voice, proposed story ID),
and an Editorial Notes section covering:
- any difficult wording decisions,
- any Scripture-boundary risks considered,
- any places where multiple faithful renderings were possible.
Then pause for review.
