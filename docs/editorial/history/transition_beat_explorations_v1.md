Each beat is the full three clauses: **look-back … look-forward … open door.** Adult uses *"or, tell me what's on your heart today"*; kid uses *"or, what's on your mind?"*

Reminder of the three signature tests: **paste-test** (could the look-back paste onto another story?), **quiet-transition test** (does it work when almost nothing happens?), **voice-fingerprint** (strip the proper nouns — still PAL?).

## Exploration drafts

### Narrative / faithfulness (baseline center)

**1. Daniel — chapter 1 → the furnace** *(aching→resolve)*
> Last time, we sat with young Daniel as he turned down the king's table for plain food and a clear conscience… Daniel is about to learn what it costs to keep praying with the windows open, once the law itself has turned against him… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**2. Joseph — the pit → Egypt** *(aching→mystery)*
> Last time, we watched Joseph disappear into the dry pit while his brothers sat down to their bread… Joseph's road is about to carry him somewhere none of them could have imagined… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**3. David — anointed → Goliath** *(gentle→solemn)*
> Last time, we stood in the field while the youngest son was called in from the sheep to be anointed… David is about to walk toward a giant carrying nothing his brothers thought was enough… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Woman-centered arc

**4. Ruth — the road from Moab → gleaning** *(gentle, quiet)*
> Last time, we walked the road out of Moab with Ruth, who would not let Naomi go home alone… Ruth is about to stoop in a stranger's field, not knowing whose it is or who is watching… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**5. Hannah — the silent prayer** *(aching)*
> Last time, we knelt beside Hannah in the temple, her lips moving over a prayer no one else could hear… Hannah is about to carry home a hope she has not yet been given… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**6. Esther — the price on her people** *(solemn, mysterious)*
> Last time, we stood with Esther as she learned her people's lives had a price on them… Esther is about to decide whether to speak in a room where speaking could cost her everything… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**7. Naomi — home to Bethlehem, empty** *(aching→gentle)*
> Last time, we came back into Bethlehem beside Naomi, who asked to be called Bitter… Naomi is about to watch the empty road fill again, from a direction she stopped watching… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Quiet / near-nothing-happens (the acceptance test)

**8. Elijah — wind, fire, then the low voice** *(quiet, mysterious)*
> Last time, we stood on the mountain with Elijah while the wind tore past and the fire went by… Elijah is about to hear the thing he came for arrive so quietly he could almost miss it… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**9. Jesus in Gethsemane** *(solemn, quiet)*
> Last time, we climbed the hill with Jesus, away from the others, to the place he went to be alone… he is about to pray the hardest prayer of his life with no one awake to hear it… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**10. Paul in prison → the joy letter** *(quiet, hopeful — epistle)*
> Last time, we sat with Paul in the cell where the chain never came off… Paul is about to fill a whole letter with joy, from the one place no one would think to look for it… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**11. Ruth — gleaning, seen** *(quiet)*
> Last time, we followed Ruth into the barley field, gathering the grain the harvesters let fall… Ruth is about to be seen by the owner of the field, in a kindness she never went looking for… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### No single human figure (escape-hatch stress case — flag for discussion)

> **These three test whether the relational-center rule survives when the "who" is God, or the text itself. Note how each keeps a "who" — the Maker, the Shepherd — rather than making the *event* the subject. If the doctrine wants an explicit God/Israel-as-"who" hatch, these are the evidence.**

**12. Creation — day → the filling** *(awe, gentle)*
> Last time, we watched the light step back from the dark, and the waters draw aside to make room for dry ground… and the Maker is about to fill all that empty room with things that move and breathe… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**13. Psalm 23 — green places → the valley** *(gentle)*
> Last time, we walked the green places beside the Shepherd, near the water that does not rush… the Shepherd is about to lead us straight through the dark valley, and not once let go of the staff… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**14. Psalm 139 — fully known → nowhere apart** *(mysterious)*
> Last time, we sat under the weight of being fully known — every word before we speak it… and God is about to be searched for in every far place at once, and found already there… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Darker next chapter / loss (anticipation can't promise something nice)

**15. Job — the ordinary morning → the afternoon** *(aching, solemn)*
> Last time, we stood at the edge of everything Job had, on an ordinary morning when it was all still there… Job is about to lose it in a single afternoon, and still not curse the God who let it go… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**16. Jeremiah — the potter's house → the hard word** *(solemn — prophecy)*
> Last time, we went down with Jeremiah to the potter's house, to watch the wheel turn and the marred clay begin again… Jeremiah is about to carry a word his own people will not want, and carry it anyway… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**17. Elisha — the road → the parting** *(aching, then hope)*
> Last time, we walked the road with Elisha, who would not leave his teacher's side no matter how many times he was told to stay… Elisha is about to watch the only man he'd follow be taken from him — and be left holding what he carried… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Joyful

**18. Zacchaeus — the tree → "today"** *(joyful, gentle — gospel)*
> Last time, we watched a small man climb a tree, just to see Jesus pass in the crowd… Zacchaeus is about to hear his own name from below, and a sentence he never expected: today, Jesus is coming to his house… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**19. David — the ark comes home** *(joyful)*
> Last time, we watched the ark come up the road toward the city with David out in front… David is about to dance before the Lord with everything in him, and not care at all who is watching… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Mysterious / prophecy-oracle

**20. Ezekiel — the valley of bones** *(mysterious — oracle)*
> Last time, we stood with Ezekiel in a valley full of bones, dry as the dust between them… Ezekiel is about to be asked a question with no reasonable answer: can these bones live?… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**21. Jonah — down to the ship → the deep** *(mysterious→gentle)*
> Last time, we watched Jonah go down to the ship, and down into the sea, running the wrong way from God… Jonah is about to pray from the one place he was sure God could not reach him… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Gospel / parable

**22. The prodigal — the far country** *(aching→hopeful — parable)*
> Last time, we watched the younger son count out his share and set off for a far country… he is about to come to himself in a field that isn't his, and turn for home with no idea what waits on the road… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**23. Peter — the courtyard fire** *(aching→grace — gospel)*
> Last time, we stood by the fire in the courtyard while Peter said, three times, that he never knew the man… Peter is about to meet those same eyes on a beach — and be asked not about the failure, but about love… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

**24. Bartimaeus — the roadside** *(hopeful — gospel; unfamous "surprise" candidate)*
> Last time, we sat with Bartimaeus at the roadside, calling out over the crowd that told him to be quiet… Bartimaeus is about to be asked what he wants, as if it weren't already written all over him… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Resolution / rest — nothing to tease (stress case)

**25. Simeon — the long wait → the child** *(gentle, quiet, aching-joy)*
> Last time, we waited in the temple with Simeon, who had been promised he would not die before he saw it… Simeon is about to hold the whole promise in his two arms, and be ready to go in peace… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Aching → grace (bridge)

**26. Noah — the ark built → the first rain** *(gentle, hopeful; also works kid)*
> Last time, we helped Noah finish the ark while the world went on laughing around him… Noah is about to feel the first drops fall, and know he was not foolish to listen… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

### Kid lane

**27. Kid — David anointed → Goliath** *(joyful)*
> Last time, we watched a shepherd boy get picked when nobody thought he'd be the one… David's about to face something way bigger than he is, with just a sling and a whole lot of trust… or, what's on your mind?

**28. Kid — Noah → first raindrop** *(gentle, hopeful)*
> Last time, we helped Noah build a giant boat while everybody else laughed… Noah's about to feel the very first raindrop, and find out he was right to listen… or, what's on your mind?

## Candidate ladders (Technically Correct → Good → Better → Promoted)

The doc wants 1–2 of these, at least one on a *quiet* story. Here are both.

### Ladder A — Ruth gleaning (QUIET story)

- **Technically Correct** *(passes the checklist, no soul):*
  > Last time, we watched Ruth gather grain in the field all day… There's more to Ruth's story if you'd like to hear it… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Person-centered, no spoiler, open door — and utterly flat. This is the "acceptable vs. worthy" gap.*

- **Good** *(alive, but the glimpse drifts abstract):*
  > Last time, we followed Ruth through the barley field, gathering what the harvesters let fall… Ruth's faithfulness is about to be repaid in a way she never asked for… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *"faithfulness repaid" could paste onto half the corpus.*

- **Better** *(rules hold, it lands):*
  > Last time, we followed Ruth into the barley field, gathering the grain that fell behind the harvesters… Ruth is about to be noticed by the owner of the field, in a kindness she never went looking for… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

- **Promoted** *(durable + instructive):*
  > Last time, we followed Ruth into the barley field, stooping for the grain the harvesters left behind… Ruth is about to look up and find the owner of the field already watching — with a gentleness she never asked for… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Why it wins: the physical stoop grounds the quiet; "look up and find… already watching" is unmistakably this moment, relational, and names the situation, not the marriage it becomes.*

### Ladder B — Daniel / lions' den (FAMOUS story)

- **Technically Correct:**
  > Last time, we were with Daniel in the lions' den… There's more to Daniel's story if you'd like to hear it… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

- **Good** *(abstraction drift):*
  > Last time, we walked with Daniel into the lions' den… faithfulness is about to carry Daniel somewhere costly… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

- **Better:**
  > Last time, we walked with Daniel into the lions' den… Daniel is about to find out what it costs to keep his windows open toward home… or, tell me what's on your heart today.

- **Promoted:**
  > Last time, we walked with Daniel into the lions' den… Daniel is about to watch the king's own law bent into a trap — and still kneel at the open window… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Why it wins: the event (the trap) is fully present but Daniel stays the subject; "still kneel at the open window" is the cost, not the rescue.*

## Teaching foils (candidate near-misses — intentionally flawed)

Each with the one-line reason, ready for the doc's near-miss section.

- **Event ate the person (plot teaser):**
  > Last time, we walked with Daniel into the lions' den… would you like to hear what happened when a king demanded everyone bow to a golden statue?… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Fail: the next event becomes the subject; Daniel vanishes. Curriculum voice.*

- **Abstraction drift:**
  > Last time, we sat with Hannah as she prayed without a sound… sometimes the deepest prayers are the ones we can't say out loud… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Fail: dissolves into a general truth; pastes onto anyone.*

- **Spoiled the payoff:**
  > Last time, we watched Joseph go down into the pit… Joseph is about to rise to second in all Egypt and save his whole family from the famine… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Fail: names the resolution; nothing left to listen for.*

- **Went cliffhanger (hook):**
  > Last time, we stood with Esther before the king… you won't believe what she risks next… or, tell me what's on your heart today.
  *Fail: engagement hook; "you won't believe" is banned; PAL selling.*

- **Correct-but-forgettable:**
  > (the Ladder-A "Technically Correct" Ruth line above)
  *Fail: passes every audit point, teaches nothing, rewards no repetition.*
