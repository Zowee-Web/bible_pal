## Method & caveats

22 canon divisions were enumerated pericope-by-pericope by parallel agents against the corpus inventory rebuilt from every `meta_*.json` (1,079 units). Every missing/partial claim went through a skeptical full-corpus verification pass (refs, titles, keywords, story keys — both lanes). Survivors were classified; a completeness critic swept division seams (added 24 that survived dedupe, incl. the Sower, the Passover night, Genesis 33's reconciliation).

**Salvage caveat:** five divisions (Joshua–Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel–Chronicles, parts of Exodus and Numbers–Deuteronomy) plus Acts / Epistles–Revelation classification and the whole Gospel-encounters division were verified and classified in a manual salvage pass after the agent run hit spend limits — same rubric, single reviewer. The Production Gate re-verified every salvaged-division item that entered near-term production; the rest of the salvaged items in this file retain single-reviewer confidence.

**Do not produce from this file directly.** Route any candidate through `FULL_CANON_PRODUCTION_GATE.md` first: kid-lane-only coverage masked several adult gaps, and severe-content items (Judges 19–21, Numbers 31) are enumerated for completeness, not recommended for authoring.
