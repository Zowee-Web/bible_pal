The Kind Neighbor's Gift

In a small town, there lived a girl named Leah who loved to help her neighbors. Every morning, she would walk down the street, looking for ways to be kind.

One day, Leah noticed that old Mr. Jonas had trouble carrying his groceries. Without being asked, she ran over to help him bring the bags inside.

"Thank you, young one," Mr. Jonas said with a warm smile. "Your kindness made my day brighter."

Leah felt happy inside. She learned that helping others was a gift that made everyone feel good, including herself.

That evening, as the sun painted the sky orange and pink, Leah sat with her family for dinner. They shared stories about kind things they had seen that day.

"Being kind is like planting seeds," her mother said. "Each small act of kindness grows into something beautiful."

Leah smiled, thinking about all the little ways she could spread kindness tomorrow. As she drifted off to sleep, she felt warm and peaceful, knowing that even small acts of love can make the world a better place.
