## Young Samuel Learns to Listen

Long ago, in a quiet temple on a peaceful hill, there lived a young boy named Samuel. The temple was a special place where people came to pray and feel close to God. Samuel helped an elderly priest named Eli take care of this holy place. Each evening, soft lamplight filled the temple with a warm golden glow.

Samuel had a small bed near the altar where he slept each night. Eli was kind and gentle, teaching Samuel many things about serving God. The two of them shared quiet meals together. They watched the sunset paint the sky in soft pink and orange. Samuel loved living in the peaceful temple.

One night, after the lamps were lit, Samuel lay down on his small bed. The air was cool and calm. Outside, crickets sang their gentle song. Samuel closed his eyes, ready for sleep. Then he heard something soft. A gentle voice called his name.

"Samuel," the voice whispered, like a warm breeze.

Samuel sat up and looked around. He thought Eli must have called him. So he got up and walked softly to where Eli slept. His small feet padded quietly on the stone floor.

"Here I am," Samuel said. "You called me."

But Eli smiled and shook his head gently. "I did not call you, dear child. Go back to bed and rest."

Samuel returned to his little bed. He pulled his blanket close and closed his eyes again. The night was so quiet and still. Then he heard it once more.

"Samuel," the gentle voice called again.

Once more, Samuel went to Eli. And once more, Eli said he had not called. "Perhaps you were dreaming," Eli said kindly. "Go rest now."

Samuel obeyed and lay down again. The lamplight flickered softly. The temple felt so peaceful and safe. Then, for a third time, the gentle voice came.

"Samuel."

This time when Samuel came to him, Eli understood. The wise old priest sat up slowly. His eyes were full of wonder. He realized that God was calling the young boy.

"If you hear the voice again," Eli said gently, "say these words: Speak, Lord. Your servant is listening."

Samuel nodded. He felt a flutter of wonder in his heart. He went back to his bed and waited. The night was calm. Stars began to twinkle outside the window.

Then the voice came once more, soft and loving.

"Samuel. Samuel."

Samuel took a deep breath. He felt surrounded by warmth and love. It was like being wrapped in the softest blanket.

"Speak, Lord," Samuel whispered. "Your servant is listening."

In that moment, Samuel felt God's presence close to him. It was gentle and kind. God spoke to Samuel with love. Samuel listened with his whole heart. He felt safe and cherished. He knew that God loved him very much.

When morning came, Samuel shared everything with Eli. The elderly priest smiled with joy. "You have learned something precious," Eli said. "God speaks gently to those who listen."

Samuel nodded. He understood now that God's voice was not loud or startling. It was soft, like a loving whisper. It brought peace and comfort.

That night, as stars twinkled softly in the evening sky, Samuel lay in his cozy bed. The temple was quiet and still. He felt warm and protected. He thought about God's gentle voice. A peaceful smile crossed his face.

Samuel closed his eyes slowly. He felt loved and safe. The night wrapped around him like a cozy blanket. And as the moon rose high above the peaceful temple, young Samuel drifted into the sweetest, most peaceful sleep.

Goodnight, little one. May you rest as peacefully as Samuel did.
