A kind shepherd had one hundred fluffy sheep. He loved each one and gave them all names. Every day he counted them to make sure they were safe.

One evening, when the shepherd counted his sheep, something was wrong. He counted again and again. Only ninety-nine sheep! One little sheep was missing.

The shepherd looked at his flock, safe inside the gate. He could stay with them where it was warm and safe. But somewhere out there, one little sheep was lost and scared.

The shepherd knew what he had to do. He closed the gate to keep the other sheep safe, then walked out into the hills to find the lost one.

The sky grew dark. The path became rocky. The shepherd called out for his lost sheep. He looked behind big rocks. He searched near the streams. He walked for a long, long time.

Then he heard it. A tiny sound. The lost sheep was caught in some prickly bushes, too tired to move. The shepherd hurried over and gently lifted the sheep free.

He placed the little sheep on his shoulders and carried it all the way home. His heart was so happy! The sheep that was lost had been found.

When he got back to the village, the shepherd called out to everyone nearby. His friends and neighbors came running. They all smiled and cheered together because the lost sheep was home safe.

The shepherd loved that one little sheep so much that he went searching until he found it. That is how much we are loved too.
