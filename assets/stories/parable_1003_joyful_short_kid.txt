The Little Shepherd's Song

In a green valley, a young shepherd named Eli watched over his small flock of sheep. Every morning, he would sing a simple song of thanks as the sun rose over the hills.

One day, a lost lamb wandered away from the flock. Eli searched everywhere, looking behind every rock and under every bush. When he finally found the lamb, it was stuck in some thorns.

Gently, Eli freed the little lamb and carried it back on his shoulders. The other sheep gathered around, happy to see their friend safe.

That night, Eli sang his song of thanks again, this time with an even happier heart. He had learned that caring for others brings the greatest joy.

As the stars came out, Eli and his sheep rested peacefully together, grateful for the good day they had shared.
