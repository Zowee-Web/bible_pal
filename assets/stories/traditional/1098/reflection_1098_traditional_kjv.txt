The accusers came with stones and certainty. They had the law. They had the proof. They had the multitude. All was prepared for condemnation.

And Jesus stooped down and wrote upon the ground.

We know not what he wrote. But we know what followed: the men with stones looked upon themselves rather than upon the woman. And one by one, they departed.

There is something in that moment — the silence, the writing, the quiet challenge — that cutteth deeper than any argument. It saith not that the wrong was not wrong. It saith: before thou castest, look upon thine own hands.

Sometimes the most powerful answer to accusation is not defence. It is a question that sendeth every accuser home.
