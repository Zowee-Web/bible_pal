One morning, Jesus was teaching people at the temple. Then some religious leaders dragged a woman into the middle of the crowd. She was scared and shaking.

"This woman did something really wrong," they said to Jesus. "The law says she should be punished with stones. What do YOU say?"

They were trying to trick Jesus. They wanted him to say something wrong so they could get him in trouble.

But Jesus didn't answer right away. He bent down and started writing in the dirt with his finger.

They kept asking. "Well? What do you say?"

Jesus stood up and said something that made everyone stop.

"Whoever has never done anything wrong — YOU throw the first stone."

Then he bent down and kept writing.

One by one, the men walked away. The oldest ones left first. Then the younger ones. Pretty soon, NOBODY was left except Jesus and the woman.

Jesus stood up. "Where did everybody go?" he asked. "Did anyone condemn you?"

"No one," she said quietly.

Jesus looked at her with kind eyes. "I don't condemn you either," he said. "Go, and don't do this anymore."
