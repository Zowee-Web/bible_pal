Jesus went to the Mount of Olives. Early in the morning he came again into the temple. All the people came to him. He sat down and taught them.

While he was teaching, the scribes and the Pharisees brought a woman. She had been taken in adultery, in the very act. They set her in the middle of the crowd, in front of Jesus, in the middle of the temple where he sat teaching.

They said to him, "Teacher, this woman was taken in adultery, in the very act. Now in our law, Moses commanded us to stone such women. What then do you say about her?"

They said this testing him, that they might have something to accuse him of. They asked the question not because they wanted an answer but because they wanted an accusation.

But Jesus stooped down and wrote on the ground with his finger. He bent to the ground and he wrote. He did not answer them. He wrote on the ground with his finger.

When they continued asking him, he stood up from the ground. He looked at them and he said, "He who is without sin among you, let him throw the first stone at her."

Again he stooped down and wrote on the ground with his finger.

They who heard it, being convicted by their own conscience, went out one by one. The oldest went first. Then the next. Then the next. Beginning from the oldest, even to the last. One by one, they went out. Until every one of them was gone.

Jesus was left alone with the woman where she was, in the middle.

Jesus stood up. He saw no one there but the woman. He said to her, "Woman, where are your accusers? Did no one condemn you?"

She said, "No one, Lord."

Jesus said, "Neither do I condemn you. Go your way. From now on, sin no more."
