The scribes and Pharisees brought a woman taken in adultery. They set her in the middle. They cited the law of Moses. They asked Jesus what he said about her.

Jesus stooped down and wrote on the ground with his finger.

When they continued asking, he stood up and said, "He who is without sin among you, let him throw the first stone at her." Then he stooped down and wrote on the ground again.

They went out one by one, beginning from the oldest to the last.

When they were gone, Jesus said to the woman, "Where are your accusers? Did no one condemn you?" She said, "No one, Lord." He said, "Neither do I condemn you. Go your way. From now on, sin no more."

Have you ever expected condemnation — and received mercy instead?
