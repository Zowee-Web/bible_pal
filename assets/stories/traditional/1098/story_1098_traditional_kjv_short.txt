Jesus went unto the mount of Olives. And early in the morning he came again into the temple. All the people came unto him. He sat down and taught them.

While he taught, the scribes and Pharisees brought unto him a woman. She had been taken in adultery, in the very act. They set her in the midst of the people, before Jesus, in the midst of the temple where he sat teaching.

They say unto him, "Master, this woman was taken in adultery, in the very act. Now Moses in the law commanded us, that such should be stoned: but what sayest thou?"

This they said, tempting him, that they might have to accuse him. They asked the question not because they wanted an answer, but that they might have an accusation.

But Jesus stooped down, and with his finger wrote on the ground. He bent to the ground and he wrote. He answered them not. He wrote on the ground with his finger.

So when they continued asking him, he lifted up himself and said unto them, "He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her."

And again he stooped down, and wrote on the ground.

And they which heard it, being convicted by their own conscience, went out one by one. The eldest went first. Then the next. Then the next. Beginning at the eldest, even unto the last. One by one, they went out. Until every one of them was gone.

And Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst.

When Jesus had lifted up himself, and saw none but the woman, he said unto her, "Woman, where are those thine accusers? Hath no man condemned thee?"

She said, "No man, Lord."

And Jesus said unto her, "Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more."
