In those days, Mary rose and went into the hill country with haste, to a city of Judah. The hills lay quiet in the morning. She entered the house of Zacharias and greeted Elizabeth her cousin.

When Elizabeth heard the greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and she was filled with the Holy Spirit. She called out with a loud voice, "Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb. Why am I so favored, that the mother of my Lord should come to me? For behold, when the voice of your greeting came into my ears, the baby leaped in my womb for joy."

Then Mary lifted up her voice and said, "My soul magnifies the Lord. My spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior."

She continued, "For he has looked on the humble state of his servant. For behold, from now on, all generations will call me blessed."

Mary said, "For he who is mighty has done great things for me, and holy is his name. His mercy is for generations and generations on those who fear him."

She said, "He has shown strength with his arm. He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts."

And she said, "He has put down princes from their thrones, and has exalted the lowly."

She said, "He has filled the hungry with good things. He has sent the rich away empty."

Then Mary said, "He has given help to Israel his servant, that he might remember mercy. As he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his offspring forever."

Elizabeth listened in the doorway as Mary's voice rose in the small house. The morning was bright over the hills of Judah. Mary stayed with her for a time, and then she returned to her own house.
