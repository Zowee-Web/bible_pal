John had been on the island of Patmos when the door was opened in heaven. He had been carried in the Spirit to a throne room he had no language for. He had seen the throne. He had seen the One who sat on it. He had seen the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders around the throne, casting their crowns down and singing.

Now he saw a scroll.

It lay in the right hand of him who sat on the throne. It was written on the inside and on the outside, and it was sealed shut with seven seals. A mighty angel proclaimed with a loud voice: who is worthy to open the scroll, and to break its seals?

No one answered. No one in heaven above. No one on the earth. No one under the earth. None among the angels, none among the elders, none among the saints. The throne room was silent.

And John wept.

He wept much, because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look in it. The history of the world, the long unfolding of what God had set in motion, was held in a hand that was holding it out — and no one was found who could read it.

One of the elders came to him. "Don't weep," he said. "Behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has overcome. He will open the scroll and its seven seals."

John looked up. He had been told to look for a Lion.

He saw a Lamb.

It stood in the middle of the throne and of the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders, and it looked as though it had been slain. It had seven horns and seven eyes. It walked forward. It took the scroll out of the right hand of him who sat on the throne.

When it had taken the scroll, every creature in heaven and on earth fell down.

The twenty-four elders fell down, each one holding a harp and golden bowls of incense, which are the prayers of the saints. They sang a new song:

"You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, for you were slain, and bought us for God with your blood out of every tribe and language and people and nation. You have made us a kingdom and priests to our God, and we will reign on the earth."

Then John heard the voice of many angels around the throne — ten thousand times ten thousand, thousands of thousands — singing:

"Worthy is the Lamb who has been slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and strength and honor and glory and blessing."

And then every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and on the sea, and everything in them, sang it back. Blessing and honor and glory and dominion to him who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb, forever and ever.

The four living creatures said: Amen. The elders fell down and worshiped. And so it was on the day John was shown the scroll, and the One who could open it, in the throne room of heaven.
