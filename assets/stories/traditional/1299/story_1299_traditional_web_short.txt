For seven seasons Nebuchadnezzar had been driven from among men. His hair had grown like eagles' feathers. His nails had grown like bird claws. He had eaten grass with the oxen, and his body had been wet with the dew of the sky. The royal robes were folded away in some chamber. The throne stood empty.

At the end of the days I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted up my eyes to heaven, and my understanding returned to me, and I blessed the Most High, and I praised and honored him who lives forever; for his dominion is an everlasting dominion, and his kingdom from generation to generation.

All the inhabitants of the earth are reputed as nothing; and he does according to his will in the army of heaven, and among the inhabitants of the earth; and no one can stop his hand, or ask him, "What are you doing?"

At the same time my understanding returned to me; and for the glory of my kingdom, my majesty and brightness returned to me; and my counselors and my lords sought to me; and I was established in my kingdom, and excellent greatness was added to me.

Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and extol and honor the King of heaven; for all his works are truth, and his ways justice; and those who walk in pride he is able to abase.

He sat on the throne again. The counselors came back to him. The kingdom went on. The mornings came, and the evenings. The years passed quietly.
