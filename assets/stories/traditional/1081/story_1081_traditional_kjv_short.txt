When Jesus heard the tidings, he departed thence by ship into a desert place apart. He sought a quiet place, away from the towns. The boat moved across the water in silence. But when the people had heard thereof, they followed him on foot out of the cities, walking round about the shore.

When Jesus went forth and saw a great multitude, he was moved with compassion toward them, and he healed their sick. The hours passed. People came forward, and one by one, he made them whole.

The sun moved across the sky. When the evening was come, a soft golden light lay upon the hills.

His disciples came to him, saying, "This is a desert place, and the time is now past. Send the multitude away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves victuals."

But Jesus said unto them, "They need not depart. Give ye them to eat."

The disciples looked one upon another. They had so little. They turned again unto Jesus.

"We have here but five loaves, and two fishes," they said.

He said, "Bring them hither to me."

And he commanded the multitude to sit down on the grass. Thousands sat down across the hillside. The grass was warm from the sun. The disciples brought the five small loaves and the two fishes and set them in his hands.

Jesus looked up to heaven. He blessed the food. He brake the loaves. And he gave them to his disciples, and the disciples gave to the multitude.

And they did all eat, and were filled. And they kept eating. There was bread in every hand. There was fish for every mouth. The disciples went to and fro among the people, bearing the food in their arms, and it was not diminished.

When all had eaten and were satisfied, the disciples took up of the fragments that remained twelve baskets full — full and heavy — with what was left over.

And they that had eaten were about five thousand men, beside women and children.
