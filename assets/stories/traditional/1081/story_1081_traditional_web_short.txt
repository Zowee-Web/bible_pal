When Jesus heard the news, he stepped into a boat and crossed the water to a deserted place apart. He sought a quiet place, away from the towns. The boat moved across the lake in silence. But the crowds heard where he was going. They set out from the cities and walked around the shore on foot, following him.

When Jesus came out of the boat, he saw them — a great multitude, gathered and waiting on the shore. He had compassion on them. He stayed among them, and he healed their sick. The hours passed. People came forward, and one by one, he made them well.

The sun moved across the sky. Evening came, and with it a soft gold light over the hills.

The disciples came to Jesus. They looked over the vast crowd. "This place is deserted," they said, "and the hour is already late. Send the crowds away, so they can go into the villages and buy themselves food."

But Jesus looked at them calmly. "They don't need to go away," he said. "You give them something to eat."

The disciples looked at one another. They had so little. They turned back to Jesus.

"We only have here five loaves and two fish," they said.

"Bring them here to me," he said.

So Jesus had the crowd sit down on the grass. Thousands of people sat down across the hillside. The grass was warm from the sun. The disciples brought the five small loaves and the two fish and placed them in his hands.

Jesus looked up to heaven. He blessed the food. He broke the loaves. And he gave the pieces to his disciples, who carried them out among the people.

The people began to eat. And they kept eating. There was bread in every hand. There was fish for every mouth. The disciples moved back and forth through the crowd, carrying the food in their arms, and the food did not run out.

When everyone had eaten and was full, the disciples gathered what was left over. They walked among the seated crowd, picking up the broken pieces. They filled twelve baskets — full and heavy — with what remained.

And the number of those who had eaten was about five thousand men, besides women and children.
