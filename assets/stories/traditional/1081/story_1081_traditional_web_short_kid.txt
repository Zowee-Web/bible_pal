Jesus wanted to find a quiet place to rest. He got into a boat and sailed across the lake. But the people saw where he was going. They walked all the way around the lake to meet him!

When Jesus got out of the boat and saw all those people waiting for him, he felt sorry for them. He stayed and helped the sick people get better.

The day went on and on. Finally, evening came. The sun was getting low.

His friends came up to him. "It's getting late," they said. "This place is out in the middle of nowhere. Send the people home so they can buy some food."

But Jesus said, "They don't need to leave. You feed them."

His friends looked at each other. "We only have five loaves of bread and two fish!" they said. "That's barely enough for us!"

"Bring them to me," Jesus said.

He told everyone to sit down on the grass. Then he took the five loaves and the two fish, looked up to heaven, and said a blessing. He broke the bread into pieces and gave them to his friends to pass out.

And something amazing happened.

Everyone got food! There was bread for every person. There was fish for every person. The food just kept coming and coming. Nobody ran out.

When everyone was full, the friends gathered up the leftovers. They filled twelve whole baskets with the pieces that were left over!

And the number of people who had eaten? About five thousand men — plus all the women and children too!
