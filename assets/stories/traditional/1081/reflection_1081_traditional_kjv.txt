Five loaves and two fishes. That was all they possessed. And it sufficed not — not nearly.

There is a manner of helplessness that cometh when a man standeth before a need plainly beyond his power to supply. He seeth what is wanted. He counteth what he hath. And the reckoning falleth short.

What Jesus bade them do was to bring what they had. Not more than they possessed. Not what they wished they might have. Only the small, insufficient thing that was in their hands.

Sometimes it beginneth there — not with enough, but with what is offered. What followed was not a thing the disciples wrought. It was a thing that came to pass when they released the little they held.
