The elder addresses the chosen lady and her children. He loves them in truth, and so do all who know the truth. That love is for the truth's sake—the truth that remains in believers and will be with them forever. His greeting joins grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, with truth and love.

Many deceivers have gone out into the world. They do not confess that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. The elder names this refusal as the mark of the deceiver and the Antichrist. His readers must watch themselves so that the fruit of faithful work is not lost and a full reward is received.

The boundary is the teaching of Christ. Whoever goes beyond it and does not remain in it does not have God. Whoever remains in that teaching has both the Father and the Son. Therefore, if someone arrives without this teaching, the readers must not receive that person into the house or offer a welcome that supports the work. To welcome such a teacher would make the giver a participant in evil deeds.

The elder has many more things to communicate, but he will not entrust them to paper and ink. He hopes to come and speak face to face so that shared joy may be made full. The children of the chosen lady's chosen sister send greetings.
