The elder writeth unto the elect lady and her children. He loveth them in the truth, and so do all they that have known the truth. That love is for the truth's sake—the truth which dwelleth in believers and shall be with them for ever. His greeting joineth grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, with truth and love.

Many deceivers are entered into the world. They confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh. The elder nameth this refusal as the mark of a deceiver and an antichrist. His readers must look to themselves, that the things wrought be not lost and that a full reward be received.

The boundary is the doctrine of Christ. Whosoever transgresseth and abideth not in it hath not God. He that abideth in that doctrine hath both the Father and the Son. Therefore, if any come without this doctrine, the readers must not receive him into the house, neither bid him God speed. To give such greeting would make the giver partaker of his evil deeds.

The elder hath many things yet to communicate, but he will not commit them to paper and ink. He trusteth to come and speak face to face, that their joy may be full.  The children of the elect lady's elect sister send their greeting.
