Manasseh king of Judah had reigned fifty and five years. He had set up the idols of Baal and made a grove, as Ahab had done before him in Israel; he had builded altars unto all the host of heaven in the courts of the house of the Lord; he had caused his sons to pass through the fire in the valley of Hinnom; and the Lord had sent prophets unto him and unto the people, but they would not hearken. And then the Lord did unto Manasseh that which no king of Judah had ever known.

And the Lord spake to Manasseh, and to his people: but they would not hearken.

Wherefore the Lord brought upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria, which took Manasseh among the thorns, and bound him with fetters, and carried him to Babylon.

And when he was in affliction, he besought the Lord his God, and humbled himself greatly before the God of his fathers.

And prayed unto him: and he was intreated of him, and heard his supplication, and brought him again to Jerusalem into his kingdom. Then Manasseh knew that the Lord he was God.

And Manasseh returned unto Jerusalem an old king with the chains of Assyria gone from his wrists. The high places he had builded he brake down; the altars in the courts of the temple he took away; the strange gods he had set among the people he removed; and the king which had been carried away in hooks unto Babylon walked the courts of the temple again, knowing the Lord which had heard him.
