The city of Jericho was straitly shut up. None went out. None came in. The walls were high. The gates were barred. The country round about was empty.

The LORD spake unto Joshua. He said: I have given into thine hand Jericho. Ye shall compass the city. Let all the men of war go round about the city once. Thus shalt thou do six days. Seven priests shall bear before the ark seven trumpets of rams' horns. On the seventh day ye shall compass the city seven times. The priests shall blow with the trumpets. When ye hear the long blast of the ram's horn, all the people shall shout with a great shout. The wall of the city shall fall down flat. The people shall ascend up every man straight before him.

Joshua called the priests. He gave the commandment. He spake unto the people. "Ye shall not shout. Let not your voice be heard. Let no word go out of your mouth until the day I bid you shout. Then shall ye shout."

The first day the armed men went before. The seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams' horns passed on before the ark of the LORD. The ark followed. The rereward came after. The trumpets blew continually. The people shouted not. They went once round the city and returned into the camp.

They did so on the second day. They did so on the third. On the fourth. On the fifth. On the sixth. Each day the silence held. The city watched from the walls.

On the seventh day they rose early about the dawning of the day. They compassed the city seven times. After the seventh time the priests blew with the trumpets with the long blast. Joshua said unto the people: shout. For the LORD hath given you the city.

The people shouted with a great shout when the priests blew with the trumpets. The wall fell down flat. The people went up into the city, every man straight before him. They took the city.
