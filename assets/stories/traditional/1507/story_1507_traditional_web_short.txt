The city of Jericho was straitly shut up. None went out. None came in. The walls were high. The gates were barred. The country around was empty.

The LORD spoke to Joshua. He said: I have given Jericho into your hand. Compass the city. Let all the men of war go round about the city once. Do this six days. Seven priests shall bear seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark. On the seventh day compass the city seven times. The priests shall blow the trumpets. When you hear the long blast of the ram's horn, all the people shall shout with a great shout. The wall of the city shall fall down flat. The people shall ascend up every man straight before him.

Joshua called the priests. He gave the command. He spoke to the people. "Do not shout. Do not let your voice be heard. Let no word go out of your mouth until the day I say: shout. Then you shall shout."

The first day the armed men went before. The seven priests bearing the seven trumpets went before the ark of the LORD. The ark followed. The rearward came after. The trumpets blew steadily. The people did not shout. They went once round the city and returned to the camp.

They did this on the second day. They did this on the third. On the fourth. On the fifth. On the sixth. Each day the silence held. The city watched from the walls.

On the seventh day they rose early about the dawning of the day. They compassed the city seven times. After the seventh time the priests blew the trumpets with the long blast. Joshua said to the people: shout. For the LORD has given you the city.

The people shouted with a great shout when the priests blew the trumpets. The wall fell down flat. The people went up into the city, every man straight before him. They took the city.
