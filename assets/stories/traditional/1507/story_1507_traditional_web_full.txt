The city of Jericho was straitly shut up. None went out. None came in. The walls were high. The gates were barred. The country around the city was empty.

The LORD spoke to Joshua. He said: I have given Jericho into your hand. Compass the city. Let all the men of war go round about the city once. Do this six days. Seven priests shall bear seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark. On the seventh day compass the city seven times. The priests shall blow the trumpets. When you hear the long blast of the ram's horn, all the people shall shout with a great shout. The wall of the city shall fall down flat. The people shall ascend up every man straight before him.

Joshua called the priests. He gave them the trumpets. He set the order of the procession. The armed men first. Seven priests with the trumpets. The ark of the LORD borne by the Levites. The rearward last. He spoke to the people. "Do not shout. Do not let your voice be heard. Let no word go out of your mouth until the day I say: shout. Then you shall shout."

The first day the procession came out of the camp. The trumpets blew steadily as they went. The ark followed. They went once round the city. The people did not shout. They returned to the camp. The watchmen on the walls of Jericho looked down at them all the while and did not understand what they saw.

The second day they did the same. The third. The fourth. The fifth. The sixth. Each day the procession went out at the morning and returned to the camp before evening. Each day the trumpets blew. Each day the people held their tongues. The city watched from the walls. Nothing was said. The walls were not touched. The men on the watch inside the city had been waiting for an attack. The attack did not come.

The seventh day they rose early about the dawning of the day. They compassed the city seven times. The morning wore on as they walked. The sun rose higher. The procession kept walking. After the seventh time the priests blew the trumpets with the long blast. Joshua said to the people: shout. For the LORD has given you the city.

The people shouted with a great shout when the priests blew the trumpets. The wall fell down flat where they stood. The wall fell along the whole circuit they had walked. The people went up into the city, every man straight before him. The streets were open to them.

Only Rahab and her household were spared, because she had hidden the messengers who had come to spy. The procession that had been silent for six days had become a great noise on the seventh. The walls that had been high were a heap of stones. The morning went on across the plain and over the broken walls, and Israel was inside the city for the first time.
