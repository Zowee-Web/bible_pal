Korah's charge sounded almost pious: everyone is holy, so why set yourselves above us? Moses fell on his face, then placed the matter before Yahweh and rebuked the Levites for seeking the priesthood as well. When judgment threatened the whole congregation, Moses and Aaron fell down again and pleaded for them. The passage holds both their appointed service and their intercession for the people. What is the difference between being called to serve and grasping for a place that was not given?
