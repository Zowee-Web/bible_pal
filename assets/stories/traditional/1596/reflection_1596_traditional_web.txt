Korah said all the congregation were holy, and Yahweh was among them — so why lift yourselves above Yahweh's assembly? Moses fell on his face, then placed the matter before Yahweh and rebuked the Levites for seeking the priesthood as well. When judgment threatened the whole congregation, Moses and Aaron fell down again and pleaded for them. What is the difference between being called to serve and grasping for a place that was not given?
