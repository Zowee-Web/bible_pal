Korah said all the congregation were holy, and the Lord was among them — so wherefore lift ye up yourselves above the congregation of the Lord? Moses fell upon his face, then placed the matter before the Lord and rebuked the Levites for seeking the priesthood also. When judgment threatened the whole congregation, Moses and Aaron fell down again and pleaded for them. What divides being called to serve from grasping for a place that was not given?
