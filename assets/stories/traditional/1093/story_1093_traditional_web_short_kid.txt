A man named Simon, who was a Pharisee, invited Jesus to his house for dinner. Everyone sat around the table eating and talking.

Then something surprising happened. A woman came in from the street. She wasn't invited. People in the town knew she had done bad things. But she came anyway, carrying a jar of very expensive perfume.

She walked over to where Jesus was sitting and stood behind him near his feet. She started crying — not a little, but A LOT. Her tears fell on Jesus' feet. She bent down and dried his feet with her hair. She kissed his feet. And she poured the beautiful perfume on them. The whole room smelled wonderful.

Simon the Pharisee watched all this and thought to himself, "If Jesus were really special, he'd know this woman is a sinner and wouldn't let her touch him."

But Jesus knew what Simon was thinking. He said, "Simon, let me tell you a story. Two people owed money. One owed a LOT and one owed a LITTLE. The person they owed the money to forgave them both. Which one do you think loved him more?"

"The one who was forgiven more, I guess," said Simon.

"Exactly right," said Jesus.

Then Jesus pointed out something important. "Simon, when I came to your house, you didn't wash my feet or greet me with a kiss. But this woman hasn't stopped crying on my feet and kissing them and pouring perfume on them."

Then Jesus said, "Her many sins are forgiven — because she loved so much."

He looked at the woman kindly. "Your faith has saved you," he said. "Go in peace."
