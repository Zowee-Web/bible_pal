One day, a man named Simon who was a Pharisee — a religious leader — invited Jesus to his house for dinner. Guests were sitting around a low table, eating and talking. Simon was watching Jesus very carefully, trying to figure out if Jesus was really as special as people said.

While they were eating, something unexpected happened. A woman came in from the street. She wasn't invited. In fact, she was known around town for doing bad things. People called her "a sinner." But she came anyway, carrying a small jar of very expensive perfume.

She walked through the room until she was standing behind Jesus, near his feet. And then she started to cry.

Not just a little bit — she cried SO much that her tears fell on Jesus' feet and made them wet. She knelt down and used her own hair to dry his feet. She kissed his feet over and over. Then she opened the jar and poured the beautiful perfume on them.

The whole room smelled amazing. But everyone was staring. Some people looked uncomfortable. Simon was thinking hard.

"If Jesus were really a prophet," Simon thought, "he'd know what kind of person this woman is. He wouldn't let her touch him."

But Jesus DID know. And he knew what Simon was thinking too.

"Simon," Jesus said, "let me tell you a little story."

"Go ahead, Teacher."

"There were two people who owed money to the same person. One owed a LOT — five hundred coins. The other owed a little — fifty coins. Neither of them could pay it back. So the person forgave BOTH of their debts — he said they didn't have to pay. Now, which one do you think loved him more?"

Simon thought about it. "I suppose the one who was forgiven more."

"Exactly right!" said Jesus.

Then Jesus looked at the woman and spoke to Simon. "See this woman? When I came to your house, you didn't offer me water to wash my feet. But SHE washed my feet with her tears and dried them with her hair. You didn't greet me with a kiss. But she hasn't stopped kissing my feet. You didn't put oil on my head. But she poured expensive perfume on my feet."

Then Jesus said something beautiful. "Her many sins are forgiven — because she loved SO much."

He looked right at the woman. "Your sins are forgiven," he said.

The other guests whispered to each other. "Who does he think he is, forgiving sins?"

But Jesus just looked at the woman with kind eyes.

"Your faith has saved you," he said. "Go in peace."
