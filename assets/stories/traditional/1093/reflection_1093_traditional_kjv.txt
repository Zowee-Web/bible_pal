She was not bidden. She belonged not there. All that she was and did in that room was wrong by the measure of them that sat at the table.

And yet she came. She came with tears and ointment and hair that she loosed in a room of men who judged her for it. She gave all that she had — not carefully, not by calculation, but utterly.

What Jesus marked was not her sin. It was her love. And he drew a line between two things that most in the room would never have joined: the depth of what she gave and the depth of what she had been forgiven.

Sometimes those who love most deeply are they that know precisely what it cost to be received again.
