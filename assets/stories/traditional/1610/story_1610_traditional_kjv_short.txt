The kingdom of heaven, said Jesus, is like unto treasure hid in a field.

A man found that treasure. He hid it again. Then, for joy thereof, he went his way. He sold all that he had, and he bought that field.

The kingdom of heaven, said Jesus again, is like unto a merchant man.

This merchant was seeking goodly pearls. When he had found one pearl of great price, he went his way. He sold all that he had, and he bought that pearl.
