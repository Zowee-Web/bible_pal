The evidence was all laid out — the dry bread, the worn shoes, the rent skins. The men of Israel took of the provisions and tasted them. They put their questions to the Gibeonites. The one thing they did not do was ask the Lord. Three days later the lie was plain, and the oath, once sworn in his name, held all the same. When dost thou stop to ask, and when dost thou decide thou hast already seen enough?
