After the multitudes had been fed with the loaves and the fishes, Jesus made the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of him to the other side of the sea while he sent the crowds away. Then he went up onto the mountain by himself to pray, and the night settled quietly around him as the last voices of the crowd faded below.

When evening came, he was there alone. By that hour the boat was already far out on the sea, distressed by the waves, for the wind was against them. The disciples strained at the oars while the dark water rose against the sides of the boat, and the small lamp at the prow swung in the spray.

In the fourth watch of the night, when the lake was still rough and the sky was without light, Jesus came to them walking on the sea. When the disciples saw him, they were troubled and cried out in fear, saying, "It is a ghost!" Immediately Jesus spoke to them and said, "Be of good cheer. It is I. Do not be afraid."

Peter answered him, "Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters." Jesus said, "Come."

Peter stepped out from the boat and began to walk on the water toward Jesus. But when he saw the strong wind and the waves rising around him, he became afraid. As he began to sink, he cried out, "Lord, save me!"

Immediately Jesus stretched out his hand and took hold of him and lifted him up. He said to him, "You of little faith, why did you doubt?"

When they came into the boat together, the wind ceased. Those who were in the boat worshiped him and said, "Truly you are the Son of God."
