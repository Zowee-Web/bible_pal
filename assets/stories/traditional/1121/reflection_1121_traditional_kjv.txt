She lifted not up her voice. She had no need. The chamber was empty enough that her silence was already a kind of speech, and her tears were already a kind of prayer.

What stayeth with thee about Hannah is not the answer she received. It is the moment before — the long unanswered ache, sitting with her in a quiet room, with none watching but God.

Eli got it wrong. People will get it wrong. Sometimes the deepest grief seemeth, from without, like something else entirely. The marvellous thing is that her honest words turned him — and that the heavens were not turned, for the heavens had been hearkening all along.

Her son's name was a sentence she could carry all the days of her life: I have asked him of the Lord. Not every prayer is answered as Hannah's was. But every prayer is heard as Hannah's was — by a God who readeth lips that no other can see move.
