The voice came to Isaiah, and it told him what to say. The country around him was very still when it began. He had not been expecting to hear anything that day. He had been sitting in his own house when the voice came. The shutters were open. The afternoon light was across the floor.

"Comfort, comfort my people," it said. "Speak comfortably to Jerusalem. Cry to her that her warfare has been finished. Her iniquity has been pardoned. She has received from the LORD's hand double for all her sins."

The voice paused. Then it went on.

It told him what would be said in the years to come. There would be one crying out in the wilderness, in a place where no one had thought to listen.

"Prepare the way of the LORD. Make a highway in the desert for our God. Every valley shall be lifted up. Every mountain and hill shall be made low. The crooked shall be made straight. The rough places shall be made plain. The glory of the LORD shall be revealed. All flesh together shall see it. The mouth of the LORD has spoken it."

Then the voice gave him words that were sharp. The words were carried on a wind. They had to be spoken even though no one wanted to hear them.

"All flesh is grass. All its glory is as the flower of the field. The grass withers. The flower fades. The breath of the LORD blows upon it. Surely the people are grass. The grass withers. The flower fades."

The voice paused again. Then the last part came.

"But the word of our God shall stand forever."

The voice softened then. The last part of what was said was the part that would be remembered longest.

"Get up onto a high mountain, O Zion, you that bring good news. Lift up your voice with strength. Lift it up. Be not afraid. Say to the cities of Judah: Behold your God."

"Behold," the voice said. "The Lord GOD will come as a mighty one. His arm rules for him. His reward is with him. His recompense is before him. He will feed his flock like a shepherd. He will gather the lambs with his arm. He will carry them in his bosom. He will gently lead those that have their young."

The voice fell silent at the end of that. Isaiah did not move for a while. The room around him was still. Then he rose from where he had been sitting. He went to the door of the house. The country opened in front of him. The long road went out to the city. The houses by the highway were quiet in the late afternoon. The lambs in the small flocks were set out across the hills. The shepherds were among them. He saw what the voice had told him should be seen. He stood at the door a long while in the late light before he went back inside.
