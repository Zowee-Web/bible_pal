David and his men came up unto the walls of Jerusalem.

The Jebusites stood upon the wall above them. They were the inhabitants of the land. They had holden the strong hold of Zion since the days before David was born.

They called down unto him.

"Except thou take away the blind and the lame, thou shalt not come in hither," they said. "David cannot come in hither."

The wall stood high above the slope. The gate was shut and barred. The slope below was steep and broken with stones.

David sat down before the strong hold with his men. He beheld the walls. He beheld the watercourse that came down out of the rock under the wall — the channel where the water of the spring ran into the city.

He said unto his men, "Whosoever smiteth the Jebusites — let him go up by the gutter."

His men went unto the watercourse.

They came unto the mouth of the shaft where it ran out beneath the wall. The water was running through it from the spring above. Their feet were wet at the entrance.

They climbed up into the rock in the dark. Their feet pressed against the wet stone. Their hands found holds above them. They could hear the water running beside them in the channel. They could not see the man before them. They went up by feel, one stone at a time.

They came out at the top of the shaft.

They came up into the city by the channel. They smote the wall from within. They opened the gate unto David below.

David came up into the strong hold of Zion.

He took the city. He called it the city of David. He built round about from Millo and inward. He dwelt in the fort which he had taken.

The wall the Jebusites had holden stood now round about the house of David. The Lord God of hosts was with him.
