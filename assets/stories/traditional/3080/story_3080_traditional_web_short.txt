Samuel called the people together before Yahweh at Mizpah. He told the children of Israel what Yahweh, the God of Israel, had said: “I brought Israel up out of Egypt. I delivered you from the Egyptians and from all the kingdoms that oppressed you.” Samuel continued, “But today you have rejected your God, who saves you from all your calamities and distresses. You have said, ‘No! Set a king over us!’” He then directed the people to present themselves before Yahweh by their tribes and thousands. Each tribe and each thousand would come forward in turn.

Samuel brought all the tribes of Israel near, and the tribe of Benjamin was chosen. He brought Benjamin near by its families, and the family of the Matrites was chosen. Then Saul son of Kish was chosen. When the people looked for Saul, however, they could not find him.

They asked Yahweh, “Is there yet a man to come here?” Yahweh answered that Saul had hidden himself among the baggage. The people ran and brought him from there. When Saul stood among them, he was taller than anyone else from his shoulders upward.

Samuel addressed all the people. “Do you see him whom Yahweh has chosen? There is no one like him among all the people.” The people shouted, “Long live the king!”

Samuel then explained the regulations of the kingdom to the people. He wrote them in a book and laid the book up before Yahweh. Afterward Samuel sent everyone away, each man to his own house.

Saul also returned to his house in Gibeah. An army went with him, men whose hearts God had touched. Yet certain worthless fellows questioned him. “How could this man save us?” they said. They despised Saul and brought him no tribute. Saul heard their rejection and held his peace.
