Samuel called the people together unto the Lord at Mizpeh. He declared unto the children of Israel the words of the Lord God of Israel: “I brought up Israel out of Egypt, and delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all kingdoms and of them that oppressed you.” Samuel continued, “Ye have this day rejected your God, who himself saved you out of all your adversities and tribulations. Ye have said unto him, ‘Nay, but set a king over us.’” Therefore he commanded them to present themselves before the Lord by their tribes and by their thousands.

Samuel caused all the tribes of Israel to come near, and the tribe of Benjamin was taken. Then Benjamin came near by its families, and the family of Matri was taken. Saul the son of Kish was taken also; but when the people sought him, he could not be found.

They enquired of the Lord further whether the man should yet come thither. The Lord answered, “Behold, he hath hid himself among the stuff.” They ran and fetched Saul thence. When he stood among the people, he was higher than any of them from his shoulders and upward.

Samuel said unto all the people, “See ye him whom the Lord hath chosen, that there is none like him among all the people?” Then all the people shouted, “God save the king.”

Samuel told the people the manner of the kingdom. He wrote it in a book and laid it up before the Lord. Then he sent all the people away, every man unto his house.

Saul likewise went home to Gibeah. A band of men went with him, men whose hearts God had touched. But the children of Belial said, “How shall this man save us?” They despised Saul and brought him no presents. Saul answered them not, but held his peace.
