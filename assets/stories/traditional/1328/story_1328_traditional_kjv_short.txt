The Psalm was sung of those who had come home. They had come from Babylon. They had come from Persia. They had come from the lands south of Egypt and from the far villages of the diaspora — from every quarter they had been carried, and from every quarter they had returned. And upon the steps of the second temple, and in the gates of Jerusalem, the redeemed of the Lord sang together, and they remembered.

O give thanks unto the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.

Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy;

And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.

They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in.

Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.

Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses.

And he led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation.

Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!

For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.

And the singers sang on. The Psalm arose over the city. The redeemed who had come home gave thanks together — they of the east beside they of the west, they of the north beside they of the south. And so it was at the temple in those days, when the redeemed of the Lord gathered to give thanks.
