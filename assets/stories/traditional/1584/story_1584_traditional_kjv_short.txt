Jesus spake a parable unto his disciples to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint, nor to lose heart in the asking, however long the answer was in coming.

There was in a certain city, said he, a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man. He answered to no one, and no man's opinion moved him.

And there was a widow in that same city. She had an adversary, one that had wronged her, and none to plead her cause but herself. So she came unto the judge, and sought justice of him. Avenge me of mine adversary. And he would not. She came again, and spake the same words. Avenge me of mine adversary. And still he would not. Yet she would not cease. She came again, and yet again, day after day, ever the same widow with the same petition, and would not be turned away.

For a long while the judge gave her no heed. He had no regard for her, and no fear of God to move him. But afterward he said within himself, I fear not God, and I regard not man. Yet this widow will not let me alone; she wearieth me with her continual coming. I will avenge her of her adversary, if only to be rid of her.

And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith. If even such a man as this will at the last give way, shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry unto him day and night? He beareth long with them, but he will not put them off for ever. I tell you of a truth that he will avenge them, and that speedily.

Nevertheless, said he, when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?
