Jesus spake a parable unto his disciples, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint.

In a certain city there was a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man. And there was a widow in that city, which came unto him continually, saying, Avenge me of mine adversary. For a while he would not. But afterward he said within himself, Though I fear not God, nor regard man, yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me.

And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith. Shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him? Though he bear long with them, I tell you that he will avenge them speedily.

Nevertheless, when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?
