One day Jesus told his disciples a parable, to teach them that they should always pray and never lose heart, however long the answer was in coming.

There was a judge, he said, in a certain city — a man who did not fear God and had no regard for anyone. He answered to no one, and no one's opinion moved him.

In that same city there was a widow. She had an adversary, someone who had wronged her, and no one to plead her case but herself. So she came to the judge and asked him for justice. "Give me justice against my adversary." He would not. She came back and asked again, in the same words. "Give me justice against my adversary." Still he refused. But she would not give up. She came again, and again, day after day, always the same widow with the same plea.

For a long while the judge paid her no attention. He had no interest in her, and no fear of God to move him. But at last he said to himself, "I do not fear God, and I care nothing for people. Yet this widow will not leave me alone. She wears me out with her endless coming. I will give her the justice she asks — if only to be rid of her."

Then the Lord said, "Listen to what that unjust judge says. If even a man like that finally gives in, will God not bring justice to his own chosen ones, who cry out to him day and night? He is patient with them, but he will not put them off forever. I tell you, he will see them vindicated, and quickly.

"And yet, when the Son of Man comes — will he find faith on the earth?"
