The first guests all gave reasons to stay away—a field, five yoke of oxen, and a marriage. The master did not cancel the banquet. He sent his servant into the city to bring in the poor, the crippled, the blind, and the lame, then farther into the roads and hedges so that his house would be filled. The first invited would not taste his banquet. What ordinary claim keeps sounding more urgent than the invitation before you?
