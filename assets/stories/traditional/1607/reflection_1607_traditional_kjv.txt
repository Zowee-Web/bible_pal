The first guests all gave reasons to stay away—a piece of ground, five yoke of oxen, and a marriage. The master did not cancel the supper. He sent his servant into the city to bring in the poor, the maimed, the halt, and the blind, then farther into the highways and hedges that his house might be filled. The first bidden would not taste his supper. What ordinary claim keepeth sounding more urgent than the invitation before thee?
