One who sat at meat with Jesus heard these things, and said unto him, "Blessed is he that shall eat bread in the kingdom of God."

Then said Jesus unto him:

A certain man made a great supper, and bade many. When supper time came, he sent his servant to tell the invited men that all things were ready, and that they should come.

But all the invited men began alike to make excuse.

The first said, "I have bought a piece of ground, and I must needs go and see it. I pray thee have me excused."

Another answered, "I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I go to prove them. I pray thee have me excused."

A third said, "I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come."

The servant came again to his lord, and shewed him these things. Then the master of the house was angry.

"Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city," he said, "and bring in hither the poor, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blind."

The servant reported, "Lord, it is done as thou hast commanded, and yet there is room."

"Go out into the highways and hedges," said his lord, "and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled. For I say unto you, That none of those men which were bidden shall taste of my supper."
