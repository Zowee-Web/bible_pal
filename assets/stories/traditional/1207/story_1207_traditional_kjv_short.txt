In the days when the people of Israel had turned away from the Lord, and the prophets cried in the cities of the north, the word of the Lord came unto Hosea the son of Beeri. He stood among his people, and he lifted up his voice, and he gave them the call of the Lord.

Hosea said, "Come, and let us return unto the Lord: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up."

"After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight."

"Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the Lord: his going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth."

Hosea beheld the cities of the north, and the fields lay before him. The rains had not yet come, and the ground was dry. The people hearkened unto his voice, and some turned one to another, and some stood still.

"O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away."

"Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of my mouth: and thy judgments are as the light that goeth forth."

"For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings."

The wind passed over the fields, and the morning waxed warm above the houses of Samaria. Hosea abode among the people, and his voice went forth into the towns of the north.

The words of the prophet were written down, and they were carried into the days that came after, and the call to return remained in the land.
