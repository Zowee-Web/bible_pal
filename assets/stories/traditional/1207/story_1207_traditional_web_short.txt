In the days when the people of Israel had turned away from the Lord, and the prophets cried in the cities of the north, the word of the Lord came to Hosea the son of Beeri. He stood among his people, and he lifted up his voice, and he gave them the call of the Lord.

Hosea said, "Come, let us return to the Lord, for he has torn us to pieces, and he will heal us. He has injured us, and he will bind up our wounds."

"After two days he will revive us. On the third day he will raise us up, and we will live before him."

"Let us acknowledge the Lord. Let us press on to know the Lord. As surely as the sun rises, the Lord will appear. He will come to us like the rain, like the spring rain that waters the earth."

Hosea looked across the cities of the north, and the fields lay before him. The rains had not yet come, and the ground was dry. The people listened to his voice, and some turned to one another, and some stood quietly.

"Israel, what shall I do with you? Judah, what shall I do with you? Your love is like a morning cloud, and like the dew that disappears early."

"Therefore I have cut them to pieces by the prophets. I have killed them by the words of my mouth. Your judgments are like a light that shines forth."

"For I desire mercy, and not sacrifice. I desire the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings."

The wind moved across the fields, and the morning grew warm above the houses of Samaria. Hosea remained among the people, and his voice went out into the towns of the north.

The words of the prophet were written down, and they were carried into the days that came after, and the call to return remained in the land.
