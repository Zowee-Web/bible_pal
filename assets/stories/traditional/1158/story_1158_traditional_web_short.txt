In the twentieth year of Artaxerxes the king, in the month Chislev, Nehemiah was in Susa the palace. Hanani, one of his brothers, came in from Judah, and Nehemiah asked him about the Jews who had escaped, and about Jerusalem.

Hanani said to him, "The remnant who are left of the captivity in the province are in great affliction and reproach. The wall of Jerusalem is also broken down, and its gates are burned with fire."

When Nehemiah heard these words, he sat down and wept, and mourned for many days. He fasted and prayed before the God of heaven.

He said, "I beg you, Lord, the God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps covenant and loving kindness with those who love him and keep his commandments. Let your ear now be attentive, and your eyes open, that you may listen to the prayer of your servant which I pray before you at this time, day and night, for the children of Israel your servants."

He continued, "I confess the sins of the children of Israel, which we have sinned against you. Yes, I and my father's house have sinned. We have dealt very corruptly against you, and have not kept the commandments which you commanded your servant Moses."

Then Nehemiah said, "Remember, I beg you, the word that you commanded your servant Moses, saying, 'If you transgress, I will scatter you abroad among the peoples. But if you return to me, and keep my commandments and do them, though your outcasts were in the uttermost part of the heavens, yet I will gather them from there, and bring them to the place that I have chosen.'"

He said, "Now these are your servants and your people, whom you have redeemed by your great power, and by your strong hand."

Then he prayed, "O Lord, I beg you, let now your ear be attentive to the prayer of your servant, and to the prayer of your servants who delight to fear your name. Please prosper your servant today, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man."

Now Nehemiah was the king's cupbearer.
