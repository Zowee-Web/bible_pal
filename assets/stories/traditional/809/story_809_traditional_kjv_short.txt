When the even was come upon the shores of the sea of Galilee, Jesus said unto his disciples, "Let us pass over unto the other side." Leaving the multitude, they took him with them, even as he was, in the ship. Other little ships were with them also. The sky grew dim, and the face of the water shifted from quiet unto uneasy as the wind began to stir. The disciples, weary from the day, felt an unrest creep upon them as the darkness settled round about.

And suddenly there arose a great storm of wind. The wind howled across the lake, and mighty waves beat against the sides of the ship. The water came in over the side, filling the vessel faster than they could empty it. The voices of the disciples were lifted up in alarm as the ship rocked and groaned, the timber straining beneath the relentless waves. The cold spray smote their faces, and their garments clave unto their bodies as they laboured to keep afloat. Above the roar of the wind, their hearts beat hard with fear, for the storm threatened to overturn the ship and drown them all.

In the midst of the tumult, Jesus was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep on a pillow. He slept on, untroubled by the danger. The disciples, frantic and afraid, came in haste and awaked him. "Master, carest thou not that we perish?" they cried, their voices trembling with fear and doubt. The sound of the water in the ship and the fury of the storm pressed upon them with every passing moment.

And he arose and stood in the battered ship, calm in the midst of the terror. With authority he rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, "Peace, be still." And straightway the wind ceased, and the waves grew gentle. The air, which had been filled with the wild noise of the storm, settled into an unearthly stillness. The sudden quiet pressed round about them — the ship resting upon calm water, the clouds parting overhead.

Then he turned unto his disciples and said, "Why are ye so fearful? how is it that ye have no faith?" And the disciples, filled now with awe and a deeper kind of fear, beheld one another and said, "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"
