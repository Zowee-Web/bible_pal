The sun was gently setting over the quiet little town. Houses stood close together, their windows catching the soft evening light. Smoke floated slowly from chimneys. The air smelled warm and cozy, like baking bread and soup on the stove. Birds settled on rooftops. In one yard, a family was finishing their supper outside. The garden flowers closed for the night, and a gentle breeze rustled through the tall grass.

Around the town, builders and helpers worked all day long. Their tools made soft tapping sounds as they placed wood and stones. Some builders swept up sawdust while others stacked bricks in neat rows. Each person moved carefully, setting each piece in just the right place. Ladders leaned against new walls, and baskets of nails rested nearby. Children walked along the path, watching the workers and humming gentle tunes.

Near the gardens, people thoughtfully checked the gates. One person opened them wide in the morning and closed them again each night. Others swept the stones, making the way clean and smooth. Door latches clicked softly. Flowers grew along the fences, pink and blue and yellow. There was no hurry, just peaceful movement and the gentle sounds of home.

As the work ended, someone quietly read words from a special scroll. The words said, “Unless the Lord builds the house, their work is not full. Unless the Lord protects the city, those who guard it do not keep it safe by themselves.” Everyone listened as the words floated through the evening air. The families remembered these words as they finished their day. The helpers put away their tools, and the keepers closed the gates safely.

A gentle hush filled the town. Soft voices sang a quiet song by the doorway. Lanterns glowed with warm light. Children sat with their parents, listening to the peaceful words. The people spread out soft blankets and fluffed their pillows. The breeze cooled the air, and the sky turned comfortable and blue. Everyone felt quiet as the listeners heard, “It is no use to wake up very early or stay up very late, working hard for bread to eat. The Lord gives sleep to those he loves.”

Soon, all through the little town, lights blinked out one by one. Windows glowed softly, then dimmed. People rested together, safe and warm. Quiet night sounds moved gently outside. In each home, families lay down in peace. The moonlight shone on rooftops and gardens. There was only calm, slow breathing. The people slept quietly, held safe in the peaceful night, ready to wake to another gentle day.
