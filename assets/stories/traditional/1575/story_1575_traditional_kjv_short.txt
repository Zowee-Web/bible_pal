Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, said Jesus, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.

Five of them were wise, and five were foolish. They that were foolish took their lamps, and took no oil with them. But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps — enough to fill them again when they burned low. So ten lamps went forth to meet the bridegroom, and only five carried oil to keep them burning.

The bridegroom did not come at once. The evening stretched on, and while he tarried, they all slumbered and slept — the wise and the foolish alike.

Then, at midnight, there was a cry made in the dark: Behold, the bridegroom cometh! Go ye out to meet him!

Then all those virgins arose, and each one trimmed her lamp to make it burn. Five lamps steadied and burned. Five were going out.

Give us of your oil, said the foolish unto the wise, for our lamps are gone out.

But the wise answered, Not so; lest there be not enough for us and you. Go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves.

So the foolish went away to buy. And while they went, the bridegroom came. They that were ready — the five whose lamps still burned — went in with him to the marriage.

And the door was shut behind them.

Afterward came also the other virgins. They stood without, at the shut door, and called, Lord, Lord, open to us!

But the answer came from within: Verily I say unto you, I know you not. And the door was not opened.

Then said Jesus, Watch therefore. For ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh.
