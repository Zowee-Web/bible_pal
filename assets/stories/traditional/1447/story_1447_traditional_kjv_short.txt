The priests came down unto the brink of Jordan with the ark of the covenant upon their shoulders.

The people came behind them down the bank from their tents.

The river was full. Jordan overflowed all his banks at the time of harvest. The water ran swift against the stones.

The feet of the priests came unto the brim of the water. The feet of the priests were dipped therein.

The hem of the priests' robes drew wet against the surface. The current pulled at their legs.

Then the waters which came down from above stood still.

They rose up upon an heap, a great way off, at the city Adam beside Zaretan. From where the people stood, the wall of water might be seen against the sky. The waters that had been below the priests went on, down unto the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, and were cut off.

The river bed was dry where the priests stood. The stones lay bare where the water had been. The mire was yet wet from the morning.

The priests bare the ark down into the midst of Jordan. They stood firm there upon dry ground. The water above them held.

The people of Israel came down unto the river. They came past the priests. They passed over on dry ground.

They came over against Jericho. The first of them set their feet upon the further bank. The last of them set their feet upon the further bank. All the people were passed clean over.

When the people were wholly gone over, the priests came up out of the river bed.

The waters returned again upon the stones. Jordan was full as before. The banks held the river as they had holden it the day before.

But the children of Israel were come unto the other shore.
