The priests came down to the edge of the Jordan with the ark of the covenant on their shoulders.

The people came behind them down the bank from their tents.

The river was full. The Jordan overflowed all its banks at the time of harvest. The water ran fast against the stones.

The feet of the priests came to the edge of the river. The feet of the priests entered the water.

The hem of the priests' robes drew wet against the surface. The current pulled at their legs.

Then the water from above stood still.

It rose up in one heap, a great way off, at the city of Adam beside Zarethan. From where the people stood, the wall of water could be seen against the sky. The water that had been below the priests went on, down to the Sea of the Arabah, down to the Salt Sea, and was cut off.

The river bed was dry where the priests stood. The stones lay exposed where the water had been. The mud was still wet from the morning.

The priests carried the ark down into the middle of the Jordan. They stood firm there on the dry ground. The water above them held.

The people of Israel came down to the river. They came past the priests. They passed over on dry ground.

They came across the Jordan opposite Jericho. The first of them set their feet on the other bank. The last of them set their feet on the other bank. The whole nation came over.

When the people had passed completely, the priests came up out of the river bed.

The water came back down over the stones. The Jordan was full again. The banks held the river the way they had held it the day before.

But the people of Israel had come to the other shore.
