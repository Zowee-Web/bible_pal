Jairus was a man who held authority. Others deferred to him. Yet when his daughter lay dying, none of that availed. He fell in the dust at a stranger's feet and besought him.

Then came the delay — the woman in the crowd, the question, the waiting. And then the worst tidings: too late. She is dead. Trouble not the Master.

What is striking is that Jesus did not hasten. He stopped for the woman. He took his time. And when the messenger came bearing the worst words, he said only this: fear not.

Sometimes the hardest part of trust is the waiting — especially when every moment seemeth to matter too greatly to be spent.
