The crowd was waiting at the shore when Jesus stepped from the boat. They pressed around him — dozens of bodies, voices rising, hands reaching.

A man came pushing through. Jairus, a ruler of the synagogue. His robes were fine but his face was gray. He fell at Jesus' feet, his knees striking the sand. He begged him to come to his house. His only daughter — twelve years old — was dying.

Jesus went with him. The crowd followed, pressing on every side in the narrow street.

A woman moved through the crowd behind Jesus. She had been bleeding for twelve years. She had spent everything on physicians, and none could heal her. She reached forward and touched the fringe of his garment. The bleeding stopped immediately.

Jesus stopped walking. "Who touched me?"

Peter said, "Master, the multitudes press you on every side."

"Someone touched me," Jesus said. "I perceived that power went out from me."

The woman came forward trembling. She fell before him and told him everything — why she had touched him, how she had been healed at once. The crowd stood still around them. Jairus stood nearby, his face tight, his daughter still dying behind him.

"Daughter," Jesus said to the woman, "your faith has made you well. Go in peace."

While he was still speaking, a man came from Jairus' house. He was out of breath. "Your daughter is dead," he said. "Do not trouble the Teacher."

Jesus turned to Jairus. "Do not be afraid. Only believe, and she will be made well."

They reached the house. Mourners were weeping outside. Jesus took only Peter, John, James, and the girl's parents inside.

"Do not weep," he said. "She is not dead, but sleeping."

They laughed at him. They knew she was dead.

Jesus crossed to where the girl lay on a low mat. He took her hand. "Child, arise," he said.

Her spirit returned. She rose up immediately. Her parents stood motionless, staring. Jesus told them to give her something to eat.
