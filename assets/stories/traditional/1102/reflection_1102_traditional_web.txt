Jairus was a man with authority. People deferred to him. But when his daughter was dying, none of that mattered. He fell in the dust at a stranger's feet and begged.

Then came the delay — the woman in the crowd, the conversation, the waiting. And then the worst news: too late. She is dead. Do not bother him anymore.

What is striking is that Jesus did not rush. He stopped for the woman. He took his time. And when the messenger came with the worst possible words, he said only this: do not be afraid.

Sometimes the hardest part of trust is the waiting — especially when every moment feels like it matters too much to waste.
