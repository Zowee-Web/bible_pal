There was a woman who had been sick for a very long time — twelve whole years. She had gone to many doctors, but none of them could help her. She had spent all her money trying to get better, but she only got worse.

Then she heard about Jesus. People were saying he could heal the sick. She believed it.

One day, she saw a big crowd of people following Jesus down the road. She squeezed into the crowd and came up behind him, very quietly.

She thought to herself, "If I can just touch his clothes, I'll be healed."

She reached out her hand and touched the edge of his robe.

Right away, she felt different. The sickness was gone. She could feel it in her whole body. She was healed!

At that very same moment, Jesus stopped walking. He turned around and looked at the crowd.

"Who touched my clothes?" he asked.

His friends said, "There are people bumping into you everywhere! Why are you asking who touched you?"

But Jesus kept looking around.

The woman was scared. She was shaking. But she came forward and knelt down in front of him. She told him everything — all about her sickness, all the years, and how she had touched his robe.

Jesus looked at her with kindness.

"Daughter," he said, "your faith has made you well. Go in peace."
