Twelve years is a long time to carry something alone. The kind of quiet suffering that no one sees — the appointments that lead nowhere, the money that runs out, the slow erosion of hope.

What she did was small. She reached out and touched the edge of a robe in a crowded street. It was not a grand gesture. It was the last thing she had left to try.

And Jesus stopped. In the middle of a pressing crowd, with people pulling at him from every side, he stopped — not because he had to, but because someone had reached for him. He wanted to know who.

Sometimes the bravest thing a person can do is reach out one more time, even when every reason to try has already been spent.
