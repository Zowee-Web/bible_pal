In a village in Galilee, a crowd pressed along a dusty road. People pushed forward from every side, trying to get close to a man named Jesus. His robe was dark with dust from the road. The sun was high, and the air was warm. Voices rose and fell around him. Children called out to their mothers. Hands stretched toward him. Everywhere he went, the people followed.

In that crowd was a woman. She had been bleeding for twelve years. Twelve long years of pain, and no cure. She had gone to one physician, and then another. She had suffered many things at their hands. She had spent all she had — every coin, every possession — and still the bleeding had not stopped. It had only grown worse.

She had heard the things people were saying about Jesus. Healings. Miracles. The sick made whole.

That day, she followed the crowd. She did not speak. She did not lift her face. She came up behind him quietly, step by step.

She said within herself, "If I just touch his clothes, I will be made well."

She reached out her hand. Her fingertips brushed the edge of his garment.

At once, something changed. The bleeding stopped. She felt it in her body, a stillness where there had been none. She knew she had been healed of her affliction.

In that same moment, Jesus stopped walking. He turned in the crowd. He had felt it too — power going out of him.

"Who touched my clothes?" he asked.

The disciples looked at him and shook their heads. "You see the multitude pressing against you on every side," they said, "and yet you ask, 'Who touched me?'"

But Jesus kept looking. His eyes moved from face to face.

The woman stood very still. She was trembling now. She knew what had been done to her. She stepped forward from the crowd and fell down at his feet. The dust rose around her knees. She lifted her eyes to his and told him everything — the twelve years, the physicians, the money spent, the reaching, the touch. She told him all the truth.

Jesus looked at her with kindness. He spoke gently.

"Daughter," he said, "your faith has made you well. Go in peace, and be healed of your disease."
