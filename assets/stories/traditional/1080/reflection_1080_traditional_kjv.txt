Twelve years is a long season to bear a thing alone. The manner of quiet suffering that none perceiveth — the remedies that avail not, the silver that runneth out, the slow fading of hope.

What she did was small. She put forth her hand and touched the hem of a garment in a thronged street. It was no grand act. It was the last thing remaining for her to try.

And Jesus stood still. In the midst of a pressing multitude, with men pulling at him from every quarter, he stopped — not because he must, but because one soul had reached for him. He would know who.

Sometimes the bravest thing a soul can do is to reach forth once more, even when every cause for trying hath already been spent.
