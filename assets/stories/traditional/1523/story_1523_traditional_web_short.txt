The work of the LORD's house had begun. The foundation had been laid. The people had wept and shouted at the laying of it. The work was going on.

Then there came the adversaries.

The adversaries were the people the king of Assyria had brought into the land long ago. They had been put in the cities of Samaria. They were not of the children of the captivity. They saw the work and came down to it.

They said unto Zerubbabel and the chief of the fathers: let us build with you. For we seek your God, as ye do. We do sacrifice unto him since the days of Esar-haddon king of Assur, which brought us up hither.

Zerubbabel said unto them: ye have nothing to do with us to build an house unto our God. We ourselves together will build unto the LORD God of Israel, as king Cyrus the king of Persia hath commanded us.

The adversaries went away from the work. They did not stay away.

They weakened the hands of the people of Judah. They troubled them in building. They hired counsellors against them. They did so all the days of Cyrus king of Persia. They did so unto the reign of Darius king of Persia.

In the days of Ahasuerus, in the beginning of his reign, they wrote an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem.

In the days of Artaxerxes, they wrote a letter against Jerusalem. The letter was sent. The letter was read before the king.

The king answered. He commanded that the work cease. The work ceased. The men went home from it. The walls were as they had been.

So the work ceased unto the second year of the reign of Darius king of Persia.

That was many years. The stones lay where they had been laid. The wood lay where it had been carried in. The people went on with their houses. The house of the LORD was left waiting.
