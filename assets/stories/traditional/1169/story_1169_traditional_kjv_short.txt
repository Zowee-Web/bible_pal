After Samuel was weaned, Hannah took him up with her, with three bullocks, and one ephah of flour, and a bottle of wine, and brought him unto the house of the Lord in Shiloh. The child was young, and Hannah held him by the hand as they went up.

She slew the bullock, and she brought the child to Eli the priest. She said unto him, "Oh my lord, as thy soul liveth, my lord, I am the woman that stood by thee here, praying unto the Lord. For this child I prayed, and the Lord hath given me my petition which I asked of him."

She said, "Therefore also I have lent him to the Lord. As long as he liveth he shall be lent to the Lord."

Then Hannah knelt down, and she let not go of the boy at the first. She looked into his face, and the lamps were burning before the ark of the Lord behind them. Then she arose, and she gave the child into the hand of Eli the priest.

She said, "He shall serve the Lord here in Shiloh."

Hannah lifted up her voice and rejoiced. She said, "My heart rejoiceth in the Lord. He hath given me my petition. The barren hath borne, and the Lord hath remembered his handmaid."

Then Eli took the child, and he led him into the chamber where the priests served. Samuel wept not, for he was given over unto the Lord.

Hannah and Elkanah went home to Ramah, and the boy abode in Shiloh to minister before Eli. The morning was clear above the road as they went, and Hannah turned her face once to look back, but she returned not.

Year by year, his mother made him a little coat, and brought it to him when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice. The Lord blessed Hannah, and she bare three more sons and two daughters. And the child Samuel grew before the Lord.
