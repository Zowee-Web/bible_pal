After Samuel was weaned, Hannah took him up with her, with three bulls, and one ephah of meal, and a container of wine, and brought him to the house of the Lord at Shiloh. The child was young, and Hannah held him by the hand as they went up.

She slew the bull, and she brought the child to Eli the priest. She said to him, "Oh, my lord, as your soul lives, my lord, I am the woman who stood by you here, praying to the Lord. For this child I prayed, and the Lord has given me my petition which I asked of him."

She said, "Therefore I have also given him to the Lord. As long as he lives he is given to the Lord."

Then Hannah knelt down, and she did not let go of the boy at first. She looked into his face, and the lamps were burning before the ark of the Lord behind them. Then she rose, and she gave the child into the hand of Eli the priest.

She said, "He shall serve the Lord here in Shiloh."

Hannah lifted up her voice and rejoiced. She said, "My heart rejoices in the Lord. He has given me my petition. The barren has borne, and the Lord has remembered his servant."

Then Eli took the child, and he led him into the chamber where the priests served. Samuel did not weep, for he was given over to the Lord.

Hannah and Elkanah went home to Ramah, and the boy stayed in Shiloh to serve before Eli. The morning was clear over the road as they went, and Hannah turned her face once to look back, but she did not return.

Year by year, his mother made him a little robe and brought it to him when she came up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice. The Lord blessed Hannah, and she bore three more sons and two daughters. The child Samuel grew before the Lord.
