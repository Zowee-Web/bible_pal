Paul wrote: behold, I shew you a mystery. We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump. Death is swallowed up in victory. O death, where is thy sting? Therefore, be ye stedfast, unmovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord. What is it to be told the trumpet shall sound, while thou art still inside the corruptible body that must put on the incorruption?
