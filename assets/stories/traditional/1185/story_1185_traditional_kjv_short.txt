In those days came John the Baptist, preaching in the wilderness of Judaea. He had his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey, and his voice rose along the banks of the Jordan.

He said, "Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand."

For this is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias, saying, "The voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.'"

Then went out unto him Jerusalem, and all Judaea, and all the region round about Jordan. And they were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins. The water was cool in the morning, and the people came down the banks unto him.

But when he beheld many of the Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism, he said unto them, "O generation of vipers, who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come?"

He said, "Bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance. And think not to say within yourselves, 'We have Abraham to our father.' For I say unto you, that God is able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham."

He said, "And now also the axe is laid unto the root of the trees. Therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire."

He said, "I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear. He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire."

He said, "Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor. And he shall gather his wheat into the garner; but the chaff he will burn with fire unquenchable."

The multitudes hearkened unto him along the banks of the Jordan, and many came forth to be baptized. The river was full of them that had come up out of the water, and the morning sun lay across the wilderness as John continued to preach.
