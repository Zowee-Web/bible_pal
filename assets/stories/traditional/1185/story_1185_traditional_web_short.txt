In those days, John the Baptizer came, preaching in the wilderness of Judea. He wore clothing made of camel's hair with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts and wild honey, and his voice rose along the banks of the Jordan.

He said, "Repent, for the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand."

For this is he who was spoken of by Isaiah the prophet, saying, "The voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Make ready the way of the Lord. Make his paths straight.'"

Then people from Jerusalem, all of Judea, and all the region around the Jordan went out to him. They were baptized by him in the Jordan, confessing their sins. The water was cool in the morning, and the people came down the banks to him.

But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming for his baptism, he said to them, "You offspring of vipers, who warned you to flee from the wrath to come?"

He said, "Therefore produce fruit worthy of repentance. Don't think to yourselves, 'We have Abraham for our father.' For I tell you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham from these stones."

He said, "Even now the ax lies at the root of the trees. Therefore every tree that doesn't produce good fruit is cut down, and cast into the fire."

He said, "I indeed baptize you in water for repentance, but he who comes after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you in the Holy Spirit and with fire."

He said, "His winnowing fork is in his hand, and he will thoroughly cleanse his threshing floor. He will gather his wheat into the barn, but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire."

The crowds listened to him along the banks of the Jordan, and many came forward to be baptized. The river was full of those who had come up out of the water, and the morning sun lay across the wilderness as John continued to preach.
