David named the waiting first.

He had waited patiently for the LORD. The waiting was the thing. The waiting had been long. He had not given it up. He had stayed at the waiting till the LORD inclined unto him and heard his cry.

The LORD heard. That was the first turn of the psalm.

Then David named the pit.

He had been in the horrible pit. He had been in the miry clay. The clay was at the bottom of the pit. It went up over the feet of him that stood in it. He could not lift his feet from it.

The LORD brought him up out of the pit. He brought him up out of the miry clay. He set his feet upon a rock. He established his goings. The rock was the opposite of the clay. The going was the opposite of the standing fixed.

Then David named the new song.

The LORD had put a new song in his mouth. The song was praise unto God. Many should see it. Many should fear. Many should trust in the LORD because of it. The new song was for them also.

Then David named the man who is blessed.

Blessed is that man, said David, that maketh the LORD his trust. He respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies. He trusteth the LORD. That is the whole of his trust.

Then David turned the psalm unto the works of the LORD.

Many, O LORD my God, said David, are thy wonderful works which thou hast done. Thy thoughts which are to us-ward cannot be reckoned up in order unto thee. If I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be numbered.

Then David came to the close.

Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire. Mine ears hast thou opened. Burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required.

Then said I, Lo, I come. In the volume of the book it is written of me.

I delight to do thy will, O my God. Yea, thy law is within my heart.

The waiting had been long. The pit had been deep. The rock was steady. The song was new. The heart had been at the bottom of the clay. The law was within it now.
