The hillside was green and the air was still. Jesus sat among the crowd, and the people were quiet, listening.

He spoke to them. "Do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink. Do not be anxious about your body, what you will put on." His voice carried across the grass. "Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing?"

He lifted his hand toward the sky. Birds moved above the hillside, dark shapes crossing the pale blue. "Look at the birds of the air," he said. "They do not sow. They do not reap. They gather nothing into barns." He paused. "And yet your heavenly Father feeds them."

The crowd watched the birds pass overhead. A soft wind stirred the grass beneath them.

"Are you not of more value than they?" Jesus asked.

No one answered.

He went on. "And which of you by being anxious can add a single hour to his span of life?"

The wind moved again. The people shifted where they sat on the slope. Some leaned forward. Some held their knees to their chests.

"And why are you anxious about clothing?" Jesus said. He gestured toward the ground beside the path, where wildflowers grew in clusters — white and violet and deep gold. "Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow. They neither toil nor spin."

The flowers bent slightly in the breeze, their petals thin and bright.

"Yet I tell you," he said, "even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these."

A murmur ran through the crowd the way a ripple runs through water.

"But if God so clothes the grass of the field, which today is alive and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, will he not much more clothe you?" He looked across the faces before him. "O you of little faith."

He continued. "Therefore do not be anxious, saying, 'What shall we eat?' or 'What shall we drink?' or 'What shall we wear?' For the Gentiles seek after all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them all."

His voice was steady. "But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you."

The hillside was quiet. The birds still circled above the trees.

"Therefore do not be anxious about tomorrow," Jesus said, "for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble."

The wildflowers along the path held still. The crowd sat on the green slope, and the wind passed low over the grass between them.
