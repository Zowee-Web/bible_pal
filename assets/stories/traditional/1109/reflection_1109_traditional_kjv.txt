The people came together to behold. When it was ended, they turned and went home, smiting their breasts. The spectacle was finished. The hill emptied.

But not all departed. The women that followed Jesus from Galilee stood afar off and beheld. They turned not away. They went not home.

They had no power to stay what was happening. They could not pull out the nails or take him down. They could not contend with the soldiers or overrule the sentence. They had nothing to offer but their presence.

They remained. That was all. They stood upon the hill and they departed not.

Sometimes remaining is the only thing left. And sometimes it is enough.
