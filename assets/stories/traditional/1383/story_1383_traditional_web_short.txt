Israel had moved on from Mount Hor and from the place of the brass serpent. They had gone around the borders of Edom, and the borders of Moab, and the brook of Zered; and they had come at last to a place that would be called Beer because of the well that the Lord gave them there. The princes of the people had taken their staves and their scepters and gone down with the diggers; and the well had opened, and the water had come up; and when the well was full, the elders of Israel sang the song over it.

From there they traveled to Beer; that is the well of which Yahweh said to Moses, "Gather the people together, and I will give them water."

Then Israel sang this song:

"Spring up, well! Sing to it,

the well, which the princes dug,
which the nobles of the people dug,
with the scepter, and with their poles."

From the wilderness they traveled to Mattanah;

And the people came up out of Beer in the morning. The water of the well stayed in the place where the princes had dug, opened for the next caravan to come through that country; the song had been sung once over the gift of it, and the song was kept among the songs of the journey; and the camp of Israel moved on toward Mattanah, with the well of Beer behind them and the rod of the giving still standing in the diggers' hands.
