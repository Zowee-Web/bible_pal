Israel had departed from mount Hor and from the place of the brasen serpent. They had compassed the borders of Edom, and the borders of Moab, and the brook of Zered; and they had come at last unto a place that should be called Beer because of the well that the Lord gave them there. The princes of the people had taken their staves and their scepters and gone down with the diggers; and the well had opened, and the water had come up; and when the well was full, the elders of Israel sang the song over it.

And from thence they went to Beer: that is the well whereof the Lord spake unto Moses, "Gather the people together, and I will give them water."

Then Israel sang this song:

"Spring up, O well; sing ye unto it:

The princes digged the well,
the nobles of the people digged it,
by the direction of the lawgiver, with their staves."

And from the wilderness they went unto Mattanah;

And the people came up out of Beer in the morning. The water of the well abode in the place where the princes had digged, opened for the next caravan that should come through that country; the song had been sung once over the gift of it, and the song was kept among the songs of the journey; and the camp of Israel removed toward Mattanah, with the well of Beer behind them and the rod of the giving yet standing in the diggers' hands.
