Thus saith the LORD concerning days when he would bring again the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem. He would gather the nations unto the valley of Jehoshaphat and plead with them for his people and his heritage. Israel had been scattered, the land parted, and the people handled as merchandise. Boys and girls had been traded to satisfy the pleasures of their captors.

Tyre, Zidon, and the coasts of Palestine had taken the LORD’s silver, gold, and pleasant things. They carried them into their temples and sold the children of Judah and Jerusalem far from their border. The LORD declared that he would raise those captives out of the places where they had been sold. The recompence intended for his people would return upon the heads of those who had trafficked them. The LORD himself hath spoken it.

Then a proclamation went among the Gentiles. They were commanded to prepare war, assemble their mighty men, and beat the instruments of husbandry into weapons. Even the weak were to say that they were strong and hasten toward the appointed valley. Their gathering did not make them judges; it brought them before the judgment of the LORD. The harvest was ripe, the press was full, and the fats overflowed because wickedness was great. Multitudes stood in the valley of decision, for the day of the LORD was near.

The sun and moon grew dark, and the stars withdrew their shining. The LORD roared out of Zion and uttered his voice from Jerusalem, so that heaven and earth shook. Yet judgment was not the only word. The LORD was the hope of his people and the strength of the children of Israel. They would know that he was their God dwelling in Zion, and Jerusalem would be holy.

The prophecy closed with abundance and enduring habitation. Mountains dropped new wine, hills flowed with milk, and Judah’s rivers carried water. A fountain came forth from the house of the LORD. Egypt and Edom faced desolation for violence and innocent blood, but Judah and Jerusalem remained through the generations. The LORD would cleanse the blood which he had not cleansed, for he dwelleth in Zion.
