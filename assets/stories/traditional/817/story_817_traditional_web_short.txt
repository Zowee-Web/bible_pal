Daniel lived in the bright, strong palace of King Darius, far from his home. King Darius liked Daniel very much. Daniel was wise and honest, and the king trusted him. Many people helped rule the kingdom, but Daniel did his work better than all of them. This made some officials feel upset. They wanted to find something wrong with Daniel, but they could not. Daniel always did what was right.

The clever officials came up with a plan. They knew Daniel loved God and prayed every day. So, they went to King Darius and said, “O king, make a new law! For the next thirty days, let no one pray to any person or god except you. If someone does, send them into the den where the lions live.” The king thought it sounded good and made it a law.

Even after the law was signed, Daniel went home to his room and opened his windows towards Jerusalem. He got down on his knees and prayed to God, just like he always did. The officials went to Daniel’s house and quietly watched him. They saw Daniel praying and ran to tell King Darius.

King Darius was very sad. He liked Daniel, but the law could not be changed. The officials reminded the king, “You must send Daniel to the lions’ den.” The king tried to find a way to save Daniel but could not. As the sun went down, they brought Daniel and put him into the lions’ den. The king said, “May your God, whom you always serve, save you!” The den was dark and cold. It was closed with a big stone, and a special royal mark was put on the stone so no one could move it.

All night, the king could not sleep. He worried about Daniel and did not eat or listen to music. Early in the morning, as the sun peeked over the palace, King Darius hurried to the lions’ den. He called out with a sad voice, “Daniel, servant of the living God, has your God saved you from the lions?” At once, Daniel answered, “My God sent his angel! He shut the lions’ mouths. They did not hurt me because I did nothing wrong.”

The king was filled with joy. He asked for Daniel to be brought out of the den. Daniel was safe and without a scratch. King Darius told everyone in his kingdom about Daniel’s God, who is strong and always saves. Daniel stayed brave and faithful, and God was with him always.
