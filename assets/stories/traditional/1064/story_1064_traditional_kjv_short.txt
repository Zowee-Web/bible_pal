The king of Syria made war against Israel. He set his camp in secret places, devising ambushes along the roads. But each time, Elisha the prophet sent word unto the king of Israel. "Pass not by that place," the message would say. "For the Syrians lie in wait there."

The king of Israel sent men to search it out. Every time, Elisha spake truly.

This came to pass again and again. The king of Syria was wroth exceedingly. He called his servants together. "Tell me, which of you betrayeth us unto the king of Israel?"

One of his servants spake. "None of us, my lord O king. It is Elisha, the prophet that is in Israel. He telleth the king of Israel the words that thou speakest in thine own bedchamber."

The king said, "Go and spy where he is, that I may send and fetch him."

And word was brought unto him. "Behold, he is in Dothan."

So the king of Syria sent thither horses and chariots and a great host. They came by night and compassed the city round about.

When the servant of Elisha rose early on the morrow and went forth, behold, a host compassed the city. Horses. Chariots. Whithersoever he looked.

He ran back within. "Alas, my master, what shall we do?"

And Elisha said, "Fear not. For they that be with us are more than they that be with them."

Then Elisha prayed. "Lord, I pray thee, open his eyes, that he may see."

And the Lord opened the eyes of the young man. He looked again upon the hills round about Dothan. And behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire, blazing round about Elisha.

The Syrians came down against him. And Elisha prayed unto the Lord. "Smite this people, I pray thee, with blindness." And the Lord smote them with blindness.

And Elisha went out unto them. "This is not the way, neither is this the city. Follow me, and I will bring you to the man whom ye seek." And he led them unto Samaria.

And it came to pass, when they were come into the city, that Elisha prayed. "Lord, open the eyes of these men, that they may see." And the Lord opened their eyes, and they saw. And behold, they stood in the midst of Samaria, compassed about by the host of Israel.

And the king of Israel saw them. "My father, shall I smite them? Shall I smite them?"

And Elisha said, "Thou shalt not smite them. Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink and go unto their master."

And the king prepared great provision for them. And the Syrian soldiers did eat and drink. Then they rose up, and he sent them away unto their master.

And the bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.
