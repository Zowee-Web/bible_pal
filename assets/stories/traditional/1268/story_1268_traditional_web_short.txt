"Don't let your heart be troubled. Believe in God. Believe also in me.

In my Father's house are many homes. If it weren't so, I would have told you. I am going to prepare a place for you. If I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and will receive you to myself; that where I am, you may be there also.

You know where I go, and you know the way."

Thomas said to him, "Lord, we don't know where you are going. How can we know the way?"

Jesus said to him, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.

I will not leave you orphans. I will come to you. Yet a little while, and the world will see me no more; but you will see me. Because I live, you will live also.

Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, give I to you.

Don't let your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid."
