When Hannah had brought Samuel to the house of the Lord at Shiloh, she gave the boy into the keeping of Eli the priest. The lamps were burning before the ark, and the courts were quiet around her. Then she lifted up her voice and prayed.

Hannah said, "My heart rejoices in the Lord. My strength is lifted up in the Lord, and I rejoice in his salvation."

She said, "There is no one holy like the Lord. There is no one besides him, and there is no rock like our God."

Hannah continued, "Do not keep talking so proudly. Do not let arrogance come out of your mouth, for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed."

Then Hannah said, "The bows of the mighty are broken, and those who stumbled are armed with strength. Those who were full have hired themselves out for bread, and those who were hungry are satisfied. The barren has borne seven, while she who has many children grows weary."

She said, "The Lord brings death, and he gives life. He brings down to the grave, and he raises up. The Lord makes poor, and he makes rich. He brings low, and he also lifts up."

She said, "He raises up the poor out of the dust. He lifts the needy from the ash heap, to set them with princes and to give them a throne of glory."

Then she said, "The pillars of the earth are the Lord's, and he has set the world upon them. He will guard the feet of his holy ones. The wicked will be silenced in darkness, for no one prevails by strength alone."

Then Hannah said, "Those who strive with the Lord will be broken. He will thunder against them from heaven. The Lord will judge the ends of the earth. He will give strength to his king, and exalt the horn of his anointed."

When she had finished her song, she went home with Elkanah her husband. The boy Samuel stayed in Shiloh to serve before the Lord.
