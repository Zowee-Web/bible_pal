Jesus sent two disciples into a village for an ass and a colt. They found the beasts even as he had said. They brought them back. He sat thereon. And the multitude cried out.

People cast their garments in the way. They cut down branches from the trees. They shouted Hosanna. Children ran alongside. All the city was moved.

Most of them knew not what was to come. The week ahead held betrayal, arrest, crucifixion. But in that hour, they laid down what they had and joined the procession.

Joy doth not always require understanding. Sometimes it asketh only that thou show up and join in.
