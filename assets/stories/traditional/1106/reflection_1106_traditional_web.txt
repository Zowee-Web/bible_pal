Jesus sent two disciples into a village for a donkey and a colt. They found the animals exactly where he said they would be. They brought them back. He sat on the colt. And the crowd erupted.

People threw their cloaks on the road. They cut branches from the trees. They shouted Hosanna. Children ran alongside. The whole city was stirred.

Most of them did not know what was coming next. The week ahead held betrayal, arrest, crucifixion. But in that moment, they laid down what they had and joined the procession.

Joy does not always require understanding. Sometimes it asks only that you show up and join in.
