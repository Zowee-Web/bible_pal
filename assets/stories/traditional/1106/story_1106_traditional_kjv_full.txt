Jesus sent two disciples before him as they drew nigh unto Jerusalem, come to Bethphage at the Mount of Olives. The road was dusty. Other travellers moved along the way toward the city for the feast.

"Go into the village over against you," Jesus said, "and straightway ye shall find an ass tied, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them unto me. And if any man say ought unto you, ye shall say, 'The Lord hath need of them;' and straightway he will send them."

The two went down the road and into the village. They found the ass and the colt tied by a doorway upon a narrow street, even as he had described. They loosed the ropes. A man standing near stepped forward and asked what they did with the beasts. They answered as Jesus had bidden them. The man looked upon them, then stepped back. He let them go.

They led the animals up the road to where Jesus tarried. The disciples cast their garments upon the colt. Jesus sat thereon.

The colt began to walk. The road from the Mount of Olives ran downhill toward Jerusalem. The city spread out below — the temple walls, the housetops, the gates. The morning light was upon the stone.

People in the multitude began to pull off their cloaks and spread them in the way before the colt. Others went unto the trees along the roadside. They cut down branches — palm fronds and leafy boughs — and strawed them in the way. The road became a carpet of cloth and greenery.

The multitude that went before Jesus began to cry out. The words came first from a few, then from many.

"Hosanna to the son of David!"

The colt stepped over the garments. Dust rose where the branches were crushed. More people joined from the sides of the road — pilgrims come for the Passover, families with children, travellers who had journeyed since dawn.

"Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord!"

The cry moved through the multitude — spreading from the front to the back. They that were behind took it up. They that went before turned and shouted back.

"Hosanna in the highest!"

The descent continued. The road curved. Jerusalem grew larger with every step. The walls rose higher. The gates came into view.

When he was come into Jerusalem, all the city was moved. People came out of their houses. They stood in doorways. They leaned from upper windows. The streets were already thronged with those come for the feast, and the noise of the procession reached them before the procession itself arrived.

"Who is this?" they asked one of another.

The multitudes that had walked with him from the Mount of Olives answered. Their voices were hoarse from crying out.

"This is Jesus the prophet of Nazareth of Galilee."

The colt passed through the gate. The branches and garments stretched behind along the road as far as any man could see. The cries echoed off the stone walls of the city. Children ran alongside. The multitude pressed in from every quarter.

Jesus rode through them all upon the colt, through the gate, into Jerusalem.
