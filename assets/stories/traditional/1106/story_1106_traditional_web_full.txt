Jesus sent two disciples ahead as they drew near to Jerusalem, approaching Bethphage at the Mount of Olives. The road was dusty. Other travelers moved along the path toward the city for the festival.

"Go into the village opposite you," Jesus said. "Immediately you will find a donkey tied, and a colt with her. Untie them and bring them to me. If anyone says anything to you, say, 'The Lord needs them,' and he will send them immediately."

The two went down the road and into the village. They found the donkey and the colt tied near a doorway on a narrow street, exactly as he had described. They untied the ropes. A man standing nearby stepped forward and asked what they were doing with the animals. They answered as Jesus had told them. The man looked at them, then stepped back. He let them go.

They led the animals up the road to where Jesus waited. The disciples laid their outer garments across the colt's back. Jesus mounted and sat.

The colt began to walk. The road from the Mount of Olives ran downhill toward Jerusalem. The city spread out below — the temple walls, the rooftops, the gates. The morning light was on the stone.

People in the crowd began to pull off their cloaks and spread them on the road in front of the colt. Others went to the trees along the roadside. They cut branches — palm fronds and leafy limbs — and laid them across the path. The road became a carpet of cloth and greenery.

The crowd walking ahead of Jesus began to shout. The words came first from a few, then from many.

"Hosanna to the son of David!"

The colt stepped over the spread garments. Dust rose where the branches were crushed. More people joined from the sides of the road — pilgrims coming for Passover, families with children, travelers who had been walking since dawn.

"Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!"

The shout moved through the crowd — spreading from front to back. Those behind picked it up. Those ahead turned and shouted back.

"Hosanna in the highest!"

The descent continued. The road curved. Jerusalem grew larger with each step. The walls rose higher. The gates came into view.

When Jesus entered the city, the whole place was stirred. People came out of their houses. They stood in doorways. They leaned from upper windows. The streets were already full with festival crowds, and the noise from the procession reached them before the procession itself arrived.

"Who is this?" they asked one another.

The crowds who had walked with him from the Mount of Olives answered. Their voices were hoarse from shouting.

"This is the prophet, Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee."

The colt walked through the gate. The branches and garments stretched behind along the road as far as anyone could see. The shouts echoed off the stone walls of the city. Children ran alongside. The crowd pressed in from every direction.

Jesus rode through them all on the colt, through the gate, into Jerusalem.
