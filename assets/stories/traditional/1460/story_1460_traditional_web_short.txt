Elijah said to all the people, "Come near to me."

They came near.

He repaired the altar of Yahweh that had been thrown down. He took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, and built an altar in the name of Yahweh.

He made a trench around it, big enough to hold two seahs of seed. He laid the wood in order on the altar. He cut the bull in pieces and laid it on the wood.

He said, "Fill four jars with water and pour it on the burnt offering, and on the wood."

They did it.

He said, "Do it a second time."

They did it a second time.

He said, "Do it a third time."

They did it a third time. The water ran around the altar. The trench was filled with water.

At the time of the evening offering, Elijah came near and said, "Yahweh, God of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Israel — let it be known today that you are God in Israel, and that I am your servant, and that I have done all these things at your word. Hear me, Yahweh. Hear me, that this people may know that you, Yahweh, are God, and that you have turned their heart back again."

Then the fire of Yahweh fell.

It came down out of the sky. It consumed the burnt offering. It consumed the wood. It consumed the stones of the altar. It consumed the dust of the ground. It licked up the water that was in the trench.

When the people saw it, they fell on their faces.

They said, "Yahweh, he is God. Yahweh, he is God."

The fire was still on the altar. The stones of the altar were burning. The faces of the people of Israel were pressed against the earth of the mountain.
