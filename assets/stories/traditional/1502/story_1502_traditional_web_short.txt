The enemies had heard of the watch. They had heard that God had brought their counsel to nothing. They drew back from the road. They did not come up against the city that day. But they had not gone home, and the wall was not yet finished.

Nehemiah went back to the wall. He set the men in a new order. Half of his servants worked at the building. The other half stood armed beside the work. They held spears. They held shields. They held bows. They wore coats of mail. The rulers stood behind the house of Judah, watching the country.

The builders worked with one hand on the stone and the other hand on a weapon. The men who carried the burdens carried them with one hand and held a weapon in the other. The men who laid the stone laid it with one hand and kept a weapon ready in the other.

Each builder had a sword girt by his side. The man who carried the trumpet stood beside Nehemiah on the wall.

Nehemiah spoke to the nobles and to the rulers and to the rest of the people. "The work is great and large. We are separated on the wall, one far from another. Wherever you hear the sound of the trumpet, come to that place. Our God shall fight for us."

The work went on. The half who held the spears stood watch from the rising of the morning till the stars appeared. The men who labored labored. The men who watched watched. Nehemiah was on the wall with the trumpeter beside him.

In the evening Nehemiah set another watch. He told the people that every man should lodge with his servant inside Jerusalem, that they might be a guard at night and a working party by day. None of them put off their clothes. Not Nehemiah. Not his brothers. Not his servants. Not the men who followed him. They slept in their clothes. They washed only when it was necessary.

The wall rose. The trumpeter stayed beside Nehemiah. The stars appeared every night while the men who held the spears were still on the wall.
