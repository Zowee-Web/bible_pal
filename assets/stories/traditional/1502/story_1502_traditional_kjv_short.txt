The enemies had heard of the watch. They had heard that God had brought their counsel unto nought. They drew back from the road. They came not up against the city that day. But they had not gone home, and the wall was not yet finished.

Nehemiah went back unto the wall. He set the men in a new order. The half of his servants wrought at the building. The other half of them stood armed beside the work. They held spears. They held shields. They held bows. They wore the habergeons. The rulers were behind all the house of Judah, watching the country.

They that builded on the wall wrought with one hand on the stone and held a weapon in the other. They that bare the burdens bare them with one hand and held a weapon in the other. They that laded laded with one hand and kept a weapon ready in the other.

Every builder had his sword girded by his side. He that sounded the trumpet stood beside Nehemiah on the wall.

Nehemiah spake unto the nobles and unto the rulers and unto the rest of the people. "The work is great and large. We are separated upon the wall, one far from another. In what place therefore ye hear the sound of the trumpet, resort ye thither unto us. Our God shall fight for us."

The work went on. The half that held the spears watched from the rising of the morning till the stars appeared. They that laboured laboured. They that watched watched. Nehemiah was upon the wall with the trumpeter beside him.

In the evening Nehemiah set another watch. He told the people that every man should lodge with his servant within Jerusalem, that they might be a guard in the night and a working party in the day. None of them put off their clothes. Not Nehemiah. Not his brethren. Not his servants. Not the men of the guard that followed him. They slept in their clothes. They washed only when it was needful.

The wall rose. The trumpeter abode beside Nehemiah. The stars appeared every night while they that held the spears were yet upon the wall.
