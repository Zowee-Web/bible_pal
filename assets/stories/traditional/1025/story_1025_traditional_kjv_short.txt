Noah was a good man, and he walked with God. He had three sons — Shem, Ham, and Japheth. They dwelt together with their households.

But the rest of the world was become a terrible place. Men did hurt one another and wrought cruel things every day. The earth was filled with wickedness. God looked down, and it grieved him at his heart.

So God spake unto Noah. "The end of all flesh is come before me. The earth is filled with violence. I will destroy them with the earth."

Then God laid upon Noah the greatest task that any man had ever been given.

"Make thee an ark of gopher wood," God said. "Rooms shalt thou make in the ark. Pitch it within and without with pitch, that no water may come through."

God gave Noah the exact measures. The ark should be three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits broad, and thirty cubits high. That was enormous — longer than a row of houses standing end to end. It should have three stories, a roof, and a door set in its side.

Noah hearkened unto every word. He gainsaid not.

"I, even I, do bring a flood of waters upon this earth," God said. "Everything that breatheth upon the land shall die."

Those words were heavy. But God was not yet finished.

"I will establish my covenant with thee," God promised. A covenant was a strong and unbreakable agreement. "Thou shalt come into the ark — thou, and thy sons, and thy wife, and thy sons' wives with thee."

God told Noah to bring of every living thing two of every sort into the ark — fowls, creatures that walk, creatures that creep. Male and female. He should also gather of every kind of food and lay it up for store on board.

It was a staggering amount of work. To build a great ark upon dry ground with no rain in sight. To gather the beasts. To lay up food enough for them all.

But Noah trusted God. He took up his tools. He cut the first beam of gopher wood and laid it upon the ground. Day after day he wrought — sawing, hammering, fitting boards together, sealing every crack with thick, dark pitch.

The Scripture saith a simple and mighty thing concerning Noah. Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he. Every board, every room, every measure — exactly as God had said. Noah skipped not a step. He trusted God, and he obeyed.
