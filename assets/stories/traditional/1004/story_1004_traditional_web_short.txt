The town of Bethlehem sat on a hillside. Stone houses lined the narrow roads. Olive trees grew along the paths outside the walls. The air smelled like bread baking and wood smoke. A cool breeze moved through the valley below.

A man named Joseph walked slowly along the road. A young woman named Mary walked beside him. They had traveled a long way from Nazareth. Caesar Augustus had sent out an announcement. Everyone had to go to their own hometown to be counted. Joseph's family came from Bethlehem, so that is where they went. Mary was going to have a baby very soon. Dust covered their sandals from the long journey. They came into town as the sun turned orange and low.

Joseph looked for a place to stay. The inn was already full of travelers. There was no room for them inside. So they found a stable where animals rested. Hay covered the ground. A donkey stood near the wall. The air was warm from the animals breathing softly. Mary and Joseph settled in among the straw.

That night, Mary's baby was born. She wrapped him snugly in strips of cloth. She laid him gently in a manger. The baby's name was Jesus. Joseph stood close by. The little one rested quietly in the soft hay.

Out on the hills nearby, shepherds sat with their sheep under the open sky. Stars filled the night above them. The wool of the sheep looked silver in the moonlight. Then an angel of the Lord appeared near them, and a bright light shone all around. The angel spoke to them. "Do not be nervous. I bring you good news of great joy for all people. Today in Bethlehem, a Savior has been born. He is Christ the Lord. You will find the baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a manger."

Then a great company of angels appeared with the first one. They all said together, "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward all people." Their voices carried across the hills. The shepherds looked up and listened.

When the angels went away into the sky, the shepherds turned to one another. "Let us go to Bethlehem," they said. "Let us see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has told us about." They got up and walked quickly down the hillside. Their sheep stayed close together on the grass.

The shepherds found Mary and Joseph in the stable. They found the baby lying in the manger, just as the angel had said. They told Mary and Joseph everything they had seen and heard on the hillside. Mary listened quietly. She kept all these words close.

The shepherds walked back to their flocks under the wide, quiet night. They praised God and thanked him for everything they had heard and seen. Their voices echoed gently across the hills. The sheep looked up as the shepherds returned. The stars still shone above Bethlehem. The baby slept in the manger, warm and safe, wrapped snugly in soft cloth.
