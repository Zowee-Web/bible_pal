Sometimes the greatest and most wondrous things come to pass in the quietest of places. The infant Jesus was not born in a fine and great house. He was born in a simple place, where the beasts of burden lay. A little manger held him, soft and warm.

The shepherds were but doing their wonted task that night. They were not princes, nor men of renown. Yet the angels came unto them first with the glad tidings. The whole heavens were filled with singing!

Good tidings ofttimes come in surprising ways. A tiny babe. A quiet night. Plain folk watching over their sheep. And on a sudden, all was changed, with so much joy and light and love.

Even small and quiet moments may hold something beautiful.
