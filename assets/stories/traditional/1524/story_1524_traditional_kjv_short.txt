It was the ninth year of Zedekiah his reign. In the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against Jerusalem with all his host. He pitched against it. He built forts against it round about.

The city was besieged unto the eleventh year of Zedekiah. That was two years and more. The famine prevailed in the city. There was no bread for the people of the land.

In the ninth day of the fourth month the wall of the city was broken up. The men of war fled by night by the way of the gate between two walls, which is by the king's garden. The Chaldees were against the city round about. They went the way toward the plain.

The army of the Chaldees pursued after the king. They overtook him in the plains of Jericho. All his army was scattered from him.

They took the king and brought him up to the king of Babylon to Riblah. And they gave judgment upon him. They slew the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes. They put out the eyes of Zedekiah. They bound him with fetters of brass. They carried him to Babylon.

In the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month, which is the nineteenth year of king Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, came Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, a servant of the king of Babylon, unto Jerusalem.

He burnt the house of the LORD. He burnt the king's house. He burnt all the houses of Jerusalem. Every great man's house burnt he with fire.

The army of the Chaldees brake down the walls of Jerusalem round about. The walls had been there a long while. Solomon had begun them. They were broken now.

The captain of the guard carried away the rest of the people that were left in the city. The fugitives that fell away to the king of Babylon, with the remnant of the multitude, did he carry away. But he left of the poor of the land to be vinedressers and husbandmen.

The land was emptied of its people. The vinedressers were left. The husbandmen were left. The houses were ash and the walls were broken. The city was the city no longer.
