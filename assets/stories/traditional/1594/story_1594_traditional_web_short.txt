Yahweh gave his people the shape of a neighborly life, and he tied it to himself. When you reap the harvest of your land, he said, do not reap to the very edges of your field, and do not gather what the harvest drops. Do not strip your vineyard bare or pick up its fallen grapes. Leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am Yahweh your God.

You shall not steal. You shall not lie. You shall not deceive one another. You shall not swear falsely by my name, and so profane the name of your God. I am Yahweh.

You shall not oppress your neighbor or rob him. Do not hold back the wages of a hired worker through the night until morning. You shall not curse the deaf, nor put a stumbling block before the blind; but you shall fear your God. I am Yahweh.

You shall do no injustice in judgment. Do not show favor to the poor or deference to the great, but judge your neighbor fairly. Do not go about as a slanderer among your people. Do not endanger the life of your neighbor. I am Yahweh.

You shall not hate your brother in your heart. Rebuke your neighbor honestly, and do not carry sin because of him. Do not take vengeance, and do not hold a grudge against your own people. You shall love your neighbor as yourself. I am Yahweh.
