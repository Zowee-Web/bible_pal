These are the commandments the Lord gave unto his people. Thus he spake unto them all, and unto each among them:

When ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field. And the gleanings that thy harvest leaveth behind thee — gather them not. So also with thy vineyard: glean it not, neither gather every grape thereof. What remaineth, thou shalt leave for the poor, and for the stranger. I am the Lord your God.

Ye shall not steal. Ye shall not deal falsely. Ye shall not lie one to another. Ye shall not swear by my name falsely. It is the name of thy God; thou shalt not profane it. I am the Lord.

Thy neighbour thou shalt not defraud, neither rob him. And of him that is hired: his wages shall not abide with thee all night until the morning. Neither shalt thou curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind. But thou shalt fear thy God. I am the Lord.

And in judgment ye shall do no unrighteousness. Respect not the person of the poor. Honour not the person of the mighty. But in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour. And among thy people, thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer. Thou shalt not stand against the blood of thy neighbour. I am the Lord.

And as for thy brother: thou shalt not hate him in thine heart. Rather, thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him. And concerning the children of thy people: thou shalt neither take vengeance nor bear any grudge. But thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. I am the Lord.
