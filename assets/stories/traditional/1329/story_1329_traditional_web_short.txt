The Psalm was a Psalm of David. He had set it down in the days of his trouble, when the answer to his prayer had come and he had stood again under the open sky. He had come into the courts of Yahweh, and he had bowed toward the holy temple, and he had given thanks. And then he had taken the harp, and he had written the words.

I will give you thanks with my whole heart. Before the gods, I will sing praises to you.

I will bow down toward your holy temple, and give thanks to your name for your loving kindness and for your truth; for you have exalted your name and your word above all.

You called on the day when I called. You encouraged me with strength in my soul.

And David set the Psalm down. The lamp was lit at his table. The harp lay beside him. The night went on as the nights had been going on, and the words of his gratitude were written into the books of the singers, that the people of Israel might sing them after him, day after day, in the years to come. And so it was kept in Israel from then on.
