When the children of Israel had returned unto Jerusalem from the captivity, they gathered to begin the work of the house of the Lord. The walls of the city were broken down, and the courts of the old temple lay in ruins. But the builders began to lay the foundation, stone upon stone, in the place where the house of God had stood.

When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the Lord, they set the priests in their apparel with trumpets. And the Levites the sons of Asaph took up their cymbals to praise the Lord, after the ordinance of David king of Israel.

They sang together by course in praising and giving thanks unto the Lord. They said, "For he is good, for his mercy endureth for ever toward Israel."

And all the people shouted with a great shout, when they praised the Lord, because the foundation of the house of the Lord was laid. The trumpets sounded, and the cymbals rang together, and the voices rose into the morning sky.

But many of the priests and Levites and chief of the fathers, who were ancient men, that had seen the first house in their youth, when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, wept with a loud voice.

And many shouted aloud for joy, so that the people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping of the people. The young men shouted, and the old men wept, and the sounds rose together over the broken walls.

And the noise was heard afar off. It went forth across the hill country, and the villages round about Jerusalem heard the great cry, and they knew not whether it were a cry of joy or a cry of sorrow.

But the foundation had been laid. The stones were set upon the ground where the first house had stood, and the people of Israel stood upon the place where the Lord had once dwelt.

The morning was bright over Jerusalem, and the trumpets sounded again before the sun rose unto his height.
