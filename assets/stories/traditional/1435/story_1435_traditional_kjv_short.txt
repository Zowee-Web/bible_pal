Isaiah the prophet had been speaking of the day when a king should reign in righteousness and princes should rule in judgment. The wilderness should become a fruitful field; the fruitful field should be counted for a forest; the spirit should be poured upon the people from on high; and the people of God should dwell in their land in the safety which the work of righteousness bringeth. The vision had been spread over many verses. And toward the end of the oracle the prophet set down a few short lines that should be remembered.

The work of righteousness shall be peace;
and the effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever.

And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation,
and in sure dwellings,
and in quiet resting places.

And the words concerning the peaceable habitation were kept among the scrolls of the prophet for the days of the kingdom that should come. The picture had been set down — the work of righteousness as peace, the dwellings as sure, the resting places as quiet — and the readers of the prophet's words in the years that followed bare the picture forward in the houses wherein the scroll was opened, in the quiet evening hours of the country of Judah.
