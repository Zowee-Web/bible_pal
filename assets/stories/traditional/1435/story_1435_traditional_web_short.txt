Isaiah the prophet had been speaking of the day when a king would reign in righteousness and princes would rule in justice. The wilderness would become a fruitful field; the fruitful field would be counted as a forest; the spirit would be poured out from on high upon the people; and the people of God would live in their land in the safety that the work of righteousness brings. The vision had spread over many verses. And toward the end of the oracle the prophet set down a few short lines that would be remembered.

The work of righteousness will be peace;
and the effect of righteousness, quietness and confidence forever.

My people will live in a peaceful habitation,
in safe dwellings,
and in quiet resting places.

And the words about the peaceful habitation were kept among the scrolls of the prophet for the days of the coming kingdom. The picture had been set down — the work of righteousness as peace, the dwellings as safe, the resting places as quiet — and the readers of the prophet's words in the years that followed carried the picture forward in the houses where the scroll was opened, in the quiet evening hours of the country of Judah.
