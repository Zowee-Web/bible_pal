The day of unleavened bread came, when the passover must be killed. Jesus sent Peter and John. "Go and prepare us the passover, that we may eat."

They said unto him, "Where wilt thou that we prepare?"

"Behold," he said, "when ye are entered into the city, there shall a man meet you, bearing a pitcher of water; follow him into the house where he entereth in. And ye shall say unto the goodman of the house, 'The Master saith unto thee, Where is the guestchamber, where I shall eat the passover with my disciples?' And he shall shew you a large upper room furnished: there make ready."

They went forth. They found even as he had said unto them. They made ready the passover.

When the hour was come, Jesus sat down at the table, and the twelve apostles with him. Oil lamps burned. The food was set. The bread was upon the table and the cup was poured.

Jesus said unto them, "With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer: for I say unto you, I will not any more eat thereof, until it be fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

He took the cup. He gave thanks. "Take this, and divide it among yourselves: for I say unto you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, until the kingdom of God shall come."

He took bread. He gave thanks. He brake it and gave unto them. "This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me."

He took the cup after supper. "This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you."

The room was quiet. The bread was broken. The cup passed from hand to hand.

Then Jesus spake again. His voice changed not. "But, behold, the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on the table."

The disciples looked one upon another. They began to enquire among themselves which of them it was that should do this thing. The lamps flickered. The bread lay broken upon the table. The cup sat among them, still wet upon the rim.
