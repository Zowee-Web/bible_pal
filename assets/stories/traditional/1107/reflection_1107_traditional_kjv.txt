Jesus said with desire he had desired to eat this passover with them. He took bread, gave thanks, and brake it. He took the cup and passed it round the table. Twelve men ate and drank together in a furnished upper room.

Then he said the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on the table.

The bread was already broken. The cup was already shared. He that would betray had already eaten. Every man in that room had received the same bread from the same hands.

Some meals carry a weight that becometh clear only afterward. The bread was but bread. The wine was but wine. Yet the room held something none of them could name.
