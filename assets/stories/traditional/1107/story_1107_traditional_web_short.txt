The day of Unleavened Bread came, on which the Passover lamb must be sacrificed. Jesus sent Peter and John. "Go and prepare the Passover for us, that we may eat."

They said, "Where do you want us to prepare?"

"When you enter the city," he said, "a man carrying a pitcher of water will meet you. Follow him into the house he enters. Tell the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, "Where is the guest room, where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?"' He will show you a large, furnished upper room. Prepare there."

They went. They found it exactly as he had told them. They prepared the Passover.

When the hour came, Jesus sat down at the table. The twelve apostles were with him. Oil lamps burned. The food was set. The bread was on the table and the cup was poured.

Jesus said to them, "I have earnestly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer. For I tell you, I will not eat of it again until it is fulfilled in the Kingdom of God."

He took a cup. He gave thanks. "Take this, and divide it among yourselves. For I tell you, I will not drink from the fruit of the vine until the Kingdom of God comes."

He took bread. He gave thanks. He broke it and gave it to them. "This is my body which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me."

He took the cup after supper. "This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you."

The room was quiet. The bread was broken. The cup was passed from hand to hand.

Then Jesus spoke again. His voice did not change. "But the hand of him who betrays me is with me on the table."

The disciples looked at one another. They began to question among themselves which of them it was who would do this thing. The lamps flickered. The bread lay broken on the table. The cup sat among them, still wet on the rim.
