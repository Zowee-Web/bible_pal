When Jesus saw the great multitudes that had come to follow him, he went up onto the mountainside and sat down. His disciples gathered around him, and the rest of the crowd settled on the slope below them. The morning light lay across the hills of Galilee, and the sea was still in the distance. The breeze was warm on the slopes.

Then Jesus opened his mouth and taught them, and he lifted up his voice over the gathering. All the people grew quiet to hear him. He said, "Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven."

Again he said, "Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted."

He continued, "Blessed are the gentle, for they shall inherit the earth."

He said, "Blessed are those who hunger and thirst after righteousness, for they shall be filled."

Then he said, "Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy."

He said, "Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God."

He went on, "Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God."

And he said, "Blessed are those who have been persecuted for righteousness' sake, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven."

Then Jesus turned his eyes upon his disciples and said, "Blessed are you when people reproach you, and persecute you, and say all kinds of evil against you falsely for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceedingly glad, for great is your reward in heaven. For so they persecuted the prophets who were before you."

When he had said these things, the disciples sat in silence on the mountainside, and the people listened. The wind moved softly across the slope, and the morning light lay on their faces. The hills of Galilee were quiet under the open sky. He sat among them and continued teaching, and the crowd remained on the slope until the day grew warm.
