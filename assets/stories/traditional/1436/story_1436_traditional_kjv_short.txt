The five sisters came together unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation in the morning light. Their father had died in the wilderness, and they had no brother. Their names were Mahlah, and Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah.

They stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the princes, and before all the congregation. The five moved not when the men of the camp beheld them. They were come to speak.

"Our father died in the wilderness," said they. "He was not among the company of them that gathered themselves together against the Lord in the company of Korah, but died in his own sin. He had no sons.

Why should the name of our father be done away from among his family, because he hath no son? Give unto us a possession among the brethren of our father."

Moses hearkened unto them. He beheld the five which stood there before the door, and he hearkened unto the wind that moved across the camp. He answered them not out of his own thought. He brought their cause before the Lord.

Then the Lord spake unto Moses, saying, "The daughters of Zelophehad speak right. Thou shalt surely give them a possession of an inheritance among their father's brethren. And thou shalt cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.

And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying: If a man die, and have no son, then ye shall cause his inheritance to pass unto his daughter. And if he have no daughter, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his brethren. And if he have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his father's brethren. And if his father have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his kinsman that is next to him of his family, and he shall possess it. And this shall be a statute and an ordinance unto the children of Israel, as the Lord commanded Moses."

So the daughters of Zelophehad were given a possession among their father's brethren, and the statute went forth among the children of Israel. And the names of the sisters — Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah — were spoken in the hearing of their people.
