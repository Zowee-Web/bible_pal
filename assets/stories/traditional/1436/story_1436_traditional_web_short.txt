The five sisters came together to the door of the Tent of Meeting in the morning light. Their father had died in the wilderness, and they had no brother. Their names were Mahlah, and Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah.

They stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the princes, and before all the congregation. The five did not move when the men of the camp looked at them. They had come to speak.

"Our father died in the wilderness," they said. "He was not among the company of those who gathered themselves together against Yahweh in the company of Korah, but he died in his own sin. He had no sons.

Why should the name of our father be taken away from among his family, because he had no son? Give to us a possession among the brothers of our father."

Moses listened to them. He looked at the five who stood there before the door, and he listened to the wind that moved across the camp. He did not answer them out of his own thought. He brought their cause before Yahweh.

Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying, "The daughters of Zelophehad speak right. You shall surely give them a possession of an inheritance among their father's brothers. You shall cause the inheritance of their father to pass to them.

You shall speak to the children of Israel, saying: If a man dies, and has no son, then you shall cause his inheritance to pass to his daughter. If he has no daughter, then you shall give his inheritance to his brothers. If he has no brothers, then you shall give his inheritance to his father's brothers. If his father has no brothers, then you shall give his inheritance to his kinsman who is next to him of his family, and he shall possess it. This shall be a statute and ordinance for the children of Israel, as Yahweh commanded Moses."

So the daughters of Zelophehad were given a possession among their father's brothers, and the statute went out among the children of Israel. And the names of the sisters — Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah — were spoken in the hearing of their people.
