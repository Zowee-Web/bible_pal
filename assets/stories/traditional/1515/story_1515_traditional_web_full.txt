Daniel had been brought before the king. He had been brought by Arioch, the captain of the king's guard. He stood before the throne. He had been given the dream and the interpretation in a vision of the night. He had blessed God before he came in. He stood now before the king.

He spoke first of what the king had seen. "You saw, O king, and behold, a great image. The image was very bright. Its form was terrible. It stood before you.

"The head of the image was of fine gold. The breast and the arms were of silver. The belly and the thighs were of brass. The legs were of iron. The feet were part iron and part clay.

"You saw a stone cut out of the mountain without hands. The stone struck the image on its feet of iron and clay. It broke them in pieces.

"Then the iron, the clay, the brass, the silver, and the gold were broken to pieces together. They became like the chaff of the summer threshing-floors. The wind carried them away. No place was found for them.

"The stone that struck the image became a great mountain. The mountain filled the whole earth."

He paused. The king was listening.

He gave the interpretation. "This is the dream. We will tell the interpretation thereof before the king.

"You, O king, are a king of kings. The God of heaven has given you a kingdom, power, and strength, and glory. You are this head of gold.

"After you shall arise another kingdom inferior to you. Then a third kingdom of brass which shall bear rule over all the earth.

"The fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron. As iron breaks in pieces all things, so shall it break in pieces and bruise. The feet and toes part iron and part clay: the kingdom shall be divided. Part of it shall be strong as iron. Part shall be broken.

"In the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed. The kingdom shall not be left to other people. It shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms. It shall stand for ever.

"The stone was cut out of the mountain without hands. It broke in pieces the iron, the brass, the clay, the silver, and the gold. The great God has made known to the king what shall come to pass hereafter. The dream is certain. The interpretation thereof is sure."

The king fell upon his face. He worshipped Daniel. He said: of a truth, your God is a God of gods, and a Lord of kings, and a revealer of secrets, seeing you could reveal this secret. He commanded that offerings be brought to Daniel and sweet odors set before him. He made Daniel a great man. He gave him many great gifts. He set him ruler over the whole province of Babylon. He made him chief of the governors over all the wise men of Babylon.
