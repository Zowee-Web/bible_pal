In the quiet darkness of the night, young Samuel lay in the house of the Lord at Shiloh. The lamp of God was still burning, casting gentle light across the place where the ark of God rested. Eli the priest, an old man whose eyes had waxed dim, slept in his own room nigh by. The air was soft and hushed, deep with the stillness that settleth before the dawn.

As Samuel rested, a voice brake the silence, calling softly in the dark, "Samuel!" The boy arose quickly and ran unto Eli, thinking that the priest had called him. "Here am I!" said Samuel, standing near unto Eli's bed. But Eli beheld the boy and answered him quietly, "I called not; lie down again, my son." Samuel returned unto his place and lay down beneath the pale, flickering light.

Again, the Lord called, "Samuel!" The voice drifted through the quiet. Samuel arose and hasted unto Eli, his heart attentive. Again he said, "Here am I; for thou didst call me." Eli shook his head, his voice gentle but firm. "I called thee not, my son. Lie down again." Samuel obeyed, lying down once more.

Samuel did not yet know the Lord. The word of the Lord had not yet been revealed unto him. For the third time, the voice came, calling his name in the peaceful dark. Samuel arose and went unto Eli, bringing the question once more: "Here am I; for thou didst call me." Then Eli perceived. He understood that the Lord had called the child. Eli spake unto Samuel with calm wisdom, "Go, lie down again: and it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, Lord; for thy servant heareth."

Samuel returned unto his place and lay still, his heart brimming with wonder and awe. When the Lord came, and stood there, calling as at the other times, "Samuel! Samuel!" the boy answered even as Eli had taught him: "Speak; for thy servant heareth." The voice of God spake unto Samuel, revealing things hidden and weighing heavy concerning the house of Eli. Samuel hearkened with care, holding the words close.

The morning light crept into the tabernacle. Samuel lay until the dawn, then opened the doors of the house of the Lord. He held the vision in his heart, afraid to tell it, but Eli called for him. Samuel came unto Eli, and at the priest's urging, he told him every whit, hiding nothing. Eli heard and said, "It is the Lord: let him do what seemeth him good." And Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him, and all Israel knew that Samuel was established to be a prophet of the Lord.
