Boaz took Ruth into his house in Bethlehem, and the marriage was solemnized in the manner of the elders. In the time that followed, the Lord gave Ruth conception, and she bare a son. The women of the neighbourhood came up the way unto Naomi's house to behold the child, and to speak the blessings that mothers and grandmothers speak at such times, and they said unto her this.

So Boaz took Ruth, and she was his wife: and when he went in unto her, the Lord gave her conception, and she bare a son.

And the women said unto Naomi, "Blessed be the Lord, which hath not left thee this day without a kinsman, that his name may be famous in Israel.

And he shall be unto thee a restorer of thy life, and a nourisher of thine old age: for thy daughter in law, which loveth thee, which is better to thee than seven sons, hath born him."

And Naomi took the child, and laid it in her bosom, and became nurse unto it.

And the women her neighbours gave it a name, saying, "There is a son born to Naomi." And they called his name Obed: he is the father of Jesse, the father of David.

And the women of Bethlehem stood round about Naomi at her door. The child slept against her shoulder — Obed, who should be the father of Jesse and the grandfather of David the king — and Naomi held him with her old hands, with the songs of the neighbour women yet going on in the courtyard without the door of the house that had been Elimelech's.
