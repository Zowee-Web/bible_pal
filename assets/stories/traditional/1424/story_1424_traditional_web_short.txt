Boaz took Ruth into his house in Bethlehem, and the marriage was solemnized in the way of the elders. In the time that followed, the Lord enabled Ruth to conceive, and she bore a son. The women of the neighborhood came up the road to Naomi's house to see the child, and to speak the blessings that mothers and grandmothers speak at such times, and they said to her this.

So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife; and he went in to her, and Yahweh enabled her to conceive, and she bore a son.

The women said to Naomi, "Blessed be Yahweh, who has not left you today without a near kinsman. Let his name be famous in Israel.

He shall be to you a restorer of life, and sustain you in your old age, for your daughter-in-law, who loves you, who is better to you than seven sons, has given birth to him."

Naomi took the child, and laid him in her bosom, and became nurse to it.

The women, her neighbors, gave him a name, saying, "A son is born to Naomi". They named him Obed. He is the father of Jesse, the father of David.

And the women of Bethlehem stood around Naomi at her door. The child slept against her shoulder — Obed, the one who would be the father of Jesse and the grandfather of David the king — and Naomi held him with her old hands, with the songs of the neighbor women still going on in the courtyard outside the door of the house that had been Elimelech's.
