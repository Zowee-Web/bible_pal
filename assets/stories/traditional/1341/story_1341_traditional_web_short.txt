The messengers had come up from Sennacherib king of Assyria. They had stood at the conduit of the upper pool and called out to the people on the wall, and they had spoken in the Jews' language so that all could hear; and they had said that no god of any nation had delivered its people from the hand of the king of Assyria. The words had reached the king. Hezekiah had taken the letter and gone up to the temple.

Hezekiah received the letter from the hand of the messengers and read it. Then Hezekiah went up to Yahweh's house, and spread it before Yahweh.

Hezekiah prayed before Yahweh, and said, "Yahweh, the God of Israel, who sit above the cherubim, you are the God, even you alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth. You have made heaven and earth.

Incline your ear, Yahweh, and hear. Open your eyes, Yahweh, and see. Hear the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to defy the living God.

Truly, Yahweh, the kings of Assyria have laid waste the nations and their lands,

and have cast their gods into the fire; for they were no gods, but the work of men's hands, wood and stone. Therefore they have destroyed them.

Now therefore, Yahweh our God, save us, I beg you, out of his hand, that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you, Yahweh, are God alone."

And the king finished his prayer and stood up. The letter lay open before the Lord in the temple, and the city of Jerusalem went on with its watching, and the army of Sennacherib went on with its waiting, and the matter had been placed in the hands where the king had placed it. And so it was in the days when Hezekiah received the letter of Sennacherib and brought it up to the house of the Lord.
