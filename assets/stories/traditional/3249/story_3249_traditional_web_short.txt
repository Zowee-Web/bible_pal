Yahweh’s word came to Jeremiah after Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, released him from Ramah. Jeremiah had been taken in chains among Jerusalem’s and Judah’s captives being carried away to Babylon.

The captain took Jeremiah and spoke of the evil Yahweh, Jeremiah’s God, had pronounced against that place. Yahweh had brought it about as he had said, the captain declared, because the people had sinned against him and disobeyed his voice.

Now the captain released Jeremiah’s hands from their chains. If Jeremiah wished to accompany him to Babylon, he could come, and the captain would care for him. If he did not wish it, he need not come. All the land lay before him; he could go wherever seemed good and right.

Before Jeremiah had gone back, the captain told him he could return to Gedaliah, son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan. Babylon’s king had appointed Gedaliah governor over Judah’s cities. Jeremiah could live among the people with him, or go wherever he thought right. The captain gave Jeremiah food and a present, and let him go.

Jeremiah went to Gedaliah at Mizpah and lived with him among those left in the land.

The captains of the forces in the fields and their men heard of Gedaliah’s appointment. Babylon’s king had committed to him the men, women, children, and poorest people who had not been taken captive. They came with their men to Gedaliah at Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah; Johanan and Jonathan, sons of Kareah; Seraiah son of Tanhumeth; the sons of Ephai the Netophathite; and Jezaniah, son of the Maacathite.

Gedaliah swore to them and their men that they need not fear serving the Chaldeans. They should remain in the land and serve Babylon’s king, and it would be well with them. He would stay in Mizpah to stand before the Chaldeans who came to them. They should gather wine, summer fruits, and oil into their vessels, and dwell in the cities they had taken.

Jews in Moab, among Ammon’s children, in Edom, and throughout the countries heard that Babylon’s king had left a remnant in Judah under Gedaliah. They returned from every place to which they had been driven, coming to Gedaliah at Mizpah in Judah. There they gathered very much wine and summer fruit.
