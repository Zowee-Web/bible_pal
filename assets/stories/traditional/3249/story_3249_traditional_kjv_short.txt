The word of the Lord came unto Jeremiah after Nebuzar–adan, captain of the guard, had released him from Ramah. Jeremiah had been taken, bound in chains, among the captives of Jerusalem and Judah being carried away unto Babylon.

The captain took Jeremiah and said that the Lord his God had pronounced evil upon that place. The Lord had now performed what he had spoken, the captain declared. The people, he said, had sinned against the Lord and had not obeyed his voice; therefore this had come upon them.

The captain loosed Jeremiah’s hands from their chains. If Jeremiah thought it good to come with him into Babylon, he might come, and the captain would look well unto him. If it seemed ill, he might forbear. All the land was before him, and he could go wherever seemed good and convenient.

Before Jeremiah had gone back, the captain directed him to Gedaliah, son of Ahikam, son of Shaphan, whom Babylon’s king had made governor over Judah’s cities. He might dwell with Gedaliah among the people, or go wherever seemed convenient. Nebuzar–adan gave Jeremiah victuals and a reward, and let him go.

Jeremiah went to Gedaliah at Mizpah, dwelling with him among those left in the land.

The captains of the forces in the fields and their men heard that Babylon’s king had appointed Gedaliah governor. Men, women, children, and the poor who had not been carried captive were committed unto him. The captains came with their men to Mizpah: Ishmael son of Nethaniah; Johanan and Jonathan, sons of Kareah; Seraiah son of Tanhumeth; the sons of Ephai the Netophathite; and Jezaniah, son of a Maachathite.

Gedaliah sware unto them and their men. Let them not fear to serve the Chaldeans; let them dwell in the land and serve Babylon’s king, and it would be well with them. He would remain at Mizpah to serve the Chaldeans who came unto them. They should gather wine, summer fruits, and oil into their vessels, and inhabit the cities they had taken.

The Jews in Moab, among the Ammonites, in Edom, and in all the countries heard of the remnant left in Judah under Gedaliah. From every place whither they had been driven, they returned to Judah, to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and gathered wine and summer fruits in abundance.
