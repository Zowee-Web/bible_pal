He looked up.

The rich men were casting their gifts into the treasury at the gate of the women's court. The coins fell heavy into the box. The sound of them came up out of the offerings in the brass.

Jesus beheld. The disciples stood with him.

A poor widow came forward. Her hand was small. Her cloak was worn. The veil of her head was thin.

She cast in two small mites of brass. The sound of them in the box was very small.

The rich passed on. The next gave their gift. The day went on in the court of the temple.

Jesus turned unto his disciples. He said —

"Of a truth I say unto you, that this poor widow hath cast in more than they all: for all these have of their abundance cast in unto the offerings of God: but she of her penury hath cast in all the living that she had."

The disciples looked. The widow was yet walking out of the court. She had not turned back unto any man. She knew not what he had said.

The day went on at the treasury. The rich kept coming. The coins kept falling into the box.

But the two small mites of the widow were therein now among the gifts of the rich. And the eyes of the Lord had been upon her hand when she opened it to let them go.
