He looked up.

The rich people were putting their gifts into the treasury at the gate of the women's court. The coins fell heavy into the box. The sound of them came up out of the offerings in the brass.

Jesus watched. The disciples stood with him.

A poor widow came forward. Her hand was small. Her cloak was worn. The veil of her head was thin.

She put in two small brass coins. The sound of them in the box was very small.

The rich passed on. The next gave their gift. The day went on in the court of the temple.

Jesus turned to his disciples. He said —

"Truly I tell you, this poor widow put in more than all of them, for all these put in gifts for God from their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, put in all that she had to live on."

The disciples looked. The widow was still walking out of the court. She had not turned back to anyone. She did not know what he had said.

The day went on at the treasury. The rich kept coming. The coins kept falling into the box.

But the two small coins of the widow were in there now among the gifts of the rich. And the eyes of the Lord had been on her hand when she had opened it to let them go.
