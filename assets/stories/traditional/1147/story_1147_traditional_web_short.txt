Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, opposite Jericho. The wind moved across the heights, and the morning lay clear over the land. The Lord showed him all the land of Gilead to Dan, and all Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah to the Western Sea. The Lord showed him also the south, and the Plain of the valley of Jericho the city of palm trees, to Zoar.

Then the Lord said to him, "This is the land which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, 'I will give it to your offspring.' I have caused you to see it with your eyes, but you shall not go over there."

So Moses the servant of the Lord died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the Lord. He was buried in the valley in the land of Moab opposite Beth Peor, but no man knows of his tomb to this day. The valley was quiet, and only the Lord knew the place.

Moses was one hundred and twenty years old when he died. His eye was not dim, and his natural force was not gone.

The children of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab for thirty days, until the days of weeping in the mourning for Moses were ended.

And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him. The children of Israel listened to him, and they did as the Lord had commanded Moses.

There has not arisen a prophet since in Israel like Moses, whom the Lord knew face to face. In all the signs and wonders he did in the land of Egypt, and in all the mighty hand and great terror that Moses worked in the sight of all Israel, there was no prophet like him after.
