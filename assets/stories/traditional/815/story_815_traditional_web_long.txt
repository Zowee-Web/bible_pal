The sun hung low, casting long shadows on the dusty paths of Jerusalem. The city hummed with the quiet whispers of farewell, for many were departing for distant lands. Among them were two heartbroken friends, their faces streaked with tears.

Their companion, a teacher beloved by all, had passed into eternity. He had shared stories of a place where pain and sorrow were no more, a place where joy filled every heart. Now, with their hearts still raw from his loss, the two friends yearned for that peace.

Guided by their teacher's words, they set out on a journey to a hilltop tomb. The stone gate creaked open to reveal a scene of silent sorrow. The tomb was dark and damp, the air thick with the scent of spices and grief.

As they approached the stone sarcophagus, a sudden tremor shook the earth. The ground beneath their feet cracked and shifted. The two friends trembled in fear, but their faith remained steadfast. They knew their teacher's words were true.

A blinding light filled the tomb, casting away the shadows. An angel stood before them, wings outstretched like a radiant tapestry. The angel spoke with a voice as gentle as the morning breeze, "Do not be afraid. He is not here. He has risen."

The friends gasped in awe. The tomb, once a place of sorrow, was now a place of victory. The angel touched the stone sarcophagus, and with a deafening crack, it shattered into a million pieces.

The two friends rejoiced. Their tears flowed freely, but this time, they were tears of joy. The teacher they loved was gone, but his spirit lived on. He had conquered death, and his friends were filled with a newfound hope.

From that day forward, the two friends shared the story of their teacher's resurrection. They told others of the peace they found in his words, the love that never fades, and the promise of eternal life. And every time they shared the story, they remembered the angel's words: "He is not here. He has risen."
