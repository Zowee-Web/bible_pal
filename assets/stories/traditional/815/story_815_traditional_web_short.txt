Two friends were walking down a dusty road that led away from Jerusalem. The sun was starting to sink, painting everything with a golden light. Their steps were slow and quiet. One of the men was named Cleopas, and the other walked closely beside him. Each of them felt sad and tired as they talked about all the things that had just happened in Jerusalem.

As they walked, a stranger came up and began to walk with them. The stranger seemed kind and gentle, but the two friends did not know who he was. He asked them, “What are you talking about as you walk?” They stopped and looked sad. Cleopas answered, “Are you the only one visiting Jerusalem who does not know what has happened there these last days?” The stranger asked, “What things?” They began to tell him about Jesus of Nazareth, who was a great teacher and did many wonderful things. They shared how so many people had hoped he would save Israel, but now everything seemed lost.

They told the stranger how some women from their group had gone to the tomb early that morning, but did not find Jesus’ body. The women saw angels, who said that Jesus was alive. Some of the friends ran to the tomb, but they did not see Jesus. Cleopas and his companion felt very confused as they walked.

The stranger listened quietly as the two spoke. Then he began to explain the old stories from God’s word. He spoke of Moses and all the prophets, showing them how these stories pointed to everything that had just happened. The two friends listened closely while the stranger spoke. As he talked, the road seemed a little less dusty and the sky seemed a little brighter.

Soon, they reached the village called Emmaus. It was evening, and the air felt cool. The stranger acted as if he would keep walking, but the two friends begged him to stay. “It’s almost night,” they said. “Come and have supper with us.” So, the stranger came inside. They sat at the table together, and the warm glow from a lamp filled the little room.

As they ate, the stranger took some bread, blessed it, and broke it into pieces to share. At once, Cleopas and his friend realized who he was. It was Jesus! Right then, Jesus disappeared from their sight. The two friends looked at each other in wonder. They remembered how their hearts felt glad and warm while he talked on the road. Even though it was dark, they hurried back to Jerusalem, eager to tell the others how they had seen Jesus and knew that he was truly alive.
