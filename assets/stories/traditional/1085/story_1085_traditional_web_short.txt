In the town of Capernaum, word spread that Jesus had come home. People gathered at the house where he was staying. More and more came, until the rooms were full and the doorway was crowded. Still they pressed in from outside. Jesus stood inside, speaking the word to them.

Outside the house, four men made their way through the crowd. They carried a paralytic man between them, lying on a mat. They wanted to bring him to Jesus. But the crowd was too thick. They could not even get near the door.

The four men looked at one another. Then they looked up.

They climbed onto the roof of the house, carrying the paralytic with them. Up on the roof, they began to break away the clay and thatch. Piece by piece, they opened a hole in the roof above the place where Jesus sat teaching.

Inside, the people looked up. Dust and bits of roof fell down. A beam of sunlight broke through. And then, through the hole, the four men lowered the paralytic on his mat, slowly, carefully, until he came to rest at Jesus' feet.

Jesus saw their faith — the faith of the four men, and of the one who lay before him. He looked at the paralytic.

"Son," he said, "your sins are forgiven you."

Among the crowd were some scribes. They sat quietly, but inside they were troubled. "Why does this man speak this way?" they thought. "Who can forgive sins but God alone?"

Jesus perceived in his spirit what they were thinking. He turned to them.

"Why do you reason these things in your hearts?" he asked. "Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Arise, take up your mat, and walk?' But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins —"

He turned to the paralytic.

"I tell you," he said, "arise. Take up your mat, and go to your house."

The man rose. Right there, in front of them all, he stood up. He picked up the mat he had been lying on. He walked out through the crowd, and the people stepped back to let him pass.

They were all amazed. They lifted their voices and gave glory to God.

"We have never seen anything like this," they said.
