In a town called Capernaum, people heard that Jesus was at a house. So many people came to see him that the whole house was packed full. There was no room left — not even by the door!

Four friends had a friend who couldn't walk. He had to lie on a mat all the time. They really wanted to bring him to Jesus, but they couldn't get through all the people.

So they came up with a plan.

They carried their friend up to the flat roof of the house. Then they started breaking a hole in the roof, right above where Jesus was standing! Dirt and pieces of roof fell down inside. Everyone looked up in surprise.

Then the four friends lowered their friend down through the hole on his mat, all the way down, until he landed right in front of Jesus.

Jesus could see how much the friends believed in him. He looked at the man on the mat and said, "Son, your sins are forgiven."

Some people sitting nearby got upset. "Only God can forgive sins!" they whispered to each other.

But Jesus knew what they were thinking. He said, "Is it easier to say 'Your sins are forgiven' or to say 'Get up and walk'? I want you to know that I have the power to forgive sins."

Then he looked right at the man on the mat and said, "Get up. Pick up your mat. Go home."

And the man stood up! Right there, in front of everyone! He picked up his mat and walked straight out the door. The crowd couldn't believe it.

"We have never seen anything like this!" they all said.
