Sometimes the way forward lieth not through the door.

The man in this account could not come unto Jesus by his own strength. He could not walk, could not press through the multitude, could not so much as cry out above the noise. But he had friends who would not be turned aside — friends who bore him when he could not bear himself, who climbed when the way was shut, who brake open a roof because the door sufficed not.

There are seasons when the faith that beareth us up is not altogether our own. When another believeth on our behalf, cometh when we cannot, and refuseth to let the press of the crowd be the last word.
