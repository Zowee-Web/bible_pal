Hilkiah the high priest was in the house of the Lord on the day they found the book.

The priests were cleansing the chambers of the temple. The workmen were repairing the doors and the floors. The money for the work had been counted out and delivered into the hand of them that had the oversight of it.

Hilkiah came up from the inner chambers. He had a scroll in his hand. He went unto Shaphan the scribe.

"I have found the book of the law," he said unto Shaphan, "in the house of the Lord."

He gave the book unto Shaphan.

Shaphan read it as he stood there. Then he sat down. He read further.

He bare it unto the king.

He went up unto the palace. He came into the chamber of the king. King Josiah was young, and Shaphan stood before him with the scroll under his arm.

"Thy servants have gathered the money that was found in the house," said Shaphan, "and have delivered it into the hand of the workmen that have the oversight of the house of the Lord."

He held the scroll forth.

"Hilkiah the priest hath delivered me a book," said Shaphan.

He unrolled the scroll. He read it before the king.

The king hearkened.

And it came to pass, when the king had heard the words of the book of the law, that he rent his clothes.

He arose from his seat. He rent the robe of the king from the shoulder unto the hem. He rent the tunic. He rent the cloak that was above it.

He called for Hilkiah the priest. He called for Ahikam the son of Shaphan. He called for Achbor the son of Micaiah. He called for Shaphan the scribe. He called for Asaiah the king's servant. They came unto him.

He spake unto them.

"Go ye, enquire of the Lord for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found. For great is the wrath of the Lord that is kindled against us. Our fathers have not hearkened unto the words of this book, to do according unto all that which is written concerning us."

They went out from him to enquire.
