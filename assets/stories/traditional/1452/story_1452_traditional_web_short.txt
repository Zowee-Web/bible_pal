Hilkiah the high priest was in the house of Yahweh on the day they found the book.

The priests were cleaning the rooms of the temple. The workmen were repairing the doors and the floors. Money for the work had been counted out and given to those who had charge of it.

Hilkiah came up from the inner rooms. He had a scroll in his hand. He went to Shaphan the scribe.

"I have found the book of the law," he said to Shaphan, "in the house of Yahweh."

He gave the book to Shaphan.

Shaphan read it standing there. Then he sat down. He read more.

He took it to the king.

He came up to the palace. He went into the room of the king. King Josiah was young, and Shaphan stood before him with the scroll under his arm.

"Your servants have emptied out the money that was found in the house," said Shaphan, "and have delivered it into the hand of the workmen who have the oversight of the house of Yahweh."

He held the scroll out.

"Hilkiah the priest has delivered me a book," said Shaphan.

He unrolled the scroll. He read it before the king.

The king listened.

When the king had heard the words of the book of the law, he tore his clothes.

He stood up from his chair. He tore the robe of the king from the shoulder to the hem. He tore the tunic. He tore the cloak above it.

He called for Hilkiah the priest. He called for Ahikam the son of Shaphan. He called for Achbor the son of Micaiah. He called for Shaphan the scribe. He called for Asaiah the king's servant. They came to him.

He spoke to them.

"Go inquire of Yahweh for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found. For great is the wrath of Yahweh that is kindled against us. Our fathers have not listened to the words of this book, to do according to all that which is written concerning us."

They went out from him to inquire.
