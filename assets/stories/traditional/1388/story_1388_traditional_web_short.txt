John the Baptist was in the prison of Herod at Machaerus. The cell was below the level of the fortress, and the keepers came down with bread and water in the morning and the evening; and the men of John who had been with him at the Jordan came to him there, bringing the reports of the country — what Jesus of Nazareth was doing in the cities and on the lake, the lepers cleansed, the dead raised up, the blind seeing. John heard these things. He sent two of his disciples up the road from the prison.

Now when John heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples

and said to him, "Are you he who comes, or should we look for another?"

Jesus answered them,

"Go and tell John the things which you hear and see:

the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear,
the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.

Blessed is he who finds no occasion for stumbling in me."

And the disciples turned back from Galilee toward Machaerus. The hill where the prison stood waited in the late sun across the Dead Sea; the answer they were carrying was the answer of the works themselves — the things that Isaiah had said the Messiah would do, set down now in the country of Galilee — and they would bring the words to the man in the cell below the fortress, that he might hear and not stumble.
