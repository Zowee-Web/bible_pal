John the Baptist was in the prison of Herod at Machaerus. The cell was below the level of the fortress, and the keepers came down with bread and water in the morning and in the evening; and the men of John that had been with him at Jordan came unto him there, bringing the tidings of the country — what Jesus of Nazareth was doing in the cities and on the lake, the lepers cleansed, the dead raised up, the blind seeing. John heard these things. He sent two of his disciples up the road from the prison.

Now when John had heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples,

And said unto him, "Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?"

Jesus answered and said unto them,

"Go and shew John again those things which ye do hear and see:

The blind receive their sight, and the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear,
the dead are raised up, and the poor have the gospel preached to them.

And blessed is he, whosoever shall not be offended in me."

And the disciples turned again from Galilee toward Machaerus. The hill whereon the prison stood waited in the late sun across the Dead Sea; the answer they bare was the answer of the works themselves — the things which Isaiah had said the Messiah should do, set down now in the country of Galilee — and they should bring the words unto the man in the cell below the fortress, that he might hear and not be offended.
