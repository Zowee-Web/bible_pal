Jesus and his disciples came to the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gerasenes. When he had come out of the boat, immediately a man with an unclean spirit met him out of the tombs. He had his dwelling in the tombs, and no one could bind him any more, not even with chains.

He had often been chained and bound with fetters, but the chains had been torn apart by him, and the fetters broken in pieces. No one had the strength to tame him. Always, night and day, in the tombs and in the mountains, he was crying out and cutting himself with stones.

When he saw Jesus from afar, he ran and bowed down to him, and crying out with a loud voice, he said, "What have I to do with you, Jesus, you Son of the Most High God? I beg you by God, don't torment me."

For Jesus had said to him, "Come out of the man, you unclean spirit."

Jesus asked him, "What is your name?" He said to him, "My name is Legion, for we are many." He begged Jesus much that he would not send them away out of the country.

Now on the mountainside there was a great herd of pigs feeding. The unclean spirits begged him, saying, "Send us into the pigs, that we may enter into them." At once Jesus gave them permission. The unclean spirits came out, and entered into the pigs. The herd, about two thousand, rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and they were drowned in the sea.

Those who fed the pigs fled, and they told it in the city and in the country. The people came to see what had happened. They came to Jesus, and saw the man who had been possessed sitting, clothed, and in his right mind. They were afraid.

The man who had been possessed begged Jesus that he might be with him. Jesus did not allow him, but said to him, "Go to your house, to your friends, and tell them what great things the Lord has done for you, and how he had mercy on you." He went his way, and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him.
