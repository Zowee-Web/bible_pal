The night before Herod would have brought him forth, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers.

He was bound with two chains. The keepers stood at the door of the cell. The prison was quiet.

And behold, the cell was filled with light.

An angel of the Lord stood by him. He smote Peter on the side. He said, "Arise up quickly."

The chains fell off from Peter's hands. The iron smote the stone of the floor. His hands lifted into the light.

The angel said, "Gird thyself, and bind on thy sandals."

And so he did.

"Cast thy garment about thee," said the angel, "and follow me."

Peter followed.

He went after the angel into the corridor. He went past the first ward. The ward stirred not. He went past the second ward. The second ward stirred not.

He came unto the iron gate that led into the city.

The iron gate opened to them of his own accord. The city air came in. The street without was empty in the night.

They went out into the street. They passed on through one street. The angel departed from him.

Peter stood alone in the street in the night.

He came to himself.

He said, "Now I know of a surety, that the Lord hath sent his angel, and hath delivered me out of the hand of Herod, and from all the expectation of the people of the Jews."

The chains were back in the cell where he had been. The keepers were yet at the door where he had been. The iron gate was shut behind him.

Peter went down the street toward the house of Mary the mother of John.
