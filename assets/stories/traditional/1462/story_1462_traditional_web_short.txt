The night before Herod meant to bring him out, Peter was sleeping between two soldiers.

He was bound with two chains. The guards stood at the door of the cell. The prison was quiet.

Then the cell filled with light.

An angel of the Lord stood by him. He struck Peter on the side. He said, "Stand up quickly."

The chains fell off Peter's hands. The iron struck the stone of the floor. His hands lifted into the light.

The angel said, "Get dressed. Put on your sandals."

Peter did it.

"Put on your cloak," said the angel. "Follow me."

Peter followed.

He went after the angel into the corridor. He went past the first guard. The guard did not stir. He went past the second guard. The second guard did not stir.

He came to the iron gate that led into the city.

The iron gate opened to them by itself. The city air came in. The street outside was empty in the night.

They went out into the street. They went down one street. The angel left him.

Peter stood alone in the street in the night.

He came to himself.

He said, "Now I truly know that the Lord has sent his angel and delivered me out of the hand of Herod, and from everything the Jewish people were expecting."

The chains were back in the cell where he had been. The guards were still at the door where he had been. The iron gate was shut behind him.

Peter went down the street toward the house of Mary the mother of John.
