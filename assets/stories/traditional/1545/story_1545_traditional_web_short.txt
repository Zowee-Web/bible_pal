Peter stood before Jesus and asked him a question. "Lord, how many times shall my brother sin against me and I forgive him? Up to seven times?"

Jesus said to him, "I do not say to you up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven."

Then Jesus told them this. The kingdom of heaven is like a certain king who decided to settle accounts with his servants. When he had begun to reckon, one servant was brought before him who owed ten thousand talents. The amount was beyond paying. The king commanded that the man be sold, along with his wife and his children and everything he owned, and that payment be made.

The servant fell down on the floor before the king. His forehead pressed against the stone. "Lord, have patience with me," he said, "and I will pay you all of it."

The king looked at the man on the ground. He was moved with compassion. He released the servant and forgave him the entire debt. Every coin of it. Gone.

The servant walked out from the king's hall. He went through the courtyard and into the streets. There he found a fellow servant who owed him a hundred pence. He seized the man by the throat with both hands. "Pay me what you owe," he said.

The fellow servant dropped to his knees. His hands gripped the other man's wrists. "Have patience with me," he said, "and I will pay you all of it."

The same words. The same posture. The same plea.

The servant would not. He dragged the man away and had him thrown into prison until the debt was paid.

Other servants saw what had happened. They were deeply grieved. They went together to the king and told him everything.

The king summoned the servant back. The man stood again in the same hall where he had been forgiven. The king's face had changed.

"You wicked servant," the king said. "I forgave you all that debt because you begged me. Should you not also have had compassion on your fellow servant, the way I had pity on you?"

The king was angry. He handed the servant over to the jailers to be tortured until he paid everything that was due.

Jesus looked at the men around him. "So also my heavenly Father will do to every one of you," he said, "if you do not forgive your brother from your heart."
