Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great, clothed with honour and majesty. Light covereth thee as a garment. Thou stretchedst out the heavens like a curtain, laidst the beams of thy chambers in the waters, madest the clouds thy chariot, and walkedst upon the wings of the wind.

Thou didst found the earth that it should not be removed. The deep covered it, and the waters stood above the mountains; at thy rebuke they fled. The mountains rose and the valleys went down unto their appointed places. Thou hast set a bound, that the waters turn not again to cover the earth.

Springs run through the valleys among the hills. They give drink to every beast of the field, and the wild asses quench their thirst. The fowls dwell nearby and sing among the branches. Thou waterest the hills. Grass groweth for cattle, and the earth bringeth forth food for man: wine, oil, and bread. The cedars of Lebanon are full; birds nest there, while high hills shelter wild goats and the rocks shelter conies.

Thou appointedst the moon for seasons, and the sun knoweth his going down. Thou makest darkness, and the beasts of the forest creep forth. Young lions seek their meat from God. When the sun ariseth, they lie down in their dens, and man goeth forth unto his labour until evening.

O Lord, how manifold are thy works! In wisdom hast thou made them all. The great and wide sea holdeth things creeping innumerable, both small and great. There go the ships, and leviathan playeth therein. These wait upon thee for meat in due season. Thou openest thine hand, and they are filled with good. Thou hidest thy face, and they are troubled. Thou takest away their breath, and they return to dust; thou sendest forth thy spirit, and they are created, renewing the face of the earth.

The glory of the Lord shall endure for ever; the Lord shall rejoice in his works. He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth; he toucheth the hills, and they smoke. I will sing unto the Lord as long as I live and praise my God while I have my being. My meditation of him shall be sweet. Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the Lord, O my soul. Praise ye the Lord.
