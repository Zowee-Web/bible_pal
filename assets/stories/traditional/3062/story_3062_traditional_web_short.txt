Bless Yahweh, my soul. Yahweh my God, you are very great, clothed with honor and majesty. Light covers you like a garment. You stretched out the heavens like a curtain, laid the beams of your rooms in the waters, made the clouds your chariot, and walked on the wings of the wind.

You founded the earth so that it would not be moved. The deep covered it, and waters stood above the mountains; at your rebuke they fled. Mountains rose and valleys sank to their appointed places. You set a boundary so the waters would not return to cover the earth.

Springs run through the valleys among the mountains. They give drink to field animals and wild donkeys, while birds nest and sing in the branches. You water the mountains. Grass grows for livestock, and plants yield food for man: wine, oil, and bread. The cedars of Lebanon are watered for nesting birds; the high mountains shelter wild goats, and rocks shelter rock badgers.

You appointed the moon for seasons, and the sun knows when to set. Darkness becomes night, and forest animals move. Young lions seek their food from God. When the sun rises, they lie down, and man goes out to labor until evening.

Yahweh, how many are your works! In wisdom you made them all. The great, wide sea holds living things without number, small and large. Ships cross it, and leviathan plays there. All of them wait for you to give food in due season. You open your hand, and they gather good things. When you hide your face, they are troubled. You take away their breath, and they return to dust; you send your Spirit, and they are created, renewing the ground.

Let Yahweh’s glory endure forever, and let him rejoice in his works. He looks at the earth, and it trembles; he touches the mountains, and they smoke. I will sing to Yahweh as long as I live and rejoice in him while I have being. Let my meditation be sweet to him. Let sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless Yahweh, my soul. Praise Yah!
