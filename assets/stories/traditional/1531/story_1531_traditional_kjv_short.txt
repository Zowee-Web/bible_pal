And John beheld a new heaven and a new earth.

For the first heaven and the first earth were passed away. The sea was no more. Where once it had lain, there remained nothing — no wave, no deep, no far line where water had met the sky. All of it was ended.

Then he saw the holy city. New Jerusalem descended out of heaven from God. It came down as a bride cometh unto her bridegroom — made ready, adorned, and bright. The city moved through the open heavens, and it was clean and whole and full of light.

And a great voice spake out of heaven. It was loud enough to fill every place that was.

"Behold. The dwelling place of God is among men. He shall dwell with them. They shall be his people. And God himself shall be with them, and shall be their God."

And the voice continued. Each word fell clear and unhurried upon the air.

"He shall wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death shall be no more. Neither shall there be sorrow, nor weeping, nor any pain henceforth. For the former things are passed away."

Then he that sat upon the throne spake. His voice came from the midst of all things, from the seat of all dominion.

"Behold. I make all things new."

And he spake again, directly unto John.

"Write thou this. For these words are faithful and true."

John heard it. The commandment was plain.

Then said the one upon the throne, "It is finished. I am the Alpha and the Omega. The beginning and the end. Unto him that thirsteth I will give of the fountain of the water of life, and freely. He that overcometh shall inherit all things. I will be his God, and he shall be my son."

The throne stood at the center. The holy city hung in the air above the new earth, gleaming, descending still. And the voice from the throne yet carried across that open, tearless, deathless world.
