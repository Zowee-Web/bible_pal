John saw a new heaven and a new earth.

The first heaven and the first earth had passed away. The sea was gone. Where it had been, there was nothing left — no waves, no deep water, no horizon line where ocean met sky. All of it was finished.

Then he saw the holy city. New Jerusalem came down out of heaven from God. It descended the way a bride walks toward her husband — prepared, adorned, bright. The city moved through open sky and it was clean and whole and shining.

A great voice spoke out of heaven. It was loud enough to fill every space.

"Look. The tabernacle of God is with men. He will dwell with them. They will be his people. God himself will be with them and be their God."

The voice continued. Each word was clear and unhurried.

"God will wipe away all tears from their eyes. There will be no more death. No sorrow. No crying. No more pain. The former things have passed away."

Then he who sat upon the throne spoke. His voice came from the center of everything, from the seat of all authority.

"Look. I make all things new."

He spoke again, directly to John.

"Write this down. These words are true and faithful."

John heard it. The command was plain.

Then the one on the throne said, "It is done. I am the Alpha and the Omega. The beginning and the end. To anyone who thirsts, I will give from the fountain of the water of life, freely. The one who overcomes will inherit all things. I will be his God, and he will be my son."

The throne stood at the center. The holy city hung in the air above the new earth, gleaming, descending. The voice from the throne still carried across that open, tearless, deathless world.
