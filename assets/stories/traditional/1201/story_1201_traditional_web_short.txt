Jesus was teaching in one of the synagogues on the Sabbath day. The light came through the high windows of the synagogue, and the people sat upon the benches along the walls.

Behold, there was a woman who had a spirit of infirmity for eighteen years. She was bent over and could in no way straighten herself up. She had come into the synagogue with the others, and she stood at the back of the room.

When Jesus saw her, he called her, and said to her, "Woman, you are freed from your infirmity."

He laid his hands on her. Immediately she stood up straight and glorified God. The bend of her back was gone, and she lifted her face toward the windows of the synagogue, and she praised the Lord.

The ruler of the synagogue, being indignant because Jesus had healed on the Sabbath, said to the multitude, "There are six days in which men ought to work. Therefore come on those days and be healed, and not on the Sabbath day."

Therefore the Lord answered him, "You hypocrites. Doesn't each one of you free his ox or his donkey from the stall on the Sabbath, and lead him away to water? Ought not this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan had bound eighteen long years, be freed from this bondage on the Sabbath day?"

As he said these things, all his adversaries were disappointed. All the multitude rejoiced for all the glorious things that were done by him.

The woman stood among the people, and she did not sit down again. She walked through the synagogue with her back straight, and the people made way for her. She went out into the courts, and she lifted her eyes to the sky as she walked.

The Sabbath continued in the synagogue, and the teachers sat back upon their benches. The crowd remained for a long time, and the voice of the woman who had been bent for eighteen years was heard again and again, praising God.

The morning sun was warm above the city, and the woman walked home with her head lifted toward the open sky.
