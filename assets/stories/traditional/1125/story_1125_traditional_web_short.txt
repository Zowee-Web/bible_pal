An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip and said, "Arise and go toward the south on the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza." This was a desert road, dry and open between the cities, where the wind moved quietly across the empty ground. Philip arose and went.

As he walked, he saw a man of Ethiopia, a eunuch of great authority under Candace the queen of the Ethiopians. He had charge of all her treasure. The man had come to Jerusalem to worship and was now returning, sitting in his chariot and reading aloud from the prophet Isaiah. The Spirit said to Philip, "Go near and join this chariot."

Philip ran up to the chariot and heard him reading Isaiah. He said, "Do you understand what you are reading?" The eunuch answered, "How can I, unless someone explains it to me?" Then he invited Philip to come up and sit with him.

The passage of scripture he was reading was this: "He was led as a sheep to the slaughter, and as a lamb before its shearer is silent, so he did not open his mouth." The eunuch asked, "Of whom does the prophet speak? Of himself, or of some other man?" Then Philip began with that scripture and preached Jesus to him.

As they went on their way, they came to some water by the roadside. The eunuch said, "Look, here is water. What hinders me from being baptized?" Philip answered, "If you believe with all your heart, you may." He said, "I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God."

He commanded the chariot to stand still, and they both went down into the water. Philip baptized him there. When they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught Philip away, and the eunuch did not see him anymore. He went on his way rejoicing.
