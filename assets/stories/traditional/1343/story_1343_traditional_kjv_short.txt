Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah was a young man of Anathoth, of the priests, in the days of King Josiah. He had not yet stood before any city to prophesy, and he had not yet opened his mouth as a prophet of the Lord, and he was at the beginning. And after, when he set down the days of his calling, he wrote them as they had been given unto him; and he wrote:

Then the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

"Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee;
and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee,
and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations."

Then said I, "Ah, Lord God! behold, I cannot speak: for I am a child."

But the Lord said unto me, "Say not, I am a child: for thou shalt go to all that I shall send thee, and whatsoever I command thee thou shalt speak.

Be not afraid of their faces: for I am with thee to deliver thee," saith the Lord.

Then the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth. And the Lord said unto me, "Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth.

See, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down, to build, and to plant."

And the call was set down. The young man went forth from the day of his calling, and the kingdom of Judah went on, and the years began to unfold in the way the word had said they would unfold. And the scroll was kept, and the calling was kept with it; and the words "I am a child" stood in the record alongside the words "I have put my words in thy mouth," so that any that came after Jeremiah and was called as Jeremiah had been called might read both. And so it was in the days when the Lord called Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, in the thirteenth year of the reign of Josiah.
