Morning light spread across the lake called Gennesaret. Small waves lapped against the rocky shore. Two wooden boats sat at the water's edge, still and empty. Nigh unto them, fishermen knelt upon the wet stones, washing their nets. Their hands moved slowly, plucking bits of seaweed from the ropes. The savour of lake water and damp wood hung in the cool air.

A multitude of people walked along the shore unto a man called Jesus. More and more people gathered, pressing close to hear him speak. The company grew so great that Jesus stepped into one of the boats. It belonged unto a fisherman named Simon. Jesus sat down in the boat and prayed Simon that he would thrust it out a little from the land. Simon set his hands upon the side of the boat and thrust it into the shallow water. The boat rocked gently. From thence Jesus spake unto all the people standing upon the beach. His voice carried across the water, and the multitude gave ear.

When Jesus had left speaking, he turned unto Simon. "Launch out into the deep," he said, "and let down your nets for a draught." Simon beheld him. "Master," Simon said, "we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing." He paused. Then he said, "Nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net." Simon and his partners rowed the boat farther out where the water turned deep and dark blue. They lifted the heavy nets and let them down over the side. The nets sank down into the lake.

Then a wondrous thing came to pass. The nets began to pull and stretch. Fishes filled them — so many fishes that the nets began to break at the seams. Simon called out unto his partners in the other ship, beckoning with his arms. "Come and help us!" James and John, the sons of Zebedee, rowed forth with haste. They came alongside and began to gather the fishes into both ships. Fishes spilled and flopped across the wooden boards. Both ships sat so low in the water that they began to sink.

Simon fell down upon his knees in the boat, there among all those fishes. He looked up unto Jesus. "Depart from me, Lord," he said, "for I am a sinful man." Every one round about him beheld the great draught. James and John stood still, their hands dripping with lake water. Jesus beheld Simon with a steady, gentle gaze. "Fear not," Jesus said. "From henceforth thou shalt catch men."

They brought both ships back unto the shore. The hulls scraped softly against the sand. Water dripped from the nets piled high with fishes. Simon, James, and John stepped forth upon the beach. They drew the ships up and forsook all there upon the shore — the fishes, the nets, the ships. Then they went away with Jesus, side by side, along the sunlit path that led from the lake.
