Fishing nets heavy with silver fish, spilling over the sides of a little boat. Simon Peter had worked all night and caught nothing at all. But when Jesus asked him to try one more time, something amazing happened. The nets were so full they almost broke!

Sometimes things feel hard, like when a puzzle piece just won't fit or a drawing doesn't look quite right. And then someone comes along and helps, and everything changes.

Peter felt so surprised that day. He didn't know what to say. Jesus just smiled and invited him to come along on a brand-new adventure. Friends can show up in the most wonderful ways.
