Morning light spread across the lake called Gennesaret. Small waves lapped against the rocky shore. Two wooden boats sat at the water's edge, still and empty. Nearby, fishermen knelt on the wet stones, washing their nets. Their hands moved slowly, pulling bits of seaweed from the ropes. The smell of lake water and damp wood hung in the cool air.

A crowd of people walked along the shore toward a man named Jesus. More and more people gathered, pressing close to hear him speak. The group grew so large that Jesus stepped into one of the boats. It belonged to a fisherman named Simon. Jesus sat down in the boat and asked Simon to push it out a little way from the shore. Simon set his hands on the side of the boat and pushed it into the shallow water. The boat rocked gently. From there, Jesus spoke to all the people standing along the beach. His voice carried across the water, and the crowd listened.

When Jesus finished speaking, he turned to Simon. "Push out into the deep water," he said. "Let down your nets for a catch." Simon looked at him. "Teacher," Simon said, "we worked hard all through the night and caught nothing." He paused. Then he said, "But because you say so, I will let down the nets." Simon and his partners rowed the boat farther out where the water turned deep and dark blue. They lifted the heavy nets and lowered them over the side. The nets sank down into the lake.

Then something wonderful happened. The nets began to pull and stretch. Fish filled them — so many fish that the nets started to come apart at the seams. Simon called out to his partners in the other boat, waving his arms. "Come and help us!" James and John, the sons of Zebedee, rowed out quickly. They pulled alongside and began scooping fish into both boats. Fish spilled and flopped across the wooden floors. Both boats sat so low in the water that the edges nearly touched the surface.

Simon fell down on his knees in the boat, right there among all those fish. He looked up at Jesus. "Go away from me, Lord," he said, "for I am a sinful man." Everyone around him stared at the enormous catch. James and John stood still, their hands dripping with lake water. Jesus looked at Simon with a steady, gentle gaze. "Don't be nervous," Jesus said. "From now on, you will be catching people."

They brought both boats back to shore. The hulls scraped softly against the sand. Water dripped from the nets piled high with fish. Simon, James, and John stepped out onto the beach. They pulled the boats up and left everything right there on the shore — the fish, the nets, the boats. Then they walked away with Jesus, side by side, along the sunlit path that led away from the lake.
