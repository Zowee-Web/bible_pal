Fishing nets heavy with silver fishes, spilling over the sides of a little ship. Simon Peter had toiled all the night and taken nothing at all. But when Jesus prayed him to try yet one more time, a wondrous thing came to pass. The nets were so full that they began to break!

Sometimes things feel hard, as when a puzzle piece will not fit or a drawing looketh not quite right. And then some one cometh along and helpeth, and all is changed.

Peter felt exceeding amazed that day. He knew not what to say. Jesus did but smile and bid him come along upon a new adventure. Friends may shew up in the most wondrous ways.
