This is one of the songs David wrote for the music director, to be sung at the time of the evening sacrifice.

He had been spoken against by men of his own people. They had been saying things in the streets and in the courts of the city. They had turned his glory into dishonor. They had loved vanity, and sought after falsehood. The words had reached him in his own house.

He did not answer them with anger.

He sat down and wrote this prayer. He gave it to the music director, to be sung in the assembly when the evening sacrifice was offered. He set it down so that the people who had heard the same accusations against him — and the people who had brought such accusations against others — could carry it home to the bed where they would lie down that night.

He said —

"Answer me when I call, God of my righteousness. Give me relief from my distress. Have mercy on me, and hear my prayer.

You sons of men, how long shall my glory be turned into dishonor? Will you love vanity, and seek after falsehood?

But know that Yahweh has set apart for himself him who is godly. Yahweh will hear when I call to him.

Stand in awe, and don't sin. Search your own heart on your bed, and be still.

Offer the sacrifices of righteousness. Put your trust in Yahweh.

Many say, 'Who will show us any good?' Yahweh, let the light of your face shine on us.

You have put gladness in my heart, more than when their grain and their new wine are increased.

In peace I will both lay myself down and sleep, for you, Yahweh alone, make me live in safety."

The song was sung at the temple that evening, with the smoke of the sacrifice rising above the city.

When it was finished, the singers went down from the temple by the long stairs. The people went home to their houses. The lamps were lit in the windows. The streets quieted. The accusations that had filled the day grew smaller as the night closed in.

David lay down in his own house. The men who had spoken against him kept speaking elsewhere in the city. They would speak again in the morning.

But the lamp in his chamber had gone low. The night had come. David slept in peace.
