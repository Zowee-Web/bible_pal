One evening, Jesus and his friends got into a boat to cross the Sea of Galilee. Jesus was very tired. He went to the back of the boat, put his head on a pillow, and fell asleep.

Everything was calm at first. The water was smooth and the sky was pretty.

Then a big storm came out of nowhere!

The wind blew hard. The waves got huge. Water started pouring into the boat. The boat rocked back and forth so much that it felt like it might flip over.

Jesus' friends were scared. They were fishermen — they knew the lake really well — and even they were terrified. They grabbed the sides of the boat and tried to bail out the water.

And Jesus? He was still asleep!

They ran to the back of the boat and shook him awake. "Jesus! Help us! We're going to drown!"

Jesus stood up. He looked at the wind and the waves.

"Peace," he said. "Be still."

And just like that — the wind stopped. The waves went flat. The water became smooth as glass. Everything was completely calm.

The friends sat there, soaking wet, with their mouths open. It was so quiet you could hear the water dripping off the oars.

Jesus looked at them. "Why were you so afraid?" he asked. "Don't you have faith?"

They looked at each other, amazed.

"Who IS this guy?" they whispered. "Even the wind and the waves do what he says!"
