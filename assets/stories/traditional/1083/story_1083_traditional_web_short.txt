In a village near Jerusalem called Bethany, a man named Lazarus was very sick. He lived there with his two sisters, Mary and Martha. The three of them were dear friends of Jesus. When Lazarus grew worse, the sisters sent a message to him. "Lord, the one you love is sick."

When Jesus heard the message, he said, "This sickness will not end in death. It is for the glory of God." And he stayed two more days in the place where he was.

By the time Jesus came to Bethany, Lazarus had already been in the tomb four days. The house was full of mourners. Voices lifted in weeping from the hillside.

Martha heard that Jesus was coming, and she went out to meet him on the road. Her face was wet with tears.

"Lord," she said, "if you had been here, my brother would not have died. But even now, I know that whatever you ask of God, God will give you."

Jesus looked at her gently. "Your brother will rise again," he said.

"I know that he will rise again," Martha said, "in the resurrection on the last day."

Jesus said to her, "I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live. And whoever lives and believes in me shall never die."

Martha went back to the house. She called to her sister. "The Teacher is here," she whispered, "and he is calling for you."

Mary came quickly. She found Jesus still on the road where Martha had left him. She fell down at his feet, weeping. "Lord," she said, "if you had been here, my brother would not have died."

When Jesus saw her weeping, and saw the others weeping with her, he was deeply moved in his spirit.

"Where have you laid him?" he asked.

"Lord, come and see," they said.

And Jesus wept.

They led him to the tomb. It was a cave in the rock, with a great stone laid across the entrance. The mourners gathered around.

"Take away the stone," Jesus said.

Martha spoke softly. "Lord, by this time there is a stench. He has been dead four days."

Jesus looked at her. "Did I not tell you," he said, "that if you believed, you would see the glory of God?"

They rolled the stone away. Jesus lifted his eyes to heaven and gave thanks. Then he cried out with a loud voice:

"Lazarus, come out!"

And the man who had been dead came out, bound hand and foot with grave clothes, his face wrapped in a cloth.

"Loose him," Jesus said, "and let him go."
