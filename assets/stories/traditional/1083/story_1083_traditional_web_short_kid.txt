Lazarus was a man who lived with his two sisters, Mary and Martha, in a little village called Bethany. They were friends with Jesus.

One day, Lazarus got very, very sick. Martha and Mary sent a message to Jesus. "Please come," they said. "Lazarus is sick."

But Jesus didn't come right away. He waited two whole days. By the time he got there, Lazarus had already died. He had been in a tomb for four days.

Martha ran out to meet Jesus on the road. She was crying. "If you had been here," she said, "my brother wouldn't have died."

Jesus said to her, "Your brother will live again. I am the resurrection and the life."

Then Mary came too. She fell at Jesus' feet, crying the same thing. "If you had been here, he wouldn't have died."

When Jesus saw everyone crying, he cried too. Jesus wept.

They all walked to the tomb together. It was a cave with a big stone in front of it.

"Take the stone away," Jesus said.

Martha was worried. "But he's been dead four days!" she said.

Jesus said, "If you believe, you will see something wonderful."

They moved the stone. Jesus looked up to heaven and prayed. Then he called out in a loud voice:

"Lazarus, come out!"

And Lazarus came out! He was still wrapped up in the cloths they had put on him.

Jesus said, "Unwrap him and let him go."
