There are seasons in a life when all that was counted upon is taken away — when the one looked to for help cometh not in time, and the thing most dreaded cometh to pass.

The sisters knew that Jesus could have healed their brother. They declared it openly. And he denied it not. He wept among them. He stood before the grave and was moved.

What followed surpassed all expectation. Yet it erased not the grief that came before. The weeping was true. The waiting was true. The silence of those four days was heavy and real.

Oftentimes the hardest portion of any trial is not the ending — it is the middle. The season where nothing hath yet changed, and all that remaineth is to wait.
