Yahweh told Moses to gather seventy men from the elders of Israel, men whom Moses knew as elders and officers of the people. He was to bring them to the Tent of Meeting so they could stand there with him. Yahweh said he would come down and speak with Moses. He would take of the Spirit that was on Moses and put it on the elders. They would share the burden of the people so Moses would not bear it alone.

Moses was also to tell the people to sanctify themselves for the next day. They had wept for meat and spoken of life in Egypt. Yahweh said they would eat meat, not for one day or two, nor for five, ten, or twenty days, but for a whole month, until it became loathsome to them. Their complaint had rejected Yahweh, who was among them.

Moses answered that the people numbered six hundred thousand men on foot. He asked whether enough flocks and herds could be slaughtered, or enough fish gathered from the sea, to feed them for a month. Yahweh asked whether his hand had grown short and said Moses would see whether his word happened.

Moses went out and repeated Yahweh’s words to the people. He gathered seventy elders and placed them around the Tent. Yahweh came down in the cloud, spoke with Moses, took of the Spirit upon him, and put it on the elders. When the Spirit rested on them, they prophesied, but did so no more.

Two men, Eldad and Medad, had remained in the camp. They were among those listed, though they had not gone out to the Tent. The Spirit rested on them, and they prophesied in the camp. A young man ran to report this to Moses.

Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ servant and one of his chosen men, urged Moses to forbid them. Moses asked whether Joshua was jealous for his sake. He declared his wish that all Yahweh’s people were prophets and that Yahweh would put his Spirit on them. Then Moses and the elders of Israel returned to the camp.
