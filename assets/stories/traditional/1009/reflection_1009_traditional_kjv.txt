Sometimes thou feelest so safe and cozy, as though wrapped in a warm blanket. That feeling of being watched over and cared for is a precious thing. Green grass beneath thy feet, cool water to drink upon a hot day — such good things are all round about thee. Even when a room groweth dark at night, one is right there with thee. A shepherd walketh with the sheep whithersoever they go, through sunny meadows and dim valleys. The sheep never walk by themselves. Goodness and mercy follow close behind, like a friendly little creature that never leaveth thy side. That kind of love abideth with thee every single day.
