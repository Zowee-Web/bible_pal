A shepherd walked across a wide green meadow. Morning dew yet rested upon the grass. Each blade sparkled where the early light touched it. The air bore a savour fresh and clean, as though rain had fallen in the quiet night. Somewhere nearby, a stream moved slowly over smooth stones. Its soft sound carried athwart the field.

Woolly sheep dotted the meadow in small companies. Some stood close together, their coats thick and white. Others lay down in the cool grass, resting. A young lamb pressed its side against its mother. Birds called one to another from a row of low trees along the water. The shepherd held a tall wooden staff in one hand. He walked with steady, unhurried steps.

He led the sheep toward the green pasture. They followed behind him, their small hooves pressing into the soft ground. He did not hasten them. He waited when they stopped to nibble the fresh grass. The meadow stretched out wide and open, and the sheep had plenty of room. They lay down when they would rest. None drave them along.

Then the shepherd guided them beside the still waters. The stream here was calm and shallow. It scarce moved. The sheep lowered their heads and drank. Water dropped from their chins back into the stream. The shepherd stood beside them, his staff resting upon his shoulder. He beheld each one drink its fill. The morning sun warmed the wool upon their backs.

After they had drunk, the shepherd began walking again. He chose a path that was easy for the sheep to follow. The ground was smooth and level. No sharp rocks nor tangled roots blocked the way. He chose this path carefully, and the sheep walked along it one after another. Their hooves made a quiet rhythm upon the packed earth. He restored their strength by leading them unto good food and clean water. He led them in paths of righteousness.

The day moved on, and the trail curved through a low valley. Tall hills rose up on both sides. The space betwixt them grew narrow, and the light turned soft and golden. Long evening light filtered through the branches overhead. It was a quiet place. The air was still and cool.

The shepherd walked ahead of his flock through this valley. His rod hung from his belt, and his staff tapped gently upon the ground. The sheep followed close behind him. He knew this path well. His feet found every turn without stopping. When a sheep slowed down, he waited. When one wandered toward the edge of the trail, he guided it back with the end of his staff. The wood touched its wool gently, and the sheep stepped back upon the path.

Even in this narrow, dim valley, the sheep stayed near the shepherd. His voice carried back unto them. He spake unto them in low, calm words. They lifted their ears at the sound. His rod and his staff were ever with him. These were his tools for keeping the flock together and guiding them forward.

The valley opened up again into a wide, bright clearing. A table of flat rock sat in the open air. The shepherd set out food thereon. He laid down bread, and fruit, and oil. He poured oil upon the head of one sheep that had a scrape upon its ear. The oil was smooth and cool. It ran down the side of the sheep's face. The sheep stood still and let him work.

Then the shepherd filled a wide cup with water from a leather pouch. He poured until the water rose unto the very brim. It spilled over the edge but a little, wetting the stone beneath. The cup ran over. There was more than enough.

Other living things moved through the clearing also. Small brown birds lighted near the table and hopped athwart the stone. A lizard sat upon a warm rock nearby, perfectly still. Bees drifted betwixt wildflowers at the edge of the grass. The shepherd drave none of them away. There was room for every one in this place.

The sheep grazed in the clearing while the shepherd sat upon a low boulder. He laid his staff athwart his knees. The sun moved lower, and the sky turned the colour of ripe peaches. A warm breeze bore the savour of wild herbs athwart the field. The shepherd's robe shifted in the wind. He looked out over his flock and counted each one.

Goodness and mercy followed this flock like steady companions upon the road. Whithersoever the shepherd led, good things came along. Fresh water appeared when they had need of it. Green pastures opened up before them. The path stayed smooth beneath their feet. Day after day, it was the same. The shepherd provided, and the sheep walked on.

As the sky deepened into soft twilight, the shepherd arose and gathered his flock. He called unto them, and they came. One by one, they moved toward his voice. He turned down a familiar path that led toward home. The sheep walked behind him in a loose line. Their wool glowed faintly in the last light of the day.

Ahead, a stone shelter came into view. The walls thereof were sturdy and the roof low. A small fire already burned within, sending a thin line of smoke into the evening air. The shepherd counted each sheep as it passed through the doorway. His hand touched their backs one at a time.

Within, the shelter was warm and dry. Clean straw covered the floor. The sheep settled down, pressing close one to another. The shepherd set his staff against the wall and sat near the entrance. The fire crackled softly. Without, the first stars appeared in the wide, open sky.

The shepherd would dwell in this good place. And the sheep would dwell with him. They should abide in the house of the Lord for ever.
