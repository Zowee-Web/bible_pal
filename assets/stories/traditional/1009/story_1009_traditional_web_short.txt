A young shepherd walked along a quiet hillside one morning. Soft grass bent under his sandals. Woolly sheep moved slowly beside him, nibbling clover. A cool breeze carried the smell of wild mint across the meadow. Small blue flowers dotted the ground near a low stone wall. Birds called to each other from the olive trees nearby.

The shepherd led his flock down a gentle slope toward still water. The pond sat calm and smooth, reflecting the pale sky above. One by one, the sheep lowered their heads and drank. Water dripped from their soft mouths back into the pond. The shepherd rested his staff against his shoulder and watched. He counted each one quietly. Every sheep was here, together and safe. Fresh green pasture stretched out on every side. He guided them to a wide, grassy place where they could lie down and rest. Their wool pressed against the cool ground. Some closed their eyes. Others chewed slowly, content.

Later in the day, the shepherd walked ahead on a narrow path. Tall rocks rose up on both sides. Soft evening light reached down between the stones. The sheep followed close behind, their small hooves tapping the dusty trail. The shepherd's rod tapped gently against the rock wall as he walked. His voice echoed in the quiet space. He hummed a low, steady tune. The sheep stayed near him, stepping where he stepped. His staff brushed lightly along their sides, guiding them forward. The path curved, and warm golden light opened up ahead.

They came out into a wide, green valley. A wooden table sat near a grove of fig trees. Someone had set out bread, oil, and cups of cool water. The shepherd sat down. Oil glistened on his hands as he smoothed it over a scratch on one sheep's ear. The animal stood still, blinking slowly. He poured water into a wide clay cup until it ran over the rim and splashed onto the ground. Bread rested in a basket lined with cloth. The air smelled like ripe figs and warm earth. Everything here was prepared and ready.

As the sun moved lower, the shepherd gathered his flock again. They walked together through a meadow of tall golden grass. Crickets began to sing from the edges of the field. The shepherd spoke out loud. "Goodness and mercy follow me," he said. "All the days of my life." His voice was steady and clear. The sheep walked close beside him, their wool brushing against his legs. Ahead, a small stone house stood beside a low wooden gate. Smoke rose gently from inside. The gate swung open. One by one, the sheep stepped through into a soft, straw-covered pen. The shepherd stood at the gate, counting again. Every one was here. He latched the gate and sat down on the stone step. Stars began to appear, one and then another. The evening was quiet and warm. The sheep rested together in the straw, breathing softly. The shepherd leaned back against the doorframe and looked up at the wide, open sky.
