A shepherd walked across a wide green meadow. Morning dew still rested on the grass. Each blade sparkled where the early light touched it. The air smelled fresh and clean, like rain had fallen during the quiet night. Somewhere nearby, a stream moved slowly over smooth stones. Its soft sound carried across the field.

Woolly sheep dotted the meadow in small groups. Some stood close together, their coats thick and white. Others lay down in the cool grass, resting. A young lamb pressed its side against its mother. Birds called to each other from a row of low trees along the water. The shepherd held a tall wooden staff in one hand. He walked with steady, unhurried steps.

He led the sheep toward the green pasture. They followed behind him, their small hooves pressing into the soft ground. He did not rush them. He waited when they stopped to nibble the fresh grass. The meadow stretched out wide and open, and the sheep had plenty of room. They lay down when they wanted to rest. No one pushed them along.

Then the shepherd guided them toward still waters. The stream here was calm and shallow. It barely moved. The sheep lowered their heads and drank. Water dripped from their chins back into the stream. The shepherd stood beside them, his staff resting against his shoulder. He watched each one drink its fill. The morning sun warmed the wool on their backs.

After they drank, the shepherd began walking again. He chose a path that was easy for the sheep to follow. The ground was smooth and level. No sharp rocks or tangled roots blocked the way. He picked this path carefully, and the sheep walked along it one after another. Their hooves made a quiet rhythm on the packed dirt. He restored their strength by leading them to good food and clean water. He led them in right paths.

The day moved on, and the trail curved through a low valley. Tall hills rose on both sides. The space between them grew narrow, and the light turned soft and golden. Long evening light filtered through the branches overhead. It was a quiet place. The air was still and cool.

The shepherd walked ahead of his flock through this valley. His rod hung from his belt, and his staff tapped gently on the ground. The sheep followed close behind him. He knew this path well. His feet found every turn without stopping. When a sheep slowed down, he waited. When one wandered toward the edge of the trail, he guided it back with the end of his staff. The wood touched its wool gently, and the sheep stepped back onto the path.

Even in this narrow, dim valley, the sheep stayed near the shepherd. His voice carried back to them. He spoke to them in low, calm words. They lifted their ears at the sound. His rod and his staff were right there with him. These were his tools for keeping the flock together and guiding them forward.

The valley opened up again into a wide, bright clearing. A table of flat rock sat in the open air. The shepherd set out food there. He laid down bread and fruit and oil. He poured oil over the head of one sheep that had a scrape on its ear. The oil was smooth and cool. It ran down the side of the sheep's face. The sheep stood still and let him work.

Then the shepherd filled a wide cup with water from a leather pouch. He poured until the water rose right to the rim. It spilled over the edge just a little, wetting the stone below. The cup overflowed. There was more than enough.

Other living things moved through the clearing too. Small brown birds landed near the table and hopped across the stone. A lizard sat on a warm rock nearby, perfectly still. Bees drifted between wildflowers at the edge of the grass. The shepherd did not shoo any of them away. There was room for everyone in this place.

The sheep grazed in the clearing while the shepherd sat on a low boulder. He laid his staff across his knees. The sun moved lower, and the sky turned the color of ripe peaches. A warm breeze carried the smell of wild herbs across the field. The shepherd's robe shifted in the wind. He looked out over his flock and counted each one.

Goodness and mercy followed this flock like steady companions on the road. Wherever the shepherd led, good things came along. Fresh water appeared when they needed it. Green pastures opened up before them. The path stayed smooth under their feet. Day after day, it was the same. The shepherd provided, and the sheep walked on.

As the sky deepened into soft twilight, the shepherd stood and gathered his flock. He called to them, and they came. One by one, they moved toward his voice. He turned down a familiar path that led toward home. The sheep walked behind him in a loose line. Their wool glowed faintly in the last light of the day.

Up ahead, a stone shelter came into view. Its walls were sturdy and its roof was low. A small fire already burned inside, sending a thin line of smoke into the evening air. The shepherd counted each sheep as it passed through the doorway. His hand touched their backs one at a time.

Inside, the shelter was warm and dry. Clean straw covered the floor. The sheep settled down, pressing close to one another. The shepherd set his staff against the wall and sat near the entrance. The fire crackled softly. Outside, the first stars appeared in the wide, open sky.

The shepherd would dwell in this good place. And the sheep would dwell with him. They would stay in the house of the Lord forever.
