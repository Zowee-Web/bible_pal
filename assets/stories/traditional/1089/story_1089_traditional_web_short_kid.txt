Right after Jesus was baptized, the Holy Spirit led him out into the desert. It was a dry, empty place — just rocks and sand and a big, hot sun. There were no trees. No shade. No food anywhere.

Jesus stayed there for forty days and forty nights. He didn't eat anything the whole time. He got very, very hungry.

That's when the devil showed up.

"If you're really God's Son," the devil said, "turn these rocks into bread."

Jesus was so hungry. The rocks even looked like bread. But he said, "The Bible says people don't live on bread alone. They need every word that comes from God."

Then the devil took Jesus to the very top of the temple in Jerusalem. They could see the whole city below.

"If you're really God's Son," the devil said, "jump off! The Bible says angels will catch you."

Jesus said, "The Bible also says you shouldn't test God."

Then the devil took Jesus to a very high mountain. He showed him all the countries in the whole world — every city, every kingdom, everything.

"I'll give you all of this," the devil said, "if you just bow down and worship me."

Jesus looked right at him. "Go away, Satan," he said. "The Bible says you should worship God and only God."

The devil left. Angels came and took care of Jesus.

He had been tested three times. And three times, he said no.
