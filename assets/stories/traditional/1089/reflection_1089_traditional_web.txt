Forty days is a long time to be hungry. A long time to be alone. A long time to sit with nothing but silence and stone and the sound of your own breathing.

The temptations were not absurd. They were reasonable. Bread when you're starving. Safety when you're vulnerable. Power when you have nothing. Each one made a kind of sense — the kind that sounds right when you are exhausted and there is no one around to hear you say yes.

What stands out is not the drama of the offers but the quietness of the refusals. Three times, Jesus answered with scripture. Not with argument. Not with explanation. Just with words he had carried into the wilderness before the test began.

Sometimes the hardest battles are the ones no one sees — fought in silence, in empty places, with nothing but what you brought with you.
