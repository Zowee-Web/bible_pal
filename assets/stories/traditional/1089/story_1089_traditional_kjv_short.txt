After his baptism, the Spirit led Jesus into the wilderness. He went eastward, away from the river, away from the green banks and the multitudes and the sound of water, into the dry hills where nothing grew. The ground was stony and pale. The sun beat down without mercy. The wind moved through the stones bearing dust and heat and silence.

He was alone. Forty days and forty nights he did eat nothing. His body grew thin. His skin darkened beneath the sun. His lips were parched. Hunger pressed upon him as a living thing.

Then the tempter came.

"If thou be the Son of God," he said, "command that these stones be made bread."

Jesus looked upon the stones about him — flat and smooth, baked by the sun, in the very shape and colour of loaves. His body craved food.

But he answered, "It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God."

Then the devil taketh him up into the holy city, and setteth him on a pinnacle of the temple. The city lay spread below — rooftops, courts, the smoke of fires, the people going about their business.

"If thou be the Son of God," the devil said, "cast thyself down: for it is written, He shall give his angels charge concerning thee: and in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone."

Jesus said unto him, "It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God."

Again, the devil taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them — cities, rivers, green valleys, harbours, fields of grain, all of it spread below, shining in the light.

"All these things will I give thee," the devil said, "if thou wilt fall down and worship me."

Jesus turned unto him. His voice was steady and clear.

"Get thee hence, Satan," he said. "For it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve."

Then the devil leaveth him. The wilderness was quiet. The wind moved through the stones. The sun was hot upon the dry ground.

And, behold, angels came and ministered unto him.
