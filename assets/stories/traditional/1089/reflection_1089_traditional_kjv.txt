Forty days is a long time to hunger. A long time to be alone. A long time to sit with naught but silence and stone and the sound of thine own breathing.

The temptations were not foolish things. They were reasonable. Bread when thou art famished. Safety when thou art exposed. Power when thou hast nothing. Each made a manner of sense — the kind that soundeth right when a man is spent and there is no one near to hear him yield.

What standeth out is not the greatness of the offers but the quietness of the refusals. Three times Jesus answered with scripture. Not with argument. Not with reasoning. Only with words he had carried into the wilderness ere the trial began.

Sometimes the hardest battles are those no man witnesseth — fought in silence, in empty places, with nothing but what was brought thither.
