The whole earth was of one language and one speech.

The generations after the Flood had grown out across the country, and they had come together at last on a plain in the land of Shinar, between two great rivers. They settled there. The land was wide. The clay was good. They had everything they needed.

They began to say to one another: come, let us make bricks, and burn them thoroughly. So they had bricks for stone, and they used tar for mortar. The kilns smoked along the river. The walls began to rise.

Then they said: come, let us build ourselves a city, and a tower whose top reaches to the sky. And let us make a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered abroad on the surface of the whole earth.

The word that ran through the camp was that word: name. They had not been given one. They wanted one. They were afraid of going out and being forgotten. They were afraid of the world being too big for them. They had survived the Flood. They were not going to be scattered again. They would build a place to be seen from, and they would stand in it together.

The tower went up. The bricks burned hot. The plain filled with the noise of work.

Yahweh came down to see the city and the tower that the children of men were building. He said: behold, they are one people, and they all have one language. And this is only the beginning of what they will do. Nothing they intend to do will be withheld from them.

Come, let us go down, he said, and confuse their language there, that they may not understand one another's speech.

And it was so.

The men on the wall called down to the men below for more bricks, and the men below did not understand the words. The men with the mortar shouted at the men with the buckets, and the words made no sense in their ears. The plans on the table could not be read by the second man at the table. By the end of the day, no one could give an order anyone else could carry out.

The work stopped. The kilns went cold. The tower stood where it was. And little by little the camp broke up into smaller camps, and the smaller camps moved off in different directions, and each one carried its own speech with it.

So Yahweh scattered them abroad from there over the face of all the earth. They stopped building the city. Therefore its name was called Babel, because there Yahweh confused the language of all the earth. From there, Yahweh scattered them abroad over the face of all the earth. And so it was in the days of the children of men on the plain of Shinar, when they tried to build a name into the sky, and the building of it was the thing that broke the gathering.
