And the whole earth was of one language, and of one speech.

The generations after the flood had grown out across the country, and they had come together at last upon a plain in the land of Shinar, between two great rivers. They dwelt there. The land was wide. The clay was good. They had everything they needed.

And they said one to another: go to, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter. The kilns smoked along the river. The walls began to rise.

Then they said: go to, let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven. And let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.

The word that ran through the camp was that word: name. They had not been given one. They desired one. They were afraid of going out and being forgotten. They were afraid of the world being too great for them. They had survived the flood. They were not going to be scattered again. They should build a place to be seen from, and they should stand in it together.

The tower went up. The bricks burned hot. The plain filled with the noise of work.

And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men builded. And the Lord said: behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do.

Go to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another's speech.

And it was so.

The men on the wall called down for more bricks, and the men below understood not the words. The men with the morter shouted at the men with the buckets, and the words made no sense in their ears. The plans upon the table could not be read by the second man at the table. By the end of the day, no man could give an order any other man could carry out.

The work ceased. The kilns went cold. The tower stood where it was. And little by little the camp broke up into smaller camps, and the smaller camps moved off in different directions, and each one bare its own speech with it.

So the Lord scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city. Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the Lord did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the Lord scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth. And so it was in the days of the children of men on the plain of Shinar, when they sought to build a name into the heavens, and the building of it was the thing that brake the gathering.
