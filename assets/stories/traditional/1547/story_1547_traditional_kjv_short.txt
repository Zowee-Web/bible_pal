Tidings were brought unto Jehoshaphat. The children of Moab and the children of Ammon, and others with them besides, had gathered themselves together and were come up against Judah. A host exceeding great, already passed beyond the sea, already encamped at Hazazon-tamar — which is En-gedi.

And Jehoshaphat was sore afraid.

He set his face to seek the LORD, and he proclaimed a fast throughout all Judah. And out of every city they came — men and women and little ones — gathering themselves unto Jerusalem, that they might enquire of the LORD.

And Jehoshaphat stood in the house of the LORD, before the new court, and all the congregation of Judah and Jerusalem were round about him. And he lifted up his voice.

"O LORD God of our fathers, art thou not God alone in the heavens? Dost thou not bear rule over every kingdom among the nations? Power and might are in thine hand, and there is none that may stand against thee. Art thou not our God, who didst drive out the dwellers of this land before thy people Israel, and gavest it unto the seed of Abraham thy friend for ever? And they have dwelt therein. They have raised up a sanctuary unto thy name in the midst of it, saying: When calamity shall come upon us — whether the sword, or judgement, or pestilence, or famine — we shall stand before this house and before thee, and shall cry unto thee in our distress, and thou wilt hear, and thou wilt deliver."

His voice rang through the courts of stone.

"And now, behold. The children of Ammon and Moab and of Mount Seir — those whom thou didst forbid Israel to smite when they came forth out of Egypt, so that they turned aside and destroyed them not — see now how they reward us, for they are come to thrust us out of thine inheritance, which thou hast given us to possess. O our God, wilt thou not execute judgement upon them? For we have no strength before this great company that cometh against us. Neither know we what we ought to do. But our eyes are upon thee."

And all Judah stood before the LORD — their little ones with them, and their wives, and their children.

Then came the Spirit of the LORD upon Jahaziel the son of Zechariah, there in the midst of the assembly. And he spake.

"Hearken, all ye of Judah, and ye that dwell in Jerusalem, and thou, King Jehoshaphat. Thus saith the LORD unto you: Fear not, neither be ye dismayed by reason of this great multitude, for the battle belongeth not unto you, but unto God. On the morrow go ye down against them. They shall come up by the ascent of Ziz, and ye shall find them at the end of the valley, over against the wilderness of Jeruel. Ye shall not need to strike a blow in this battle. Take your place. Be still. And behold the deliverance which the LORD shall work for you, O Judah and Jerusalem. Fear not. Be not dismayed. Go forth on the morrow to meet them, for the LORD goeth with you."

And Jehoshaphat bowed himself, and set his face upon the ground. And all Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell down before the LORD and worshipped him. Then rose up the Levites — of the children of the Kohathites and of the children of the Korhites — and they praised the LORD God of Israel with a great voice, lifting it on high.
