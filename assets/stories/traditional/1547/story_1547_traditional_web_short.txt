Word came to Jehoshaphat. The children of Moab, the children of Ammon, and others beside them had joined together and were marching toward Judah. A great multitude, already past the sea, already at Hazazon-tamar — the place called En-gedi.

Jehoshaphat was afraid.

He set himself to seek the LORD. He proclaimed a fast throughout all of Judah. And from every city they came — men, women, children — gathering at Jerusalem to ask help of the LORD.

Jehoshaphat stood in the house of the LORD, before the new court, with all the congregation of Judah and Jerusalem around him. He lifted his voice.

"O LORD God of our fathers, are you not God in heaven? Do you not rule over all the kingdoms of the nations? In your hand is power and might, so that none is able to withstand you. Are you not our God, who drove out the inhabitants of this land before your people Israel and gave it to the descendants of Abraham your friend forever? They have lived in it. They built you a sanctuary in it for your name, saying: If evil comes upon us — the sword, judgment, pestilence, or famine — we will stand before this house and before you and cry out in our affliction, and you will hear and help."

His voice carried across the stone court.

"And now — look. The children of Ammon and Moab and Mount Seir, whom you would not let Israel invade when they came out of the land of Egypt — they turned away from them and did not destroy them. And see how they repay us, coming to cast us out of your possession, which you gave us to inherit. O our God, will you not judge them? For we have no might against this great company that comes against us. We do not know what to do. But our eyes are on you."

All Judah stood before the LORD. Their little ones stood with them. Their wives. Their children.

Then the Spirit of the LORD came upon Jahaziel the son of Zechariah, there in the middle of the congregation. He spoke.

"Listen, all Judah, and you inhabitants of Jerusalem, and you, King Jehoshaphat. This is what the LORD says to you: Do not be afraid or dismayed because of this great multitude. The battle is not yours, but God's. Tomorrow go down against them. They come up by the cliff of Ziz, and you will find them at the end of the brook, before the wilderness of Jeruel. You will not need to fight in this battle. Set yourselves. Stand still. And see the salvation of the LORD with you, O Judah and Jerusalem. Do not fear. Do not be dismayed. Tomorrow go out against them, for the LORD will be with you."

Jehoshaphat bowed his head, his face to the ground. All Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell before the LORD and worshipped. Then the Levites — the children of the Kohathites and the children of the Korhites — stood up and praised the LORD God of Israel with a loud voice on high.
