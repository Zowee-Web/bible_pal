After these things, God did tempt Abraham. "Abraham!" God called.

"Behold, here I am," Abraham said.

God said, "Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac, whom thou lovest, and get thee into the land of Moriah. Offer him there for a burnt offering upon one of the mountains which I will tell thee of."

Abraham lay awake that night. Isaac was his boy — the son for whom he had waited so many years. But Abraham trusted God. He rose up early on the morrow before the sun was up. He clave the wood for the burnt offering and laded it upon an ass. He took Isaac and two of his young men, and they began to journey.

The way took three days. Three long days of dusty roads and sleeping under the stars. On the third day, Abraham lifted up his eyes and saw the place afar off.

"Abide ye here with the ass," Abraham said unto the young men. "I and the lad will go yonder and worship, and come again to you."

Abraham took the wood and laid it upon Isaac's back. He bore the fire and the knife himself. And they went, both of them together, up the mountain side by side.

Isaac looked upon his father. "My father?" he said.

"Here am I, my son."

"Behold the fire and the wood," Isaac said. "But where is the lamb for a burnt offering?"

Abraham swallowed hard. "My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering."

So they went both of them together. They came to the place which God had told him of. Abraham built an altar of stones and laid the wood in order. He bound Isaac his son, and laid him on the altar upon the wood.

Abraham stretched forth his hand and took the knife.

"Abraham, Abraham!" the angel of the Lord called out of heaven.

"Here am I," Abraham said. His hand stayed.

"Lay not thine hand upon the lad," the angel said. "Neither do thou any thing unto him. For now I know that thou fearest God, seeing thou hast not withheld thy son, thine only son, from me."

Abraham lifted up his eyes. His whole body shook with relief. And behold, behind him was a ram caught in a thicket by his horns. It had not been there before. The Lord had set it there.

Abraham went and took the ram, and offered him up for a burnt offering in the stead of his son. The smoke rose up into the blue sky.

Abraham called the name of that place "The Lord Will Provide." And in the mount of the Lord it shall be seen.
