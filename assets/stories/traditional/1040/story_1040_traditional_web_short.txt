After these things, God tested Abraham. "Abraham!" God called.

"Here I am," Abraham said.

God said, "Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love, and go to the land of Moriah. Offer him there as a burnt offering on one of the mountains I will tell you about."

Abraham lay awake that night. Isaac was his boy — the son he had waited so many years for. But Abraham trusted God. He got up early the next morning before the sun came up. He cut wood for the offering and loaded it on a donkey. He took Isaac and two servants, and they started walking.

The journey took three days. Three long days of dusty roads and sleeping under the stars. On the third day, Abraham looked up and saw the mountain in the distance.

"Stay here with the donkey," Abraham told the servants. "The boy and I will go up there and worship, and we will come back to you."

Abraham took the wood and put it on Isaac's back. He carried the fire and the knife himself. The two of them walked up the mountain together, side by side.

Isaac looked at his father. "Father?" he said.

"Here I am, my son."

"I see the fire and the wood," Isaac said. "But where is the lamb for the burnt offering?"

Abraham swallowed hard. "God will provide himself the lamb for a burnt offering, my son."

They kept walking. They reached the place God had told him about. Abraham built an altar out of stones and laid the wood on it. He bound Isaac and laid him on the altar, on top of the wood.

Abraham reached out his hand and took the knife.

"Abraham! Abraham!" the angel of the Lord called from heaven.

"Here I am," Abraham said. His hand stopped.

"Do not lay your hand on the boy," the angel said. "Do not do anything to him. Now I know that you fear God, since you have not withheld your son, your only son, from me."

Abraham looked up. His whole body shook with relief. There in the bushes behind him was a ram, caught by its curly horns. It had not been there before. God had put it there.

Abraham went and took the ram and offered it as a burnt offering instead of his son. The smoke rose up into the blue sky.

Abraham called that place "The Lord Will Provide." And on the mountain of the Lord, it will be provided.
