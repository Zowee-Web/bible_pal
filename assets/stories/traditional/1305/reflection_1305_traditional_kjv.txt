Moses stood on the plains of Moab before the crossing and told Israel of the good land: brooks of water, fountains, valleys and hills, wheat and barley, vines and fig trees and pomegranates, oil olive and honey. Thou shalt eat and be full, and bless the Lord thy God for the good land. What is it to be promised a land with the names of its fruits already written down, before thou hast yet crossed the river to taste them?
