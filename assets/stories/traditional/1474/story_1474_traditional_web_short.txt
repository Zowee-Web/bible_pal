This is one of the songs of the steps — one of the songs they sang as they came up to Jerusalem for the feasts.

It is a short song.

The pilgrims sang it as they climbed the road to the city, when the brothers and sisters were walking together, and the families had joined on the way, and the houses had been left empty behind them.

The song was this —

"See how good, and how pleasant it is, for brothers to live together in unity.

It is like the precious oil on the head, that ran down on the beard — even Aaron's beard, that came down on the edge of his robes.

It is like the dew of Hermon, that comes down on the hills of Zion.

For there Yahweh gives the blessing — even life forever more."

The oil they sang of had been poured out on Aaron the high priest at the door of the tent of meeting in the days of Moses. It had been the sign of his being set apart.

The dew they sang of came down on Mount Hermon in the north of the land each morning, and it came down on the hills around Jerusalem.

The blessing came down upon the people the way the oil had come down, and the way the dew came down.

The pilgrims came up to Zion singing it, and when they came to the gates of Yahweh's city, they were no longer climbing alone.
