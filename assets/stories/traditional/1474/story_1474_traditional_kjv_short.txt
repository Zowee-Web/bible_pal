This is one of the songs of the steps — one of the songs which they sang as they came up unto Jerusalem for the feasts.

It is a short song.

The pilgrims sang it as they climbed the road unto the city, when the brethren and the sisters were walking together, and the families had joined upon the way, and the houses had been left empty behind them.

The song was this —

"Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!

It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron's beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments.

It is as the dew of Hermon, and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Zion.

For there the Lord commanded the blessing, even life for evermore."

The oil whereof they sang had been poured forth upon Aaron the high priest at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation in the days of Moses. It had been the sign of his being set apart.

The dew whereof they sang came down upon mount Hermon in the north of the land each morning, and it came down upon the hills round about Jerusalem.

The blessing came down upon the people in the manner the oil had come down, and in the manner the dew came down.

The pilgrims came up unto Zion singing it, and when they came unto the gates of the Lord's city, they were no longer climbing alone.
