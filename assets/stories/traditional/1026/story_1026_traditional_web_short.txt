Jacob left Beersheba and headed toward a place called Haran. The road was long and lonely. He walked until the sun began to set, painting the sky orange and pink. When it grew dark, he stopped to rest for the night.

He found a stone and placed it under his head as a pillow. The ground was hard beneath him. Stars appeared one by one overhead, filling the sky. Jacob closed his eyes and fell asleep.

Then he had a dream. In the dream, a great ladder stood on the earth. The top of it reached all the way up to heaven. Angels were going up and down on the ladder, their robes shining softly. It was the most amazing thing Jacob had ever seen.

And there at the very top stood the Lord. God spoke to Jacob. "I am the Lord, the God of Abraham your grandfather, and the God of Isaac your father. The land you are lying on — I will give it to you and to your children."

God promised that Jacob's family would spread out in every direction, like dust spreading across the earth. And through his family, all the families of the earth would be blessed. Then God said something especially kind. "I am with you. I will keep you wherever you go. I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you."

Jacob woke up. The night was quiet. Stars still burned above him. He sat up and whispered, "Surely the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it." His heart beat fast. "How awesome is this place! This is the gate of heaven."

Early in the morning, Jacob took the stone he had used as a pillow and stood it straight up like a pillar. He poured oil over the top of it. Then he named the place Bethel, which means "house of God."

Jacob made a promise. "If God will be with me and keep me safe on this journey and bring me back to my father's house in peace, then the Lord will be my God. And this stone will be God's house."
