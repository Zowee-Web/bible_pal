Mountains stand tall and strong, even when clouds cover them up. Sometimes when things feel great or a little hard, it helpeth to lift up thine eyes and remember that the Lord watcheth over all things. He never slumbereth — no, not for one tiny second. That meaneth when thou art playing without in the sunshine or tucked under thy blanket at night, he is right there. He is as a cool shadow upon a very hot day, keeping thee safe and close. Thy going out in the morning and thy coming in at evening, his care wrappeth about every step thou takest.
