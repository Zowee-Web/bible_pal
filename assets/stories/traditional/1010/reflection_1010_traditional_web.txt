Mountains stand tall and strong, even when clouds cover them up. Sometimes when things feel big or a little tricky, it helps to look up and remember that God is watching over everything. He never falls asleep — not even for one tiny second. That means when you are playing outside in the sunshine or tucked under your blanket at night, God is right there. He is like a cool shade on a really hot day, keeping you safe and close. Going out the door in the morning and coming back home in the evening, his care wraps around every step you take.
