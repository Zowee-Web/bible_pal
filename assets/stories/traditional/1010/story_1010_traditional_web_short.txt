A traveler stood on a dusty road at the bottom of a wide valley. Dry grass bent in a soft breeze. Small white wildflowers dotted the path ahead. The road curved upward toward a long row of green hills. Birds called to each other from the branches of an old fig tree nearby.

The traveler lifted a hand to shade tired eyes and looked up at those hills. The hills were tall and round, covered in low bushes and smooth stones. A walking staff rested against one shoulder. Sandals pressed into the warm dirt. The traveler spoke out loud, voice calm and clear. "Where does my help come from?" The words floated up toward the wide blue sky. Then the traveler answered, still looking at the hills. "My help comes from the Lord, who made heaven and earth."

One foot stepped forward, then another. The road began to climb. Loose pebbles rolled underfoot. The traveler's staff tapped the ground with each steady step. A warm wind carried the smell of wild herbs growing between the rocks. The traveler spoke again while walking. "He will not let my foot slip." Each step landed firm and sure on the rising path. "The one who watches over me will not fall asleep."

Higher and higher the road went. The sun moved across the sky. Heat pressed down, but a cloud drifted over and gave cool shade for a while. The traveler wiped dust from both hands and kept walking. "The sun will not harm me by day," the traveler said. A lizard sat on a flat rock, blinking in the light. "The moon will not harm me by night." The words came out like a song, easy and unhurried.

The path leveled out near the top of the hills. Olive trees grew in rows along the ridge. Their silver-green leaves rustled and flickered. A shepherd sat under one of the trees, watching a small flock of sheep nibble grass. The shepherd lifted a hand in greeting. The traveler waved back. From up here, the whole valley spread out below, golden and green. A stream sparkled far down in the distance.

The traveler stopped under an olive tree and set the walking staff against the trunk. Cool shade covered the ground. A gentle breeze moved through the branches overhead. The traveler sat down on the soft grass and stretched both legs out. "The Lord watches over my coming and going," the traveler said quietly, "from this time on and forever."

Sheep bells clinked nearby. A bird landed on a low branch and sang three bright notes. The afternoon light turned everything warm and golden. The traveler rested there on the hilltop, safe and warm, with the whole beautiful land stretching out below.
