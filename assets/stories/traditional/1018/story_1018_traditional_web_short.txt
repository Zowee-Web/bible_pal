Jacob went out from Beersheba and went toward Haran. The road stretched north across dry hills, and the sun moved westward as he walked.

He came to a certain place and stayed there all night because the sun had set. He took one of the stones of the place and put it under his head, and lay down in that place to sleep.

The ground was hard. The stone pressed against the side of his skull. The air cooled as darkness settled over the open land. He was alone.

He dreamed. In the dream, a ladder was set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven. The angels of God were ascending and descending on it. They moved up and down in steady procession, their forms bright against the dark sky.

The Lord stood above it. He spoke.

"I am the Lord, the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac. The land on which you lie, I will give to you and to your offspring. Your offspring will be like the dust of the earth. You will spread abroad to the west, to the east, to the north, and to the south. In you and in your offspring all the families of the earth will be blessed."

Then God said, "I am with you. I will keep you wherever you go. I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you."

Jacob awoke out of his sleep. He said, "Surely the Lord is in this place, and I did not know it." He was afraid. He said, "How awesome is this place! This is none other than the house of God. This is the gate of heaven."

He rose early in the morning. He took the stone he had placed under his head and set it up as a pillar. He poured oil on the top of it. He called the name of that place Bethel.

Then Jacob made a vow. "If God will be with me and will keep me in this way that I go, and will give me bread to eat and clothing to wear, so that I come again to my father's house in peace, then the Lord will be my God. This stone which I have set up as a pillar will be God's house. Of all that you give me, I will surely give a tenth to you."
