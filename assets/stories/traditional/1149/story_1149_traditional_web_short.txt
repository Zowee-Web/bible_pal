In those days, two women came to King Solomon and stood before him in the hall of judgment. The first woman said to the king, "Oh, my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house. I gave birth to a child while she was in the house. It happened on the third day after I gave birth, that this woman also gave birth to a child."

She said, "We were together. There was no stranger with us in the house, only the two of us. This woman's child died in the night, because she lay on it. She arose at midnight and took my son from beside me, while your handmaid slept, and laid it in her bosom. She laid her dead child in my bosom."

She said, "When I rose in the morning to nurse my child, behold, it was dead. But when I looked at it carefully in the morning, behold, it was not my son whom I had borne."

The other woman said, "No, but the living one is my son, and the dead one is your son." But the first woman said, "No, but the dead one is your son, and the living one is my son." So they spoke before the king.

Then the king said, "The one says, 'This is my son who lives, and your son is dead.' The other says, 'No, your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.'" The king said, "Bring me a sword." So they brought a sword before the king.

The king said, "Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one and half to the other."

Then the woman whose son was alive spoke to the king, for her heart yearned over her son. She said, "Oh, my lord, give her the living child, and in no way kill it." But the other said, "It shall be neither mine nor yours. Divide it."

Then the king answered and said, "Give the first woman the living child, and in no way kill it. She is its mother."

When all Israel heard of the judgment which the king had judged, they feared the king. They saw that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.
