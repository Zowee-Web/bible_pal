Yahweh told Moses to go up into the mountain of Abarim and look upon the land given to the children of Israel. After Moses had seen it, he would be gathered to his people as Aaron had been. Yahweh recalled the strife of the congregation in the wilderness of Zin, where Moses and Aaron had rebelled against his word instead of honoring him as holy at the waters before Israel. These were the waters of Meribah at Kadesh.

Moses answered by asking Yahweh, the God of the spirits of all flesh, to appoint a man over the congregation. He asked for someone who could go out before the people and come in before them, leading them out and bringing them in. Moses did not want Yahweh’s congregation to remain like sheep without a shepherd.

Yahweh named Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom was the Spirit. Moses was to take Joshua and lay his hand on him. He was to set Joshua before Eleazar the priest and before the whole congregation of Israel, then commission him in their sight. Moses would give Joshua authority so that the children of Israel would obey him.

Joshua would also stand before Eleazar. The priest would inquire for him by the judgment of the Urim before Yahweh. At the word given there, Joshua and the congregation would go out, and at that word they would come in.

Moses did exactly as Yahweh commanded. He took Joshua and placed him before Eleazar and all the congregation. In the sight of the people who would follow Joshua, Moses laid his hands on him and commissioned him. The man who had led Israel now openly prepared another servant to guide the congregation after him.

All Israel witnessed the transfer of authority from Moses to Joshua.
