The Lord told Moses to get up into mount Abarim and behold the land he had given unto the children of Israel. After Moses had seen it, he would be gathered unto his people as Aaron his brother had been. The Lord recalled the strife of the congregation in the desert of Zin, where Moses and Aaron had rebelled against his commandment instead of sanctifying him at the water before Israel. This was the water of Meribah in Kadesh.

Moses spake unto the Lord and asked the God of the spirits of all flesh to set a man over the congregation. He sought one who might go out before them and go in before them, leading them out and bringing them in. He asked that the congregation of the Lord not be as sheep which have no shepherd.

The Lord named Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom was the spirit. Moses was commanded to take him and lay his hand upon him. He was to set Joshua before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation, then give him a charge in their sight. Moses would put some of his honour upon Joshua so that the children of Israel might be obedient.

Joshua would stand before Eleazar, who would ask counsel for him after the judgment of Urim before the Lord. At his word they would go out, and at his word they would come in, both Joshua and all the congregation with him.

Moses did as the Lord commanded him. He took Joshua and set him before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation. There Moses laid his hands upon Joshua and gave him a charge, as the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses. The congregation saw the servant appointed to lead them onward.
