When the days of Jacob drew near to die, he called for his sons, and gathered them around his bed. He said to them, "Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which will happen to you in the days to come. Assemble yourselves, and hear, you sons of Jacob. Listen to Israel your father."

The sons of Jacob came one by one to his bedside, and he spoke a word over each.

To Reuben he said, "Reuben, you are my firstborn, my might, and the beginning of my strength, excelling in dignity, and excelling in power. Boiling over as water, you shall not excel."

To Judah he said, "Judah, your brothers shall praise you. Your hand shall be on the neck of your enemies. Your father's sons shall bow down before you. Judah is a lion's whelp. The scepter shall not depart from Judah, nor the ruler's staff from between his feet, until he comes to whom it belongs. To him shall the obedience of the peoples be."

To Joseph he said, "Joseph is a fruitful vine, a fruitful vine by a spring. His branches run over the wall. The blessings of your father have prevailed above the blessings of the eternal mountains. They shall be on the head of Joseph, on the crown of the head of him who is separated from his brothers."

To Benjamin he said, "Benjamin is a ravenous wolf. In the morning he shall devour the prey. At evening he shall divide the plunder."

When Jacob had finished blessing his sons, he gathered up his feet into the bed, and breathed his last. He was gathered to his people.

Joseph fell on his father's face, and wept on him, and kissed him. He commanded the physicians who were with him to embalm his father.

The morning came over the house, and the sons of Israel stood around the bed where their father lay. They mourned for him many days. The household was quiet, and the words their father had spoken remained among them, sons by son, blessing by blessing.
