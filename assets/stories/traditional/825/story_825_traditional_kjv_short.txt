Elijah came thither unto a cave. He lodged there, alone and restless. As the darkness pressed in, the word of the Lord came unto him.

"What doest thou here, Elijah?" the Lord said.

Elijah answered quickly, his voice trembling, "I have been very jealous for the Lord God of hosts: for the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, thrown down thine altars, and slain thy prophets with the sword; and I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away."

The Lord answered, "Go forth, and stand upon the mount before the Lord."

Elijah arose and stepped forth. The air of the mount was tense and still. And, behold, the Lord passed by. A great and strong wind rent the mountains, and brake in pieces the rocks before the Lord. But the Lord was not in the wind.

And after the wind an earthquake. Stones shifted. The ground rumbled beneath Elijah's feet. But the Lord was not in the earthquake.

When the shaking ceased, fire came. Bright flames licked across the mount, scorching the darkness. Yet the Lord was not in the fire.

And after the fire, a stillness settled — a still small voice.

When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his mantle. He went out and stood at the entering in of the cave. Again he heard a voice, quiet but clear: "What doest thou here, Elijah?"

Elijah answered as before, sorrow pressing in. "I have been very jealous for the Lord God of hosts: for the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, thrown down thine altars, and slain thy prophets with the sword; and I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away."

The Lord spake unto him, patient and resolute. "Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus. And when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria. And Jehu the son of Nimshi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel. And Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abel-meholah shalt thou anoint to be prophet in thy room."

He continued, "And it shall come to pass, that him that escapeth the sword of Hazael shall Jehu slay; and him that escapeth from the sword of Jehu shall Elisha slay. Yet I have left me seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him."

Elijah hearkened in the anxious quiet and made ready to obey all that the Lord had spoken.
