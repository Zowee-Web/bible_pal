The Lord said, “Go out, and stand on the mountain before me.”

Behold, the Lord passed by.

A great strong wind tore the mountains and broke in pieces the rocks before the Lord. But the Lord was not in the wind.

After the wind, an earthquake. But the Lord was not in the earthquake.

After the earthquake, a fire. But the Lord was not in the fire.

And after the fire — a still, small voice.

When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his mantle. He went out, and stood in the entrance of the cave.

And behold, a voice came to him, and said:

“What are you doing here, Elijah?”
