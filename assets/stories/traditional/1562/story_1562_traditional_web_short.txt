In the beginning was the Word. And the Word was with God. And the Word was God. He was there in the very beginning, with God. Through him everything came to be; without him, nothing came to be at all. In him was life, and that life was the light of men.

The light shines in the darkness. And the darkness has not overcome it.

There was a man sent from God, whose name was John. He came to point to the light, so that through him others might believe. He was not the light himself. He came only to point to it.

The true light, the light that gives light to everyone, was coming into the world. He was in the world — the world that was made through him — and the world did not know him. He came to what was his own, and his own did not receive him. But to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become the children of God — born not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.

And then the Word became flesh.

He lived among us.

And we saw his glory. The glory of the only Son, come from the Father, full of grace and truth. And John bore him witness, and cried aloud: “This is the one I spoke of! He who comes after me is greater than I, for he was before me.”

Out of his fullness we have all received one grace after another. The law came through Moses. Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ. No one has ever seen God. But the only Son, who is closest to the Father’s heart, has made him known.
