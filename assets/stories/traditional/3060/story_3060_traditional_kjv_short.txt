When Abijah slept with his fathers, Asa his son reigned in his stead, and the land was quiet ten years. Asa did that which was good and right in the eyes of the Lord his God. He took away strange altars and high places, brake down the images, cut down the groves, and commanded Judah to seek the Lord and to do the law. In the years of rest he built fenced cities, making walls, towers, gates, and bars.

Then Zerah the Ethiopian came unto Mareshah with an host of a thousand thousand and three hundred chariots. Asa brought forth Judah and Benjamin, and the battle was set in array in the valley of Zephathah. Asa cried unto the Lord: “It is nothing with thee to help, whether with many, or with them that have no power. Help us, O Lord our God; for we rest on thee.”

The Lord smote the Ethiopians before Asa and before Judah. They fled, and Asa’s company pursued them unto Gerar. The host was overthrown and could not recover itself. Judah carried away very much spoil, smote the cities round about Gerar, took sheep and camels from the tents of cattle, and returned to Jerusalem.

The Spirit of God came upon Azariah the son of Oded. He went forth to meet Asa and declared that the Lord was with Judah and Benjamin while they were with him. If they sought him, he would be found of them; if they forsook him, he would forsake them. “Be ye strong therefore,” said Azariah, “and let not your hands be weak: for your work shall be rewarded.”

Asa took courage. He put away the abominable idols from Judah, Benjamin, and the cities taken from mount Ephraim, and renewed the altar before the porch of the Lord. Many out of Israel joined the gathering at Jerusalem. They offered of the spoil and entered into covenant to seek the Lord with all their heart and soul. Whosoever would not seek him was to be put to death, whether small or great, man or woman. With a loud voice, shouting, trumpets, and cornets, they sware unto him. All Judah rejoiced at the oath, and the Lord gave them rest round about.

Asa removed Maachah from being queen because she had made an idol in a grove. He cut down her idol, stamped it, and burnt it at the brook Kidron. Though the high places were not taken away out of Israel, Asa brought dedicated silver, gold, and vessels into the house of God. There was no more war unto the five and thirtieth year of his reign.
