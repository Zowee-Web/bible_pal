Six times God looked upon that which he had made, and six times the text declareth it good. The seventh time — after the man and the woman, after all things — the word changeth. Very good. Not merely ordered or fitting, but delighted in.

There is something striking in a Creator who pauseth to look. The making itself is swift — God speaketh and it is so. But the seeing is deliberate. Light existeth, and then God seeth the light. The act of creation and the act of beholding are separate moments.

Joy dwelleth in that space between. Between the making and the noticing. Between the labour of a day and the moment one stoppeth to behold what hath come of it.
