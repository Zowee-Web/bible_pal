In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. The earth was formless and empty. Darkness covered the surface of the deep. The Spirit of God moved over the waters.

God said, "Let there be light."

Light broke across the void. God saw that it was good. He divided the light from the darkness. He called the light Day and the darkness Night. Evening came, and morning. The first day.

God said, "Let there be an expanse in the middle of the waters." He made the expanse and separated the waters below from the waters above. He called the expanse Sky. Evening came, and morning. The second day.

God said, "Let the waters under the sky be gathered to one place, and let the dry land appear." It was so. The waters drew back and the land rose, dark and bare. He called the dry land Earth and the gathered waters Seas. God saw that it was good.

He said, "Let the earth produce vegetation." The ground split open. Grasses pushed up through the soil. Herbs rose bearing seed. Fruit trees climbed with figs and olives on their limbs. The air smelled fresh and new. God saw that it was good. Evening and morning. The third day.

God said, "Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky." He made two great lights — the greater to rule the day and the lesser to rule the night. He made the stars also and set them in the expanse. God saw that it was good. Evening and morning. The fourth day.

God said, "Let the waters swarm with living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth." He created great sea creatures and every living thing that moves in the waters, and every winged bird after its kind. The oceans churned with life. Wings filled the air. God blessed them. "Be fruitful, and multiply." Evening and morning. The fifth day.

God said, "Let the earth produce living creatures after their kind." It was so. Cattle, creeping things, and beasts of the earth — each according to its kind. God saw that it was good.

Then God said, "Let's make man in our image, after our likeness." God created man in his own image. Male and female he created them. He blessed them. "Be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth."

God said, "I have given you every seed-bearing plant and every tree with fruit. They will be your food."

God saw everything that he had made. It was very good.

Evening came, and morning. The sixth day.
