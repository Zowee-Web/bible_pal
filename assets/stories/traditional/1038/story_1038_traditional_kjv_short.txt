In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. The earth was without form, and void. Darkness was upon the face of the deep, and the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.

God said, "Let there be light."

There was light. It broke across the void. God saw the light, that it was good. He divided the light from the darkness. He called the light Day, and the darkness Night. The evening and the morning were the first day.

God said, "Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters." He made the firmament and divided the waters below from the waters above. He called the firmament Heaven. The evening and the morning were the second day.

God said, "Let the waters be gathered unto one place, and let the dry land appear." It was so. The waters drew back and the land rose. He called the dry land Earth, and the waters Seas. God saw that it was good.

God said, "Let the earth bring forth grass, the herb yielding seed, and the fruit tree yielding fruit." The ground split open. Green rose — grass spreading across bare soil, trees climbing with fruit upon their limbs. God saw that it was good. The evening and the morning were the third day.

God said, "Let there be lights in the firmament of heaven." He made two great lights — the greater to rule the day and the lesser to rule the night. He made the stars also. God saw that it was good. The evening and the morning were the fourth day.

God said, "Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl that may fly above the earth." He created great whales and every living creature that moveth in the waters, and every winged fowl. God saw that it was good. He blessed them. "Be fruitful, and multiply." The evening and the morning were the fifth day.

God said, "Let the earth bring forth the living creature after his kind." It was so. Cattle and creeping things and beasts of the earth — each after his kind. God saw that it was good.

God said, "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness." He created man in his own image. Male and female created he them. He blessed them. "Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth."

God said, "I have given you every herb bearing seed and every tree yielding fruit. To you it shall be for meat."

God saw every thing that he had made. It was very good.

The evening and the morning were the sixth day.
