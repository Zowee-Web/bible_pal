And Jesus came unto a city of Samaria which is called Sychar, nigh unto the parcel of ground that Jacob gave unto his son Joseph. And Jacob's well was there. Jesus therefore, being wearied with his journeying, sat himself down beside the well. And it was about the sixth hour.

There cometh a woman of Samaria to draw water. And Jesus saith unto her, "Give me to drink." For his disciples were gone away into the city to buy provisions.

Then the woman looked upon him. And she said, "How is it that thou, being a Jew, dost ask drink of me, who am a woman of Samaria?" For the Jews kept no company with the Samaritans.

Jesus answered and said unto her, "If thou knewest the gift that God offereth, and who it is that saith unto thee, Give me to drink, thou wouldest have asked of him. And he would have given thee water that is living."

The woman said unto him, "Sir, thou hast nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. From whence then hast thou this living water? Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who gave us this well?"

The sun stood high above them. The stone rim of the well was warm beneath the heat of the day. The rope and the vessel remained yet in the woman's hands.

Jesus said unto her, "Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst yet again. But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him, it shall not be so. For the water that I shall give him shall become in him a fountain, rising up unto life everlasting."

The woman saith unto him, "Sir, give me this water, that I thirst not, neither come hither any more to draw."

Jesus saith unto her, "Go, call thy husband, and return hither."

She answered and said, "I have no husband."

Jesus saith unto her, "Thou hast spoken rightly, saying, I have no husband. For thou hast had five husbands, and he whom thou hast now is not thy husband. In this thou hast spoken what is true."

The woman set down her vessel upon the stone edge of the well. She said unto him, "Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet. Our fathers worshipped upon this mountain, yet ye say that Jerusalem is the place where men ought to worship."

Jesus saith unto her, "Woman, believe me, the hour draweth nigh when ye shall worship the Father neither upon this mountain, nor yet at Jerusalem. Ye worship that which ye know not. We worship that which we know, for deliverance cometh forth out of the house of the Jews." He paused. The wind moved across the stones. "But the hour cometh," he said, "and even now is come, when those who truly worship shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth. For the Father seeketh such as these to come before him. God is Spirit, and they that worship him must worship in spirit and in truth."

The woman saith unto him, "I know that the Messiah cometh, he that is called the Anointed One. When he is come, he shall declare unto us all things."

And Jesus looked upon her. The well stood between them, and the water lay far beneath in the dark shaft of stone. He saith unto her, "I that speak unto thee am he."
