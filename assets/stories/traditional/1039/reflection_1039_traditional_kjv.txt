The widow received not a storehouse. She received an handful of meal each morning. The barrel never filled to the brim. The cruse never overflowed. There was only enough for the day, and then the day ended, and she must needs reach into the barrel again the next morning, not knowing what her hand would find.

Provision that cometh one day at a time asketh something particular of the one who receiveth it. It asketh the willingness to lay down to sleep without certainty of the morrow. The widow had already come to the end of her substance when Elijah arrived. She was gathering two sticks. She had a purpose: to eat, and to die.

What changed was not the measure of her supply. It was that the supply ceased not. Weariness and provision dwelt in the same house, at the same table. The drought ended not. The heaven remained empty. But the barrel had meal in the morning, and that sufficed to make bread.
