When the high priest asked Stephen if these accusations were true, Stephen stood before the council and lifted up his voice. He said, "Men, brethren, and fathers, hearken. The God of glory appeared unto our father Abraham, when he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Charran. He said unto him, 'Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and come into the land which I shall shew thee.'"

Then he told them how Abraham came into the land, and how God gave the covenant of circumcision. He told how the patriarchs were moved with envy against Joseph, and sold him into Egypt, but God was with him.

He said, "When the time of the promise drew nigh, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt, till another king arose, which knew not Joseph. The same dealt subtilly with our kindred, and evil entreated our fathers."

Stephen told them how Moses was born, and how he fled into Midian, and how the Lord appeared unto him in the bush. He told how Moses brought the people out of Egypt, and how they made a calf in those days, and offered sacrifice unto the idol.

He said, "Our fathers had the tabernacle of witness in the wilderness, even as he that spake unto Moses appointed him to make it according to the fashion that he had seen. But Solomon built him an house. Howbeit the most High dwelleth not in temples made with hands."

Then Stephen said, "Ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Ghost: as your fathers did, so do ye. Which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted? They have slain them which shewed before of the coming of the Just One, of whom ye have been now the betrayers and murderers."

He said, "Ye who have received the law by the disposition of angels, and have not kept it."

Now when they heard these things, they were cut to the heart. They gnashed on him with their teeth.
