When the high priest asked Stephen if these accusations were true, Stephen stood before the council and lifted up his voice. He said, "Brothers and fathers, listen. The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham, when he was in Mesopotamia, before he lived in Haran. He said to him, 'Get out of your land, and from your relatives, and come into a land which I will show you.'"

Then he told them how Abraham came into the land, and how God gave the covenant of circumcision. He told how the patriarchs were jealous of Joseph, and sold him into Egypt, but God was with him.

He said, "When the time of the promise came, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt, until another king arose who didn't know Joseph. The same dealt deceitfully with our race, and mistreated our fathers."

Stephen told them how Moses was born, and how he fled to Midian, and how the Lord appeared to him in the bush. He told how Moses brought the people out of Egypt, and how they made a calf in those days, and offered a sacrifice to the idol.

He said, "Our fathers had the tabernacle of the testimony in the wilderness, even as he who spoke to Moses commanded him to make it according to the pattern that he had seen. But Solomon built him a house. However, the Most High doesn't dwell in temples made with hands."

Then Stephen said, "You stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, you always resist the Holy Spirit. As your fathers did, so you do. Which of the prophets didn't your fathers persecute? They killed those who foretold the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you have now become betrayers and murderers."

He said, "You received the law as it was ordained by angels, and didn't keep it."

Now when they heard these things, they were cut to the heart. They gnashed at him with their teeth.
