God gave Jesus Christ a revelation to show his servants what must happen soon. Jesus made it known through his angel to John, who testified to God’s word, the testimony of Jesus Christ, and everything he saw. A blessing rested on the reader, the hearers, and those who kept what was written, for the time was near.

John addressed the seven assemblies in Asia with grace and peace from the one who is, was, and is to come; from the seven Spirits before his throne; and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, firstborn of the dead, and ruler of the earth’s kings. Jesus loved his people, washed them from their sins by his blood, and made them a Kingdom and priests to his God and Father. He was coming with the clouds, and every eye would see him. The Lord God declared himself Alpha and Omega, the Almighty.

John was on the isle called Patmos because of God’s Word and the testimony of Jesus. He shared in oppression, the Kingdom, and perseverance in Christ Jesus. In the Spirit on the Lord’s day, John heard behind him a loud voice like a trumpet. It commanded him to write what he saw and send it to the seven assemblies: Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.

John turned toward the voice. Seven golden lamp stands stood before him, and among them was one like a son of man. A long robe reached his feet, and a golden sash crossed his chest. His head and hair were white like wool and snow. His eyes were a flame of fire; his feet resembled brass refined in a furnace; his voice sounded like many waters. Seven stars rested in his right hand. A sharp two-edged sword came from his mouth, and his face shone like the sun at its brightest.

John fell at his feet like a dead man. The figure laid his right hand on John and said, “Do not be afraid. I am the first and the last, and the Living one. I was dead, and behold, I am alive forever and ever. I have the keys of Death and Hades.” He told John to write what he had seen, what was, and what would happen afterward.

He also gave the mystery’s meaning. The seven stars were the angels of the seven assemblies, and the seven golden lamp stands were the seven assemblies.
