Peter's own words make the sin plain, and it was not that they kept part of the money. "While it remained, was it not your own?" The land was theirs to keep, the price theirs to give in part or in whole. What they died for was the lie — pretending to give all while holding part back — and it was, Peter said, a lie to God, not merely to men. Sapphira was given a chance to tell the truth and repeated the false amount. What is the difference between giving little and pretending to give much?
