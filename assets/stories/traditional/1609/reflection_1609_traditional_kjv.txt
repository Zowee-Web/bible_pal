Peter's own words make the sin plain, and it was not that they kept back part of the price. "Whiles it remained, was it not thine own?" The land was theirs to keep, the money theirs to give in part or in whole. What they died for was the lie — pretending to give all while keeping part — and it was, Peter said, a lie unto God, and not unto men. Sapphira was given a chance to speak truth, and repeated the false sum. What is the difference between giving little and pretending to give much?
