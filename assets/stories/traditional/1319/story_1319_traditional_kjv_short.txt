The disciples had come back unto Jesus from the villages of Galilee. They had been sent forth two by two with no purse and no scrip, only sandals and a staff, to preach repentance and to cast out devils and to heal the sick. They had walked the roads and slept in the houses of them that received them and shaken the dust off their feet at the houses of them that would not. Now they were come again, and they were telling Jesus every thing — every healing, every word, every door that had opened, every door that had closed. And the multitudes were yet pressing in round about them, so that they could not so much as sit down to eat.

And the apostles gathered themselves together unto Jesus, and told him all things, both what they had done, and what they had taught.

And he said unto them, "Come ye yourselves apart into a desert place, and rest a while." For there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much as to eat.

And they departed into a desert place by ship privately.

And the ship moved out across the waters of the lake of Galilee. The multitudes stood upon the shore and beheld it go. The disciples sat in the bottom of the ship, and the wind moved across the waters, and the sound of the village fell away behind them. And so it came to pass on that day, when Jesus took the disciples away to rest.
