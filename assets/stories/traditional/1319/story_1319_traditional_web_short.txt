The disciples had come back to Jesus from the villages of Galilee. They had been sent out two by two with no purse and no bag, only sandals and a staff, to preach repentance and to cast out demons and to heal the sick. They had walked the roads and slept in the houses of those who received them and brushed the dust off their feet at the houses of those who would not. Now they had come back, and they were telling Jesus everything — every healing, every word, every door that had opened, every door that had closed. And the crowds were still pressing in around them, so that they could not even sit down to eat.

The apostles gathered themselves together to Jesus, and they told him all things, whatever they had done, and whatever they had taught.

He said to them, "You come apart into a deserted place, and rest awhile." For there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much as to eat.

They went away in the boat to a deserted place by themselves.

And the boat moved out across the water of the Galilee lake. The crowds stood on the shore and watched it go. The disciples sat in the bottom of the boat, and the wind moved across the water, and the sound of the village fell away behind them. And so it was on that day, when Jesus took the disciples away to rest.
