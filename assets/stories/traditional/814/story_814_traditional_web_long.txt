The sun hung like a golden shield over Samaria, casting long shadows on the dusty paths. Tired travellers wandered through the ancient city, seeking refuge from the midday heat. Among them was a woman named Mary, her heart burdened by grief. Her beloved brother, Lazarus, lay cold in his tomb, and no amount of prayer seemed to bring him back.

Desperation gnawed at Mary. The whispers of her other siblings echoed her own doubts. Could their faith be strong enough to overcome the grip of death? They had heard stories of a man in the town of Bethany, who could heal the sick and even bring the dead back to life. His name was Jesus.

Hope flickered within Mary. With unwavering faith, she set out for Bethany, her heart pounding with anticipation. The journey was long and arduous, but each step brought her closer to the possibility of seeing Lazarus rise again.

When she arrived, she found a welcoming crowd gathered around Jesus. He spoke with kindness and compassion, offering words of solace to those who mourned. As he approached Mary, she poured out her grief, confessing her faith that Jesus had the power to restore her brother to her.

Jesus took her hand gently and replied, "I am here to comfort you. But your grief will soon turn into joy, for I am the resurrection and the life. Have faith in me, and your brother will live again."

A sense of peace settled over Mary as she listened to Jesus' words. She believed. With unwavering trust, she followed him to the tomb, where Lazarus' body lay wrapped in linen cloths. Jesus prayed, his voice filled with authority. The stone that sealed the tomb was rolled away, and a blinding light filled the space.

Then, a miracle occurred. Lazarus' body, once cold and still, began to move. His eyes opened, a gasp escaped his lips, and he reached for Mary's hand. He spoke, his voice weak but filled with life. "Sister, I am alive!"

Joy erupted in Mary's heart like a burst of sunlight. Tears of relief flowed freely as she embraced her brother, thanking Jesus profusely for his wondrous power. The town of Bethany echoed with tales of the resurrection of Lazarus, spreading the news of Jesus, the Son of God, who had overcome death itself.
