Obadiah feared Yahweh greatly; when Jezebel cut off Yahweh’s prophets, he hid one hundred of them in caves and fed them with bread and water. When Elijah sent him to Ahab, Obadiah said, “He will kill me.” Elijah answered, “I will surely show myself to him today.” So Obadiah went to meet Ahab. What brave step is before you today?
