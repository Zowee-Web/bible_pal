Peter was asleep. That is the detail which arrests thee. The night before his trial — almost certainly his death — and he slept between two guards with chains upon his wrists.

The church was awake. They prayed through the night, earnest and desperate. But Peter slept.

And when the angel came and the chains fell and the gates opened, Peter passed through it all supposing it a vision. He believed not his own deliverance until he stood alone upon the street.

Sometimes rescue cometh in the middle of the night, when the one being delivered is the last to know it is real.
