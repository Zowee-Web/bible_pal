Imagine standing by a great lake early in the morning. The sky is just turning pink. Thou hast laboured hard at somewhat all the night long, but nothing hath come of it. Then someone calleth out with a kind voice and helpeth thee in a way thou didst not look for. On a sudden, everything is changed.

So it came to pass with the disciples by the sea. They took no fish all the night. Then Jesus shewed himself upon the shore and surprised them with more fishes than they could draw in. He even prepared their breakfast for them.

Sometimes help cometh from one that loveth us, right when we feel most weary. A warm meal and a kind voice can make all things feel well again.
