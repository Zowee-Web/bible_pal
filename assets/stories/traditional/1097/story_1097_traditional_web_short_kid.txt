King Jehoshaphat got some really scary news. A HUGE army was coming to attack his country. The army was so big nobody could even count all the soldiers.

Jehoshaphat was afraid. He knew his army wasn't big enough to win. So instead of getting ready to fight, he did something else first — he prayed.

He gathered ALL the people together at the temple. Men, women, kids — everyone came. Then Jehoshaphat prayed out loud.

"God, you're in charge of everything," he said. "We don't know what to do. But we're looking to YOU for help."

Everyone stood there quietly, waiting.

Then God's Spirit came on a man named Jahaziel. He said, "Don't be afraid! This battle isn't yours — it's GOD'S. You won't even have to fight."

The next morning, the army went out. But here's the crazy part — Jehoshaphat put SINGERS at the front of the army! Not soldiers. SINGERS. And they sang, "Thank the Lord, because his love lasts forever!"

When they started singing, God made the enemy armies turn on EACH OTHER. They destroyed themselves! By the time Jehoshaphat's army arrived, the battle was already over.

It took three whole days to collect everything the enemy had left behind. Then the people went back to Jerusalem singing and playing music, full of joy.

They didn't have to fight at all. God did it for them.
