King Jehoshaphat got some really scary news one day. A MASSIVE army — bigger than anything he'd ever seen — was marching straight toward his country. They were already close. Really close.

The king was terrified. His army was way too small to fight back. He knew they couldn't win on their own.

So instead of getting ready for battle, Jehoshaphat did something different. He called EVERYONE together — every man, woman, and child in the whole country — and brought them to the temple to pray.

Jehoshaphat stood in front of all those people and prayed out loud to God. He reminded God of all the powerful things He had done before. Then he said something very honest:

"God, we have no power against this huge army. We don't know what to do. But our eyes are on YOU."

Everyone stood there silently — moms holding their babies, old men with walking sticks, little kids standing next to their parents. They all waited.

Then something amazing happened. God's Spirit came on a man named Jahaziel, and he said, "Don't be afraid! Don't be scared! This battle is NOT yours — it belongs to GOD. You won't even have to fight. Just show up, stand still, and watch what God does."

Jehoshaphat bowed his face to the ground. Everyone fell down and worshiped God.

The next morning, the army got ready to march out. But Jehoshaphat made a very unusual decision. Instead of putting soldiers at the front, he put SINGERS there! Regular people, singing praise to God, walking ahead of the whole army:

"Thank the Lord, because his love lasts forever!"

And when they started singing — God made the enemy armies turn against EACH OTHER! They fought among themselves and destroyed themselves completely. By the time Jehoshaphat's people arrived and looked down from a hill, the battle was already over. Not a single enemy soldier was left.

It took THREE DAYS to gather up everything the enemy had left behind — that's how much stuff there was!

On the fourth day, everyone gathered in a valley and thanked God. They named the place "The Valley of Blessing."

Then they marched back to Jerusalem, playing harps and trumpets and singing with joy. They went straight to God's house to say thank you.

And after that, the kingdom was peaceful. God gave them rest on every side.
