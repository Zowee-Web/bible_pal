Jehoshaphat stood before the people and said, "We have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do, but our eyes are upon thee."

He proclaimed a fast. All Judah gathered — men, women, and children. They stood before the Lord.

The word came through Jahaziel: "The battle is not yours, but God's. Ye shall not need to fight in this battle. Set yourselves, stand ye still, and see the salvation of the Lord."

The next morning, Jehoshaphat appointed singers to go out before the army. They said, "Praise the Lord; for his mercy endureth for ever." And when they began to sing and to praise, the Lord set ambushments against the enemy. The enemy destroyed themselves.

When Judah arrived, the battle was already done. They had not fought. They had sung. And the enemy was gone.

What would it look like to face something thou canst not overcome — and stand still?
