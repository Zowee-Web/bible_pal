There is a manner of courage that looketh not like courage. It looketh like singing.

The host of Judah went forth to face a force they could not overcome — and in place of weapons, they sent the choir. Not after the battle, in triumph. Before it. Whilst the outcome was yet unknown.

What Jehoshaphat confessed — "we know not what to do, but our eyes are upon thee" — is not the prayer of a weak man. It is the prayer of an honest one. The kind of prayer that costeth something to speak aloud, before thy people, when all are watching and waiting for thee to have an answer.

Sometimes the bravest thing is not having a plan. It is confessing thou hast none — and showing forth nonetheless.
