Word came unto King Jehoshaphat that a great multitude was come against Judah — the children of Moab and Ammon, with others beside them, a host beyond numbering. They were already across the sea. They were already coming.

Jehoshaphat feared, and set himself to seek the Lord, and proclaimed a fast throughout all Judah. The people gathered in the court of the temple at Jerusalem — men, women, and their little ones — and stood before the Lord.

Jehoshaphat stood in the congregation and prayed. "O Lord God of our fathers, art not thou God in heaven? And rulest not thou over all the kingdoms of the heathen? And in thine hand is there not power and might, so that none is able to withstand thee?" He set forth the peril plainly. The enemy was come. The people had no strength against them. "O our God," he said, "we have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but our eyes are upon thee."

All Judah stood before the Lord, with their little ones, their wives, and their children.

Then upon Jahaziel the Levite came the Spirit of the Lord. He spake: "Hearken ye, all Judah and ye inhabitants of Jerusalem, and thou King Jehoshaphat. Thus saith the Lord unto you, Be not afraid nor dismayed by reason of this great multitude; for the battle is not yours, but God's."

He told them where the enemy would come. "Ye shall not need to fight in this battle," he said. "Set yourselves, stand ye still, and see the salvation of the Lord."

Jehoshaphat bowed his head with his face to the ground. All the people fell before the Lord.

In the morning they went forth unto the wilderness of Tekoa. Jehoshaphat stood and said, "Believe in the Lord your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper."

Then he appointed singers unto the Lord, that they should go out before the army, saying, "Praise the Lord; for his mercy endureth for ever."

And when they began to sing and to praise, the Lord set ambushments against the enemy. The children of Ammon and Moab turned upon one another and destroyed themselves. When Judah came to the watch tower, the earth was covered with dead bodies. None had escaped.

Three days they were in gathering of the spoil. On the fourth day they assembled in the valley of Berachah, and there they blessed the Lord.

Then they returned to Jerusalem with joy, with psalteries and harps and trumpets, and came to the house of the Lord.
