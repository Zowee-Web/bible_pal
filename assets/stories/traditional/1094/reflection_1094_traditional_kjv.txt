To flee from a thing one hath been bidden do is not always a matter of great drama. Sometimes it looketh like the buying of a passage, the boarding of a ship, and the laying down to sleep.

Jonah argued not with God. He made no bargain. He simply departed — quietly, with purpose — in the contrary direction. And the sea, which had been perfectly calm, became the instrument that brought all things to a stay.

What is most striking is not that the tempest came. It is that Jonah already knew. "Cast me forth," he said. "It is for my sake." He was not astonished. He had known all along.

Sometimes the hardest thing is not the hearing of the call. It is the owning that thou heardest it clearly — and chose to walk the other way.
