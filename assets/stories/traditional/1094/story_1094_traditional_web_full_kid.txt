God had an important job for a man named Jonah. "Go to the city of Nineveh," God said. "Tell them to stop doing bad things."

Nineveh was a really big, scary city far to the east. Jonah did NOT want to go there. Not one bit.

So instead of going east to Nineveh, Jonah went west — as far from Nineveh as he could possibly get! He went to a port city called Joppa and found a ship going to a place called Tarshish. He bought a ticket, went below deck, and fell fast asleep. He was running away from God.

The ship sailed out to sea. Everything seemed fine at first. But then God sent a powerful storm.

The wind started howling. The waves got HUGE — crashing over the sides of the ship. The boat rocked so hard it felt like it would break into pieces! Water poured in everywhere.

The sailors were terrified! They threw boxes and barrels overboard to make the ship lighter. They each prayed to their own gods. Nothing helped.

And where was Jonah? Still sleeping below deck!

The captain found him. "HOW ARE YOU SLEEPING?!" he shouted. "Get up and pray! Maybe your God can help us!"

The sailors decided to figure out whose fault this storm was. They drew straws. And — surprise, surprise — the straw pointed to Jonah.

"Who are you?" they asked. "What did you do?"

"I'm running away from God," Jonah admitted. "This storm is my fault. If you throw me into the sea, it will stop."

The sailors didn't want to throw a man overboard. They tried rowing to shore instead. But the waves just got bigger and bigger.

Finally, they had no choice. They prayed to God to forgive them, and then they picked up Jonah and threw him into the sea.

WHOOOOSH — the storm stopped. Just like that. The wind quit. The waves went flat. The sea was perfectly calm.

The sailors were amazed. They knew this was the work of the real God.

But what about Jonah?

God had a plan for that too. He sent a GIANT fish — and the fish swallowed Jonah whole!

Jonah was inside that fish for three days and three nights. It was dark. It was probably really smelly. But Jonah was alive.

And inside that fish, Jonah finally started to pray.
