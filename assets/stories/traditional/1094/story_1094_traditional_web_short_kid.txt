God told a man named Jonah to go to a big city called Nineveh. "Tell them to stop doing bad things," God said.

But Jonah didn't want to go. Instead of going to Nineveh, he ran away! He went to a port city, found a ship going the OPPOSITE direction, and climbed aboard. He went below deck and fell asleep. He was trying to run away from God.

But you can't run from God.

God sent a huge storm. The wind was howling. The waves were crashing. The ship was rocking back and forth so hard that the sailors thought it would break apart!

Everyone was scared. The sailors threw boxes and barrels overboard to make the ship lighter. They prayed to their own gods. Nothing worked.

And Jonah? He was STILL asleep down below!

The captain found him. "How can you sleep?" he shouted. "Get up and pray!"

The sailors decided to find out whose fault the storm was. They drew straws, and — you guessed it — it was Jonah.

"I'm running from God," Jonah told them. "This storm is because of me. Throw me into the sea and it will stop."

The sailors didn't want to do it. They tried rowing to shore instead. But the storm only got worse.

Finally, they picked up Jonah and threw him into the water.

And IMMEDIATELY — the storm stopped. The wind quit. The waves went flat. Everything was calm.

But what about Jonah?

God sent a HUGE fish. The fish swallowed Jonah whole!

And Jonah was inside that fish for three whole days and three whole nights.
