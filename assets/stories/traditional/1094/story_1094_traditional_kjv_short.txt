Now the word of the Lord came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying, "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me."

But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the Lord. He went down to Joppa, a port upon the coast, and found a ship bound for Tarshish — as far west as the known world reached. He paid the fare, went aboard, and descended into the hold. He was fleeing from the Lord.

The ship set forth. The coast fell behind. The sea was wide and open.

Then the Lord sent out a great wind into the sea. A mighty tempest struck the ship — waves crashing over the bow, timbers groaning, the deck pitching beneath the mariners' feet. The storm was so violent that the ship was like to be broken.

The mariners were afraid. Every man cried unto his god. They cast the cargo into the sea to lighten the ship. The wind howled. The waves battered the hull.

And Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship, and was fast asleep.

The shipmaster found him. "What meanest thou, O sleeper?" he said. "Arise, call upon thy God, if so be that God will think upon us, that we perish not."

The men cast lots to discover for whose cause this evil was upon them. The lot fell upon Jonah.

They said unto him, "Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause is this evil upon us? What is thine occupation? Whence comest thou?"

Jonah said, "I am an Hebrew; and I fear the Lord, the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land." And he told them that he fled from the presence of the Lord.

The men were exceedingly afraid. "What shall we do unto thee," they said, "that the sea may be calm?"

"Take me up," said Jonah, "and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you."

The men rowed hard to bring the ship to land, but they could not, for the sea wrought and was tempestuous. Then they cried unto the Lord, "We beseech thee, O Lord, let us not perish for this man's life." And they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea.

And the sea ceased from her raging.

Now the Lord had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights.
