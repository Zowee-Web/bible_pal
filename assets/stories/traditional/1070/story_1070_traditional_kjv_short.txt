Far to the east, wise men studied the night sky. A star appeared — one they had not seen before. They gathered provisions, loaded their camels, and set out upon the long road toward Jerusalem.

The journey crossed dry plains and rocky hills. Dust coated their robes. The camels swayed beneath heavy packs of supplies and carefully wrapped treasures. The men watched the sky each night and pressed westward each morning.

They arrived in Jerusalem and began asking in the streets. "Where is he that is born King of the Jews?" they said. "We have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him."

Word spread through the city. It reached the palace.

When Herod the king had heard these things, he was troubled. And all Jerusalem was troubled with him. He gathered together the chief priests and scribes and demanded of them where Christ should be born.

"In Bethlehem of Judaea," they told him. They cited the prophet: out of Bethlehem should come a Governor, that shall rule my people Israel.

Herod privily called the wise men unto him. He enquired of them diligently what time the star appeared. They told him. He leaned forward and spake carefully.

"Go and search diligently for the young child," he said. "And when ye have found him, bring me word again, that I may come and worship him also."

The wise men departed Jerusalem. The road southward was short — only a few miles unto Bethlehem. And lo, the star which they saw in the east went before them. It moved ahead through the darkening sky, then stopped. It stood still over the place where the young child was.

When they saw that the star had stopped, they rejoiced with exceeding great joy. They spake one to another. They pointed upward. Their voices carried across the quiet road.

They came into the house. They entered and saw the young child with Mary his mother. The wise men knelt. They fell down and worshipped him.

Then they opened their treasures. They brought out gold, bright and heavy. They brought frankincense, its sharp sweetness filling the small room. They brought myrrh, dark and rich in its wrapping.

The gifts lay before the child.

And being warned of God in a dream that they should not return to Herod, the wise men rose in the morning, packed their camels, turned east, and departed into their own country another way.
