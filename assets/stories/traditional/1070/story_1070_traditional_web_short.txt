Far to the east, wise men studied the night sky. A star appeared — one they had not seen before. They gathered provisions, loaded camels, and set out on the long road toward Jerusalem.

The journey crossed dry plains and rocky hills. Dust coated their robes. The camels swayed beneath heavy packs of supplies and carefully wrapped treasures. The men watched the sky each night and pressed westward each morning.

They arrived in Jerusalem and began asking in the streets. "Where is he who has been born king of the Jews?" they said. "We saw his star when it rose, and we have come to worship him."

Word spread through the city. It reached the palace.

When King Herod heard this, he was troubled. All Jerusalem was troubled with him. He gathered the chief priests and scribes and demanded to know where the Christ was to be born.

"In Bethlehem of Judea," they told him. They cited the prophet: out of Bethlehem would come a ruler to shepherd the people of Israel.

Herod summoned the wise men in secret. He asked them exactly when the star had appeared. They told him. He leaned forward and spoke carefully.

"Go and search diligently for the child," he said. "And when you have found him, bring me word, that I too may come and worship him."

The wise men departed Jerusalem. The road south was short — only a few miles to Bethlehem. And the star they had seen when it rose went before them. It moved ahead through the darkening sky, then stopped. It stood still over one place.

When they saw the star had stopped, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy. They spoke to one another. They pointed upward. Their voices carried across the quiet road.

They came to the house. They entered and saw the young child with Mary his mother. The wise men knelt. They fell down and worshiped him.

Then they opened their treasures. They brought out gold, bright and heavy. They brought frankincense, its sharp sweetness filling the small room. They brought myrrh, dark and rich in its wrapping.

The gifts lay before the child.

That night, God warned them in a dream not to return to Herod. In the morning, the wise men packed their camels, turned east, and departed for their own country by another road.
