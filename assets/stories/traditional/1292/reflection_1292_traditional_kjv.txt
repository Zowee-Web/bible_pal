Mordecai went out from the presence of the king in royal apparel of blue and white, and with a great crown of gold, and the city of Shushan shouted and was glad. The Jews had light, and gladness, and joy, and honour. Mourning gave way to feasting in courtyards from Shushan to the far ends of the empire. What is it to walk into a feast on a day that had been written down for thy destruction?
