When the temple in Jerusalem was finished, Solomon assembled the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes before him. The priests took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and they brought it into the inner sanctuary of the house, into the most holy place, under the wings of the cherubim.

When the priests came out of the holy place, the cloud filled the house of the Lord, so that the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud. The glory of the Lord had filled the house.

Then Solomon stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of all the assembly of Israel, and spread out his hands toward heaven. He said, "Lord, the God of Israel, there is no God like you, in heaven above, or on earth beneath, who keeps covenant and loving kindness with your servants who walk before you with all their heart."

He said, "You have kept with your servant David my father what you promised him. Yes, you spoke with your mouth, and have fulfilled it with your hand, as it is this day."

Solomon said, "But will God indeed dwell on the earth? Behold, heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain you. How much less this house that I have built. Yet have respect for the prayer of your servant, and for his supplication, Lord my God."

He said, "Listen to the cry and to the prayer which your servant prays before you today, that your eyes may be open toward this house night and day, even toward the place of which you have said, 'My name shall be there.'"

Solomon stretched out his hands and continued, "Listen to the supplication of your servant, and of your people Israel, when they shall pray toward this place. Listen in heaven, your dwelling place, and when you hear, forgive."

The priests stood in their places, and the people stood in the courts of the house, and the cloud was still upon them. The light of the late afternoon lay across the stones of the temple, and the silence of the people was great around the altar.
