When they had finished their breakfast on the shore of the Sea of Tiberias, the morning was still and quiet over the water. The fire of coals was burning low between them, and the fish and the bread lay where Jesus had placed them. The mist had lifted from the water, and the early sun lay on the stones. Peter sat among the disciples on the sand, and the Lord turned to him.

Jesus said to Simon Peter, "Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me more than these?" Peter answered, "Yes, Lord. You know that I have affection for you." Jesus said to him, "Feed my lambs."

The other disciples stood at the fire, and Jesus asked him a second time, "Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?" Peter said, "Yes, Lord. You know that I have affection for you." Jesus said to him, "Tend my sheep."

As the water lapped softly against the stones of the shore, Jesus asked him the third time, "Simon, son of Jonah, do you have affection for me?" Peter was grieved because he asked him the third time. He said to him, "Lord, you know everything. You know that I have affection for you." Jesus said to him, "Feed my sheep."

Then Jesus said to him, "Most certainly I tell you, when you were young, you dressed yourself and walked where you wanted to go. But when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will dress you, and carry you where you do not wish to go." He said this signifying by what kind of death Peter would glorify God.

When he had said this, Jesus rose to his feet and said to him, "Follow me." And the morning sea moved softly behind them on the stones of the shore.
