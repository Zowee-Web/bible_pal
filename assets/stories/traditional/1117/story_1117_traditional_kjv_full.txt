When Pharaoh drew nigh, the children of Israel lifted up their eyes. On the horizon behind them, a long line of dust was rising. Chariots. Horsemen. The army of Egypt was marching after them. The dust came on apace.

They were sore afraid. They cried out unto the Lord. And then they turned upon Moses.

"Because there were no graves in Egypt, hast thou taken us away to die in the wilderness? Wherefore hast thou dealt thus with us, to bring us forth out of Egypt? Did we not say unto thee in Egypt, 'Let us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians'? For it had been better for us to serve the Egyptians, than that we should die in the wilderness."

Their voices rose against him. Children were lifted upon shoulders. Women clutched their bundles. The sea was at their backs and the army was before them, and there was no place to flee.

Moses stood before the people. "Fear ye not. Stand still, and see the salvation of the Lord, which he will shew to you this day. For the Egyptians whom ye have seen this day, ye shall see them again no more for ever. The Lord shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace."

And the Lord said unto Moses, "Wherefore criest thou unto me? Speak unto the children of Israel, that they go forward. But lift thou up thy rod, and stretch out thine hand over the sea, and divide it. And the children of Israel shall go into the midst of the sea upon the dry ground."

The angel of God, which went before the camp of Israel, removed. The pillar of cloud, which had stood before them through the day, lifted and turned. It passed over the heads of the people and came down behind them. It came between the camp of the Egyptians and the camp of Israel. And there was the cloud, and there was darkness, and yet unto Israel it gave light by night. The one came not near the other all the night.

And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. And the Lord caused the sea to go back by a strong east wind all that night. The wind tore at the cloaks of the people. The waters were divided. They lifted up in two great walls on the right hand and on the left. The bed of the sea was made dry land between them.

And the children of Israel went into the midst of the sea upon the dry ground. They walked between the walls of water. They walked all the night.

The Egyptians pursued, and went in after them — even all Pharaoh's horses, his chariots, and his horsemen. The chariot wheels cut into the floor of the sea. The hooves of the horses beat down between the walls of water.

And it came to pass, that in the morning watch the Lord looked unto the host of the Egyptians through the pillar of fire and of the cloud, and troubled them. He took off their chariot wheels, that they drave them heavily. The chariots faltered. The horses staggered. And the Egyptians said one unto another, "Let us flee from the face of Israel, for the Lord fighteth for them against us."

But it was too late. Israel had already come unto the far shore.

And the Lord said unto Moses, "Stretch out thine hand over the sea, that the waters may come again upon the Egyptians, upon their chariots, and upon their horsemen."

And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea. The sea returned to his strength when the morning appeared. The Egyptians fled against it, and the walls fell. The waters came down upon the chariots, and upon the horsemen, even upon all the host of Pharaoh that came into the sea after Israel. There remained not so much as one of them.

But the children of Israel walked upon dry land in the midst of the sea, and they came up out of it on the other side, and they stood upon the shore at the first light.

They looked back. The water lay still where the sea had been opened. The bodies of the Egyptians washed up along the seashore in the morning sun. The host that had pursued them was gone.

And Israel saw that great work which the Lord did upon the Egyptians. And the people feared the Lord. They believed in the Lord, and in his servant Moses, and they stood upon the shore in the new light of the morning.
