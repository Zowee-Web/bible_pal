The next day, around noon, the men sent from Cornelius were still on the road, drawing close to Joppa. Peter went up to the flat roof of the house to pray.

He was very hungry. The meal was being prepared below, and the smell of cooking rose through the warm air. But before the food was ready, a trance fell over him.

He saw the sky open. A large object came down toward him — something like a great sheet, held at its four corners, lowering slowly to the earth. Inside it moved every kind of animal. Four-footed beasts and wild creatures. Things that crawled along the ground. Birds of the air. Clean and unclean together, all gathered in one vessel.

A voice spoke. "Rise, Peter. Kill and eat."

Peter said, "Not so, Lord. I have never eaten anything common or unclean."

The voice came a second time. "What God has cleansed, do not call common."

This happened three times. Three times the voice corrected him. Then the sheet was taken back up into heaven, and the sky closed, and Peter was alone on the rooftop with the sun overhead and the clay tiles warm beneath his feet.

While he was still thinking, the men from Cornelius arrived below. They had asked their way through the streets and now stood at the gate of Simon the tanner's house. They called out, asking whether a man named Simon, also called Peter, was staying there.

The Spirit spoke to Peter. "Three men are looking for you. Get up. Go down. Go with them without hesitation, for I have sent them."

Peter went down from the roof. He found the three men standing at the gate. He said, "I am the one you are looking for. Why have you come?"

They answered him. "Cornelius the centurion sent us. He is a just man who fears God, and he has a good reputation among all the Jewish people. A holy angel told him to send for you, to bring you to his house and to hear what you have to say."

Peter looked at them. Then he called them inside and gave them a place to stay for the night.

The next morning, Peter left with them. Several of the believers from Joppa walked alongside, and the group moved together down the road, heading north toward Caesarea.
