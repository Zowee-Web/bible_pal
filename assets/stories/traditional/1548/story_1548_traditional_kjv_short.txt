And on the morrow, when the sun stood high, the men whom Cornelius had sent were yet upon the road, and drew nigh unto Joppa. And Peter went up upon the housetop to pray.

Now he was sore hungry. The meal was being made ready below, and the savour of it rose up through the warm air. But ere the food was set before him, a trance came upon him.

And he saw the heavens opened wide. A great vessel descended toward him — as it were a vast cloth, bound at its four corners, and it came down slowly unto the earth. And within it stirred every manner of beast. Creatures upon four feet, and wild things of the field. Things that crept upon the ground. Fowls of the air. The clean and the unclean together, all gathered into one vessel.

And a voice spake unto him. "Arise, Peter. Slay, and eat."

But Peter said, "It shall not be so, Lord. For I have never taken unto my mouth any thing that is common or defiled."

And the voice came unto him a second time. "That which God hath made clean, thou shalt not call common."

And this was done three times. Three times did the voice reprove him. Then was the vessel drawn up again into heaven, and the sky closed above him, and Peter stood alone upon the housetop, with the sun over him and the clay tiles warm beneath his feet.

And while he yet pondered these things within himself, the men sent from Cornelius came nigh. They had inquired their way through the streets, and now they stood before the gate of Simon the tanner's house. And they called out, asking whether one Simon, who was called also Peter, lodged there.

Then the Spirit spake unto Peter. "Behold, three men seek thee. Arise therefore, and go down. Go with them, and doubt nothing, for I have sent them."

And Peter went down from the roof. He found the three men standing at the gate, and he said unto them, "I am he whom ye seek. For what cause are ye come?"

And they answered him, saying, "Cornelius, a centurion, hath sent us. He is an upright man, one that feareth God, and he is well spoken of among all the people of the Jews. And a holy angel charged him to send for thee, that thou shouldest come unto his house and that he might hear the words thou hast to speak."

And Peter looked upon them. Then he called them in and gave them lodging for the night.

And when the morning was come, Peter departed with them. And certain of the brethren from Joppa went with him, and together they set forth upon the road, journeying northward toward Caesarea.
