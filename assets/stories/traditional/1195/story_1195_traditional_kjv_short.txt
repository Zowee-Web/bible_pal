As Jesus was teaching by the way, parents brought young children unto him, that he should touch them. The mothers held the babes in their arms, and the elder children walked with their hands in the hands of their fathers. The road was warm under the feet of the people, and the morning sun lay across the way.

The disciples saw the families coming and rebuked those that brought them. They said unto the parents, "The Master is busied. Trouble him not with the children."

But when Jesus saw it, he was much displeased, and said unto them, "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God."

He said, "Verily I say unto you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter therein."

He took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them. The children stood quietly before him, and the youngest sat upon his knee. He laid his hands upon their heads, one after another, and he spake unto each of them.

The mothers stood by, and they beheld the Master holding their children. The fathers waited along the path, and the disciples drew back to make room.

When Jesus had finished, the parents took their children up again. They went on their way, and the children walked beside them with their hands in their hands. The morning had grown warm, and the sun was high above the road.

The disciples remained beside Jesus. They had seen what he had done, and they spake not as they walked on with him. The road stretched onward toward the next village.

Jesus went on his way unto the cities of Judea, and the children which had been blessed went home unto their houses. The day grew warm above the hills, and the morning was already turning into afternoon when they came again unto the gate of the town.
