As Jesus was teaching by the way, parents were bringing little children to him, that he might touch them. The mothers held the babies in their arms, and the older children walked with their hands in the hands of their fathers. The road was warm under the feet of the people, and the morning sun lay across the way.

The disciples saw the families coming and rebuked them. They said to the parents, "The teacher is busy. Don't trouble him with the children."

But when Jesus saw it, he was moved with indignation, and said to them, "Allow the little children to come to me. Don't forbid them, for the Kingdom of God belongs to such as these."

He said, "Most certainly I tell you, whoever will not receive the Kingdom of God like a little child, he will in no way enter into it."

He took them in his arms and laid his hands on them and blessed them. The children stood quietly before him, and the youngest sat upon his knee. He laid his hands upon their heads, one after another, and he spoke to each of them.

The mothers stood by, and they watched as the teacher held their children. The fathers waited along the path, and the disciples drew back to make room.

When Jesus had finished, the parents took their children up again. They went on their way, and the children walked beside them with their hands in their hands. The morning had grown warm, and the sun was high above the road.

The disciples remained beside Jesus. They had seen what he had done, and they did not speak as they walked on with him. The road stretched ahead toward the next village.

Jesus went on his way to the cities of Judea, and the children who had been blessed went home to their houses. The day grew warm above the hills, and the morning was already turning into afternoon when they came again to the gate of the town.
