In those days the word of the LORD was precious in Israel. There was no open vision. The harvest came in, and the rains came down, and the lamps were lit at evening in the houses of Shiloh, and the people did that which they did, but the voice that had spoken unto the fathers spake seldom now. Men hearkened and heard it not. The priests at the temple offered the daily offerings, and the smoke rose, and the days passed.

In the temple of the LORD at Shiloh a lamp burned. It stood within a vessel of clay set upon a stand of bronze. The flame thereof was no bigger than a man's thumb. By night it kept a small bright point in the dim, and the light reached just so far as to shew the seams of the curtain and the shapes of the low things upon the floor. Beyond the curtain was the ark of God. Beyond the temple walls was the night.

The child Samuel was laid down in his place. He was a boy, and his place was near. He had drawn his blanket up about his shoulders, and his breath had grown slow and even. He had ministered unto the LORD that day before Eli, as he had every day since his mother had brought him up to Shiloh in the linen tunic she had made for him. The day was done. He was asleep, or near sleep, in the small warm room he kept within the house of the LORD.

Eli was laid down in his own place also. His eyes had begun to wax dim, that he could not see, and his lying down was the lying down of an old man whose body settleth by stages. He had said his prayers. He had put aside his outer robe. He breathed slowly in the dark, and the dark held him as it held the boy.

And the LORD called Samuel.

The boy sat up.

"Here am I."

He pushed back the blanket and stood up in the dark and ran. His bare feet were quick upon the stones. He went the way he knew unto where Eli lay, and he bent close to the old man, and he said, "Here am I; for thou calledst me."

Eli stirred in his place. His voice came up from sleep, gentle and a little surprised. "I called not. Lie down again."

Samuel went back. He lay down in his place. He pulled the blanket up. The lamp was yet burning. He hearkened a little while in the dark. Then his breath settled again.

And the LORD called yet again, "Samuel."

He arose. He went the same way a second time. He stood at the old man's side and bent close. "Here am I; for thou didst call me."

Eli answered, "I called not, my son. Lie down again."

The boy went back. He lay down. The temple was quiet. The lamp held its small bright point against the dark. Without, somewhere far off, a dog barked once, then was still. The boy lay hearkening. Nothing came.

Now Samuel did not yet know the LORD, neither was the word of the LORD yet revealed unto him. He was a child in the house of the LORD, and he knew not yet the voice of the LORD.

And the LORD called Samuel again, the third time.

The boy arose. He went unto Eli a third time, and he said again, "Here am I; for thou didst call me."

Eli perceived that the LORD had called the child.

He raised himself up a little where he lay. His hand found the boy's shoulder where the cloth of his tunic gathered. The old man's voice was low. "Go, lie down," Eli said. "And it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, LORD; for thy servant heareth."

So Samuel went and lay down in his place.

The lamp was yet burning.

He lay upon his back this time. He kept his eyes open. He looked up into the dim where the curtain hung. He spake not. He waited.

And the LORD came, and stood, and called as at other times, "Samuel, Samuel."

Then Samuel said, "Speak; for thy servant heareth."

And the LORD spake unto the child. He told him what he would do. He said, "Behold, I will do a thing in Israel, at which both the ears of every one that heareth it shall tingle. In that day I will perform against Eli all that I have spoken concerning his house, from the beginning even unto the end. For I have told him that I will judge his house for ever, for the iniquity which he knoweth; because his sons made themselves vile, and he restrained them not. Therefore I have sworn unto the house of Eli, that the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be purged with sacrifice nor offering for ever."

The boy lay still in the dark. The word came down upon him as he lay. He moved not from his place. He turned not his head. The lamp burned beside him, smaller now, the flame thereof low, and the dim held him.

When the word was finished, the temple was quiet again.

And Samuel lay until the morning.

He slept not. He lay in his place, and the night went over him slowly. He turned the word in his mind. He kept it where the LORD had laid it. After a long time the gray came unto the high windows. The shapes of the curtain shewed. The shapes of the doors shewed.

He arose. He set his blanket aside. He went unto the doors of the house of the LORD, and he set his hands against the wood, and he opened them. The cool morning air came in. Birds were calling somewhere among the houses of Shiloh. The lamp was very low now, almost finished, but it was yet burning.

And Samuel feared to shew Eli the vision.

He went about the small tasks of the morning. He went not in unto where the old man lay. He did the things he did every day. His hands moved as they had always moved. The word he had been given lay within him like a stone he knew not yet how to carry.

Then Eli called Samuel from his place. "Samuel, my son."

"Here am I."

"What is the thing that the LORD hath said unto thee? I pray thee hide it not from me."

Samuel came near. He stood by the old man. He told him every whit, and hid nothing from him.

Eli hearkened. He lifted not up his hand. He cried not out. He turned not his face to the wall. He received the word in his lying down, as a man receiveth a thing he hath long known was coming. When the boy was finished he said, "It is the LORD: let him do what seemeth him good."

That was the beginning.

Samuel grew. He grew in the house of the LORD and afterward he grew in Israel. The LORD was with him. He did let none of his words fall to the ground. The word he gave unto Samuel went out and stood and held, and what Samuel spake came to pass. And all Israel from Dan even to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established to be a prophet of the LORD. And the LORD appeared again in Shiloh; for the LORD revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the Lord.
