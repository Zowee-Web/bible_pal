Abijam began to reign over Judah in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam, the son of Nebat. He ruled in Jerusalem for three years, and his mother was Maacah, the daughter of Abishalom. He followed all the sins his father had committed before him. Unlike David his father, his heart was not perfect with Yahweh his God.

Nevertheless, for David’s sake Yahweh gave Abijam a lamp in Jerusalem, raising up his son after him and establishing the city. David had done what was right in Yahweh’s eyes and had not turned aside from his commands throughout his life, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.

War continued between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days of his life. There was also war between Abijam and Jeroboam. Abijam’s other acts and all that he did were written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. When he slept with his fathers, they buried him in David’s city, and his son Asa became king in his place.

Asa’s reign over Judah began in the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel. He ruled forty-one years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Maacah, the daughter of Abishalom. Asa did what was right in Yahweh’s eyes, as David his father had done.

He put away the sodomites from the land and removed all the idols his fathers had made. He also removed his mother Maacah from her position as queen because she had made an abominable image for an Asherah. Asa cut down her image and burned it at the brook Kidron.

The high places, however, were not taken away. Even so, Asa’s heart was perfect with Yahweh all his days. He brought into Yahweh’s house the things dedicated by his father and the things he himself had dedicated: silver, gold, and utensils.
