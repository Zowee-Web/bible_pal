In the eighteenth year of king Jeroboam the son of Nebat, Abijam began his reign over Judah. He reigned three years in Jerusalem. His mother was Maachah, the daughter of Abishalom. Abijam walked in all the sins his father had done before him; his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as the heart of David his father had been.

Yet for David’s sake the Lord gave him a lamp in Jerusalem, setting up his son after him and establishing Jerusalem. David had done right in the Lord’s eyes and had not turned aside from his commandments all the days of his life, save in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.

There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days of his life, and war between Abijam and Jeroboam also. The rest of Abijam’s acts, and all that he did, were written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. Abijam slept with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. His son Asa reigned in his stead.

In the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa began to reign over Judah. Forty and one years he reigned in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Maachah, the daughter of Abishalom. Asa did right in the eyes of the Lord, as David his father had done.

He took away the sodomites from the land and removed all the idols his fathers had made. Maachah his mother had made an idol in a grove, and Asa removed her from being queen. He destroyed her idol and burnt it by the brook Kidron.

The high places were not removed; nevertheless Asa’s heart was perfect with the Lord all his days. Into the house of the Lord he brought the things his father had dedicated, together with the things he himself had dedicated: silver, gold, and vessels.
