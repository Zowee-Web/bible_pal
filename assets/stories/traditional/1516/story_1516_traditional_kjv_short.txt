Elijah came unto the cave at Horeb. He had come a long way. He had walked forty days and forty nights through the wilderness in the strength of the meat the angel had given him beside the juniper-tree. He came at length unto the mount of God. He found the cave in the side of the mount. He came unto the cave. He lodged there.

The word of the LORD came to him in the cave. The word said: what doest thou here, Elijah?

Elijah answered. He spake of his labour. "I have been very jealous for the LORD God of hosts. The children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant. They have thrown down thine altars. They have slain thy prophets with the sword. I, even I only, am left. They seek my life to take it away."

The LORD said: go forth, and stand upon the mount before the LORD.

The LORD passed by.

A great and strong wind came up against the mountain. It rent the mountains. It brake in pieces the rocks before the LORD. The wind was loud where he was. The dust of the broken rocks rose up in the air. The LORD was not in the wind.

After the wind, an earthquake. The ground shook beneath him. The stones rolled in the country below the cave. The LORD was not in the earthquake.

After the earthquake, a fire. The fire passed across the rock. The heat of it came near him. The LORD was not in the fire.

After the fire, a still small voice.

When Elijah heard the still small voice, he wrapped his face in his mantle. He went out. He stood in the entering in of the cave. A voice came to him there. The voice said: what doest thou here, Elijah?
