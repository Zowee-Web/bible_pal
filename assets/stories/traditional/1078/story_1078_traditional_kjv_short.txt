Far unto the east, in a land of wide deserts and ancient cities, wise men studied the night sky. They watched the stars the way the husbandmen watch the rain. They knew the patterns. They knew when a thing was new.

One night, a star appeared which they had not seen before. It was bright. It was strange. The wise men arose from their charts and said one to another: "A king is born — a king of the Jews."

So they laded their camels. They gathered treasures. They set out upon a long, long journey, following that star toward the land of Israel.

When they were come to Jerusalem, they began to enquire of every man they met. "Where is he that is born King of the Jews?" they said. "For we have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him."

Word spread quickly through the narrow streets. Soon King Herod himself heard tell of these strangers and their strange question.

Herod was troubled. And all Jerusalem was troubled with him.

He gathered together all the chief priests and scribes of the people. "Where should Christ be born?" he demanded of them.

They answered straightway. "In Bethlehem of Judaea: for thus it is written by the prophet: 'And thou Bethlehem, in the land of Juda, art not the least among the princes of Juda: for out of thee shall come a Governor, that shall rule my people Israel.'"

Then Herod, when he had privily called the wise men, enquired of them diligently what time the star appeared. He hearkened unto every detail.

"Go unto Bethlehem," Herod said unto them. "And search diligently for the young child. And when ye have found him, bring me word again, that I may come and worship him also."

The wise men departed from Jerusalem. And lo, the star — the same star they had seen in the east — went before them. When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceeding great joy.

The star moved across the sky and stood still. It stood over where the young child was.

The wise men came into the house. They saw the young child with Mary his mother. They fell down. Their foreheads touched the ground.

Then they opened their treasures. They presented unto him gold, gleaming and heavy. They presented frankincense, rich and sweet of savour. They presented myrrh, dark and fragrant.

That night, being warned of God in a dream that they should not return to Herod, the wise men laded their camels again and departed into their own country another way, winding eastward through the hills, back toward home.
