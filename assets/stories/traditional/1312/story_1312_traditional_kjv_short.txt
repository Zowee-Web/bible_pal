Five and twenty years had passed since the Lord had called Abraham out of Haran. Five and twenty years since the promise had been spoken under the stars — that his seed should be as the stars themselves for number. Sarah had grown old in the long waiting. Abraham had grown old. They had dwelt in the land as strangers, and the tent had stood through many seasons, and the promised son had not yet come.

Then the visitors had come to the tent at Mamre. They had spoken of a son at the appointed time. Sarah had laughed behind the tent door, for she was old and Abraham was old. And the visitor had said, "Wherefore did Sarah laugh? Is any thing too hard for the Lord?"

And the time came.

The Lord visited Sarah as he had said, and the Lord did unto Sarah as he had spoken.

For Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him.

And Abraham called the name of his son that was born unto him, whom Sarah bare to him, Isaac.

And Abraham circumcised his son Isaac being eight days old, as God had commanded him.

And Abraham was an hundred years old, when his son Isaac was born unto him.

And Sarah said, "God hath made me to laugh, so that all that hear will laugh with me."

And she said, "Who would have said unto Abraham, that Sarah should have given children suck? for I have born him a son in his old age."

And Isaac grew at his mother's breast. The tent at Mamre was filled with the sound of his small voice. The fires were kindled in the evening, and the lamps in the night, and the boy slept beside Sarah and beside Abraham. And so it was at the tent of the promise, in the days after Isaac was born.
