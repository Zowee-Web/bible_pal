Elisha came to Damascus while Benhadad, the king of Syria, was sick. The king was told, “The man of God has come here.” Benhadad spoke to Hazael: “Take a present in your hand, and go meet the man of God, and inquire of Yahweh by him, saying, ‘Will I recover from this sickness?’”

Hazael set out to meet Elisha, the man of God. With him he took a present that contained every good thing of Damascus, forty camels’ burden. Coming to Elisha, Hazael stood before him and delivered the message sent by Benhadad, the king of Syria: “Your son Benhadad king of Syria has sent me to you, saying, ‘Will I recover from this sickness?’”

Elisha told Hazael what to say when he returned to the king: “You will surely recover.” Yet Yahweh had shown Elisha something else, which he also told Hazael: the king would surely die.

Elisha kept his gaze fixed on Hazael until Hazael was ashamed. Then the man of God wept. Hazael asked, “Why do you weep, my lord?”

Elisha answered, “Because I know the evil that you will do to the children of Israel. You will set their strongholds on fire, and you will kill their young men with the sword, and will dash their little ones in pieces, and rip up their pregnant women.”

Hazael asked, “But what is your servant, who is but a dog, that he could do this great thing?” Elisha answered, “Yahweh has shown me that you will be king over Syria.”

Leaving Elisha, Hazael returned to his master. Benhadad asked, “What did Elisha say to you?” Hazael answered, “He told me that you would surely recover.”

The next day, Hazael took a thick cloth and dipped it in water. He spread it over Benhadad’s face, so that the king died. Hazael reigned in his place.
