Paul’s conflict for believers who had not seen his face had a clear end: that their hearts might be comforted, that they might be knit together in love, and that they might possess the full assurance of understanding. The mystery they needed to know centred upon Christ, in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. Enticing words must not beguile them from him.

They had received Christ Jesus the Lord; therefore they were to walk in him. Rooted as a living plant and built up upon a sure foundation, they were to be established in the faith which they had been taught and abound with thanksgiving. The traditions of men, vain deceit, and the rudiments of the world offered spoil rather than wisdom, for they led not unto Christ.

In Christ dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily, and believers are complete in him. He is the head of all principality and power. Their true circumcision was made without hands. They had been buried with Christ in baptism and raised with him through faith in the operation of God, who hath raised him from the dead. Though dead in sins, they were quickened together with Christ and forgiven. The handwriting that stood against them was blotted out and nailed to the cross. He spoiled principalities and powers and triumphed over them openly.

Let no man therefore judge them in meat, drink, holy days, new moons, or sabbath days. These were shadows, but the body is of Christ. Neither voluntary humility nor worshipping of angels should beguile them of their reward. Such practices sprang from a fleshly mind vainly puffed up and failed to hold the Head. From Christ the whole body receiveth nourishment, is knit together, and doth increase with the increase of God.

Since they were dead with Christ from the rudiments of the world, they were not to return to ordinances such as “Touch not, taste not, handle not.” Things which perish with use cannot impart life. Human commandments may display an appearance of wisdom through will worship, humility, and neglecting of the body, but they have no power against the satisfying of the flesh. Completeness, forgiveness, growth, and victory are found in Christ.
