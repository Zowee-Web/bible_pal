Daniel’s spirit was grieved within his body, and the visions of his head troubled him. He came near to one of those who stood by and asked him the truth concerning all this. The one he asked told him the interpretation: “These great animals, which are four, are four kings, who will arise out of the earth. But the saints of the Most High will receive the kingdom, and possess the kingdom forever, even forever and ever.”

Daniel then desired to know the truth concerning the fourth animal. It was different from all the others, exceedingly terrible, with iron teeth and bronze nails. It devoured, broke in pieces, and stamped the residue with its feet. He also asked concerning its ten horns and the other horn which came up, before which three fell. That horn had eyes and a mouth that spoke arrogantly, and its look was more stout than its fellows.

Daniel saw that the same horn made war with the saints and prevailed against them until the Ancient of Days came. Judgment was given to the saints of the Most High, and the time came that they possessed the kingdom.

The one who was explaining said that the fourth animal would be a fourth kingdom on earth, different from all the kingdoms. It would devour the whole earth, tread it down, and break it in pieces. Ten kings would arise out of this kingdom. Another would arise after them, different from the former, and would put down three kings.

He would speak words against the Most High and wear out the saints of the Most High. He would plan to change the times and the law, and they would be given into his hand until a time and times and half a time. But the judgment would be set, and they would take away his dominion, consuming and destroying it to the end. The kingdom, dominion, and greatness of the kingdoms under the whole sky would be given to the people of the saints of the Most High. His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions would serve and obey him.

Daniel ended by saying, “As for me, Daniel, my thoughts troubled me greatly, and my face was changed in me; but I kept the matter in my heart.”
