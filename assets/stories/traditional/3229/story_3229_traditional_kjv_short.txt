Daniel was grieved in his spirit in the midst of his body, and the visions of his head troubled him. He came near unto one of them that stood by and asked him the truth of all this. That one told him the interpretation: “These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth. But the saints of the most High shall take the kingdom, and possess the kingdom for ever, even for ever and ever.”

Then Daniel would know the truth of the fourth beast, diverse from all the others and exceeding dreadful. Its teeth were of iron and its nails of brass. It devoured, brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with its feet. He asked also of the ten horns in its head and of the other which came up, before whom three fell. That horn had eyes and a mouth that spake very great things, and its look was more stout than its fellows.

Daniel beheld the same horn making war with the saints and prevailing against them until the Ancient of days came. Judgment was given to the saints of the most High, and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.

The one interpreting said that the fourth beast would be the fourth kingdom upon earth, diverse from all kingdoms. It would devour the whole earth, tread it down, and break it in pieces. The ten horns were ten kings that would arise out of this kingdom. Another would arise after them, diverse from the first, and would subdue three kings.

He would speak great words against the most High, wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws. They would be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time. But the judgment would sit, and they would take away his dominion, consuming and destroying it unto the end. The kingdom, dominion, and greatness of the kingdom under the whole heaven would be given to the people of the saints of the most High, whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom. All dominions would serve and obey him.

Daniel closed by saying, “As for me Daniel, my cogitations much troubled me, and my countenance changed in me: but I kept the matter in my heart.”
