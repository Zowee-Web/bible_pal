Elisha had lain sick in the room where he died, and Joash king of Israel had come down to him from Samaria. The king had stood by the bed and wept and called out the words that Elisha himself had cried out when his master Elijah had been taken up to heaven: "My father, my father, the chariots of Israel and its horsemen!" The room was still. The old prophet looked up at him. And then he gave the king a final word.

Now Elisha became sick with the illness of which he died; and Joash the king of Israel came down to him, and wept over him, and said, "My father, my father, the chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"

Elisha said to him, "Take bow and arrows"; and he took bow and arrows for himself.

He said to the king of Israel, "Put your hand on the bow"; and he put his hand on it. Elisha laid his hands on the king's hands.

He said, "Open the window eastward"; and he opened it. Then Elisha said, "Shoot!" and he shot. He said, "Yahweh's arrow of victory, even the arrow of victory over Syria; for you will strike the Syrians in Aphek, until you have consumed them."

He said, "Take the arrows"; and he took them. He said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground"; and he struck three times, and stopped.

The man of God was angry with him, and said, "You should have struck five or six times. Then you would have struck Syria until you had consumed it; whereas now you will strike Syria just three times."

And the prophet lay back on the bed in the room where he had been sick. The king of Israel stood at the bedside with the three arrows in his hand and his face wet from the weeping over the prophet; the eastern window stood open over the country of Aphek where the Syrians had their camps; and the prophet closed his eyes a final time, with the bow laid down between them in the silent room.
