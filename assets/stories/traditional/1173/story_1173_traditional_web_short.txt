After Joshua had divided the land of Canaan among the tribes of Israel, the children of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal. Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite came forward, and he stood before Joshua among his people.

Caleb said to him, "You know the thing that the Lord spoke to Moses the man of God concerning me and concerning you in Kadesh-barnea. I was forty years old when Moses the servant of the Lord sent me from Kadesh-barnea to spy out the land. I brought him word again as it was in my heart."

He said, "Nevertheless my brothers who went up with me made the heart of the people melt. But I wholly followed the Lord my God."

He said, "Moses swore on that day, saying, 'Surely the land where you walked shall be an inheritance to you and to your children forever, because you have wholly followed the Lord my God.'"

Caleb said, "Now, behold, the Lord has kept me alive these forty-five years, from the time that the Lord spoke this word to Moses while Israel walked in the wilderness. Now, behold, I am eighty-five years old today."

He said, "I am still as strong today as I was in the day that Moses sent me. As my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war and to go out and to come in."

He said, "Now therefore give me this hill country, of which the Lord spoke in that day. For you heard in that day how the Anakim were there, and great cities fortified. It may be that the Lord will be with me, and I shall drive them out, as the Lord said."

Then Joshua blessed him, and he gave Hebron to Caleb the son of Jephunneh for an inheritance. The men of his house gathered around him, and the elders of Judah gave thanks. The morning was clear over the hill country, and Caleb went up to take the land that had been promised to him.
