Jesus went into a city called Nain.

His disciples went with him. Much people went with him also. They came up the road toward the gate of the city in the light of the afternoon.

As they drew nigh unto the gate, another multitude was coming out.

A dead man was being carried out upon the bier.

He was the only son of his mother.

His mother was a widow.

The men that bare the bier came slowly through the gate. The women of the city walked behind them weeping. The sound of mourning carried out upon the road before the procession itself appeared.

And when the Lord saw her, he had compassion on her.

He came nigh unto her.

He said unto her, "Weep not."

The widow looked upon him, but the men that bare the bier kept walking.

Then Jesus stepped beside the bier and laid his hand upon it.

They that bare him stood still where they stood in the gate.

The multitude behind Jesus became quiet. The mourning women became quiet. The road without the city became still round about the bier.

Jesus beheld the young man that lay there wrapped for burial.

"Young man," he said, "I say unto thee, Arise."

He that was dead sat up.

He began to speak.

The hands of the men that bare the bier trembled beneath it. The widow stood beholding her son, as though she could not yet move toward him.

Jesus delivered him unto his mother.

Then the widow came forward and took hold of her son with both hands.

There came a fear on all the people there.

They glorified God, saying, "A great prophet is risen up among us," and, "God hath visited his people."

And the procession that had come out from Nain in mourning went not on unto the tomb that day.
