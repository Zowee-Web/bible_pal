Jesus went to a city called Nain.

His disciples went with him. A great multitude went with him also. They came up the road toward the gate of the city in the light of the afternoon.

As they drew near to the gate, another multitude was coming out.

A dead man was being carried out on the bier.

He was the only son of his mother.

His mother was a widow.

The men bearing the bier came slowly through the gate. The women of the city walked behind them weeping. The sound of mourning carried out onto the road before the procession itself appeared.

And when the Lord saw her, he had compassion on her.

He came near to her.

He said, "Don't cry."

The widow looked at him, but the men carrying the bier kept walking.

Then Jesus stepped beside the bier and laid his hand on it.

The bearers stopped where they stood in the gate.

The crowd behind Jesus became quiet. The mourning women became quiet. The road outside the city became still around the bier.

Jesus looked at the young man who lay there wrapped for burial.

"Young man," he said, "I tell you, arise."

The dead man sat up.

He began to speak.

The hands of the men carrying the bier trembled beneath it. The widow stood looking at her son as though she could not yet move toward him.

Jesus gave him back to his mother.

Then the widow came forward and took hold of her son with both hands.

Fear took hold of all the people there.

They glorified God, saying, "A great prophet has arisen among us," and, "God has visited his people."

And the procession that had come out from Nain in mourning did not go on to the tomb that day.
