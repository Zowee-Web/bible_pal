In the sixth month, God sent the angel Gabriel to a town in the hills of Galilee. The town was small. It was called Nazareth. Stone houses clustered along narrow dirt paths. Olive trees grew on the slopes around the village. The day was ordinary. Smoke drifted from cooking fires. Children moved between the houses. Somewhere in the town, a young woman named Mary was going about her day.

Mary was pledged to be married to a man named Joseph, from the house of David. She was a virgin. Her life, up until now, had been small and quiet — the ordinary shape of a young woman in a country village.

Then the angel came to her.

He appeared in the place where she was. Gabriel stood before her, and he spoke.

"Rejoice, you highly favored one," he said. "The Lord is with you. Blessed are you among women."

Mary was greatly troubled at his words. She did not move. She did not speak. She stood there and tried to understand what kind of greeting this could be.

The angel saw her trouble, and he spoke again.

"Do not be afraid, Mary," he said. "For you have found favor with God."

His voice was steady. His words were kind.

"Behold, you will conceive in your womb, and bring forth a son. You will call his name Jesus. He will be great. He will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his father David. He will reign over the house of Jacob forever. There will be no end to his kingdom."

Mary listened. Her hands were quiet at her sides.

"How can this be," she said, "seeing I am a virgin?"

The angel answered her gently.

"The Holy Spirit will come on you," he said, "and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore the holy one who is born from you will be called the Son of God."

He paused.

"Behold, Elizabeth your relative — she too has conceived a son in her old age. This is the sixth month with her who was called barren. For nothing will be impossible with God."

The room grew still. Outside, the wind moved through the village. Somewhere a door closed softly. Somewhere a child called to another and then fell quiet.

Mary stood in the light. She breathed in, and she breathed out.

Then she spoke.

"Behold, the servant of the Lord," she said. "Be it to me according to your word."

And the angel departed from her.
