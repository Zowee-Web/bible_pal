She was not chosen for that she was mighty. She was not chosen for that she was great. She was a young woman in a small town, living a quiet life, doing the manner of work that none recordeth.

And yet the word came unto her. Not unto a priest. Not unto a scribe. Not unto one prepared for such a thing. Unto her.

What standeth out is not the tidings themselves — it is what she did with them. She asked an honest question. She sat with the weight of what could not be understood. And then she gave her answer. Not because she comprehended, but because she was willing.

There are seasons when life asketh of us a thing that seemeth without reason. When the answer is not certainty but trust. When the bravest word a soul can speak is simply: let it be.
