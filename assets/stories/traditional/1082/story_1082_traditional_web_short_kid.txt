In a little town called Nazareth, there lived a young woman named Mary. She was kind and quiet, and she was going to marry a man named Joseph.

One day, something amazing happened. An angel appeared right in front of her! His name was Gabriel, and God had sent him.

"Hello, Mary!" the angel said. "God is with you. You are very special."

Mary didn't know what to think. She was a little scared. Why would an angel come to see her?

The angel could tell she was afraid. "Don't worry, Mary," he said. "God is very happy with you."

Then the angel told her something incredible.

"You are going to have a baby boy," he said. "You will name him Jesus. He will be very great. He will be called the Son of God. He will be a king, and his kingdom will last forever and ever."

Mary listened carefully. Then she asked a question.

"How can that happen?" she said. "I'm not married yet."

The angel said, "God's Holy Spirit will make it happen. Nothing is impossible with God."

He also told her that her older relative Elizabeth was going to have a baby too, even though everyone thought she was too old. God was doing amazing things!

Mary thought about all of this. Then she said something very brave.

"I am God's servant," she said. "Let it happen just the way you said."

And then the angel left. Mary was alone again in her little room, but everything was different now.
