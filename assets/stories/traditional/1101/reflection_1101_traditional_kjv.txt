He had sat beside that road for years. The same dust, the same sounds, the same place at the margin of things. When he heard that Jesus passed by, he did not wait to be noticed. He cried out. When they bade him hold his peace, he cried the louder.

There is something arresting in what followed. Jesus asked what he desired. The answer was plain and direct — no elaborate petition, no bargaining. Merely the thing itself: that he might see.

Sometimes the hardest part is not the asking but the believing that it matters — that a voice from the roadside is not too small to reach the one who passeth by.
