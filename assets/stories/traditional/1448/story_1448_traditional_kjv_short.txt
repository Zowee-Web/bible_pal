David came up alone unto the cave of Adullam.

He went in through the mouth thereof. The stone of the hills was cool against his shoulder. The day was hot without. The day was quiet within.

He sat down against the wall of the cave. He had fled from Saul. He had fled from Achish at Gath. He had come away with no men.

The day wore on. He slept not. He hearkened for footsteps at the mouth of the cave that came not.

He abode in the cave. The sun moved across the mouth thereof. The water in the skin grew low. He went down unto the spring. He filled it again. He came back.

On the morrow his brethren came. They came down from the hills and found him there. All his father's house came with them. They sat with him in the cave.

Word went forth from the cave.

Then others began to come.

A man that could not pay his debts came down unto the cave. A man whose master had cast him out came down unto the cave. A man whose house had fallen sick came down unto the cave. They had heard where David was. They came in twos and threes through the rocks.

Every one that was in distress came. Every one that was in debt came. Every one that was discontented came.

By the end of the days, they were four hundred.

David was no longer alone in the cave.

He arose among them. He became a captain over them. The cave was full of voices in the heat of the day. The four hundred men of Adullam were with him in the hills.
