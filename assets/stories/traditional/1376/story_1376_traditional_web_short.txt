The cloud had filled the temple in the days before. The priests had brought the ark into the most holy place under the wings of the great cherubim of olive wood, and the offerings had gone up day after day, and Solomon had stood and knelt before the brazen altar in the inner court — his hands spread out toward the heavens — and prayed for the people he was about to bless. The prayer had been long. The fire of the altar had burned down low while he prayed. And then the king arose.

It was so, that when Solomon had finished praying all this prayer and supplication to Yahweh, he arose from before Yahweh's altar, from kneeling on his knees with his hands spread out toward heaven.

He stood, and blessed all the assembly of Israel with a loud voice, saying,

"Blessed be Yahweh, who has given rest to his people Israel, according to all that he promised. There has not failed one word of all his good promise, which he promised by Moses his servant.

May Yahweh our God be with us, as he was with our fathers. Let him not leave us or forsake us;

that he may incline our hearts to him, to walk in all his ways, and to keep his commandments, and his statutes, and his ordinances, which he commanded our fathers.

Let these my words, with which I have made supplication before Yahweh, be near to Yahweh our God day and night, that he may maintain the cause of his servant, and the cause of his people Israel, as every day requires;

that all the peoples of the earth may know that Yahweh himself is God. There is no one else.

"Let your heart therefore be perfect with Yahweh our God, to walk in his statutes, and to keep his commandments, as it is today."

And the king lowered his hands. The assembly of Israel stood before him in the great court — the elders at the front, the heads of the houses behind them, and behind those the men and women of the city, and behind those again the peoples of the outlying districts who had come up for the dedication. The trumpets of the priests waited in their hands; the oxen and sheep stood ready for the offering; and the words "there has not failed one word of all his good promise" stayed in the air over the court of the new house.
