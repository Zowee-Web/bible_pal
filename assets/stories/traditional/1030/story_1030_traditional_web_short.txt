God spoke to Samuel. "How long will you be sad about Saul? I have rejected him as king over Israel. Fill your horn with oil and go. I am sending you to Jesse in Bethlehem. I have chosen a king among his sons."

Samuel was worried. "If Saul hears about it, he will try to hurt me," he said.

God told Samuel to take a young cow and say he was coming to offer a sacrifice. God would show him what to do.

So Samuel went to Bethlehem. When he arrived, the elders of the town came to meet him, trembling. They asked, "Do you come in peace?"

Samuel said, "In peace. I have come to sacrifice to the Lord." He invited Jesse and his sons to come.

When they arrived, Samuel saw the oldest son, Eliab. He was tall and strong. Samuel thought, "Surely this is the one the Lord has chosen."

But the Lord said to Samuel, "Do not look at how he looks or how tall he is. I have rejected him. The Lord does not see the way people see. People look at the outside. The Lord looks at the heart."

Jesse brought his second son, Abinadab, and passed him before Samuel. "The Lord has not chosen this one either," Samuel said. Then came Shammah. Same answer. One by one, seven of Jesse's sons walked before Samuel. God said no to every single one.

Samuel turned to Jesse. "Are these all the sons you have?"

Jesse paused. "There is still the youngest," he said. "He is out watching the sheep."

"Send for him," Samuel said. "We will not sit down until he comes."

Jesse sent someone to get him. The boy came in from the fields. He had rosy cheeks and bright eyes. His name was David.

The Lord said to Samuel, "Rise and anoint him. This is the one."

Samuel took his horn of oil and poured it over David's head right there, in front of all his brothers. The oil ran down David's hair and face. From that day on, the Spirit of the Lord came powerfully upon David.
