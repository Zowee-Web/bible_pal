Israel had served Moab for eighteen years before they cried out to Yahweh. Yahweh raised up Ehud, a left-handed man who carried his sword on his right thigh. The account tells the killing plainly, then follows Ehud's escape, Israel's victory, and the land's eighty years of rest. Eighteen years under Moab, then eighty years of rest. What does it take to cry out after a long season of bondage?
