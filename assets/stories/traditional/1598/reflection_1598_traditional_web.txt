Israel had served Moab eighteen years before they cried out, and the deliverer Yahweh raised up was an unlikely one — a left-handed man who could hide a sword where no one thought to look. The account does not linger to admire the killing; it moves quickly to a freed people and a land at rest. Eighteen years of bondage, and then eighty of rest. What has kept its grip on you far longer than it should have?
