Israel had served Moab eighteen years before they cried unto the Lord. The Lord raised up Ehud, a lefthanded man who carried his dagger upon his right thigh. The account tells the killing plainly, then follows Ehud's escape, Israel's victory, and the land's fourscore years of rest. Eighteen years under Moab, then fourscore years of rest. What doth it take to cry out after a long season of bondage?
