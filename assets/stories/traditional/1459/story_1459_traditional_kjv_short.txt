The foundation of the second temple was laid in the second year after the people of Judah had returned from Babylon.

The builders had cleared the place. The stones had been hewn. The first stone was set down in his place that day.

The priests came forth in their apparel, with the silver trumpets in their hands. The Levites — the sons of Asaph — came forth with the cymbals. They stood at the foundation where the stone had been laid, according to the ordinance of David king of Israel that had been given them long ago.

They began to praise the Lord.

They sang together by course, the priests calling and the Levites answering, in the words which the people had sung in the first temple — "For he is good, for his mercy endureth for ever toward Israel."

The trumpets sounded. The cymbals rang.

The people shouted with a great shout when they praised the Lord, because the foundation of the house of the Lord had been laid.

But among them, many of the priests and Levites and chief of the fathers, who were ancient men, that had seen the first house, when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, wept with a loud voice.

These were the men that remembered Solomon's temple. They had beheld the gold of the walls, and the brass of the sea, and the cherubims of olive wood, and the lampstands burning in the holy place. They had seen Nebuchadnezzar's army break it down in their own days.

They wept at the laying of this foundation, because they had known that which had been lost, and because the foundation under their feet was lesser than the foundation they had once beheld.

And the young men shouted for joy at the same hour. They had not seen the first house. They had been born in the years after the carrying away. The foundation under their feet was the first foundation they had ever known.

The shouting and the weeping rose up together.

The people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping of the people: for the people shouted with a loud shout, and the noise was heard afar off.
