The five men of the king came down from the palace unto the second quarter of Jerusalem.

They came unto the house of Huldah the prophetess.

She was the wife of Shallum, the son of Tikvah, the son of Harhas — the keeper of the wardrobe. She dwelt in the second quarter of the city, where the houses of them that served the king's household were.

The five came unto her door — Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Achbor the son of Micaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asaiah the king's servant.

They told her what had come to pass.

They told her of the book of the law that had been found in the house of the Lord. They told her of the king's rending of his garments. They told her of his command to inquire of the Lord for him, and for the people, and for all Judah.

Huldah heard them.

She arose not to answer. She lifted not up her voice. She gave them the Lord's word from where she sat in her own house.

She said, "Thus saith the Lord God of Israel — tell the man that sent you unto me, Thus saith the Lord: Behold, I will bring evil upon this place, and upon the inhabitants thereof, even all the words of the book which the king of Judah hath read. Because they have forsaken me, and have burned incense unto other gods, that they might provoke me to anger with all the works of their hands; therefore my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and shall not be quenched."

She paused.

Then she gave them the second word — the word for the king himself.

"But to the king of Judah, who sent you to inquire of the Lord, thus shall ye say unto him — Thus saith the Lord God of Israel: Concerning the words which thou hast heard, because thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before the Lord, when thou heardest what I spake against this place, and against the inhabitants thereof — and thou hast rent thy clothes, and wept before me — I also have heard thee, saith the Lord. Behold therefore, I will gather thee unto thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered into thy grave in peace; and thine eyes shall not see all the evil which I will bring upon this place."

She made an end.

The five men of the king stood still in her chamber for a moment. Then they went forth out of the second quarter of the city, and back up unto the palace, and they brought the message unto the king.
