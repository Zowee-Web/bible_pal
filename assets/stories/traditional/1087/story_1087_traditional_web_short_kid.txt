A man named John was baptizing people in the Jordan River. People came from all over to be baptized. They walked into the water, and John dipped them under and brought them back up.

One day, Jesus came to the river. He walked right up to John and asked to be baptized.

John was surprised. "I should be baptized by you!" he said. "Why are you coming to me?"

Jesus said gently, "It's the right thing to do. Please, let it happen."

So John agreed. He put his hands on Jesus and lowered him into the water. The river closed over Jesus — cool and quiet. For a moment, everything was still.

Then John brought him back up.

And something incredible happened.

The sky opened up — like a curtain being pulled back. The Spirit of God came down, gentle as a dove, and rested on Jesus.

Then a voice came from heaven. It said:

"This is my Son, whom I love. I am very pleased with him."

Everyone standing by the river heard it. John stood still in the water. Jesus stood in the shallows with his face turned up toward the sky.

It was the moment everything began.
