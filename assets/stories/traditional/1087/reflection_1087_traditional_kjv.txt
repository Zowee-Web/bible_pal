There are moments that mark a beginning — not because a thing of great note cometh to pass, but because a thing is declared. A door openeth. A name is spoken. A purpose is confirmed aloud, before witnesses, in a manner that cannot be undone.

Jesus had no need to be baptized. He was not confessing sin. He was not beginning again. Yet he stepped into the water — into the same river, alongside the same people, doing the very thing all others did. And in that common act, something uncommon was spoken.

Sometimes the most weighty moments in a life bear not the appearance of weight. They appear as standing in a river. As saying yes to a thing not fully understood. As taking a step that seemeth small, yet changeth all that followeth.
