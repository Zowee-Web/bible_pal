There came two angels to Sodom at even. Lot sat in the gate, and rose and bowed his face to the ground.

"My lords, turn in into your servant's house. Tarry the night, and rise early and go your ways."

"Nay," they said. "We will abide in the street all night."

He pressed upon them greatly, and they turned in. He made them a feast of unleavened bread, and they ate.

Before they lay down, the men of the city compassed the house round, old and young.

They called unto Lot, requiring that he bring the men out unto them.

Lot went out at the door, and shut it after him.

"I pray you, brethren, do not so wickedly." He offered them his own two daughters instead. "Only unto these men do nothing; they came under the shadow of my roof."

"Stand back," they said. "This fellow came in to sojourn, and he will needs be a judge. We will deal worse with thee than with them."

They pressed sore upon Lot, and came near to break the door.

Then hands were put forth and pulled Lot into the house and shut the door, and smote the crowd with blindness till they wearied to find it.

The angels bade him bring out whomsoever he had — son in law, sons, daughters. "We will destroy this place: the cry of them is waxen great before the Lord, who hath sent us."

Lot went out unto the men which married his daughters. "Up, get out of this place; the Lord will destroy this city."

He seemed as one that mocked.

In the morning the angels hastened him. "Arise, take thy wife and thy two daughters, lest thou be consumed in the city's iniquity."

He lingered.

So the men laid hold upon his hand, and his wife's hand, and his two daughters' hands, the Lord being merciful unto him, and set him without the city.

"Escape for thy life. Look not behind thee, neither stay in the plain. Escape to the mountain."

Lot begged a smaller thing: he could not reach the mountain, but a little city was near. "Oh, let me escape thither, and my soul shall live."

"See, I have accepted thee in this also. I will not overthrow that city. Haste — I cannot do any thing till thou come."

The name of the city was called Zoar. The sun was risen when Lot came in.

Then the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrah brimstone and fire out of heaven, and overthrew those cities and all the plain and all that dwelt therein and all that grew there.

His wife looked back from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt.

Abraham gat up early to the place where he stood before the Lord, and looked toward Sodom and Gomorrah. The smoke of the country went up as from a furnace.

When God destroyed the cities of the plain, God remembered Abraham, and sent Lot out of the overthrow.
