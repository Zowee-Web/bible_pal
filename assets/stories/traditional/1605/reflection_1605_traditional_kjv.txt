Every labourer received a full day's wage, even those who came at the eleventh hour. The ones who had borne the burden and heat of the day were not cheated; they received exactly what they had agreed to. What galled them was seeing the latecomers paid the same. "Is thine eye evil, because I am good?" asked the householder. When hast thou found it hard to accept another labourer receiving the same wage?
