Jesus said, "The kingdom of heaven is like a landowner who went out early in the morning to hire laborers for his vineyard. He agreed with them on a denarius for the day and sent them into his vineyard.

"About the third hour he went out and saw others standing idle in the marketplace. 'You also go into the vineyard,' he told them, 'and I will pay you whatever is right.' So they went. He did the same about the sixth hour and the ninth hour. And about the eleventh hour he went out and found still others standing around. 'Why have you stood here idle all day?' he asked. 'Because no one has hired us,' they said. 'You also go into the vineyard,' he told them.

"When evening came, the owner said to his foreman, 'Call the laborers and pay them their wages, beginning with the last and going on to the first.' Those hired at the eleventh hour came, and each received a denarius. So when the first came, they expected to receive more; but each of them also received a denarius. When they got it, they grumbled against the landowner. 'These last men worked only one hour,' they said, 'and you have made them equal to us, who have borne the burden of the day and the scorching heat.'

"But he answered one of them, 'Friend, I am doing you no wrong. Did you not agree with me on a denarius? Take what belongs to you and go. I want to give the last man the same as I gave you. Am I not allowed to do what I choose with what is mine? Or is your eye envious because I am generous?'

"So the last will be first, and the first last. For many are called, but few are chosen."
