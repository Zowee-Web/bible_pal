The kingdom of heaven, Jesus said, is like unto a householder. Early in the morning he went out to hire labourers into his vineyard. He and the labourers agreed together upon a penny for the day, and he sent them in.

At the third hour he went out again, and there were others standing idle in the marketplace.

"Go ye also into the vineyard," he said unto them, "and whatsoever is right I will give you."

And they went their way. At the sixth hour he came again and did likewise; and at the ninth hour, likewise again.

Then, about the eleventh hour, he went out again. Others were standing there yet.

"Why stand ye here all the day idle?" he said.

"Because no man hath hired us," they answered.

"Go ye also into the vineyard, and whatsoever is right, that shall ye receive."

When even was come, the lord of the vineyard called his steward to him. The labourers were to be paid their hire, he said, beginning from the last unto the first.

So they came that were hired at the eleventh hour, and every man of them received a penny.

Then came the first, who had gone in at the morning. They supposed that they should have received more. Every man of them received a penny.

Then they murmured against the householder.

"These last have wrought but one hour, and thou hast made them equal unto us, which have borne the burden and heat of the day."

The householder answered one of the murmurers.

"Friend, I do thee no wrong," he said. "Didst not thou agree with me for a penny? Take that thine is, and go thy way."

And he said unto him, "I will give unto this last, even as unto thee. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own? Is thine eye evil, because I am good?"

And Jesus said, So the last shall be first, and the first last: for many be called, but few chosen.
