The apostles returned unto Jesus and declared unto him all things. What deeds they had wrought. What doctrine they had taught. The words they had spoken in the towns, and what manner of answer the people had given.

And Jesus said unto them, "Come ye apart into a solitary place, and rest awhile."

For there were many coming and going, such that they had no leisure so much as to take bread. And they departed by ship unto a desolate place, they alone.

But the multitudes beheld them going. Men recognized Jesus from the shore, and they ran afoot out of every city round about. They pressed along the coast with great haste and came unto the far side before the vessel drew near. And when the hull ground upon the shallows, a great company was already gathered upon the bank.

Jesus came forth and looked upon them. Hundreds of faces. Thousands. They stood without order, without a shepherd, as sheep scattered upon open ground where none doth tend them. And compassion stirred within him, and he began to teach them. He taught them many things, and the hour grew late.

The sun descended low. The shadows stretched themselves long upon the grass. Then came his disciples unto him, saying, "This place is desolate, and the day is now far spent. Send the people hence, that they may go into the villages and farms lying round about and buy for themselves bread, for they have nothing."

And Jesus said unto them, "Give ye them to eat."

They looked upon him and were astonished. "Shall we go and lay out two hundred denarii upon bread, and set it before them?"

He said, "How many loaves have ye? Go and see."

And they went among the people and returned again. "Five loaves. And two fish."

Then Jesus commanded that all should sit down by companies upon the green grass. And the people sat in their ranks — some by hundreds, some by fifty. And the hillside was filled with colour, row upon row across the bright slope.

And Jesus took the five loaves and the two fish. He lifted his eyes toward heaven and gave thanks. Then he brake the loaves and gave the pieces unto his disciples, and they carried them forth among the people. The two fish also he divided among them all.

And every soul did eat. And every soul was filled.

Afterward the disciples passed among the companies, gathering up that which remained. They took up twelve baskets, heavy and full, laden with broken bread and fragments of fish. And they that had eaten were about five thousand men, seated yet in their ranks upon the grass, the empty baskets of the meal still warm beside them.
