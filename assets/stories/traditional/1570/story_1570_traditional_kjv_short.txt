The same day Jesus went forth from the house, and sat down by the sea side. And so great were the multitudes that gathered unto him, that he entered into a ship, and sat there, while all the people stood upon the shore. And he spake many things unto them in parables.

Behold, said he, a sower went forth to sow. And as he sowed, some seeds fell by the way side, and the fowls came and devoured them. Some fell upon stony places, where was little earth; and they sprung up quickly, but when the sun was risen they were scorched, and having no root, they withered away. Some fell among thorns; and the thorns grew, and choked them. But other fell upon good ground, and brought forth fruit — some an hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty. Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.

And when the disciples asked him wherefore he spake in parables, he answered that unto them it was given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but not unto the multitude. For whosoever hath, to him shall more be given, till he abound; but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken even that he hath. As for the crowds, said he, seeing they saw not, and hearing they heard not, neither did they understand; their heart was waxed gross, their ears were dull of hearing, and their eyes they had closed, lest at any time they should see, and hear, and understand, and turn, and be healed. But blessed are your eyes, said he, for they see; and your ears, for they hear; for many prophets and righteous men have longed to behold that which ye behold, and beheld it not, and to hear that which ye hear, and heard it not.
