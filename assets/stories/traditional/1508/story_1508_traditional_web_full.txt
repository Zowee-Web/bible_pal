Nahash the Ammonite came up against Jabesh-Gilead. He besieged the city. The men of Jabesh sent to him: make a covenant with us, and we will serve you.

Nahash answered them: on this condition I will make a covenant. I will thrust out all your right eyes and lay it as a reproach upon all Israel.

The elders of Jabesh asked seven days. They would send messengers throughout the borders of Israel. If no one came to save them, they would come out to him. Nahash gave them the seven days. He waited at the walls of the city.

The messengers went out across the country. They went to Gibeah of Saul among other places. They told the news in the hearing of the people. The people lifted up their voices and wept. The matter was very heavy upon them. They did not know what to do.

Saul came after the herd out of the field that evening. He had been plowing all the morning behind the oxen. He heard the people weeping when he came up to the gate. He asked what the news was. They told him about Nahash. They told him about the men of Jabesh. They told him about the seven days.

The Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard the words. His anger was kindled greatly. He took a yoke of oxen there in the place where he had been plowing. He hewed them in pieces with his own hand. He sent the pieces throughout all the borders of Israel by the hand of messengers. Word went with them: whoever does not come out after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done to his oxen.

The fear of the LORD fell on the people. They came out as one man. Three hundred thousand of Israel. Thirty thousand of Judah.

Saul gathered them at Bezek. He numbered them there. He sent word to the men of Jabesh: tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot, you shall have help. The men of Jabesh sent back word to Nahash that they would come out to him the next day, on the appointed seventh day. Nahash believed them. He kept the siege through the night without setting an outer watch toward the north.

Saul divided the people into three companies. They went up in the night. The country was dark. The men carried their weapons without speaking. They came down on the Ammonite camp from three sides while it was still dark.

They came into the middle of the camp of the Ammonites in the morning watch. The Ammonites were caught between the three companies before the sky was fully light. They tried to gather to their captains. The captains were already cut off from the men. The men were cut off from the captains. They struck the Ammonites until the heat of the day. The Ammonite ranks broke before the sun was high. Those that remained were scattered. Two of them were not left together.
