Nahash the Ammonite came up against Jabesh-Gilead. He besieged the city. The men of Jabesh sent to him: make a covenant with us, and we will serve you.

Nahash answered them: on this condition I will make a covenant. I will thrust out all your right eyes and lay it as a reproach upon all Israel.

The elders of Jabesh asked seven days. They would send messengers throughout the borders of Israel. If no one came to save them, they would come out to him.

The messengers came to Gibeah of Saul. They told the news in the hearing of the people. The people lifted up their voices and wept.

Saul came after the herd out of the field. He had been plowing all the morning behind the oxen. He heard the people weeping when he came up to the gate. He asked what the news was. They told him.

The Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard the words. His anger was kindled greatly. He took a yoke of oxen. He hewed them in pieces. He sent the pieces throughout all the borders of Israel by the hand of messengers. Word went with them: whoever does not come out after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done to his oxen.

The fear of the LORD fell on the people. They came out as one man. Three hundred thousand of Israel. Thirty thousand of Judah.

Saul gathered them at Bezek. He sent word to the men of Jabesh: tomorrow, by the time the sun is hot, you shall have help.

He divided the people into three companies. They came into the middle of the camp of the Ammonites in the morning watch. They slew the Ammonites until the heat of the day. Those that remained were scattered. Two of them were not left together.
