Nahash the Ammonite came up against Jabesh-gilead. He encamped against the city. The men of Jabesh said unto him: make a covenant with us, and we will serve thee.

Nahash answered them: on this condition will I make a covenant with you. I will thrust out all your right eyes and lay it for a reproach upon all Israel.

The elders of Jabesh asked seven days respite. They would send messengers unto all the coasts of Israel. If there were no man to save them, they would come out to him. Nahash gave them the seven days. He tarried at the walls of the city.

The messengers went forth across the country. They came to Gibeah of Saul among other places. They told the tidings in the ears of the people. The people lifted up their voices and wept. The matter was very heavy upon them. They knew not what to do.

Saul came after the herd out of the field. He had been plowing all the morning behind the oxen. He heard the people weeping when he came up to the gate. He said: what aileth the people that they weep? They told him about Nahash. They told him about the men of Jabesh. They told him about the seven days.

The Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard those tidings. His anger was kindled greatly. He took a yoke of oxen there in the place where he had been plowing. He hewed them in pieces with his own hand. He sent the pieces throughout all the coasts of Israel by the hands of messengers. The word went with them: whosoever cometh not forth after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done unto his oxen.

The fear of the LORD fell on the people. They came out with one consent. Three hundred thousand of Israel. Thirty thousand of Judah.

Saul gathered them together at Bezek. He numbered them there. He sent word unto the men of Jabesh: tomorrow, by that time the sun be hot, ye shall have help. The men of Jabesh sent back word unto Nahash that they would come out unto him on the morrow, on the appointed seventh day. Nahash believed them. He kept the siege through the night without setting an outer watch toward the north.

Saul put the people in three companies. They went up in the night. The country was dark. The men bare their weapons without speaking. They came down upon the host of the Ammonites from three sides while it was yet dark.

They came into the midst of the host of the Ammonites in the morning watch. The Ammonites were caught between the three companies before the sky was fully light. They sought to gather unto their captains. The captains were already cut off from the men. The men were cut off from the captains. They slew the Ammonites until the heat of the day. The Ammonite ranks brake before the sun was high. They that remained were scattered. Two of them were not left together.
