Nahash the Ammonite came up against Jabesh-gilead. He encamped against the city. The men of Jabesh said unto him: make a covenant with us, and we will serve thee.

Nahash answered them: on this condition will I make a covenant with you. I will thrust out all your right eyes and lay it for a reproach upon all Israel.

The elders of Jabesh asked seven days respite. They would send messengers unto all the coasts of Israel. If there were no man to save them, they would come out to him.

The messengers came to Gibeah of Saul. They told the tidings in the ears of the people. The people lifted up their voices and wept.

Saul came after the herd out of the field. He had been plowing. He heard the people weeping. He said: what aileth the people that they weep? They told him.

The Spirit of God came upon Saul when he heard those tidings. His anger was kindled greatly. He took a yoke of oxen. He hewed them in pieces. He sent them throughout all the coasts of Israel by the hands of messengers. The word went with them: whosoever cometh not forth after Saul and after Samuel, so shall it be done unto his oxen.

The fear of the LORD fell on the people. They came out with one consent. Three hundred thousand of Israel. Thirty thousand of Judah.

Saul gathered them together at Bezek. He sent word unto the men of Jabesh: tomorrow, by that time the sun be hot, ye shall have help.

He put the people in three companies. They came into the midst of the host of the Ammonites in the morning watch. They slew the Ammonites until the heat of the day. They that remained were scattered. Two of them were not left together.
