The walls of Jericho stood ahead of Israel, silent and shut. Joshua had walked out from the camp alone, to look at the city in the morning. The gates were closed. No watchman called from the towers. The plain between him and the wall was empty.

When Joshua was by Jericho, he lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, a man stood in front of him with his sword drawn in his hand.

Joshua went to him and said to him, "Are you for us, or for our enemies?"

He said, "No; but I have come now as commander of Yahweh's army."

Joshua fell on his face to the earth, and worshiped, and asked him, "What does my lord say to his servant?"

The commander of Yahweh's army said to Joshua, "Take off your sandals, for the place on which you stand is holy."

Joshua did so.

His sandals lay in the dust beside him. He kept his face to the ground. The man with the sword stood and did not move. Far off, the city of Jericho was silent. The wind moved across the plain. The morning was very still. Then it was quiet again.
