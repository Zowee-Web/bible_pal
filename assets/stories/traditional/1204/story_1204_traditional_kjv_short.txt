In the days of the sabbath, the Levites gathered together in the courts of the temple, and they took up the song of the sabbath day. The lamps were burning in the house of the Lord, and the morning was clear above the city.

The chief musician lifted up his voice, and he sang the song that was written for the sabbath day.

He said, "It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, and to sing praises unto thy name, O most High: To shew forth thy lovingkindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night, upon an instrument of ten strings, and upon the psaltery, upon the harp with a solemn sound."

He sang, "For thou, Lord, hast made me glad through thy work: I will triumph in the works of thy hands. O Lord, how great are thy works! and thy thoughts are very deep."

The musician played upon the lyre, and the strings sounded in the courts of the house. The Levites sang together, and their voices rose into the morning.

He said, "A brutish man knoweth not; neither doth a fool understand this. When the wicked spring as the grass, and when all the workers of iniquity do flourish; it is that they shall be destroyed for ever. But thou, Lord, art most high for evermore."

He said, "The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree: he shall grow like a cedar in Lebanon. Those that be planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of our God."

He sang, "They shall still bring forth fruit in old age; they shall be fat and flourishing; To shew that the Lord is upright: he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him."

The Levites ended the song, and the harps grew still in their hands. The lamps burned softly along the walls of the temple, and the people which were gathered for the sabbath stood in the courts and gave thanks.

The morning was warm above Jerusalem, and the day of rest was kept by the people, and the words of the song were sung again at the evening sacrifice.

The sabbath came and went over the temple, and the words of the song remained, sung in the days that came after by the children of Israel.
