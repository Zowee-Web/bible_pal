Then Amalek came and fought with Israel at Rephidim.

Moses said to Joshua, "Choose men for us, and go out, fight with Amalek. Tomorrow I will stand on the top of the hill, with God's rod in my hand."

Joshua did as Moses had told him. He chose his men. He went out to fight.

Moses went up the hill with Aaron and Hur.

He stood at the top with the rod of God in his hand.

He held up his hand.

When his hand was up, Israel prevailed in the valley below. When his hand came down, Amalek prevailed.

He held the hand up.

The morning went on. The sun moved across the sky. The dust came up from the valley where Joshua and the men of Israel were fighting. The shouting carried up to the top of the hill, and faded, and came up again.

Moses' hand began to grow heavy.

He let it drop, and Amalek prevailed.

Aaron and Hur saw it.

They took a stone, and they put it under Moses, and Moses sat down on the stone. Then Aaron stood on one side of him, and Hur stood on the other side. Aaron held up one of Moses' hands. Hur held up the other.

His hands were steady from that hour until the going down of the sun.

Joshua defeated Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.

And the three men came down off the hill, with the rod still in Moses' hand, at the going down of the sun.
