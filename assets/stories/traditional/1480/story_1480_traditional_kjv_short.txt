Then came Amalek, and fought with Israel in Rephidim.

And Moses said unto Joshua, "Choose us out men, and go out, fight with Amalek. To morrow I will stand on the top of the hill with the rod of God in mine hand."

So Joshua did as Moses had said to him. He chose out men. He went out to fight.

Moses went up the hill with Aaron and Hur.

He stood on the top with the rod of God in his hand.

He held up his hand.

And it came to pass, when Moses held up his hand, that Israel prevailed: and when he let down his hand, Amalek prevailed.

He held the hand up.

The morning went on. The sun moved across the sky. The dust came up from the valley where Joshua and the men of Israel were fighting. The shouting carried up to the top of the hill, and faded, and came up again.

Moses' hands were heavy.

He let his hand drop, and Amalek prevailed.

Aaron and Hur saw it.

They took a stone, and they put it under him, and he sat thereon. Then Aaron stood on one side, and Hur stood on the other side. And they stayed up his hands, the one on the one side, and the other on the other side.

His hands were steady until the going down of the sun.

And Joshua discomfited Amalek and his people with the edge of the sword.

And the three men came down from the hill, with the rod yet in Moses' hand, at the going down of the sun.
