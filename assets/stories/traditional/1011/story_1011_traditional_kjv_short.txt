A warm afternoon sun lit the hillside. Green grass covered the slopes. Wildflowers grew in patches of yellow and purple between the rocks. A soft breeze bore the savour of earth and herbs across the open land.

Jesus sat upon the hillside with his disciples gathered close round about him. Some of them leaned upon their elbows in the grass. Others sat cross-legged with their sandals dusty from the road. A few birds lighted nigh, pecking at seeds in the dirt.

Jesus beheld the birds hopping along the ground. He pointed unto them and spake. "Consider the ravens," he said. "They sow not. They gather not grain into barns. God feedeth them." He paused. The birds tilted their small heads and pecked again at the earth. "How much more are ye of value than the fowls," Jesus said.

He opened his hands as he spake. "Which of you with taking thought can add unto his stature one cubit?" The disciples hearkened. The wind moved through the tall grass round about them.

Then Jesus pointed unto the flowers growing along the hillside. Bright petals swayed in the breeze. "Consider the lilies how they grow," he said. "They toil not. They spin not." He touched one of the stems gently. "But I say unto you, that Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these."

A few of the disciples looked down upon the flowers near their feet. Small purple blossoms grew between the stones, open and still.

"If then God so clothe the grass of the field," Jesus continued, "which is to day, and to morrow is cast into the oven — how much more shall he clothe you?" His voice was steady and clear in the open air.

He looked round about upon each of them. "And seek not ye what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink," he said. "Neither be ye of doubtful mind." He folded his hands together. "For all these things do the nations of the world seek after. But your Father knoweth that ye have need of these things."

A bird lifted off the ground and flew low over the wildflowers. Its wings made a quiet sound against the air.

Jesus spake again. "But rather seek ye the kingdom of God, and all these things shall be added unto you."

The breeze settled. The hillside was quiet and still. Sunlight rested upon the grass and upon the disciples sitting together. A few of them brushed dust from their hands. One man drew forth a piece of bread from his bag and brake it slowly.

The wildflowers kept swaying. The ravens hopped and pecked at the ground nigh, full and unhurried. Jesus sat among his friends upon the warm hillside, and the afternoon stretched on, gentle and bright.
