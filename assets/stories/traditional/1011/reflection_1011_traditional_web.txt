Birds wake up each morning and find their breakfast waiting. Flowers open their petals without worrying about how pretty they look. God takes care of them quietly, like a parent who sets out your clothes before you even get up.

Sometimes our tummies feel tight when we wonder about things — like whether we will have enough or whether everything will be okay. That tight feeling is so normal. Everybody gets it sometimes, even grown-ups.

But look outside at the birds hopping in the grass. Look at the wildflowers growing by the sidewalk. They are wrapped in God's care, and so are you — right now, just as you are.
