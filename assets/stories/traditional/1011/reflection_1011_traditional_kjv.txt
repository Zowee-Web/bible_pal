Birds wake each morning and find their meat waiting. Flowers open their petals without taking thought how fair they look. God taketh care of them quietly, as a parent that layeth out thy clothing ere thou art risen.

Sometimes our hearts feel tight when we wonder about things — whether we shall have enough, or whether all shall be well. That tight feeling is so common. Every man hath it sometimes, even those of full age.

But behold the birds hopping in the grass without. Behold the wildflowers growing by the way. They are wrapped in the care of God, and so art thou — even now, just as thou art.
