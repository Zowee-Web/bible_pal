A warm afternoon sun lit the hillside. Green grass covered the slopes. Wildflowers grew in patches of yellow and purple between the rocks. A soft breeze carried the smell of earth and herbs across the open land.

Jesus sat on the hillside with his disciples gathered close around him. Some of them leaned on their elbows in the grass. Others sat cross-legged with their sandals dusty from the road. A few birds landed nearby, pecking at seeds in the dirt.

Jesus looked at the birds hopping along the ground. He pointed toward them and spoke. "Look at the ravens," he said. "They don't plant seeds. They don't gather grain into barns. God feeds them." He paused. The birds tilted their small heads and pecked again at the earth. "How much more valuable you are than birds," Jesus said.

He opened his hands as he talked. "Which of you, by worrying, can add one moment to your life?" The disciples listened. Wind moved through the tall grass around them.

Then Jesus pointed to the flowers growing along the hillside. Bright petals swayed in the breeze. "Think about how the lilies grow," he said. "They don't work. They don't spin thread." He touched one of the stems gently. "But I tell you, even Solomon in all his fine robes was not dressed as beautifully as one of these."

A few of the disciples looked down at the flowers near their feet. Small purple blossoms grew between the stones, open and still.

"If God clothes the grass of the field this way," Jesus continued, "grass that is here today and tomorrow is tossed into an oven — how much more will he clothe you?" His voice was steady and clear in the open air.

He looked around at each of them. "Don't keep striving for what you will eat or what you will drink," he said. "Don't worry." He folded his hands together. "The nations of the world seek after all these things. But your Father knows that you need them."

A bird lifted off the ground and flew low over the wildflowers. Its wings made a quiet sound against the air.

Jesus spoke again. "Instead, seek God's care, and these things will be given to you as well."

The breeze settled. The hillside was quiet and still. Sunlight rested on the grass and on the disciples sitting together. A few of them brushed dust from their hands. One man pulled a piece of bread from his bag and broke it slowly.

The wildflowers kept swaying. The ravens hopped and pecked at the ground nearby, full and unhurried. Jesus sat among his friends on the warm hillside, and the afternoon stretched on, gentle and bright.
