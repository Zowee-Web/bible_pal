In the seventh year, Jehoiada the priest summoned the captains over hundreds, the Carites, and the guard to Yahweh’s house. There he made a covenant with them and showed them the king’s son.

Jehoiada divided their duties for the Sabbath. Some would watch the king’s house, some would stand at the gate Sur, and some would guard the gate behind the guard. Those leaving duty would remain at Yahweh’s house around the king. Every man was to keep his weapon in his hand and stay with the king whenever he went out or came in.

The captains obeyed. They brought both the men beginning their Sabbath duty and those finishing it. Jehoiada gave them the spears and shields that had belonged to King David and had been kept in Yahweh’s house. Armed guards stood from the right side of the house to the left, beside the altar and the house, surrounding the king.

Then Jehoiada brought out the king’s son. He placed the crown on him and gave him the covenant. They made him king, anointed him, clapped their hands, and cried, “Long live the king!”

Athaliah heard the guard and the people. She entered Yahweh’s house and saw the king standing by the pillar, with captains and trumpets beside him. The people rejoiced and blew trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, “Treason! Treason!”

Jehoiada ordered the captains to bring her outside the ranks and to kill anyone who followed her. She was not to be slain in Yahweh’s house. They seized her, took her by the horses’ entry to the king’s house, and killed her there.

Jehoiada made a covenant between Yahweh, the king, and the people that they would be Yahweh’s people, and another covenant between king and people. The people broke down Baal’s house, destroyed its altars and images, and killed Mattan, Baal’s priest, before the altars. Jehoiada appointed officers over Yahweh’s house. Then they brought the king to the royal house, and he sat on the throne. The people rejoiced, and the city was quiet.
