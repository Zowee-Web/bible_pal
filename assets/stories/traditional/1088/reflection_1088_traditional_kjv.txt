It was the last night. He knew what was at hand. And rather than give a discourse or issue a charge, he took up a towel and a bason and kneeled upon the ground.

There is something that disarmeth a man utterly — to be served by one he holdeth in reverence. It overturns the order of things. It saith: this concerneth not rank. This concerneth presence. This concerneth what thou art willing to do for the one before thee.

Peter resisted, for it seemed unfit — the master kneeling before the servant, the teacher washing the feet of those he taught. But Jesus was making plain a truth that words alone could not convey. He was shewing them what love looketh like when it hath nothing left to prove.

Sometimes the mightiest thing a soul can do is the smallest — wrought quietly, upon the knees, for the sake of another.
