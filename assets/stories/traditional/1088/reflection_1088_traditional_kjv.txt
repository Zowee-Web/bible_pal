It was the last night. Rather than give a discourse or issue a charge, he took up a towel and a bason and kneeled upon the ground. Peter resisted, for it seemed unfit — the teacher serving the student. What is it to be served by one thou holdest in reverence, in a way that saith this is not about rank, this is about presence, this is what love looketh like when it hath nothing left to prove?
