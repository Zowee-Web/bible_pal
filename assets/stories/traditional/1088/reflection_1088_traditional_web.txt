It was the last night. He knew what was coming. And instead of giving a speech or issuing commands, he picked up a towel and a basin and got on his knees.

There is something disarming about being served by someone you look up to. It rearranges the order of things. It says: this is not about position. This is about presence. This is about what you are willing to do for the person in front of you.

Peter resisted because it felt wrong — the teacher serving the student, the master kneeling before the servant. But Jesus was making a point that words alone could not carry. He was showing them what love looks like when it has nothing left to prove.

Sometimes the most powerful thing a person can do is the smallest thing — done quietly, on their knees, for someone else.
