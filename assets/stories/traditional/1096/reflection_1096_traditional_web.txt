Jesus asked the man a question: "Do you want to be made well?"

The man did not answer the question. He explained why healing had not happened. He had no one to help him into the pool. Someone else always stepped in first. For thirty-eight years, this had been his situation.

Jesus did not respond to the explanation. He did not address the pool or the water or the system that had failed the man. He said three things: Arise. Take up your mat. Walk.

And the man was made well. Immediately. Not through the pool. Not through the water. Not through anyone carrying him. Through a word spoken directly to him.

What has held you in place — and what would it mean to hear the words "arise and walk"?
