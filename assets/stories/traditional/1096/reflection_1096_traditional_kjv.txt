Jesus saith unto the man, "Wilt thou be made whole?"

The man answered not the question. He told why he had not been healed. He had no man to put him into the pool. Another always steppeth down before him. For thirty and eight years, this had been his case.

Jesus did not answer his explanation. He did not speak of the pool or the water or of any man to carry him. He said three things: Rise. Take up thy bed. Walk.

And the man was made whole. Immediately. Not through the pool. Not through the troubled water. Not through another man's help. Through a word spoken directly unto him.

What hath held thee in place — and what would it mean to hear the words "rise and walk"?
