Eight and thirty years is a long time to wait for something that never cometh — long enough to leave off asking, long enough to put explanation in the place of hope. When Jesus asked, wilt thou be made whole, the man did not say yea; he explained why it was impossible. Sometimes the thing we most need is not a better system. It is one who speaketh unto us as though change is still possible — even when we have ceased to believe it ourselves.
