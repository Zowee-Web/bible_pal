In Jerusalem, there was a pool called Bethesda. Sick people lay all around it on mats — blind people, people who couldn't walk, people who couldn't move at all. They waited there day after day, hoping to be healed.

One man had been sick for thirty-eight years. That's a really, really long time. He couldn't walk, and he had nobody to help him.

Jesus came to the pool and saw the man lying there. He already knew how long the man had been waiting.

"Do you want to get well?" Jesus asked.

The man didn't say yes or no. He said, "Sir, I don't have anyone to help me into the pool. Every time the water moves, someone else gets in before me."

Jesus said, "Stand up. Pick up your mat. Walk."

And right away — the man was healed! He stood up for the first time in thirty-eight years. He picked up his mat and WALKED.

Some leaders got upset because it happened on the Sabbath — the special day of rest. "You're not supposed to carry your mat today!" they said.

"The man who healed me told me to carry it," he answered.

Later, Jesus found the man in the temple — STANDING there, on his own two feet.

After thirty-eight years of waiting, he was finally well.
