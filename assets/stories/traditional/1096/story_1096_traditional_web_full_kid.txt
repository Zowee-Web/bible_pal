In Jerusalem there was a special pool called Bethesda. It had five covered walkways around it, and under those walkways lay lots of sick people — blind people, people who couldn't walk, people who couldn't move at all. They waited there day after day, hoping the water would move and they could be healed.

One man had been sick for THIRTY-EIGHT YEARS. That's longer than most people's whole lives! He lay on a thin mat near the pool, watching everyone else get help while he just waited. He had nobody to carry him into the water.

One day, Jesus came to the pool. He walked past all the sick people until he stopped right next to this man. Jesus already knew how long the man had been lying there.

"Do you want to get well?" Jesus asked.

The man didn't answer yes or no. Instead he said, "Sir, I don't have anybody to help me get into the pool. Every time the water moves, someone else gets there first."

He wasn't really answering the question. He was just explaining why things would never change. That's what happens when you wait so long — you stop believing things CAN change.

But Jesus didn't talk about the pool or the water or the waiting. He just said three words:

"Stand up. Pick up your mat. Walk."

And IMMEDIATELY — the man was healed! His legs, which hadn't worked in thirty-eight years, suddenly held his weight. He stood up. He actually STOOD UP! He picked up his old mat, tucked it under his arm, and walked right out of those covered walkways and into the sunshine.

But there was a problem. It was the Sabbath — the special day of rest — and the religious leaders got upset when they saw him carrying his mat.

"You can't carry that today!" they said. "It's against the rules!"

"The man who healed me told me to carry it," he said.

"Who was this man?" they wanted to know. But the healed man had no idea — Jesus had already walked away into the crowd.

Later that day, Jesus found the man in the temple. The man was STANDING there — standing! — in the beautiful temple courts.

"You're well now," Jesus said to him. "Don't sin anymore, so nothing worse happens to you."

The man went and told everyone that it was Jesus who had made him well. After thirty-eight years of waiting, he was finally free.
