It was a warm afternoon in Galilee. The sun shone down, and the air felt heavy and quiet. A multitude was gathered to behold Jesus. Men, women, and children sat together upon the dusty ground, their sandals covered with earth from the long walk. Many of them were weary. Their faces shewed care, and their shoulders were bowed down. Some laboured hard every day, bearing water, baking bread, or helping in the fields. Others felt heavy laden by the rules and demands they heard from the teachers of the law. Every one seemed weary and longing for somewhat gentle.

Jesus stood before them. His eyes were kind, and his voice was calm and clear. The people hearkened closely, hoping to hear words that would bring them hope. They were accustomed to hearing of all the things they must do to please God: heavy rules, long prayers, and so many things that seemed impossible to bear. Their hearts were weary also.

Then Jesus spake, and his words fell as a gentle rain. "Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest." His voice was gentle, and the sound thereof made the afternoon seem softer. The children stopped fidgeting, and even the weary grown folk sat up straighter, hope flickering in their eyes.

He looked upon them with understanding. "Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me," Jesus said, "for I am meek and lowly in heart. Ye shall find rest unto your souls." As he spake, it seemed as though their burdens were lightened somewhat. The people imagined laying off somewhat heavy and putting on somewhat much lighter, somewhat that would not make them weary. The hard lines on their faces softened. Shoulders eased, and some even smiled at their neighbour.

"For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light," Jesus finished. The multitude hearkened unto his gentle promise. In that quiet moment, they knew that rest was not somewhat they must earn. It was a gift that Jesus offered unto every one that felt weary and worn down. The sun was yet warm, but the people's hearts felt lighter. They departed from that place holding fast unto Jesus' words, ready to trust in him, knowing he would care for them and help them rest.
