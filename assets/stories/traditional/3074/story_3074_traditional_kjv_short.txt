Jeroboam the son of Nebat was an Ephrathite of Zereda and a servant of Solomon. His mother was Zeruah, a widow woman. Jeroboam lifted up his hand against the king.

This was the reason Jeroboam lifted up his hand against the king: Solomon had built Millo and repaired the breaches of the city of David his father. Jeroboam was a mighty man of valour. When Solomon saw that the young man was industrious, he made him ruler over all the charge of the house of Joseph.

At that time Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem. The prophet Ahijah the Shilonite found him in the way. Ahijah was clad with a new garment, and the two men were alone in the field.

Ahijah took the new garment and rent it in twelve pieces. He told Jeroboam, “Take thee ten pieces.” Ahijah then delivered the word of the Lord: the kingdom would be rent out of Solomon’s hand, and ten tribes would be given unto Jeroboam. One tribe would remain for the sake of the Lord’s servant David and for Jerusalem, the city chosen out of all the tribes of Israel.

Ahijah said this division came because they had forsaken the Lord, worshipped Ashtoreth, Chemosh, and Milcom, and had not walked in his ways or kept his statutes and judgments as David had done.

Howbeit, Ahijah said the whole kingdom would not be taken from Solomon during his life. Solomon would remain prince for David’s sake. The kingdom would be taken from Solomon’s son, and ten tribes would be given unto Jeroboam. One tribe would remain with Solomon’s son, that David might have a light alway before the Lord in Jerusalem.

Ahijah told Jeroboam that he should reign over Israel. The prophet also declared that if Jeroboam would hearken unto all that the Lord commanded him, walk in his ways, do that which was right, and keep his statutes and commandments as David did, the Lord would be with him and build him a sure house as he built for David. The Lord would give Israel unto him. The seed of David would be afflicted for this, but not for ever.

Solomon therefore sought to kill Jeroboam. Jeroboam arose and fled into Egypt unto Shishak king of Egypt. He abode there until the death of Solomon.
