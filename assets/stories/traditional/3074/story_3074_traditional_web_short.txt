Jeroboam son of Nebat was an Ephraimite from Zeredah and a servant of Solomon. His mother was Zeruah, a widow. Jeroboam lifted up his hand against the king.

This was the reason Jeroboam lifted up his hand against the king: Solomon had built Millo and repaired the breach of his father David’s city. Jeroboam was a mighty man of valor. When Solomon saw that the young man was industrious, he placed him over all the labor of the house of Joseph.

At that time Jeroboam went out from Jerusalem. The prophet Ahijah the Shilonite met him on the road. Ahijah was wearing a new garment, and the two men were alone in the field.

Ahijah took his new garment and tore it into twelve pieces. He told Jeroboam to take ten pieces. Ahijah then delivered Yahweh’s word: the kingdom would be torn from Solomon’s hand, and ten tribes would be given to Jeroboam. One tribe would remain for the sake of Yahweh’s servant David and for Jerusalem, the city Yahweh had chosen from all Israel’s tribes.

Ahijah said this division came because they had forsaken Yahweh, worshiped Ashtoreth, Chemosh, and Milcom, and had not walked in Yahweh’s ways or kept his statutes and ordinances as David had done.

Yet Ahijah said the whole kingdom would not be taken from Solomon during his lifetime. Solomon would remain prince for David’s sake. The kingdom would be taken from Solomon’s son, and ten tribes would be given to Jeroboam. One tribe would remain with Solomon’s son so that David would always have a lamp before Yahweh in Jerusalem.

Ahijah told Jeroboam that he would reign over Israel. The prophet also stated a condition: if Jeroboam listened to all Yahweh commanded, walked in his ways, did what was right, and kept his statutes and commandments as David had, Yahweh would be with him and build him a sure house as he had built for David. Yahweh would give Israel to him. David’s offspring would be afflicted because of these things, but not forever.

After this, Solomon sought to kill Jeroboam. Jeroboam arose and fled into Egypt to Shishak, king of Egypt. He remained there until Solomon’s death.
