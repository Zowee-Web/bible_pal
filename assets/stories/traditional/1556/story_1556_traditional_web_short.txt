The man with the measuring line brought him back to the door of the house. And behold, water came out from under the threshold of the house eastward, for the front of the house faced toward the east. The water came down from under the right side of the house, on the south of the altar. Ezekiel looked at it. It was a small flow, no wider than a hand.

The man brought him out by the way of the north gate and led him around by the way outside to the outer gate that looked toward the east. The water was running on the right side of the gate.

Then the man went out eastward with the line in his hand. He measured a thousand cubits. He took Ezekiel by the hand, and he caused him to pass through the water, and the water was to the ankles.

He measured another thousand. He caused him to pass through. The water was to the knees.

He measured another thousand. The water was to the waist.

He measured another thousand. And it was now a river that he could not pass through. The water had risen. It was a river to swim in. A river that could not be walked through.

The man turned to him. "Son of man," he said, "have you seen this?"

And he brought Ezekiel back along the bank of the river. When Ezekiel had returned, he saw that on the bank of the river were very many trees on the one side and on the other.

"These waters flow out toward the eastern region," the man said. "They will go down into the Arabah. They will go to the sea, and the waters of the sea will be healed. Every living creature which swarms will live wherever the river comes. There will be a very great multitude of fish. Fishermen will stand on its banks. From En Gedi even to En Eglaim it will be a place to spread nets."

He pointed to the trees.

"By the river on its banks, on this side and on that side, will grow every tree for food. Their leaves will not wither, and their fruit will not fail. They will bring forth new fruit every month, because the waters issue out of the sanctuary. Their fruit will be for food, and their leaves for healing."

The river ran past Ezekiel's feet, going east, going down toward the dead place, carrying the trees of healing along both its banks. He stood and watched it for a long time. And so it was shown to him, in the vision of the man with the measuring line, what the threshold of the house would one day pour out.
