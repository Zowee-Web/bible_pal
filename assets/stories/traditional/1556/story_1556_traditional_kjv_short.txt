The man with the measuring line brought him again unto the door of the house. And behold, waters issued out from under the threshold of the house eastward, for the forefront of the house stood toward the east. And the waters came down from under from the right side of the house, at the south side of the altar. Ezekiel looked upon it. It was a small flow, no wider than a hand.

The man brought him out of the way of the gate northward, and led him about the way without unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward. And, behold, there ran out waters on the right side.

Then the man that had the line in his hand went forth eastward. He measured a thousand cubits. He brought Ezekiel through the waters. The waters were to the ankles.

He measured a thousand. He brought him through. The waters were to the knees.

He measured a thousand. The waters were to the loins.

Afterward he measured a thousand. And it was a river that he could not pass over. The waters had risen. Waters to swim in. A river that could not be passed over.

The man turned unto him. "Son of man," he said, "hast thou seen this?"

And he brought him, and caused him to return to the brink of the river. Now when Ezekiel had returned, behold, at the bank of the river were very many trees on the one side and on the other.

"These waters issue out toward the east country," the man said, "and go down into the desert, and go into the sea. Being brought forth into the sea, the waters shall be healed. Every thing that liveth, which moveth, whithersoever the rivers shall come, shall live. There shall be a very great multitude of fish, because these waters shall come thither. The fishers shall stand upon it from En-gedi even unto En-eglaim. They shall be a place to spread forth nets."

He pointed unto the trees.

"By the river upon the bank thereof, on this side and on that side, shall grow all trees for meat, whose leaf shall not fade, neither shall the fruit thereof be consumed. It shall bring forth new fruit according to his months, because their waters they issued out of the sanctuary. The fruit thereof shall be for meat, and the leaf thereof for medicine."

The river ran past Ezekiel's feet, going east, going down toward the dead place, bearing the trees of healing along both its banks. He stood and beheld it a long time. And so it was shown unto him, in the vision of the man with the measuring line, what the threshold of the house should one day pour forth.
