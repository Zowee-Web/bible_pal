The children of Israel again did that which was evil in Yahweh’s sight. They served the Baals, the Ashtaroth, the gods of Syria, the gods of Sidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines. They abandoned Yahweh and did not serve him.

Yahweh’s anger burned against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of the Philistines and into the hand of the children of Ammon. That year they troubled and oppressed the children of Israel, and for eighteen years they oppressed all the children of Israel who were beyond the Jordan in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead. The children of Ammon also passed over the Jordan to fight as well against Judah, against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim, so that Israel was very distressed.

Then the children of Israel cried to Yahweh, “We have sinned against you, even because we have forsaken our God, and have served the Baals.”

Yahweh answered the children of Israel. Had he not saved them from the Egyptians, and from the Amorites, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines? The Sidonians also, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites had oppressed them; they had cried to him, and he had saved them out of their hand. Yet they had forsaken him and served other gods. “Therefore I will save you no more,” he said. “Go and cry to the gods which you have chosen. Let them save you in the time of your distress!”

But the children of Israel said to Yahweh, “We have sinned! Do to us whatever seems good to you; only deliver us, please, today.” Then they put away the foreign gods from among them and served Yahweh; and his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel.
