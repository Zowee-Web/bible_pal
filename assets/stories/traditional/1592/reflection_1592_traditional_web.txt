The cup was found in Benjamin's sack, and Judah did not argue. He offered himself in the boy's place — to stay as a slave so the youngest could go home, and his father would not go down to the grave in grief. He had become surety for the boy, and here he kept his word. The passage ends with the plea still hanging, unanswered. What does it ask of someone to stand in the place of the one who cannot pay?
