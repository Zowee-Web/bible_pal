When the council heard these things, they were cut to the heart, and they gnashed at him with their teeth. The chamber was crowded with elders and scribes of the Sanhedrin, and the stone walls echoed with their shouting. But Stephen was full of the Holy Spirit, and he stood among them and looked up steadfastly into heaven.

He saw the glory of God in heaven, and Jesus standing on the right hand of God. He said to them, "Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of Man standing on the right hand of God."

Then they cried out with a loud voice, and they stopped their ears with their hands, and they rushed at him with one accord. They seized him and drove him out of the city, through the gate and onto the open ground beyond the walls. The dust of the road rose around their feet as they went.

The hill outside was bare and stony, and the sun stood high above them. The witnesses laid their long garments at the feet of a young man named Saul, who stood watching with the cloaks gathered in his arms. He was young, and he did not move from where he stood. Then the men gathered up stones from the field, and the noise of their feet on the dry earth was steady and close.

They began to stone Stephen as he called out to heaven, saying, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit." The stones struck him, and he kneeled down on the rough ground. The men did not stop, and the stones came again and again.

Then he cried with a loud voice, "Lord, do not hold this sin against them." When he had said this, his voice grew quiet, and he fell asleep. The field was still around him.
