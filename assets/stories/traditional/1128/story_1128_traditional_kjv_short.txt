When the council heard these things, they were cut to the heart, and they gnashed on him with their teeth. The chamber was thronged with elders and scribes of the Sanhedrin, and the stone walls echoed with their shouting. But Stephen, being full of the Holy Ghost, stood among them and looked up stedfastly into heaven.

He saw the glory of God in heaven, and Jesus standing on the right hand of God. And he said unto them, "Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing on the right hand of God."

Then they cried out with a loud voice, and stopped their ears, and ran upon him with one accord. They laid hold on him and cast him out of the city, through the gate and unto the open ground without the walls. The dust of the road rose about their feet as they went.

The hill without was bare and stony, and the sun stood high above them. The witnesses laid down their long garments at the feet of a young man named Saul, who stood watching with the cloaks gathered in his arms. He was young, and he stirred not from where he stood. Then the men gathered up stones from the field, and the noise of their feet upon the dry earth was steady and close.

They began to stone Stephen as he called upon heaven, saying, "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit." The stones smote him, and he kneeled down upon the rough ground. The men ceased not, and the stones came again and again.

Then he cried with a loud voice, "Lord, lay not this sin to their charge." And when he had said this, his voice grew quiet, and he fell asleep. And the field was still about him.
