On the seventh morning they arose about the dawning of the day.

The army of Israel came forth out of the camp. The priests came forth, seven of them, with the rams' horns in their hands. The ark of the Lord came forth, borne upon the shoulders of the Levites. The armed men came forth before the ark. The rereward came forth after.

They began to walk.

They compassed the city of Jericho. They compassed it once. The priests blew with the trumpets. The horns sounded long and low above the walls.

They compassed it twice. The horns went on.

The dust came up off the way round about the wall of the city. It clave unto the sandals of the priests. It settled upon the wood of the ark.

They compassed it a third time. The horns sounded long above the walls. From the city, no answer came. The watchmen upon the wall called not out. The gates abode shut.

They compassed it a fourth time. The breath of the priests was hard in the morning air between the blasts of the horns.

They compassed it a fifth time. The men of Israel walked in silence behind the horns. They had been commanded not to shout, neither to lift up the voice, neither to make any sound out of the mouth, until Joshua should give the word.

The silence held.

They compassed the city the sixth time.

Then they compassed it the seventh.

Joshua said unto the people, "Shout; for the Lord hath given you the city! The city shall be accursed, even it, unto the Lord. Only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house — because she hid the messengers that we sent."

The seventh circuit closed. The priests blew with the trumpets with a long blast.

Then the people shouted.

They shouted with a great shout. The shout filled the plain of Jericho.

The wall fell down flat.

The men of Israel went up into the city, every man straight before him. They took it.

The houses of Jericho stood empty by evening. The walls were down. The dust that the army had stirred all the morning was settling over the stones.
