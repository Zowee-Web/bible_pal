On the seventh morning they rose at the break of day.

The army of Israel came out of the camp. The priests came out, seven of them, with the rams' horns in their hands. The ark of Yahweh came out, carried on the shoulders of the Levites. The armed men came out before the ark. The rear guard came out after.

They began to walk.

They walked around the city of Jericho. They walked once. The priests blew the horns. The horns sounded long and low over the walls.

They walked twice. The horns went on.

The dust came up off the road around the wall of the city. It coated the sandals of the priests. It settled on the wood of the ark.

They walked a third time. The horns sounded long over the walls. From the city, no answer came. The watchmen on the wall did not call out. The gates stayed shut.

They walked a fourth time. The breath of the priests was hard in the morning air between the blasts of the horns.

They walked a fifth time. The men of Israel walked in silence behind the horns. They had been commanded not to shout, not to raise the voice, not to make a sound out of the mouth, until Joshua should give the word.

The silence held.

They walked the sixth time around the city.

Then they walked the seventh.

Joshua said to the people, "Shout! Yahweh has given you the city! The city is to be devoted to Yahweh. Only Rahab the prostitute shall live, and all who are with her in her house — because she hid the messengers we sent."

The seventh circuit closed. The priests blew the horns with a long blast.

Then the people shouted.

They shouted with a great shout. The shout filled the plain of Jericho.

The wall fell down flat.

The men of Israel went up into the city, every man straight before him. They took it.

The houses of Jericho stood empty by evening. The walls were down. The dust the army had stirred all morning was settling over the stones.
