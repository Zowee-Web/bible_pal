Everything was taken from Job's own body, and his wife bade him curse God and die. He would not. "Shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil?" It is no tidy answer; it is a man holding fast while covered in boils. And when his friends came, the wisest thing they did was the first: they sat with him seven days and spake not a word. What is it worth to sit with someone in silence before trying to explain their pain?
