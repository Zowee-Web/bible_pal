Everything was taken from Job's own body, and his wife told him to be done with God and die. He would not. "Shall we receive good from God, and shall we not receive evil?" It is not a tidy answer; it is a man refusing to let go while covered in sores. And when his friends came, the wisest thing they did was the first: they sat with him seven days and said nothing. What is it worth to sit with someone in silence before trying to explain their pain?
