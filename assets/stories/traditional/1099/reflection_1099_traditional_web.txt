Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a high mountain. He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun. His garments became white as light.

Moses and Elijah appeared and talked with him.

Peter said, "Lord, it is good for us to be here. Let us make three tents."

While he was still speaking, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A voice from the cloud said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him."

The disciples fell on their faces. They were very afraid.

Jesus came and touched them. He said, "Get up, and don't be afraid."

They lifted up their eyes and saw no one except Jesus alone.
