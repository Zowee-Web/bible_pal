After six days, Jesus took with him Peter, James, and John his brother. He brought them up into a high mountain by themselves. He took the three of them apart from the other disciples. He brought them up the mountain privately. It was just Jesus and Peter and James and John, and they went up the mountain together.

He was transfigured before them.

His face shone like the sun. His garments became as white as the light. There on the mountain, before the three of them, Jesus was changed. His face shone like the sun shines. His garments became white — as white as the light itself. The man they had followed, the one who walked the roads with them, the one who taught and ate bread and grew tired — his face shone like the sun. His garments became as white as the light.

And behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them. Moses and Elijah were there on the mountain. They were talking with Jesus. The three disciples saw Moses and Elijah, and they saw them speaking with Jesus. Two figures from the scriptures — Moses and Elijah — appeared and spoke with him.

Peter answered and said to Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you want, let's make three tents here: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

Peter spoke. He said it was good for them to be there. He offered to make three tents — one for Jesus, one for Moses, one for Elijah.

While he was still speaking — while Peter was still saying these words — behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A cloud came over them. It was a bright cloud. It overshadowed them all. Peter was still speaking when the cloud came.

And a voice came out of the cloud. The voice said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him."

The voice spoke from within the bright cloud. It said, "This is my beloved Son." It said, "In whom I am well pleased." It said, "Listen to him."

When the disciples heard the voice, they fell on their faces. They fell to the ground, face down. They were very afraid. The three of them — Peter, James, and John — heard the voice from the cloud and they fell on their faces and they were very afraid.

Jesus came to them. He walked to where they lay on the ground. He touched them. He said, "Get up, and don't be afraid."

He came to them. He touched them. He told them to get up. He told them not to be afraid.

They lifted up their eyes. They looked around. Moses was gone. Elijah was gone. The bright cloud was gone. The voice was silent. They saw no one.

They saw no one except Jesus alone.

Peter, James, and John had gone up the mountain with Jesus. They had seen his face shine like the sun. They had seen his garments become white as light. They had seen Moses and Elijah appear and speak with him. A bright cloud had overshadowed them. A voice had spoken from the cloud. They had fallen on their faces. They had been very afraid.

Then Jesus came. He touched them. He told them to get up. He told them not to be afraid.

They lifted up their eyes. And they saw no one except Jesus alone.
