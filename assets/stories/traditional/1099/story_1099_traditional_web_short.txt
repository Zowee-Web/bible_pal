After six days, Jesus took with him Peter, James, and John his brother. He brought them up into a high mountain by themselves. He took the three of them apart from the others and brought them up the mountain. It was just Jesus and Peter and James and John.

He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun. His garments became as white as the light. There on the mountain, before the three of them, his face shone like the sun and his garments became white as the light.

And behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them. Moses and Elijah were there on the mountain, talking with Jesus. The three disciples saw Moses and Elijah, and they saw them talking with Jesus.

Peter answered and said to Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you want, let's make three tents here: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

While he was still speaking — while Peter was still saying these words — behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A cloud came over them, a bright cloud, and it overshadowed them all.

A voice came out of the cloud. The voice said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him."

When the disciples heard the voice from the cloud, they fell on their faces. They fell to the ground. They were very afraid.

Jesus came to them. He touched them. He said, "Get up, and don't be afraid."

They lifted up their eyes. They looked around. Moses was gone. Elijah was gone. The bright cloud was gone. The voice was silent. They saw no one but Jesus. They saw no one except Jesus alone.

They had gone up the mountain with him. They had seen his face shine like the sun. They had heard the voice from the cloud. And now they saw no one except Jesus alone.
