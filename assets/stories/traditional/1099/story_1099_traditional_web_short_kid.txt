Jesus took three of his closest friends — Peter, James, and John — and hiked up a really high mountain. They didn't know why.

When they got to the top, something AMAZING happened.

Jesus started to GLOW. His face shone bright like the sun! His clothes turned so white they were like pure light. The whole mountaintop was filled with brightness.

Then two people appeared right next to Jesus — Moses and Elijah! These were two of the most important people in all of history. And there they were, talking with Jesus!

Peter was terrified but also excited. "Lord, this is incredible!" he said. "Should I build three shelters — one for you, one for Moses, one for Elijah?"

While he was still talking, a bright, shining cloud came down over all of them. The disciples couldn't see anything. They fell flat on the ground, shaking with fear.

Then a voice spoke from the cloud.

"This is my Son, whom I love. Listen to him."

The disciples pressed their faces into the ground. They were SO scared.

Then they felt a hand touch them. It was Jesus.

"Get up," he said gently. "Don't be afraid."

They opened their eyes. The cloud was gone. Moses and Elijah were gone. The bright light was gone. Jesus was standing there looking completely normal — just like he always looked.

They walked back down the mountain together. Nobody said a word.
