Jesus picked three of his best friends — Peter, James, and John — and took them on a hike up a really tall mountain. He didn't tell them why. They just followed him.

The climb was steep and tiring. The path got rockier. The air got cooler. The villages below got smaller and smaller until they looked like toys. The lake in the distance sparkled like a mirror.

When they got to the very top, it was quiet. Just wind and rock and sky.

Then something INCREDIBLE happened.

Right in front of their eyes, Jesus started to CHANGE. His face began to glow — not like a candle or a campfire, but like the SUN. His clothes turned white — so bright, so pure, so dazzling that it was almost impossible to look at. Light was pouring out of him from the inside.

And THEN — two people appeared right next to him! Moses and Elijah — two of the most important people in the entire Bible. They were TALKING with Jesus, like old friends catching up.

Peter was freaking out. He was scared but also amazed. He blurted out, "Lord, this is AWESOME! Let me build three little shelters — one for you, one for Moses, one for Elijah!"

But right while he was talking, a bright, shining cloud came down and covered the whole mountaintop. Not a dark, scary cloud — a BRIGHT one, glowing with light. The disciples couldn't see anything. They fell flat on their faces, absolutely terrified.

Then a voice spoke out of the cloud.

"This is my Son. I love him. I am pleased with him. LISTEN TO HIM."

The disciples pressed their faces into the rock. They were shaking so hard they couldn't move.

Then they felt a hand. A regular, human hand touching their shoulders.

It was Jesus.

"Get up," he said gently. "Don't be afraid."

They opened their eyes. The cloud was gone. Moses and Elijah were gone. The blinding light was gone.

Jesus was standing there looking totally normal. Same dusty clothes. Same kind face. Same calloused hands.

They walked back down the mountain together. Nobody said a word the whole way down.
