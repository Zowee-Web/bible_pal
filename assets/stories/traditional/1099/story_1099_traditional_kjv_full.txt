And after six days Jesus taketh Peter, James, and John his brother, and bringeth them up into an high mountain apart. He took the three of them apart from the other disciples. He brought them up the mountain privately. It was Jesus and Peter and James and John alone, and they went up the mountain together.

And he was transfigured before them.

His face did shine as the sun. His raiment was white as the light. There upon the mountain, before the three of them, Jesus was changed. His face did shine as the sun shineth. His raiment was white — as white as the light itself. The man they had followed, the one who walked the roads with them, the one who taught and ate bread and grew weary — his face did shine as the sun. His raiment was white as the light.

And behold, there appeared unto them Moses and Elias. Moses and Elias were there upon the mountain. They were talking with him. The three disciples saw Moses and Elias, and they saw them speaking with Jesus. Two figures from the scriptures — Moses and Elias — appeared and spake with him.

Then answered Peter, and said unto Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias."

Peter spake. He said it was good for them to be there. He offered to make three tabernacles — one for Jesus, one for Moses, one for Elias.

While he yet spake — while Peter yet said these words — behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A cloud came over them. It was a bright cloud. It overshadowed them all. Peter yet spake when the cloud came.

And behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him."

The voice spake from within the bright cloud. It said, "This is my beloved Son." It said, "In whom I am well pleased." It said, "Hear ye him."

And when the disciples heard it, they fell on their face. They fell to the ground, face down. They were sore afraid. The three of them — Peter, James, and John — heard the voice from the cloud and they fell on their face and they were sore afraid.

And Jesus came and touched them, and said, "Arise, and be not afraid."

He came unto them. He touched them. He told them to arise. He told them to be not afraid.

And when they had lifted up their eyes, they looked about them. Moses was gone. Elias was gone. The bright cloud was gone. The voice was silent. They saw no man.

They saw no man, save Jesus only.

Peter, James, and John had gone up into the mountain with Jesus. They had seen his face shine as the sun. They had seen his raiment become white as light. They had seen Moses and Elias appear and speak with him. A bright cloud had overshadowed them. A voice had spoken from the cloud. They had fallen on their face. They had been sore afraid.

Then Jesus came. He touched them. He told them to arise. He told them to be not afraid.

They lifted up their eyes. And they saw no man, save Jesus only.
