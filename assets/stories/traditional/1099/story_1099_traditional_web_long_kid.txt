Jesus picked just three of his friends for this trip: Peter, James, and John. He didn't tell them where they were going. He just said, "Come with me."

They walked out of the village and toward a big mountain. At first the path was easy — through olive trees and green grass. But it got harder. The trees thinned out. The ground got rocky. The air got cooler. They climbed and climbed and climbed.

Below them, the valley spread out like a painting. The lake in the distance looked like a piece of silver. The towns along the shore were tiny dots.

At the top, it was just bare rock and sky and wind. The four of them stood there, catching their breath.

And then — the most amazing thing any of them had ever seen.

Jesus CHANGED. Right there in front of them.

His face started GLOWING. Not like a flashlight or a lantern — like the SUN. Actual, blazing, can't-look-at-it sunlight was shining from his face. And his clothes — they turned WHITE. Not just white like clean laundry. White like LIGHT ITSELF. So bright, so pure, so blinding that nothing on earth could compare to it.

The disciples stumbled backward. They could barely look at him. The man they ate lunch with, the man who walked dusty roads and got tired and made jokes — was BLAZING LIKE THE SUN.

Then two MORE people appeared out of nowhere! Just — appeared. Standing next to Jesus, talking with him like old friends.

The disciples somehow knew who they were. Moses and Elijah. MOSES AND ELIJAH! Two of the most important people in the entire history of their religion — people who had lived hundreds and hundreds of years before — standing right there on the mountain, having a conversation with Jesus.

Peter was shaking. He was terrified but also excited, and he didn't know what to do, so he just started talking.

"Lord! This is AMAZING! We should build three little huts — one for you, one for Moses, one for Elijah — so we can stay here forever!"

But while he was still talking, a cloud came over the mountain. Not a regular cloud. A BRIGHT cloud, shining with light, so thick they couldn't see through it. It covered everything. The disciples couldn't see Jesus. They couldn't see each other. They couldn't even see their own hands.

They fell flat on the ground. Face down on the cold rock. Shaking.

Then a VOICE spoke from inside the cloud.

"This is my Son. I love him. I am pleased with him. LISTEN TO HIM."

The voice wasn't shouting. It didn't need to. It filled everything — the cloud, the mountain, their ears, their chests, the ground beneath them.

The disciples didn't move. They barely breathed. They lay face-down on the rock, more afraid than they'd ever been in their lives — not afraid of danger, but afraid of being CLOSE to something so huge, so powerful, so HOLY that their minds couldn't handle it.

Then they felt a touch.

A hand. A warm, human hand, touching their shoulders. One by one.

"Get up," said a familiar voice. "Don't be afraid."

They opened their eyes. They looked up.

The cloud was gone. Moses and Elijah were gone. The blinding light was gone.

Just Jesus. Standing there on the bare rock. Looking completely normal. Same dusty clothes. Same gentle face. Same calloused hands — the same hands that had just touched them.

They walked back down the mountain without saying a word. The valley below looked totally ordinary. The lake was just a lake. The towns were just towns. But the three disciples carried something with them that they would never forget — something they had seen that changed how they looked at everything, especially the ordinary man walking ahead of them down the path.
