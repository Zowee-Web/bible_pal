Jesus took Peter, James, and John up into an high mountain. He was transfigured before them. His face did shine as the sun. His raiment was white as the light.

Moses and Elias appeared and talked with him.

Peter said, "Lord, it is good for us to be here. Let us make here three tabernacles."

While he yet spake, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A voice out of the cloud said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him."

The disciples fell on their face. They were sore afraid.

Jesus came and touched them, and said, "Arise, and be not afraid."

They lifted up their eyes and saw no man, save Jesus only.

Hast thou ever witnessed something that changed how thou saw everything after?
