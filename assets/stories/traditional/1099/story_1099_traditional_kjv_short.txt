And after six days Jesus taketh Peter, James, and John his brother, and bringeth them up into an high mountain apart. He took the three of them apart from the others and brought them up the mountain. It was Jesus and Peter and James and John alone.

And he was transfigured before them. His face did shine as the sun. His raiment was white as the light. There upon the mountain, before the three of them, his face did shine as the sun and his raiment was white as the light.

And behold, there appeared unto them Moses and Elias. Moses and Elias were there upon the mountain, talking with him. The three disciples saw Moses and Elias, and they saw them talking with Jesus.

Then answered Peter, and said unto Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias."

While he yet spake — while Peter yet said these words — behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A cloud came over them, a bright cloud, and it overshadowed them all.

And behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him."

And when the disciples heard the voice from the cloud, they fell on their face. They fell to the ground. They were sore afraid.

And Jesus came and touched them, and said, "Arise, and be not afraid."

And when they had lifted up their eyes, they looked about them. Moses was gone. Elias was gone. The bright cloud was gone. The voice was silent. They saw no man but Jesus. They saw no man, save Jesus only.

They had gone up into the mountain with him. They had seen his face shine as the sun. They had heard the voice from the cloud. And now they saw no man, save Jesus only.
