And immediately, while Jesus yet spake, came Judas.

Judas was one of the twelve. He had been with them. Now he was come down through the trees of the garden of Gethsemane, and a great multitude was come with him — sent from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders, with swords and with staves.

Judas had given them a token before they came.

"Whomsoever I shall kiss," he had said unto them, "that same is he; take him, and lead him away safely."

He went straightway to Jesus.

He came up unto him in the dark of the garden. He said, "Master. Master." And he kissed him.

Then they that came with him laid their hands upon Jesus, and took him.

One of them that stood by drew a sword. He smote the servant of the high priest. He cut off his ear.

And Jesus answered and said unto them.

"Are ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and with staves to take me? I was daily with you in the temple teaching, and ye took me not. But the scriptures must be fulfilled."

The disciples did not stay.

They all forsook him, and fled.

They fled into the dark of the garden, away from the lamps of them that had come with Judas.

Jesus stood between the men.

Judas stood where he had kissed him.
