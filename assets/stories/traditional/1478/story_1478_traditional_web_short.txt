While Jesus was still speaking, Judas came.

Judas was one of the twelve. He had been with them. Now he had come down through the trees of the garden of Gethsemane, and a multitude had come with him — sent by the chief priests and the scribes and the elders, with swords and with clubs.

Judas had given them a sign before they came.

"Whomever I will kiss," he had told them, "that is he. Seize him, and lead him away safely."

He went straight to Jesus.

He came up to him in the dark of the garden. He said, "Rabbi. Rabbi." He kissed him.

Then the men with him laid their hands on Jesus and seized him.

One of those who stood by drew his sword. He struck the servant of the high priest. He cut off his ear.

Jesus answered them.

"Have you come out, as against a robber, with swords and clubs to seize me? I was daily with you in the temple teaching, and you didn't arrest me. But this is so that the Scriptures might be fulfilled."

The disciples did not stay.

They all left him.

They fled into the dark of the garden, away from the lamps of the men who had come with Judas.

Jesus stood between the men.

Judas stood where he had kissed him.
