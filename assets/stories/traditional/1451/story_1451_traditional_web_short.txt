All this happened to Nebuchadnezzar the king.

At the end of twelve months, he was walking on the high roof of the royal palace of Babylon. He looked out across the city he had built.

He said aloud to no one, "Is not this great Babylon, which I have built for the royal dwelling place, by the might of my power and for the glory of my majesty?"

While the word was still in his mouth, a voice came from the sky.

"O king Nebuchadnezzar, to you it is spoken: The kingdom has departed from you. You shall be driven from men. Your dwelling shall be with the animals of the field. You shall be made to eat grass as oxen. Seven times shall pass over you, until you know that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men."

The same hour the thing was fulfilled.

He was driven from men. He went out from the palace. He went out from the gardens. He went out from the city.

He came to the open field. He ate grass as oxen ate it. The dew of the sky wet his body in the night. The sun dried him in the morning. He did not speak to any man.

The first season passed. The second season passed.

His hair began to grow long. His nails began to lengthen.

The third season passed. The fourth.

By the fifth season his hair had grown like the feathers of eagles. His nails had grown like the claws of birds.

The sixth season passed.

At the end of the seventh, he lifted up his eyes to the sky.

He told it himself afterwards, in the testimony he wrote in the second year of his return —

"I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted up my eyes, and my understanding returned to me. I blessed the Most High. I praised him who lives forever. For his dominion is an everlasting dominion. His kingdom is from generation to generation. The inhabitants of the earth are nothing before him."

His counselors found him in the field. They brought him back. His majesty came back to him. His lords sought him again. He was established in his kingdom.

He stood in the palace where he had once boasted. He stood with the field still in his hair. He stood as one who had learned that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men.
