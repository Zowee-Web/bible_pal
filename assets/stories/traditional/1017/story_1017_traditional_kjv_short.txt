Noah was a just man, and perfect in his generations. Noah walked with God. And Noah begat three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

The earth about him had turned. Violence filled it openly. All flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth. Men struck one another in the roads and took what they desired. Blood dried in the dust, and none called it strange.

God looked upon the earth. He saw what it had become.

God said unto Noah, "The end of all flesh is come before me, for the earth is filled with violence through them. Behold, I will destroy them with the earth."

The words were final.

Then God gave instruction. Precise. Detailed. Every measure exact.

"Make thee an ark of gopher wood. Rooms shalt thou make in the ark, and shalt pitch it within and without with pitch."

The length thereof: three hundred cubits. The breadth thereof: fifty cubits. The height thereof: thirty cubits. A window near the top, finished in a cubit above. The door set in the side thereof. With lower, second, and third stories shalt thou make it.

The dimensions set forth a vessel unlike any Noah had beheld. Three hundred cubits of hull. The timber would require years to hew. The pitch must be gathered in great quantity, heated, and spread by hand upon every surface.

God declared what was to come. "Behold, I do bring a flood of waters upon the earth, to destroy all flesh wherein is the breath of life from under heaven. Every thing that is in the earth shall die."

But with Noah, God established his covenant. "Thou shalt come into the ark — thou, and thy sons, and thy wife, and thy sons' wives with thee. And of every living thing, two of every sort shalt thou bring into the ark, to keep them alive. They shall be male and female."

Of fowls after their kind, of cattle after their kind, of every creeping thing after his kind — two of every sort would come unto Noah. He was also to gather of all food that is eaten and store it for provision.

The scope was vast. Not the building alone, but the provisioning. Food for his household and every creature. Stored in measure enough to last he knew not how long.

The world about him went on as before. And in the midst of it, upon open ground, Noah set his hand to the work. He hewed the first timber. He measured the first beam.

Thus did Noah. According to all that God commanded him, so did he.
