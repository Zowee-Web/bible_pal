In the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel, the word of the Lord came to Hosea the son of Beeri. He stood up among his people in the land of the northern kingdom, and he lifted up his voice with the words the Lord had given him.

He said, "When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt."

He said, "The more they were called, the more they went from them. They sacrificed to the Baals, and burned incense to engraved images. Yet I taught Ephraim to walk. I took them by their arms, but they didn't know that I healed them."

He said, "I drew them with cords of a man, with ties of love. I was to them like those who lift up the yoke on their necks. I bent down to them and fed them."

The Lord spoke through Hosea, "How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, Israel? How can I make you like Admah? How can I make you like Zeboiim?"

He said, "My heart is turned within me, my compassion is aroused. I will not execute the fierceness of my anger. I will not return to destroy Ephraim. For I am God, and not man, the Holy One in your midst, and I will not come in wrath."

Hosea lifted his voice across the cities of the north. The people heard the words of the prophet, and some turned to listen, and others went on their way.

The morning came over the hills of Samaria, and the words of the Lord went out through Hosea into the land. The prophet stood among his people, and he spoke of the love of the Lord that did not let go.

The day was warm above the houses, and the words of his prophecy were written down, and they were carried into the days that came after.
