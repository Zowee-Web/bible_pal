In the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel, the word of the Lord came unto Hosea the son of Beeri. He arose among his people in the land of the northern kingdom, and he lifted up his voice with the words the Lord had given him.

He said, "When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt."

He said, "The more they were called of them, the more they went from them. They sacrificed unto the Baals, and burned incense to graven images. Yet I taught Ephraim also to go, taking them by their arms; but they knew not that I healed them."

He said, "I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love: and I was unto them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws, and I laid meat unto them."

The Lord spake through Hosea, "How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? how shall I deliver thee, Israel? how shall I make thee as Admah? how shall I set thee as Zeboim?"

He said, "Mine heart is turned within me, my repentings are kindled together. I will not execute the fierceness of mine anger, I will not return to destroy Ephraim: for I am God, and not man; the Holy One in the midst of thee: and I will not enter into the city."

Hosea lifted up his voice across the cities of the north. The people heard the words of the prophet, and some turned to hearken, and others went on their way.

The morning came over the hills of Samaria, and the words of the Lord went forth through Hosea into the land. The prophet stood among his people, and he spake of the love of the Lord that did not let go.

The day was warm above the houses, and the words of his prophecy were written down, and they were carried into the days that came after.
