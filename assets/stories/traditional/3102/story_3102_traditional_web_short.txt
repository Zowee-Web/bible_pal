Hezekiah sent word to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh as well, that they should come to Yahweh’s house at Jerusalem to keep the Passover to Yahweh, the God of Israel. The king, his princes, and all the assembly had taken counsel to keep it in the second month, for the priests had not sanctified themselves in sufficient number, and the people had not gathered. The decree went out from Beersheba even to Dan.

So the couriers carried the letters throughout all Israel and Judah. “You children of Israel, turn again to Yahweh, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel,” the message said, “that he may return to the remnant of you that have escaped out of the hand of the kings of Assyria.” The letters urged them not to be stiff-necked like their fathers, but to yield themselves to Yahweh, who is gracious and merciful and would not turn away his face from those who return to him.

From city to city through Ephraim and Manasseh, even to Zebulun, people ridiculed the couriers and mocked them. Nevertheless some men of Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem. In Judah, the hand of God gave the people one heart to do the commandment of the king and the princes by Yahweh’s word.

A very great assembly gathered to keep the feast of unleavened bread in the second month. They took away the altars that were in Jerusalem, removed the altars for incense, and threw them into the brook Kidron. They killed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the second month, and the priests and the Levites, ashamed, sanctified themselves and brought burnt offerings into Yahweh’s house.

Many of the people had not cleansed themselves, yet they ate the Passover other than the way it is written. For them Hezekiah prayed, “May the good Yahweh pardon everyone who sets his heart to seek God, Yahweh, the God of his fathers, even if they aren’t clean according to the purification of the sanctuary.” Yahweh listened to Hezekiah, and healed the people.

For seven days the children of Israel kept the feast with great gladness, while the Levites and the priests praised Yahweh day by day with loud instruments. Hezekiah spoke encouragingly to the Levites who served Yahweh with good understanding. Then the whole assembly took counsel to keep another seven days, and kept them with gladness, for Hezekiah gave the assembly a thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep, and the princes a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep.

All the assembly of Judah rejoiced, with the priests and the Levites, everyone who came out of Israel, and the foreigners who lived in Judah. There was great joy in Jerusalem, for since the time of Solomon there had been nothing like this in Jerusalem. Then the Levitical priests arose and blessed the people, and their voice was heard; their prayer came up to his holy habitation, even to heaven.
