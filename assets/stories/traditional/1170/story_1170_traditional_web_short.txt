When Jesus had entered into Capernaum, a centurion came to him in the streets and asked him for help. He came up to Jesus before the crowd and made his request. He said, "Lord, my servant lies in the house paralyzed, grievously tormented." Jesus said to him, "I will come and heal him."

The centurion answered, "Lord, I am not worthy that you should come under my roof. Just say the word, and my servant will be healed."

He continued, "For I am also a man under authority, having under myself soldiers. I tell this one, 'Go,' and he goes; and another, 'Come,' and he comes; and to my servant, 'Do this,' and he does it."

When Jesus heard it, he marveled and said to those who followed him, "Most certainly I tell you, I haven't found so great a faith, not even in Israel."

He went on, "I tell you that many will come from the east and the west, and will sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the Kingdom of Heaven, but the children of the Kingdom will be thrown out into the outer darkness."

Then Jesus said to the centurion, "Go your way. Let it be done for you as you have believed."

The centurion turned and went his way back to his house. The streets of Capernaum were warm in the noon, and the dust rose around his feet as he walked.

When he came into his house, his servant was healed in that hour, just as Jesus had said.

Jesus continued his teaching in the city, and the multitudes followed him along the streets. The day grew toward evening, and many came to him before the door of the house where he stayed.
