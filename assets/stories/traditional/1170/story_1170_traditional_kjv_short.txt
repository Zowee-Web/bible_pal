When Jesus was entered into Capernaum, there came unto him a centurion in the streets, beseeching him for help. He came up unto Jesus before the multitude and made his request. He said, "Lord, my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, grievously tormented." Jesus saith unto him, "I will come and heal him."

The centurion answered and said, "Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof. But speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed."

He continued, "For I am a man under authority, having soldiers under me. And I say to this man, 'Go,' and he goeth; and to another, 'Come,' and he cometh; and to my servant, 'Do this,' and he doeth it."

When Jesus heard it, he marvelled, and said to them that followed, "Verily I say unto you, I have not found so great faith, no, not in Israel."

He went on, "And I say unto you, that many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, in the kingdom of heaven. But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness."

Then Jesus said unto the centurion, "Go thy way. And as thou hast believed, so be it done unto thee."

The centurion turned and went his way back unto his house. The streets of Capernaum were warm in the noon, and the dust rose about his feet as he walked.

And when he came into his house, his servant was healed in the selfsame hour, even as Jesus had said.

Jesus continued his teaching in the city, and the multitudes followed him along the streets. The day grew toward evening, and many came unto him before the door of the house wherein he abode.
