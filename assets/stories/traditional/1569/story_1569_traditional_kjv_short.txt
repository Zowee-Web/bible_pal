When Mary his mother was espoused to Joseph, it was found, before ever they came together, that she was with child of the Holy Ghost. Now Joseph was a just man, and loath to make her a public shame; and so he purposed to put her away privily, and to bear the grief himself.

But as he pondered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream. Fear not, Joseph, thou son of David, said the angel, to take unto thee Mary thy wife; for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost. And the angel told him more: that she should bring forth a son, and that he should call the child's name Jesus, for he should save his people from their sins.

Thus was the word of the prophet fulfilled, which said that a virgin should be with child, and should bring forth a son, and they should call his name Emmanuel — which is to say, God with us.

So Joseph, being raised from sleep, did as the angel had bidden him. He took unto him his wife, and kept her as his own; and he knew her not till she had brought forth her son. And he called his name Jesus.
