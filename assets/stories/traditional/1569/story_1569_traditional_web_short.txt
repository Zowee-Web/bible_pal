This is how the birth of Jesus Christ came about. His mother Mary was pledged to be married to Joseph, but before they came together, she was found to be pregnant by the Holy Spirit.

Joseph was a righteous man. He knew what the law allowed him, and he did not want to hold her up to public shame. So he decided to divorce her quietly, and to carry the sorrow without exposing her.

But while he was still turning this over in his mind, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream. "Joseph, son of David," the angel said, "do not be afraid to take Mary home as your wife. What has been conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. She will give birth to a son, and you are to give him the name Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins."

All of this happened to fulfill what the Lord had spoken through the prophet: "The virgin will be with child and will give birth to a son, and they will call him Immanuel" — a name that means, God with us.

When Joseph woke from his sleep, he did exactly as the angel of the Lord had commanded him. He took Mary home to be his wife. He kept her as his own, but did not know her as a husband until she had given birth to her son. And he gave the child the name Jesus.
