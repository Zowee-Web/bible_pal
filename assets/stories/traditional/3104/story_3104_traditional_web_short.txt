The apostles and the brothers who were in Judea heard that the Gentiles had also received the word of God. When Peter came up to Jerusalem, those who were of the circumcision contended with him. “You went in to uncircumcised men and ate with them!” they said.

Peter began and explained to them in order what had happened. He had been praying in the city of Joppa, and in a trance he saw a vision: a certain container descending, like a great sheet let down from heaven by four corners, and it came as far as him. He looked intently at it and saw the four-footed animals of the earth, wild animals, creeping things, and birds of the sky. A voice said to him, “Rise, Peter, kill and eat!” But he said, “Not so, Lord, for nothing unholy or unclean has ever entered into my mouth.” A voice answered the second time out of heaven, “What God has cleansed, don’t you call unclean.” This was done three times, and all were drawn up again into heaven.

Immediately, Peter continued, three men stood before the house where he was, sent from Caesarea to him. The Spirit told him to go with them without discriminating. Six brothers went with him as well, and they entered the man’s house. The man told them how he had seen the angel standing in his house and saying, “Send to Joppa and get Simon, who is called Peter, who will speak to you words by which you will be saved, you and all your house.”

As Peter began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell on them, “even as on us at the beginning.” Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, “John indeed baptized in water, but you will be baptized in the Holy Spirit.” If God gave the Gentiles the same gift that he gave those who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was Peter, that he could withstand God?

When they heard these things, they held their peace and glorified God. “Then God has also granted to the Gentiles repentance to life!” they said.
