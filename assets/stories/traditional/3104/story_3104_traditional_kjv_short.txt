The apostles and brethren that were in Judea heard that the Gentiles had also received the word of God. When Peter was come up to Jerusalem, they that were of the circumcision contended with him. “Thou wentest in to men uncircumcised, and didst eat with them,” they said.

But Peter rehearsed the matter from the beginning and expounded it by order unto them. He had been praying in the city of Joppa, and in a trance he saw a vision: a certain vessel descending, as it had been a great sheet let down from heaven by four corners, and it came even to him. He fastened his eyes upon it and saw fourfooted beasts of the earth, and wild beasts, and creeping things, and fowls of the air. A voice said unto him, “Arise, Peter; slay and eat.” But he said, “Not so, Lord: for nothing common or unclean hath at any time entered into my mouth.” The voice answered again from heaven, “What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common.” This was done three times, and all were drawn up again into heaven.

Behold, Peter continued, immediately three men were come unto the house where he was, sent from Cesarea unto him. The spirit bade him go with them, nothing doubting. Six brethren went with him also, and they entered into the man’s house. The man shewed them how he had seen an angel in his house, which stood and said, “Send men to Joppa, and call for Simon, whose surname is Peter; who shall tell thee words, whereby thou and all thy house shall be saved.”

As Peter began to speak, the Holy Ghost fell on them, “as on us at the beginning.” Then Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how that he said, “John indeed baptized with water; but ye shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost.” Forasmuch then as God gave them the like gift as he did unto them that believed on the Lord Jesus Christ, what was Peter, that he could withstand God?

When they heard these things, they held their peace and glorified God. “Then hath God also to the Gentiles granted repentance unto life,” they said.
