Jesus said, "Suppose one of you has a friend, and you go to him at midnight and say, 'Friend, lend me three loaves of bread, because a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him.' And suppose the man inside answers, 'Do not bother me. The door is already shut, and my children are here with me in bed. I cannot get up and give you anything.'

"I tell you, even though he will not get up and give him the bread simply because he is his friend, yet because of the man's persistence he will get up and give him as much as he needs.

"So I say to you: ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks the door will be opened.

"Which of you fathers, if your son asks for bread, would give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, would give him a snake instead of a fish? Or if he asks for an egg, would give him a scorpion? If you then, who are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?"
