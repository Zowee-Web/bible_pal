And Jesus said unto them, Suppose a man among you hath a friend. At midnight he goeth unto that friend, and saith unto him, Friend, lend me three loaves. For a friend of mine in his journey is come to me, and I have nothing to set before him.

But the man within answereth him, and saith, Trouble me not. The door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed. I cannot rise and give thee.

Then said Jesus, I say unto you, Not for friendship's sake will the man within rise and give the loaves. Yet because of the importunity of him that asketh, he will rise. He will give him as many loaves as he needeth.

And Jesus said moreover unto them, I say unto you, Ask, and it shall be given you. Seek, and ye shall find. Knock, and it shall be opened unto you. For every one that asketh receiveth. He that seeketh findeth. And to him that knocketh it shall be opened.

Ye that are fathers, if your son ask bread of you, will ye give him a stone? Or if he ask a fish, will ye for a fish give him a serpent? Or if he shall ask an egg, will ye offer him a scorpion?

If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children: how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?
