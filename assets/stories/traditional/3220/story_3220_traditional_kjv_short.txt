The Lord commanded Moses to observe that which he commanded him that day. Before Israel he would drive out the Amorite, Canaanite, Hittite, Perizzite, Hivite, and Jebusite. Moses was to take heed lest a covenant with the inhabitants of the land become a snare in their midst. Their altars were to be destroyed, their images broken, and their groves cut down. They were to worship no other god, for the Lord, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God.

They were to make no covenant with those who went a whoring after their gods. One might call an Israelite to eat of his sacrifice; their daughters, taken unto Israel’s sons, might make those sons go a whoring after their gods. No molten gods were to be made.

The feast of unleavened bread was to be kept for seven days at the appointed time in Abib, for in that month Israel came out of Egypt. All that opened the matrix belonged unto the Lord, and every male firstling of cattle, whether ox or sheep. The firstling of an ass was to be redeemed with a lamb; if it were not redeemed, its neck was to be broken. All the firstborn sons were to be redeemed. None should appear before the Lord empty.

Six days were appointed for work, but on the seventh they were to rest, in earing time and in harvest. They were to observe the feast of weeks, of the firstfruits of wheat harvest, and the feast of ingathering at the year’s end. Thrice in the year all their men children were to appear before the Lord God, the God of Israel. He would cast out nations and enlarge their borders; no man should desire their land when they went up to appear before him.

The blood of his sacrifice was not to be offered with leaven, neither should the sacrifice of the passover remain unto the morning. The first of the firstfruits of the land was to be brought into the house of the Lord their God. They were not to seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

The Lord said unto Moses, “Write thou these words: for after the tenor of these words I have made a covenant with thee and with Israel.” Moses was there with the Lord forty days and forty nights; he ate no bread and drank no water. Upon the tables he wrote the words of the covenant, the ten commandments.
