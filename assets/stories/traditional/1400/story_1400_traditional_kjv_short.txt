Ahaziah king of Judah was dead. He had been smitten down by Jehu the son of Nimshi at the going up to Gur, which is by Ibleam, and he had fled to Megiddo and died there, and his servants had carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in the city of David. Word had come unto his mother Athaliah, which was the daughter of Ahab and Jezebel. And Athaliah did that which no mother of a fallen king had ever done before.

And when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the seed royal.

But Jehosheba, the daughter of king Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of Ahaziah, and stole him from among the king's sons which were slain, even him and his nurse, and put them in the bedchamber; and they hid him from Athaliah, so that he was not slain.

And he was with her hid in the house of the Lord six years. And Athaliah did reign over the land.

And the child lay in the chamber of the priests in the house of the Lord. The nurse was with him in the small inner room. Without the temple, Athaliah sat upon the throne in the palace of her son; the city of Jerusalem went on under her rule for six years; and the only heir of David — the one infant that had not been slain — slept hid among the priestly rooms, watched by his aunt Jehosheba and her husband Jehoiada the priest of the house of the Lord.
