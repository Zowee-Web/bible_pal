Ahaziah king of Judah was dead. He had been struck down by Jehu the son of Nimshi at the gardens of Ibleam, and he had fled to Megiddo and died there, and his servants had carried him back in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in the City of David. Word had come to his mother Athaliah, who was the daughter of Ahab and Jezebel. And Athaliah did what no mother of a fallen king had ever done before.

Now when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal offspring.

But Jehosheba, the daughter of king Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of Ahaziah, and stole him away from among the king's sons who were slain, even him and his nurse, and put them in the bedroom; and they hid him from Athaliah, so that he was not slain.

He was with her hidden in Yahweh's house six years while Athaliah reigned over the land.

And the child lay in the chamber of the priests in the house of Yahweh. The nurse was with him in the small inner room. Outside the temple, Athaliah sat upon the throne in the palace of her son; the city of Jerusalem went on under her rule for six years; and the only heir of David — the one infant who had not been slain — slept hidden among the priestly rooms, watched by his aunt Jehosheba and her husband Jehoiada the priest of Yahweh's house.
