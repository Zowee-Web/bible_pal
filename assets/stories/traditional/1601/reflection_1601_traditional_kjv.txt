Elijah cast his mantle upon Elisha. Elisha ran after him, asked leave to kiss his father and mother, then returned and did something decisive: he slew the yoke of oxen, boiled their flesh with the instruments, fed the people, and followed Elijah as his servant. His words said he would follow; his actions made the departure real. What would it be to answer a call without keeping one foot in the life thou art leaving?
