Elijah found Elisha son of Shaphat plowing a field. There were twelve yoke of oxen ahead of him, and Elisha was driving the twelfth. Elijah went over to him and threw his mantle across him.

Elisha left the oxen and ran after him. "Let me kiss my father and my mother goodbye," he said, "and then I will follow you."

"Go back again," Elijah said. "What have I done to you?"

So Elisha turned back. He took his yoke of oxen and slaughtered them, and with the wooden gear of the oxen he made a fire and boiled the meat, and he gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he rose and followed Elijah, and became his servant.
