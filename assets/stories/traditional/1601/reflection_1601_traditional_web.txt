Elijah cast his mantle over Elisha. Elisha ran after him, asked to kiss his father and mother, then returned and did something decisive: he slaughtered his yoke of oxen, used the wooden gear to cook the meat, fed the people, and followed Elijah as his servant. His words said he would follow; his actions made the departure real. What would it look like to answer a call without keeping one foot in the life you are leaving?
