Elijah said almost nothing — he only threw his mantle over Elisha's shoulders and kept walking. Elisha's answer was not in words but in what he did next. He slaughtered the very oxen he had been plowing with and burned the gear for the fire, then fed the people and followed. He left himself no way back to the field. What would it look like to answer a call without keeping one foot in the life you are leaving?
