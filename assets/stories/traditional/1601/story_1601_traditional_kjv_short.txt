Elijah came away from that place and found Elisha the son of Shaphat at his plowing. Twelve yoke of oxen went before him, and he himself was with the twelfth. Elijah passed by him, and cast his mantle upon him.

Elisha left the oxen. He ran after Elijah.

"Let me, I pray thee, kiss my father and my mother," he said, "and then I will follow thee."

"Go back again," said Elijah. "For what have I done to thee?"

So Elisha turned back from him. He took a yoke of oxen and slew them. He made a fire of the instruments of the oxen, and boiled the flesh upon it, and gave it to the people, and they did eat.

Then he rose up, and went after Elijah, and ministered unto him.
