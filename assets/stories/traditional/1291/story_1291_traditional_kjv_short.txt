The wars were ended. The tribes had taken their inheritance. The cities of refuge had been named, and the towns and pasturelands had been given unto the Levites. After the long years of marching and fighting and waiting, Israel was settled in the land that had been promised so long ago.

The fields had been measured. The borders had been drawn. Joshua, who had once been a young man standing beside Moses upon the mountain, was waxed old in the leading. Tents settled into villages. Flocks moved out across the hills. The smoke of cooking fires arose from a thousand new hearths.

So the Lord gave unto Israel all the land which he sware to give unto their fathers; and they possessed it, and dwelt therein.

And the Lord gave them rest round about, according to all that he sware unto their fathers: and there stood not a man of all their enemies before them; the Lord delivered all their enemies into their hand.

Generations had passed since the Lord had spoken the promise unto Abraham. Generations had passed since Moses had brought them out of Egypt. The wilderness years, the crossing of Jordan, the walls of Jericho, the long campaigns — all of it had come unto this rest in the land.

There failed not ought of any good thing which the Lord had spoken unto the house of Israel; all came to pass.
