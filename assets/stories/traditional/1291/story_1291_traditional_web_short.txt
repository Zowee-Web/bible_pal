The wars were over. The tribes had taken their inheritance. The cities of refuge had been named, and the towns and pasturelands had been given to the Levites. After the long years of marching and fighting and waiting, Israel settled in the land that had been promised so long ago.

The fields had been measured. The boundaries had been drawn. Joshua, who had once been a young man standing beside Moses on the mountain, had grown old in the leading. Tents settled into villages. Flocks moved out across the hills. The smoke of cooking fires rose from a thousand new hearths.

So Yahweh gave to Israel all the land which he swore to give to their fathers. They possessed it, and lived in it.

Yahweh gave them rest all around, according to all that he swore to their fathers. Not a man of all their enemies stood before them. Yahweh delivered all their enemies into their hand.

Generations had passed since Yahweh had spoken the promise to Abraham. Generations had passed since Moses had brought them out of Egypt. The wilderness years, the crossing of the Jordan, the walls of Jericho, the long campaigns — all of it had come to this rest in the land.

Nothing failed of any good thing which Yahweh had spoken to the house of Israel. All came to pass.
