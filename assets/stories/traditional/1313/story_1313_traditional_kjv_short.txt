The ark had come unto Jerusalem at last. David had brought it up from the house of Obed-edom with shouting and the sound of horns. He had pitched a tent for it in the city of David. He had offered sacrifices before the Lord. And then he had appointed certain of the Levites to minister before the ark — to celebrate, and to thank, and to praise the Lord God of Israel. Asaph was the chief, and his brethren were with him, with cymbals, and with stringed instruments, and with harps.

And on that day David first delivered this psalm to give thanks unto the Lord, into the hand of Asaph and his brethren.

O give thanks unto the Lord. Call upon his name. Make known his deeds among the people.

Sing unto him. Sing psalms unto him. Talk ye of all his wondrous works.

Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord.

Seek the Lord and his strength. Seek his face continually.

Remember his marvellous works that he hath done, his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;

O ye seed of Israel his servant, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

And the psalm was sung that day before the ark, and on the days thereafter, as Asaph and his brethren ministered continually before the tent. The cymbals carried out across the city of David. The strings carried out. And so it was in the days when the ark first dwelt in Jerusalem.
