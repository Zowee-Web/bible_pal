Ezra proclaimed a fast there at the river of Ahava. The company was gathered to begin the long road back unto Jerusalem. They had been in Babylon many years. The road was perilous. Men lay in wait along it. Treasure was traveling with them.

Ezra had been ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help them against the enemy in the way. He had said unto the king that the hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him. He had not been willing to ask for the king's spear after he had said it. He had been afraid of seeming to mean less than he had said.

So the company fasted at the river. They besought God for a right way for themselves and for their little ones and for all their substance. He was entreated of them.

Ezra separated twelve of the chief priests. He weighed unto them the silver and the gold and the vessels for the house of God. Six hundred and fifty talents of silver. An hundred talents of gold. An hundred talents of vessels. Twenty basins of gold of a thousand drams. Two vessels of fine copper, precious as gold.

He said unto them: ye are holy unto the LORD. The vessels are holy also. The silver and the gold are a freewill offering unto the LORD God of your fathers. Watch ye, and keep them, until ye weigh them before the chief of the priests and the Levites, and chief of the fathers of Israel, at Jerusalem, in the chambers of the house of the LORD.

The priests received the weight. The treasure was entrusted unto them.

They departed from the river of Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month. They went forth on the long road. The hand of our God was upon them. He delivered them from the hand of the enemy, and of such as lay in wait by the way.

They came up out of Babylon unto Jerusalem with the treasure, and the hand of God was upon them all the way.
