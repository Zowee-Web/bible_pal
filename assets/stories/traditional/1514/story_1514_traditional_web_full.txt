The company had gathered at the river that runs to Ahava. Ezra had brought them up out of Babylon to go back to Jerusalem. They had been in Babylon many years. The road from Babylon to Jerusalem was long and dangerous. Men lay in wait along it for those who carried treasure. Their company carried much. The silver and the gold and the vessels for the house of God had been given them by the king and by the king's counsellors and by the people of Israel that were left in Babylon.

Ezra had not asked the king for soldiers. He had been ashamed to. He had told the king that the hand of God is upon all those who seek him for good. He had not been willing to send back and ask for a band of soldiers and horsemen to keep them on the road after he had said that. He had been afraid of seeming to mean less than he had said.

He proclaimed a fast there at the river. The company fasted. They afflicted themselves before God. They sought of him a right way for themselves and for their little ones and for all their substance. They prayed many hours. He was entreated of them.

When the fast was done, Ezra separated twelve of the chief priests. Sherebiah and Hashabiah were among them. He weighed unto them the silver and the gold and the vessels for the house of God. Six hundred and fifty talents of silver. A hundred talents of silver vessels. A hundred talents of gold. Twenty basins of gold of a thousand drams. Two vessels of fine copper, precious as gold.

He said to them: you are holy unto the LORD. The vessels are holy also. The silver and the gold are a freewill offering unto the LORD God of your fathers. Watch them and keep them. Carry them safe to Jerusalem. Weigh them again before the chief of the priests and the Levites and the chief of the fathers of Israel, in the chambers of the house of the LORD.

The priests received the weight on their own scales. They received the vessels. The treasure was entrusted to them for the long road ahead.

They departed from the river of Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month. They went on the long road. The road went over open country and through narrow valleys. The hand of God was upon them. He delivered them from the hand of the enemy and of such as lay in wait by the way.

They came up out of Babylon to Jerusalem with the treasure. They abode in the city three days. Then they went up into the house of the LORD. The silver and the gold and the vessels were brought into the chambers. They were weighed again before the priests, before Meremoth the priest and the others, as Ezra had said at the river. The count came right at the end of the road, and not one piece was missing, and the journey was at peace.
