And it came to pass, while Jesus was in a certain city, a man came unto him.

The man was full of leprosy. His skin had been eaten by the disease in many places. He had been put apart from his people for years. He had not been touched by another man in a long time.

He saw Jesus.

He fell on his face on the ground before him.

He besought him, saying, "Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean."

Jesus put forth his hand.

He touched him.

He said, "I will. Be thou clean."

And immediately the leprosy departed from him. The skin where the sores had been began to close. The man, who had not been clean for many years, was clean before he had risen to his feet again.

And Jesus charged him to tell no man. "But go," he said, "and shew thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing, according as Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them."

The man went.

But so much the more went there a fame abroad of him. And great multitudes came together to hear him, and to be healed by him of their infirmities.

Jesus withdrew himself.

He went into the wilderness. He prayed there. The crowds were left in the city below him, and he was alone in the empty country with the silence of his own breath.
