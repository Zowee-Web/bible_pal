While Jesus was in one of the cities, a man came to him.

The man was full of leprosy. His skin had been eaten by the disease in many places. He had stayed apart from his people for years. He had not been touched by another human being in a long time.

He saw Jesus.

He fell on his face on the ground before him.

He said, "Lord, if you want to, you can make me clean."

Jesus stretched out his hand.

He touched him.

He said, "I want to. Be made clean."

Immediately the leprosy left him. The skin where the sores had been began to close. The man, who had not been clean for many years, was clean before he had risen to his feet again.

Jesus said to him, "Tell no one. But go your way, and show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing according to what Moses commanded, for a testimony to them."

The man went.

But the report concerning Jesus spread much more than before. Great multitudes came together to hear him, and to be healed by him of their infirmities.

Jesus withdrew himself.

He went into the desert. He prayed there. The crowds were left in the city below him, and he was alone in the empty country with the silence of his own breath.
