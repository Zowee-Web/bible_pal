Six years had passed since Athaliah the queen mother had ordered the killing of all the royal seed and Jehosheba the priest's wife had hidden the infant Joash with his nurse in a chamber of the temple. The boy had grown up in the secret rooms of the priests. He had walked the inner courts; he had heard the Levites at their hours; he had been taught his lineage from David; and he had never seen the sun over the city. In the seventh year, Jehoiada the high priest called the captains of hundreds together in the house of the Lord on the Sabbath, and showed them the king's son. They swore the oath. The doors were closed. The Levites were arrayed at the entrances with their weapons. And on the appointed day, they brought the boy out.

Then they brought out the king's son, and put the crown on him, and gave him the testimony, and made him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and they said, "Long live the king!"

When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people into Yahweh's house.

Then she looked, and, behold, the king stood by his pillar at the entrance, and the captains and the trumpets by the king. All the people of the land rejoiced, and blew trumpets. The singers also played musical instruments, and led the singing of praise. Then Athaliah tore her clothes, and said, "Treason! treason!"

And the boy stood by the pillar at the entrance of the temple in the crown his fathers had worn. The trumpets sounded across the inner court of the house of the Lord; the captains stood at their stations with the swords from David's storerooms; the singers led the singing of praise as the people of the land shouted; and Athaliah, hearing the trumpets and the cry, came running through the courts of the king's house only to find the king already crowned, with her own torn clothes already in her hands and the word "Treason" already on her lips.
