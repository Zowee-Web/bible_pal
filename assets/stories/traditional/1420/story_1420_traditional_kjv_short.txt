Six years were passed since Athaliah the queen mother had commanded the slaying of all the royal seed and Jehoshabeath the priest's wife had hid the infant Joash with his nurse in a chamber of the temple. The child had grown up in the secret rooms of the priests. He had walked the inner courts; he had heard the Levites at their hours; he had been taught his lineage of David; and he had never beheld the sun over the city. In the seventh year, Jehoiada the high priest called the captains of hundreds together in the house of the Lord on the sabbath, and shewed them the king's son. They sware the oath. The doors were shut. The Levites were arrayed at the entrances with their weapons. And on the appointed day, they brought the boy forth.

Then they brought out the king's son, and put upon him the crown, and gave him the testimony, and made him king. And Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and said, "God save the king."

Now when Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people into the house of the Lord:

And she looked, and, behold, the king stood at his pillar at the entering in, and the princes and the trumpets by the king: and all the people of the land rejoiced, and sounded with trumpets, also the singers with instruments of musick, and such as taught to sing praise. Then Athaliah rent her clothes, and said, "Treason, Treason."

And the boy stood at the pillar at the entering in of the temple in the crown his fathers had worn. The trumpets sounded across the inner court of the house of the Lord; the captains stood at their stations with the swords from the storehouses of David; the singers led the singing of praise as the people of the land shouted; and Athaliah, hearing the trumpets and the cry, came running through the courts of the king's house only to find the king already crowned, with her own rent clothes already in her hands and the word "Treason" already on her lips.
