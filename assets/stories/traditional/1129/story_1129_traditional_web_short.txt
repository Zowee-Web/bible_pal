In Caesarea there was a man named Cornelius, a centurion of the Italian regiment. He was a devout man, and he feared God with all his household. He gave alms to the people and prayed always.

About the ninth hour of the day, he saw clearly in a vision an angel of God coming to him and saying, "Cornelius!" Cornelius looked at him and was afraid, and he said, "What is it, Lord?" The angel said, "Your prayers and your alms have come up as a memorial before God. Now send to Joppa for a man named Peter. He is staying with a tanner named Simon, whose house is by the seaside."

When the angel had departed, Cornelius called two of his household servants and a devout soldier. He sent them on the road to Joppa, and the morning was clear as they went out.

The next day, as they were on their journey, Peter went up on the housetop to pray about the sixth hour. He became hungry and desired to eat, but while they were preparing the meal, he fell into a trance.

He saw the sky opened, and a great sheet descended toward him, let down by four corners on the earth. In it were all kinds of four-footed animals of the earth, and creeping things, and birds of the sky. A voice came to him, "Rise, Peter, kill and eat."

But Peter said, "Not so, Lord, for I have never eaten anything that is common or unclean." A voice came to him again the second time, "What God has cleansed, you must not call common." This was done three times, and the sheet was taken up into the sky.

Peter went down with the men sent from Caesarea, and the next day he entered the city. When he came into the house, Cornelius met him and fell down at his feet to worship him. But Peter raised him up and said, "Stand up. I myself am also a man."

Peter said to them, "Truly I perceive that God shows no favoritism. In every nation, he who fears him and works righteousness is acceptable to him." While he was still speaking, the Holy Spirit fell on all those who heard the word, and they began to speak in other languages and to magnify God.

Then Peter said, "Can anyone forbid these people from being baptized?" And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ.
