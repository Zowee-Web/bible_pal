Early in the morning, while the dew yet lay upon the ground, Jerubbaal—who is Gideon—rose up and gathered the people that were with him.

They pitched beside the well of Harod.

Across the valley, below them, the host of Midian stretched across the plain beneath the hill of Moreh.

The tents filled the land.

Their camels were without number—as the sand on the sea shore.

⸻

The Lord spake unto Gideon:
"The people that are with thee are too many for me to give the Midianites into their hands, lest Israel vaunt themselves against me, saying, 'Mine own hand hath saved me.'

Now therefore go to, proclaim in the ears of the people, saying, 'Whosoever is fearful and afraid, let him return and depart early from mount Gilead.'"

Gideon went among the men and gave the call:
"Whosoever is fearful and afraid, let him return."

The line shifted.

Then it brake.

Men turned back in great numbers.

Twenty and two thousand departed, and there remained ten thousand.

⸻

The Lord said:
"The people are yet too many; bring them down unto the water, and I will try them for thee there."

Gideon led them down from the heights unto the stream.

At the water's edge, the men bowed to drink.

Some bowed down upon their knees, and drank straightway from the water.
Others took up water with their hands and brought it unto their mouths, lapping as they drank.

The Lord said:
"By the three hundred men that lapped will I save you, and deliver the Midianites into thine hand. Let all the other people return every man unto his place."

Gideon set apart the three hundred.

The rest he sent away.

They stood upon the hillside above the vast host below.

In their hands were trumpets… and empty pitchers with lamps within.

No swords.

⸻

As evening came, the host of Midian stirred below them.

Voices and movement rose from the valley.

That same night the Lord said unto Gideon:
"Arise, get thee down unto the host; for I have delivered it into thine hand.

But if thou fear to go down, go thou with Phurah thy servant down to the host, and thou shalt hear what they say."

⸻

Gideon went down with Phurah.

They moved quietly through the darkness unto the outside of the camp.

Hard by a tent, they heard men speaking.

One said:
"Behold, I dreamed a dream, and, lo, a cake of barley bread tumbled into the host of Midian. It came unto a tent, and smote it that it fell."

Another answered:
"This is nothing else save the sword of Gideon the son of Joash, a man of Israel: for into his hand hath God delivered Midian, and all the host."

⸻

Gideon heard.

He bowed himself in worship.

Then he returned.

⸻

He gathered the three hundred men.

"Arise," he said,
"for the Lord hath delivered into your hand the host of Midian."

He divided them into three companies.

Into every man's hand he put a trumpet… and an empty pitcher, with a lamp within.

He said:
"Look on me, and do likewise.

And, behold, when I come to the outside of the camp, it shall be that, as I do, so shall ye do. When I blow with a trumpet, I and all that are with me, then blow ye the trumpets also on every side of all the camp, and say, 'The sword of the Lord, and of Gideon!'"

⸻

They moved into position under cover of darkness.

They compassed the camp round about.

It was in the beginning of the middle watch, when they had but newly set the watch.

All was still.

⸻

Gideon lifted up his trumpet.

He blew.

⸻

Forthwith, the three hundred men blew the trumpets.

They brake the pitchers that were in their hands.

Clay shattered.
Lamps flared.

Light burst across the hills.

Voices rose together:
"The sword of the Lord, and of Gideon!"

⸻

The host woke in confusion.

Men ran forth from their tents.
Shouts filled the night.

The sound of trumpets was heard from every side.

⸻

The Lord set every man's sword against his fellow.

The Midianites turned every man upon his neighbour in the darkness.

The host brake.

They fled—

unto Bethshittah toward Zererath,
and to the border of Abelmeholah, unto Tabbath.

⸻

The three hundred blew the trumpets, and the Lord set every man's sword against his fellow, even throughout all the host.
