The servants wanted to pull the weeds out at once, but the owner said no — not yet, or the wheat would come up with them. Both would grow together until the harvest. Jesus did not soften what the harvest means: a furnace, and weeping, and then the righteous shining like the sun. The sorting is real, but it is not ours to do early, and it is not yet. What do you do with a field where good and evil are still growing side by side?
