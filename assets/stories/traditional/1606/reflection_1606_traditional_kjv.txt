The servants would have pulled the tares out at once, but the householder said no — not yet, lest the wheat be rooted up with them. Both must grow together until the harvest. Jesus did not soften what the harvest means: a furnace, and wailing, and then the righteous shining as the sun. The sorting is real, but it is not ours to do early, and it is not yet. What do you do with a field where wheat and tares are still growing side by side?
