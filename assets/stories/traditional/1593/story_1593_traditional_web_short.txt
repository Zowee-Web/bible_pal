Yahweh said to Moses, "Cut two stone tablets like the first ones, and I will write on them the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke. Be ready by the morning, and come up in the morning to Mount Sinai, and present yourself to me there on the top of the mountain. No one is to come up with you. Let no one even be seen anywhere on the mountain, and let no flocks or herds graze in front of it."

So Moses cut two stone tablets like the first, and early in the morning he went up Mount Sinai, as Yahweh had commanded him, with the two tablets in his hand.

Then Yahweh came down in the cloud and stood there with him, and proclaimed his name. Yahweh passed in front of Moses and called out:

"Yahweh, Yahweh, a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abounding in loving kindness and truth, keeping loving kindness for thousands, forgiving iniquity and disobedience and sin — and yet who will by no means clear the guilty, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children, and on the children's children, on the third and on the fourth generation."

Moses hurried to bow his head toward the earth, and he worshiped. "If I have now found favor in your sight, my Lord," he said, "then let the Lord go among us. This is a stiff-necked people; but pardon our iniquity and our sin, and take us for your inheritance."

And Yahweh said, "Behold, I am making a covenant. Before all your people I will do marvels such as have never been worked in all the earth or in any nation. All the people around you will see the work of Yahweh, for it is an awesome thing that I am doing with you."
