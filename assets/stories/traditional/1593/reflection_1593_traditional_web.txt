Yahweh proclaimed his own name to Moses, and it held two things at once: mercy and truth. Slow to anger, abounding in loving kindness, forgiving iniquity — and by no means clearing the guilty. Moses did not explain it or choose between them. He bowed to the ground and worshiped, and asked this God to go with a stiff-necked people anyway. What do you do with a God who is both merciful and just?
