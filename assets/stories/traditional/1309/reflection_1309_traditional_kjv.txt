Joshua called the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh and blessed them and sent them away. They had not left their brethren all the days of the fighting. They went home over Jordan to the land of their possession, with much riches and with very much cattle. What is it to be sent home with a blessing after standing with thy brethren through the whole war that was not thine to win alone?
