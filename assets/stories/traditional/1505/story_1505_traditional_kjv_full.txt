The king of Syria warred against Israel. He took counsel with his servants. He said: in such and such a place shall be my camp. He chose his ambush. He set the order of his men.

Elisha sent word unto the king of Israel. "Beware that thou pass not such a place. The Syrians are come down thither." The king of Israel sent unto the place and looked. He found it as Elisha had said. He saved himself there.

And it came to pass more than once. It came to pass more than twice. The Syrian king moved his camp. Elisha sent word again. The king of Israel saved himself again.

The king of Syria was sore troubled for this thing. He gathered his servants together. He said: will ye not shew me which of us is for the king of Israel?

One of his servants spake. "None, my lord, O king — but Elisha, the prophet that is in Israel, telleth the king of Israel the words that thou speakest in thy bedchamber."

The king said: go and spy where he is, that I may send and fetch him.

It was told the king: behold, he is in Dothan. The king sent horses and chariots and a great host. They came by night. They compassed the city about.

The servant of the man of God was risen early in the morning. He went forth unto the wall to look. He saw the host with horses and chariots round about the city.

"Alas, my master!" he said. "How shall we do?"

Elisha looked not. He answered without turning. "Fear not. They that be with us are more than they that be with them."

Then he prayed. "LORD, I pray thee, open his eyes, that he may see."

The young man's eyes were opened. He looked again at the hills. The mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.

The Syrian army came down. Elisha prayed again. "Smite this people, I pray thee, with blindness." The men were smitten with blindness. Elisha went out unto them. He said: this is not the way, neither is this the city. Follow me. I will bring you to the man whom ye seek.

He led them to Samaria. The walk was a long one. The men walked in their blindness, not knowing the country they were passing through. When they were come in, he prayed once more. "LORD, open the eyes of these men, that they may see." They looked up. They were in the midst of the king of Israel's city, compassed about by Israel.

The king of Israel said unto Elisha: my father, shall I smite them? Shall I smite them?

Elisha said: nay. Wouldest thou smite those whom thou hast taken captive with thy sword and with thy bow? Set bread and water before them. Let them eat and drink. Let them go to their master.

A great provision was prepared in the city. The tables were spread. The men did eat. They drank. They were sent away with their lives. They went back unto their king.

The bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.
