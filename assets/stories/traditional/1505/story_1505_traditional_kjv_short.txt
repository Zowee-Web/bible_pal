The king of Syria warred against Israel. He took counsel with his servants. He said: in such and such a place shall be my camp.

Elisha sent word unto the king of Israel. "Beware that thou pass not such a place." And it came to pass more than once or twice. The king of Israel was saved.

The king of Syria was sore troubled. He gathered his servants. He said: will ye not shew me which of us is for the king of Israel?

One of his servants spake. "None, my lord, O king — but Elisha, the prophet that is in Israel, telleth the king of Israel the words that thou speakest in thy bedchamber."

The king sent horses and chariots and a great host. They came by night. They compassed the city about wherein Elisha was. The city was called Dothan.

The servant of the man of God was risen early in the morning. He went forth unto the wall. He saw the host with horses and chariots round about the city.

"Alas, my master!" he said. "How shall we do?"

Elisha looked not. He answered without turning. "Fear not. They that be with us are more than they that be with them."

Then he prayed. "LORD, I pray thee, open his eyes, that he may see."

The young man's eyes were opened. He looked again at the hills. The mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.

Elisha brought the army down into the city. The king of Israel asked him: shall I smite them?

Elisha said: nay. Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, and go to their master.

A great provision was prepared. The men did eat. They drank. They went their way. The bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.
