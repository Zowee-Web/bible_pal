The king of Syria was warring against Israel. He took counsel with his servants. He said: in such and such a place will be my camp. He chose his ambush. He set the order of his men.

Elisha sent word to the king of Israel. "Beware that you do not pass this place. The Syrians are come down there." The king of Israel sent and checked the place. He found it as Elisha had said. He saved himself there.

It happened more than once. It happened more than twice. The Syrian king moved his camp. Elisha sent word again. The king of Israel saved himself again.

The king of Syria was sore troubled in his heart. He gathered his servants together. He said: will you not show me which of us is for the king of Israel?

One of his servants spoke. "None, my lord, O king — but Elisha the prophet that is in Israel tells the king of Israel the words that you speak in your bedchamber."

The king said: go and spy where he is, that I may send and fetch him.

It was told the king: behold, he is in Dothan. The king sent horses and chariots and a great host. They came by night. They surrounded the city.

The servant of the man of God rose early in the morning. He went out to the wall to look. He saw the host with horses and chariots all around the city.

"Alas, my master!" he said. "How shall we do?"

Elisha did not look. He answered without turning. "Fear not. They that be with us are more than they that be with them."

Then he prayed. "LORD, I pray you, open his eyes, that he may see."

The young man's eyes were opened. He looked again at the hills. The mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.

The Syrian army came down. Elisha prayed again. "Smite this people, I pray you, with blindness." The men were struck blind. Elisha went out to them. He said: this is not the way, neither is this the city. Follow me. I will bring you to the man whom you seek.

He led them to Samaria. The walk was a long one. The men walked in their blindness, not knowing the country they were passing through. When they came in, he prayed once more. "LORD, open the eyes of these men, that they may see." They looked up. They were in the middle of the king of Israel's city, surrounded by Israel.

The king of Israel said to Elisha: my father, shall I smite them? Shall I smite them?

Elisha said: no. Would you smite those you have taken captive with your sword and with your bow? Set bread and water before them. Let them eat and drink. Let them go to their master.

A great provision was prepared in the city. The tables were spread. The men ate. They drank. They were sent away with their lives. They went back to their king.

The bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.
