The king of Syria was warring against Israel. He took counsel with his servants. He said: in such and such a place will be my camp.

Elisha sent word to the king of Israel. "Beware that you do not pass this place." It happened more than once or twice. The king of Israel was saved.

The king of Syria was troubled. He gathered his servants. He said: will you not show me which of us is for the king of Israel?

One of his servants spoke. "None, my lord, O king — but Elisha the prophet that is in Israel tells the king of Israel the words that you speak in your bedchamber."

The king sent horses and chariots and a great host. They came by night. They surrounded the city where Elisha was. The city was called Dothan.

The servant of the man of God rose early in the morning. He went out to the wall. He saw the host with horses and chariots all around the city.

"Alas, my master!" he said. "How shall we do?"

Elisha did not look. He answered without turning. "Fear not. They that be with us are more than they that be with them."

Then he prayed. "LORD, I pray you, open his eyes, that he may see."

The young man's eyes were opened. He looked again at the hills. The mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.

Elisha brought the army down into the city. The king of Israel asked him: shall I smite them?

Elisha said: no. Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, and go to their master.

A great provision was prepared. The men ate. They drank. They went away. The bands of Syria came no more into the land of Israel.
