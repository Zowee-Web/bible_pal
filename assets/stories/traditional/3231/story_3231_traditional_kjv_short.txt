Saul heard that David and the men with him had been discovered. At Gibeah, beneath a tree in Ramah, the king held his spear while his servants stood about him.

Addressing them as Benjamites, he asked whether the son of Jesse would bestow fields and vineyards upon them all, or appoint them captains of thousands and hundreds. Saul charged them with conspiracy. None, he said, had told him of his son’s league with David. None was sorry for him, nor disclosed that his son had stirred David to lie in wait against him.

Doeg the Edomite, set over Saul’s servants, answered. He had seen Jesse’s son come to Ahimelech, the son of Ahitub, at Nob. Doeg reported that Ahimelech had enquired of the Lord for David and supplied him with victuals and Goliath the Philistine’s sword.

The king sent for Ahimelech and all his father’s house, the priests of Nob. All came before him. Saul called to the son of Ahitub, and the priest answered, “Here I am, my lord.”

Saul demanded why Ahimelech and David had conspired against him. He charged that the bread, sword, and enquiry of God had aided David in rising against the king to lie in wait.

Ahimelech spoke of David’s faithfulness: the king’s son-in-law went at Saul’s bidding and was honoured in his house. “Did I then begin to enquire of God for him? be it far from me,” he answered. He pleaded that neither he nor his father’s house be charged, for he knew nothing of the matter, less or more.

The king declared that Ahimelech and all his father’s house would die. He commanded the footmen to slay the Lord’s priests, alleging that they sided with David and had concealed his flight. His servants refused to lift a hand against them.

Saul turned to Doeg with the command. Doeg fell upon the priests, slaying fourscore and five who wore linen ephods. Nob also he smote with the sword: men, women, children and sucklings, oxen, asses, and sheep.

One son of Ahimelech the son of Ahitub escaped. Abiathar fled after David and reported that Saul had slain the Lord’s priests. David said he had known, when Doeg was there that day, that he would surely tell Saul. David acknowledged that he had occasioned the deaths of Abiathar’s father’s house.

“Abide thou with me, fear not,” David said. The same man sought both their lives. “With me thou shalt be in safeguard.”
