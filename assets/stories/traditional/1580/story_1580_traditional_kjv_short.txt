A man in the multitude called unto Jesus. "Master, speak to my brother, that he divide the inheritance with me."

But he would not take the case. "Man, who made me a judge or a divider over you?" And he said unto them all, "Take heed, and beware of covetousness; for a man's life consisteth not in the abundance of the things which he possesseth."

Then he spake a parable. The ground of a certain rich man brought forth so plentifully that he had no room to bestow his fruits. So he reasoned within himself: he would pull down his barns and build greater, and gather all his fruits and his goods there; and then he would say to his own soul that it had much laid up for many years, and might take its ease — eat, drink, and be merry.

But God said unto him, "Thou fool, this night thy soul shall be required of thee; then whose shall those things be, which thou hast provided?"

So is he, said Jesus, that layeth up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.
