A man in the crowd called out to Jesus. "Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me."

But Jesus would not take the case. "Man, who made me a judge or an arbitrator over you?" And to them all he said, "Beware! Keep yourselves from all covetousness, for a man's life doesn't consist in the abundance of the things he possesses."

Then he told them a parable. A certain rich man's ground produced so abundantly that he had nowhere left to store the harvest. So he reasoned it out with himself: he would pull down his barns and build bigger ones, and gather all his grain and his goods there; and then he would tell his own soul that it had plenty laid up for years to come, and could take its ease — eat, drink, and be merry.

But God said to him, "You foolish one. Tonight your soul is required of you. Now the things you have prepared — whose will they be?"

So it is, Jesus said, with everyone who lays up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.
