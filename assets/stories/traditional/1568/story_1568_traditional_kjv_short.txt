Now Israel journeyed from mount Hor by the way of the Red sea, to go about the land of Edom; and upon that long road the soul of the people grew much discouraged.

And they spake against God, and against Moses, saying, Wherefore have ye brought us up out of Egypt, to die here in the wilderness? For there is no bread, neither is there any water, and our soul loatheth this light bread.

Then the Lord sent fiery serpents among them; and the serpents bit the people, and much people of Israel died. So the people came unto Moses, and their words were changed. We have sinned, said they, in that we have spoken against the Lord, and against thee; pray thou unto the Lord, that he take these serpents from us. And Moses prayed for the people.

And the Lord answered, though not as they had asked. He bade Moses fashion the likeness of a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole; and whosoever was bitten, when he looked upon it, should live. So Moses made a serpent of brass, and set it high upon the pole. And it came to pass, that when a serpent had bitten any man, he had but to look upon the serpent of brass, and he lived.
