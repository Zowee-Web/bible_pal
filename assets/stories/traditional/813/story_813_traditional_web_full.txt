As the sun began to go down, Jesus and His friends climbed into a small wooden boat by the edge of the Sea of Galilee. The sky was turning orange and pink, and the air was gentle and cool. Jesus said to them, “Let’s go across to the other side.” The disciples helped untie the boat and pushed it away from shore. Other little boats floated nearby, following along as they set sail. The water was dark, and the gentle waves rocked their boat as they headed out onto the sea.

Not long after they started, the wind began to blow much harder. The quiet evening changed quickly. Dark clouds moved over the water, and the gentle waves became big and strong. Soon, a fierce storm came over the lake. The wind howled loudly and shook the boat. Tall waves crashed against the sides, sending cold, salty spray over the disciples. The water inside the boat grew higher as each wave splashed in, making the wooden planks slippery and wet. All around them, the wind roared, and the disciples tried to keep the boat from tipping. The sky grew dark, and the stars and moon were hidden behind angry clouds.

While the storm was shaking the boat, Jesus was sleeping at the back. He lay on a cushion, calm and quiet, even as the rain pelted down and the waves smashed around Him. The disciples looked at one another with wide eyes. The water kept rising inside their boat, and the wind seemed to grow even louder. Their hands shook as they tried to keep the boat steady, but the storm’s power was too much for them. Even though many of the disciples were fishermen, and they knew the sea well, the strength of the storm made them feel afraid and small.

Finally, one of the disciples ran to Jesus and shook Him gently. “Teacher, don’t you care that we are about to sink?” he asked, his voice shaking with fear. The storm did not stop, and the water still poured into the boat. Jesus woke up and looked around at His friends and at the high, rolling waves. He did not look frightened. He stood up, water dripping from His clothes, and walked to the front of the boat. The wind whipped His hair and the waves washed around His feet.

Jesus looked at the wild wind and the high waves. In a strong, calm voice, He spoke to the wind and the sea. “Peace! Be still!” He said. At once, the wind stopped blowing. The waves settled down. The noise that filled the air was gone in a moment. The boat, which had rocked and bounced in the water a moment before, was now still. The water became smooth and quiet, and the clouds began to drift away. All around them, there was a gentle silence, as if the whole world was listening.

After the storm stopped, Jesus turned to His disciples and said, “Why are you afraid? Do you still have no faith?” The disciples looked at each other with wide eyes and whispered to one another. They felt amazed and a little surprised. “Who is this man, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?” they said. The water was calm, and their boat floated quietly on the Sea of Galilee. Jesus was with them, and the storm was over.

Slowly, the disciples let out the breaths they had been holding. The fear in their eyes began to fade, replaced by wonder. Some of them wiped the rain from their faces, trying to understand what had just happened. The little boats that had followed were now quiet too, their sailors staring in awe at Jesus. The clouds broke apart above them, letting the last colors of the sunset shine through. The cool evening breeze no longer felt frightening—it was soft and peaceful again. One of the disciples reached over and squeezed his friend’s shoulder, grateful to be safe and together. Gently, they steered the boat toward the far shore, their hearts still full of questions but also of hope. Jesus looked at them with warmth, and they knew they were not alone. Even after the fiercest storms, they could trust that He would see them through.
