The people of Midian were mean to Israel. They stole crops and animals. When they came, the Israelites hid in caves and dens in the mountains.

A young man named Gideon was threshing wheat by a winepress. He was hiding it from the Midianites so they would not steal it. He worked fast, looking over his shoulder.

The angel of the Lord appeared and sat down under an oak tree near him. "The Lord is with you, mighty man of valor," the angel said.

Gideon looked up. He did not feel like a mighty man. He felt small and scared. "If the Lord is with us, why has all this happened?" Gideon asked. "Where are all the miracles our fathers told us about? The Lord has left us."

The Lord turned to him. "Go in this strength of yours and save Israel from the hand of Midian. Have I not sent you?"

Gideon shook his head. "How can I save Israel? My family is the weakest in our whole tribe. And I am the youngest in my father's house."

"Surely I will be with you," the Lord said. "You will strike the Midianites as one man."

Gideon wanted to believe it. "If I have found favor in your sight," he said, "show me a sign that it is really you talking to me. Please do not leave. Let me go get an offering."

"I will wait until you come back," the Lord said.

Gideon hurried inside. He cooked a young goat and made bread without yeast. He put the meat in a basket and the broth in a pot and brought it all out under the oak tree.

The angel of God said, "Take the meat and the bread and lay them on this rock. Pour the broth over them." Gideon did it.

Then the angel of the Lord reached out the tip of his staff and touched the meat and the bread. Fire shot up from the rock. The flames swallowed the meat and bread completely. When Gideon looked up, the angel was gone.

Gideon's eyes went wide. "I have seen the angel of the Lord face to face!" he whispered.

"Peace be to you," the Lord said. "Do not be afraid. You will not die."

Gideon built an altar there and called it "The Lord is Peace."
