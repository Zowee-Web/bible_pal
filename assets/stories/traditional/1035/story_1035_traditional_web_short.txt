The angel of the Lord came and sat under the oak at Ophrah, which belonged to Joash the Abiezrite. His son Gideon was beating out wheat in the winepress to hide it from the Midianites.

The winepress was a low stone basin cut into the hillside. It was not built for threshing. The walls blocked the wind. But a threshing floor sat open to the sky, visible for miles. The Midianites watched for grain. So Gideon worked in the winepress, swinging the flail in the cramped space. Chaff stuck to his arms and face.

The angel of the Lord said to him, "The Lord is with you, O mighty man of valor."

Gideon looked up. His hands were dusty. He said, "Please, my lord, if the Lord is with us, why has all this happened? Where are all his wonderful deeds our fathers told us about? Now the Lord has forsaken us and given us into the hand of Midian."

The Lord turned to him. "Go in this might of yours and save Israel from the hand of Midian. Do not I send you?"

Gideon said, "How can I save Israel? My clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my father's house."

The Lord said, "I will be with you. You shall strike the Midianites as one man."

Gideon said, "If I have found favor in your eyes, show me a sign. Do not depart until I bring out my offering."

He said, "I will stay until you return."

Gideon went inside. He prepared a young goat and unleavened cakes. He put the meat in a basket and the broth in a pot. He brought them out under the oak.

The angel of God said, "Take the meat and the cakes and lay them on this rock. Pour out the broth." Gideon did so.

The angel of the Lord reached out the tip of the staff in his hand and touched the meat and the cakes. Fire sprang up from the rock and consumed them. The angel vanished from his sight.

Gideon cried out, "Alas, O Lord God! I have seen the angel of the Lord face to face."

The Lord said, "Peace be to you. Do not fear. You shall not die."

Then Gideon built an altar there to the Lord. He called it "The Lord Is Peace."
