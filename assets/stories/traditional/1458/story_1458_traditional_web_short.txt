So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife.

Yahweh gave her conception, and she bore a son.

The women of Bethlehem came to Naomi when the boy was born. They had walked the same streets where she had walked when she had returned from Moab with no husband and no son and no field of her own. They had seen her come back empty. Now they had come to her in her own house, where a child cried.

They said to her, "Blessed be Yahweh, who has not left you today without a near kinsman. Let his name be famous in Israel. He shall be to you a restorer of life and a sustainer in your old age, for your daughter-in-law, who loves you, who is better to you than seven sons, has borne him."

Naomi took the child.

She laid him on her bosom and became nurse to him.

The women, her neighbors, gave him a name. They said, "A son is born to Naomi." They named him Obed.

The years went by in Bethlehem.

Obed grew up in his father's house. He became a man in Bethlehem of Judah. He took a wife and had a son. The son of Obed was named Jesse.

The years went by again.

Jesse grew up in Bethlehem. He became a man. He kept his father's fields. He had eight sons. The youngest of his sons was sent out into the hills to keep his father's sheep.

The youngest of his sons was named David.

This is the history of the generations of Perez: Perez became the father of Hezron, and Hezron became the father of Ram, and Ram became the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab became the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon became the father of Salmon, and Salmon became the father of Boaz, and Boaz became the father of Obed, and Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse became the father of David.

In the line of a Moabite woman who had said "your people shall be my people," and in the line of a man who had spread his garment over her on the threshing floor at night, a king of Israel was born.
