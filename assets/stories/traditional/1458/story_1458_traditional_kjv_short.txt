So Boaz took Ruth, and she was his wife.

The Lord gave her conception, and she bare a son.

The women of Bethlehem came unto Naomi when the boy was born. They had walked the same streets wherein she had walked when she had returned from Moab with no husband, and no son, and no field of her own. They had seen her come back empty. Now were they come to her in her own house, where a child cried.

They said unto her, "Blessed be the Lord, which hath not left thee this day without a kinsman. And let his name be famous in Israel. And he shall be unto thee a restorer of thy life, and a nourisher of thine old age: for thy daughter in law, which loveth thee, which is better to thee than seven sons, hath born him."

And Naomi took the child.

And she laid it in her bosom, and became nurse unto it.

The women her neighbours gave it a name. They said, "There is a son born to Naomi." And they called his name Obed.

The years went by in Bethlehem.

Obed grew up in his father's house. He became a man in Bethlehem of Judah. He took him a wife and had a son. The son of Obed was named Jesse.

The years went by again.

Jesse grew up in Bethlehem. He became a man. He kept his father's fields. He had eight sons. The youngest of his sons was sent forth into the hills to keep his father's sheep.

The youngest of his sons was named David.

Now these are the generations of Pharez: Pharez begat Hezron, and Hezron begat Ram, and Ram begat Amminadab, and Amminadab begat Nahshon, and Nahshon begat Salmon, and Salmon begat Boaz, and Boaz begat Obed, and Obed begat Jesse, and Jesse begat David.

In the line of a Moabitish woman that had said "thy people shall be my people," and in the line of a man that had spread his skirt over her on the threshing floor by night, a king of Israel was born.
