Naaman was captain of the host of the king of Syria. He was a great man with his master. He was honorable. By him the LORD had given deliverance to Syria. He was a mighty man in valor. He was a leper.

A little maid had been carried captive out of the land of Israel. She waited on Naaman's wife. She said to her mistress: would that my lord were with the prophet who is in Samaria. He would recover him of his leprosy.

Naaman told the king of Syria. The king sent a letter with him to the king of Israel. Naaman went, with him ten talents of silver and six thousand pieces of gold and ten changes of raiment.

The king of Israel rent his clothes when he read the letter. He said: am I God, to kill and to make alive, that this man sends to me to recover a man of his leprosy?

Elisha the man of God heard. He sent to the king: let him come to me, and he shall know that there is a prophet in Israel.

Naaman came with his horses and with his chariots. He stood at the door of the house of Elisha.

Elisha sent a messenger to him. The messenger said: go and wash in the Jordan seven times. Your flesh shall come again to you. You shall be clean.

Naaman was angry. He went away. He said: I thought he would come out to me. I thought he would stand and call on the name of the LORD his God. I thought he would strike his hand over the place and recover the leper. Are not Abana and Pharpar, the rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? May I not wash in them and be clean?

His servants came near to him. They said: my father, if the prophet had asked you to do some great thing, would you not have done it? How much rather then, when he says to you, wash and be clean?

Naaman went down. He dipped himself seven times in the Jordan, according to the saying of the man of God. His flesh came again like the flesh of a little child. He was clean.
