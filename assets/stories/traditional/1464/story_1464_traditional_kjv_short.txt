The servant of Elisha rose early.

He went forth from the house where Elisha sojourned in Dothan. The morning had but newly come over the hills.

He looked toward the city wall, and beyond the wall, and he beheld that which had not been there the night before.

The host of Aram was round about the city.

The horses stood in their lines. The chariots stood behind them. The captains in their armour were on the field already. The light of the morning was upon the iron of their spears.

The servant turned and ran back into the house.

He came to Elisha where Elisha was sitting. He could not steady his voice when he spake.

"Alas, my master," said he. "How shall we do?"

Elisha beheld him.

"Fear not," said he. "For they that be with us are more than they that be with them."

The servant understood not. There were many of them without the city wall. There were two men within the house.

Then Elisha prayed.

"Lord," said he, "I pray thee, open his eyes, that he may see."

The Lord opened the young man's eyes.

The servant turned and beheld again toward the wall.

The mountain that stood above Dothan was on fire.

There were horses of fire upon the slopes thereof. There were chariots of fire upon the ridge. The hillside above the city was an host of fire, and the host of fire was round about Elisha. The fire moved as fire doth, but it burned not the grass of the hillside, and it heated not the morning air.

The servant spake not.

He saw that which had been round about them all along. The fire stood upon the hillside above Dothan.
