God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Therefore we will not fear, though the earth be removed and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea — though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling of them. Though the whole earth be shaken and the sea surge, though the firmest hills tremble upon their ancient foundations, yet the hearts of the faithful will not be moved. For the Lord God is their shelter and abiding stronghold, whose word endures forevermore.

There is a river, the streams of which make glad the city of God. Its waters flow gently, bringing peace and delight to the dwellings of the Most High. In the holy place of the dwellings of the Most High, the Lord is in the midst of her. She will not be moved. The city will stand unafraid, for God will help her, and that right early, at the dawn of morning.

The nations raged. The peoples made a tumult. Kingdoms were moved. He lifted his voice, and the earth melted away. At the sound of his mighty voice, chaos is silenced. The furious throng is dissolved. The roaring of tumultuous peoples is made as nothing. The Lord of hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.

Come, behold the works of the Lord, what desolations he has made in the earth. He makes wars to cease unto the end of the earth. He breaks the bow, and cuts the spear in pieces. He burns the chariot in the fire. The instruments of battle he destroys. The thunder of war is stilled by his command. He scatters the weapons of men, and their proud inventions he reduces to nothing.

Amid all the terrors and after the storms, a sacred voice speaks to the children of men: "Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations. I will be exalted in the earth." So all tumult will cease, and silence will fall upon every warring heart, and the Lord alone will be magnified over every nation and in all the earth. The Lord of hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.

Hear now the unfolding of these mighty truths. In the hour of trembling, when the world reels upon its axes, and fear stalks wide as floodwaters, the people of God look not to chariots, nor to horsemen, nor to their own strength, but to the Lord, who is their shield and strong tower. Though the mountains be torn from their place and hurled into the boiling sea — though the depths rage and the billows roar, though foundations shake and shadows lengthen — the children of the Lord lift no cry of despair, but stand unshaken, upheld by the might of their Defender.

In the disturbance of the nations, in the sounding of armies and the swelling pride of kings, the Lord remains in his temple, his presence calm as the river that flows through the city of God. The dwellings of the Most High, the home of his people, are not forsaken nor overthrown. God is in the midst of her, and she will not be moved. He will help her at the turning of the morning, and his deliverance will rise with the breaking of dawn.

Among the nations the tumult falls. At his voice kingdoms are shaken. The earth itself melts at his utterance. Yet the Lord of hosts, the Lord of angel armies, remains among his own, shielding them. The God of Jacob, the covenant-keeper, is a refuge to all who trust in him.

Let all who draw breath come and see the Master's works: the desolation he has wrought upon the proud, the peace he has spread upon the earth after storm and ruin. He stops the clash of sword against shield. He causes the burning chariot to be utterly consumed. He breaks the bow and shatters the spear. Every device of war lies broken, and the sound of battle falls quiet at his decree.

In the hush that follows, the thunder of God's word resounds: "Be still, and know that I am God. Let the striving of men be silent. Let their devices fall vain, for I alone will be exalted over the nations and above all the earth." Before him, every mouth is stopped, every heart is hushed, acknowledging the sovereignty of the Most High.

The Lord of hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge, our place of safety, our unassailable shelter. So, from the greatest height to the lowest depth, from the mount trembling to the quiet river, all things serve to glorify him. In the hour of fear and in the day of peace, his name is our steadfast hope.

So the Psalm is remembered and recited — a song of trust and calm assurance in the power and presence of Almighty God, who rules amid the chaos, who sets all strife to rest, and who makes glad the hearts of his people. The Lord of hosts is with us evermore. The God of Jacob is our refuge and strength. Selah.
