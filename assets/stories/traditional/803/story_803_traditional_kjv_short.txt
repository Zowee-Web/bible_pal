In the days of trouble, when the earth was moved and the mountains were cast into the heart of the sea, there gathered a company of the faithful; their faces were turned unto the Lord, and their voices lifted as one. The waters roared and were troubled, yea, the mountains shook with the swelling thereof. But midst the tumult and the storm, the people proclaimed: “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Therefore will we not fear.”

Amid the city, where the river ran serenely, making glad the dwelling of the Most High, the sanctuary of the Lord abode unshaken. Hearts were made firm, for God was in the midst thereof; she should not be moved. At the dawning of the morning, the Lord did help her, for His word endureth.

Though the heathen raged and kingdoms were moved, the Lord uttered His voice, and the earth melted away before it. The Lord of hosts was with His people; the God of Jacob was their refuge and shield.

The company beheld the works of the Lord: He it was who made desolations in the earth. He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the world; He breaketh the bow in sunder, and cutteth the spear asunder; He burneth the chariot in the fire. Even as the hosts of armies gathered and the tumult of battle rumbled nigh, the voice of the Lord thundered above all: “Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.”

Then a hush fell upon the assembly, for the presence of the Lord was among them. They spake again together, “The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge.” And though the mountains quaked and the waves roared, the hearts of the righteous remained steadfast, sheltered beneath the shadow of the Almighty, for God Himself was their strength and their song in the day of trouble.
