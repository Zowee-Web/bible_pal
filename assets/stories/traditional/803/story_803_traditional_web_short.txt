In the days of trouble, when the earth was moved and the mountains were cast into the heart of the sea, a company of the faithful gathered. Their faces were turned toward the Lord, and their voices lifted as one. The waters roared and were troubled. The mountains shook with the swelling of them. But in the midst of the tumult and the storm, the people proclaimed: "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Therefore we will not fear."

In the city, where the river ran serenely, making glad the dwelling of the Most High, the sanctuary of the Lord stood unshaken. Hearts were made firm, for God was in the midst of her. She would not be moved. At the dawning of the morning, the Lord helped her, for his word endures.

Though the nations raged and kingdoms were moved, the Lord lifted his voice, and the earth melted away before it. The Lord of hosts was with his people. The God of Jacob was their refuge and shield.

The company saw the works of the Lord — he it was who made desolations in the earth. He makes wars to cease unto the end of the world. He breaks the bow in pieces. He cuts the spear asunder. He burns the chariot in the fire. Even as the armies gathered and the tumult of battle rumbled near, the voice of the Lord thundered above all: "Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations. I will be exalted in the earth."

Then a hush fell upon the assembly, for the presence of the Lord was among them. They spoke again together: "The Lord of hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge." And though the mountains quaked and the waves roared, the hearts of the righteous remained steadfast, sheltered beneath the shadow of the Almighty, for God himself was their strength and their song in the day of trouble.
