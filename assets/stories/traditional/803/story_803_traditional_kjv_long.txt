God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Lo, we begin:

In ages of old, midst a world sorely shaken, 'tis declared: God is our refuge, an high tower that faileth not, a shield steadfast, yea, and exceeding strong, a dwelling wherein no evil may befall; and our strength, a mighty fortification, answering to all want and wrestling, our sure stay in every storm. Even now, He is both help and hope: no distance betwixt His mercy and our cry—the Lord God of hosts, a present help in trouble; He abideth nigh, full ready in every distress.

Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and mountains totter into the heart of the sea. Though tumult and quaking afflict the land, and the very foundations of creation shake, yet the hearts of those that trust in the Lord remain steadfast. For though the earth tremble, and though the mountains, ancient as the primeval word, do slip and are cast into the midst of the roaring sea, yet the people of the Most High are unshaken. The seas rise and cover the deep, crying aloud with their voice and fury, yet God’s own abide.

Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Behold the waves, surged high as the cedars, white with foam, unresting, their continual voice crying ruin; the turbulent waters lifting up themselves, a power to bear down ships and cast cities down. The mountains, solid and unyielding, do tremble at their increase, their roots shaken to the depths below. Selah.

But—there is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God, the holy place of the tabernacles of the Most High. A river unseen by mortal eye, yet purer than crystal, an unfailing flow; its silver streams winding, gladdening the city set apart for the King. Therein, amidst the holy habitation of the tabernacles of the Most High, do these streams bring refreshing; joy is in the midst of her, comfort flowing, holy gladness moving within her walls. The city is not moved, for God is in the midst of her; His presence is her sure foundation and her perpetual delight.

God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: God shall help her, and that right early. While dawn yet goldens the eastern sky, before man ariseth to labor and the cocks have crowed, God bringeth forth His help. The Lord breaketh forth as the morning, defending, upholding, never tarrying. Though shadows press on every side, the daystar of His faithfulness shall sudden appear.

The heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved: He uttered His voice, the earth melted. Yea, the nations gathered in tumult, the peoples gave forth clamour and fury: their banners raised, their armies assembling, counsels standing against the Lord’s own. But at a word spoken from His throne, the tumult stilled; He uttered His voice, and lo, the earth melted away as wax before the fire—mighty kings bowing, cities dissolving, all creation yielding unto the voice of Him who sitteth as King forever.

The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah. Pause and ponder: He that led Jacob, wrestling angel and mortal both, He who covenanted by promise and by blessing, remaineth our fortress to all generations. He is not far nor unhearing, but present and sure, setting His standards round about His people in the day of trouble.

Come, behold the works of the Lord, what desolations He hath wrought in the earth. O ye children of men, lift up your eyes upon His handiwork: behold, how He stretcheth forth His hand and the nations are scattered, how He maketh waste the pride of the earth, how He bringeth down the strongholds of them that set themselves against His will. Wonders beyond telling and judgments terrible in their swiftness; desolations wrought in the earth, that all flesh may know He alone is God.

He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the earth; He breaketh the bow, and cutteth the spear in sunder; He burneth the chariot in the fire. Where men bestir themselves, devising battle and weapons, seeking dominion by sword and by spear, the Lord maketh an end. No more the rattling of chariots nor the shout of captains; no more the muster of armies nor the coming of battle. The fiery bow is snapped as a branch in the hand; the storied spear dashed and shattered; the chariots, engines of terror, committed to the flame. Quiet reigneth in their place, for where the Lord decrees Peace, who can make war?

Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth. Thus the voice of the Lord speaketh: “Cease thy strivings, O man, and hush thine anxious care. Refrain thy hand, for here is One greater than all; be still before thy Maker and consider. Know thou that I am God—high above the raging nations, above all kings and princes, among every tribe and tongue. Exalted am I among the heathen, above every name, lifted on high among the peoples unto the ends of earth. The glory is the Lord’s; the power is the Lord’s; the praise is the Lord’s forever and always.

The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah. Let every heart bow, and every tongue confess: the Lord of angels and armies, all of heaven’s host, tarrieth not afar off but abideth with His loved ones. The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob—ancient of days, faithful through every lineage and every fleeting age—He is a stronghold, an ever-present help. Under the shadow of His wings do His people rest, and their safety abideth sure.

Thus, amid trembling earth and disquieted nations, amid roar of flood and breaking bough, amid wars and desolations and all the vain attempts of men to make themselves strong, the people of God fear not, for God is their refuge, and the Most High their strength; in Him is found steadfast gladness, and His dwelling is their safeguard forever.

Behold, the Lord reigneth. The tumult subsides at His rebuke, the weapons of war vanish at His command, peace flows like a river from His presence, and joy fills the city dedicated unto His name. The mountains may fall and the earth depart, but His promise standeth unshaken, His help is swifter than the dawn, and His presence an high fort that faileth not, world without end.

So, be still and know: the Lord of hosts abideth with His people. The God of Jacob is the refuge of all that call upon His name. Selah.
