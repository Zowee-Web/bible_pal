God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. So we begin.

In ages of old, in a world sorely shaken, it is declared: God is our refuge, a high tower that does not fail, a shield steadfast and exceeding strong, a dwelling where no evil may befall. He is our strength — a mighty fortification, answering all want and wrestling, our sure stay in every storm. Even now, he is both help and hope. There is no distance between his mercy and our cry — the Lord God of hosts, a present help in trouble. He stays near, fully ready in every distress.

Therefore we will not fear, though the earth be removed, and the mountains totter into the heart of the sea. Though tumult and quaking afflict the land, and the very foundations of creation shake, yet the hearts of those that trust in the Lord remain steadfast. For though the earth tremble, and though the mountains, ancient as the primeval word, slip and are cast into the midst of the roaring sea, yet the people of the Most High are unshaken. The seas rise and cover the deep, crying aloud with their voice and fury, yet God's own remain.

Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling of them. Behold the waves, surging high as the cedars, white with foam, restless, their continual voice crying ruin — the turbulent waters lifting up themselves, a power to bear down ships and cast cities down. The mountains, solid and unyielding, tremble at their rising, their roots shaken to the depths below. Selah.

But — there is a river, the streams of which make glad the city of God, the holy place of the dwellings of the Most High. A river unseen by mortal eye, yet purer than crystal, an unfailing flow. Its silver streams wind through the city set apart for the King, gladdening it. There, amid the holy habitation of the dwellings of the Most High, these streams bring refreshing. Joy is in the midst of her. Comfort flows. Holy gladness moves within her walls. The city is not moved, for God is in the midst of her. His presence is her sure foundation and her perpetual delight.

God is in the midst of her. She will not be moved. God will help her, and that right early. While dawn still gilds the eastern sky, before man rises to labor and the roosters have crowed, God brings forth his help. The Lord breaks forth as the morning, defending, upholding, never delaying. Though shadows press on every side, the day-star of his faithfulness will suddenly appear.

The nations raged. The kingdoms were moved. He lifted his voice. The earth melted. Yes, the nations gathered in tumult. The peoples gave forth clamor and fury. Their banners were raised. Their armies assembled. Their counsels stood against the Lord's own. But at a word spoken from his throne, the tumult stilled. He lifted his voice, and the earth melted away as wax before the fire — mighty kings bowing, cities dissolving, all creation yielding to the voice of him who sits as King forever.

The Lord of hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah. Pause and ponder. He who led Jacob, wrestling angel and mortal both, he who covenanted by promise and by blessing, remains our fortress to all generations. He is not far, nor unhearing, but present and sure, setting his standards round about his people in the day of trouble.

Come, behold the works of the Lord, what desolations he has wrought in the earth. O children of men, lift up your eyes upon his handiwork: see how he stretches forth his hand and the nations are scattered, how he lays waste the pride of the earth, how he brings down the strongholds of those that set themselves against his will. Wonders beyond telling and judgments terrible in their swiftness — desolations wrought in the earth, that all flesh may know he alone is God.

He makes wars to cease unto the end of the earth. He breaks the bow, and cuts the spear in pieces. He burns the chariot in the fire. Where men stir themselves, devising battle and weapons, seeking dominion by sword and spear, the Lord makes an end. No more the rattling of chariots, nor the shout of captains. No more the muster of armies, nor the coming of battle. The fiery bow is snapped as a branch in the hand. The storied spear is dashed and shattered. The chariots, engines of terror, are committed to the flame. Quiet reigns in their place, for where the Lord decrees peace, who can make war?

Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations. I will be exalted in the earth. So the voice of the Lord speaks: "Cease your strivings, O man, and hush your anxious care. Hold back your hand, for here is One greater than all. Be still before your Maker and consider. Know that I am God — high above the raging nations, above all kings and princes, among every tribe and tongue. Exalted am I among the nations, above every name, lifted on high among the peoples to the ends of the earth. The glory is the Lord's. The power is the Lord's. The praise is the Lord's forever and always."

The Lord of hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah. Let every heart bow, and every tongue confess: the Lord of angels and armies, of all heaven's host, does not stay far off, but remains with his loved ones. The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — ancient of days, faithful through every lineage and every fleeting age — he is a stronghold, an ever-present help. Under the shadow of his wings his people rest, and their safety remains sure.

So, amid trembling earth and disquieted nations, amid the roar of flood and breaking bough, amid wars and desolations and all the vain attempts of men to make themselves strong, the people of God fear not, for God is their refuge, and the Most High their strength. In him is found steadfast gladness, and his dwelling is their safeguard forever.

Behold, the Lord reigns. The tumult subsides at his rebuke. The weapons of war vanish at his command. Peace flows like a river from his presence, and joy fills the city dedicated to his name. The mountains may fall and the earth depart, but his promise stands unshaken. His help is swifter than the dawn. His presence is a high fortress that does not fail, world without end.

So, be still and know: the Lord of hosts remains with his people. The God of Jacob is the refuge of all that call upon his name. Selah.
