God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Therefore shall not we fear, though the earth be removed and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea; though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Though the whole earth be shaken and the sea surge, though the firmest hills tremble upon their ancient bases, yet shall the hearts of the faithful not be moved. For the Lord God is their shelter and abiding stronghold, whose word endureth evermore.

There is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God. Its waters flow gently, bringing peace and delight to the tabernacles of the Most High. In the holy place of the tabernacles of the Most High, the Lord is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved, the city shall stand unafraid, for God shall help her, and that right early, at the dawn of morning.

The nations raged, the heathen did make a tumult; kingdoms were moved; he uttered his voice, and the earth melted away. At the sound of His mighty voice, chaos is silenced, the furious throng is dissolved, and the roaring of tumultuous peoples is made as nought. The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.

Come, behold the works of the Lord, what desolations He hath made in the earth. He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the earth; He breaketh the bow, and cutteth the spear in sunder; He burneth the chariot in the fire. The implements of battle doth He destroy, and the thunder of war is stilled by His commandment. He scattereth the armaments of men, and their proud inventions He reduceth to nothing.

Amid all the terrors and after the storms, a sacred voice speaketh unto the children of men saying: “Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.” So shall all tumult cease, and silence fall upon every warring heart, and the Lord alone shall be magnified over every nation and in all the earth. The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.

Hearken now unto the unfolding of these mighty truths. In the hour of trembling, when the world reel’th upon its axes, and fear stalketh wide as floodwaters, the people of God look not to chariots nor to horsemen nor to their own strength, but unto the Lord, who is their shield and strong tower. Though the mountains be torn from their place and hurled into the boiling sea—though the depths rage and the billows roar, though foundations shake and shadows lengthen—the children of the Lord lift not up a cry of despair, but stand unshaken, upheld by the might of their Defender.

Midst the disturbance of the nations, amidst the sounding of armies and the swelling pride of kings, the Lord abideth in His temple, His presence calm as the river that floweth through the city of God. The tabernacles of the Most High, the dwelling-place of His people, are not forsaken nor overthrown; God is in the midst of her, and she shall not be moved. He shall help her at the turning of the morn, and His deliverance shall arise with the breaking of dawn.

Among the nations the tumult falleth, at His voice kingdoms are shaken; the earth itself melteth at His utterance. Yet the Lord of hosts, the Lord of Angel armies, abideth among His own, shielding them. The God of Jacob, the covenant-keeper, is a refuge unto all who trust in Him.

Let all who draw breath come and see the Master’s works: the desolation He hath wrought upon the proud, the peace He hath spread upon the earth after storm and ruin. He stoppeth the clash of sword against shield, causeth the burning chariot to be utterly consumed, breaketh the bow and shattereth the spear. Every device of war lieth broken, and the sound of battle falleth quiet at His decree.

In the hush that followeth, the thunder of God’s word resoundeth: “Be still, and know that I am God. Let the striving of men be silent, let their devices fall vain, for I alone shall be exalted over the nations and above all the earth.” Before Him, every mouth is stopped, every heart is hushed, acknowledging the sovereignty of the Most High.

The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge, our place of safety, our unassailable shelter. Thus, from the greatest height to the lowest depth, from the mount trembling to the quiet river, all things serve to glorify Him. In the hour of fear and in the day of peace, His name is our steadfast hope.

Thus is the Psalm remembered and recited, a song of trust and calm assurance in the power and presence of Almighty God, who ruleth amid the chaos, who setteth all strife to rest, and maketh glad the hearts of His people. The Lord of hosts is with us evermore; the God of Jacob is our refuge and strength. Selah.
