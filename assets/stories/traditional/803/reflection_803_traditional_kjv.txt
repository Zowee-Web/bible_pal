In the midst of trembling earth and roaring waters, the six-and-fortieth Psalm setteth forth an image of a steadfast refuge. Patterns of turmoil and steadfastness stand side by side: mountains may shake, yet there is a river whose streams make glad the city of God. The listener may notice how, even as troubles swirl, the refrain pointeth again and again unto presence and stillness.

There are times, both in ancient poetry and in daily hours, when chaos and calm intermingle. The psalm offereth language that holdeth these together — a quiet center amid the storm. Such words seem to rest in tension, inviting reflection upon where unshakable peace and shelter are glimpsed amid ordinary commotion.
