In the eighteenth year of his reign, Josiah kept a Passover to Yahweh in Jerusalem. They killed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

He set the priests in their offices and encouraged them in the service of Yahweh's house.

Then he spoke to the Levites, who taught all Israel and were holy to Yahweh.

"Put the holy ark in the house which Solomon the son of David king of Israel built. It will no longer be a burden on your shoulders. Now serve Yahweh your God and his people Israel."

The carrying was over.

"Prepare yourselves by your fathers' houses and your divisions, according to the writing of David king of Israel and of Solomon his son. Stand in the holy place. Kill the Passover lamb, sanctify yourselves, and prepare for your brothers, to do according to Yahweh's word by Moses."

Then the king opened his own herds. Thirty thousand lambs and young goats went out to the people, and three thousand bulls, all from the king's substance.

His princes gave freely too. Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, the rulers of God's house, gave the priests two thousand six hundred small livestock and three hundred cattle. The chiefs of the Levites — Conaniah, Shemaiah, Nethanel, Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad — gave the Levites five thousand small livestock and five hundred cattle.

So the service was prepared. The priests stood in their place, and the Levites by their divisions, according to the king's commandment.

They killed the Passover lambs. The priests sprinkled the blood they received from the Levites' hands, and the Levites skinned the animals.

They set the burnt offerings aside to give out by the fathers' houses, to offer to Yahweh as it is written in the book of Moses. They did the same with the cattle.

They roasted the Passover with fire according to the ordinance. The holy offerings they boiled in pots, in cauldrons, and in pans, and carried them quickly to all the people.

Afterward the Levites prepared for themselves and for the priests, because the sons of Aaron were busy with the burnt offerings and the fat until night.

The singers, the sons of Asaph, stood in their place, according to the commandment of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun the king's seer. The gatekeepers were at every gate. Not one of them had to leave his post, because their brothers the Levites prepared for them.

All the service of Yahweh was prepared that same day — the Passover kept, the burnt offerings offered on Yahweh's altar, at the commandment of King Josiah.

The children of Israel who were present kept the Passover, and the feast of unleavened bread seven days.

There had been no Passover like it in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. No king of Israel had kept such a Passover, with the priests, the Levites, all Judah and Israel, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
