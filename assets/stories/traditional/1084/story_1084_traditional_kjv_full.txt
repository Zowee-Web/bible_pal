On a certain day, as Jesus taught the people in the open country, the sun hung low and warm above the gathering. Men and women sat upon the dry ground or leaned against the trunks of scattered trees, hearkening unto his words. Among them was a certain lawyer, a man learned in the law of Moses, who rose up from his place with careful bearing.

"Master," said he, "what shall I do to inherit eternal life?"

Jesus said unto him, "What is written in the law? How readest thou?"

He answered and said, "Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thy mind; and thy neighbour as thyself."

Jesus said unto him, "Thou hast answered right: this do, and thou shalt live."

But he, willing to justify himself, pressed the matter further. "And who is my neighbour?" he asked.

Jesus answered him with a parable.

"A certain man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho. The road was long and wound through barren hills and rocky country where thieves were known to hide. And he fell among thieves, which stripped him of his raiment, and wounded him, and departed, leaving him half dead at the side of the way.

"And by chance there came down a certain priest that way. He was a man of the temple, one who served before the altar. And when he saw the wounded man, he passed by on the other side, and went on his way.

"And likewise a Levite, when he was at the place, came and looked on him. He beheld the blood and the torn raiment and the stillness of the body. And he also passed by on the other side.

"But a certain Samaritan, as he journeyed, came where he was: and when he saw him, he had compassion on him. He went to him and knelt down in the dust. He bound up his wounds, pouring in oil and wine. He set him on his own beast, and brought him to an inn, and took care of him through the night. The lamps burned low, and the road grew quiet without.

"And on the morrow when he departed, he took out two pence, and gave them to the host, and said unto him, 'Take care of him; and whatsoever thou spendest more, when I come again, I will repay thee.'"

Jesus looked upon the lawyer. The crowd was still.

"Which now of these three, thinkest thou, was neighbour unto him that fell among the thieves?"

The lawyer answered quietly. "He that shewed mercy on him."

Then said Jesus unto him, "Go, and do thou likewise."
