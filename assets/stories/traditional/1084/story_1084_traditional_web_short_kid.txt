One day, a man asked Jesus a question. "What do I need to do to live forever?"

Jesus asked him back, "What does the law say?"

The man answered, "Love God with all your heart, and love your neighbor as yourself."

"That's right," Jesus said. "Do that and you will live."

But the man wanted to know more. "Who is my neighbor?" he asked.

So Jesus told him a story.

A man was walking down a long, lonely road from Jerusalem to Jericho. Robbers jumped out and hurt him badly. They took his clothes and left him lying on the ground. He couldn't move.

A priest came walking down the road. He saw the hurt man lying there. But he walked right past him on the other side.

Then another important man came by. He looked at the hurt man too. But he also walked right past.

Then a Samaritan came along. When he saw the man lying in the road, he felt sorry for him. He stopped right away. He cleaned up the man's cuts and wrapped them with bandages. He put the man on his own donkey and took him to a safe place where he could rest and get better.

The next morning, the Samaritan gave money to the innkeeper. "Take care of him," he said. "If it costs more, I'll pay you back when I come through again."

Jesus looked at the man who had asked the question.

"Which one of those three people was a good neighbor to the man who got hurt?"

The man said, "The one who helped him."

Jesus said, "Go and do the same thing."
