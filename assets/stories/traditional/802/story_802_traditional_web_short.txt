The Lord is my shepherd. I lack nothing. He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside still waters. In the soft morning light, I walk beneath his watchful eye, wanting for nothing. The grass beneath me is fresh and cool, and the breeze moves gently all around. With every step, the cup of my needs is filled. My Shepherd's hand provides for all.

He restores my soul. He leads me in paths of righteousness for his name's sake. When my strength fails and weariness reaches into my bones, he lifts me up, and in his kindness sets my feet again upon the way. Each turning of the road he watches, so my steps do not wander after empty things, but stay close to his uprightness, for the Lord's glory alone.

Yes, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil — for you are with me. Even as I pass beneath the gloom, where shadows press hard upon my spirit and the way seems shrouded in deep danger, my heart does not tremble. For your presence is my company, and the strength of your nearness holds me up. Your rod and your staff, they comfort me. Your scepter of guidance, your stave of defense — these are both my safeguard and my solace.

You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies. You anoint my head with oil. My cup overflows. Even within sight of those who wish me harm, my Lord sets out a feast. He pours sweet oil upon my head, the sign of favor and honor, and the vessel of my life fills to the brim, until it overflows with abundance.

Surely goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the Lord forever. As the sun rises and sets, so the lovingkindness of the Lord surrounds me. His favor and his compassion are my constant companions, and his dwelling place will be my abiding home through all the ages. So I walk, ever in the light of his keeping, and there I will remain, evermore.
