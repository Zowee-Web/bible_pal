The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: He leadeth me beside the still waters. Thus in the soft light of the morning, I walk beneath His watchful eye, lacking for nothing. The grass beneath me is verdant and cool, and all about me the breeze moveth gently. With every step, my cup of needs is filled; the hand of my Shepherd provideth for all.

He restoreth my soul; He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name’s sake. When my strength faltereth and weariness creepeth unto my bones, He lifeth me up, and in His good pleasure setteth my feet again upon the way. Each turning of the road He marketh, so my tread wandereth not after vanity, but cleaveth close unto His uprightness, for the Lord’s glory alone.

Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for Thou art with me. Even as I pass ‘neath the gloom where shadows press hard upon my spirit and the way seemeth shrouded in deep peril, yet my heart quaketh not. For Thy presence is my compan’y, and the strength of Thy nearness girdeth me. Thy rod and Thy staff, they comfort me. Thy scepter of guidance, Thy stave of defense—these art both my safeguard and solace.

Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies; Thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Behold, in the very sight of them that wish me ill, my Lord layeth forth a feast. He poureth sweet oil upon my head, the sign of favor and honor, and the vessel of my life brimmeth full, till it overfloweth with abundance.

Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever. As the sun riseth and setteth, so the lovingkindness of the Lord encompasseth me. His favor and compassion are my constant companions, and His dwelling-place shall be my abiding home unto the ages. Thus walk I, ever in the light of His keeping, and there shall I remain, evermore.
