The Lord is my Shepherd; I shall not want. Thus it is written, and thus it came to pass in the ordering of the days of David, the sweet psalmist of Israel. Attend, O ye children of men, to the song of the shepherded soul, and behold in the word the quiet pasture, the stillness of cool waters, and the path made straight by the guiding hand of the Most High. 

The Lord—Jehovah, the Keeper of Israel—He tendeth the sheep of His pasture as a good shepherd. He abideth with them; He watcheth o’er them by day, and in the watches of the night. He knoweth each by name; none is hidden from His eyes, neither the strong ram nor the feeble lamb, neither the wanderer nor the meek, for all are His, the flock of His hand.

Because the Lord liveth, and because His rod and staff comfort, there abideth no want in the soul that trusteth in Him. Though lean the land may seem, and though burning sun smite upon the hills, the sheep of the Lord want nothing; for He supplieth them daily, according to His goodness and lovingkindness.

He maketh me to lie down in green pastures; so He leadeth, so He giveth rest. See now the sheep lying down, the grass beneath them dewy and fresh, each blade appointed to the nourishing of their life. Not in haste do they hasten; for the Shepherd, with wise voice and gentle crook, setteth them to rest in safety. The wolf may lurk abroad, and the winds may rise, yet there, in the fold of the Lord’s choosing, the soul hath sweet repose.

He leadeth me beside the still waters: no torrent rageth, neither is the stream troubled. Tranquil and clear is the brook appointed of God, flowing softly, reflecting light from above. The sheep drink and are satisfied; their thirst quenched in the peace bestowed by the hand that made heaven and earth. In the noonday glare or in the hush of dawn, the Shepherd knoweth where the waters run calm, and He bringeth His own unto the place of cool refreshment.

He restoreth my soul: when the weak falleth, He raiseth them; when one wandereth and groweth faint, He seeketh and upholdeth. His hand bindeth up the broken, His balm healeth the wounded heart. Where shadows gather, and the spirit is cast down, the Shepherd’s presence giveth life anew; fear is vanquished, courage is restored, and the soul findeth her joy again.

He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name’s sake. Not in cunning byways of deceit, nor in the dark wood of sin, but in the fair and open way, the Shepherd guideth. He counteth it for His praise to keep His flock in holiness; His law shineth as a light on the path, and His promise filleth the air with hope. For His name—holy and mighty—He leadeth His sheep, lest any forget whose they are and whom they serve.

Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil. Behold the descent, the hollow beneath the mountain, where gloom setteth upon the earth and the air is thick with dread. Yet the sheep go forward, for the Shepherd goeth before them; His presence is their shield, His voice their assurance. There is no peril, no lurking foe, no shadow so deep, but the rod and staff of the Lord comfort and defend. The valley is but a passage; and beyond, the sun of righteousness ariseth.

Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies. See how the Lord setteth sustenance and bounty for His beloved, even while adversaries look on. Dangers press round about, snares are laid in the secret places, but the Shepherd feedeth His own with the bread of peace and the cup of blessing. None can hinder, none can take away, for the table is spread by the hand of the Almighty.

Thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. The oil of gladness, the sign of favour, the blessing that cometh down as dew upon the flock—so the Lord poureth forth abundance. The cup is not meagre nor scant, but presseth to the brim and overfloweth; and the soul made whole lifteth her voice in thanksgiving.

Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life. Mark, O soul, the pursuers that are patient and kind: goodness, that sheltereth; mercy, that pitieth. They shadow the steps of the faithful, from the dawn of youth until the evening of age. In every field, in every storm, goodness leadeth, mercy upholdeth.

And I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever. The way leadeth not to a withered end, nor to a lonely field; but even as the Shepherd abideth, so shall I abide, to behold the beauty of the Lord, to inquire in His temple, world without end.

Thus speaketh the Psalm, and thus resteth the soul, content and secure in the Shepherd’s keeping, from evermore to evermore.
