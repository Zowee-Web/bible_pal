It was the third hour of the day when they put him on the cross. The crowd was around him on the hill that men called the Place of the Skull. The soldiers were dividing his garments below him, casting lots for the seamless robe. The women who had followed him from Galilee stood at a distance and watched.

The first word he said was for the soldiers.

"Father, forgive them, for they don't know what they are doing."

It was not the kind of thing said in that place. The rulers passed beneath him and scoffed. "He saved others," they said. "Let him save himself, if this is the Christ of God, his chosen one." The soldiers, coming and going, mocked him. They offered him vinegar. One called up to him about saving the king of the Jews.

Two criminals had been crucified with him, one on his right and one on his left. The one on the left railed against him in the same words the rulers had used. The one on the right answered him back. "Don't you even fear God, seeing you are under the same condemnation? We are here for what we have done. This man has done nothing wrong."

Then he turned his face toward Jesus.

"Lord, remember me when you come into your kingdom."

Jesus turned his head. His second word was for the man beside him.

"Assuredly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise."

It was about the sixth hour now. The sun was darkened. A heavy darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour. The veil of the temple, far off in the city, was torn in two. The people stood beholding. No one knew what to do.

Then Jesus cried with a loud voice. It was his third word.

"Father, into your hands I commit my spirit."

And having said this, he breathed his last.

The centurion who stood facing him saw what happened and glorified God. He said: certainly, this was a righteous man. And the people who had come together to behold returned home beating their breasts. The women who had followed him from Galilee stood at a distance and watched these things. And so it was on the day the King of the Jews was crucified, on the hill outside the city, between the sixth hour and the ninth.
