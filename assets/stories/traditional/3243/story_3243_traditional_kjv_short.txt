Three years of famine came upon the land in David’s days, year after year. When David enquired of the Lord, the answer named Saul and his bloody house, because he had slain the Gibeonites.

The king summoned the Gibeonites, a remnant of the Amorites rather than children of Israel. Israel had sworn unto them, yet Saul had sought to slay them in his zeal for Israel and Judah.

David asked what he should do, and how he should make atonement, that they might bless the Lord’s inheritance. They would have neither silver nor gold of Saul or his house. Neither, they said, should David kill any man in Israel for them. David promised to do as they said.

They named Saul as the man who had consumed them and devised their destruction from Israel’s coasts. They asked for seven of his sons, to hang them unto the Lord at Gibeah of Saul, whom the Lord had chosen. David answered, “I will give them.”

For the Lord’s oath between himself and Jonathan, David spared Jonathan’s son Mephibosheth. But he took Rizpah’s two sons by Saul, Armoni and Mephibosheth. Rizpah was the daughter of Aiah. He took also the five sons of Michal, Saul’s daughter, whom she brought up for Adriel son of Barzillai the Meholathite.

David delivered the seven unto the Gibeonites. They hanged them in the hill before the Lord. All seven fell together in the first days of barley harvest.

Upon a rock Rizpah spread sackcloth for herself. From the beginning of harvest until water dropped upon them out of heaven, she suffered no birds to rest on the bodies by day, nor beasts of the field by night.

Word reached David of what Rizpah, Saul’s concubine, had done. He went to the men of Jabesh–gilead for the bones of Saul and Jonathan. These men had stolen them from the street of Beth–shan, where the Philistines had hanged them when Saul was slain in Gilboa.

David brought up their bones, and the bones of those who had been hanged were gathered also. Saul and Jonathan were buried at Zelah in Benjamin, in the sepulchre of Saul’s father Kish. All that the king commanded was done. After that, God was intreated for the land.
