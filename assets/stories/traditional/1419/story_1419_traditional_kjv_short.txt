The thirteenth and fourteenth days of the month Adar were passed. The Jews in all the provinces of the king Ahasuerus had gathered themselves together for their lives against them that hated them, and they had been delivered; and on the fourteenth and the fifteenth days they had rested, according as they were in the unwalled towns or in the walled cities. And Mordecai, sitting now in the palace at Shushan with the king's seal that had once been Haman's, took up the pen and wrote.

And Mordecai wrote these things, and sent letters unto all the Jews that were in all the provinces of the king Ahasuerus, both nigh and far,

To stablish this among them, that they should keep the fourteenth day of the month Adar, and the fifteenth day of the same, yearly,

As the days wherein the Jews rested from their enemies, and the month which was turned unto them from sorrow to joy, and from mourning into a good day: that they should make them days of feasting and joy, and of sending portions one to another, and gifts to the poor.

And the posts went forth from Shushan with the letters in their hands unto all the provinces of the kingdom. The Jews in the unwalled towns and the Jews in the walled cities received the words; the days of the fourteenth and fifteenth of Adar were marked upon their calendars for the years to come; and the feast that should be called by the lots — Purim, after the Pur that Haman had cast — was established in the houses of the people, with portions sent from neighbour to neighbour and gifts sent out unto the poor.
