After Jacob their father had been buried in the cave of Machpelah, Joseph and his brothers returned together from Canaan to Egypt. The roads were long, and the days of mourning had been many. They came back to their houses in the land of Goshen.

The brothers saw that their father was now dead, and they said among themselves, "It may be that Joseph will hate us, and will fully pay us back for all the evil which we did to him."

So they sent a message to Joseph, saying, "Your father commanded before he died, saying, 'You shall tell Joseph: please forgive the disobedience of your brothers, and their sin, because they did evil to you.' Now, please forgive the disobedience of the servants of the God of your father."

When Joseph heard their words, he wept. The room was silent around him, and the messengers stood waiting. He had not seen his brothers' faces in many days, and the message reached him in the chamber of his house.

Then his brothers themselves went to Joseph. They came before his face and fell down on the ground, and they said, "Behold, we are your servants." Their robes touched the floor, and none of them looked up at his face.

But Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid. Am I in the place of God? As for you, you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good, to bring to pass, as it is today, to save many people alive."

He said again, "Now therefore do not be afraid. I will nourish you and your little ones." So he comforted them and spoke kindly to them. The days of fear came to an end, and the brothers stayed with him in the land of Goshen with their children and their flocks.
