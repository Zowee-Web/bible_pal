The messengers came back to Jerusalem with a letter. They had been sent by Hezekiah unto the camp of the Assyrians at Lachish. The letter was from Sennacherib king of Assyria. Hezekiah read it.

The letter said: let not thy God in whom thou trustest deceive thee, saying Jerusalem shall not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria. The letter named the cities that the king of Assyria had destroyed. The letter named the gods of those cities that had not delivered them. The letter said: the gods of the nations have not delivered them. The God of Israel shall not deliver thee either.

Hezekiah went up unto the house of the LORD. He carried the letter with him through the streets of the city. He went up the steps of the temple. He came in before the LORD. He spread the letter open upon the stone floor.

He prayed. "O LORD God of Israel, which dwellest between the cherubims, thou art the God, even thou alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth. Thou hast made heaven and earth. Lord, bow down thine ear and hear. Open, LORD, thine eyes and see. Hear the words of Sennacherib which hath sent him to reproach the living God. Of a truth, LORD. The kings of Assyria have destroyed the nations and their lands. They have cast their gods into the fire. They were no gods. They were the work of men's hands, of wood and of stone. Therefore they have destroyed them. Now therefore, O LORD our God, I beseech thee, save thou us out of his hand. Let all the kingdoms of the earth know that thou art the LORD God, even thou only."

Isaiah the son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah. The word came: thus saith the LORD God of Israel: I have heard thee. The word of the LORD concerning the king of Assyria is this: He shall not come into this city. He shall not shoot an arrow there. He shall not come before it with shield. He shall not cast a bank against it. By the way that he came, by the same shall he return. He shall not come into this city.

That night the angel of the LORD went forth into the camp of the Assyrians. The camp was without the city. The fires were burning low. The watchmen were at the edges. The horses were tied. The men were sleeping in their tents.

The angel passed through the camp. The angel smote in the camp of the Assyrians an hundred fourscore and five thousand. When the camp awoke in the morning, the men were not there. The morning found them dead corpses in their tents. The few that remained alive looked across the camp. They could not believe what they saw.

Sennacherib king of Assyria departed. He went and returned. He dwelt at Nineveh. There he was slain in his own house by his own sons, in the temple wherein he was worshipping.
