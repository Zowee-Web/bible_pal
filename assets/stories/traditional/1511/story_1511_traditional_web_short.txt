Hezekiah received the letter from the messengers. He read it. The letter said: do not let your God deceive you, saying Jerusalem shall not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria. The letter named the cities the king of Assyria had taken. The letter named the gods of those cities that had not delivered them.

Hezekiah went up to the house of the LORD. He spread the letter before the LORD on the floor of the temple.

He prayed. "O LORD God of Israel, who dwells between the cherubim, you are the God, even you alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth. You have made heaven and earth. Open your eyes, O LORD, and see. Hear the words of Sennacherib that he has sent to reproach the living God. It is true, LORD. The kings of Assyria have destroyed the nations and their lands. They have cast their gods into the fire. They were no gods. They were the work of men's hands, of wood and stone. Therefore they have destroyed them. Now therefore, O LORD our God, save us out of his hand. Let all the kingdoms of the earth know that you are the LORD God, even you only."

Isaiah the son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah. The word came: thus says the LORD God of Israel: I have heard you. As for the king of Assyria, he shall not come into this city. He shall not shoot an arrow there. He shall not come before it with shield. He shall not cast a bank against it. By the way that he came, by the same shall he return. He shall not come into this city.

That night the angel of the LORD went out. He smote in the camp of the Assyrians a hundred and eighty-five thousand. When they arose early in the morning, they were all dead bodies.

Sennacherib king of Assyria departed. He went and returned. He dwelt at Nineveh.
