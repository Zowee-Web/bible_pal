Hezekiah received the letter from the hand of the messengers. He read it. The letter said: let not thy God in whom thou trustest deceive thee, saying Jerusalem shall not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria. The letter named the cities that the king of Assyria had destroyed. The letter named the gods of those cities that had not delivered them.

Hezekiah went up unto the house of the LORD. He spread the letter before the LORD upon the floor of the temple.

He prayed. "O LORD God of Israel, which dwellest between the cherubims, thou art the God, even thou alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth. Thou hast made heaven and earth. Lord, bow down thine ear and hear. Open, LORD, thine eyes and see. Hear the words of Sennacherib which hath sent him to reproach the living God. Of a truth, LORD. The kings of Assyria have destroyed the nations and their lands. They have cast their gods into the fire. They were no gods. They were the work of men's hands, of wood and of stone. Therefore they have destroyed them. Now therefore, O LORD our God, I beseech thee, save thou us out of his hand. Let all the kingdoms of the earth know that thou art the LORD God, even thou only."

Isaiah the son of Amoz sent to Hezekiah. The word came: thus saith the LORD God of Israel: I have heard thee. As for the king of Assyria, he shall not come into this city. He shall not shoot an arrow there. He shall not come before it with shield. He shall not cast a bank against it. By the way that he came, by the same shall he return. He shall not come into this city.

That night the angel of the LORD went forth. He smote in the camp of the Assyrians an hundred fourscore and five thousand. When they arose early in the morning, behold, they were all dead corpses.

Sennacherib king of Assyria departed. He went and returned. He dwelt at Nineveh.
