The children of Israel stood upon the edge of the sea, and behind them the host of Egypt drew nigh, and the sound of chariots grew loud upon the flat ground.

And Moses stretched forth his hand over the water.

Then the Lord sent a mighty wind out of the east. It came hard and without ceasing across the face of the deep, and it did not relent. All through the night it blew. And the water moved. It drew back from the midst of the sea, slow at the first, then swiftly. The bed of the sea was laid bare — dark sand, then pale stone, then great stretches of ground made dry beneath the wind. And the water rose up on the right hand and rose up on the left hand, standing as walls, and between them lay a way broad enough for a nation to pass through.

And Israel went forward. They set their feet upon dry ground where but hours before the deep had lain. Families walked together. Cattle and flocks moved among them. The wind pressed hard against their faces and their garments. On either side the water stood high and still, held fast in its place, and the people passed between those walls beneath the night sky above them.

And the Egyptians followed after. The chariots of Pharaoh and his horsemen drove down upon the dry seabed in pursuit. Horses and riders, wheels turning upon the hard ground — the full strength of the army came between the walls of water.

Then the Lord looked down upon the host of Egypt and cast them into confusion. The wheels of the chariots locked and brake from their axles. Horses stumbled. The heavy frames dragged upon the ground. And the Egyptians strove to press forward, but every wheel turned sideways or came away entirely. The chariots ground to a halt.

And the Egyptians cried out among themselves, saying, "Let us flee from before Israel, for the Lord doth fight for them against us."

And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying, "Stretch forth thine hand over the sea, that the waters may return upon the Egyptians, upon their chariots, and upon their horsemen."

And Moses stretched forth his hand over the sea.

The dawn was breaking. And the water moved. The walls fell inward, and the sea returned unto its strength, rushing across the ground where the army stood. The water covered the chariots. It covered the horsemen. It covered every soldier of Pharaoh's host that had followed after Israel upon the seabed. Not one of them remained.

And Israel stood upon the far shore. The wind had fallen. The sea lay flat and broad and still in the early light. Along the water's edge the bodies of the soldiers of Egypt washed against the sand. And the people of Israel looked out across the water at the great work which the Lord had wrought against Egypt. And they fell upon their knees upon the wet sand.
