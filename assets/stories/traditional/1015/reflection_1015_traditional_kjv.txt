A man sat by a great and beautiful gate every single day. He could not walk, and so he asked an alms of them that passed by. But Peter and John gave him something better than money. They shared with him the power of God's love, and the man rose up upon his own two feet! He was so filled with joy that he began to leap and to praise God right there in the sight of all.

Sometimes the best gifts are not things we may hold in our hands. A kind word, a helping hand, or simply being there for someone may feel like the greatest gift in all the wide world. Joy cometh in the most surprising of ways.
