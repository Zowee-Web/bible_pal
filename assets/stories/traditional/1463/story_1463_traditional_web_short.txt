A woman in the crowd had been sick for twelve years.

The flow of her blood had not stopped in all that time. She had been to many physicians. She had spent everything she had. Each year she had grown worse, not better.

She had heard about Jesus.

She came up behind him through the crowd.

She did not call out to him. She did not say her name. She only reached for his garment.

She touched the hem of his cloak. She said in her own heart, "If I just touch his clothes, I will be made well."

The flow of her blood stopped.

She felt in her body that she had been healed of her affliction.

Jesus stopped walking.

He felt that the power had gone out from him. He turned around in the crowd.

"Who touched my clothes?" he said.

His disciples looked at him. "You see the multitude pressing against you," they said, "and you say, 'Who touched me?'"

He did not move. He looked around in the crowd to see who had done this thing.

The woman saw that she could not hide.

She was fearing and trembling, knowing what had happened to her.

She came forward. She fell down at his feet on the road. She told him all of it — the twelve years, and the physicians, and the spending of all that she had, and what she had said to herself when she reached for the hem.

He said to her, "Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace. Be cured of your disease."

She stood up where she had fallen. She went back through the crowd to her own house. The bleeding was gone. She walked alive into her own door.
