A great river stood right in the way. The waters were deep and ran swiftly. But the people were not constrained to pass over by themselves. God went before them, and the waters ceased to flow. The ground was made dry enough that every one might walk across — fathers, mothers, grandchildren, even the little ones.

Sometimes things feel exceeding great. A new school feeleth great. A dark room feeleth great. A hard day feeleth great. But even when something seemeth too wide to pass over, thou art never standing at the brink by thyself. One who loveth thee is already there, making a way through.
