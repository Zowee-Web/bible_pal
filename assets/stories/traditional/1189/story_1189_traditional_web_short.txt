Mary stood outside the tomb weeping. As she wept, she stooped and looked into the tomb, and she saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head, and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.

They said to her, "Woman, why are you weeping?"

She said to them, "Because they have taken away my Lord, and I don't know where they have laid him."

When she had said this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing, and didn't know that it was Jesus.

Jesus said to her, "Woman, why are you weeping? Who are you looking for?"

She, supposing him to be the gardener, said to him, "Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away."

Jesus said to her, "Mary."

She turned and said to him in Hebrew, "Rabboni!" which is to say, Teacher.

Jesus said to her, "Don't hold me, for I haven't yet ascended to my Father, but go to my brothers, and tell them, 'I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.'"

Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that he had said these things to her. The morning sun was rising over the garden, and the dew was still on the leaves of the trees as she went.

She walked back along the path she had come in the dark hours before dawn. Now the light was upon her, and she went to find the disciples.

The disciples were gathered together in the house, and Mary came in to them. She told them what she had seen, and what the Lord had said. The lamps were still burning in the room, and the day was beginning to come up over the city of Jerusalem.

The disciples heard her words, and they marveled at what she said. The dawn lay across the streets of the city, and the morning began to grow into day.
