They were given a choice: to bow, or to burn. The reckoning was plain. The furnace was real. The king's power was absolute. Every prudent calculation pointed but one way.

And they said: but if not.

That word is the hinge of the account. Not "God will deliver us" — they believed that well. But "if not." Faith that hangeth not upon the outcome. Trust that requireth not deliverance. A resolve made ere the answer cometh.

The hardest moments in a life are seldom about knowing what shall come to pass. They are about determining who thou art before thou findest out.
