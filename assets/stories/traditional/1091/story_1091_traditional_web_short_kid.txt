A king named Nebuchadnezzar built a giant golden statue. It was really, really tall — about ninety feet high! He put it out on a big flat field and told everyone in his whole kingdom to come and stand in front of it.

Then a man shouted out the king's rule: "When the music plays, everyone has to bow down and worship the statue. If you don't, you'll be thrown into a fiery furnace!"

The music played. Everyone fell to the ground and bowed.

But three men didn't bow. Their names were Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. They were Jewish men who believed in God, and they would not worship anyone else — not even the king's golden statue.

When the king found out, he was furious. He brought them in and said, "I'll give you one more chance. Bow down, or I'll throw you into the fire. And no god can save you!"

The three men answered bravely. "Our God CAN save us from the fire," they said. "But even if he doesn't — we still won't worship your statue."

The king got so angry that he had the furnace heated up seven times hotter. Soldiers tied up the three men and threw them in.

But then the king jumped up from his seat. He couldn't believe what he was seeing.

"Didn't we throw THREE men into the fire?" he asked.

"Yes," his helpers said.

"Well, I see FOUR men walking around in there! They're not tied up. They're not hurt. And the fourth one looks like... a son of the gods!"

The king called out to them: "Come out!"

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego walked right out of the fire. Everyone crowded around them. Their hair wasn't burned. Their clothes weren't burned. They didn't even smell like smoke!

The king said, "Praise the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego! He sent his angel and saved them because they trusted in him."
