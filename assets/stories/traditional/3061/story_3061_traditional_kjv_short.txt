Surely there is a vein for silver and a place where gold is refined. Iron is taken out of the earth, and brass is molten from the stone. Man setteth an end to darkness and carrieth his search unto the stones of darkness and the shadow of death. The flood breaketh out from the inhabitant, and waters forgotten of the foot dry up and go away from men.

Bread cometh from the face of the earth, yet beneath it the ground is turned up as fire. Sapphires are found in its stones, and it hath dust of gold. No fowl knoweth that secret path. The vulture’s eye hath not seen it; the lion’s whelps have not trodden it, nor hath the fierce lion passed thereby.

Man putteth forth his hand upon the rock and overturneth the mountains by the roots. He cutteth out rivers among the stones. His eye seeth every precious thing; he bindeth the floods from overflowing and bringeth the hidden thing forth to light.

But where shall wisdom be found, and where is the place of understanding? Man knoweth not the price thereof, neither is it found in the land of the living. The depth saith, “It is not in me,” and the sea saith, “It is not with me.” Gold cannot purchase it, nor shall silver be weighed for its price. The gold of Ophir, the onyx, the sapphire, crystal, coral, pearls, rubies, and the topaz of Ethiopia cannot equal it.

Whence then cometh wisdom? It is hid from the eyes of all living and kept close from the fowls of the air. Destruction and death have heard only the fame thereof.

God understandeth its way and knoweth its place. He looketh unto the ends of the earth and seeth under the whole heaven. He maketh weight for the winds and weigheth the waters by measure. When he made a decree for the rain and a way for the lightning of thunder, then did he see wisdom and declare it; he prepared it and searched it out.

Unto man he said, “Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding.”
