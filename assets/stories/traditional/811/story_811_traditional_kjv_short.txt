The sun was bowed low, casting long shadows upon the desolate road. The disciples, wearied and grief-stricken, trudged along, their spirits as drooping branches before the coming storm. At one moment they had beheld their Master ascend into the heavens; in the next they were left with an echoing silence that mocked their faith.

Among them was Luke, a faithful companion who had followed Jesus from the beginning. He had beheld the wonders, the teachings, the love. But now, even his deepest convictions seemed to crumble beneath the weight of their loss. The world seemed to continue its ceaseless turning, unmindful of the chasm in their hearts.

As they journeyed onward, an old woman with sun-bleached hair and eyes as weathered stones drew near. She perceived their grief, having lost her own son to sickness. With gentle words and a trembling hand, she offered comfort, putting them in remembrance of the words of Jesus: "Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted."

Her words struck deep within Luke. He called to mind the teachings of Jesus concerning joy in tribulation, of faith that transcended even death. A spark of hope was kindled within him. He perceived that their grief was not a denial of Jesus' victory over death, but a testimony to the love they shared.

The disciples continued upon their way, their hearts yet heavy, but their steps lighter. The old woman's words echoed in their ears, putting them in remembrance that their grief was not their defeat, but a stepping stone unto deeper faith.

The flickering spark of hope urged Luke to share the old woman's wisdom with the others. He spake softly, putting his companions in remembrance of the words of Jesus and the promise of comfort.

His words struck deep. The disciples looked one upon another with understanding, their eyes welling up with tears. They perceived that their shared loss was not weakness, but a testimony to the love they had known.

As they journeyed onward, they beheld a faint glow shining forth from a hill nearby. They drew near with caution. And as they ascended, they marvelled. A radiant light compassed about a stately temple of stone, its golden gates adorned with carvings of great workmanship.

A voice sounded forth from the entrance, saying, "Welcome, ye that mourn. Enter in and find solace in the presence of your Lord."

The disciples looked one upon another once more, their hearts filled with new-found purpose. They knew they had found their way home.
