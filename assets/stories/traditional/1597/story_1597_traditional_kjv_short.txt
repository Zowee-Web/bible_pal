Balaam rose that morning and saddled his ass, and he set out after the princes of Moab. Two servants went with him. And God's anger was kindled because he went.

There was one standing in that road whom Balaam could not see. The angel of the Lord had taken his place in the way as an adversary against him, and his sword was drawn in his hand.

The ass saw him. She turned aside out of the way and went off into the field, and her rider, who saw nothing, smote her to bring her back into the way.

Farther on, the road narrowed between the vineyards, a wall upon this side and a wall upon that. The angel was standing there. The ass beheld him and pressed herself against the wall, and Balaam's foot was crushed against it. He smote her a second time.

Then the angel went on before them. He took his stand in a narrow place, where there was no room to turn to the right hand nor to the left. The ass came to it. She saw him, and she fell down beneath him, and Balaam's anger was kindled, and he took up his staff and smote her.

And the Lord opened the mouth of the ass.

"What have I done unto thee," she said, "that thou hast smitten me these three times?"

"Because thou hast mocked me," said Balaam. "I would there were a sword in mine hand, for now would I kill thee."

"Am not I thine ass," she said, "upon which thou hast ridden ever since I was thine unto this day? Was I ever wont to do so unto thee?"

"Nay," he said.

Then the Lord opened the eyes of Balaam. The angel of the Lord stood before him in the way, his sword drawn in his hand. Balaam bowed down his head and fell flat upon his face.

"Wherefore hast thou smitten thine ass these three times?" the angel said. "Behold, I went out to withstand thee, because thy way is perverse before me. The ass saw me, and turned from me these three times. Unless she had turned from me, surely now also I had slain thee, and saved her alive."

"I have sinned," said Balaam, "for I knew not that thou stoodest in the way against me. Now therefore, if it displease thee, I will get me back again."

"Go with the men," said the angel. "But only the word that I shall speak unto thee, that thou shalt speak."

So Balaam went on with the princes of Balak.
