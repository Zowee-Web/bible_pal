After Nathan the prophet had come to David and shown him his sin, David spoke to the Lord. He poured out his soul in the chamber of his house, and he wrote a prayer in the days that followed.

He said, "Have mercy on me, O God, according to your loving kindness. According to the multitude of your tender mercies, blot out my transgressions."

He said, "Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. For I know my transgressions, and my sin is constantly before me."

He said, "Against you, and you only, I have sinned, and done that which is evil in your sight, that you may be proved right when you speak, and justified when you judge."

Then David said, "Behold, I was born in iniquity. In sin my mother conceived me. Behold, you desire truth in the inward parts. You teach me wisdom in the inmost place."

He said, "Purify me with hyssop, and I shall be clean. Wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. Let me hear joy and gladness, that the bones which you have broken may rejoice."

He cried out, "Hide your face from my sins, and blot out all of my iniquities. Create in me a clean heart, O God. Renew a right spirit within me."

Then David prayed, "Do not throw me from your presence, and do not take your Holy Spirit from me. Restore to me the joy of your salvation, and uphold me with a willing spirit."

He laid his hand on his head and was silent for a time. Then he wrote down the words of his prayer, and the words went up to the Lord. The lamps were burning low in the chamber, and the night was quiet around him. The hours passed, and he did not move from where he sat.
