Before Nehemiah returned unto Jerusalem, Eliashib the priest had the oversight of the chamber of the house of God. Because he was allied unto Tobiah, Eliashib prepared for him a great chamber in the courts and placed his household stuff therein. Aforetime that place held the meat offerings, frankincense, vessels, and the tithes of corn, new wine, and oil appointed for the Levites, singers, and porters, with the offerings of the priests.

Nehemiah was not at Jerusalem when this was done. In the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon, he had come unto the king. After certain days, Nehemiah obtained leave and returned to Jerusalem. There he found the evil that Eliashib had done for Tobiah by preparing him a chamber in the courts of the house of God.

The matter grieved Nehemiah sore. He cast forth all the household stuff of Tobiah out of the chamber. At his commandment, the chambers were cleansed. Then he brought again the vessels of the house of God, with the meat offering and the frankincense.

Nehemiah also perceived that the portions of the Levites had not been given them. The Levites and the singers that did the work had fled every one to his field. Nehemiah contended with the rulers and asked, “Why is the house of God forsaken?” He gathered the servants together and set them in their place.

Then brought all Judah the tithe of corn, new wine, and oil unto the treasuries. Nehemiah made treasurers over the storehouses: Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites. Hanan, the son of Zaccur and grandson of Mattaniah, was next unto them. These men were counted faithful, and their office was to distribute unto their brethren.

Nehemiah prayed. He asked his God to remember him concerning these deeds and not wipe out the good deeds that he had done for the house of his God and for the offices thereof.
