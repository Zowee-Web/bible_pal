The kingdom of God, Jesus said, is like this. A man scatters seed on the ground. Night and day, whether he sleeps or gets up, the seed sprouts and grows, though he himself does not know how. All by itself the earth produces the crop — first the blade, then the head, then the full grain in the head. And as soon as the grain is ripe, he puts the sickle to it, because the harvest has come.
