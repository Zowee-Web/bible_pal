The kingdom of God, Jesus said, is like unto this.

A man casteth seed into the ground. He sleepeth, and he riseth, night and day. The seed springeth and groweth up — and how, he knoweth not.

The earth bringeth forth the fruit of herself. First the blade. Then the ear. After that, the full corn in the ear.

When the fruit is brought forth, the man putteth in the sickle immediately, because the harvest is come.
