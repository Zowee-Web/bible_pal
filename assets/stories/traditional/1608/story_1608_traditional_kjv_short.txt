Jesus likened the kingdom of God unto a man casting seed into the ground.

Night and day pass. The man sleepeth and riseth, while the seed springeth up and groweth; yet how it groweth, he knoweth not.

The earth bringeth forth fruit of herself. A blade appeareth first, then the ear, and afterward the full corn in the ear.

When the fruit is brought forth, the man putteth in the sickle at once, for the harvest is come.
