The army of Israel came back from Judah with the spoil.

They brought the captives up the road to Samaria. The men were bound at the wrists. The women carried what they could. The children walked between them. The plunder was piled in the carts behind.

A prophet of Yahweh was at the gate of Samaria. His name was Oded.

He went out to meet the army. He stood in the road before them. The dust of their march was still rising.

He said, "Behold, because Yahweh, the God of your fathers, was angry with Judah, he has delivered them into your hand, and you have slain them in a rage which has reached up to heaven.

Now you intend to degrade the children of Judah and Jerusalem as male and female slaves for yourselves. Aren't there even with you trespasses of your own against Yahweh your God?

Now hear me therefore, and send back the captives that you have taken captive from your brothers, for the fierce wrath of Yahweh is on you."

Then four heads of the children of Ephraim stood up — Azariah the son of Johanan, and Berechiah the son of Meshillemoth, and Jehizkiah the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of Hadlai.

They stood against the army that came from the war.

They said, "You must not bring in the captives here, for you intend that which will bring on us a trespass against Yahweh, to add to our sins and to our guilt; for our guilt is great, and there is fierce wrath against Israel."

So the armed men set the captives and the plunder down before the princes and all the assembly.

Then the four men whose names had been mentioned rose up. They took the captives. They opened the bundles of plunder. They clothed all who were naked among them. They dressed them. They put sandals on the bare feet. They gave them something to eat. They gave them something to drink. They anointed their wounds.

The feeble could not walk. They carried them. They laid them upon donkeys. They led them out of Samaria with the captives walking behind.

They brought them to Jericho — the city of palm trees — to their brothers. And the four men of Ephraim turned, and went back to Samaria.
