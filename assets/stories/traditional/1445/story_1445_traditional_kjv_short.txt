The army of Israel came back from Judah with the spoil.

They brought the captives up the way to Samaria. The men were bound at the wrists. The women carried what they could. The children walked between them. The plunder was piled in the carts behind.

A prophet of the Lord was at the gate of Samaria. His name was Oded.

He went out to meet the host. He stood in the way before them. The dust of their march was yet rising.

He said, "Behold, because the Lord God of your fathers was wroth with Judah, he hath delivered them into your hand, and ye have slain them in a rage that reacheth up unto heaven.

And now ye purpose to keep under the children of Judah and Jerusalem for bondmen and bondwomen unto you. Are there not with you, even with you, sins against the Lord your God?

Now hear me therefore, and deliver the captives again, which ye have taken captive of your brethren: for the fierce wrath of the Lord is upon you."

Then arose four of the heads of the children of Ephraim — Azariah the son of Johanan, and Berechiah the son of Meshillemoth, and Jehizkiah the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of Hadlai.

They stood against the host that came from the war.

They said, "Ye shall not bring in the captives hither: for whereas we have offended against the Lord already, ye intend to add more to our sins and to our trespass: for our trespass is great, and there is fierce wrath against Israel."

So the armed men left the captives and the spoil before the princes and all the congregation.

Then the men which were expressed by name rose up. They took the captives. They opened the bundles of the spoil. They clothed all that were naked among them. They arrayed them. They shod the bare feet. They gave them somewhat to eat. They gave them somewhat to drink. They anointed their wounds.

The feeble could not walk. They carried them. They laid them upon asses. They brought them out of Samaria with the captives walking behind.

They brought them unto Jericho — the city of palm trees — unto their brethren. And the four men of Ephraim turned, and went back to Samaria.
