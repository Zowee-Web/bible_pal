The three men rose up from Abraham's tent and looked toward Sodom. Abraham walked with them to see them on their way.

Yahweh did not hide from Abraham what he was about to do.

He said, "Because the cry of Sodom and Gomorrah is great, and because their sin is very grievous, I will go down now, and see whether their deeds are as bad as the reports which have come to me."

The two men who were with him turned from there and went on toward Sodom.

But Abraham stood yet before Yahweh.

Abraham came near.

He said, "Will you sweep away the righteous with the wicked? What if there are fifty righteous within the city? Will you consume and not spare the place for the fifty righteous who are in it? Be it far from you to do things like that. Shouldn't the Judge of all the earth do right?"

Yahweh said, "If I find in Sodom fifty righteous within the city, then I will spare the whole place for their sake."

Abraham answered, "See now, I have taken it on myself to speak to the Lord, although I am dust and ashes. What if there will lack five of the fifty? Will you destroy all the city for lack of five?"

He said, "I will not destroy it if I find forty-five there."

Abraham spoke again. "What if there are forty found there?"

"I will not do it for the forty's sake."

"Oh don't let the Lord be angry, and I will speak. What if there are thirty found there?"

"I will not do it if I find thirty there."

"See now, I have taken it on myself to speak to the Lord. What if there are twenty?"

"I will not destroy it for the twenty's sake."

"Oh don't let the Lord be angry, and I will speak just once more. What if ten are found there?"

"I will not destroy it for the ten's sake."

Then Yahweh went his way, as soon as he had finished communing with Abraham.

Abraham returned to his place.

The two men had gone on toward Sodom while Abraham was speaking. The road back to the place where his tent stood was longer than the road out had been.
