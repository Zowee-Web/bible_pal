Now in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra the priest went up from Babylon. He was a ready scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord God of Israel had given; and the king granted him all his request, according to the hand of the Lord his God upon him. And there went up with him some of the priests and the Levites, the singers and the porters and the temple servants. He began to go up on the first day of the first month, and came to Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month, according to the good hand of his God upon him. For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the Lord, and to do it, and to teach in Israel statutes and judgments.

And the king gave him a letter. Artaxerxes, king of kings, unto Ezra the priest, a scribe of the law of the God of heaven: I make a decree, that all they of Israel in my realm who are minded of their own freewill to go up to Jerusalem, may go with thee. Thou art sent to carry the silver and gold which the king and his counsellors have freely offered unto the God of Israel, and to buy therewith bullocks, rams, and lambs, to offer upon the altar of the house of your God. And whatsoever more shall be needful for the house of thy God, bestow it out of the king's treasure house. And it shall not be lawful to impose toll or tribute upon the ministers of this house of God. And thou, Ezra, set magistrates and judges who know the laws of thy God; and teach ye them that know them not.

Then Ezra said, Blessed be the Lord God of our fathers, which hath put such a thing as this in the king's heart, to beautify the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem, and hath extended mercy unto me before the king and his counsellors. And I was strengthened as the hand of the Lord my God was upon me, and I gathered together out of Israel chief men to go up with me.
