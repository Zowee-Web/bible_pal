Three times the account says the same thing: the good hand of his God was on Ezra. A foreign king funded the journey, opened his treasury, and sent Ezra home with authority — and Ezra saw whose hand was behind it. He had set his heart to study the law, to do it, and to teach it; and when it was done, he blessed the God who had put it all in the king's heart. Where do you see a hand behind the doors that have opened for you?
