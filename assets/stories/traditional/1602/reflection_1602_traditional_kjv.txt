Three times the record says it: the good hand of his God was upon Ezra. A foreign king granted his every request, opened his treasure house, and sent him home with authority — and Ezra saw whose hand was behind it. He had prepared his heart to seek the law, to do it, and to teach it; and when it was done, he blessed the Lord who had put such a thing in the king's heart. Where do you see a hand behind the doors that have opened for you?
