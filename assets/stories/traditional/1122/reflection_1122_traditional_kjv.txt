He said he would not believe. He said it aloud, with confidence, before men who had every reason to dispute with him. He had drawn his line. And then the Lord against whom he had drawn it walked into the room.

What stayeth with thee is not the rebuke. There was no rebuke. Jesus came nigh. He held forth the hands. He bade the very thing Thomas had said he would have to do. There is something tender therein: the way grace meeteth the place where doubt hath been most stubborn.

Thomas had no need to touch him. The nearness undid him.

Sometimes the deepest faith cometh not from they that never doubted. It cometh from they that said it plainly, were met where they stood, and were given more than they had asked.
