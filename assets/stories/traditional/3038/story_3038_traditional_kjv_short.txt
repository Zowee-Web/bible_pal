God at sundry times and in divers manners spake unto the fathers by the prophets. In these last days, he hath spoken by his Son. The Son is appointed heir of all things; by him God made the worlds. He is the brightness of God's glory and the express image of his person, upholding all things by the word of his power. Having purged sins, he sat on the right hand of the Majesty on high.

God calleth him Son, commandeth angels to worship him, and addresseth his throne as everlasting. The Son loveth righteousness and hateth iniquity. He laid earth's foundation; heavens are the work of his hands. Creation waxeth old and changeth as a garment, but he remaineth and his years fail not. Angels are ministering spirits sent for heirs of salvation; the Son sitteth at God's right hand until his enemies are his footstool.

Therefore the hearers ought to give more earnest heed, lest the things heard should slip. If the word spoken by angels brought just recompence for disobedience, there is no escape in neglecting so great salvation. The Lord first spake it, hearers confirmed it, and God bare witness by signs, wonders, divers miracles, and gifts of the Holy Ghost.

The world to come is not subjected unto angels. Scripture asketh why God is mindful of man, made a little lower than angels yet crowned with glory and honour. All things are subjected, though that completion is not yet seen. But Jesus is seen: made lower for the suffering of death, now crowned with glory and honour, tasting death for every man by God's grace.

It became him, for whom and by whom are all things, to bring many sons unto glory and make the captain of their salvation perfect through sufferings. He that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one, so Jesus is not ashamed to call them brethren.

Because the children partake of flesh and blood, he likewise took part of the same. Through death he destroyed him that had the power of death and delivered them bound by its fear. He took hold of Abraham's seed, not angels. Made like his brethren, he became a merciful and faithful high priest, making reconciliation for the people's sins. Because he suffered being tempted, he is able to succour them that are tempted.
