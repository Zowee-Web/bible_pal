Jesus fell on the ground and prayed. He asked for the cup to be removed. He asked three times. The answer did not come as removal. The cup remained.

He went back to the three he had asked to watch with him. Each time they were asleep. He was alone in the asking.

The garden was dark. The olive trees stood around him. The city was lit below. He prayed until the torches came through the trees.

He did not leave. He did not hide. He stood up and walked toward the light.

Sometimes the answer to the prayer is not the removal of the thing. It is the strength to walk into it.
