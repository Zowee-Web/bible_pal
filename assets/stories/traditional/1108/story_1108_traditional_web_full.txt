They came to a place called Gethsemane. The garden sat on the slope of the Mount of Olives, east of the city. Olive trees stood in thick rows, their gnarled trunks dark in the moonlight. The air was cool. The ground was soft beneath their feet — packed earth and fallen leaves. Below them, across the Kidron Valley, the walls of Jerusalem were lit by lamps and torches.

Jesus said to his disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

Most of them stopped. They found places among the trees — backs against trunks, cloaks pulled tight. The night was quiet. The meal was heavy in them.

Jesus took Peter, James, and John further into the garden. The trees grew closer together. The moonlight came through in patches on the ground. They walked in silence.

Jesus stopped. His steps slowed. His shoulders dropped. He was deeply troubled and distressed. He turned to the three.

"My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here and watch."

He went forward — a stone's throw beyond them. He fell on the ground. His knees hit the earth. His hands pressed into the dirt. His forehead touched the ground.

"Abba, Father, all things are possible to you. Remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not what I want, but what you want."

The olive branches moved overhead. The wind passed through the garden. The three men sat against the trees behind him. The city sounds were far away.

He rose. He walked back to where he had left them. All three were asleep. Peter's head was tilted against a tree trunk. James and John lay on the ground, their cloaks bunched beneath them.

Jesus stood over Peter.

"Simon, are you sleeping? Couldn't you watch one hour? Watch and pray, that you may not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak."

Peter opened his eyes. He shifted. He sat up. Jesus turned and walked back into the darkness.

He fell to the ground again. He prayed the same words. The same request. The same surrender. His breath was heavy. Sweat ran down his face in the cool air.

He rose and returned. They were asleep again. Their eyes were heavy. They looked up at him and did not know what to answer. Their faces were blank with exhaustion.

He left them the third time. He went back to the same spot. The ground was marked where his knees had pressed. He fell again. He prayed again. The garden held no sound but the wind in the branches and his voice against the earth.

The minutes passed. The moon moved across the sky. The olive leaves shifted and resettled.

He came back the third time. Peter, James, and John were sleeping still. Their breathing was slow and even.

"Sleep on now, and take your rest. It is enough. The hour has come. The Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners. Rise up. Let us go. Look, he who betrays me is at hand."

Through the olive trees, light appeared — torches bobbing between the trunks. The sound of many footsteps carried through the garden. Voices murmured. Metal clinked. The light grew brighter, moving toward them through the dark.

The three woke. They stood. They looked toward the approaching torches.

Jesus stood facing the light. He did not move.
