They came to a place called Gethsemane. The garden was dark. Olive trees stood thick around them, their branches black against the sky. The air was cool and still.

Jesus said to his disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

He took Peter, James, and John with him. They walked deeper into the garden. The ground was uneven, roots crossing the path. The city lights of Jerusalem flickered through the trees behind them.

Jesus stopped. He was deeply troubled. His body was heavy. He said to the three, "My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here and watch."

He went forward a little, fell on the ground, and prayed. His face pressed against the earth. His hands gripped the dirt.

"Abba, Father, all things are possible to you. Remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not what I want, but what you want."

He rose. He walked back to the three. They were asleep. Their heads rested against the tree trunks. Their eyes were closed.

"Simon, are you sleeping? Couldn't you watch one hour? Watch and pray, that you may not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak."

He went away again. He prayed the same words. He returned. They were asleep again. Their eyes were heavy. They did not know what to answer him.

He left them a third time. He fell to the ground. He prayed. The garden was silent except for the wind in the olive branches.

He came back the third time. They were sleeping still.

"Sleep on now, and take your rest. It is enough. The hour has come. The Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners. Rise up. Let us go. He who betrays me is near."

Through the trees, torchlight appeared. Voices carried on the night air. Footsteps crunched on the garden path.
