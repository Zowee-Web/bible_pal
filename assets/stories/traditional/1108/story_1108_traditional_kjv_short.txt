They came to a place which was named Gethsemane. The garden was dark. Olive trees stood thick about them, their branches black against the sky. The air was cool and still.

Jesus saith unto his disciples, "Sit ye here, while I shall pray."

He took with him Peter, James, and John. They walked deeper into the garden. The ground was uneven, roots crossing the path. The city lights of Jerusalem flickered through the trees behind them.

Jesus stopped. He began to be sore amazed and very heavy. He saith unto the three, "My soul is exceeding sorrowful unto death: tarry ye here, and watch."

He went forward a little, fell on the ground, and prayed. His face pressed against the earth. His hands gripped the dirt.

"Abba, Father, all things are possible unto thee; take away this cup from me: nevertheless not what I will, but what thou wilt."

He rose. He came back unto the three. They were sleeping. Their heads rested against the tree trunks. Their eyes were shut.

"Simon, sleepest thou? Couldest not thou watch one hour? Watch ye and pray, lest ye enter into temptation. The spirit truly is ready, but the flesh is weak."

He went away again. He prayed the selfsame words. He returned. They were asleep again. Their eyes were heavy. They wist not what to answer him.

He departed the third time. He fell to the ground. He prayed. The garden was silent save for the wind in the olive branches.

He came the third time. They were sleeping still.

"Sleep on now, and take your rest: it is enough, the hour is come; behold, the Son of man is betrayed into the hands of sinners. Rise up, let us go; lo, he that betrayeth me is at hand."

Through the trees, torchlight appeared. Voices carried upon the night air. Footsteps sounded upon the garden path.
