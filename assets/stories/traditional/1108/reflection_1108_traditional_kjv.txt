Jesus fell on the ground and prayed. He asked that the cup be taken from him. He asked three times. The answer came not as removal. The cup remained.

He went back unto the three he had bidden watch with him. Each time they were sleeping. He was alone in the asking.

The garden was dark. The olive trees stood about him. The city was lit below. He prayed until the torches came through the trees.

He departed not. He hid not. He stood up and walked toward the light.

Sometimes the answer to the prayer is not the taking away of the thing. It is the strength to walk into it.
