Daniel set his face unto the Lord with fasting and sackcloth and ashes, and cried: O Lord, hear. O Lord, forgive. O Lord, hearken and do. Gabriel came unto him at the time of the evening oblation and said: at the beginning of thy supplications the commandment came forth. What is the hurt of confessing the sins of thy people on their behalf, while the answer is already on its way to thy room?
