The Pharisees came out and began to question Jesus, seeking from him a sign from heaven and testing him. He sighed deeply in his spirit. “Why does this generation seek a sign?” he said. “Most certainly I tell you, no sign will be given to this generation.” He left them, entered the boat again, and departed to the other side.

The disciples had forgotten to take bread, and they had no more than one loaf in the boat with them. Jesus warned them, “Take heed: beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and the yeast of Herod.” They reasoned with one another, “It’s because we have no bread.”

Jesus perceived it. Why did they reason that it was because they had no bread? “Don’t you perceive yet or understand?” he asked. “Having eyes, don’t you see? Having ears, don’t you hear? Don’t you remember? When I broke the five loaves among the five thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces did you take up?” They told him, “Twelve.” “When the seven loaves fed the four thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces did you take up?” They told him, “Seven.” He asked them, “Don’t you understand yet?”

He came to Bethsaida, and they brought a blind man to him and begged him to touch him. Jesus took hold of the blind man by the hand and brought him out of the village. When he had spat on his eyes and laid his hands on him, he asked him if he saw anything. The man looked up and said, “I see men, but I see them like walking trees.” Then Jesus again laid his hands on his eyes. The man looked intently, and was restored, and saw everyone clearly. Jesus sent him away to his house, saying, “Don’t enter into the village, nor tell anyone in the village.”
