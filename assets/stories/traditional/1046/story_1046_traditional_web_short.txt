In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. The earth was empty and dark. Deep water covered everything. The Spirit of God moved over the surface of the waters.

God said, "Let there be light." And there was light. It burst out bright and warm — the very first light that ever existed. God saw that the light was good. He separated the light from the darkness. He called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. That was the first day.

On the second day, God made the sky — a wide blue dome stretching from one end of the world to the other. Water below, sky above.

On the third day, God gathered the waters into one place so that dry land appeared. Hills and mountains rose up from the deep. He called the dry land Earth and the waters he called Seas. Then God said, "Let the earth put forth grass, herbs, and trees bearing fruit." And up from the ground came green grass, wildflowers in every color, and fruit trees heavy with apples and figs and pomegranates. The whole earth smelled fresh and new. God saw that it was good.

On the fourth day, God made two great lights — the sun to rule the day and the moon to rule the night. He made the stars too. Thousands and thousands of them, scattered across the darkness like sparkling sand. God set them all in place.

On the fifth day, God filled the seas with living creatures — whales and dolphins and fish of every shape and size. He filled the sky with birds. Eagles soaring high, sparrows darting between branches, herons standing still by the water. "Be fruitful and multiply!" God said.

On the sixth day, God made the animals. Lions with golden manes. Bears padding through the forest. Rabbits hopping in the grass. Cattle grazing in the meadows. Lizards sunning on warm rocks. Every kind of creature that walks or creeps on the ground.

Then God said something special. "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness." God created man in his own image. Male and female he created them. He blessed them. "Be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth," he said. "I have given you every plant and every tree for food."

God stepped back and looked at everything he had made. All of it — the light, the sky, the seas, the plants, the sun and moon and stars, the fish and birds and animals, and the people standing in the middle of it all.

God saw everything that he had made, and it was very good.
