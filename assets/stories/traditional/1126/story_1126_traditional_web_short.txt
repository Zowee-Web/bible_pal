Before the feast of the Passover, Jesus knew that his hour had come. Having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end.

The room was lit by oil lamps along the walls, and the disciples were gathered at the table for the Passover meal. During supper, he rose from the table and laid aside his outer garments. He took a towel and tied it around his waist, then poured water into a basin.

He began to wash the disciples' feet, wiping each one with the towel. He moved from one disciple to the next, kneeling at each man in turn, while the basin rested between them on the floor.

When he came to Simon Peter, Peter said, "Lord, do you wash my feet?" Jesus answered, "What I am doing you do not understand now, but you will know afterward."

Peter said, "You will never wash my feet." Jesus replied, "If I do not wash you, you have no part with me." Then Peter said, "Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head."

Jesus said, "He who is bathed needs only to wash his feet. He is then completely clean. You are clean, though not all of you." For he knew the one who would betray him.

When he had finished washing their feet, he put on his outer garments and returned to the table. He said to them, "Do you understand what I have done to you? You call me Teacher and Lord, and you are right, for so I am.

"If I then, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet. I have given you an example, that you also should do as I have done to you. If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them."
