Doth not wisdom cry, and understanding put forth her voice? She standeth in high places by the way, where paths meet, and at the city gates, even at the entry of its doors, she crieth aloud. Her voice goeth unto the sons of men, calling the simple to learn wisdom and fools to gain an understanding heart.

Wisdom promiseth excellent and right things. Her mouth speaketh truth; wickedness is an abomination unto her lips. All her words are in righteousness, with nothing froward or perverse in them. Unto him that understandeth they are plain, and unto them that find knowledge they are right.

Wisdom calleth men to receive instruction rather than silver and knowledge rather than choice gold. Rubies and every desired thing fail as measures of her worth. Wisdom dwelleth with prudence and findeth knowledge of witty inventions. The fear of the Lord is to hate evil. Wisdom hateth pride, arrogancy, the evil way, and the froward mouth.

Counsel and sound wisdom are hers. She is understanding and hath strength. By her kings reign; princes decree justice; princes, nobles, and all judges of the earth rule by her. Wisdom loveth them that love her, and those that seek her early shall find her. Riches, honour, durable riches, and righteousness are with her, but her fruit is better than fine gold and her revenue than choice silver. She leadeth in the way of righteousness and in the midst of the paths of judgment. Those that love her inherit substance, and she filleth their treasures.

The Lord possessed wisdom in the beginning of his way, before his works of old. Wisdom was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth. She was brought forth when there were no depths and no fountains abounding with water. Mountains and hills had not yet been settled, nor had the Lord made fields or the highest part of the dust.

When God prepared the heavens above the deep, wisdom was there beside him. She was present when he set a compass upon the deep and established the clouds above. The fountains of the deep were strengthened beneath them. He gave the sea his decree that the waters should not pass his commandment, and he appointed the foundations of the earth. Wisdom speaketh of being by him, as one brought up with him, daily his delight and always rejoicing before him. Her rejoicing was in the habitable part of his earth, and her delights were with the sons of men.

Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children, wisdom saith. They that keep her ways are blessed. She calleth them to hear instruction, be wise, and refuse it not. Her voice still crieth at the gates, offering life unto them that hear.

Blessing belongeth unto the man that heareth, watching daily at wisdom's gates and waiting at her doors. Whoso findeth wisdom findeth life and obtaineth favour of the Lord. He that sinneth against wisdom wrongeth his own soul, and all they that hate her love death.
