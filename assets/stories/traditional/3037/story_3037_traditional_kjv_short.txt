Wisdom putteth forth her voice where the paths meet and at the gates of the city. Her call is open: men and the simple are summoned to understand wisdom and gain an understanding heart. She speaketh excellent, right, and true things. Wickedness is an abomination unto her lips; nothing froward or perverse is in her words. They are plain unto him that understandeth and right unto them that find knowledge.

Wisdom asketh to be received rather than silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold. She is better than rubies, beyond comparison with desirable things. Wisdom dwelleth with prudence and findeth knowledge of witty inventions. The fear of the Lord is to hate evil, so she hateth pride, arrogancy, the evil way, and the froward mouth. Counsel, sound wisdom, understanding, and strength are hers. By wisdom kings reign, princes decree justice, and judges rule.

Wisdom loveth them that love her, and they that seek her early shall find her. Riches and honour abide with her, yet her fruit is better than fine gold and her revenue than choice silver. She leadeth in righteousness and judgment, causing those that love her to inherit substance.

Wisdom speaketh of the time before the earth. The Lord possessed wisdom in the beginning of his way. Before depths, fountains, mountains, hills, fields, or dust, wisdom was brought forth. When God prepared the heavens, compassed the deep, established clouds and fountains, decreed the sea's bounds, and appointed the earth's foundations, wisdom was there. She was by him, daily his delight, rejoicing before him and in the habitable earth, with delight in the sons of men.

Therefore wisdom again calleth her children to hearken. Blessed are they that keep her ways, hear instruction, and refuse it not. He that heareth watcheth daily at her gates and waiteth at her doors. Whoso findeth wisdom findeth life and obtaineth favour of the Lord. He that sinneth against her wrongeth his own soul; all that hate her love death.
