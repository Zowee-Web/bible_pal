Wisdom cries out, and understanding raises her voice. She stands on the heights beside the way, where paths meet, and at the gates where the city opens. At the city’s entry doors, her voice reaches the sons of humanity and calls the simple to learn prudence and the foolish to gain an understanding heart. She asks them to hear the right things her lips will speak.

Wisdom promises excellent and right things. Her mouth speaks truth; wickedness is an abomination to her lips. All her words are righteous, with nothing crooked or perverse in them. To the one who understands they are plain, and to those who find knowledge they are right.

Wisdom calls people to receive instruction instead of silver and knowledge instead of choice gold. Rubies and every desired possession fail as measures of her worth. Wisdom dwells with prudence and discovers knowledge and discretion. To fear Yahweh is to hate evil. Wisdom hates pride, arrogance, the evil way, and the perverse mouth.

Counsel and sound knowledge belong to wisdom. She has understanding and power. Kings reign by her; princes decree justice; nobles and righteous rulers of the earth exercise authority by her. Wisdom loves those who love her, and diligent seekers find her. Riches, honor, enduring wealth, and prosperity accompany her, but her fruit is better than fine gold and her yield better than choice silver. She walks in the way of righteousness and in the middle of the paths of justice. She gives wealth to those who love her and fills their treasuries.

Yahweh possessed wisdom in the beginning of his work, before his ancient deeds. Wisdom was established from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth existed. She speaks of being born when there were no depths and no springs abounding with water. Mountains had not yet settled in place, hills had not risen, and Yahweh had made neither fields nor the first dust of the world.

When God established the heavens, wisdom was there. She was present when he set a circle on the surface of the deep and established the clouds above. She was there when the springs of the deep became strong, when he gave the sea its boundary so the waters could not pass his command, and when he marked out the foundations of the earth. Wisdom was the craftsman beside him, a delight day by day, always rejoicing before him. Her rejoicing extended through his inhabited world, and her delight was with the children of humanity.

“Now therefore, my sons, listen to me,” wisdom says. Those who keep her ways are blessed. She calls them to hear instruction, become wise, and not refuse it. Her voice still calls at the gates, offering life to those who listen.

Blessing belongs to the person who listens, watching daily at wisdom's gates and waiting at her doorposts. Whoever finds wisdom finds life and obtains favor from Yahweh. The one who sins against wisdom harms his own soul, and all who hate wisdom love death.
