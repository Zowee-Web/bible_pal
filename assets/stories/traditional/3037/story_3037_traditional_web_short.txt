Wisdom raises her voice where paths meet and beside the city's gates. Her call is public: people and the simple are summoned to understand prudence and gain an understanding heart. What she speaks is excellent, right, and true. Wickedness is hateful to her lips; nothing crooked or perverse belongs to her words. Those who understand find them plain and right.

Wisdom asks to be received before silver and knowledge before choice gold. She is better than rubies, beyond comparison with desirable things. Wisdom dwells with prudence and finds knowledge and discretion. The fear of Yahweh means hating evil, so she hates pride, arrogance, the evil way, and the perverse mouth. Counsel, sound knowledge, understanding, and power belong to her. By wisdom kings reign, princes decree justice, and rulers govern rightly.

Wisdom loves those who love her, and diligent seekers find her. Riches and honor are with her, yet her fruit is better than fine gold and her yield better than silver. She walks in righteousness and justice, giving wealth to those who love her and filling their treasuries.

Wisdom speaks of the time before the earth. Yahweh possessed wisdom at the beginning of his work. Before depths, springs, mountains, hills, fields, or dust, wisdom was born. When God established heavens, bounded the deep and sea, strengthened clouds and springs, and marked the earth's foundations, wisdom was there. She was the craftsman beside him, a delight day by day, rejoicing before him and in his inhabited world, delighting in humanity.

Therefore wisdom again calls her children to listen. Blessed are those who keep her ways, hear instruction, and refuse it not. The one who listens watches daily at her gates and waits at her doorposts. Whoever finds wisdom finds life and obtains Yahweh's favor. Whoever sins against wisdom harms his own soul; those who hate her love death.
