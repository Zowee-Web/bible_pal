In the days when Israel had again done evil in the sight of the Lord, the people were oppressed by Jabin the king of Canaan and Sisera the captain of his army. He had nine hundred chariots of iron, and for twenty years he oppressed Israel. The people cried out to the Lord.

Now Deborah, a prophetess, judged Israel at that time. She sat under the palm tree of Deborah in the hill country of Ephraim, and the children of Israel came up to her for judgment.

She sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam, and she said to him, "Has not the Lord, the God of Israel, commanded you? Go and draw out to Mount Tabor with ten thousand men of Naphtali and Zebulun. I will draw Sisera and his chariots to the river Kishon, and I will deliver him into your hand."

Barak said to her, "If you will go with me, then I will go. But if you will not go with me, I will not go."

She said, "I will surely go with you. Yet the journey that you take will not be for your honor. The Lord will sell Sisera into a woman's hand." So Deborah arose and went with Barak to Kedesh.

When Sisera heard that Barak had gone up to Mount Tabor, he gathered all his chariots and all his men, from Harosheth to the river Kishon. Deborah said to Barak, "Up, for this is the day in which the Lord has delivered Sisera into your hand. Has not the Lord gone out before you?"

So Barak went down from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men after him. The Lord confused Sisera and all his chariots and all his army before Barak, and Sisera came down from his chariot and fled away on his feet. Barak pursued the chariots and the army, and all the army of Sisera fell by the sword.

But Sisera fled to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite. Jael went out to meet him and said, "Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me. Do not be afraid." So he came into her tent, and she covered him with a rug.

He said, "Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty." She opened a bottle of milk and gave him to drink, and she covered him again. Then weariness fell upon him, and he slept.

Then Jael took a tent peg and a hammer, and she went softly to him. She struck the peg into his temple, and he died.

When Barak came in pursuit, Jael went out to meet him and said, "Come, and I will show you the man whom you seek." So Barak came in, and Sisera lay dead, with the tent peg in his temple.

So God subdued Jabin the king of Canaan before the children of Israel that day. And the hand of Israel prevailed more and more against Jabin, until they had destroyed him.
