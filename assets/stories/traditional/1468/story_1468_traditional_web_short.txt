Moses came down from the mountain.

He had the two tablets of the testimony in his hand. The stone was cool against his fingers, and the writing on the stone was deep.

He did not know that the skin of his face shone.

He had spoken with Yahweh on the mountain, and the light of that speaking was on him as he came down the slope toward the camp. He did not know it was there.

Aaron saw him first.

Aaron was standing at the edge of the camp, looking up the path. When Moses came near, Aaron drew back.

The children of Israel saw him next.

They saw the skin of his face shine. They were afraid to come near him.

Moses called to them.

He called Aaron first. He called the rulers of the congregation. They came back to him slowly, watching his face.

He spoke to them.

He gave them the commandments that Yahweh had spoken with him on the mountain.

When he was done speaking, he put a veil on his face.

He put it on so that the people could come near again.

After that day, this was how Moses moved between Yahweh and the people.

He went in before Yahweh to speak with him. He took the veil off when he went in.

He came out from speaking with Yahweh. He came out shining.

He spoke to the children of Israel what he had been commanded.

Then he put the veil back on his face, until he went in to speak with Yahweh again.

The veil hung between the speaking and the speaking — between the mountain where Yahweh's voice came down to him, and the camp where his own voice came out to the people.

The light came under the veil. The light went out from under the veil. The days of Moses passed between them.
