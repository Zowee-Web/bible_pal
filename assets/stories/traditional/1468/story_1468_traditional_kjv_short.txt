Moses came down from the mount.

He had the two tables of the testimony in his hand. The stone was cool against his fingers, and the writing upon the stone was deep.

He wist not that the skin of his face shone.

He had spoken with the Lord upon the mount, and the light of that speaking was upon him as he came down the slope toward the camp. He wist not that it was there.

Aaron beheld him first.

Aaron was standing at the edge of the camp, looking up the path. When Moses drew nigh, Aaron drew back.

The children of Israel beheld him next.

They saw the skin of his face shine. They were afraid to come nigh him.

Moses called unto them.

He called Aaron first. He called the rulers of the congregation. They came back unto him slowly, beholding his face.

He spake unto them.

He gave them in commandment all that the Lord had spoken with him upon the mount.

When he had done speaking with them, he put a vail upon his face.

He put it on, that the people might come nigh again.

After that day, thus did Moses move between the Lord and the people.

He went in before the Lord to speak with him. He took the vail off when he went in.

He came out from speaking with the Lord. He came out shining.

He spake unto the children of Israel that which he had been commanded.

Then he put the vail upon his face again, until he went in to speak with the Lord.

The vail hung between the speaking and the speaking — between the mount whereon the Lord's voice came down unto him, and the camp whereunto his own voice came out unto the people.

The light came under the vail. The light went out from under the vail. The days of Moses passed between them.
