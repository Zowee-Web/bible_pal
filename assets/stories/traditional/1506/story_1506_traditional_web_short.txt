Daniel had a dream in his bed in the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon. He saw visions of his head upon his bed. He wrote the dream down and told the sum of the matters.

He saw four winds of heaven striving on the great sea. He saw four great beasts come up from the sea. Each one was different from the others.

The first was like a lion with eagle's wings. Its wings were plucked. It was lifted up from the earth and made to stand on its feet like a man. A man's heart was given to it.

The second was like a bear. It raised up itself on one side. Three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth. They said to it: arise, devour much flesh.

The third was like a leopard. It had four wings on its back. It had four heads. Dominion was given to it.

The fourth beast was dreadful and terrible and strong exceedingly. It had iron teeth. It devoured. It broke in pieces what was left. It stamped on what remained. It was different from all the beasts before it. It had ten horns. A little horn came up among them. The little horn had eyes like the eyes of a man. It had a mouth speaking great things.

Daniel beheld until the thrones were cast down. The Ancient of Days sat. His garment was white as snow. The hair of his head was like pure wool. His throne was like the fiery flame. The wheels of the throne were burning fire. A fiery stream issued and came out from before him. Thousand thousands ministered to him. Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him. The judgment was set. The books were opened.

The fourth beast was slain because of the great words the little horn spoke. The body was destroyed and given to the burning flame. The other beasts had their dominion taken away.

Daniel saw in the night visions one like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven. He came to the Ancient of Days. He was brought near before him. There was given him dominion and glory and a kingdom, that all peoples and nations and languages should serve him. His dominion would not pass away. His kingdom would not be taken from him.
