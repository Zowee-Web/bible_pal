In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream upon his bed. He saw visions of his head as he lay there in the night. When he waked, he took up his pen, and he wrote the dream down, and he told the sum of the matters that had passed before him.

This is what he wrote.

He had seen the four winds of the heaven striving upon the great sea. The waters had not been still. The winds had come at them from the four quarters at once, and the surface of the sea had risen and broken under the pressure of them.

Then, out of the sea, the beasts had come up. Four of them. Each one diverse from the others.

The first was like a lion. It had eagle's wings. Daniel beheld, in the dream, while the wings thereof were plucked. He saw it lifted up from the earth and made to stand upon the feet as a man. A man's heart was given to it.

The second was like to a bear. It raised up itself on one side. Three ribs were in the mouth of it between the teeth of it. A voice spake over it. "Arise. Devour much flesh."

The third was like a leopard. It had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl. It had four heads. Dominion was given to it.

Then came the fourth.

The fourth beast was dreadful and terrible. It was strong exceedingly. The teeth thereof were of iron. It devoured. It brake in pieces what it could not swallow. It stamped the residue with the feet of it. It was diverse from all the beasts that were before it. It had ten horns. Daniel beheld the horns. While he beheld, there came up among them another little horn. The little horn had eyes like the eyes of man. It had a mouth, and the mouth spake great things.

Daniel beheld till the thrones were cast down.

Then the Ancient of Days did sit.

His garment was white as snow. The hair of his head was like the pure wool. His throne was like the fiery flame. The wheels thereof as burning fire. A fiery stream issued and came forth from before him. Thousand thousands ministered unto him. Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him. The judgment was set. The books were opened.

The little horn still spake the great words when Daniel turned his eyes back to it. The voice had not ceased. Daniel beheld until the fourth beast was slain because of the words the little horn spake. The body was destroyed and given to the burning flame. As concerning the rest of the beasts, they had their dominion taken away, yet their lives were prolonged for a season and time.

Then Daniel saw, in the night visions, one like the Son of man coming with the clouds of heaven.

He came to the Ancient of Days. He was brought near before him. There was given him dominion and glory and a kingdom — that all people, nations, and languages should serve him. His dominion was an everlasting dominion. It should not pass away. His kingdom was one that should not be destroyed.

Daniel waked. The night was still around him. He lay there with the dream still within his head. The beasts. The thrones. The Ancient of Days. The one like the Son of man, walking upon the clouds.

He took up his pen. He wrote it down.
