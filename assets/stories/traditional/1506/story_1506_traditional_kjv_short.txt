Daniel had a dream upon his bed in the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon. He saw visions of his head upon his bed. He wrote the dream down and told the sum of the matters.

He saw four winds of the heaven striving upon the great sea. He saw four great beasts come up from the sea. Each was diverse from the others.

The first was like a lion with eagle's wings. The wings thereof were plucked. It was lifted up from the earth and made stand upon the feet as a man. A man's heart was given to it.

The second was like to a bear. It raised up itself on one side. It had three ribs in the mouth of it between the teeth. They said thus unto it: arise, devour much flesh.

The third was like a leopard. It had upon the back of it four wings. It had four heads. Dominion was given to it.

The fourth beast was dreadful and terrible and strong exceedingly. It had great iron teeth. It devoured. It brake in pieces. It stamped the residue with the feet of it. It was diverse from all the beasts that were before it. It had ten horns. A little horn came up among them. The little horn had eyes like the eyes of a man. It had a mouth speaking great things.

Daniel beheld till the thrones were cast down. The Ancient of Days did sit. His garment was white as snow. The hair of his head was like the pure wool. His throne was like the fiery flame. The wheels thereof as burning fire. A fiery stream issued and came forth from before him. Thousand thousands ministered unto him. Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him. The judgment was set. The books were opened.

The fourth beast was slain because of the voice of the great words which the little horn spake. The body was destroyed and given to the burning flame. The rest of the beasts had their dominion taken away.

Daniel saw in the night visions, and behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven. He came to the Ancient of Days. He was brought near before him. There was given him dominion and glory and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages should serve him. His dominion should not pass away. His kingdom should not be taken from him.
