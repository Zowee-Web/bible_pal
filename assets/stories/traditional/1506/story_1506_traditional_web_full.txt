In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream upon his bed. He saw visions of his head as he lay there in the night. When he woke, he took up his pen, and he wrote the dream down, and he told the sum of the matters that had passed before him.

This is what he wrote.

He had seen the four winds of heaven striving on the great sea. The waters had not been still. The winds had come at them from the four quarters at once, and the surface of the sea had risen and broken under the pressure of them.

Then, out of the sea, the beasts had come up. Four of them. Each one different from the others.

The first was like a lion. It had the wings of an eagle. Daniel watched, in the dream, while the wings were plucked from it. He saw it lifted up from the earth and made to stand on its feet like a man. A man's heart was given to it.

The second was like a bear. It raised itself up on one side. Three ribs were in its mouth, held between its teeth. A voice spoke over it. "Arise. Devour much flesh."

The third was like a leopard. It had four wings on its back. It had four heads. Dominion was given to it.

Then came the fourth.

The fourth beast was dreadful and terrible. It was strong exceedingly. Its teeth were of iron. It devoured. It broke in pieces what it could not swallow. It stamped what was left of its prey beneath its feet. It was different from all the beasts that had come before it. It had ten horns. Daniel watched the horns. While he watched, another horn came up among them — a little one. The little horn had eyes like the eyes of a man. It had a mouth, and the mouth was speaking great things.

Daniel beheld in his dream until the thrones were cast down.

Then the Ancient of Days sat.

His garment was white as snow. The hair of his head was like pure wool. The throne where he sat was like the fiery flame. The wheels of the throne were burning fire. A fiery stream issued and came out from before him. Thousand thousands ministered to him. Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him. The judgment was set. The books were opened.

The little horn was still speaking its great words when Daniel turned his eyes back to it. The voice had not stopped. Daniel watched until the fourth beast was slain because of the words the little horn spoke. The body was destroyed and given to the burning flame. The other three beasts had their dominion taken away from them, but their lives were prolonged for a season and a time.

Then Daniel saw, in the night visions, one like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven.

He came to the Ancient of Days. He was brought near before him. There was given to him dominion and glory and a kingdom — that all peoples and nations and languages should serve him. His dominion was an everlasting dominion. It would not pass away. His kingdom was one that would not be broken.

Daniel woke. The night was still around him. He lay there with the dream still inside his head. The beasts. The thrones. The Ancient of Days. The one like the Son of Man, walking on the clouds.

He took up his pen. He wrote it down.
