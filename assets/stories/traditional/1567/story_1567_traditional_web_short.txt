When Eve gave birth to her first son, she named him Cain, and said, "I have gotten a man with Yahweh's help." Later she gave birth to his brother Abel. As the boys grew, they took up different work: Abel became a keeper of sheep, and Cain worked the ground.

As time passed, the day came when each brother brought an offering to Yahweh. Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground he had worked. Abel brought the firstborn of his flock, and their fat — the best and the first of everything he had. Yahweh looked with favor on Abel and his offering, but on Cain and his offering he did not look with favor. And Cain was very angry, and his face fell.

Yahweh saw it, and came to Cain with a question. "Why are you angry? Why has your face fallen? If you do well, will you not be lifted up? But if you do not do well, sin is crouching at your door. Its desire is for you — but you must rule over it."

The warning did not turn him. Cain said to his brother, "Let us go out to the field." And while they were there in the field, Cain rose up against his brother Abel and killed him.

Then Yahweh came again with a question. "Where is your brother Abel?" "I do not know," Cain answered. "Am I my brother's keeper?"

"What have you done?" said Yahweh. "The voice of your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground. And now you are cursed from that very ground, which opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood from your hand. When you work it, it will no longer give you its strength. You will be a restless wanderer on the earth."

"My punishment is more than I can bear," Cain said. "Today you have driven me from the land, and from your face I will be hidden. I will be a wanderer on the earth, and whoever finds me will kill me."

But even now Yahweh did not leave him without protection. "Therefore, anyone who kills Cain will suffer vengeance seven times over," Yahweh said. And Yahweh set a mark on Cain, so that no one who found him would strike him down.

So Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh, and settled in the land of Nod, east of Eden.
