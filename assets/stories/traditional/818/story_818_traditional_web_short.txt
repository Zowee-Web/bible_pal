Long ago, in the quiet place called Shiloh, Samuel lived and served in the Lord’s house. He was still a boy, gentle and kind, helping Eli the priest with many little things each day. Each night, Samuel slept close by the lamp of God, the soft golden light glowing in the quiet room. It was very still when everyone lay down to rest, and even Eli, who was old and whose eyes were weak, was sleeping.

One night, while the hush of evening filled the tabernacle, Samuel heard a voice call his name, “Samuel!” Startled, Samuel quickly got up. He hurried to Eli’s side and said, “Here I am! You called me.” But Eli, sleepy and confused, told him, “I didn’t call. Lie down again.” Samuel went back, curling up in his blanket under the gentle light.

Again, he heard the voice, clear and soft, “Samuel!” He jumped up and ran to Eli once more. “Here I am, for you called me,” he said. But Eli shook his head. “I did not call, my son. Lie down again.” So Samuel returned a second time to his warm bed, still not knowing who was calling.

A third time the voice rang out, “Samuel!” He rose and went to Eli, not wanting to miss his call. “Here I am, for you called me.” At last, Eli realized this was the Lord calling to Samuel. Kindly, Eli said to the boy, “Go and lie down. If you hear the voice again, say, ‘Speak, LORD, for your servant hears.’” Samuel listened carefully to every word.

Samuel settled down, the lamp’s glow steady in the darkness. Then the Lord came and stood close, calling as before, “Samuel! Samuel!” This time, Samuel answered just as Eli taught him. “Speak, LORD, for your servant hears.” The Lord spoke kindly to Samuel, telling him important things that would soon happen in Israel. In the warmth and calm, Samuel listened well.

From that night, the Lord was near to Samuel and spoke with him often. Samuel became known as someone who heard God’s words and listened with a gentle, open heart. All of Israel heard that the Lord had chosen Samuel, and Samuel grew up with courage and wisdom, for the Lord was always with him.
