Long ago, in the quiet place called Shiloh, Samuel dwelt and ministered in the house of the Lord. He was yet a child, gentle and kind, helping Eli the priest with many little things each day. Each night, Samuel slept close by the lamp of God, the soft golden light glowing in the quiet chamber. It was very still when all had laid them down to rest, and even Eli, who was old and whose eyes were dim, was sleeping.

One night, while the hush of evening filled the tabernacle, Samuel heard a voice call his name, "Samuel!" Being startled, Samuel arose quickly. He hastened unto Eli's side and said, "Here am I, for thou calledst me." But Eli, drowsy and confused, said unto him, "I called not. Lie down again." Samuel went back, curling up in his blanket beneath the gentle light.

Again, he heard the voice, clear and soft, "Samuel!" He arose and ran unto Eli once more. "Here am I, for thou calledst me," he said. But Eli shook his head. "I called not, my son. Lie down again." So Samuel returned a second time unto his warm bed, not yet knowing who called him.

A third time the voice rang out, "Samuel!" He arose and went unto Eli, not wishing to miss his call. "Here am I, for thou calledst me." At the last, Eli perceived that the Lord had called the child. Kindly, Eli said unto the boy, "Go, lie down; and it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, Lord; for thy servant heareth." Samuel hearkened unto every word.

Samuel settled down, the lamp's glow steady in the darkness. Then the Lord came, and stood, and called as at other times, "Samuel! Samuel!" This time, Samuel answered even as Eli had taught him. "Speak; for thy servant heareth." The Lord spake kindly unto Samuel, telling him weighty things that should soon come to pass in Israel. In the warmth and calm, Samuel hearkened well.

From that night, the Lord was nigh unto Samuel and spake with him often. Samuel became known as one who heard the words of God and hearkened with a gentle, open heart. All Israel heard that the Lord had chosen Samuel, and Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him, for the Lord was with him in all his ways.
