They were in the deepest cell. Their backs were torn. Their feet were made fast. It was midnight.

And they sang.

There is nothing in the account that explaineth why. No word concerning their thoughts. They sang praises. And the prisoners — men with their own causes for being in that place — heard them.

What followed was striking. But the singing came first. Before the earthquake, before the doors opened, before the keeper's question. The singing came when nothing had yet changed.
