Paul and Silas had been stripped in the marketplace before the crowd. The magistrates gave the order, and they were beaten with rods. When it was done, they were brought to the jailer, and he was commanded to keep them securely.

The jailer received the charge. He brought them through the outer prison and into the inner cell — the deepest part, far from the entrance. He fastened their feet in the stocks. The door closed. The cell was dark.

The prisoners in the other cells settled into silence.

At midnight Paul and Silas were praying. Their voices were low at first. Then they began to sing — hymns to God. Their voices rose together, filling the cell and carrying through the prison. Every prisoner could hear them.

The sound did not falter.

Then the earth moved.

A great earthquake struck. The foundations of the prison were shaken. Every door was opened at once. Every chain came loose.

The shaking stopped.

The jailer woke. He ran to the corridor and saw the doors standing open. He drew his sword.

Paul's voice came out of the darkness — loud and clear.

"Do not harm yourself! We are all here!"

The jailer stopped. "Bring a light!" he called.

A torch was brought. He rushed in and came trembling before Paul and Silas. He fell down before them.

He brought them out and said, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"

Paul answered. "Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved — you and your household."

They spoke the word of the Lord to him and to all who were in his house.

That same hour of the night, he took them and washed their wounds. Then he and all his household were baptized.

He brought them into his house and set food before them. The lamps burned. His household gathered around.

He rejoiced greatly, with all his house, having believed in God.
