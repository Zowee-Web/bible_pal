Paul and Silas were beaten with rods in the marketplace. The magistrates gave the order, and they were handed over to the jailer with a charge to keep them securely. He thrust them into the inner prison and fastened their feet in the stocks. The door closed. The cell was dark.

The hours passed.

At midnight Paul and Silas were praying. Then they began to sing — their voices rising through the prison, carrying from cell to cell. The other prisoners listened. No one called out. The singing continued.

Suddenly a great earthquake struck. The foundations of the prison shook. Every door flew open. Every chain came loose. The stocks split apart.

The jailer woke from sleep. He saw the doors standing open — every one. He drew his sword.

Paul cried out into the darkness, "Do not harm yourself! We are all here!"

The jailer called for a light. He rushed in, trembling, and fell on the ground before Paul and Silas.

"Sirs," he said, "what must I do to be saved?"

They said, "Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved — you and your household."

They spoke the word of the Lord to him and to all in his house. That same hour of the night, he took them and washed their wounds. Then he and all his household were baptized.

He brought them into his house and set food before them. He rejoiced greatly, with all his household, having believed in God.
