Paul and Silas were stripped before the multitude in the marketplace. The magistrates gave command, and they were beaten with rods. When it was ended, they were brought to the keeper of the prison, and he was charged to keep them safely.

The keeper received the charge. He brought them through the outer prison and thrust them into the inner cell — the deepest part, far from the entrance. He made their feet fast in the stocks. The door was shut. The cell was dark.

The prisoners in the other cells lay silent.

At midnight Paul and Silas prayed. Their voices were low at first — words into the darkness. Then they sang praises unto God. Their voices rose together, filling the cell and carrying through the prison. Every prisoner within those walls could hear them.

The sound did not waver.

Then the earth moved.

A great earthquake struck, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken. Immediately all the doors were opened. Every one's bands were loosed.

The shaking ceased.

The keeper of the prison, awaking out of his sleep, ran to the corridor and saw the doors open — every one. He drew out his sword.

Paul cried with a loud voice out of the darkness.

"Do thyself no harm: for we are all here."

The keeper stood still. "Bring a light!" he called.

A torch was brought. He sprang in and came trembling before Paul and Silas. He fell down before them.

He brought them out and said, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?"

They said, "Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house."

They spake unto him the word of the Lord, and to all that were in his house.

That same hour of the night, he took them and washed their stripes. And was baptized, he and all his, straightway.

He brought them into his house and set meat before them. The lamps burned. His household gathered about him.

He rejoiced greatly, believing in God with all his house.
