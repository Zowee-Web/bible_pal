There was a man named Saul who was VERY religious. He studied the Bible his whole life. He followed every single rule. And he was absolutely sure — one hundred percent certain — that the people who followed Jesus were wrong.

So Saul decided to stop them. He got official letters from the religious leaders that said he could go to a city called Damascus and arrest anyone who believed in Jesus. Men OR women — he'd bring them all back to Jerusalem in chains.

Saul set out on the road to Damascus with some helpers. He walked for days through dusty hills, feeling very determined. He knew exactly what he was going to do.

And then everything changed.

Right when he was almost to Damascus, a light from heaven suddenly BLAZED all around him. It was brighter than the sun — so bright he couldn't see ANYTHING. He fell flat on his face in the dust.

Then a voice spoke.

"Saul, Saul, why are you hurting my people?"

Saul was shaking. "Who... who are you?" he whispered.

"I am Jesus."

JESUS! The very person whose followers Saul had been chasing!

"Get up," Jesus said. "Go into the city. Someone will tell you what to do next."

The light went away. Saul tried to open his eyes, but he couldn't see. The bright light had made him completely blind. His friends had to take him by the hand and lead him into Damascus — the man who had set out to be powerful was now as helpless as a little child.

For three whole days, Saul sat in a dark room. He couldn't see. He didn't eat. He didn't drink. He just sat there and prayed.

Meanwhile, God spoke to a believer named Ananias. "Go find Saul," God said. "He's praying on a street called Straight."

Ananias was scared. "But God — that's the guy who's been ARRESTING people like me!"

"Go," God said. "I have special plans for him. He's going to tell the whole world about me."

So Ananias was brave. He went to the house, found Saul sitting in the dark, and put his hands on him.

"Brother Saul," he said gently, "Jesus sent me so you can see again."

And right away, something like scales fell off Saul's eyes — and he could SEE!

Saul stood up. He was baptized. He ate some food and got his strength back.

And from that day on, Saul spent the rest of his life telling everyone about Jesus — the very person he had once tried to stop.
