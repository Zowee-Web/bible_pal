There was a man named Saul who really, really did not like the followers of Jesus. He thought they were wrong about everything, and he wanted to stop them. He even got special letters from the leaders in Jerusalem that said he could go to another city called Damascus and arrest anyone who believed in Jesus.

So Saul set out on the road to Damascus. He was walking with some other men, feeling very sure of himself.

Then something incredible happened.

A light from heaven — brighter than the sun — suddenly flashed all around him! It was so bright that Saul fell right down to the ground.

Then he heard a voice.

"Saul, Saul, why are you hurting my people?"

Saul was shaking. "Who are you?" he asked.

"I am Jesus," the voice said. "Get up and go into the city. Someone there will tell you what to do."

When Saul opened his eyes, he couldn't see ANYTHING. The bright light had made him blind! His friends had to hold his hand and lead him into Damascus like he was a little kid.

For three whole days, Saul sat in the dark. He didn't eat. He didn't drink. He just prayed.

Then God told a man named Ananias to go find Saul. Ananias was scared — he knew Saul had been hurting people who believed in Jesus!

But God said, "Go. I have special plans for him."

So Ananias went. He put his hands on Saul and said, "Brother Saul, Jesus sent me so you can see again."

Right away, something like scales fell off Saul's eyes — and he could see! He got up, was baptized, ate some food, and got his strength back.

The man who came to Damascus to stop Jesus' followers had become one himself.
