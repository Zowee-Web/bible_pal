Walking by the sea of Galilee, Jesus saw two brothers, Simon who is called Peter and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea. They were fishermen, and the morning light lay across the water. The boats of other fishermen rocked gently in the shallows, and the nets were drying along the stones of the shore.

Jesus said to them, "Come after me, and I will make you fishers of men."

They immediately left their nets and followed him. The net fell into the water, and Peter and Andrew did not stop to gather it. They walked beside Jesus along the edge of the sea.

Going on from there, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets. The needles were in their hands, and the threads of the broken net lay across their knees.

He called them. They immediately left the boat and their father, and they followed him. Zebedee watched from the boat, and the hired servants stood beside him. The brothers walked away with Jesus along the shore.

Jesus went about in all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the Good News of the Kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.

The report concerning him went out into all Syria. They brought to him all who were sick, those who were afflicted with various diseases and torments, possessed with demons, epileptics, and paralytics. He healed them.

Great multitudes from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and from beyond the Jordan followed him. The disciples walked beside him, and the crowds came after them.

The four fishermen who had left their nets that morning walked behind him into a new life, and the sound of the sea was behind them, and the road of the Galilean villages stretched ahead.
