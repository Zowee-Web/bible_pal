Yahweh spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt. “Let the children of Israel keep the Passover in its appointed season,” he said. “On the fourteenth day of this month, at evening, you shall keep it in its appointed season. You shall keep it according to all its statutes and according to all its ordinances.”

Moses told the children of Israel that they should keep the Passover, and they kept the Passover in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month at evening, in the wilderness of Sinai. According to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so the children of Israel did.

But there were certain men who were unclean because of the dead body of a man, so that they could not keep the Passover on that day, and they came before Moses and Aaron on that day. “We are unclean because of the dead body of a man,” those men said. “Why are we kept back, that we may not offer the offering of Yahweh in its appointed season among the children of Israel?”

Moses answered them, “Wait, that I may hear what Yahweh will command concerning you.”

Then Yahweh spoke to Moses. Moses was to say to the children of Israel, “If any man of you or of your generations is unclean by reason of a dead body, or is on a journey far away, he shall still keep the Passover to Yahweh. In the second month, on the fourteenth day at evening they shall keep it; they shall eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. They shall leave none of it until the morning, nor break a bone of it. According to all the statute of the Passover they shall keep it.”

But the man who was clean, and was not on a journey, and failed to keep the Passover, that soul would be cut off from his people. Because he did not offer the offering of Yahweh in its appointed season, that man would bear his sin.

If a foreigner lived among them and desired to keep the Passover to Yahweh, then he was to do so according to the statute of the Passover, and according to its ordinance. “You shall have one statute,” Yahweh said, “both for the foreigner and for him who is born in the land.”
