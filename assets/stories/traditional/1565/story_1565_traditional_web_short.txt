They seized him and led him away, and they brought him into the high priest's house. Peter followed from a distance.

In the middle of the courtyard they kindled a fire. They sat down together around the fire, and Peter sat among them.

A certain servant girl saw him as he sat there in the light. She looked intently at him. "This man also was with him," she said.

He denied him. "Woman, I don't know him."

After a little while someone else saw him and said, "You also are one of them!"

"Man, I am not!"

About an hour later, another confidently affirmed, "Truly this man also was with him, for he is a Galilean."

"Man, I don't know what you are talking about!"

He was still speaking when a rooster crowed.

The Lord turned and looked at Peter. And Peter remembered the word the Lord had spoken to him, how he had said to him, "Before the rooster crows you will deny me three times."

He went out. He wept bitterly.
