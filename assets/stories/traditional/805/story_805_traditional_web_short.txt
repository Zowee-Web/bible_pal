The morning sun rose over the hills, casting gold light across the land. People from every village drew near to the house of the Lord, their voices rising like a joyful river. Men, women, and children walked along the dusty road, clapping hands and singing aloud, for the day was bright with gladness.

At the gates, they paused as one. The doors loomed high, wide open to all. Some lifted their faces, smiling, as if meeting an old friend. They entered the courts together, laughter echoing, song filling the air:  

“Shout to Yahweh, all the earth!  
Serve Him with gladness!  
Come before His presence with singing!”

Children spun in circles, their laughter blending with the songs. Grandmothers hummed softly and swayed from side to side. Shepherds, still smelling like grass and wool, raised their voices, their gratitude plain on sun-browned faces.

Inside, the people greeted one another with kindness. From the highest to the least, All came to give thanks. They brought baskets of fruit and loaves still warm from the fire, setting them down with gentle hands.

A father lifted his son onto his shoulders. The little one looked about in wonder as his father spoke, steady and sure:

“Know that Yahweh is God.  
It is He who has made us, and we are His.  
We are His people, the sheep of His pasture.”

The people pressed forward, filling the courtyard with their warmth. They bowed their heads in thanks, voices weaving together:

“Enter into His gates with thanksgiving,  
And into His courts with praise.  
Give thanks to Him; bless His name.”

A cool breeze stirred the air. Sunlight flickered on the old stones. The gathered crowd recalled all that had come before—trials and blessings, hunger and bounty. Through it all, God had been faithful.

One elder, leaning on his staff, spoke for all:

“For Yahweh is good.  
His loving kindness endures forever,  
His faithfulness to all generations.”

With those words, the crowd lifted their praise higher yet. The sound rolled through the temple courts and spilled out into the streets, ringing across fields and rooftops, until it seemed all creation echoed the song.

The morning moved gently toward noon. The people, filled with thanks and joy, lingered in the courts. Their hearts rested, certain in the goodness of the Lord—whose love never faded, whose promise belonged to children yet unborn.
