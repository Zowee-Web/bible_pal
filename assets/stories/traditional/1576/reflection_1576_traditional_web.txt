Plague after plague fell, and each time the heart hardened again — first Pharaoh's own doing, and then Yahweh's. But the people who were spared were not spared by their strength. They were spared by a lamb's blood struck on the door, and a command to stay inside until morning. What is it to be kept safe not by the walls you trust, but by a mark you were told to make and then wait behind?
