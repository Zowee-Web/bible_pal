Plague after plague fell, and each time the heart was hardened again — first Pharaoh's own doing, and then the Lord's. But they that were spared were not spared by their strength. They were spared by a lamb's blood struck upon the door, and a word to abide within until morning. What is it to be kept safe, not by the walls thou trustest, but by a token thou wast bidden to make, and then to wait behind?
