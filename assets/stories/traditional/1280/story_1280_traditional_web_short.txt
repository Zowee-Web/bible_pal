I rejoice greatly that I have found certain of your children walking in truth, even as we have been commanded by the Father.

Now I beg you, dear lady, not as though I wrote a new commandment to you, but that which we had from the beginning, that we love one another.

This is love, that we should walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, even as you heard from the beginning, that you should walk in it.
