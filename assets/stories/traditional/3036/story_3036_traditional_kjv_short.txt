Nahum's burden speaketh unto Nineveh as he that dasheth in pieces cometh up. The city is commanded to keep the munition, watch the way, make the loins strong, and fortify power mightily. Yet the reason looketh beyond Nineveh: the Lord restoreth the excellency of Jacob and Israel after the emptiers marred their vine branches.

The prophecy proceedeth in burning images. Red shields, scarlet men, flaming chariots, and shaken fir trees fill the day of preparation. Chariots rage through the streets like torches and lightning. Worthies stumble as they haste unto the wall. River gates open, the palace dissolveth, and maids mourn as doves. Nineveh, of old like a pool of water, emptieth as they flee and none looketh back. Silver and gold are taken; the city is empty, void, and waste.

The oracle asketh where the lions' dwelling hath gone. The lion once filled his holes with prey, but the Lord of hosts declareth, Behold, I am against thee. Chariots shall burn, the sword shall devour the young lions, prey shall cease, and messengers shall be heard no more.

Then cometh the woe against the bloody city, full of lies, robbery, and prey. The noise of whip, wheels, horses, and chariots accompanieth the vision of slaughter. Nineveh's selling of nations is named, and the Lord again saith that he is against her. Her shame shall be uncovered. They that behold her shall flee and say, Nineveh is laid waste: who will bemoan her? Then the Lord asketh, Whence shall I seek comforters for thee?

Populous No, though compassed with waters and strengthened by Ethiopia, Egypt, Put, and Lubim, went into captivity. Nineveh shall not escape by multiplying strong holds, merchants, crowned men, or captains. Fortresses shall fall like firstripe figs; fire and sword shall consume. Captains shall vanish like grasshoppers when the sun ariseth. The king of Assyria's shepherds slumber, his nobles lie in dust, and his people are scattered with none to gather them. The burden endeth with no healing for Nineveh's grievous wound. They that hear the bruit clap their hands, for her wickedness hath passed continually upon them all.
