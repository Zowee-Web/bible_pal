In the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah, Jeroboam son of Joash king of Israel began his reign in Samaria. Forty and one years he reigned. He did evil in the sight of the Lord, departing not from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who made Israel to sin.

He restored the coast of Israel from the entering of Hamath unto the sea of the plain. Thus was fulfilled the word of the Lord God of Israel, spoken by his servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet of Gath–hepher. The Lord saw Israel’s very bitter affliction: there was not any shut up, nor any left, nor any helper for Israel. He had not said that he would blot out Israel’s name from under heaven, but saved them by the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash.

The rest of Jeroboam’s acts, his might, his warfare, and his recovery of Damascus and Hamath for Israel, which had belonged to Judah, were written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. Jeroboam slept with his fathers, the kings of Israel, and Zachariah his son reigned in his stead.

In the twenty and seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah son of Amaziah began to reign over Judah. He was sixteen years old when his reign began, and he ruled two and fifty years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Jecholiah of Jerusalem.

Azariah did right in the Lord’s sight, according to all his father Amaziah had done. Yet the high places were not removed, and the people still sacrificed and burnt incense there. The Lord smote the king, and he was a leper unto the day of his death. He dwelt in a several house. His son Jotham was over the house, judging the people of the land.

All the other acts of Azariah were written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. He slept with his fathers, and they buried him with them in the city of David. Jotham his son reigned in his stead.
