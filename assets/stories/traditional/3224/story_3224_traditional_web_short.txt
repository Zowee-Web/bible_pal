Jeroboam, the son of Joash king of Israel, began to reign in Samaria in the fifteenth year of Amaziah son of Joash, king of Judah. His reign lasted forty-one years. He did evil in Yahweh’s sight and did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, who had made Israel sin.

He restored Israel’s border from the entrance of Hamath to the sea of the Arabah. This was according to the word of Yahweh, the God of Israel, spoken through his servant Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath Hepher. Yahweh had seen Israel’s bitter affliction, affecting slave and free alike, with no helper for Israel. He had not said he would blot out Israel’s name from under the sky. Instead, he saved them by the hand of Jeroboam son of Joash.

Jeroboam’s other acts, his might in battle, and his recovery for Israel of Damascus and Hamath, which had belonged to Judah, were written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel. Jeroboam slept with his fathers, the kings of Israel, and his son Zechariah reigned in his place.

In Jeroboam’s twenty-seventh year, Azariah son of Amaziah began to reign over Judah. He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.

Azariah did right in Yahweh’s eyes, according to what his father Amaziah had done. The high places remained, however, and the people continued sacrificing and burning incense there. Yahweh struck Azariah, and the king remained a leper until his death. He lived in a separate house, while his son Jotham was over the household and judged the people of the land.

The rest of Azariah’s acts and all that he did were written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah. When he slept with his fathers, they buried him with them in David’s city. Jotham his son reigned in his place.
