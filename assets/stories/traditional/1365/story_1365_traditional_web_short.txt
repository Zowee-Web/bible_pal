Naomi and Ruth had come up out of the country of Moab and had crossed the Jordan and walked the road that led to Bethlehem. They came at last into the city, dusty and weary, in the days of the beginning of the barley harvest. The women of the city looked out and saw them; and a stir went through the streets, and the women said one to another, "Is this Naomi?" And Naomi answered them.

So they both went until they came to Bethlehem. When they had come to Bethlehem, all the city was excited about them, and they asked, "Is this Naomi?"

She said to them, "Don't call me Naomi. Call me Mara; for the Almighty has dealt very bitterly with me.

I went out full, and Yahweh has brought me home again empty. Why do you call me Naomi, since Yahweh has testified against me, and the Almighty has afflicted me?"

And the two of them went into the house that had been Elimelech's, where Naomi had lived in the years before she had gone away. The barley harvest was coming on in the fields outside; the empty hands of Naomi held what she had brought back from Moab, which was Ruth; and the city of Bethlehem began the days of receiving her again. And so it was in the days when Naomi came home from Moab to the house of her people.
