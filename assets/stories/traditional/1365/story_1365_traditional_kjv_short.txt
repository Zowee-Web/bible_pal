Naomi and Ruth had come up out of the country of Moab and had passed over Jordan and walked the road that led unto Bethlehem. They came at last into the city, dusty and weary, in the days of the beginning of the barley harvest. The women of the city looked forth and beheld them; and a stir went through the streets, and the women said one to another, "Is this Naomi?" And Naomi answered them.

So they went until they came to Bethlehem. And it came to pass, when they were come to Bethlehem, that all the city was moved about them, and they said, "Is this Naomi?"

And she said unto them, "Call me not Naomi, call me Mara: for the Almighty hath dealt very bitterly with me.

I went out full, and the Lord hath brought me home again empty: why then call ye me Naomi, seeing the Lord hath testified against me, and the Almighty hath afflicted me?"

And the two of them went into the house that had been Elimelech's, where Naomi had dwelt in the years before she had gone away. The barley harvest was coming on in the fields without; the empty hands of Naomi held what she had brought back from Moab, which was Ruth; and the city of Bethlehem began the days of receiving her again. And so it was in the days when Naomi came home from Moab unto the house of her people.
