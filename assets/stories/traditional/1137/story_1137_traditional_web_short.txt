Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem. He called for the elders, the heads, the judges, and the officers of Israel, and they presented themselves before God. The morning was clear, and the people stood quietly under the sky.

Joshua said to all the people, "Now therefore fear the Lord, and serve him in sincerity and in truth. Put away the gods which your fathers served beyond the River and in Egypt, and serve the Lord."

He said, "If it seems evil to you to serve the Lord, choose today whom you will serve. You may serve the gods of your fathers from beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you dwell. But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord."

The people answered, "Far be it from us that we should forsake the Lord to serve other gods. For it is the Lord our God who brought us and our fathers up out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage, and did great signs in our sight. Therefore we also will serve the Lord, for he is our God."

Joshua said to the people, "You cannot serve the Lord on your own, for he is a holy God. He is a jealous God. If you forsake him and serve foreign gods, he will turn and do you harm, after he has done you good." But the people said to Joshua, "No, but we will serve the Lord."

Joshua said, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen the Lord, to serve him." And they said, "We are witnesses." Then he said, "Now therefore put away the foreign gods which are among you, and incline your heart to the Lord, the God of Israel."

The people said, "We will serve the Lord our God, and we will listen to his voice." So Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and made for them a statute and an ordinance in Shechem.

Then Joshua wrote these words in the book of the law of God. He took a great stone and set it up under the oak that was by the sanctuary of the Lord.

Then Joshua said to all the people, "Behold, this stone shall be a witness against us. It has heard all the words which the Lord spoke to us. It shall be a witness against you, lest you deny your God." So Joshua sent the people away, every man to his inheritance.
