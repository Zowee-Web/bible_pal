Solomon's temple was builded. The cedar walls were carved with cherubims and palm trees and open flowers; the doors of olive wood stood overlaid with gold; and the ark of the covenant had been brought up out of the city of David by the priests and set within the most holy place under the wings of the great cherubims of olive wood. The priests were come out of the holy place. The court of the temple was full of people. The Levites which were the singers stood at the east end of the altar in fine linen — Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and their brethren — with cymbals and psalteries and harps; and with them stood an hundred and twenty priests sounding with silver trumpets.

And it came to pass, when the priests were come out of the holy place: (for all the priests that were present were sanctified, and did not then wait by course:

Also the Levites which were the singers, all of them of Asaph, of Heman, of Jeduthun, with their sons and their brethren, being arrayed in white linen, having cymbals and psalteries and harps, stood at the east end of the altar, and with them an hundred and twenty priests sounding with trumpets:)

It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the Lord; and when they lifted up their voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of musick, and praised the Lord, saying,

"For he is good;
for his mercy endureth for ever:"

that then the house was filled with a cloud, even the house of the Lord,

So that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud: for the glory of the Lord had filled the house of God.

The cloud filled the temple. It rose not from any altar that the priests had kindled; it came not in through the doors. It was simply there — heavy as the mist that rolleth in over a mountainside, but bright at its inner places — and the priests that had been standing in their orders by the altar could not see the man beside them, and could not hold their stations. They stepped back through the cloud toward the porch. The trumpets were fallen silent. The cymbals hung still at the singers' sides. The court of the temple kept the singing in the air thereof even after the singers had ceased, and the glory of the Lord remained within the house that Solomon had builded for him.
