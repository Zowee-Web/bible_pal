On a gentle hillside covered in green grass, a shepherd watched over his flock as the morning sun rose above the world. The fields stretched out as far as any sheep could wander. In every direction, the air was filled with the sound of wind rustling through tall grass and the gentle bleating of a hundred sheep. The shepherd loved each and every one of his sheep. He counted them under his breath, making sure they were all close by and safe. They nibbled on sweet plants under the wide open sky, their woolly backs glowing soft and white. Beyond the grassy fields stood quiet hills, dotted with rocks and wildflowers, where bushes hid in the shadows and the ground became uneven and rough. The shepherd’s heart was peaceful as he kept watch, his eyes always moving, making sure no sheep drifted too far from the rest.

In the flock, one little sheep grew curious about the world beyond the soft, safe grass. The sheep had always stayed near the others, sometimes nudging close to its friends, other times exploring the shade of a tree or the edge of the flock. But on this day, as the shepherd counted and the other sheep grazed, the little sheep felt something calling from beyond the meadow. Maybe it was the bright blue of a distant flower, or a patch of fresh grass that looked even softer than where it stood. Quietly, almost unnoticed, the little sheep began to wander away from the ninety-nine. Its tiny hooves carried it through patches of wildflowers and then onto rocky ground, where the grass grew thinner.

Step by step, the little sheep moved further and further away. The comfort of familiar voices faded behind it. In their place came new sounds—the whisper of wind through tall, dry grass, the chirping of birds hidden among the stones, and the crunch of pebbles beneath its feet. The path was not smooth anymore. Rocks poked at its soft ankles, and bushes tugged at its wool. The sun shone bright, but here the shadows felt longer, the world larger and more strange. The little sheep looked back, but it could not see the others. For the first time, its heart skipped with worry.

At that moment, as the flock grazed peacefully, the shepherd began to count again. He called quietly, listening for a returning bleat. Ninety-eight…ninety-nine…but there was no hundredth sheep. His face grew serious, and he looked all around the hillside. The wind carried only the sounds of the grass and the faintest echo. He turned his head, his eyes searching the horizon, and called again. Still, the little sheep did not answer. The shepherd’s heart beat faster, because every sheep in his care mattered very much to him, and he knew one was missing.

Leaving the rest of the flock in the peaceful meadow, the shepherd stepped carefully away from the ninety-nine, following the narrow trail where the grass was pressed down by tiny hooves. He passed blooming flowers and rocks warm from the sun. The sheep in the flock watched him go, their tails swishing and their eyes gentle, safe together on the soft field. The shepherd’s sandals brushed against the pebbles. His staff helped him walk along the steep, uneven ground. He listened as the breeze whispered and waited, hoping to hear the familiar sound of the lost sheep. Even though the way was rough, and the ground sometimes hard to cross, he did not stop. His strong arms were ready to carry the little sheep back home.

Then, out on the wild hillside, he heard it—a faint bleat, carried by the wind. The shepherd’s face lit with hope. He called the lost sheep’s name again, moving faster towards the sound. He climbed over stones and through prickly bushes, never giving up. Finally, in a patch of shade beneath a little tree, he saw the lost sheep standing still, wool a bit rumpled. The sheep’s eyes showed worry, but as it saw the shepherd, it trusted him right away. The shepherd smiled with great joy, his warm hands reaching out gently. He lifted the little sheep and placed it across his strong shoulders, right where it felt safest. The lost sheep could feel the shepherd’s kindness and the steady beat of his heart through the soft wool.

With the lost sheep safely on his shoulders, the shepherd turned back toward the sunlit hills and green grass. Each step felt lighter than before, even though he was carrying the extra weight, because his heart was filled with happiness. The shepherd walked with care, as the little sheep nuzzled against his neck, warm and safe. The breeze brushed against them as they returned to the meadow, where the ninety-nine sheep stood waiting. The other sheep watched and gave soft, merry bleats when they saw their friend had come home. The shepherd was overjoyed. He called out to his neighbors and friends, his voice ringing merrily across the fields, “Rejoice with me! I have found my sheep which was lost.”

The birds sang, and the day felt brighter as the flock was whole again. The shepherd’s laughter rose like a song. His love for each sheep was clear, and his joy could not be hidden. He cared deeply for every single one, and not one was forgotten or left behind. The little lost sheep was safely back, hugged by the gentle arms of the shepherd, safe among the green hills and its friends.

Just as the shepherd’s heart was filled with gladness over finding his lost sheep, so there is more joy in heaven over one who comes back, than over ninety-nine who needed no finding. The flock was together again, and the hillside echoed with happiness, for the shepherd had found his lost sheep, and brought it home.

The sun rose higher and warmed the grass, casting long golden stripes across the hillside. The little sheep snuggled closer to its friends, still feeling the gentle touch of the shepherd’s arms. It remembered the rough ground beyond the meadow, but now the comfort of the flock soothed its worry away. Around it, the others grazed quietly, calm and unafraid, with the shepherd’s eyes watching over them all.

The shepherd knelt by his flock, resting for a moment as the breeze fluttered through his hair. He touched each sheep as he moved among them, offering a pat here, a soft word there. Even the rambunctious lambs gathered close, drawn by his warmth and steady voice. He lifted his wooden flute and played a soft tune, letting the gentle notes dance across the meadow. The flock listened and nibbled at the grass, safe beneath the open sky.

Later, as the afternoon turned the hills dusky blue, the shepherd led his flock toward home. The little sheep kept close to his side, no longer curious for distant places, only content to follow where he walked. Night birds called from the shadows, but the gentle ring of the shepherd’s staff promised safety with every step. The flock moved together, each sheep finding its place, all of them secure.

When they reached the fold, the shepherd guided his sheep one by one through the gate, making sure no one was missing this time. The little sheep trotted through last, pausing to look back at the big wide hills. It felt a new peace, knowing the kindness of its shepherd could always find a lost soul, no matter how far it wandered.

As stars glimmered above, the shepherd settled at the gate, smiling as he watched over his flock. Safe and whole, the sheep nestled in the sweet hay, and the quiet hope of the hillside wrapped itself around them like a soft blanket. In that quiet night, every heart—shepherd and sheep—rested easy, knowing they belonged and would never be lost in the fields alone.
