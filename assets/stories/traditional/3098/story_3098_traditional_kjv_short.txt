After two days, Jesus departed thence and went into Galilee. He had testified that a prophet hath no honour in his own country. Yet when he came into Galilee, the Galileans received him. They had been at the feast in Jerusalem and had seen the things he did there.

Jesus came again to Cana of Galilee, where he had made the water wine. At that time, a certain nobleman in Capernaum had a son who was sick. The child was at the point of death.

When the nobleman heard that Jesus was come out of Judea into Galilee, he went unto him. He besought Jesus to come down to Capernaum and heal his son.

Jesus answered, “Except ye see signs and wonders, ye will not believe.”

The nobleman answered, “Sir, come down ere my child die.”

Jesus gave him an answer without going with him: “Go thy way; thy son liveth.”

The man believed the word that Jesus had spoken unto him, and he went his way. As he was going down, his servants met him. Their report repeated the words Jesus had spoken: “Thy son liveth.”

The father enquired of them the hour when his son began to amend. They answered that yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him. The father knew the time. It was the same hour in which Jesus had said unto him, “Thy son liveth.”

The nobleman believed, and his whole house believed with him. He had come to Cana beseeching Jesus for a child at the point of death. He returned with the word of Jesus, met servants bearing news of life, and found that the word and the healing had come at the same hour. This was the second miracle Jesus did when he was come out of Judea into Galilee.
