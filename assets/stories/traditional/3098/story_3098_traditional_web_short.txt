After two days, Jesus left and went into Galilee. He had testified that a prophet has no honor in his own country. Yet when he entered Galilee, the Galileans received him. They had been at the feast in Jerusalem and had seen the things he did there.

Jesus came again to Cana, the place in Galilee where he had made the water into wine. At that time, a nobleman in Capernaum had a son who was sick. The boy was at the point of death.

When the nobleman heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee, he went to him. He begged Jesus to come down to Capernaum and heal his son.

Jesus answered, “Unless you see signs and wonders, you will in no way believe.”

The nobleman answered Jesus with another plea: “Sir, come down before my child dies.”

Jesus gave him an answer without going with him: “Go your way. Your son lives.”

The man believed the word Jesus spoke to him, and he went his way. The nobleman had asked Jesus to come down; instead, he departed because Jesus told him that his son lived. While he was going down, his servants met him. Their report repeated the words Jesus had spoken: “Your child lives!”

The father asked them when his son had begun to get better. They answered that the fever had left him yesterday at the seventh hour. The father recognized the time. It was the same hour in which Jesus had told him, “Your son lives.”

The nobleman believed, and his whole house believed with him. What began with a father coming to Cana to plead for a dying child ended with a living son and a household that believed. This was the second sign Jesus did after coming out of Judea into Galilee.
