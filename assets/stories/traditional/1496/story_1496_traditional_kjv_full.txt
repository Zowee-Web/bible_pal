The walk up unto Shiloh was long. Hannah and Elkanah were come up from Ramah with the offering — the bullock for the sacrifice, the ephah of flour, the wine. The boy walked between them. He was very small. He had but lately been weaned. He had not been to Shiloh aforetime.

The road went up through the hill country. They stayed to rest in the heat of the day under an oak that had been by the road as long as any could remember. Hannah sat with the boy in her lap. He fell asleep against her shoulder. She moved not for a while. Elkanah watched her without speaking.

She was musing, as she sat there, of the last time she was come to this place. She had come up unto Shiloh that year not able to eat. She had stood in the court of the house of the LORD and wept until she could weep no more. She had spoken in her heart. She had not spoken aloud. Eli had thought she was drunken. She had answered him that no, she had poured out her soul before the LORD. He had blessed her and let her go in peace.

The boy in her lap now was of that day. She had named him Samuel — "I have asked him of the LORD." She had told Elkanah, when the boy was born, that she would bring him unto the LORD when he should be weaned. The day was come.

They went on in the afternoon. They came to Shiloh by evening. They tarried the night with friends. In the morning they brought the offering unto the priest. Eli was told who the woman was and wherefore she was come. She was come to give the child unto the LORD as she had vowed.

Then she began to speak. She wept not. The boy was holding the hem of her robe. Her hand was lifted up. The song came forth out of her that none had heard before.

"My heart rejoiceth in the LORD," she said. "Mine horn is exalted in the LORD."

She spake of the bows of the mighty broken. She spake of the feeble girded with strength. She spake of the full that had hired themselves out for bread, and of the hungry that had ceased. She spake of the barren that had borne seven, and of her that had many children that was waxed feeble.

"The LORD killeth, and maketh alive. He bringeth down to the grave, and bringeth up. The LORD maketh poor, and maketh rich. He raiseth up the poor out of the dust. He lifteth up the beggar from the dunghill, to set them among princes, to make them inherit the throne of glory."

The boy stood at her side. He understood not the song. His hand was yet upon the hem of her robe.

She came to the end of it. "He shall give strength unto his king, and exalt the horn of his anointed."

She lowered her arm. She laid her hand upon the head of the boy and held it there. The day went on round about them in the court. The smoke of the offerings was going up. The priests were moving among the altars. Eli stood watching her.

Then she and Elkanah turned. They went down the long road home unto Ramah. The boy was left with Eli. The song abode with Hannah along the way.

She should come back every year to bring him a little coat. She knew it. She should see him grow up in the courts of the house. She wept not on the way home that day. She had wept her weeping already, the year before. This year she sang what had been given her to sing instead.
