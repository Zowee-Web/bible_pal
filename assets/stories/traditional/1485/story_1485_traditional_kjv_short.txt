And in the morning, rising up a great while before day, Jesus went out.

He departed from the house where he had been sleeping. The streets of Capernaum were quiet at that hour. He walked through the town and out beyond it, into a solitary place where no man came at that early hour.

There he prayed.

He had no scroll with him. He had no lamp. He stood on the open ground and lifted his face to the dark sky above him, and he spake unto his Father in the silence before the day came up.

The first light began to rise behind the hills.

Simon and they that were with him awoke and found his place was empty. They went out from the house where he had been. They asked along the streets. They walked the road outside the town.

They found him.

He was yet standing in the solitary place where he had been praying.

They said unto him, "All men seek for thee."

He answered them.

"Let us go into the next towns, that I may preach there also. For therefore came I forth."

He came in from the open ground. The first light was on the hills behind him.

He preached in the synagogues throughout all Galilee, and cast out devils. The morning went on after them across the hills.
