Early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus rose.

He went out of the house where he had been sleeping. The streets of Capernaum were quiet at that hour. He walked through the town and out beyond it, into a deserted place where no one came at that early hour.

He prayed there.

He had no scroll with him. He had no lamp. He stood on the open ground and lifted his face to the dark sky above him, and he spoke to his Father in the silence before the day came up.

The first light began to rise behind the hills.

Simon and the others who had been with him woke and found his place was empty. They went out from the house where he had been. They asked along the streets. They walked the road outside the town.

They found him.

He was still standing in the deserted place where he had been praying.

They said to him, "Everyone is looking for you."

He answered them.

"Let us go elsewhere — into the next towns, that I may preach there also. Because I came out for this reason."

He came in from the open ground. The first light was on the hills behind him.

He went with them into the next towns of Galilee, preaching in their synagogues, and casting out demons. The morning went on after them across the hills.
