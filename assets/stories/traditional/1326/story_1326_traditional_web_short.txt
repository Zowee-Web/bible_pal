David had grown old. He had taken Solomon, his son, to stand before all the assembly of Israel at Jerusalem — the princes, the captains, the elders, the men of valor. He had laid out before them the work that lay ahead: the building of the house of Yahweh, the gathering of the gold and the silver and the bronze and the iron and the wood and the precious stones. He had given of his own treasure. And the people had freely given — for the work of the house. And the assembly rejoiced. And David, before them all, lifted up his voice.

Therefore David blessed Yahweh before all the assembly; and David said, "You are blessed, Yahweh, the God of Israel our father, forever and ever.

Yours, Yahweh, is the greatness, the power, the glory, the victory, and the majesty! For all that is in the heavens and in the earth is yours. Yours is the kingdom, Yahweh, and you are exalted as head above all.

Both riches and honor come from you, and you rule over all; and in your hand is power and might; and it is in your hand to make great, and to give strength to all.

Now therefore, our God, we thank you, and praise your glorious name."

And the assembly listened to the words of the king. The gold lay before them. The silver lay before them. The plans for the house of Yahweh were spread upon the ground. David's hands trembled now with age, but his voice carried, and the people heard, and the day went on as the day of preparation had been going on. And so it was in Jerusalem, in the last days of the king, before the building of the house began.
