David was waxed old. He had taken Solomon, his son, to stand before all the congregation of Israel at Jerusalem — the princes, the captains, the elders, the men of valour. He had laid before them the work that lay ahead: the building of the house of the Lord, the gathering of the gold and the silver and the brass and the iron and the wood and the precious stones. He had given of his own treasure. And the people had freely given — for the work of the house. And the congregation rejoiced. And David, before them all, lifted up his voice.

Therefore David blessed the Lord before all the congregation: and David said, "Blessed be thou, Lord God of Israel our father, for ever and ever.

Thine, O Lord, is the greatness, and the power, and the glory, and the victory, and the majesty: for all that is in the heaven and in the earth is thine; thine is the kingdom, O Lord, and thou art exalted as head above all.

Both riches and honour come of thee, and thou reignest over all; and in thine hand is power and might; and in thine hand it is to make great, and to give strength unto all.

Now therefore, our God, we thank thee, and praise thy glorious name."

And the congregation hearkened unto the words of the king. The gold lay before them. The silver lay before them. The plans for the house of the Lord were spread upon the ground. David's hands trembled now with age, but his voice carried, and the people heard, and the day went on as the day of preparation had been going on. And so it was in Jerusalem, in the last days of the king, before the building of the house began.
