Hilkiah the high priest found the book in the house of the LORD. He had been counting silver for the workmen that were repairing the temple. The book was the book of the law. It had been lost. None knew when it had been put there or how it had come to be hidden.

He brought it unto Shaphan the scribe. Shaphan read it. He took it up unto the king.

He read it before Josiah. Josiah was a young king. He hearkened unto the words of the book all the way through. When the reading was done he rent his clothes.

He called for Hilkiah and Ahikam and Achbor and Shaphan and Asaiah. He sent them out. He said: go ye, enquire of the LORD for me and for the people and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found. Great is the wrath of the LORD that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not hearkened unto the words of this book.

They went unto Huldah the prophetess. She dwelt in Jerusalem in the college. They came in unto her. They told her what the king had said.

She spake unto them. "Thus saith the LORD God of Israel. Tell the man that sent you to me: thus saith the LORD: behold, I will bring evil upon this place, and upon the inhabitants thereof, even all the words of the book which the king of Judah hath read. Because they have forsaken me, and have burned incense unto other gods, my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and shall not be quenched."

She had more to say. "But to the king of Judah which sent you to enquire of the LORD, tell him: thus saith the LORD God of Israel. Because thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before the LORD when thou heardest what I spake against this place. Because thou hast rent thy clothes and wept before me. I also have heard thee, saith the LORD. I will gather thee unto thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered into thy grave in peace. Thine eyes shall not see all the evil which I will bring upon this place."

They brought the king word again.
