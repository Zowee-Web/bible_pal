Hilkiah the high priest had been counting silver in the house of the LORD. The workmen were repairing the temple. The silver was being weighed out for them. Carpenters had been at the place. Masons. Hewers of stone. Timber and stone had been brought in. The work had been going on for some days. Hilkiah had been moving among the rooms of the house with the silver and the tally.

He found the book in one of the chambers. It was the book of the law. It had been lost. None knew when it had been put there or how it had come to be hidden among the other things of the house.

He took it up. He brought it unto Shaphan the scribe. Shaphan read it. He took it up unto the king.

He read it before Josiah. Josiah was a young king. He hearkened unto the words of the book all the way through. The reader's voice was steady in the chamber. When the reading was done the king rent his clothes.

He called for Hilkiah and Ahikam and Achbor and Shaphan and Asaiah. They stood before him. He sent them out. He said: go ye, enquire of the LORD for me and for the people and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found. Great is the wrath of the LORD that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not hearkened unto the words of this book.

They went forth from the king. They went unto Huldah the prophetess. She dwelt in Jerusalem in the college. They came in unto her. They told her what the king had said. They named the book and the rending of the clothes.

She spake unto them. "Thus saith the LORD God of Israel. Tell the man that sent you to me: thus saith the LORD: behold, I will bring evil upon this place, and upon the inhabitants thereof, even all the words of the book which the king of Judah hath read. Because they have forsaken me, and have burned incense unto other gods, my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and shall not be quenched."

She had more to say. "But to the king of Judah which sent you to enquire of the LORD, tell him: thus saith the LORD God of Israel. Because thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before the LORD when thou heardest what I spake against this place. Because thou hast rent thy clothes and wept before me. I also have heard thee, saith the LORD. I will gather thee unto thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered into thy grave in peace. Thine eyes shall not see all the evil which I will bring upon this place."

The five men went back unto the king. They brought him word again. They told him what Huldah had spoken. They told him both parts of it. The book abode in the king's house. The reading had been done. The word had been brought back. The king sat where they had left him.
