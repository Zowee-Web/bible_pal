Hilkiah the high priest had been counting silver in the house of the LORD. The workmen were repairing the temple. The silver was being weighed out for them. Carpenters had been at the place. Masons. Hewers of stone. Timber and stone had been brought in. The work had been going on for some days. Hilkiah had been moving among the rooms of the house with the silver and the tally.

He found the book in one of the chambers. It was the book of the law. It had been lost. No one knew when it had been put there or how it had come to be hidden among the other things of the house.

He took it up. He brought it to Shaphan the scribe. Shaphan read it. He took it up to the king.

He read it before Josiah. Josiah was a young king. He listened to the words of the book all the way through. The reader's voice was steady in the chamber. When the reading was done the king rent his clothes.

He called for Hilkiah and Ahikam and Achbor and Shaphan and Asaiah. They stood before him. He sent them out. He said: go, inquire of the LORD for me and for the people and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that has been found. Great is the wrath of the LORD that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not hearkened to the words of this book.

They went out from the king. They went to Huldah the prophetess. She dwelt in Jerusalem in the second quarter. They came in to her. They told her what the king had said. They named the book and the rending of the clothes.

She spoke to them. "Thus says the LORD God of Israel. Tell the man that sent you to me: thus says the LORD: behold, I will bring evil upon this place and upon the inhabitants thereof, even all the words of the book that the king of Judah has read. Because they have forsaken me, and have burned incense to other gods, my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and shall not be quenched."

She had more to say. "But to the king of Judah who sent you to inquire of the LORD, tell him: thus says the LORD God of Israel. Because your heart was tender, and you did humble yourself before the LORD when you heard what I spoke against this place. Because you rent your clothes and wept before me. I also have heard you, says the LORD. I will gather you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your grave in peace. Your eyes shall not see all the evil that I will bring upon this place."

The five men went back to the king. They brought him word again. They told him what Huldah had spoken. They told him both parts of it. The book stayed in the king's house. The reading had been done. The word had been brought back. The king sat where they had left him.
