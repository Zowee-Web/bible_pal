Moses, the servant of Yahweh, was dead. His body lay buried in the valley in the land of Moab, and the days of weeping had passed. The camp of Israel spread across the plains east of the Jordan, thousands of tents staked into dry ground, smoke rising from cook fires in the still morning air.

Joshua son of Nun stood at the edge of the camp. He had served Moses for years, walking beside him, carrying his words to the people, standing guard at the tent of meeting. Now the camp was quiet in a different way. The voice that had commanded them out of Egypt no longer spoke from among them.

Then Yahweh spoke to Joshua. "Moses my servant is dead. Now therefore arise, go across this Jordan, you and all this people, to the land which I am giving to them, even to the children of Israel. I have given you every place that the sole of your foot will tread on, as I told Moses. From the wilderness and this Lebanon, even to the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and to the great sea toward the going down of the sun, shall be your border."

Joshua stood listening, the wind off the Jordan plain pressing against his robes.

"No man will be able to stand before you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not fail you nor forsake you."

Across the river, the land of Canaan stretched green and brown under the open sky, its hills visible from where Joshua stood.

"Be strong and courageous, for you shall cause this people to inherit the land which I swore to their fathers to give them. Only be strong and very courageous. Be careful to do according to all the law which Moses my servant commanded you. Don't turn from it to the right hand or to the left, that you may have good success wherever you go."

Somewhere behind him, the sounds of the camp continued — children calling, animals shifting, the clank of bronze tools against stone.

"This book of the law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate on it day and night, that you may observe to do according to all that is written in it; for then you shall make your way prosperous, and then you shall have good success. Haven't I commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Don't be afraid. Don't be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you wherever you go."

The voice ceased. The river moved below, brown and steady between its banks. Joshua turned back toward the tents of Israel, his sandals pressing into the dust, and began walking through the camp to find the officers of the people.
