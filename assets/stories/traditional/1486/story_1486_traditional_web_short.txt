They had been brought into Babylon from Jerusalem in the third year of the king's reign. They had been selected from among the children of the captives — young men without blemish, well-favored, skillful in all wisdom, fit to stand in the king's palace.

Daniel was one of them.

The king's portion was set before them at the appointed hour. Wine from the king's table. Meat from the king's table. Three years of training, and then they would stand before the king.

Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king's food, nor with the wine which the king drank.

He went to the prince of the eunuchs. He asked that he might not defile himself.

The prince was afraid. He said, "I fear my lord the king, who has appointed your food and your drink. Why should he see your faces worse than the other youths who are of your own age? You would endanger my head."

Daniel did not press him there.

He went to the steward whom the prince had set over him and over Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.

He said to the steward, "Test your servants, I beg you, ten days. Let them give us vegetables to eat, and water to drink. Then let our faces be examined before you, and the faces of the youths who eat the king's portion. As you see, deal with your servants."

The steward listened to him in this matter. He tested them for ten days.

The ten days passed.

At the end of them, their faces appeared fairer, and they were fatter in flesh, than all the youths who ate of the king's food.

The steward took away the king's portion. He gave them vegetables, from that day on.

The three years passed. Daniel and the three with him grew in knowledge. God gave them skill in all learning and wisdom. Daniel had understanding in visions and dreams.

When the days were finished that the king had appointed, the prince of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar.

The king spoke with them.

Among them all was found no one like Daniel and Hananiah and Mishael and Azariah. The king found them ten times better in wisdom than all the magicians and enchanters in his realm.

Daniel continued before the king from that year. He continued unto the first year of King Cyrus.
