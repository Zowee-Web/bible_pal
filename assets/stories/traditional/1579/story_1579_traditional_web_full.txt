Joseph was brought down to Egypt, and Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, bought him from the Ishmaelites who had brought him there. And Yahweh was with Joseph. He prospered in the house of his Egyptian master, and his master saw that Yahweh was with him, and made all that he did succeed in his hand. So Joseph found favor in Potiphar's sight, and was made overseer of his house; and all that he had, Potiphar put into Joseph's hand. From that time Yahweh blessed the Egyptian's house for Joseph's sake — his blessing on all that Potiphar had, in the house and in the field — so that his master left everything in Joseph's hand, and gave no thought to anything but the food he ate. Now Joseph was well-built and handsome.

After a time, his master's wife set her eyes on Joseph, and said, "Lie with me." But he refused. "My master has put all that he has into my hand and kept back nothing from me but you, because you are his wife," he said. "No one in this house is greater than I am. How then could I do this great wickedness, and sin against God?" Day by day she spoke to him, but he would not listen to her, or lie beside her, or be with her.

One day he went into the house to do his work, and none of the men of the house were inside. She caught him by his garment and said, "Lie with me!" But he left the garment in her hand, and ran outside.

When she saw that he had left his garment in her hand and had fled, she called to the men of her house. "See," she said, "he has brought in a Hebrew to mock us. He came in to me to lie with me, and I cried out with a loud voice; and when he heard me lift my voice and cry, he left his garment beside me and ran outside." She kept his garment beside her until his master came home, and then she told him the same words: "The Hebrew servant you brought to us came in to me to mock me; but when I lifted my voice and cried, he left his garment beside me and ran outside."

When Joseph's master heard his wife say, "This is what your servant did to me," his wrath was kindled. He took Joseph and put him in the prison, the place where the king's prisoners were bound; and Joseph was there in custody.

But Yahweh was with Joseph even there, and showed him kindness, and gave him favor in the sight of the keeper of the prison. The keeper put all the prisoners into Joseph's hand, and whatever was done there, Joseph was the one who saw to it. The keeper gave no thought to anything under Joseph's care, because Yahweh was with him; and whatever he did, Yahweh made it prosper.
