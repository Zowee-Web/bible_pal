When the wise men had gone, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. "Get up," he said, "take the child and his mother, and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, for Herod is going to search for the child, to destroy him."

So Joseph got up that night, took the child and his mother, and left for Egypt. And there he stayed until Herod died. In this way the word the Lord had spoken through the prophet was fulfilled: "Out of Egypt I called my son."

When Herod saw that the wise men had outwitted him, he was filled with rage. He gave the order, and in Bethlehem and all the country around it, every boy two years old and under was killed, according to the time he had learned from the wise men.

Then the word spoken through the prophet Jeremiah was fulfilled: "A voice was heard in Ramah, weeping and great mourning — Rachel weeping for her children, and refusing to be comforted, because they are no more."
