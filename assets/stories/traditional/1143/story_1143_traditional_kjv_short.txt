Now the parents of Jesus went unto Jerusalem every year at the feast of the passover. When he was twelve years old, they went up to the city after the custom of the feast. The roads from Galilee were full of travellers, and they went up in a great company.

When they had fulfilled the days, as they returned, the child Jesus tarried behind in Jerusalem, and Joseph and his mother knew not of it. They, supposing him to have been in the company, went a day's journey. They sought him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance, but they found him not.

When they found him not, they turned back again to Jerusalem, seeking him. The streets were thronged with pilgrims yet departing the city, and they searched among the gates and the markets and the courts of the temple. They enquired among friends and kindred, and the days passed without tidings of him.

And it came to pass, that after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the doctors, both hearing them and asking them questions. And all that heard him were astonished at his understanding and answers.

When they saw him, they were amazed. His mother said unto him, "Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing."

He said unto them, "How is it that ye sought me? wist ye not that I must be about my Father's business?" But they understood not the saying which he spake unto them.

Then he went down with them, and came to Nazareth, and was subject unto them. His mother kept all these sayings in her heart.

And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man. He grew up in the household at Nazareth, and the years of his youth passed in the home of his parents.
