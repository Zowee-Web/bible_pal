The parents of Jesus went every year to Jerusalem at the Feast of the Passover. When he was twelve years old, they went up to the city according to the custom of the feast. The roads from Galilee were full of travelers, and they went up in a great company.

When the days were fulfilled and they were returning home, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem, but Joseph and his mother did not know it. They supposed him to be in the company of their travelers, and they went a day's journey. They looked for him among their relatives and acquaintances, but they did not find him.

When they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem, looking for him. The streets were crowded with pilgrims still leaving the city, and they searched among the gates and the markets and the courts of the temple. They asked among friends and family, and the days passed without word of him.

After three days they found him in the temple. He was sitting in the middle of the teachers, both listening to them and asking them questions. All who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

When they saw him, they were astonished. His mother said to him, "Son, why have you treated us this way? Behold, your father and I were anxiously looking for you."

He said to them, "Why were you looking for me? Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house?" But they did not understand the saying which he spoke to them.

Then he went down with them and came to Nazareth, and he was subject to them. His mother kept all these sayings in her heart.

And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men. He grew up in the household at Nazareth, and the years of his youth passed in the home of his parents.
