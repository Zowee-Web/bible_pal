Now at that feast the governor was wont to release unto the people a prisoner, whomsoever they would, and set him at liberty before them all, whatsoever he had done. And they had then a notable prisoner in the jail, called Barabbas. So when they were gathered together before the judgment seat, Pilate said unto them, Whom will ye that I release unto you? Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ? For he knew well that it was for envy they had delivered him up.

And while he was set down on the judgment seat, his wife sent unto him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that just man: for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him.

But the chief priests and elders moved among the multitude, from one to another, stirring them up, and persuaded them that they should ask Barabbas, and destroy Jesus. So the governor answered and said unto them again, Whether of the twain will ye that I release unto you? And they said, Barabbas.

Pilate saith unto them, What shall I do then with Jesus which is called Christ? They all say unto him, Let him be crucified.

And the governor said, Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more exceedingly, again and yet again, Let him be crucified.

When Pilate saw that he could prevail nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, where all could see, saying, I am innocent of the blood of this just person: see ye to it. Then answered all the people, His blood be on us, and on our children.

Then released he Barabbas unto them: and when he had scourged Jesus, he delivered him to be crucified.
