Paul stood upon a ship that was breaking asunder. The tempest had lasted fourteen days. The cargo was cast out. The tackling was gone. Neither sun nor stars in many days appeared. Every rational measure said it was over.

Then Paul stood forth among them and said he believed God. He took bread upon the deck of a breaking ship and gave thanks. He brake it and did eat. And the men about him took meat also.

The ship yet brake apart. They yet had to swim for shore. But all two hundred threescore and sixteen escaped safe to land. Every one alive.

Sometimes holding is not the absence of tempest. It is the presence of something the tempest cannot reach.
