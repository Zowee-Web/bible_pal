Moses turned and went down from the mountain, carrying the two tablets of the covenant. Written on both sides, the tablets were God’s work, with God’s writing engraved on them.

Joshua heard the people shouting and told Moses there was the noise of war in the camp. Moses answered that it was neither the shout of victory nor the cry of defeat, but singing that he heard.

Near the camp, Moses saw the calf and the dancing. His anger grew hot. He threw down the tablets, breaking them beneath the mountain. He burned the calf, ground it to powder, scattered it on the water, and made the children of Israel drink it.

Moses asked Aaron what the people had done to him, that he had brought such great sin on them. Aaron asked his lord not to be angry, saying Moses knew the people were set on evil. They had demanded gods to go before them, saying they did not know what had become of Moses, who brought them from Egypt. Aaron said, “I said to them, ‘Whoever has any gold, let them take it off.’ So they gave it to me; and I threw it into the fire, and out came this calf.”

Moses saw that the people were out of control, because Aaron had let them lose control, causing derision among their enemies. Standing in the camp’s gate, Moses called everyone on Yahweh’s side to come to him. All the sons of Levi gathered to him. He declared Yahweh, the God of Israel’s command: each man was to put his sword on his thigh, go back and forth from gate to gate throughout the camp, and kill his brother, companion, and neighbor. The sons of Levi obeyed Moses, and about three thousand men fell that day. Moses told them to consecrate themselves to Yahweh, every man having been against his son and brother, that Yahweh might give them a blessing that day.

The next day Moses told the people they had sinned greatly. He would go up to Yahweh; perhaps he could make atonement for their sin. Returning to Yahweh, Moses acknowledged that they had sinned greatly and made gods of gold. He asked for their forgiveness; if not, he asked to be blotted out of the book Yahweh had written.

Yahweh answered that whoever had sinned against him would be blotted out of his book. Moses was to lead the people to the place Yahweh had named. His angel would go before Moses, but when he punished, he would punish their sin. Yahweh struck the people because of what they did with the calf, which Aaron made.
