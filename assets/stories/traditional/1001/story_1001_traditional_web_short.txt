A shepherd walks at the front of his flock across a wide valley floor. The grass here grows thick and green, fed by a stream that runs clear over smooth stones. He carries a rod in one hand and a staff in the other, both worn from use. The sheep move behind him in a loose group, their hooves pressing soft prints into the damp ground.

He leads them to the water. The stream pools in a shallow bend where the current slows and the surface barely moves. One by one the sheep lower their heads and drink. No rushing current pulls at them. The water is still, and the shepherd stands at the edge, watching.

When they finish, he turns and walks again. The flock follows. The path he chooses runs along the valley where the footing is sure and the way is straight. His voice carries back to them — a low, steady call — and the animals keep pace behind him.

The trail narrows as it passes between two ridges. Shadow falls across the ground. The air cools. Rock walls rise on either side, and the light dims until the path ahead is hard to see. The sheep press closer together. Their bleating echoes off the stone. The shepherd does not stop. He walks ahead of them through the dark passage, tapping his rod against the rock face so the sound carries back. His staff brushes against the nearest animals, guiding them forward. Step by step, the flock moves through the shadowed valley.

Then the ground opens again. Light returns. The grass stretches out wide on the far side, and the shepherd leads them into it.

He stops at a broad, flat place and sets out provisions. A low stone serves as a table. He lays bread on it, pours oil from a vessel, and fills a cup until it brims over and runs down the side. Around the edges of the clearing, other animals watch from the brush — but the shepherd sits at the stone and eats. The cup overflows onto the ground beside him.

The flock grazes nearby. The shepherd rises and walks among them, pressing oil into the wool on their heads where thorns have scratched the skin. He moves from one animal to the next, his hands steady.

As the day lengthens, the shepherd gathers the flock and turns them back toward a stone-walled enclosure visible against the hillside. Goodness and mercy — provision and care — have followed this flock all through the day's journey. The shepherd walks them home, toward the house where they will dwell, and the gate stands open ahead of them.

He calls out once more, and the sheep pass through.
