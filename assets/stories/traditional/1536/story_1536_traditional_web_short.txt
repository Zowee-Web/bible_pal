The one who dwells in the secret place of the Most High rests under the shadow of the Almighty. He says of the LORD, "He is my refuge and my fortress. My God. In him I will trust."

The Almighty delivers him from the snare of the fowler. From the deadly pestilence that creeps unseen. He covers him with his feathers, and under his wings the man finds shelter. The truth of God stands around him like a shield and a heavy wall.

There is no fear of terror by night. No fear of the arrow that flies by day. No fear of the pestilence that moves through darkness, or the destruction that strikes at noon. The man stands still. The shadow of the Almighty does not move from over him.

A thousand fall at his left side. Ten thousand fall at his right hand. The ground is thick with the fallen. But the destruction does not come near him. He looks out with his own eyes and sees the reward of the wicked laid bare across the field.

Because he has made the LORD his refuge — the Most High his dwelling place — no evil reaches him. No plague comes near his door. The threshold is clean. The walls stand. The air inside is still.

God gives his angels charge over him. They keep him in all his ways. Their hands bear him up so that his foot does not strike against a stone. The path ahead is rough ground, loose rock, sharp edges. But his step holds firm.

He treads upon the lion. He treads upon the serpent. The young lion and the great beast he tramples under his feet. They do not rise.

Then the voice of God speaks over him. "He will call upon me, and I will answer him. I will be with him in trouble. I will deliver him and honor him. With long life I will satisfy him, and show him my salvation."

The shadow of the Almighty holds steady overhead. The man stands beneath it.
