He that abideth in the hidden place of the Most High shall find his rest beneath the shadow of the Almighty. And he declareth of the LORD, "He is my stronghold and my fortress. My God. In him do I place my trust."

The Almighty delivereth him out of the snare of the fowler, and from the grievous pestilence that creepeth unseen among men. He covereth him with his pinions, and beneath his wings the man taketh refuge. The faithfulness of God standeth round about him as a shield and as a wall of hewn stone.

He shall not fear the terror that cometh by night. Nor the arrow that flieth in the brightness of the day. Nor the pestilence that walketh through the darkness, nor the destruction that wasteth at the noontide. The man standeth still. And the shadow of the Almighty departeth not from over him.

A thousand shall fall at his left hand, and ten thousand at his right. The ground is strewn thick with the fallen. Yet the destruction draweth not nigh unto him. He looketh out with his own eyes, and he beholdeth the recompense of the wicked spread bare upon the field.

For he hath made the LORD his refuge — the Most High his dwelling place — and no evil shall reach unto him. No plague cometh nigh his door. The threshold is clean. The walls do stand. The air within is still.

For God giveth his angels charge concerning him, to keep him in all his ways. They bear him up upon their hands, lest his foot dash against a stone. The path before him is rough ground, and loose rock, and sharp edges. Yet his step holdeth firm.

He treadeth upon the lion. He treadeth upon the serpent. The young lion and the great beast he trampleth beneath his feet. And they rise not.

Then the voice of God speaketh over him. "He shall call upon me, and I will answer him. I will be with him in his trouble. I will deliver him, and bring him unto honour. With length of days will I satisfy him, and I will shew him my salvation."

The shadow of the Almighty holdeth steady overhead. And the man standeth beneath it.
