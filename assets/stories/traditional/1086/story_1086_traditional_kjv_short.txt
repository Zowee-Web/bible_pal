As Jesus passed through Jerusalem, he came upon a man which had been blind from his birth. The man sat by the wayside, his hands upon his knees, his face turned toward the warmth of the sun he had never beheld. His world was darkness, and it had ever been so.

His disciples asked him, saying, "Master, who did sin, this man, or his parents, that he was born blind?"

Jesus answered, "Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him."

He stooped down and spat on the ground and made clay of the spittle, and he anointed the eyes of the blind man with the clay. "Go," he said, "wash in the pool of Siloam."

The man arose and went his way through the streets of the city, guided by the sounds about him, until he came to the pool. He knelt at the edge, and washed.

And he received sight.

Light poured in upon him. The world took shape — the water, the stone, the sky, the green of the trees along the wall. He beheld his own hands, wet and trembling. He stood at the pool's edge, turning slowly, looking upon all things as one born again into the world.

His neighbours saw him and questioned among themselves. "Is not this he that sat and begged?" Some said it was he. Others said, "He is like him." But the man said, "I am he."

"How then were thine eyes opened?" they asked.

He told them all plainly. "A man that is called Jesus made clay, and anointed mine eyes, and said unto me, Go to the pool of Siloam, and wash: and I went and washed, and I received sight."

They brought him before the Pharisees, who questioned him closely. Some said, "This man is not of God, because he keepeth not the sabbath day." Others said, "How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles?" And there was a division among them.

They asked him again. "What sayest thou of him, that he hath opened thine eyes?"

He said, "He is a prophet."

They were not pleased. They called his parents. They called the man again. They reviled him. And when he would not change his testimony, they cast him out.

When Jesus heard that they had cast him out, he found him.

"Dost thou believe on the Son of God?" he asked.

The man answered, "Who is he, Lord, that I might believe on him?"

Jesus said unto him, "Thou hast both seen him, and it is he that talketh with thee."

The man said, "Lord, I believe."

And he worshipped him.
