One day, Jesus saw a man who had been blind his whole life. He had never seen anything — not the sky, not the trees, not even his own hands.

Jesus' friends asked, "Why was he born blind? Did someone do something wrong?"

Jesus said, "No one did anything wrong. This happened so that God's power could be shown."

Then Jesus did something surprising. He bent down and made some mud. He put the mud on the man's eyes.

"Go wash in the pool of Siloam," Jesus said.

The man walked through the city to the pool. He washed the mud off his face.

And he could see!

For the very first time, he saw the water. He saw the sky. He saw the world all around him. It was amazing.

When his neighbors saw him, they couldn't believe it. "Aren't you the man who was blind?" they asked.

"Yes! It's me!" he said.

Some important leaders called the Pharisees got upset because Jesus healed the man on a day of rest. They asked the man lots of questions. They even asked his parents. But the man kept telling the truth.

"I don't know everything about Jesus," the man said. "But I know one thing — I was blind, and now I can see."

The leaders got angry and kicked him out.

When Jesus heard about it, he found the man.

"Do you believe in the Son of God?" Jesus asked.

"Who is he?" the man said. "Tell me, so I can believe."

Jesus said, "You're looking at him right now. He's the one talking to you."

The man smiled. "Lord, I believe," he said.
