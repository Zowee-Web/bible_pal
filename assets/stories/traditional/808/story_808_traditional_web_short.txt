The gentle sun rested low above the fields. Soft golden light touched the grassy hills. Trees stood still, their leaves shining in the warm glow. Birds fluttered quietly from branch to branch. The air felt calm and peaceful, with a soft breeze moving through the garden. A family sat together on the grass. Their sheep lay nearby, munching on sweet green grass. Everything felt still and safe, and the soft sounds of evening filled the air.

The family looked up at the wide, open sky. High above, the blue sky stretched from one end to the other. White clouds drifted slowly, shaped by the gentle wind. The father pointed up, showing the children the faraway sun and the soft shapes of the clouds. He spoke softly. "The heavens show much beauty. Look — the sky tells wonderful things, with no words at all."

As the sun moved lower, it painted the world in warm orange and gold. Day slipped quietly into gentle twilight. The sky's colors shifted, soft and rich, from gold to purple and rose. The father stood, raising his hand toward the wide sky, and reminded the children, "Every day speaks to the next. Every night shares its beauty. There is no speech, nor any language, yet their voice is heard everywhere."

A small boy copied the father, stretching his arms wide. The family watched the sun move along its path across the sky, steady and sure. Each day's bright light spread everywhere, touching the hills, the rivers, and all living things. When the sun finished its path, the father said, "It brings its light with gladness. From one end of the sky to the other, its warmth is felt by all." The sheep rested quietly at their feet, calm and cared for.

Soon, soft twilight covered the land. The air grew cool and gentle. The birds grew quiet. The sheep nestled closer together in the grass. The family gathered their blankets. They lay down side by side, under the peaceful sky. The glow of sunset faded, leaving a gentle blanket of night. The children's eyes grew heavy. The father's voice grew soft and low. All around, the quiet night grew deep and still. Stars twinkled far above, watching over everyone. The family rested safe and warm. The world grew quiet, ready for sleep.
