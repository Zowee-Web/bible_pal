The sky is as a great, soft blanket above us. In the daytime, the sun climbeth high and shineth ever so bright. At night, the stars gently twinkle as tiny night-lights. All these things are very quiet. They speak not with words. But they show forth how wonderful and how mighty God is. Every day, the sky shareth its pretty story with any soul that looketh up. When thou seest the clouds floating or the sunlight sparkling, it is as though the sky smileth down upon thee. It feeleth safe and peaceful beneath so beautiful a sky.
