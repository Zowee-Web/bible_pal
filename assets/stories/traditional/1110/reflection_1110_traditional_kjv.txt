Jesus led them out as far as to Bethany. He lifted up his hands and blessed them. And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them and carried up into heaven.

They beheld him go. Their faces were turned upward. The hilltop was empty. The wind blew across the ridge.

Then they returned to Jerusalem with great joy.

Not grief. Joy. They had but watched him depart. They would walk with him no more upon the roads of Galilee or sit with him at a table in Bethany. He was gone from their sight.

And they went back praising God. Something in the blessing — in the manner of his departing — turned their beholding into worship and their loss into joy.
