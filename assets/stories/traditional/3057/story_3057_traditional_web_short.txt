James began with a direct command: believers in the glorious Lord Jesus Christ were not to hold the faith with partiality. He set before them a synagogue entered by two men, one wearing a gold ring and fine clothing and the other wearing filthy clothing. Giving the well-dressed man a good seat while telling the poor man to stand or sit by a footstool made them partial judges with evil thoughts. God had chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith and heirs of the promised Kingdom, yet they had dishonored the poor.

James pointed them to the royal law, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.” To fulfill it was to do well; to show partiality was to commit sin and be convicted as a transgressor. Whoever kept the whole law and yet stumbled in one point had become guilty of all. The one who said, “Do not commit adultery,” also said, “Do not commit murder.” James therefore called his readers to speak and act as people who would be judged by the law of freedom. Judgment would be without mercy to the one who had shown no mercy, but mercy triumphs over judgment.

James then asked, “What good is it” if someone said he had faith but had no works. If a brother or sister lacked clothing and daily food, words of peace, warmth, and fullness supplied none of the things the body needed. In the same way, faith without works was dead. A claim to faith could not be displayed apart from works, while faith could be shown by works. Even demons believed that God is one, and they shuddered.

Abraham was justified by works when he offered Isaac his son on the altar. His faith worked with his works, and by works faith was perfected. The Scripture saying that Abraham believed God and it was accounted to him as righteousness was fulfilled, and he was called the friend of God. James concluded that by works a man is justified, and not only by faith. In the same way, Rahab the prostitute was also justified by works when she received the messengers and sent them out another way. As the body apart from the spirit is dead, so faith apart from works is dead.
