Saul was consenting to Stephen's death. He stood by the stones in the morning.

A great persecution arose that day. It came up against the assembly in Jerusalem.

The believers scattered. They went out through the gates. They went north. They went south. They went into the regions of Judea. They went into Samaria. Only the apostles stayed.

Devout men buried Stephen. They carried his body. They laid him in the grave. They lamented greatly over him.

But Saul did not stop. He went into the houses. He pulled the believers out. He dragged men out by the arm. He dragged women out by the arm. He took them to prison.

The doors of the houses were broken. The cloaks were left on the floor. The tables were overturned.

Saul ravaged the assembly. He took them, house by house. He sent them to prison, one after another.

But those who had scattered did not stop either.

They walked the dust of the roads. They came to the towns. They came to the villages. They came to the markets and the fields. They came to the houses of the people of Judea and Samaria.

They opened their mouths. They preached the word.

They preached it on the road. They preached it at the well. They preached it in the field. They preached it at the gate.

Therefore those who were scattered abroad went around preaching the word.

The word was moving. The word was not staying. The word had gone out of Jerusalem and was running ahead of them.

The apostles stayed in Jerusalem. Saul stayed in Jerusalem. The believers were already gone.

The roads were full of them.
