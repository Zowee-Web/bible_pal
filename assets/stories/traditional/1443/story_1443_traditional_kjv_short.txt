Saul was consenting unto Stephen's death. He stood by the stones in the morning.

A great persecution arose that day. It came up against the church which was at Jerusalem.

The believers were scattered abroad. They went out through the gates. They went north. They went south. They went into the regions of Judaea. They went into Samaria. Only the apostles tarried behind.

Devout men buried Stephen. They carried his body. They laid him in the grave. They made great lamentation over him.

But Saul stayed not. He went into the houses. He haled the believers out. He haled men out by the arm. He haled women out by the arm. He committed them to prison.

The doors of the houses were broken. The cloaks were left upon the floor. The tables were overturned.

Saul made havock of the church. He took them, house by house. He sent them to prison, one after another.

But they that were scattered abroad stayed not either.

They walked the dust of the highways. They came unto the cities. They came unto the villages. They came unto the markets and the fields. They came unto the houses of the people of Judaea and Samaria.

They opened their mouths. They preached the word.

They preached it in the way. They preached it at the well. They preached it in the field. They preached it at the gate.

Therefore they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word.

The word was moving. The word abode not still. The word was gone forth out of Jerusalem and was running on before them.

The apostles tarried in Jerusalem. Saul tarried in Jerusalem. The believers were already gone.

The highways were full of them.
