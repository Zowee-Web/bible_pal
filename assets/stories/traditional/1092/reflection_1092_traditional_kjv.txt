She came to tend a body. She came bearing spices, expecting death, expecting silence, expecting the worst to hold true still.

And then one spake her name.

Not a title. Not a word of explanation. Her name — spoken as only one soul had ever spoken it. And in that single word, all she had thought she knew of what was possible broke open.

There is something in being known — truly known, by name, by one thou hadst thought lost — that no argument nor proof can match. It is not evidence. It is presence.

Sometimes the thing that changeth all is not some great event. It is a familiar voice, speaking thy name, in a place where thou didst not look to hear it.
