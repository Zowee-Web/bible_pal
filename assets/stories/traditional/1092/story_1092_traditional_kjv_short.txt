Early on the first day of the week, while it was yet dark, cometh Mary Magdalene unto the sepulchre. The garden was still. The air was cold. The first grey light of morning had not reached the treetops. She bore spices she had prepared, and her eyes were heavy with weeping.

When she came unto the tomb, she saw that the stone was taken away from the entrance.

She turned and ran. She found Simon Peter and the other disciple, whom Jesus loved, and said unto them, "They have taken away the Lord out of the sepulchre, and we know not where they have laid him."

Peter and the other disciple ran unto the tomb. The other disciple did outrun Peter and came first, and looking in, saw the linen clothes lying; but he went not in. Simon Peter came after, and went into the sepulchre. He saw the linen clothes, and the napkin that had been about his head, folded and set apart. Then went the other disciple in also, and he saw, and believed. As yet they understood not the scripture, that he must rise again from the dead. The disciples went away unto their own homes.

But Mary stood without at the sepulchre, weeping. She stooped down and looked within. Two angels in white were sitting where the body of Jesus had lain — one at the head, one at the feet.

"Woman, why weepest thou?" they said.

"Because they have taken away my Lord," she said, "and I know not where they have laid him."

She turned about. A man stood behind her in the garden. She knew him not. The light was dim. Her eyes were filled with tears.

"Woman, why weepest thou?" the man said. "Whom seekest thou?"

She, supposing him to be the gardener, saith unto him, "Sir, if thou have borne him hence, tell me where thou hast laid him, and I will take him away."

Jesus saith unto her, "Mary."

She turned. Something broke within her — recognition, wonder, joy past all understanding.

"Rabboni!" she said.

Jesus saith unto her, "Touch me not; for I am not yet ascended to my Father: but go to my brethren, and say unto them, I ascend unto my Father, and your Father; and to my God, and your God."

Mary Magdalene went unto the disciples. Her face was streaked with tears, but her eyes shone. "I have seen the Lord," she said. And she told them those things which he had spoken unto her.
