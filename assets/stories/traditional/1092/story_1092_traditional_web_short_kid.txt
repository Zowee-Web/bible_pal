Very early on Sunday morning, while it was still dark, a woman named Mary Magdalene went to the tomb where Jesus had been buried. She loved Jesus very much and had been crying for days.

When she got there, she saw that the big stone had been rolled away from the entrance. The tomb was open!

She ran to find Peter and another disciple. "Someone took Jesus!" she said. "He's gone and we don't know where!"

Peter and the other disciple ran to the tomb. They looked inside and saw the cloth that had been wrapped around Jesus' body. It was lying there, but Jesus was gone. They didn't understand what had happened, so they went home.

But Mary stayed. She stood outside the tomb, crying.

She looked inside one more time. Two angels in white were sitting where Jesus' body had been.

"Why are you crying?" they asked her.

"Because they took my Lord away," she said, "and I don't know where he is."

She turned around. A man was standing right behind her in the garden. She didn't recognize him.

"Why are you crying?" the man asked. "Who are you looking for?"

Mary thought he was the gardener. "Sir, if you moved him, please tell me where he is."

Then the man said one word.

"Mary."

And she knew. She KNEW that voice. It was Jesus!

"Teacher!" she cried.

Jesus told her, "Go tell the others that I am alive."

Mary ran to find the disciples. Her face was still wet with tears, but she was smiling now.

"I have seen the Lord!" she told them.
