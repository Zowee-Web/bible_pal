It was very early in the morning — so early it was still dark outside. A woman named Mary Magdalene was walking through a quiet garden toward a tomb. She loved Jesus so much, and she had been crying for days because he had died on a cross.

She had brought special spices to put on his body. That was how people showed love for someone who had died.

But when she got to the tomb, she saw something that made her heart drop. The big, heavy stone that covered the entrance had been rolled away. The tomb was OPEN.

Mary turned and ran as fast as she could. She found Peter and another disciple — one Jesus really loved — and told them, "Someone has taken Jesus out of the tomb! We don't know where he is!"

Peter and the other disciple ran to see for themselves. The other disciple got there first and peeked inside. He saw the cloths that had been wrapped around Jesus' body — but no Jesus. Peter ran right inside. He saw the same thing — the cloths were there, neatly folded, but the body was gone.

They didn't understand what had happened. So they went back home.

But Mary stayed behind. She stood outside the tomb crying. After a while, she looked inside one more time.

Two angels in white were sitting inside! One at the head and one at the feet of where Jesus had been.

"Why are you crying?" they asked her.

"Because someone took my Lord," she said, "and I don't know where he is."

Then she turned around. A man was standing right behind her in the garden. She couldn't see his face clearly because her eyes were full of tears. She thought he was the gardener.

"Why are you crying?" the man asked. "Who are you looking for?"

"Sir," she said, "if you moved him, just tell me where he is."

Then the man said one word.

"Mary."

Just her name. But she knew that voice. She had heard it a hundred times before. She KNEW who it was.

"TEACHER!" she cried.

It was Jesus. He was alive!

Jesus told her, "Go tell the others that I am going to be with my Father."

Mary ran through the streets of Jerusalem. The sun was coming up. Doors were opening. People were starting their day. And Mary had the most amazing news anyone had ever carried.

She found the disciples and said, "I have seen the Lord!"

And she told them everything.
