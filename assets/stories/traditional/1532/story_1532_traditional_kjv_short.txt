Then Joshua called for the priests and gave commandment unto them. Seven among them were to bear seven trumpets fashioned of rams' horns, and to pass before the ark of the LORD. Armed men should go before the priests, and a rear guard should come after the ark. And the trumpets were to sound without ceasing.

They gathered at the edge of the camp, and went forth toward the city.

Jericho was shut up, fast and sure. Her walls were of stone and of mud brick, broad enough that a man might walk upon them. None went out. None came in. The gates were barred.

The armed men passed first, and their sandals struck the hard ground in steady measure. After them came the seven priests, each man with a ram's horn unto his lips, and the sound thereof was low and unbroken, a raw droning that carried across the dry plain. After the priests came the ark of the LORD, borne upon poles across the shoulders of them that bare it. And after the ark walked the rear guard, weapons at the ready, their faces set forward.

They compassed the city once about. The trumpets ceased not. The people of Jericho looked down upon them from the walls. No man in the marching company spake a word. And when the circuit was finished, they returned unto the camp.

So did they on the second day. And on the third. And on the fourth, and the fifth, and the sixth. One circuit in a day. The trumpets sounding. The column moving in order. Then back to the camp.

And on the seventh day they rose at the dawning, while the sky was yet pale and the air yet cool. They formed the same array: armed men, then priests with trumpets, then the ark, then the rear guard. And they compassed the city. Once. Twice. Three times. Four. Five. Six times about. The trumpets blew and ceased not. The dust rose beneath their feet and hung in the morning air.

And on the seventh circuit the priests blew a long blast upon the trumpets.

Then brake the voice of Joshua through the sound. "Shout! for the LORD hath given you the city!"

And the people opened their mouths and shouted. The sound that came forth from them was a mighty sound, a roar from every throat together, rising above the blast of the trumpets.

The walls trembled. The broad face of stone and brick that was Jericho cracked, and buckled, and fell down straightway, collapsing flat upon the ground in a cloud of dust and broken rubble. And every man went straight before him, up over the fallen stone, into the city. And they took it.
