Yahweh's word came to Jonah the second time. It said, "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and preach to it the message that I give you."

So Jonah arose and went.

He entered the city a day's journey and cried out, "In forty days, Nineveh will be overthrown."

The people believed God. They proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest to the least. The king heard, arose from his throne, removed his robe, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat in ashes. He decreed that every person and every animal should fast, put on sackcloth, cry to God, and turn from evil. He said, "Who knows whether God will not turn and relent, and turn away from his fierce anger, so that we might not perish?"

God saw their works. He saw that they turned from their evil way. And God relented.

Have you ever changed direction — not because you understood everything, but because you knew it was time?
