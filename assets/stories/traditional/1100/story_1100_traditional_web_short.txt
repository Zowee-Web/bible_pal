Yahweh's word came to Jonah the second time, saying, "Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and preach to it the message that I give you."

So Jonah arose and went to Nineveh, according to Yahweh's word.

Now Nineveh was an exceedingly great city, three days' journey across.

Jonah began to enter into the city. He walked a day's journey in. He cried out, and he said, "In forty days, Nineveh will be overthrown!"

The people of Nineveh believed God. They proclaimed a fast. They put on sackcloth, from their greatest even to their least.

The news reached the king of Nineveh. When the king heard, he arose from his throne. He took off his royal robe. He covered himself with sackcloth. He sat in ashes.

He made a proclamation and published it through Nineveh by the decree of the king and his nobles. The decree said, "Let neither man nor animal, herd nor flock, taste anything. Let them not feed, nor drink water. But let them be covered with sackcloth, both man and animal, and let them cry mightily to God. Yes, let everyone turn from his evil way, and from the violence that is in his hands. Who knows whether God will not turn and relent, and turn away from his fierce anger, so that we might not perish?"

The people of Nineveh did as the decree commanded. They did not eat. They did not drink water. They covered themselves with sackcloth, both man and animal. They cried mightily to God. They turned from their evil way. They turned from the violence that was in their hands.

God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way. God relented of the disaster which he had said he would do to them, and he did not do it.
