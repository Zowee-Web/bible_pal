Remember Jonah? The guy who ran away from God and got swallowed by a giant fish?

Well, after the fish spit him out on dry land, God talked to Jonah AGAIN.

"Go to Nineveh," God said. Same city. Same job. Same message.

This time, Jonah went.

He walked for weeks until he finally reached Nineveh. It was HUGE — it took three whole days just to walk across it! Jonah went right into the middle of the city and started shouting:

"In forty days, this city will be destroyed!"

And something nobody expected happened. The people actually LISTENED!

They believed God's message. Everyone — from regular people to rich people to the king himself — stopped what they were doing. They put on rough clothes to show they were sorry. They stopped eating. They prayed and asked God to forgive them. They promised to stop doing bad things.

Even the KING got off his throne, took off his fancy robes, and sat in ashes. He made an announcement: "Everyone must pray to God and stop doing evil. Maybe God will change his mind and not destroy us."

And you know what?

God saw that they really, truly changed. They weren't just saying sorry — they actually STOPPED doing the bad things.

So God changed his mind. He didn't destroy the city.

The people of Nineveh were saved — because they listened, and because they changed.
