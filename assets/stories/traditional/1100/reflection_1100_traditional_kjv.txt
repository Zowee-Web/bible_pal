The mightiest city upon the earth heard eight words from a stranger and changed all things.

No miracles. No signs. No parley. Only a man standing in the street with a message he wished not to deliver, speaking to a people that had no cause to hearken — and they hearkened.

What moved Nineveh was not learning. It was honesty. The king's decree promised nothing. It said: "Who can tell if God will turn?" That is not certainty. That is hope held with open hands.

Sometimes the most needful thing a man or a city can do is stop. Stop the custom. Stop the excuses. Stop the violence. And say: we know not whether it shall avail, but we shall turn nonetheless.

Nineveh earned not forgiveness. They simply turned about. And it sufficed.
