The word of the Lord came unto Jonah the second time, saying, "Arise, go unto Nineveh, that great city, and preach unto it the preaching that I bid thee."

So Jonah arose and went.

He entered the city a day's journey and cried, "Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown."

The people believed God. They proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth, from the greatest even to the least. The king heard, arose from his throne, laid aside his robe, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat in ashes. He decreed that every man and every beast should fast, put on sackcloth, cry unto God, and turn from evil. He said, "Who can tell if God will turn and repent, and turn away from his fierce anger, that we perish not?"

God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way. And God repented of the evil.

Hast thou ever changed direction — not because thou understood everything, but because thou knew it was time?
