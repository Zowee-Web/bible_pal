In the year that King Uzziah died, Isaiah came into the temple of the Lord.

The air within was still. Stone pillars rose up on either side, tall and smooth, reaching upward into shadow. The smell of incense hung in the space between them, faint and sharp at once.

Then Isaiah saw the Lord, sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up. The train of his robe filled the temple, spreading across the floor in folds of heavy cloth, covering the stones from wall to wall.

Above the throne stood the seraphim. Each one had six wings. With twain each covered his face. With twain each covered his feet. With the remaining twain, each one did fly.

One cried unto another. The voice shook the air.

"Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts. The whole earth is full of his glory."

The posts of the door shook at the voice of him that cried. The great stone thresholds trembled in their fittings. Smoke filled the temple, thick and white, rolling between the pillars and rising toward the ceiling until the walls could scarce be seen.

Isaiah cried out. His voice brake against the smoke and stone.

"Woe is me! For I am undone! For I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips — for mine eyes have seen the King, the Lord of hosts."

One of the seraphim flew toward him. His wings beat the smoky air. In his hand he held a live coal, glowing bright, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar. The heat thereof pulsed against the dimness.

The seraph reached forth and laid the coal upon the mouth of Isaiah. The burning pressed against his lips.

"Lo," the seraph said. "This hath touched thy lips. Thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin is purged."

The coal was withdrawn. The smoke still filled the temple. The throne remained high and lifted up, and the train of the robe still covered the floor.

Then the voice of the Lord came through the smoke.

"Whom shall I send, and who will go for us?"

Isaiah stood in the temple, the taste of fire still upon his lips, the smoke curling about his feet. He opened his mouth and spake.

"Here am I. Send me."
