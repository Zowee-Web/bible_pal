The seventy disciples had returned from the towns of Galilee with great joy, telling Jesus that even the devils had been subject unto them in his name. Jesus had spoken of Satan falling as lightning from heaven, and of the authority given unto the disciples to tread upon serpents and scorpions. And in that moment, with the seventy standing about him in the place where they had gathered, something arose in Jesus himself, and he rejoiced.

In that hour Jesus rejoiced in spirit, and said, "I thank thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them unto babes: even so, Father; for so it seemed good in thy sight."

Turning unto the disciples, he said, "All things are delivered to me of my Father: and no man knoweth who the Son is, but the Father; and who the Father is, but the Son, and he to whom the Son will reveal him."

Turning unto the disciples, he said privately, "Blessed are the eyes which see the things that ye see:

For I tell you, that many prophets and kings have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them."

And the seventy stood about him. The dust of the ways they had walked yet clave unto their sandals. The wind moved across the place. And the day went on as the days of his ministry had been going on, and the joy of Jesus and the joy of his disciples carried into the evening. And so it was on that day, when the seventy came back and the Son rejoiced in spirit.
