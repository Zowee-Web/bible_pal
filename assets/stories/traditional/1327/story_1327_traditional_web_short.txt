The seventy disciples had returned from the towns of Galilee with great joy, telling Jesus that even the demons had been subject to them in his name. Jesus had spoken of Satan falling like lightning from heaven, and of the authority given to the disciples to tread on serpents and scorpions. And in that moment, with the seventy standing around him in the place where they had gathered, something rose up in Jesus himself, and he rejoiced.

In that same hour Jesus rejoiced in the Holy Spirit, and said, "I thank you, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that you have hidden these things from the wise and understanding, and revealed them to little children. Yes, Father, for so it was well-pleasing in your sight."

Turning to the disciples, he said, "All things have been delivered to me by my Father. No one knows who the Son is, except the Father, and who the Father is, except the Son, and he to whomever the Son desires to reveal him."

Turning to the disciples, he said privately, "Blessed are the eyes which see the things that you see,

for I tell you that many prophets and kings desired to see the things which you see, and didn't see them, and to hear the things which you hear, and didn't hear them."

And the seventy stood around him. The dust of the roads they had walked still clung to their sandals. The wind moved across the place. And the day went on as the days of his ministry had been going on, and the joy of Jesus and the joy of his disciples carried into the evening. And so it was on that day, when the seventy came back and the Son rejoiced in the Holy Spirit.
