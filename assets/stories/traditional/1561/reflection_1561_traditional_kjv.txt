Moses had been on the mountain forty days. The people grew weary of waiting and made a calf of gold. The Lord said unto Moses: let me alone, that I may consume them. And Moses said: if thou wilt not forgive their sin, blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book which thou hast written. What is it to stand between God and the people thou art set over, and to offer thyself in their place before they have seen what is coming?
