The sabbath was coming. The cross stood upon Golgotha and the body of Jesus had been there for some hours. The disciples had scattered. Peter had wept bitterly in the dark of the courtyard and was now hidden somewhere in the city. The women which had followed Jesus from Galilee stood afar off and beheld. And in the gathering of the council, one of the honourable members which had not consented unto the death of Jesus, a man named Joseph of Arimathaea, waited for the kingdom of God. The hour was late, and what he was about to do would mark him before all of Jerusalem. And he went in unto Pilate.

When the even was come, because it was the preparation, that is, the day before the sabbath,

Joseph of Arimathaea, an honourable counsellor, which also waited for the kingdom of God, came, and went in boldly unto Pilate, and craved the body of Jesus.

And Pilate marvelled if he were already dead: and calling unto him the centurion, he asked him whether he had been any while dead.

And when he knew it of the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph.

And he bought fine linen, and took him down, and wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a sepulchre which was hewn out of a rock, and rolled a stone unto the door of the sepulchre.

And Joseph went unto his house. The sepulchre in the rock was sealed by the stone he had rolled against it, and the body that had been left upon the cross now lay wrapped in the fine linen he had bought, and the sabbath was beginning in the city. The man which had not consented unto the council's verdict had taken the verdict upon himself in another way. And so it was in the days when Joseph of Arimathaea boldly craved of Pilate the body of Jesus.
