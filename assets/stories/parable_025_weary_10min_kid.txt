"Mama?" she called out, her voice small and tired. "I just feel so...heavy."

"Just like the rabbit," Mama said softly, "you are strong and capable. Even if you have a heavy backpack or a big mountain to climb, remember to take breaks, rest your mind, and trust that you can get through anything."

Mama, sensing her daughter's restful drift, softly left the room. The quiet whispers of the night seemed to carry soothing messages. Willow's worries, like the scattered puzzle pieces, seemed to fall into place.
