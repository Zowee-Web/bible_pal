Esther lived in a big palace with King Xerxes. She was queen, but she had a secret: she came from the people of Israel. Her cousin Mordecai cared for her very much. One day, Mordecai learned that something very hard was going to happen to Esther’s people. A man named Haman did not like them. He made a plan and told the king to write a new law. This law was not fair and would hurt Esther’s people.

Mordecai put on simple clothes and sat outside the king’s gate. He was very sad. He sent a message to Esther and told her everything. He asked her to go to the king and help. Esther was afraid. Everyone in the palace knew that you could not walk into the throne room unless the king called you. If you went in without being called, you might be sent away. But Mordecai told Esther that maybe she was queen for a special reason, to help her people “for such a time as this.”

Esther told Mordecai to gather all her people and not to eat or drink for three days. She and her helpers would do the same. Then she would go see the king, even if it was dangerous. After three days, Esther dressed in her best royal robes. The palace was quiet as she walked through the heavy curtains. The throne room was covered in gold, and King Xerxes was seated. Everyone watched Esther, and the room was still. The king saw Esther and was happy to see her. He smiled and held out his golden scepter. This meant Esther was safe and welcome.

Esther touched the tip of the scepter and walked closer. The king asked, “What do you want, Queen Esther?” Esther invited the king and Haman to a special feast. At the feast, the king asked again what she wanted. Esther invited them to another meal the next day. On the second day, the king asked one more time. Esther told him about Haman’s plan against her people. The king was surprised and stopped Haman’s plan right away.

Esther was brave and kind. Mordecai was safe, and the people of Israel were saved. The palace was filled with gladness and thankfulness.
