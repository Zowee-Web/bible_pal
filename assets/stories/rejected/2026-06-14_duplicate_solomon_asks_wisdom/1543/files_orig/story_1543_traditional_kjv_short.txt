The high place at Gibeon stood upon a hill above the town. And Solomon had gone up thither, and upon the altar he offered a thousand burnt offerings. The smoke thereof ascended all the day long, and into the evening it rose still.

That night Solomon lay down to sleep at Gibeon. And the LORD appeared unto him in a dream.

And God spake unto him, saying, "Ask of me what thou wilt, and I shall give it thee."

Then Solomon answered. He spake as a man speaketh who knoweth the burden that hath been set upon him.

"Thou hast dealt with thy servant David my father in great mercy," he said, "for he walked before thee in faithfulness and in righteousness and in sincerity of heart. And thou hast preserved unto him this surpassing kindness, that thou hast set a son upon his throne after him, even as it is this day."

He was silent a moment.

"And now, O LORD my God, thou hast made thy servant king in the place of David my father. Yet I am but a youth, and I know not the way before me — how to go forth, or how to return. Thy servant standeth in the midst of a people whom thou thyself hast chosen, a people so vast that no man can reckon the number thereof."

Then did Solomon set forth his petition.

"Grant unto thy servant a heart of understanding, that I may judge this people aright and discern the good from the evil. For who among men is sufficient to govern so great a nation as thine?"

And the thing that Solomon had asked was pleasing in the sight of the Lord.

And God said unto him, "Because thou hast asked this thing — because thou hast not sought for thyself length of days, nor sought riches for thine own hand, nor sought the destruction of thine enemies, but hast sought rather the discernment to hear and to judge rightly —" He paused, and spake again. "Behold, I have granted it according to thy words. I have given thee a heart both wise and discerning, such that none before thee hath possessed its like, neither shall any arise after thee to equal it."

And God spake yet further. "Moreover I have bestowed upon thee that which thou didst not ask — both wealth and honour in such measure that no king in all thy days shall be found to compare with thee. And if thou wilt walk in my ways, keeping my statutes and my ordinances even as David thy father walked, then will I make thy days long upon the earth."

And Solomon awoke. And behold, it had been a dream.

Then he arose and came unto Jerusalem, and stood before the ark of the covenant of the LORD. And he offered burnt offerings and peace offerings there. Afterward he prepared a feast for all his servants. The tables were spread, and the meat was set in order, and his servants were gathered together before him in the hall.
