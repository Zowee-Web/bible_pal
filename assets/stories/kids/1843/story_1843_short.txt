The Lord's Prayer

When Jesus' friends wanted to learn how to talk to God, Jesus taught them a prayer. People all over the world have prayed it ever since. You can pray it too, anytime — even right now, as you rest.

Jesus said, pray like this:

Our Father in heaven, may your name be kept holy.
Let your Kingdom come. Let your will be done on earth as it is in heaven.
Give us today our daily bread.
Forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.
Bring us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.
For yours is the Kingdom, the power, and the glory forever. Amen.

God always hears you when you pray. He is your Father in heaven, and he loves you very much. You can talk to him about anything at all. Rest now, little one. You are not alone.
