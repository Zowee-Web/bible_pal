Jesus Cried Too

In a little town called Bethany, there lived three people Jesus loved very much: two sisters, Mary and Martha, and their brother, Lazarus. Whenever Jesus came to their town, he would stop at their house, and they would share a meal, and talk and laugh late into the evening. They were some of his dearest friends in all the world.

But one day, Lazarus became sick. Not a little sick — very, very sick. Mary and Martha were frightened. So they sent a message to Jesus, far away. "Lord," it said, "the one you love is sick. Please come."

But Jesus did not come right away. The sisters watched the road, and they waited, and they wondered why their dear friend was taking so long. But the road was long, and it took some days for Jesus to come. And by the time he drew near to Bethany, something very sad had happened. Lazarus had died. The whole house was full of crying. The sisters' hearts were broken.

When Martha heard that Jesus was finally coming, she ran down the road to meet him. "Lord," she said, with tears on her face, "if only you had been here, my brother would not have died." And Jesus spoke to her gently, and told her to keep hold of hope.

Then Mary came too. She fell down at Jesus' feet, weeping. "Lord," she cried, "if only you had been here." And all the friends who had come with her were weeping along with her. Everyone was so very sad.

And when Jesus saw Mary crying, and saw all of them crying, something happened that the people never forgot.

Jesus cried.

Right there, on the road, the Son of God wept. Big tears rolled down his cheeks. He was not too strong to be sad. He was not too important to cry. He loved Lazarus, and he loved Mary and Martha, and their sadness became his sadness too.

The people watching saw his tears, and they whispered to one another, "See how much he loved him."

Then Jesus walked to the quiet garden place where they had laid Lazarus, with a great stone rolled across the front. "Roll the stone away," Jesus said. Then he lifted his eyes up to heaven, and he prayed to his Father. And then, in a loud and wonderful voice, Jesus called out:

"Lazarus! Come out!"

For one moment, everything was still. And then — there was movement in the doorway. And out walked Lazarus. Alive! Warm and breathing, well again! Mary and Martha could hardly believe their eyes. They ran to their brother and threw their arms around him, and now their tears were happy ones.

And everyone who stood watching that day was filled with wonder. They had seen Jesus weep real tears, and they had seen his great love call their friend right back home.

So remember this, little one. When you are sad, and your eyes fill up with tears, you are not alone. Jesus knows just what sad feels like. He cried too. And whether your tears are sad ones or happy ones, he stays close beside you, always.
