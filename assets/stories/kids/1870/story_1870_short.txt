Jesus Cried Too

Jesus had some very dear friends. Their names were Mary, and Martha, and their brother Lazarus. Jesus loved them all, and he loved to visit their home.

But one day, Lazarus grew very, very sick. His sisters waited and waited for Jesus to come. But the road was long, and by the time Jesus arrived, something sad had happened. Lazarus had died.

Mary ran out to meet Jesus on the road. Her eyes were full of tears. "Oh, if only you had been here," she cried. And all around her, her friends were crying too.

And do you know what Jesus did?

Jesus cried.

The strong, kind Son of God stood right there in the road, and his own eyes filled with tears, and he wept along with them. He was not too big to be sad. He was not too important to cry. Jesus loved his friends so much that he cried right along with them.

The people saw it, and they whispered, "Look how much he loved Lazarus."

Then Jesus walked to the quiet place where Lazarus had been laid, and he asked them to roll the stone away. He looked up to heaven, and he prayed. And then, in a great, strong voice, Jesus called out:

"Lazarus! Come out!"

And — oh! — out walked Lazarus, alive and well! Mary and Martha threw their arms around him. Their sad tears turned into happy ones.

So whenever you are sad, little one, and your eyes fill up with tears, remember this: Jesus understands. He cried too. And he stays close to you, always.
