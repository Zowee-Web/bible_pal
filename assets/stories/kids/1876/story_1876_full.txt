Jesus Shines Like the Sun

One day, Jesus took three of his closest friends with him — Peter, and James, and John. Together they climbed up a tall mountain, high above the towns and the fields, to pray.

It was very quiet up there, with the wide sky all around them. The friends were tired from the long climb, and their eyes felt heavy. But then, all at once, something happened that woke them right up — something so amazing that they would remember it for the rest of their lives.

Jesus began to shine.

It started in his face, which grew brighter and brighter, until it shone like the sun itself. Then his clothes turned a dazzling white — whiter than fresh snow, whiter than anything in all the world, shining as though they were made of light. Peter and James and John stared and stared, their eyes wide and their mouths open. They had never seen anything so beautiful in all their lives. The whole mountaintop seemed to glow with the light pouring out of him. It was the most wonderful sight in all the world.

And that was not all. For suddenly, two more people were there, standing beside Jesus and talking with him. They were Moses and Elijah — two of God's great friends from long, long ago. And here they were, on the mountaintop, with shining Jesus.

Peter was so amazed, and so excited, that he could hardly think straight. So he blurted out the very first thing that popped into his head. "Lord!" he said. "This is wonderful! It is so good to be here! Let us build three little tents up here on the mountain — one for you, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah — so that we can all stay!"

But while Peter was still talking, something gentle happened. A bright, shining cloud came softly down from above, and it covered them all over. And from inside the cloud came a voice. It was the voice of God himself.

It was not a frightening voice. It was warm, and gentle, and full of love. And this is what it said:

"This is my Son, the one I love. Listen to him."

The three friends were filled with wonder. And then, as softly as it had come, the cloud lifted away. Moses and Elijah were gone. And there stood Jesus, all alone — looking just as he always did, their own dear friend and teacher.

The friends were lying with their faces to the ground, full of awe. But Jesus came over to them, and reached out, and gently touched them. "Get up," he said kindly. "Do not be afraid." So they stood up, and together they all walked back down the mountain. All the way down, the three friends were quiet, their hearts full to the very brim. And whenever they closed their eyes, they could still see that bright and shining light.

They did not tell anyone about it, not for a long while. But not one of them ever, ever forgot the day they climbed a mountain — and saw Jesus shine like the sun.
