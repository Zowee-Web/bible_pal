Jesus Shines Like the Sun

One day, Jesus took three of his closest friends — Peter, James, and John — up a tall mountain, to pray.

It was quiet up there, high above everything. But then, all at once, something amazing happened.

Jesus began to shine.

His face shone bright as the sun. His clothes turned white — whiter than snow, whiter than anything you have ever seen, shining like light itself. The three friends stared with their mouths wide open. They had never, ever seen anything so wonderful.

And then — there were two more people, standing with Jesus and talking with him! They were Moses and Elijah, two of God's great friends from long, long ago.

Peter was so amazed that he hardly knew what to say. So he blurted out the first thing that popped into his head. "Lord, this is wonderful! Let's build three little tents up here — one for each of you — so we can all stay!"

But while Peter was still talking, a bright cloud came softly down and covered them. And out of the cloud came a voice — the voice of God himself. It was not a scary voice. It was warm, and kind, and full of love. And it said:

"This is my Son, whom I love. Listen to him."

When the cloud lifted, Moses and Elijah were gone. And there stood Jesus, just as he always was — their own dear friend.

Then Jesus came over to them, and gently touched them, and said, "Do not be afraid." And together they walked back down the mountain.

But the three friends never forgot the day they saw Jesus shine.
