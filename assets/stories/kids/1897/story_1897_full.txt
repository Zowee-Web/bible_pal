The Kindest Woman in Town

In a town by the sea called Joppa, there lived a woman named Tabitha. And Tabitha was just about the kindest person you could ever hope to meet. While other people were busy with their own lives, Tabitha spent her days quietly doing good for others — especially for those who were poor, or sad, or all alone.

Now, Tabitha had a special gift: she was wonderful at sewing. Her hands were always busy, stitching away. She made warm coats, and soft dresses, and all sorts of clothes — and then she gave them away, free, to people who had none. There were many widows in the town, women whose husbands had died, who had very little money. And whenever one of them was cold, or in need, there was Tabitha, with a kind smile and a warm new coat she had made just for them. Everyone in Joppa loved her dearly. She never asked for any thanks, and she never wanted a fuss. She simply loved to help, quietly and gladly, day after day after day.

But one day, Tabitha became sick. And then, very gently and quietly, she died. Oh, how the whole town grieved! The widows she had cared for gathered together, and they wept and wept. They held up the coats and dresses she had sewn for them. "Look at this one — Tabitha made it for me," they said through their tears. "She was so good to us. Whatever shall we do without her?"

Now, it so happened that one of Jesus' helpers, a man named Peter, was staying in a town not far away. So Tabitha's friends quickly sent two men to find him. "Please," they begged when they reached him, "please come, and come quickly!" And Peter did not waste a moment. He got up and went with them, all the way back to Joppa.

When Peter arrived, they led him up the stairs to the room where Tabitha lay, and it was full of weeping widows, all showing him the lovely things she had made. Peter's heart was deeply moved. Gently, he asked everyone to step outside for a little while. Then, alone in the quiet room, Peter knelt down beside Tabitha, and he prayed to God with all his heart.

And then he turned to her, and he said, softly, "Tabitha — get up."

And Tabitha opened her eyes. She saw Peter there, and she sat right up, alive and well, as though she had only been sleeping! Peter reached out and took her hand, and helped her gently to her feet. Then he opened the door and called everyone back inside — and there stood Tabitha, alive! The whole room burst into tears of joy, hugging her, and laughing, and thanking God. And soon the wonderful news spread all through the town.

So a kind and loving life is a beautiful thing indeed, little one. Tabitha spent hers caring for others — and she was loved by all her town, and held close by the God who made her.
