The Kindest Woman in Town

In a town by the sea, there lived a woman named Tabitha. And Tabitha was just about the kindest person you could ever meet. She spent her days doing good for others, especially for those who were poor, or sad, or all alone.

Tabitha was very good at sewing. So she made coats, and dresses, and warm clothes, and she gave them away to people who had none. Whenever someone was cold, or in need, Tabitha was there, with a gentle smile and something she had made just for them. Everyone in town loved her.

But one day, Tabitha became sick. And then, very gently, she died. Oh, how sad the whole town was! The widows she had helped stood about and wept, holding up the clothes she had sewn for them. "Look what she made me," they said through their tears. "She was so good to us."

Now, the friends of Tabitha had heard that one of Jesus' helpers, a man named Peter, was nearby. So they sent for him, quickly. And Peter came.

When Peter arrived, he gently sent everyone out of the room. Then he knelt down, and he prayed. And then he turned to her and said, "Tabitha, get up."

And Tabitha opened her eyes! She looked at Peter, and she sat right up, alive and well again. Peter took her hand and helped her to her feet, and then he called everyone back in. How they cried for joy to see their dear, kind friend alive!

So a kind and loving life is a beautiful thing, little one. Tabitha spent hers caring for others — and she was loved by all her town, and held close by the God who made her.
