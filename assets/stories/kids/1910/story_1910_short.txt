The Night Jacob Held On

Night had fallen, and Jacob was all alone.

He had sent everyone he loved on ahead, across the river, and now he sat by himself in the dark. In the morning he would meet his brother again, after many long years apart — and Jacob was afraid. He did not know what the new day would bring.

Then, in the darkness, someone was there with him. And all through the night, Jacob held on to him, and would not let go.

The night was long. Jacob grew tired, but still he held on tight. And when at last the sky began to turn soft and gray with the very first light of morning, the One in the dark said to him, "Let me go now, for the sun is coming up."

But Jacob only held on tighter. "I will not let you go," he said, "unless you bless me." Then Jacob knew. He was holding on to God.

And God did bless him. "What is your name?" God asked him. "Jacob," he answered. "From now on," God said, "your name will be Israel — for you have held on to God, and you would not let go."

When the morning came, Jacob walked on to meet his brother, carrying his brand-new name. He had been alone, and afraid, in the dark. But he had held on to God all through the long night — and God had stayed right there with him, until the morning light came, and had blessed him.

God is like that. Even on the darkest, most frightening night, when you feel all alone, God is closer than you know. And you can hold on to him, and not let go, all the way until morning.
