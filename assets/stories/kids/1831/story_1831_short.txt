Zacchaeus

In the town of Jericho, there lived a little man named Zacchaeus. Now, Zacchaeus was not very tall — in fact, he was quite short. And I am sorry to say, he had not been very kind, either. His job was collecting money, and he often took more than he should have, and kept it for himself. So most people did not like him, and Zacchaeus was often lonely.

One day, wonderful news swept through Jericho: Jesus was coming! Zacchaeus wanted to see him so badly. But a great crowd had gathered along the road, and Zacchaeus was far too short to see over all the tall people. He jumped, and he stood on his tiptoes, but it was no use at all.

Then Zacchaeus had an idea. He ran on ahead, and he climbed up — up, up, up — into the branches of a big sycamore tree, to get a better look.

And as Jesus came down the road, he stopped — right underneath that very tree. He looked up into the branches, straight at Zacchaeus, and he smiled.

"Zacchaeus!" Jesus called. "Hurry and come down. For today, I am coming to your house!"

Zacchaeus could hardly believe his ears. Jesus wanted to come to his house? But nobody ever wanted to be with Zacchaeus! He scrambled down that tree as fast as he could, his heart bursting with joy.

And being near Jesus changed everything inside him. "I will give back all the money I ever took," said Zacchaeus, "and more besides!"

For Jesus had come looking for the one person everybody else had walked right past. And Zacchaeus was happy, all the way down to his toes.
