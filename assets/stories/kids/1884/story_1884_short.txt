The Tall, Tall Tower

Long, long ago, everyone in the whole world spoke the very same language. Every single person could understand every other person, because they all used exactly the same words.

In those days, the people decided to build something. Not just a house, or a wall — but a tower. A great, tall tower, made of bricks, that would go up, and up, and up — all the way into the clouds! They wanted it to be the tallest thing anyone had ever seen.

So they got to work. They made bricks, and they stacked them higher and higher. The tower climbed up toward the sky.

But as they built, the people had begun to forget something. They forgot that they needed God. And they forgot that they needed each other, too. They started to think that they could do anything they wanted, all on their own.

So God did a surprising thing. All at once, he gave the people different languages!

Suddenly, one person would say something — but his friend could not understand a single word! People were talking and talking, but nobody could understand anybody else. What a muddle it was! No one could work together anymore, and so they stopped building the tall, tall tower.

Then, little by little, the people who spoke the same words found one another. They gathered into families and groups, and they went off in every direction, all across the whole wide world, to fill it with new homes and towns.

And God still loved them — every family, in every land, speaking every language. He loved them then, and he loves them still.

And that, little one, is how the whole world came to be filled with so many different languages — all the way back from one tall, tall tower.
