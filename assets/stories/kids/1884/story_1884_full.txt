The Tall, Tall Tower

Long, long ago, when the world was still young, something was true that is hard to imagine now: everyone, everywhere, spoke exactly the same language. Every single person used the very same words. So everyone could understand everyone else, perfectly, all the time.

In those days, a great many people came and settled together in one place, on a wide flat plain. And there, they had an idea. "Let us build a city," they said. "And in the middle of it, let us build a tower — a tower so tall that it reaches all the way up into the sky!"

So they set to work. They learned how to make strong bricks out of clay, and to bake them hard. They stacked the bricks up, one on top of another, with sticky mud to hold them all together. And slowly, the tower began to rise. Up, and up, and up it went, higher and higher, reaching toward the clouds. The people were very proud of it.

But as they built their great tower, something had quietly gone wrong inside their hearts. The people had begun to forget. They forgot that they needed God, who had made them and who loved them. And they forgot that they needed each other, too. They started to believe that they were so clever and so strong that they could do absolutely anything, all by themselves.

So God did a most surprising thing — a thing no one expected at all.

All at once, in a single moment, he gave the people different languages.

Imagine it! One worker turned to another and asked for more bricks — but the words came out all different, and his friend just stared, not understanding a single word! All over the building site, people were talking and talking, and waving their arms, trying so hard to be understood. But nobody could understand anybody else anymore. What a muddle! What a wonderful, mixed-up jumble of voices!

And of course, when no one could understand each other, no one could work together. So the people put down their tools, and they stopped building the tall, tall tower. It was never finished.

But here is the lovely part. Little by little, the people who shared the same new words began to find one another. They came together into families, and into groups, each speaking their own language. And then they set off — this way and that, over the hills and across the rivers — traveling out in every direction to fill the whole wide world. They built new homes, and new towns, in faraway lands all over the earth.

And God loved them, every one. He loved each family, in each land, speaking each different language. He loved them all the way back then — and he loves them, every one, still today.

And that, little one, is the story of how our whole big world came to be filled with so many different peoples, and so many different languages — all the way back from one tall, tall tower that reached for the clouds.
