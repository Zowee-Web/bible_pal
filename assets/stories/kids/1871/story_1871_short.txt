Thomas Wanted to See

After Jesus rose again, alive, he came to visit his friends. But one friend, named Thomas, was not there that day. He missed the whole thing.

When he came back, his friends were full of joy. "We have seen Jesus!" they told him. "He is alive!" But Thomas shook his head. It seemed far too wonderful to believe. "I want to believe you," Thomas said. "I really do. But I need to see him for myself. I need to see his hands."

A whole week went by. And then, one day, Jesus came again. And this time, Thomas was there.

Now, Jesus could have been cross with Thomas for doubting. He could have said, "Thomas, why didn't you just believe?" But that is not what Jesus did. Not at all.

Jesus turned to Thomas with kind eyes. "Come closer, Thomas," he said gently. "Here. Look at my hands. Reach out and touch. It is really me."

Thomas looked. And it was true! It was really Jesus, alive, standing right in front of him. Thomas's heart filled right up, and all his doubts melted away. "My Lord!" he said. "It really is you!"

Jesus was not angry that Thomas had questions. He was glad to help him see.

So if you ever have questions, little one, or if something feels too big to understand, you do not have to be afraid. You can bring your questions to God, just like Thomas did. God is never angry when you wonder. He only says, ever so gently, "Come closer."
