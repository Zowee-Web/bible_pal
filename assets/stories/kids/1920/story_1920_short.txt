The Day the King Danced

Some days you feel so happy and so glad that you simply cannot hold still. Your feet want to jump, and your arms want to wave, and a laugh comes bubbling up all on its own. That is just how King David felt on one very special day — because the Ark of God was coming home! The Ark was a special golden chest that helped God's people remember that God was always with them, and it had been away from the city for a long, long time. But now, at last, it was coming back.

King David's whole heart was glad, all the way up to the brim. So he gathered the people — all of them — to walk the Ark up the road together, and bring it home with the biggest celebration anyone had ever seen.

And oh, what a sound they made! Trumpets rang out, bright and golden. Horns blew. Harps sang, and cymbals crashed — clash, clash, clash! The people sang at the top of their voices, and they shouted for joy until the whole road seemed to ring with happiness.

But it was the king himself who was the gladdest of all.

David was so full of joy that he could not walk like an ordinary man. He could not hold it inside. So right there in the road, in front of everyone, the king began to dance. He leaped into the air. He spun and twirled. He danced with all his might, with his whole heart, down the road before God — too happy to stop, too thankful to stand still.

A king, dancing in the dust like a child! And David did not mind one bit. He only wanted God to know how glad he was.

At last they carried the Ark to the special place made ready for it, and they set it safely down. And there it rested, home at last, right in the middle of the city.

Then King David turned to all the people who had come. And because he was so happy, he wanted everyone to be happy too. So he gave a gift to every single person there — to every man, to every woman, and to every child. He gave them good bread and sweet things to eat, so that no one went home without a present from the king.

And all the people went home glad, carrying their gifts, remembering the day the Ark came home — the day the king danced in the road for joy.

Because some days your heart is so full and so thankful that you simply cannot keep it in. You have to sing. You have to shout. You have to dance. And God loves it — God smiles to see his children that happy — when they celebrate him with their whole, glad, dancing hearts.
