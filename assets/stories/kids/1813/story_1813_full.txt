Crossing the Red Sea

God's people had walked a long, long way out of Egypt, where they had been slaves for many years. Now they were free, and they walked together toward a new home — all the way to the shore of a great, wide sea.

But as they rested by the water, someone looked back the way they had come. And there, far off on the road, was a cloud of dust, rising higher and higher. It was the king of Egypt and his whole army, with horses and chariots, chasing after them to bring them back!

The people were terrified. The deep sea was in front of them, and the great army was rushing up behind them. There was nowhere to run. "What will we do?" they cried out. "We are trapped!"

But Moses, their leader, was not afraid. He lifted his voice so they could all hear. "Do not be afraid," he told them. "Stand still, and watch. You will see God take care of us today."

Then God spoke to Moses, and told him what to do. "Lift up your staff," God said, "and stretch out your hand over the sea."

So Moses lifted his shepherd's staff high over the water. And all through that long night, God sent a strong, strong wind that blew and blew across the sea. And then the most amazing thing happened. The water itself split apart, right down the middle! It piled up high on the one side, and high on the other, like two great shining walls of water. And there, in the middle, lay a wide path of smooth, dry ground.

"Come!" Moses called. And God's people walked forward, out onto the path, right through the very middle of the sea. The walls of water stood tall on their left hand and on their right, but not a single drop fell on them. Step by step — mothers and fathers and children and all their animals — they crossed all the way to the far shore, safe and dry.

And when the very last of them had reached the other side, Moses stretched out his hand over the sea one more time. With a great rush, the walls of water came tumbling back together, and the sea filled up again — so the army could not follow them anymore.

God's people stood on the far shore, safe and sound, and looked back at the water in wonder. God had made a road for them, straight through the middle of the sea. And right there on the shore, they began to sing, and to dance, and to thank God with all their hearts — because he had brought them safely through.
