Crossing the Red Sea

God's people had walked a long, long way out of Egypt, all the way to the shore of a great wide sea. But when they looked back, they saw a cloud of dust rising on the road behind them. The king's army was coming after them!

The people were frightened. The sea was in front of them, and the army was behind them. "What will we do?" they cried.

But Moses, their leader, was not afraid. "Do not be afraid," he told them. "Stand still, and watch. God will take care of us today."

Then God told Moses what to do. "Lift up your staff," God said, "and stretch out your hand over the sea."

So Moses raised his staff high over the water. And all that night, God sent a strong, strong wind that blew across the sea. The water split apart — right down the middle! It piled up high on one side and high on the other, like two great walls of water. And there, in between, was a path of smooth, dry ground.

"Come!" said Moses. And God's people walked out onto the path, right through the middle of the sea, with walls of water standing tall on their left and on their right. Step by step, they crossed all the way to the other side, safe and dry.

When the last of them reached the far shore, Moses stretched out his hand once more. And the great walls of water came rushing back together, filling the sea again — so the army could not follow them anymore.

God's people were safe. They looked back at the sea in wonder. God had made a road right through the water to bring them home. And they sang and they danced on the far shore, so thankful, because God had kept them safe.
