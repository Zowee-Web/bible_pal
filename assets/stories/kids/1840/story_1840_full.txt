A New Heaven and a New Earth

Once, long ago, on a quiet island far out in the sea, there lived a man named John, who loved God with all his heart. And one day God gave John a wonderful gift — a dream, a glimpse of something beautiful that God has promised is coming one day.

In his dream, John saw something brand new.

He saw a new heaven and a new earth, shining and lovely, as fresh and clean as the very first morning of the world. And he saw a city coming down out of heaven, sparkling with light, as beautiful as a bride dressed up for her wedding day.

Then John heard a great and gentle voice, ringing out clear like a bell across the whole sky:

"Look! God's home is now with his people. He will live right here, close beside them. They will be his very own children, and he will always be their God."

And the voice spoke a promise so tender that John never, ever forgot it. God himself would come close to each and every person. And with his own hand, he would reach out and wipe away every tear from their eyes.

There would be no more crying. No more being hurt or afraid. No more sad goodbyes. All the old, hard, sorrowful things would be gone forever — washed away, like a long storm that has finally passed, leaving the whole sky clean and blue and bright.

"Look!" said God, and his voice was full of joy. "I am making everything new!"

And in that wonderful new place, John saw that there would be no darkness at all. No scary nights. There would be no need for lamps, or candles, or even the sun — because God's own light would shine over everyone, warm and golden and soft, forever and ever.

It is a place God is making ready right now, a home full of light and laughter, where God's people will live together with him. There will be a sparkling river, clear as crystal, running right down the middle of the city. There will be trees full of good fruit, growing all year long. And there will be room to run and play and wonder, and no one will ever be tired or sad or afraid, not one single time, ever again.

That is God's promise to his children. And the most wonderful part of all is this: God always, always keeps his promises.
