A New Heaven and a New Earth

Long ago, a man named John was given a wonderful dream — a little peek at something God has promised is coming one day.

In his dream, John saw something brand new. A new heaven, and a new earth, shining and beautiful, fresh as the very first morning. And coming down from heaven he saw a city of light, sparkling like a bride dressed for her wedding day.

Then John heard a great, kind voice, ringing out like a bell:

"Look! God's home is now with his people. He will live right here, close beside them. They will be his children, and he will be their God."

And the voice promised something so gentle that John never forgot it. God himself would come close to every person. And with his own hand, he would wipe away every tear from their eyes.

There would be no more crying. No more being hurt. No more sad goodbyes. All the old, hard things would be gone, like a storm that has finally passed and left the sky washed clean.

"Look," said God. "I am making everything new!"

And in that bright new place, there would be no darkness at all — no scary night, no need for lamps or even the sun. For God's own light would shine warm over everyone, soft and golden, forever and ever.

It is a place God is making ready, full of light and joy, where God's people will be together with him. And nothing sad will ever happen there again.

That is God's promise. And God always, always keeps his promises.
