Water into Wine

One happy day, Jesus and his mother, Mary, and his friends were all invited to a wedding. There was music, and dancing, and laughing, and a great feast — for a wedding was the most joyful party of all.

But in the middle of all the fun, a worry began. Quietly, behind the scenes, the family who was hosting the party had run out of something to drink. There was no more left for all the thirsty guests! How embarrassed they would be, and how sad, with the party only halfway through.

Mary knew that her son, Jesus, could help. So she found the servants and told them simply, "Do whatever he tells you."

Nearby stood six huge stone jars, tall and empty. Jesus looked at the servants and said, "Fill these jars up with water — all the way to the top."

It seemed a strange thing to ask. But the servants did just as Jesus said. They carried water and poured it in, splash after splash, until all six big jars were filled right to the brim.

"Now," said Jesus, "pour some out, and take it to the man in charge of the feast."

And here is the wonderful part. When they poured it out, it was not water anymore. Jesus had turned it into the very best, most delicious drink anyone had ever tasted! The party was saved, and the celebration went on, happier than ever.

It was the very first miracle Jesus ever did. And his friends, who saw it all, began to understand just how special he truly was.
