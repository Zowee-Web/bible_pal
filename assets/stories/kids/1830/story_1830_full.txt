Water into Wine

One happy day, in a town called Cana, there was a wedding. And Jesus, and his mother, Mary, and his friends were all invited to come.

Oh, what a celebration it was! In those days, a wedding was the most joyful party of all. There was music playing, and people dancing, and lots of laughing, and a great big feast spread out on the tables. Everyone was having a wonderful time.

But in the middle of all the fun, a quiet worry began.

Behind the scenes, where the guests could not see, the family who was giving the party had run into trouble. They had run out of something for everyone to drink — and there were still so many thirsty guests, and the party was only halfway through! It was the kind of thing that would make the family feel very embarrassed and sad, on what was meant to be the happiest day of their lives.

Now, Mary noticed what was happening. And Mary knew that her son, Jesus, could help, in a way that no one else could. So she went quietly to the servants, and she told them, "Whatever he tells you to do, just do it."

Standing nearby were six enormous stone jars. They were tall, and heavy, and completely empty.

Jesus looked at the servants, and he gave them an instruction. "Fill these jars with water," he said. "Fill them all the way up to the very top."

It seemed a strange thing to ask. What good was plain water? But the servants remembered what Mary had told them, and they did exactly as Jesus said. Back and forth they went, carrying water, pouring it in — splash, splash, splash — until all six great jars were filled right up to the brim.

"Now," said Jesus, "dip some out, and carry it to the man in charge of the feast."

So the servants dipped a cupful from the jar, and carried it to the man in charge. And here is the most wonderful part of all.

When the man tasted it, it was not water anymore. Somehow, quietly, without any fuss at all, Jesus had turned all that plain water into the very best and most delicious drink that anyone at the whole wedding had ever tasted! The man was amazed. "This is the finest of all!" he said. "You have saved the very best for last!"

And so the party was saved, and the celebration carried on, more joyful than ever.

This was the very first miracle that Jesus ever did. And his friends, who had watched the whole thing happen, began to understand something wonderful: that Jesus was no ordinary man, and that something very special had come into the world with him.
