The Wise Men and the Star

One clear night, far away in the East, some wise men were watching the sky, the way they did every night. They knew the stars by heart. But on this night, among all the familiar stars, they saw a brand-new one — bigger and brighter than all the rest.

They knew at once that this was no ordinary star. "A new king has been born," they said to one another. "Let us follow the star and go and find him."

So they packed their camels and set out on a long, long journey. Night after night, the bright star went on ahead of them, leading the way over hills and across wide deserts and through faraway lands. They did not know how long the road would be, but they trusted the star, and they kept on going.

They traveled for many, many miles. And then, one night, the star stopped — it shone and shimmered right over a little house in a town called Bethlehem.

The wise men's hearts filled with wonder. They stepped softly inside, and there he was — the child they had crossed the whole world to find: little Jesus, with his mother, Mary.

They knelt down low and were still. Then they opened the treasures they had carried all the way from home, and gave him their finest gifts — shining gold, and sweet-smelling frankincense, and precious myrrh.

They had set out following a star, not quite knowing where it would lead them. And it had led them here — to Jesus, just as God had planned all along.
