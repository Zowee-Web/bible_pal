The Two Tiny Coins

One day, Jesus sat down near the temple, in a busy place where people came to give their gifts to God. There was a special box for the gifts, made of metal, and Jesus watched as people walked by and dropped their coins inside.

Many rich people came. They had lots and lots of money, and they dropped in big, heavy handfuls of coins.

Clink! Clink! Clink!

The big coins made a loud, important sound as they fell into the box.

Then, after them, a poor woman came along. She was a widow, which means her husband had died, and she had very little money at all. In her hand she held just two tiny coins — the smallest coins there were. And they were all the money she had in the whole world.

She dropped them both into the box.

Ting. Ting.

Such a small, quiet sound. Hardly anyone noticed at all.

But Jesus noticed. And Jesus smiled.

He turned to his friends. "Do you see that woman?" he said. "She has given more than anyone else today."

His friends were surprised. More? But her coins were so tiny!

"The rich people," Jesus said, "gave a little bit out of all the riches they had. But this woman gave everything she had. And that makes her gift the biggest one of all."

For God does not measure a gift by how big it is. God looks at the love inside it. And a small gift, given with a whole heart, is precious to him.

So you do not have to have a lot to give, little one. Whatever you have, given with love, is precious to him.
