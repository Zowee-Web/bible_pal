Jesus and the Children

It had been a long and busy day. Jesus had been teaching crowds of people, and his friends were tired.

Then some mothers and fathers came up the road, bringing their children to see Jesus. Little ones toddled along holding their hands. Babies rode on their shoulders. They wanted Jesus to hold their children and bless them.

But Jesus's friends, the disciples, tried to send them away. "Not now," they said. "Jesus is busy. He has no time for little children today."

When Jesus saw this, he stopped them at once. "No," he said. "Let the children come to me. Don't ever turn them away." And he opened his arms wide.

The children ran to him. Some climbed right up into his lap. Little hands reached for him, and little faces looked up at him. Jesus laughed with them. He listened to their stories. One little girl showed him a smooth stone she had found, and he turned it over in his hand and smiled. He noticed the shy ones peeking out from behind their mothers, and he smiled at them until they came close too.

One by one, he held them. He placed his hands on their heads and blessed them. "God's kingdom belongs to little children," Jesus said.

The grown-ups grew quiet, watching. They had thought Jesus was too important and too busy for little ones. But Jesus was never too busy for a child.

He held them until the sun grew low. And when at last it was time to go, every child went home knowing they had sat with Jesus — and that Jesus was glad they came.
