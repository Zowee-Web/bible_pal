The Woman at the Well

One hot afternoon, Jesus was resting beside an old well. The sun was high, and he was tired and thirsty from a long walk. His friends had gone to find some food, so Jesus sat there all alone.

Then a woman came to the well, carrying a jar to fetch some water. She came alone, in the heat of the day, when no one else was around — for she was the sort of person other people often passed by, and left out.

But Jesus did not pass her by. He looked right at her, kindly, and said, "Would you give me a drink of water?"

The woman was surprised. People like Jesus did not usually speak to people like her! "Why are you talking to me?" she asked.

So Jesus began to talk with her, gently and kindly. He told her about a special gift he could give — a living water, he called it, a gladness deep inside that never, ever runs dry. And as they talked, the woman began to realize something amazing. This man knew her. He knew everything about her, all the things she had ever done — and yet he was not unkind. He looked at her with love.

She had never felt so seen, and so cared for, in all her life. And she knew, somehow, that this must be someone very special indeed.

The woman was so full of joy that she left her water jar right there, and ran back to her town. "Come and see!" she called to everyone. "Come and meet a man who told me everything about myself — and was kind!"

So remember, little one: there is no one Jesus passes by. He sees you — all of you — and he loves you. You are never invisible to him.
