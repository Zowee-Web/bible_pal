The Brother Who Ran to Hug Him

Jacob was going home at last. He had been away for many, many years. And as he traveled, his heart was full of worry — because he was about to see his brother, Esau.

You see, long ago, Jacob had done something that hurt Esau very much. And the two brothers had been apart ever since. Now Jacob was coming back, and he did not know what would happen. Would Esau still be angry? Jacob was afraid.

So Jacob did all he could to make things right. He gathered together gifts — lots and lots of them — flocks of sheep, and goats, and camels — and he sent them on ahead, to show his brother how sorry he was. And that night, Jacob prayed to God for help. "Please," he prayed, "watch over us."

The next day, Jacob looked up — and there, in the distance, was Esau, coming toward him with a great crowd of men. Jacob's heart pounded. This was the moment.

But then something wonderful happened. Esau did not come to fight. Esau began to run — run! — straight toward Jacob. And he threw his arms around his brother's neck, and he hugged him tight, and he kissed him. And both brothers cried — happy tears, with all the worry melting away.

After all those years, and all that hurt, Esau had forgiven his brother. And the two of them were together again at last.

So if you have ever had a falling-out with someone you love, little one, do not lose hope. Hearts can heal. Sorry can be said. And sometimes, the very person you are most afraid to face will be the one who runs to throw their arms around you.
