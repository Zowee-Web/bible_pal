Hannah's Prayer

Long ago, there lived a woman named Hannah. Hannah had a kind husband who loved her very much. But still, Hannah carried a quiet sadness in her heart. For more than anything else in all the world, Hannah longed for a baby of her own — a little child to hold, and rock, and love. Yet year after year went by, and no baby came. And oh, how it made her heart ache.

Now, once each year, Hannah's family would travel to God's house, to worship and to pray. And one year, when they arrived, Hannah's sadness felt heavier than ever before. While the others were eating, she could not eat a thing. So she slipped away, all by herself, to the house of God. And there, she began to pray.

But Hannah did not pray a tidy little prayer. She was far too sad for that. The tears came rolling down her cheeks, and she let them come. She did not hide them. She did not pretend that everything was fine. She brought her whole, true, aching heart straight to God, just exactly as it was.

Her lips moved, but no sound came out — she was praying quietly, deep down inside. "O God," she prayed, "please look at me. Please remember me. You see how sad I am. More than anything, I long for a child. If only you would give me a little son, I would give him right back to you, to love you and to serve you all the days of his life."

On and on she prayed, with the tears still falling. It was a prayer so important, and so full of longing, that it made her weep.

Now, the old priest, whose name was Eli, was sitting nearby, and he saw her lips moving with no words coming out. At first, he did not understand. But when Hannah explained that she was pouring out her heart to God, Eli spoke to her very kindly. "Go in peace," he said. "And may God give you what you have asked of him."

And do you know — when Hannah stood up, something had changed inside her. She was not sad anymore. She had handed her great big worry over to God, and now her heart felt light, as though a heavy load had been lifted right off her shoulders. She wiped away her tears, and she ate, and her face was happy again. She went home in peace, trusting God.

And God had heard her. God had heard every single word.

Before very long, the most wonderful thing happened. Hannah had a baby — a beautiful baby boy, just as she had longed for! She named him Samuel. And she held him close, and kissed his little head, and thanked God over and over again, for God had remembered her, and had answered her tearful prayer.

So remember this, little one. When something matters to you so very much that it brings tears to your eyes, you never have to hide those tears away. You can bring them straight to God, exactly the way Hannah did. He is always listening. He is never too busy. And he will always, always remember you.
