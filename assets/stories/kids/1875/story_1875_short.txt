Hannah's Prayer

There was once a woman named Hannah, and Hannah was very sad. More than anything in all the world, she wanted a baby of her own to love. But year after year went by, and still no baby came. And it made her heart ache.

One day, Hannah went to God's house to pray. She was so sad that the tears spilled right down her cheeks. But she did not hide them. She did not pretend to be happy. She brought her whole sad heart to God, just exactly as it was.

Quietly, with her lips moving but no sound coming out, Hannah poured out everything to God. "Please," she prayed through her tears. "Please remember me. I want a child so much. And if you give me a little son, I will give him back to you, to serve you all his life."

It was such an important prayer that it made her cry.

When Hannah finished praying, something had changed. She was not sad anymore. She had given her heavy worry to God, and now her heart felt light again. She dried her tears, and she went home in peace.

And God had heard every word.

A while later — oh, joy! — Hannah had a baby boy, just as she had asked. She named him Samuel. She held him close and thanked God again and again, for God had remembered her, and answered her prayer.

So when something matters to you so much that it makes you cry, little one, you do not have to hide your tears. You can bring them to God, just like Hannah did. He is always listening. And he always, always remembers you.
