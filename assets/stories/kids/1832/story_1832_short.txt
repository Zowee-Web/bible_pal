The Joyful Parade

One sunny morning, Jesus came walking toward the great city of Jerusalem with all his friends. And he was about to be welcomed like a king.

But Jesus did not come riding a big, fierce war horse, the way a proud king might. Instead, Jesus asked his friends to bring him a little donkey — a gentle, ordinary, baby donkey. And Jesus climbed onto its back, and rode toward the city, slow and humble and kind.

When the people of Jerusalem heard that Jesus was coming, oh, what excitement! Crowds came pouring out to meet him, more than you could ever count. They were so happy and so full of joy that they wanted to give Jesus a welcome fit for a king.

Some of the people pulled off their coats and spread them across the road, to make a soft and special path for the donkey to walk on. Others ran to the palm trees and cut down big green branches, and they waved them high in the air, back and forth, back and forth.

And everybody began to shout for joy. "Hosanna!" they cried. "Hosanna! Blessed is the one who comes! Welcome to our king!"

Children waved their branches and skipped along beside the donkey. The whole road was a sea of green palms and happy, smiling faces. It was the most joyful parade the city had ever seen.

And there, in the middle of it all, rode gentle Jesus on his little donkey — not proud, not grand, but loved by all the happy, cheering people.
