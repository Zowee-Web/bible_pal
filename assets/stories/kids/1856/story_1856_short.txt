Peter Set Free from Prison

Peter was one of Jesus' very best friends, and he loved to tell everyone the good news about Jesus. But a king named Herod did not like that at all. So he had Peter put in prison, behind a heavy locked door, with soldiers to guard him.

But Peter's friends did not give up. All together, they gathered in a house, and they prayed and prayed for Peter, late into the night.

That night, Peter was fast asleep in the prison, when suddenly a bright light filled the dark cell. An angel of God was standing right beside him! "Quick — get up!" the angel said. And the heavy chains fell right off Peter's wrists, clink, onto the floor.

"Put on your sandals," said the angel, "and follow me." So Peter did. They walked right past the sleeping guards. And the great iron gate of the prison swung open all by itself! Out they went, into the quiet street — and then the angel was gone. Peter was free!

Peter hurried to the house where his friends were praying, and he knocked on the door. A servant girl named Rhoda came to answer it. When she heard Peter's voice, she was so happy and so surprised that she forgot to open the door! She ran back inside shouting, "It's Peter! Peter is here!"

"You must be dreaming!" they said. But Peter kept on knocking. And when they finally opened the door — there he stood, safe and sound. Oh, how they laughed and hugged and thanked God! For God had heard their prayers, and had set their friend free.
