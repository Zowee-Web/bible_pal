Peter Set Free from Prison

Peter was one of Jesus' very best friends. After Jesus went back to heaven, Peter traveled all around, telling everyone the wonderful good news: that Jesus loved them, and that he was alive. Crowds gathered to listen, and more and more people came to love Jesus too.

But a king named Herod did not like this one bit. He wanted to stop Peter from talking about Jesus. So one day, he had Peter arrested and put in prison, behind a great locked door, with soldiers standing guard so that he could not get out.

Now, Peter's friends loved him dearly, and they were not about to give up on him. They gathered together in a house, all of them, and they did the most important thing they could do. They prayed. They prayed for Peter through the evening, and on into the night — asking God, again and again, to please watch over their friend and help him. It was late, and they were tired, and some of them must have wondered how God could possibly help now, with Peter shut away so tightly behind those prison walls. But they did not give up, and they did not stop. They kept right on praying together, deep into the night.

And inside the prison, that very night, God was already at work.

Peter was fast asleep. Even there, with a chain on each wrist and soldiers close beside him, his heart was so at peace that he slept soundly. And then, all at once, a bright light filled the dark little cell. An angel of God was standing right there beside him! The angel gave Peter a gentle tap on the side. "Quick — get up!" he said.

And as Peter sat up, the heavy iron chains simply slipped right off his wrists — clink, clink — and fell to the floor.

"Get dressed," said the angel, calm and kind. "Put on your sandals. Wrap your coat around you, and follow me." So Peter did, though he could hardly believe what was happening. He thought he must be having a dream!

Together, they walked right past the first set of guards. Then past the second set. And not one of the soldiers stirred. At last they came to the great iron gate that led out into the city — the big, heavy gate, locked up tight. And as they reached it, the gate swung wide open, all by itself! Out into the cool night they walked, down the quiet street.

They went a little way together. And then, as suddenly as he had come, the angel was gone.

Peter stood alone in the moonlight, wide awake now, and finally he understood. This was no dream at all. God had truly sent his angel and set him free!

So Peter hurried through the dark, empty streets, straight to the house where he knew his friends would still be praying. And he knocked on the door.

A servant girl named Rhoda came to see who was there. When she heard Peter's voice through the door, she was so overjoyed, and so amazed, that she completely forgot to open it! Instead, she spun around and ran back inside, calling out at the top of her voice, "It's Peter! Peter is here! He's standing at the door!"

"Rhoda, that can't be true," her friends said. "You must be imagining it." But she was certain, and she kept on insisting that Peter was really there. "You're out of your mind!" one of them said. "Then it must be his angel!" said another. And so they stood about, going back and forth — while all the while, poor Peter was still outside in the night, knocking, and knocking, and knocking.

At last, someone went and opened the door — and there stood Peter, real and alive and smiling at them all. For a moment they could only stare, hardly able to believe their own eyes. And then — oh, what joy! They pulled him inside, and they hugged him, and they laughed, and together they thanked God with all their hearts. What a night it had been! They had gathered only to ask God for help — and God had answered them by walking their dear friend straight out of a locked prison, past every guard, and right back to their own front door.

For they had prayed and prayed for their dear friend. And God had heard every single word — and had set Peter free.
