Peter Set Free from Prison

Peter was one of Jesus' very best friends. After Jesus went back to heaven, Peter traveled all around, telling everyone the wonderful good news: that Jesus loved them, and was alive. Crowds came to listen, and many people believed.

But a king named Herod did not like this at all. He wanted to stop Peter. So one day, he had Peter arrested and put in prison, behind a heavy locked door, with soldiers standing guard to make sure he could not get out.

Now, Peter's friends loved him dearly, and they did not give up on him. They gathered together in a house, all of them, and they prayed and prayed for Peter — late into the night, asking God to help him.

And inside the prison, that very night, something wonderful was about to happen.

Peter was fast asleep, resting peacefully even there, with a chain on each wrist and guards close by. Suddenly, a bright light filled the dark cell. An angel of God was standing right beside him! The angel gently tapped Peter on the side. "Quick — get up!" he said.

And as Peter sat up, the heavy chains simply fell right off his wrists — clink, clink — and dropped to the floor.

"Get dressed," said the angel, calm and kind. "Put on your sandals, and wrap your coat around you, and follow me." Peter did just as he was told, though he could hardly believe it was real. He thought he might be dreaming!

Together they walked right past the first guards, and then the second guards — and not one of them stirred. They came to the great iron gate that led out to the city. And the huge, heavy gate swung wide open, all by itself! Out they walked, into the cool and quiet night street.

They went a little way down the road — and then, just like that, the angel was gone. And Peter stood there alone in the moonlight, wide awake now, and he understood. This was no dream. God had truly sent his angel and set him free!

So Peter hurried through the dark streets to the house where he knew his friends would be praying. And he knocked on the door.

A servant girl named Rhoda came to see who it was. When she heard Peter's voice through the door, she was so overjoyed, and so surprised, that she completely forgot to open it! Instead, she ran back inside, calling out, "It's Peter! Peter is at the door! He's here!"

"Rhoda, you must be dreaming," her friends said. "It can't be!" But she kept insisting it was true. And all the while, poor Peter was still standing outside, knocking and knocking and knocking.

At last, they hurried to the door and opened it — and there stood Peter, safe and sound and smiling. They could hardly believe their eyes! Oh, how they laughed, and hugged him, and thanked God together. For they had prayed and prayed — and God had heard every word, and had set their dear friend free.
