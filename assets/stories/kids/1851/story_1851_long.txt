Paul Meets Jesus on the Road

There once was a man named Saul. Saul was clever, and strong, and very sure of himself. And he did not like the friends of Jesus at all. In fact, he wanted to stop them, so they could not tell people the good news about Jesus anymore.

So one day, Saul set off on a journey. He took the long road toward a faraway city called Damascus. He was going there to look for even more of Jesus' friends.

But God had a very different plan for Saul.

Saul was walking along the hot, dusty road, thinking his own proud thoughts. And then it happened. Suddenly — flash! — a light blazed down from heaven. It shone all around him, brighter than the noonday sun. It was brighter than anything Saul had ever seen in all his life. It was so bright, and so sudden, that Saul fell right down to the ground and threw his arm across his eyes. His proud heart was pounding. He had never in all his life felt so small, or so amazed.

Then a voice came out of the middle of the light. "Saul," it said. "Saul. Why are you trying to hurt me?"

Saul began to tremble. "Who — who are you?" he whispered.

"I am Jesus," said the voice. It was gentle, but it was strong and clear. "Now get up, and go on into the city. There you will be told what to do."

Slowly, Saul climbed to his feet. He opened his eyes — but everything was dark. He could not see at all! The men traveling with him had heard the voice too, and they stood there, amazed and silent. So they took Saul by the hand, and they led him the rest of the way into the city, one careful step at a time.

For three whole days, Saul could not see a thing. He did not eat. He did not march about giving orders, the way he used to. He simply sat, very still, in the quiet dark. And he thought. And he prayed. All his proud and angry thoughts grew quiet. Again and again, he remembered the light, and the voice, and the name: Jesus. And he began to wonder if everything he had believed about Jesus had been wrong all along. And little by little, deep inside, he began to change.

Now, in that same city lived a kind man named Ananias, who loved Jesus. And God spoke to him. "Go and find Saul," God said, "and help him." At first, Ananias felt a little afraid, because he had heard about Saul. But he trusted God, and so he went. It took courage to go to a man like Saul — but Ananias did exactly what God asked of him. He found Saul, and he gently laid his hands on him. "Jesus, who met you on the road, has sent me," he said, "so that you can see again."

And at that very moment, something like little scales fell away from Saul's eyes. And he could see! He could see the room. He could see the bright daylight. And he could see the kind face of Ananias, smiling down at him.

But his eyes were not the only thing that had changed. Deep inside, Saul was a brand-new man. His proud, hard heart had grown soft, and warm, and full of love for Jesus. The man who had once wanted to stop Jesus' friends now wanted nothing more in all the world than to be one of them.

And he did not wait even a little while to begin. Right away, Saul started telling everyone the good news about Jesus. People could hardly believe their ears! "Is this really the same man?" they asked one another. "The one who used to fight against Jesus? And now look — he loves him more than anyone!"

From that day on, Saul — who came to be called Paul — was one of Jesus' very happiest followers. And he spent the rest of his whole life traveling from town to town, and country to country, telling every single person he met how very, very much God loved them. He made new friends, and started little churches, and wrote letters full of encouragement that people still read to this very day. And it all began on the road to Damascus — the day Jesus met him, and softened his heart, and made him brand-new.

For God can change any heart, and he loves to give people a fresh new beginning.
