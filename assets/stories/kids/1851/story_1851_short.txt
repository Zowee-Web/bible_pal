Paul Meets Jesus on the Road

There once was a man named Saul, and Saul did not like the friends of Jesus at all. In fact, he wanted to stop them. So one day he set off down the long road to Damascus, looking for the friends of Jesus.

But God had a different plan for Saul.

As Saul walked along the dusty road, suddenly — flash! — a light from heaven shone all around him, brighter than the noonday sun. It was so bright that Saul fell right down to the ground, and he covered his eyes.

Then, out of the light, came a voice. "Saul, Saul," it said, gently and clearly. "Why are you trying to hurt me?"

"Who — who are you?" Saul whispered.

"I am Jesus," said the voice. "Get up now, and go into the city, and you will be told what to do."

When Saul stood up and opened his eyes, he found that he could not see at all. So his friends took him by the hand and led him into the city. For three days Saul could not see — and he sat very still, and he thought, and he prayed.

Then God sent a kind man named Ananias. Ananias laid his hands on Saul, and at once Saul could see again! Saul was not the same anymore.

From that day on, Saul — who came to be called Paul — was no longer a man who fought against Jesus. He became one of Jesus' happiest followers, and he spent the rest of his life telling everyone, everywhere, how very much God loved them.

For God can change any heart, and he loves to give people a fresh new beginning.
