The Hills Full of Fire

Early one morning, Elisha's young helper stepped outside — and his heart nearly stopped.

All around the city, as far as he could see, was a great enemy army. Soldiers and horses and chariots, covering the hills, ringing the whole town. They had crept up in the night while everyone slept. And now, it seemed, there was no way out at all.

The young man ran back inside. "Oh, my master!" he cried. "What are we going to do? They are all around us!"

But Elisha was not afraid. Not even a little. "Don't be afraid," he said gently. "There are more on our side than on theirs."

More on our side? The young man looked around the room. There was only him, and Elisha. Just the two of them. How could that possibly be more?

So Elisha prayed. "Lord," he said, "please open his eyes, so that he can see."

And God opened the young man's eyes.

And oh — what he saw then! All over the hills, all around Elisha, were horses and chariots of fire. Shining, and blazing, and more than he could ever count. They had been there the whole time, all around Elisha, watching over them — far, far more than all the enemy soldiers below.

And just like that, the young man's fear melted away. He had thought he and Elisha were all alone, with danger on every side. But they had never been alone. God had been right there, all around them, the entire time. The young man simply hadn't been able to see it.

And that day, God kept them both completely safe.

Sometimes we feel small, and afraid, and surrounded by things that seem far too big. But Elisha and his helper were never really alone — and neither are we. God is with us the whole time, closer than we could ever see.
