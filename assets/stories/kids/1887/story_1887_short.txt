The Hidden Treasure

Jesus loved to tell stories, and here is a little one he told.

Once, there was a man walking across a big, open field. It was an ordinary day, and he was busy with his work. But as he walked, his foot bumped against something hard, buried in the ground.

He stopped. He bent down and began to dig. And there, hidden under the dirt, he found something wonderful — a treasure! A box full of gold and shining, precious things, buried long ago and forgotten.

The man could hardly believe his eyes. His heart leaped for joy! He had never seen anything so wonderful in all his life. He wanted that treasure more than anything in the whole world.

So he carefully covered it back up again, to keep it safe. And then he ran home, as happy as could be. He sold his house. He sold all the things he owned. He gathered up every coin he had — and he used it all to buy that field, so the treasure could be his.

And do you know — he was not sad about it, not even a little! He gave up everything he had gladly, with a great big smile, because the treasure was worth so much more than all of it put together.

Jesus told this story to show us something. God, and his kingdom of love, is like that hidden treasure. It is the most wonderful thing of all — worth more than anything else in the whole wide world. And when you find it, your heart fills right up with joy.

So keep your eyes open, little one. The very best treasure of all is God's love — and it is yours for the finding.
