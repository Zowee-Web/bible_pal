Joseph Forgives His Brothers

When Joseph was a boy, he had eleven brothers. His father loved him dearly and gave him a beautiful coat of many colors. But this made his older brothers jealous — so jealous that one day they sent Joseph far away from home, all the way to the land of Egypt.

It was a hard and lonely thing to happen to a young boy. But God did not leave Joseph. God was with him through every part of it, in the good days and the hard ones.

Many years went by. Joseph grew up into a wise and kind man. And in Egypt, something amazing happened: he became a great and important leader — the helper of the king himself. The king put Joseph in charge of saving up food, so that when a hard time came, no one would go hungry.

And a hard time did come. The rain stopped falling, and food became very difficult to find — not just in Egypt, but in faraway places too. People traveled from all over to buy food from Egypt, because Joseph had saved up so much.

And one day, who should come walking up, looking for food to buy, but Joseph's very own brothers!

They bowed low before the important man, not knowing who he was. How could they? The last time they had seen Joseph, he was only a boy. But Joseph knew them right away — and his heart began to pound.

For a moment he could hardly breathe. Then his heart became so full that he began to cry. He could not hold it in any longer.

"It is me," he said to them. "I am Joseph. I am your brother."

His brothers were afraid, and they did not know what to say. Surely, after everything they had done, after all these years, Joseph would be angry with them now.

But Joseph was not angry. He came close, and he wrapped his arms around them, one by one. "Do not be afraid," he said softly. "Yes, you meant to hurt me, long ago. But God was still working for good, even then — and see how he has brought me here now, with food to keep us all alive. I forgive you, my brothers. Now please — let us bring our father here, and all be together again."

And the brothers wept, and held on to Joseph, and held on to one another. A whole family, broken for so long, was together once more.
