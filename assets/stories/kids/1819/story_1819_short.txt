Joseph Forgives His Brothers

When Joseph was a boy, his older brothers grew jealous of him, and they sent him far away from home, all the way to the land of Egypt. It was a hard and lonely thing to happen. But God was with Joseph through all of it. Even in that faraway land, God never once left his side.

Many years went by. Joseph grew up, and in Egypt he became a great and important man — the helper of the king himself. He was put in charge of all the food in the whole land.

Then a hard time came, when food was difficult to find, even in faraway places. And who should arrive in Egypt one day, looking for food to buy, but Joseph's very own brothers! They did not know that the important man in front of them was the little brother they had sent away so long ago. But Joseph knew them at once.

His heart became so full that he began to cry. "It is me," he told them. "I am Joseph, your brother."

His brothers were afraid. Surely, after all these years, Joseph would be angry.

But Joseph was not angry. He wrapped his arms around them, one by one. "Do not be afraid," he said softly. "You meant to hurt me, but God was still working for good, all along. I forgive you. Now come — let us bring our father, and all be together again."

And the brothers wept, and held on to one another — a whole family, together once more.
