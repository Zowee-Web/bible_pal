The Boy Given Back to His Mother

One day, Jesus came walking toward a little town called Nain. A big crowd of people walked along the road with him.

But at the gate of the town, Jesus met another crowd coming the other way — and this crowd was very, very sad. A boy had died. He had been his mother's only son, and now she was all alone. She walked behind, crying and crying, and all her neighbors wept along with her.

When Jesus saw the mother weeping, his heart was filled with love for her. He did not even wait to be asked. He came right up to her, and very gently he said, "Please don't cry."

Then Jesus stepped close to the boy. And he spoke to him, the way you might speak to someone who is fast asleep. "Young man," Jesus said, "I tell you, get up."

And the boy opened his eyes. He sat right up. And he began to talk!

Then Jesus took the boy by the hand and gave him back to his mother. Oh, can you imagine that hug? The mother who had no one left now held her own dear son again, the two of them laughing and crying all at once.

All the people were filled with wonder. "God has come to help his people," they said. And the happy news spread out across the whole countryside.

Jesus had seen one sad mother, weeping at a town gate. He did not pass her by. He saw her tears, and he came close, and he gave her back the one she loved.

That is how much Jesus cares when someone is crying. He always sees. And he always comes close.
