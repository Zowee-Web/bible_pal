The Garden at Night

One night, after supper with his friends, Jesus went out to a quiet garden, full of olive trees, to pray. And he took some of his closest friends along with him.

Now, Jesus knew that a very hard day was coming — the hardest day of all. And his heart was heavy, and sad, and even afraid. Yes — even Jesus felt afraid.

"My heart is so full of sorrow," he told his friends. "Please, stay here, and stay close to me, while I pray."

Then Jesus went a little way off, and he knelt down on the ground. And he prayed to his Father in heaven — honestly, holding nothing back. He told God exactly how he felt.

"Father," he prayed, "if there is any other way, please let this hard thing pass me by. But — not what I want. What you want. I will do what you ask."

It was a brave and honest prayer. For Jesus was afraid, and yet he was willing to do the hard thing anyway. That is what real courage is: not never being scared, but trusting God even when you are.

And God did not leave his Son all alone in the dark. He sent an angel, down from heaven, to be with Jesus — to give him strength, and to comfort him. And Jesus was not alone anymore.

So when you feel scared, little one — at bedtime, or any time at all — remember the garden. You can tell God exactly how you feel, just like Jesus did. You can be brave and afraid at the very same time. And you are never, ever alone in the dark. God is right there with you, to give you strength, and to comfort you.
