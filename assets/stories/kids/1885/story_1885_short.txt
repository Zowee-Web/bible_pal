The Oil That Did Not Stop

There was once a poor mother, and she was so very worried. Her husband had died, and now she owed some money that she could not pay. She did not know what to do.

So she went to God's helper, a man named Elisha. "Please help me," she said. "I have nothing left."

"Tell me," said Elisha kindly, "what do you have in your house?"

The woman thought. "Nothing at all," she said, "except one little jar of oil."

"Then here is what you must do," said Elisha. "Go to all your neighbors, and borrow as many empty jars as you can. Big ones, small ones — gather lots and lots of them. Then go inside, shut the door, and begin to pour your little jar of oil into them."

It seemed a strange thing to do! But the woman trusted God. So she and her two sons gathered empty jars from all around, until the house was full of them. Then she shut the door, and she began to pour.

And the oil poured out — and it kept on pouring! She filled one jar, and then another, and another. Her little jar of oil did not run dry. On and on it flowed, filling every empty jar in the house!

"Bring me another jar," she said to her son.

"There are no more," he said. "Every jar is full!"

And only then did the oil stop.

The woman ran to tell Elisha. "Now," he said, "go and sell the oil. There will be plenty to pay what you owe, and plenty left over to live on."

God had taken her one small jar, and her trust, and made it more than enough.

So when your hands feel empty, little one, you can bring them to God. He loves to fill what we bring to him, right up to the brim.
