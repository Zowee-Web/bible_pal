The Walls of Jericho

In front of God's people stood the great city of Jericho. Its walls were so high and so thick that no one could ever climb over them or knock them down. The big gates were shut up tight. However would God's people get inside?

But God had a plan — a surprising one. "Do not fight," God told their leader, Joshua. "March around the city instead. One time around, each day, for six days. Let the priests carry trumpets as they go."

So that is just what they did. Quietly, with no shouting at all, God's people marched all the way around the tall walls of Jericho. The only sounds were the soft tramp of their feet and the long, low call of the trumpets. High on the walls, the people of the city watched, wondering whatever this could mean. Then God's people went back to their tents to rest.

On the seventh day, they marched around the city not one time, but seven times. And then Joshua called out, "Now! Shout! For God is with us!"

The priests blew their trumpets as loud as they could. And all the people shouted one great, big shout, all together.

And the mighty walls of Jericho came tumbling down — crash! — flat to the ground.

God's people could hardly believe their eyes. The walls that no one could ever climb, the walls that no one could ever knock down, had fallen flat — all because God was with them. And they stood together in the bright morning, their hearts full of wonder at all the things God could do.
