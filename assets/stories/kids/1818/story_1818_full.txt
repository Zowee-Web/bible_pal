The Walls of Jericho

After many long years of traveling, God's people came at last to the land God had promised them. But right in front of them stood a great city called Jericho.

Oh, what walls that city had! They were so high you had to tip your head all the way back to see the top. They were so thick that houses were built right on top of them. The big wooden gates were shut up tight, and locked. However in the world would God's people ever get inside?

But God had a plan — and it was a very surprising one.

"Do not fight the city," God told their leader, Joshua. "Instead, march around it. One time around, each day, for six days. Have the priests walk in front, carrying trumpets. And the people are to stay quiet — no shouting, not yet."

It was a strange plan. But God's people trusted God. So that is just what they did.

On the first day, they marched all the way around the tall walls of Jericho, one time, and went home. The only sounds were the soft tramp of their feet and the long, low call of the trumpets. The people up on the walls watched, and wondered what in the world was happening.

On the second day, they did it again. And the third day. And the fourth, and the fifth, and the sixth. Around and around, once each day, quiet and steady, while the trumpets sang. And still, nothing happened. The walls stood as tall and strong as ever. But God's people kept marching, and kept trusting, and waited to see what God would do.

Then came the seventh day. This time, God's people marched around the city not once, but seven whole times. Around, and around, and around. And when they had finished the seventh time, everything went quiet and still. Then Joshua lifted his voice and called out, "Now! Shout! For God is with us!"

The priests blew their trumpets as loud as they possibly could. And all the people shouted one great, big shout, all together, so loud it filled the whole sky.

And the mighty walls of Jericho came tumbling down — crash! — flat to the ground.

God's people could hardly believe their eyes. The walls that no one could ever climb, the walls that no one could ever knock down, had fallen flat — all because God was with them. And God's people stood together in the morning light, their hearts full of wonder at all the things God could do.
