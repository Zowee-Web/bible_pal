Daniel in the Lions' Den

In a great and faraway kingdom, there lived a man named Daniel, and he loved God with all his heart. Three times every day — morning, noon, and evening — Daniel would kneel down by his open window and pray to God. It was his very favorite thing to do, and he had done it for as long as he could remember.

Now, the king of that land liked Daniel more than all his other helpers, because Daniel was wise and good and could always be trusted. But this made some of the other men terribly jealous. They wanted to get Daniel into trouble. So they came up with a sneaky, sneaky plan.

They went to the king and tricked him into making a new rule. For thirty whole days, they said, no one in all the land was allowed to pray to anyone — except the king himself. And anyone who broke the rule would be thrown into a den full of hungry lions. The king, not knowing the trick, agreed and signed it.

But Daniel still loved God. And so Daniel did exactly what he always did: he went home, knelt down by his open window, and prayed.

The jealous men were watching, and the moment they saw him, they ran to tell the king. The king was very sad — he did not want to hurt Daniel at all. But a rule was a rule, and it could not be undone. So, with a heavy heart, the king let Daniel be lowered down into the den of lions.

All night long, the king could not sleep. He paced and he worried. But down in the dark den, something wonderful was happening that the king could not see. God had sent an angel, and the angel had gently closed the lions' great mouths. The lions did not roar. They did not bite. They simply lay down beside Daniel, quiet and calm, like enormous gentle cats. And Daniel was not afraid at all, because he knew that God was right there with him.

As soon as the first light of morning came, the king leaped up and ran all the way to the den. "Daniel!" he called out, hardly daring to hope. "Daniel — did your God keep you safe?"

And up came Daniel's voice, strong and glad. "Yes! My God sent an angel, and the angel shut the lions' mouths. I am not hurt at all — not even a scratch!"

The king was overjoyed. He had Daniel lifted up out of the den at once, safe and whole and well. And from that day on, the king told everyone in his whole kingdom about Daniel's God — the God who had kept his friend safe, all night long, among the lions.
