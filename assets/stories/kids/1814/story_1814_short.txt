Daniel in the Lions' Den

In a great kingdom far away, there lived a man named Daniel, and he loved God with all his heart. Every day, three times a day, Daniel knelt by his window and prayed to God. It was his favorite thing to do.

Now, the king liked Daniel very much. But some other men were jealous of Daniel, and they made a sneaky plan. They tricked the king into making a new rule: for thirty days, no one was allowed to pray to anyone but the king. And anyone who broke the rule would be thrown into a den of lions.

But Daniel loved God. So Daniel did just what he always did — he knelt by his open window and prayed.

When the jealous men saw him, they ran to tell the king. The king was very sad, because he did not want to hurt Daniel. But a rule was a rule. So Daniel was lowered down into the den of lions.

All night, the king could not sleep. And all night, down in the den, something wonderful was happening. God had sent an angel, and the angel had gently shut the lions' mouths. The big lions did not roar. They did not bite. They lay down beside Daniel, quiet and calm, like great gentle cats. And Daniel was not afraid, because he knew God was with him.

As soon as the sun came up, the king ran to the den. "Daniel!" he called. "Did your God keep you safe?"

"Yes!" came Daniel's voice. "God sent an angel to close the lions' mouths. I am not hurt at all!"

The king was overjoyed. He had Daniel lifted up out of the den, safe and sound, without a single scratch. And the king told everyone in all his kingdom about Daniel's God, who had kept him safe among the lions.
