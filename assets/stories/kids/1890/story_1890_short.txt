Singing in the Dark

Paul and his friend Silas loved Jesus, and they traveled from town to town, telling everyone the good news about him. But one day, in a faraway city, some people did not like what they were saying. And so Paul and Silas were put into prison.

It was a dark, cold prison, deep inside the jail. Their feet were locked fast, and there was no way out. Now, you might think Paul and Silas would be sad and afraid in such a place. But do you know what they did instead?

They sang.

Right there in the dark, at midnight, Paul and Silas began to sing songs to God! They prayed, and they sang out loud, full of trust, even in that gloomy prison. And all the other prisoners lay still in the dark, listening, and wondering — how could anyone sing in a place like this?

Then, all at once — rumble, rumble — the ground began to shake! It was a great earthquake. The whole prison trembled. And then the prison doors flew wide open, and every prisoner's chains fell right off!

Paul and Silas could have run away. But they did not. They stayed, calm and kind. And the jailer, who looked after the prison, was amazed. "What must I do?" he asked them. So Paul and Silas told him all about Jesus. And that very night, the jailer and his whole family became friends of God. He even took them home and gave them a warm meal.

So even in a dark and scary place, little one, you are never alone. God is right there with you. And like Paul and Silas, you can always find a song.
