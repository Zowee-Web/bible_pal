Singing in the Dark

Paul and his good friend Silas loved Jesus with all their hearts. Together they traveled from town to town, and from city to city, telling everyone they met the wonderful good news about Jesus. Many people were glad to hear it. But in one faraway city, some people did not like what Paul and Silas were saying. And so the two friends were arrested and put into prison.

It was not a nice prison. They were taken deep inside, into the darkest, coldest cell of all. Their feet were locked tightly into heavy wooden blocks, so they could not move. The door was shut, and bolted, and there was no way out at all.

Now, you might think that Paul and Silas would sit there feeling sad, and frightened, and sorry for themselves. And no one would have blamed them. But that is not what they did. Not at all.

Late that night, at midnight, when everything was dark and still — Paul and Silas began to sing.

That's right! They prayed out loud to God, and they sang him happy songs, right there in the gloomy prison. Their voices rose up through the darkness, strong and full of trust. And all around them, the other prisoners woke up and lay very still, listening in wonder. They had never heard anything like it. How could these two men sing, locked up in chains, in a place as dark as this?

But Paul and Silas knew a secret: God was right there with them, even in the darkness. And so they kept right on singing.

And then — it happened. All at once, deep down in the earth, came a rumble, and then a mighty shaking. It was a great earthquake! The whole prison trembled from top to bottom. And then, with a great crash, every prison door flew wide open — and the chains fell right off every single prisoner!

The jailer, the man whose job was to guard the prison, woke up with a fright. When he saw all the doors hanging open, his heart sank, for he was sure all the prisoners had run away. But Paul called out to him through the dark: "Do not worry! We are all still here!"

For Paul and Silas had not run. They had stayed, calm and kind, right where they were.

The jailer could hardly believe it. He came in, trembling, and knelt down before them. "What must I do?" he asked. So Paul and Silas told him all about Jesus, and how much God loved him. And that very night, the jailer believed — and his whole family too! He gently washed their hurts, and he took them into his own home, and gave them a warm meal, and they were all filled with joy together.

So remember this, little one: even in a dark and scary place, you are never, ever alone. God is right there with you, just as he was with Paul and Silas. And like them, even in the dark, you can always find a song to sing.
