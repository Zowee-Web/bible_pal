Nets Full of Fish

Early one morning, by the edge of a big lake, some tired fishermen were washing their nets. One of them was named Peter. And Peter was very tired indeed, because he and his friends had been out fishing all night long — and they had not caught a single fish. Not one!

Just then, Jesus came walking by. He climbed into Peter's boat and taught the crowds from the water. And when he was finished, he turned to Peter and said something surprising.

"Peter," said Jesus, "row out into the deep water, and let down your nets to catch some fish."

Peter was puzzled. "But Master," he said, "we fished all night, and caught nothing at all." He was so very tired. Still, Peter trusted Jesus. "But if you say so," he said, "I will let down the nets."

So out they rowed, and down went the nets into the deep water.

And then — oh my! The nets began to wriggle and pull. They were filling up with fish! More and more fish, until there were so many that the nets started to break! Peter called to his friends in the other boat to come and help. They filled up both boats with fish, until the boats were so full they nearly began to sink!

Peter could hardly believe his eyes. He knew that only Jesus could have done such a wonderful thing. And from that day on, Peter left his nets and followed Jesus wherever he went.

So if Jesus ever asks you to do something, little one — even when you feel tired, or it seems strange — you can trust him. He knows far more than we do. And he can take your empty nets, and fill them right to overflowing.
