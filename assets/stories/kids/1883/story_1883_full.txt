Nets Full of Fish

Early one morning, by the shore of a wide blue lake, some weary fishermen were washing out their nets. One of them was named Peter. And Peter was tired, right down to his bones. All night long — the whole night through — he and his friends had rowed their boats across the lake, casting their nets again and again, hoping to catch some fish. But they had caught nothing. Not one single fish. Now the sun was up, and they were worn out and discouraged. They had nothing at all to show for their long night's work — only empty nets to wash and to mend.

Just then, Jesus came walking along the shore. A great crowd was pressing close around him, all wanting to hear him speak. So Jesus stepped into Peter's empty boat, and asked him to push out a little way from the land. And from there, sitting in the boat, Jesus taught all the people on the shore.

When he had finished teaching, Jesus turned to Peter, and he said something that must have seemed very strange. "Peter," he said, "row out into the deep water, and let down your nets, and you will catch some fish."

Peter looked at him, puzzled and tired. "But Master," he said, "we worked hard all night long, and we caught nothing at all." How could there be fish now, in the bright morning, when there had been none in the dark? Yet Peter looked at Jesus, and something in his heart decided to trust him. "But if you say so," Peter said, "then I will let down the nets again."

So Peter and his friends rowed their boat out into the deep water, and they lowered their nets down beneath the waves.

And then — oh, what a surprise! The nets gave a great tug. They grew heavy, and then heavier still. They were filling up with fish — more, and more, and more fish, wriggling and shining and tumbling in, until the nets were so full they began to tear! Peter shouted to his partners in the other boat to hurry over and help. Together they hauled in the enormous catch, and they filled up both boats, right to the very top — so full of fish that the boats began to sink down low in the water! There were fish piled everywhere, shining and flopping all across the boards.

Peter could hardly believe what he was seeing. He knew, deep down, that no ordinary person could have done such a thing. Only Jesus could. And so, when they had brought the boats safely back to land, Peter and his friends left everything behind, and they followed Jesus, to be his close friends from that day on.

So if Jesus ever asks you to do something, little one — even when you feel tired, or even when it seems a little strange — you can trust him, just as Peter did. Jesus knows far more than we could ever know. And he can take your empty nets, and fill them right up to overflowing.
