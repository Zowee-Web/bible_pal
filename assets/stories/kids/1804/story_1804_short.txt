Jesus Walks on the Water

The little boat rocked on the dark water, far from shore. It was the middle of the night, and the wind would not stop blowing. The waves splashed higher and higher, and Jesus' friends, the disciples, did not know what to do.

Jesus was not in the boat. He had gone up a quiet hill by himself to pray. But he had not forgotten his friends.

In the darkest part of the night, the disciples saw something coming toward them across the water. A figure, walking — right on top of the waves! They were frightened. "What is it?" they cried.

Then a voice came over the water, warm and kind. "Take courage. It is I. Don't be afraid."

It was Jesus, walking on the water as easily as walking on a path.

Peter, one of the disciples, called out, "Lord, if it's really you, let me come to you!"

"Come," said Jesus.

So Peter climbed over the side of the boat and stepped out onto the water. And he walked! Step by step, Peter walked across the waves toward Jesus.

But then Peter looked at the wind and the big waves all around him. He grew afraid, and he began to sink down into the water. "Lord, save me!" he cried.

Right away — at once — Jesus reached out his hand and caught him, and held him up, safe and sound. "Why were you afraid?" Jesus said gently. "I am right here with you."

Together they climbed into the boat. And the moment Jesus stepped in, the wind went quiet. The waves grew calm and still, smooth as glass.

The disciples looked at Jesus with wonder. They knew, now, that he was the Son of God.

And Peter never forgot the feeling of that strong, kind hand catching him the very moment he called.
