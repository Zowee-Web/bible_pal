Jesus Walks on the Water

The little boat rocked on the dark water, far from shore. Evening had turned to night, and the night was growing long. The wind blew harder and harder, and the waves rose up and slapped against the sides of the boat. Jesus' friends, the disciples, pulled on the oars with all their might, but the boat would not go forward. Hour after hour they rowed, and still the far shore came no closer.

Jesus was not with them in the boat. Earlier that day, he had sent his friends on ahead and gone up a quiet hillside, all by himself, to pray. But even alone on the hill, he had not forgotten them. He knew exactly where their little boat was, out on the wind-tossed water.

The night grew darker and darker. And then, in the very darkest part, when the disciples were tired and afraid, they saw something. Out on the water, a figure was coming toward them — walking, right on top of the waves.

The disciples were filled with fear. "What is it?" they cried, holding tight to the boat.

But then a voice came across the water, warm and steady and kind. "Take courage," it said. "It is I. Don't be afraid."

It was Jesus. He was walking across the stormy sea as easily as you might walk across a garden.

Peter, one of the disciples, leaned out toward him. "Lord," he called, "if it is really you, let me come to you on the water!"

"Come," said Jesus.

And so Peter did something very brave. He climbed over the side of the rocking boat and put one foot, and then the other, down onto the water. And the water held him! It was firm beneath his feet, like a path made just for him. Step by step, his eyes locked on Jesus, Peter walked out across the rolling waves. The wind whipped all around him, but he kept his eyes on his friend and kept walking, closer and closer.

But then Peter looked away from Jesus. He looked at the wild wind and the tall, dark waves crashing all around him. Fear crept into his heart, and he began to sink down into the cold water. "Lord," he cried out, "save me!"

And right away — at that very moment, before Peter could sink any lower — Jesus reached out his hand and took hold of him. He lifted Peter up and held him, safe and sound, above the waves. "Why were you afraid?" Jesus said gently. "I am right here with you."

Together, hand in hand, they climbed into the boat. And the moment Jesus stepped inside, something amazing happened. The wind stopped blowing. The waves stopped crashing. The whole sea grew quiet and calm and still, smooth as glass under the night sky.

The disciples looked at Jesus, their hearts full of wonder. "Truly," they said, "you are the Son of God."

And Peter — wet and safe and warm again — never, ever forgot the feeling of that strong and gentle hand, catching hold of him the very moment he called out.
