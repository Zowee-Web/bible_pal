The Man in the Chariot

One day, God gave Philip, a follower of Jesus, a special job. "Go and walk down that desert road," God told him. So Philip went, not quite knowing why.

And as he walked, a fine chariot came rolling along the road. Inside sat an important man — a traveler from a faraway land called Ethiopia. He had come a very long way to learn about God, and now he was riding home. And as he rode, he was reading aloud from the Scriptures, God's holy book.

But the man had a puzzled look on his face. He was reading beautiful words about someone special — but he could not work out who they were about.

Then God whispered to Philip, "Go over to that chariot." So Philip ran up alongside it. "Do you understand what you are reading?" Philip asked.

"How can I," said the man, "unless someone explains it to me?" And he kindly invited Philip to climb up and sit beside him.

So Philip did. And starting right from those very words, Philip told the man all about Jesus — how much God loves us, and all the wonderful things Jesus had done. The man listened, and his face lit up with joy. At last, it all made sense!

Just then, they passed by some water at the side of the road. "Look — water!" said the man. "May I be baptized, right now?" And so they stopped, and Philip baptized him, right there.

When the man came up out of the water, he was so happy he could hardly sit still. And he went on his way home rejoicing, his heart full of the good news.

So the good news of Jesus is for everyone, little one — for every person, in every land, near or far. And there is nothing in all the world quite so joyful to discover.
