The Man in the Chariot

One day, God gave a follower of Jesus named Philip a rather surprising job. "Get up," God told him, "and go down to that road that runs through the desert." Now, that seemed a strange place to go — a quiet, dusty road in the middle of nowhere. But Philip trusted God, and so off he went, not yet knowing why.

And as Philip walked along, a fine and fancy chariot came rolling down the road. Inside sat a very important man. He had traveled all the way from a faraway land called Ethiopia, and he worked for the queen there, taking care of all her treasure. He had come a long, long way to worship God, and now he was riding the long way home again.

As the chariot rolled along, the man was reading aloud to himself from the Scriptures — God's holy book. But there was a puzzled frown on his face. He was reading beautiful words about someone special, someone who would come to help and to save — but he simply could not work out who the words were about.

Just then, God spoke quietly to Philip. "Go over to that chariot," he said, "and walk along beside it." So Philip hurried over, jogging alongside the rolling chariot. And he could hear the man reading aloud. "Excuse me," Philip called up to him. "Do you understand what you are reading?"

The man looked down, and sighed. "How can I," he said, "unless somebody explains it to me?" And kindly, he invited Philip to climb up into the chariot and sit beside him.

So Philip climbed up. And beginning with those very words the man had been reading, Philip explained the whole wonderful story. He told the Ethiopian all about Jesus — how God loved the world so much, and how Jesus had come, and all the good and beautiful things he had done. The man listened to every single word, and slowly, a great smile spread across his face. At last — at last! — it all made sense. His heart filled right up with joy.

As they rode along together, they came to some water beside the road. The man pointed to it, eagerly. "Look!" he said. "Here is water! Is there anything to stop me from being baptized, right now?" So they stopped the chariot, and the two of them went down to the water together, and Philip baptized him.

And when the Ethiopian came up out of the water, he was so full of happiness that he could hardly contain it. He climbed back into his chariot and went on his way home — rejoicing all the way, his heart overflowing with the wonderful good news he had found.

So you see, little one, the good news of Jesus is for everyone — for every single person, in every land, near and far, no matter who they are or where they come from. And there is nothing in all the world quite so joyful to discover.
