Samson and the Lion

Long ago, there lived a man named Samson, and Samson was the strongest man who ever lived. He could do things no other person could do. But Samson's great strength was not really his own. It was a gift — a special gift that God had given him, even before he was born. And as long as Samson trusted God, that strength was always there.

God had chosen Samson for something special, even before he was born. And so, all through the years of his growing up, the hand of God was upon him.

One sunny day, Samson was walking along a road in the countryside, past green fields and rows of grapevines. The birds were singing, and the warm sun was shining, and Samson walked along without a care in the world. He was on his way to visit a town not far off, and the road wound past a hillside thick with vines and tangled bushes.

But all of a sudden, he heard something. A low, rumbling growl, coming from the bushes right beside the road. Samson stopped, and he listened. For a moment, all was still. He looked this way and that, searching the shadows among the leaves. Then the growl came again — closer this time.

And then, with a great roar — out leaped a lion!

It was huge. Its eyes were fierce, and its roar was so loud it seemed to shake the very ground. A wild lion, springing out like that, would frighten almost anybody. Samson's heart must have pounded.

But Samson did not turn and run. For in that very moment, God filled him with strength and with courage. All at once Samson felt strong, and steady, and brave — the kind of brave you feel deep inside when you know that God is right there with you, and that you are not alone. It was not that Samson stopped feeling the danger. It was that, right in the middle of it, he was not facing the danger by himself.

The lion leaped toward him. But Samson was ready. With the strength that God had given him, Samson took firm hold of the lion — and he was kept completely safe. As mighty as that lion was, it could not hurt Samson, not even a little.

And then, just like that, it was over. For a long moment, Samson simply stood there, catching his breath. He looked down at his own two hands, almost surprised. How had he done such a thing? Deep down, he knew it had not been by his own power at all. Samson stood on the quiet road, safe and sound, his heart still beating fast. And he knew, deep in his heart, exactly where his courage had come from. It had not come from his big strong arms. It had come from God.

So remember Samson, little one, whenever something feels too big or too scary for you. You do not have to be brave all on your own. When you need it most, God can fill you with a courage far bigger than yourself. For God is always with you — and that, in the end, is what makes you brave.
