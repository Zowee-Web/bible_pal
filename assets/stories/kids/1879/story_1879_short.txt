Samson and the Lion

There was once a man named Samson, and Samson was the strongest man who ever lived. But here is the secret of his strength: it did not come from Samson himself. It was a special gift from God.

One day, Samson was walking along a road, past green fields and rows of grapevines. The sun was warm, and all was quiet. Samson was not afraid of anything.

But then — a sound! A low, rumbling growl, coming from the bushes beside the road.

And out leaped a lion!

It was big, and it was fierce, and it roared a mighty roar that seemed to shake the air. A lion like that could be very frightening indeed.

But Samson did not run away. For at that very moment, God filled Samson with strength and courage. He felt brave and strong — the kind of brave you feel when you know that you are not alone.

The lion sprang toward him. But Samson was ready. With the strength God had given him, Samson took hold of the lion, and he was kept completely safe. The lion could not hurt him at all.

When it was over, Samson stood there, safe and sound, and he knew exactly where his courage had come from. Not from himself. From God.

So if you ever feel small, or scared, remember Samson. When something feels too big for you, God can fill you with a courage that is bigger still. You do not have to be brave all by yourself. God is with you — and that makes all the difference.
