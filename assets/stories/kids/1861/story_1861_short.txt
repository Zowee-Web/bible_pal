Friends Through the Roof

One day, Jesus was teaching inside a house, and so many people came to hear him that there was no room left at all. People filled every corner, and a crowd spilled right out the door.

Now, in that town lived a man who could not walk. And he had four wonderful friends who loved him very much. "Let's take him to Jesus," they said. "Jesus can help him!" So they laid their friend on a mat, and each one picked up a corner, and they carried him all the way to the house.

But when they got there — oh no! The house was packed full. They could not get through the door. They could not even get close.

Most people would have given up and gone home. But not these four friends. They had an idea. Carefully, they carried their friend up the outside stairs, all the way to the flat roof. And then they began to dig — scratch, scratch, scrape — making a hole right through the roof, until there was an opening big enough. And gently, with ropes, they lowered their friend down, down, down on his mat — right into the middle of the room, right in front of Jesus!

Jesus looked up and saw the man, and he saw the four friends peering down through the hole. He smiled at their love and their faith. "Get up," Jesus said to the man. "Pick up your mat, and walk home."

And the man did! He stood up — he could walk! He rolled up his mat, and he walked right out the door, while everyone gasped and cheered and praised God. Four good friends had not given up — and look what love had done.
