Friends Through the Roof

One day, Jesus was teaching inside a house in the town of Capernaum. And so many people wanted to hear him that the house filled right up. People squeezed into every room. They stood shoulder to shoulder, packed all the way to the walls, and a big crowd spilled out the door and into the street. There was not one inch of space left.

Now, in that same town there lived a man who could not walk. His legs did not work, and so he spent his days lying on a mat, and he could not go anywhere unless someone carried him.

But this man had four wonderful friends, who loved him dearly. And when they heard that Jesus — who made sick people well — was right there in their town, they knew exactly what to do. "Let's take him to Jesus!" they said. "Jesus can help him!"

So the four friends gathered around their friend's mat. Each one took hold of a corner — and together they lifted him up and carried him, all the way through the streets, to the house where Jesus was.

But when they arrived, their hearts sank. The house was so crowded they could not get in. They pushed and they waited, but there was no way through the door, and no way even to get near a window. The crowd was simply too big.

Now, most people might have sighed, and turned around, and carried their friend back home. But not these four. They loved their friend far too much to give up that easily. They put their heads together — and they had a daring idea.

Very carefully, they carried their friend up the outside stairs to the flat roof of the house. And then they got to work. They began to dig — scratch, scratch, scrape — pulling away the mud and the branches, making a hole right through the roof. Bits of dust drifted down onto the crowd below! They worked and worked until the hole was big enough. And then, with strong ropes tied to each corner of the mat, they slowly, gently lowered their friend down — down, down, down — right into the middle of the room, and set him softly on the floor, right at Jesus' feet.

Jesus looked up. He saw the man lying on the mat. And he saw four eager, dusty faces peering down hopefully through the hole in the roof. And Jesus smiled, because he could see how much these friends believed.

"My friend," Jesus said gently to the man, "your sins are forgiven. And now — get up. Pick up your mat, and walk home."

And the man felt something wonderful happen. Strength came flooding into his legs! He wiggled his toes. He bent his knees. And then, slowly, he stood right up — he could walk! He bent down, rolled up the very mat he had been lying on, tucked it under his arm, and walked straight out through the crowd.

Everyone gasped, and then they cheered, and they praised God, saying, "We have never seen anything like this!" And high up on the roof, four good friends laughed and hugged each other for joy. They had refused to give up — and their love had carried their friend all the way to Jesus.
