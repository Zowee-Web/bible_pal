Friends Through the Roof

One day, Jesus was teaching inside a house in the town of Capernaum. Word had spread that he was there — Jesus, who told such wonderful things about God, and who made sick people well — and so the people came. They came from all over town, and from the villages round about, until the little house was full to bursting. People squeezed into every room and stood shoulder to shoulder, packed all the way to the walls. More crowded around the doorway, and still more filled the street outside, everyone leaning in to hear. There was not one inch of space left anywhere.

Now, in that same town there lived a man who could not walk. His legs did not work, and so he spent his days lying on a mat, and he could not go anywhere at all unless someone carried him.

But this man had four wonderful friends, who loved him dearly. And when they heard that Jesus was right there in Capernaum, their hearts leaped. "Let's take him to Jesus!" they said to one another. "If anyone can help him, Jesus can!"

So the four friends came and gathered around their friend's mat. Each one took hold of a corner. And together — one, two, three, lift! — they hoisted him up and set off through the streets, carrying him carefully all the way to the house where Jesus was.

But when they finally arrived, their hearts sank right down. The house was packed so full that they could not get anywhere near the door. They pushed, and they waited, and they tried to squeeze through — but there was simply no way in. No door. No window. No gap at all.

Now, this is the moment when most people would have given up. They might have sighed, and said, "Oh well, we tried," and carried their friend all the way back home. But not these four. They loved their friend far, far too much to turn around now. So they put their heads together — and they came up with a bold and daring plan.

Very carefully, still carrying the mat between them, the four friends climbed the outside stairs that led up to the flat roof of the house. And once they were up on top, they got to work. They began to dig — scratch, scratch, scrape — pulling away the packed mud and the branches the roof was made of. Little bits of dust came drifting down onto the heads of the people below! But the friends kept right on digging, making the hole bigger and bigger, until at last it was just big enough.

Then they tied strong ropes to the four corners of the mat. And slowly, and gently, and very carefully, they began to lower their friend down through the hole — down, down, down — past all the surprised, upturned faces of the crowd, until the mat came to rest softly on the floor, right in the middle of the room, right at Jesus' feet.

Everyone went quiet. Jesus stopped, and looked down at the man lying on the mat before him. Then he looked up — and there, peering down hopefully through the hole in the roof, were four dusty, eager faces. And Jesus smiled, because he could see, plain as day, how much these friends loved their friend, and how much they believed.

"My friend," Jesus said gently to the man on the mat, "your sins are forgiven." And then he said, "Now — get up. Pick up your mat, and walk home."

And the man felt it happen. Warmth and strength came flooding into his legs, all the way down to his toes. He wiggled his feet. He bent his knees. And then, in front of everyone, he stood right up — he could stand! He could walk! Hardly able to believe it, he bent down, rolled up the very mat he had been lying on, tucked it under his arm, and walked — walked! — straight out through the crowd, which parted in amazement to let him pass.

The whole house erupted. People gasped, and laughed, and cheered, and praised God, saying, "We have never, ever seen anything like this!" And high up on the roof, four good friends threw their arms around each other and laughed for pure joy. They had refused to give up — and their love had carried their friend all the way to Jesus. And the man walked home that day on his own two feet, with the happiest heart in all of Capernaum.
