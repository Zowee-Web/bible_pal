The Big Picnic

One day, a great big crowd of people followed Jesus up a grassy hillside. There were so many of them that no one could count — mothers and fathers, grandmas and grandpas, and ever so many children.

All day long, Jesus taught them and healed them. But as the sun began to dip low, the people grew hungry, and there was no food anywhere to be found.

"How will all these people ever eat?" the helpers asked. "We have nothing to give them."

Then a little boy stepped forward. In his hands he held his own small lunch — five little loaves of bread and two little fish. "You may have mine," he said. It was not very much at all. But the boy gave everything he had.

Jesus smiled and took the little lunch. He lifted it up, and he thanked God for it. And then — wonder of wonders — Jesus began to share it out.

He gave, and he gave, and somehow the food did not run out. The helpers passed it through the crowd, and everyone ate until their tummies were full. All those thousands of people, fed from one boy's little lunch!

And when everyone had finished, there was still food left over — twelve whole baskets of it!

The people could hardly believe it. And somewhere in that happy crowd, one little boy watched with wide and shining eyes — his own small lunch had fed them all. From one small gift, given with a glad heart, God had made far more than enough for everyone.
