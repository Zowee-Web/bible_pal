The River That Stood Still

After many long years, God's people had finally come to the edge of the new land God had promised them. It was a beautiful land, just across the river. But oh, what a river it was! The Jordan River was deep, and fast, and very, very wide. How would they ever get across?

God had a plan. He spoke to their leader, Joshua. "Tell the priests to carry the holy ark, and to step right into the river. And watch what I will do."

Now, this took great courage. The water was deep and fast! But the priests trusted God. They lifted up the ark, and they walked straight toward the rushing river. And the very moment their feet touched the water —

the river stopped.

It truly did! The rushing water piled up high in a heap, far upstream, and stood still, like a great wall of water waiting. And right in front of everyone, the riverbed turned to dry ground!

So all the people walked across — every family, every child, every animal — straight through the middle of the river, on dry land, while the water waited on either side. Not a single foot got wet!

When everyone was safely across, Joshua had twelve strong men each carry a big stone up from the riverbed. They stacked the stones together to make a special marker. "Whenever your children ask, 'What are these stones for?'" said Joshua, "you can tell them: this is where God made a way for us, right through the river."

And the people never forgot. When you trust God, and take that first step, he can make a way where there seems to be no way at all.
