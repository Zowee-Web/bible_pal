Abraham and the Stars

Abraham loved God, and he tried to follow him in everything. But Abraham carried a quiet sadness in his heart. He and his wife, Sarah, had grown old together, and they had never had a child of their own. More than anything, Abraham longed for a family — and he had begun to wonder if it would ever, ever happen.

One night, God spoke gently to Abraham. "Do not be afraid, Abraham," God said. "Come outside with me."

So Abraham stepped out of his tent, into the cool night air. Above him, the sky was deep and dark and quiet.

"Look up, Abraham," said God. "Look up at the sky. Now — count the stars, if you can."

So Abraham tipped his head back and looked up. And oh, the stars! There were thousands upon thousands of them, scattered all across the great dark sky, shining and twinkling — far, far too many to ever count. He could not even begin.

"That," said God softly, "is how big my promise to you is. One day, your family will be as many as those stars in the sky."

Abraham stood there in the dark, gazing up at all those countless, shining stars. It seemed impossible. And yet — God had said it. And so Abraham believed him, deep in his heart. He trusted that God would keep his wonderful promise.

And from that night on, whenever Abraham looked up at the stars, he remembered: God had made him a promise as big as the whole night sky.
