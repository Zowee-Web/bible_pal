Gideon's Three Hundred

There was once a man named Gideon, and God gave him a very big job. A huge enemy army had come to hurt God's people, and God chose Gideon to lead his people to safety.

So Gideon gathered an army. But it was a small army — and the enemy's army was enormous, spread across the valley like a great swarm of grasshoppers. Gideon felt afraid.

Then God said something surprising. "Gideon, your army is too big."

Too big? But Gideon already felt he had far too few! Yet God wanted everyone to see that the victory would come from him, and not from a great big army. So God made Gideon's army smaller, and smaller, until only three hundred men were left.

Just three hundred — against thousands and thousands!

But God had a plan. He told Gideon to give each man a trumpet, and a clay jar, with a burning torch hidden inside.

That night, the three hundred crept quietly all around the enemy camp in the dark. And then, all at once, Gideon gave the signal.

They smashed their jars — CRASH! — and the torches blazed out bright in the darkness. They blew their trumpets as loud as they could. And they shouted together with all their might!

The enemy army woke up in a terrible fright. There were lights, and trumpets, and shouting all around them in the night! They were so frightened and confused that they turned and ran away, every last one of them.

And God's people were safe — not because they were strong, but because God was with them.

So when something feels too big for you, little one, remember Gideon's three hundred. You do not need to be the biggest or the strongest. You only need to know that God is with you.
