The Thankful Leper

In those days, there were ten men who all shared the very same sad sickness. It made their skin hurt — but the hardest part of all was this: they were not allowed to live in their towns, or to stay with their own families. So the ten of them lived together, out on their own, far away from everyone they loved. They kept one another company as best they could, but day after day, oh, how they longed to go home.

One day, wonderful news came down the road to them: Jesus was coming this way — Jesus, the one who made sick people well! The ten men hurried as close as they were allowed to go. And from far down the road, they lifted up their voices and called out to him all together, as loud as they possibly could. "Jesus! Jesus, please help us!"

Jesus stopped. He looked down the road at the ten men standing far away, and his heart filled with kindness for them. "Go," he called back to them, "and show yourselves to the priests in the village." (For in those days, it was the priest who looked at a person and said when they were truly well again.)

Now, the men were not well yet — but they trusted what Jesus had told them, and so they turned and set off toward the village. And as they walked along the road — oh, wonderful! — their skin began to change. The sores faded away. Their skin grew clean, and smooth, and healthy and new. Every single one of them was healed! They stopped, and stared at themselves, and stared at each other, hardly able to believe it. They were well! They could go home to their families at last!

Nine of the men were so excited to get home that they ran off down the road and never once looked back.

But one man stopped, right there in the middle of the road. His heart was so full, and so thankful, that he could not take another step without saying so. So he turned all the way around, and he ran back to Jesus, praising God at the top of his voice the whole way. And when he reached him, he knelt down low at Jesus' feet. "Thank you!" he cried. "Oh, thank you, thank you!"

Jesus smiled down at him, warm and glad. "Weren't all ten of you made well?" he asked gently. "Yet you are the one who came back to give thanks to God." Then he said to the man, "Stand up now, and go on your way. Your faith has made you well."

So the thankful man rose to his feet and hurried off home — home to his family at last — carrying two wonderful gifts that day: clean, healthy skin, and a heart that was full, all the way to the very top, with joy. And for the rest of his days, wherever he went, he loved to tell people about the time Jesus made him well.
