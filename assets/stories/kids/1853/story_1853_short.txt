The Thankful Leper

One day, as Jesus was walking toward a village, ten men called out to him from far away. All ten of them had a sickness that made them very sore and very sad, and they had to live apart from everyone else. When they saw Jesus coming, they cried out together, "Jesus! Jesus, please help us!"

Jesus looked at them with kindness. "Go," he told them, "and show yourselves to the priests in the village."

So the ten men set off down the road. And as they walked — oh, wonderful! — their skin grew well and clean and healthy again. Every single one of them was healed! They could go home now, back to their families. They could hardly believe it.

Nine of the men hurried off, so excited to get home that they did not look back.

But one man stopped right where he was. His heart was so full of thankfulness that he simply had to tell Jesus. So he turned around, and he ran all the way back, praising God at the top of his voice. He knelt down at Jesus' feet. "Thank you!" he said. "Oh, thank you!"

Jesus smiled down at him warmly. "Weren't all ten of you made well?" he said. "Yet you are the one who came back to give thanks to God." And he said to the man, "Stand up now, and go on your way. Your faith has made you well."

And the thankful man went home that day with two wonderful gifts — well, healthy skin, and a heart full of joy.
