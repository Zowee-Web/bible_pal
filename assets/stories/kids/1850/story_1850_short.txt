The Last Supper

It was a special evening, and Jesus gathered his closest friends together for supper, in a quiet upstairs room. Soft lamplight glowed on the table, and they were all together, the way they loved to be.

Jesus knew that very soon, he would have to go away from his friends for a while. And he loved them so much — he loved them all the way to the end. So he wanted to give them something to hold onto, something to help them remember him whenever he was gone.

During the supper, Jesus picked up a loaf of bread. He gave thanks to God, broke the bread, and shared a piece with each of his friends. Then he took a cup, gave thanks again, and passed it around the table for everyone to share.

"Whenever you eat this bread, and drink this cup," Jesus told them gently, "remember me. Remember that I love you, and that I am always with you."

His friends did not understand it all yet. But they could feel how much love was in the room that night. They ate together, and they were close, and it was peaceful and good.

And ever since that evening, all around the world, people who love Jesus still share bread and a cup together — just the way he showed them — to remember him, and to remember that he loves them still.

So whenever you feel far away from Jesus, you can remember it too: he loves you, and he is always near.
