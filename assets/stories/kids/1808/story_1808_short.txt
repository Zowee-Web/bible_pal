Noah's Rainbow

For many days and nights, the rain had fallen, and Noah's great big boat had floated safely on the wide, wide water. Inside, Noah and his family and all the animals were warm and dry and safe.

Then one morning, Noah noticed something. The rain had stopped. The sky was quiet. And the boat had come to rest on the top of a mountain.

Noah wanted to know if the water had gone down, so he opened a window and let a little dove fly out. The dove flew far and wide, but it found no place to land, so it came back. Noah gently took it in.

Seven days later, he sent the dove out again. This time it came back carrying a fresh green leaf in its beak! Noah smiled. Somewhere out there, trees were growing again.

He waited seven more days and sent the dove once more. This time it did not come back at all — because now there was dry land, and a whole new world for the little dove to live in.

So Noah opened the great door, and out they all came — Noah and his family, and the animals two by two, blinking in the bright sunshine.

And then God did something beautiful. He stretched a rainbow across the sky, in red and orange and yellow and green and blue and purple.

"This rainbow is my promise," God said. "Whenever you see it, remember: I will always care for the earth and everything in it."

And to this very day, when the rain clears and a rainbow shines, it is God keeping his promise still.
