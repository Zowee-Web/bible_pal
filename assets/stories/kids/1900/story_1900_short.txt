The Stairway to Heaven

Jacob was on a long journey, all alone. He had left his home far behind, and he had a long, long way still to go. As the sun went down, he found himself out in the wild, with no house, and no bed, and no one to keep him company. He felt very small, and very far from home.

Night was falling. So Jacob found a smooth stone, and he rested his head upon it, like a pillow. And there, under the wide and starry sky, he fell fast asleep.

And as he slept, Jacob had a wonderful dream.

He dreamed he saw a great stairway, rising up from the ground, all the way up to heaven. And on the stairway, shining angels of God were going up, and coming down, up and down, between heaven and earth. And at the very top stood God himself.

And God spoke to Jacob, with a voice full of love. "I am with you," God said. "I will watch over you, wherever you go. And one day, I will bring you safely home again. I will never, ever leave you."

When Jacob woke up, he was filled with wonder. "God was here," he said softly, "in this very place — and I did not even know it!" He no longer felt alone, or afraid. For now he knew that God was with him.

So wherever you are, little one — even far from home, even in the dark — you are never, ever alone. God is right there with you. He watches over you all through the night, and he will never leave your side.
