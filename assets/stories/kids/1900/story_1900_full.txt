The Stairway to Heaven

Jacob was on a long, long journey, all by himself. He had said goodbye to his mother and father, and left his home far behind, to travel to a faraway land where some of his family lived. It was a long way to go — many days of walking — and Jacob had set out all alone.

As the first day drew to a close, the sun sank low, and the sky began to grow dark. Jacob looked around. He was out in the open countryside, with no town nearby, no house, no warm bed, and no one at all to keep him company. He felt very small under the great wide sky, and very far away from everyone he loved.

But night was coming, and he had to sleep. So Jacob found a smooth stone, and he set it down, and rested his head upon it, like a pillow. And there, beneath the deep dark sky with all its twinkling stars, Jacob closed his tired eyes, and fell fast asleep.

And as he slept, Jacob dreamed the most wonderful dream.

He dreamed that he saw a great stairway. It rose up from the ground, right beside him, and went up, and up, and up — all the way into heaven itself. And on that shining stairway were the angels of God! They were going up, and coming down, up and down, between heaven and earth, bright and beautiful. And there, at the very top of the stairway, stood God himself.

And God spoke to Jacob. His voice was strong, but oh, so full of love. "Jacob," he said, "I am the Lord, the God of your father and your grandfather. And I want you to know this: I am with you. I will watch over you, wherever you go. I will never leave you, not for one moment. And one day, I will bring you safely back home again."

Then Jacob woke up. The stars were still shining above him, and the world was quiet. But everything felt different now. Jacob was filled with wonder, right down to his toes. "God was here," he whispered, looking all around. "God was in this very place — and I did not even know it!"

He was not lonely anymore. He was not afraid. For now he knew, deep in his heart, that he was not traveling alone after all. God was with him, every step of the way.

In the morning, Jacob took the stone he had used for a pillow, and he stood it up tall, like a little monument, to remember that special place. "This," he said, "is the house of God." And then he went on his way — no longer alone, and no longer afraid, because God was with him.

So wherever you find yourself, little one — even far from home, even in the dark of night — you are never, ever alone. God is right there beside you, just as he was with Jacob. He watches over you all through the night. And he will never, ever leave your side.
