The Shepherds and the Angels

On the very night that baby Jesus was born in Bethlehem, not far away, some shepherds were out on the dark hillsides, watching over their sheep. It was the middle of the night. The world was quiet and still. The stars twinkled high above, and the woolly sheep lay sleeping all around, while the shepherds kept careful watch so that no harm would come to them.

These shepherds were ordinary, hard-working people. They were not rich. They were not famous. But God had chosen them, of all the people in the world, to be the very first to hear the wonderful news.

For all at once, a bright light came shining in the darkness! An angel of God appeared, glowing like the morning sun, right there in the sky above the shepherds. They had never in all their lives seen anything so dazzling and so wonderful, and they trembled, and were filled with fear.

But the angel spoke to them in the kindest, gentlest voice. "Do not be afraid!" the angel said. "For I have come to bring you good news — the best news there has ever been — and it is news of great, great joy, for you and for all people! This very night, in the town of Bethlehem, a baby has been born. He is the one God promised long, long ago — a Savior, come to bring love to the whole wide world. And here is how you will know him: you will find the little baby wrapped up snug and warm, and sleeping in a manger."

And then came the most wonderful thing of all.

Suddenly, the whole sky was full of angels! Not one, not two, but more than anyone could ever count — a great shining army of them, filling the heavens from end to end. And all together they began to sing, the most beautiful song that ever was. "Glory to God in the highest!" they sang. "And peace on earth, and goodwill to all!" The music was so lovely that it seemed as if the stars themselves had joined in.

Then, as softly as they had come, the angels went back up into heaven, and the hillside was quiet once more.

The shepherds looked at one another with wide and shining eyes. "Come!" they said. "Let us go straight to Bethlehem, and see this wonderful thing that has happened!" So they hurried down the hills, as fast as they could go. And there, just as the angel had promised, they found him — little baby Jesus, asleep in the manger, with Mary and Joseph close beside him.

The shepherds knelt down softly, full of wonder. And then they went back to their hills, telling everyone they met along the way about the baby and the angels — their hearts full to bursting with joy.
