The Shepherds and the Angels

On the very night that baby Jesus was born, some shepherds were out on the hills near Bethlehem, watching over their sheep. It was dark and quiet, and the stars were twinkling, and the sheep were sleeping softly all around them.

All at once, a bright light shone in the sky! An angel appeared, glowing like the morning, right there above the shepherds. They had never seen anything so wonderful, and they were filled with fear.

But the angel spoke to them in a kind and gentle voice. "Do not be afraid!" the angel said. "For I bring you good news — the best news of all — and it is news of great joy! Tonight, in Bethlehem, a baby has been born. He is the one God promised long ago, come to save the whole world. You will find him wrapped up warm, sleeping in a manger."

And then — oh, the most wonderful thing of all! Suddenly the whole sky filled up with angels, too many to count, shining and singing together. "Glory to God!" they sang. "And peace on earth!" The music was so beautiful it seemed the very stars were singing.

When the angels had gone back up into heaven, the shepherds looked at one another with wide and shining eyes. "Come!" they said. "Let us go and find this baby!" So they hurried off to Bethlehem, and there they found him — little baby Jesus, asleep in the manger, just as the angel had said.

And the shepherds went back to their hills, telling everyone they met along the way, their hearts full to bursting with joy.
