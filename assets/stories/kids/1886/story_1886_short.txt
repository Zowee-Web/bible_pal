Bread from Heaven

After God led his people out of Egypt, they traveled through a great, dry desert. There were no shops, and no gardens, and no food anywhere. The people grew hungry, and they began to worry. "What will we eat out here?" they cried.

But God heard them. And God said, "Do not worry. I will send you bread from heaven."

The very next morning, when the people woke up and peeked out of their tents, they saw something strange. All over the ground, covering everything like a soft white frost, were thin little flakes — small and round and white, sparkling in the morning sun.

"What is it?" the people asked one another. They had never seen anything like it!

"It is the bread God has sent you," said Moses. "Go and gather it up."

So they did, scooping it up by the handful. And when they tasted it — mmm! It was lovely, sweet like honey. Soon the whole camp was full of happy, munching sounds. God told them to gather just enough for that day, and no more. And every single morning, there it was again — fresh bread from heaven, waiting on the ground, enough for everyone.

The people called it manna. And God fed them with it, morning after morning, all the way through the desert. He never forgot them. Not even once.

So you do not have to worry about tomorrow, little one. The same God who sent the manna knows exactly what you need. And he gives us what we need, gently, one day at a time.
