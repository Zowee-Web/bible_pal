The Girl at the Well

A servant set out on a long, long journey, with ten camels and a very important job to do. His master, Abraham, had sent him far away to find a wife for his son Isaac — a kind woman who loved God. But how in the world would the servant ever know which one she was?

So when he reached a town at the end of his long journey, he stopped beside the well where the women came to draw water. And he prayed.

"God," he said quietly, "please help me. Let it happen like this: I will ask a girl for a drink of water. And if she is the right one, let her say, 'Yes — and I will bring water for your camels too.'"

He had hardly finished praying when a girl named Rebekah came walking up with her water jar balanced on her shoulder.

The servant asked her, "Please, may I have a little drink of water?"

"Of course you may!" said Rebekah, and she gave him a cool drink right away. And then she looked over at his ten thirsty camels, and she said, "I will draw water for your camels too, until every one of them has had enough."

Now, ten camels drink a LOT of water! But Rebekah hurried back and forth, again and again, filling the trough, until every last camel had drunk its fill. What a kind and willing heart she had.

The servant could hardly believe it. It was the very thing he had prayed for — almost word for word. God had answered him! God had led him, across all those long miles, straight to the right girl.

And so Rebekah went with the servant, back across the desert, and became Isaac's wife. God had been guiding every single step, the whole way along.

God is like that. He leads us, and he listens when we pray — and sometimes he answers in the kindest, most wonderful ways.
