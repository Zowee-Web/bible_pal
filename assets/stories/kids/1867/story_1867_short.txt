Don't Worry, Look at the Birds

One day, Jesus sat with the people on a grassy hillside. He could see that many of them were worried about all sorts of things. So he wanted to help their worried hearts feel lighter. And to do it, he pointed to the world all around them.

"Look at the birds," Jesus said. And everyone looked up. The little birds were swooping and singing in the bright blue sky. "The birds do not plant gardens. They do not gather food into barns. And yet, not one of them goes hungry, and not one of them is ever forgotten."

Then Jesus pointed to the flowers, growing wild in the green grass. "Look at the lilies," he said. "They do not rush about, and they do not worry. And yet — see how lovely they are! Not even a king, in his finest, most beautiful robes, was ever dressed as wonderfully as one little wildflower."

Then Jesus said, gently: "If God gives the birds their food, and dresses the flowers so beautifully, then think of how much he loves you. You are more precious to him than all the birds and all the flowers in the world."

"So you do not have to carry your worries all by yourself," Jesus said. "When something feels too big, you can hand it over. God is with you. God sees you. And God cares about every little thing in your life."

The birds did not worry. The flowers did not worry. And you, little one, are loved even more than they are.
