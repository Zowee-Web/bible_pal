Don't Worry, Look at the Birds

One day, Jesus sat down with the people on a soft, grassy hillside, with the warm sun above and the wide world spread out all around. As he looked at the crowd, he could see that many of them carried worries in their hearts. Some were worried about food. Some were worried about clothes. Some were worried about tomorrow. So Jesus wanted, more than anything, to help their worried hearts feel lighter. And he did it in the gentlest way — he simply showed them the world God had made.

"Look at the birds," Jesus said.

And everyone turned to look. All around, little birds were swooping and darting and singing through the bright blue sky. "Think about the birds for a moment," Jesus said. "They do not plant gardens. They do not gather up food and store it away in barns for later. They wake up each morning with nothing saved at all. And yet — have you ever seen a little bird go hungry? God knows every one of them. God feeds every one of them. And not a single sparrow is ever, ever forgotten."

Then Jesus pointed to the flowers, growing wild and bright among the green grass.

"And look at the lilies," he said. "Look how they grow. They do not rush about. They do not work, and they do not worry about a thing. And yet — just see how beautiful they are! Not even a great king, dressed up in his very finest, most dazzling robes, was ever as lovely as one of these little wildflowers. God dresses each tiny flower with such tender care — and a flower is here today, and gone tomorrow."

Then Jesus looked at the people, and his voice was very gentle and kind.

"Now," he said, "if God feeds all the little birds, and if God dresses even the wild grass and flowers so beautifully — then think, dear ones, of how very much he must love you. You are far, far more precious to God than the birds, and more precious than all the flowers in the world."

"So you do not need to carry your worries all by yourself," Jesus said. "Worry is a heavy thing to hold. But you were never meant to hold it alone. When something feels too big, or too scary, you can hand it over. God is with you. God sees you. And God cares about every single thing in your life, even the small ones."

The birds in the sky did not worry. The flowers in the grass did not worry. They simply woke each morning and lived their small lives, trusting the God who made them and watched over them all the day long.

And you, little one — you are loved even more than the birds and the flowers. So you can let your worries go soft, and rest in the arms of the One who loves you, and who is with you, always.
