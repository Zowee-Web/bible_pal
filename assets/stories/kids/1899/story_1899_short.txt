The Day the Sun Stood Still

Joshua was the brave leader of God's people. One day, some of their friends were in great danger — a powerful enemy had come to attack them. So they sent a message to Joshua: "Come quickly! Help us!"

Joshua did not wait. He gathered his people and marched all through the night to come to the rescue. And God told him, "Do not be afraid. I am with you, and I will help you."

And God did help, in amazing ways. When Joshua and his people arrived, the enemy was so frightened that they turned and ran. God even sent great hailstones tumbling down from the sky to chase them away! God was fighting for his people.

But the day was passing, and the sun was sinking low. Soon it would be dark, and there was still more to be done. So Joshua did a very bold thing. In front of everyone, he prayed out loud to God. "Sun," he called, "stand still! Stay right where you are!"

And do you know what happened? The sun stopped. It truly did! The sun stood still in the sky, and the day stretched on, and on, and on — longer than any day before or since — until at last God's people were completely safe.

There had never been a day like it, when God listened to the voice of one man and held the very sun still in the heavens.

So nothing is too hard for God, little one — nothing at all. The same God who once stopped the sun in the sky is listening, even now, to you.
