The Day the Sun Stood Still

Joshua was the brave and faithful leader of God's people. He loved God, and he listened to him, and God was always with him. He had led the people safely across rivers and into a wonderful new land, and through it all, God had never once let him down.

Now, one day, some friends of God's people were in terrible danger. A group of powerful kings had joined their armies together and come to attack them. The frightened friends sent an urgent message to Joshua. "Please!" it said. "Come quickly, and help us! Do not leave us on our own!"

Joshua did not wait a single moment. He gathered all his people, and they marched the whole night long, up the hills in the dark, to come to the rescue of their friends. And as they went, God spoke to Joshua and gave him courage. "Do not be afraid of them," God said. "I am with you, and I myself will help you. Not one of them will be able to stand against you."

And God kept his word in the most amazing ways. When Joshua and his people came up at dawn, the enemy was thrown into such a fright that they turned and ran away as fast as they could go. And then God did something wonderful: he sent great hailstones tumbling down from the sky, driving the enemy far away. God himself was fighting for his people that day.

But there was a problem. The day was wearing on. The sun was beginning to sink down toward the hills, and soon it would be night — and the work of keeping the people safe was not yet finished.

So Joshua did a wonderfully bold thing. Right there in front of all his people, he lifted his eyes to the sky, and he prayed to God out loud. "O sun," he cried, "stand still! And moon — you stop too! Stay right where you are in the sky!"

And — can you believe it? — the sun stopped.

It truly did! The sun stood perfectly still, high in the sky, and it did not go down. The day stretched on, and on, and on — longer than any day that had ever been before. The shadows did not move, and the evening simply waited. The light lingered for hours and hours, until at last God's people were completely safe, and every bit of the danger was past. Every one of them was safe and sound, just as God had promised.

There had never, ever been a day like it — a day when God listened to the prayer of one man, and held the very sun still in the heavens. For God was fighting for his people with all his great and wonderful power.

So nothing is ever too hard for God, little one — nothing at all. The very same God who once stopped the sun in the sky still hears every prayer, and he is listening, even now, to you.
