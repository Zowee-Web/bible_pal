God Calls Samuel

Long ago, a boy named Samuel lived and helped in the house of God. He slept there at night, in a quiet little room, close to an old priest named Eli. Samuel was still very young, but he loved to help, and kind old Eli looked after him.

One night, as Samuel lay resting and the lamp of God glowed soft and golden, he heard a voice call his name.

"Samuel!"

He jumped up and ran to Eli. "Here I am! Did you call me?"

But Eli said, "No, my child, I did not call. Go back and lie down." So Samuel did.

"Samuel!" the voice called again.

And again the boy hurried to Eli. And again the old man said gently, "I did not call you, my son. Lie back down."

It happened a third time. And now old Eli understood: it was God who was calling the boy. "Go and lie down," he told Samuel kindly. "And if the voice comes again, say this: 'Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening.'"

So Samuel went back, and lay down in the quiet, and waited.

And the voice came once more, soft in the dark. "Samuel. Samuel."

"Speak," said Samuel. "For your servant is listening."

And God spoke to him there in the stillness. From that night on, all through his life, Samuel listened for God's voice. He grew up to be one of God's greatest helpers — the boy who first learned to listen, simply by saying, "Here I am."
