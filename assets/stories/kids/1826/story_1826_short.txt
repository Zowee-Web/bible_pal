The Lost Sheep

High on the green hills, under a wide blue sky, a shepherd watched over his sheep. He had one hundred of them, woolly and white, and he knew and loved every single one.

Every evening, the shepherd counted his sheep as they came home. "One, two, three..." all the way up to one hundred. But one evening, as he counted, something was wrong. "Ninety-eight... ninety-nine..." And then no more. One little sheep was missing.

Now, the shepherd did not say, "Oh well. I still have ninety-nine." No — that one little sheep mattered to him. So he made sure the others were safe and warm, and then he set out into the hills to find the one who was lost.

The sky grew dark, and the wind grew cold. But the shepherd did not give up. He climbed over rocks and searched through the brambles, calling out into the night. "Where are you, little one?"

And then, at last, he heard it — a small, frightened bleat, far down in a thicket. There was his little sheep, tangled and afraid and all alone.

"There you are," the shepherd said softly. "I found you."

He did not scold the little sheep, not even once. He gently lifted it up, and laid it across his shoulders, and carried it all the way home — close, and warm, and safe.

And when he reached home, he called out to everyone, "Be glad with me! My sheep that was lost is found!"

When Jesus had finished the story, he explained what it meant. "God loves you," he said, "just like that good shepherd loved his one little sheep. So if you ever wander far, or feel lost, or all alone — remember this: God will come looking for you, and looking, all through the hills, until you are found, and gathered up, and carried safely home."
