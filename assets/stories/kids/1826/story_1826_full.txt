The Lost Sheep

One day, to help people know how much God loves them, Jesus told a story. It went like this.

High on the green hills, under a wide blue sky, there lived a shepherd who watched over his sheep. He had one hundred of them, woolly and white, and he knew and loved every single one. He knew the ones who liked to run ahead, and the ones who liked to stay close, and the little ones who were still learning to walk on wobbly legs.

Every evening, when the sun grew low and golden, the shepherd led his sheep back home to the fold. And as they passed through the gate, he counted them, every one. "One, two, three..." all the way up to one hundred.

But one evening, as he counted, something was wrong. "Ninety-seven... ninety-eight... ninety-nine..." And then no more. The shepherd counted again, slowly. It was true. One little sheep was missing.

Now, the shepherd could have said, "Oh well. I still have ninety-nine. That is plenty." But he did not say that. Because that one little sheep mattered to him, every bit as much as all the others. He could not rest, not while one of his own was lost and alone in the dark.

So he made sure the ninety-nine were safe inside the fold. And then, as the sky grew dark and the wind grew cold, he set out into the hills to find the one who was lost.

He climbed up rocky paths. He pushed through prickly brambles. He looked behind every bush and down into every shadow, calling out as he went. "Where are you, little one? Where are you?"

On and on he searched, and he did not give up.

And then, at last — he heard it. A small, frightened bleat, coming from far down in a tangled thicket. The shepherd hurried toward the sound, and there was his little sheep, caught in the branches, trembling and afraid.

"There you are," the shepherd said softly, kneeling down beside it. "I found you."

He did not scold the little sheep, not even once. He simply reached out his strong, gentle hands, and lifted it free, and laid it across his shoulders. And he carried it all the way home — close, and warm, and safe — while the little sheep rested its tired head against him.

And when the shepherd reached home, he was so happy that he called out to all his friends and neighbors. "Be glad with me!" he said. "I have found my sheep that was lost!"

When Jesus had finished the story, he told everyone what it meant. "God loves you," he said, "just like that good shepherd loved his one little sheep. Even if you wander far away, God will never forget you. God comes looking, and looking, all through the hills — until you are found, and lifted gently onto his shoulders, and carried safely home."
