A House for God

King Solomon wanted to do something wonderful for God. "God has been so good to us," he said. "I will build him a temple — a beautiful house, where everyone can come to worship him and be near him."

So Solomon set to work, and it was the grandest building anyone had ever seen. Workers brought tall cedar trees from faraway forests, and great blocks of stone, and shining gold. They carved flowers, and palm trees, and angels into the walls. They covered the inside with pure gold, until the whole temple gleamed. It took seven long years to build, but Solomon did not hurry. He wanted everything to be just right, and beautiful, for God.

At last, the temple was finished. And the people gathered from all over the land for a great celebration. They sang, and they played music, and they thanked God with all their hearts.

Then something amazing happened. A bright cloud came and filled the temple — and the people knew that God himself had come to be with them, in the beautiful house they had built for him.

Solomon was so thankful. He lifted up his hands and prayed, "O God, there is no one like you. Thank you for being so good to us, and for coming to live here, close to your people."

So whatever you can give to God, little one — your time, your songs, your love — give it with a thankful heart. For God loves to come close, and to make his home with those who love him.
