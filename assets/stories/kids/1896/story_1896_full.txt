A House for God

King Solomon's father, King David, had once dreamed of building a special house for God. But God had said, "Not you, David — but your son will build it." And now David's son, Solomon, was king. And Solomon remembered.

"God has been so good to us," Solomon said. "He has given us this beautiful land, and watched over us all our days. So now I will build him a temple — a grand and beautiful house, where all the people can come to worship him, and to be near him."

So Solomon began the greatest building project anyone had ever seen. From the faraway forests, workers cut down tall, sweet-smelling cedar trees, and floated them down the sea on great rafts. From the hills, they cut huge blocks of fine white stone. And Solomon gathered gold, and silver, and precious jewels.

Then, stone by stone, the temple began to rise. And it was magnificent. Skilled workers carved beautiful pictures into all the walls — flowers in bloom, and tall palm trees, and angels with outstretched wings. Then they covered the carvings over with pure, shining gold, until the whole inside of the temple gleamed and glowed like sunshine. Everything, from the doors to the floors, was made as beautiful as it could possibly be — for it was a house for God.

It took seven long years to finish. But Solomon never rushed, not once. He wanted every single part to be just right. Not one corner, and not one carving, would be left plain or ordinary — for this was the house of God.

And at last — at last — the great temple was done.

Then Solomon called all the people together, from every corner of the land, for an enormous celebration. The priests carefully carried in the holy ark of God and set it in its special place. Trumpets sounded, and singers sang, and music filled the air, as everyone gave thanks to God together. It was a day of pure and shining gladness.

And then, something wonderful happened. A bright cloud came down and filled the whole temple — so full of God's glory that the priests could hardly stand. And all the people understood: God himself had come. He had come to be with them, in the beautiful house they had built for him.

Solomon's heart overflowed. He stood before all the people, and he lifted up his hands to heaven, and he prayed. "O God," he said, "there is no one in all the world like you. You keep your promises, and your love never ends. Thank you for your goodness to us. And thank you for coming to live here, so close to your people."

So whatever you have to give to God, little one — your time, or your songs, your kindness, or your love — give it gladly, and give it your best, with a thankful heart. For there is nothing God loves more than to come close, and to make his home with those who love him.
