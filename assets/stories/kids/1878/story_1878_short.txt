The Donkey Who Talked

Once there was a man named Balaam, and Balaam had a donkey. She was a good and faithful donkey, who had carried him for many, many years.

One day, Balaam climbed onto his donkey and set off down the road. But what Balaam did not know was this: God had sent an angel to stand in the road ahead, to stop him from going the wrong way.

Now, Balaam could not see the angel. But the donkey could!

When the donkey saw the angel standing there, shining and bright, she was afraid. She did not want Balaam to be hurt. So she stopped right where she was, and she would not take another step.

Balaam did not understand. He could not see what the donkey could see. He grew frustrated, and he gave her a little smack to make her go. But still the donkey would not move.

And then — the most surprising thing happened. God let the donkey speak!

"Balaam," said the donkey, "why are you cross with me? Haven't I always been your good donkey? Have I ever, ever done this before?"

Balaam was so astonished that he answered her right back! And just then, God opened Balaam's eyes — and at last he saw the angel standing in the road.

"Your donkey saved you," the angel told him. "She could see what you could not."

Balaam was very sorry then. And he was very thankful for his faithful donkey.

So you see, little one, God can use anyone, and anything, to help us — even a donkey! And it is always, always good to stop, and look, and pay attention.
