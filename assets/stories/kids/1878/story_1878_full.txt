The Donkey Who Talked

Once, long ago, there was a man named Balaam, and Balaam had a donkey. She was a good and faithful donkey, who had carried Balaam safely along the roads for many, many years. He rode her everywhere he went.

One morning, Balaam climbed up onto his donkey's back, and the two of them set off down the road together. But there was something Balaam did not know. God had sent one of his angels to stand in the road up ahead — a shining angel, sent to stop Balaam from going the wrong way.

Now, here is the curious thing. Balaam, riding along, could not see the angel at all. But the donkey — the donkey could see him perfectly!

When the donkey saw the angel standing in the middle of the road, she was frightened, and she did not want her master to be hurt. So she turned aside, off the road and into a field. Balaam did not understand why. He tugged her back onto the path.

A little farther on, the angel stood in the road again, in a narrow place between two walls. The donkey saw him, and squeezed over to one side to stay away, right up against the wall. Still Balaam could not see a thing.

And a third time, the angel stood in the road, in a place so narrow there was no way around at all. This time the donkey simply lay down, right there beneath Balaam. She would not budge.

Poor Balaam grew very frustrated. He could not see the angel, and he could not understand why his donkey kept stopping. He gave her a little smack to make her get up and go.

And then — the most surprising thing in all the world happened.

God let the donkey speak!

"Balaam," said the donkey, turning her head to look at him, "why are you cross with me? Haven't I always been your good and faithful donkey, all these years? Have I ever once behaved like this before?"

Balaam was so astonished that he answered her right back, as if talking donkeys were the most ordinary thing in the world! "No," he admitted. "You never have."

And right then, God opened Balaam's eyes. And at last — at last — he saw the angel standing there in the road, shining and bright. Balaam gasped, and he bowed his head down low.

"Your donkey saved you today," the angel said gently. "Three times she turned away, because she could see me, even though you could not. If she had not stopped, you might have walked right into trouble."

Balaam was very sorry then for being cross with her. And oh, how thankful he was for his faithful, clever donkey, who had seen what he could not, and had kept him safe.

So you see, little one — God can use anyone, and anything, to watch over us and to help us. Even a humble little donkey! And it is always, always a good and wise thing to stop, and look, and pay attention.
