The Tiny Mustard Seed

One day, Jesus held up something so small you could barely see it. It was a tiny mustard seed, smaller than a crumb, smaller than the tip of your littlest finger.

"Let me tell you about this little seed," Jesus said.

A man once took a mustard seed, just like this one, and planted it in his garden. He pressed it down into the dark, soft earth, and he covered it over, and he waited.

For a while, nothing seemed to happen at all. The little seed lay hidden in the ground, where no one could see it.

But deep down in the dark, something wonderful was beginning. A tiny green root reached down. A tiny green shoot reached up. And one morning, a little sprout pushed its way out of the soil and into the sunshine.

The sun shone, and the rain fell, and the little sprout grew. It grew, and it grew, and it grew some more. Days became weeks, and weeks became months, and the tiny seed became the biggest plant in the whole garden — a great, tall tree, with strong branches spreading wide and high.

And do you know what happened then? The birds of the sky came flying. They built their nests in its branches, and they rested in its cool green shade, and they sang.

"That is what God's kingdom is like," said Jesus, smiling. "It begins as small as a seed. But God makes small things grow into something great and good and beautiful."
