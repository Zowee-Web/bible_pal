David Plays for King Saul

In a grand palace, there lived a king named Saul. He had everything a king could want — but often, his heart felt sad and troubled, and he could not find any peace. On those days, a heavy gloom would settle over him, and nothing seemed to help.

His servants wanted to help their king feel better. "Music might soothe him," they said. "Let us find someone who plays beautifully." And someone remembered a shepherd boy named David, who played the harp out on the hills, and sang gentle songs to God.

So they sent for David. And David came to the palace, with his harp in his hands.

Whenever the sadness came over King Saul, David would sit nearby, and he would begin to play. His fingers moved softly over the strings, and out floated the most gentle, beautiful music — the very same tunes he had played to his sheep, under the wide and quiet sky.

And as David played, something wonderful happened. The king's tight shoulders loosened. The heavy gloom lifted, little by little, like clouds drifting away from the sun. Saul closed his eyes, and breathed slowly, and his troubled heart grew calm and peaceful once more.

For there was something in David's music — something gentle, and good, and full of God's own peace — that quieted the king and made him feel better. And so, whenever Saul was troubled, David would play. And the soft music would fill the room, and peace would come stealing quietly back in.
