The Fiery Furnace

Long ago, in a faraway land, there lived three friends who loved God with all their hearts. Their names were Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. They lived far from home, in the land of a powerful king — but even there, they never forgot to worship God.

Now, the king had built an enormous golden statue. It was tall as a tree and shining in the sun, and the king was very proud of it. He gathered all the people together and gave a command. "When you hear the music play," he said, "everyone must bow down to my golden statue. And anyone who will not bow will be thrown into a blazing-hot furnace!"

The music played. And all across the land, the people bowed down low to the ground.

But not the three friends. They would only ever worship God, and no statue of gold could take God's place. So while everyone else was bowing, the three friends quietly stayed standing, tall and still.

When the king heard, he was furious. He called the three friends before him. "I will give you one more chance," he warned them. "Bow down to my statue — or into the fire you go. And what god could ever save you then?"

The three friends were not angry, and they did not run away. They stood together, and they answered the king calmly. "Our God is able to save us," they said. "But even if he does not, we want you to know — we will still worship only him. We will never bow to your statue of gold."

This made the king angrier than ever. He ordered the furnace made hotter, and the three friends were placed inside.

But then the king looked into the fire, and what he saw made him leap up from his throne. He could hardly believe his eyes. "Did we not throw three men into the fire?" he cried out. "But look — I see four! Four men, walking around in the flames, and they are not hurt at all! And the fourth one shines like an angel!"

For God had not left his friends alone in the fire. God was right there in the flames, walking beside them, keeping them safe and unafraid.

"Come out!" called the king. "Come out, servants of the Most High God!" And the three friends walked out of the furnace, safe and well. Their hair was not burned, their clothes were not scorched — there was not even the smell of smoke upon them.

The king looked at the three friends, and then he praised their God. "Wonderful is the God of these three," he said softly, "who sent his help, and stayed with them, and kept them safe."
