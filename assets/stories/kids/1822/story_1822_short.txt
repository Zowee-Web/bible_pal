The Fiery Furnace

Long ago, in a faraway land, there lived three friends who loved God with all their hearts. Their names were Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego.

Now, the king of that land had built an enormous golden statue, tall as a tree and shining in the sun. "Everyone must bow down to my statue," the king commanded. "Anyone who will not bow will be thrown into a blazing-hot furnace!"

But the three friends would only ever worship God. So when everyone else bowed down low, the three friends quietly stayed standing.

The king was furious. "Bow down," he warned them, "or into the fire you go!"

The three friends were not angry, and they did not run. They simply said, "Our God is able to save us. But even if he does not, we will still worship only him. We will never bow down to a statue of gold."

So the three friends were placed into the great hot furnace. But then the king looked closer, and he leaped up from his seat, amazed. "Did we not throw in three?" he cried. "But I see four men walking in the fire — and they are not hurt at all!"

For God had not left his friends alone in the flames. God was right there in the fire with them. And the three friends walked out again, safe and well, without even the smell of smoke upon them.

"Praise the God of these three," said the king in wonder, "who sent help and kept them safe."
