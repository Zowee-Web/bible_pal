The Wise and Foolish Builders

One day, Jesus told a story about two people who each wanted to build a house of their very own.

The first builder was wise. Before she built anything at all, she walked about, looking carefully for the right place. At last she found it: a big patch of solid rock. Now, building on rock is hard work. She had to dig down deep, and chip and dig and dig, and it took a long, long time. Day after day she worked, laying her house down firm upon the stone. Sometimes she grew tired. But she wanted her house to last.

The second builder was in a hurry. He did not want to work for very long. So he found a smooth, easy patch of soft sand, right down near the beach. "This will be quick and simple," he said. And it was! He did not have to dig at all. In no time, his house was finished, and he sat back to rest in the sunshine while the wise builder was still hard at work.

For a while, the warm and sunny days went by. And if you had walked past, both houses would have looked just fine. The one on the rock, and the one on the sand — both with their doors and their windows and their little roofs.

But then, one dark night, a great storm came rolling in.

The rain came pouring down, drip and splash and pour. The wind began to howl, and it pushed and pushed against the walls. And the water rose up higher and higher, swirling all around both houses.

The wise builder's house, way up high on the solid rock, did not move at all. The rain beat down and the wind blew hard, but the house stood firm, and strong, and dry. Everyone inside stayed warm and cozy and safe, and they were not afraid.

But the house on the sand had nothing solid underneath it to hold it up. As the water rushed and swirled, it began to wash the soft sand right out from under the house. And slowly — quietly — with no one in it, the little house leaned, and slipped, and washed away down the beach.

At last the long night ended. The storm blew itself out, and the morning sun peeked over the hills. And there, high on the rock, the wise builder's house was still standing, just as strong as ever.

Then Jesus told everyone what the story meant. "When you hear my words, and you do them," he said gently, "you are like that wise builder. You are building your life on something strong — so that whenever the storms of life come, you will stand firm and steady."
