The Wise and Foolish Builders

One day, Jesus told a story about two people who each wanted to build a house.

The first one was wise. She looked and looked until she found a big patch of solid rock. It was hard work to build there, and it took a long, long time. But she dug down deep, and she built her house strong, right on top of the rock.

The second one was in a hurry. He found a smooth, easy patch of sand near the beach. "This will be quick," he said. And it was! In no time at all, his house was finished, and he sat back to rest while the wise builder was still working away.

For a while, the sunny days went by, and both houses looked just fine.

But then, one night, a great storm came. The rain poured down. The wind howled and pushed. The water rose up higher and higher, swirling around both houses.

The wise builder's house, way up on the rock, did not move at all. It stood firm and strong and dry, and everyone inside stayed cozy and safe.

But the house on the sand had nothing solid to hold it. The rushing water washed the sand right out from under it, and slowly, quietly, the little house slipped away.

When the morning came and the sun peeked out, the house on the rock was still standing.

"Listen to my words, and do them," said Jesus, "and you will be like the wise builder — strong and steady, even when the storms come."
