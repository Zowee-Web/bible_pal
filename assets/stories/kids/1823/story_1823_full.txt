God Calls Abraham

Long, long ago, there lived a man named Abraham. He had a home, and a wife named Sarah, and he lived in the land where he had grown up, where everything was familiar and known.

One quiet day, God spoke to Abraham.

"Abraham," God said, "I want you to do something big and brave. I want you to leave your home, and your town, and the land you have always known. Go to a new land — a land that I will show you. And do not be afraid, for I will take care of you. I will bless you, and I will make your name great. I will give you a great big family, with more children and grandchildren than you could ever count — as many as the stars in the sky. And through your family, I will send a blessing to every person in the whole wide world."

It was a wonderful promise. But there was one part Abraham did not know. God did not tell him exactly where the new land was. He did not point to it on any map, or show him the whole long road, or tell him how many days it would take. God simply said, "Come. Follow me. And I will show you the way as you go."

Now, that is not an easy thing — to set out when you cannot see where you are going. But Abraham believed that God was good, and that God would keep his promise. And so Abraham trusted God.

He gathered his wife, Sarah, and their animals, and packed up everything they owned. And they set out together, leaving the only home they had ever known. They did not know exactly where they were headed. They only knew that God was leading them, and that God would be with them every single step of the way.

So they traveled. They walked over rolling hills and across wide, open lands. They set up their tents at night under the great wide sky, full of more stars than they could ever count — and Abraham remembered God's promise. And in the morning, they would pack up and follow God a little farther.

And at last, after their long journey, they came to the place God had been leading them to all along. It was a good land, with room to grow. "This is the land," God told him gently. "I am giving it to you, and to your family after you."

Abraham looked around at the new land that would become his home, and his heart was full and thankful. He had not been able to see the way ahead — not any of it. But God had seen it the whole time. And God had been right beside him, every step, all along the way.
