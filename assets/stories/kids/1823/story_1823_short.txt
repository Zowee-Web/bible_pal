God Calls Abraham

Long, long ago, there lived a man named Abraham. One quiet day, God spoke to him.

"Abraham," God said, "I want you to leave your home, and the land you have always known. Go to a new land — a land that I will show you. And do not be afraid, for I will bless you. I will give you a great big family, with more children and grandchildren than you can count. And through your family, I will bring a blessing to the whole world."

Now, God did not tell Abraham exactly where this new land was. He did not show him the whole long way, or how the journey would end. God only said, "Come. Follow me. I will show you."

And Abraham trusted God. He did not know the way ahead, but he knew the One who was leading him.

So Abraham gathered his wife, Sarah, and all that they had, and they set out. They did not know just where they were going. They only knew that God was leading them, and that God would be with them every step.

They traveled over hills and across wide, open lands. And at last they came to the place God had been leading them to all along. "This is the land," God told him. "I am giving it to your family."

Abraham looked around at the new land, and he was thankful. He had not been able to see the way ahead. But God had seen it. And God had been with him the whole way.
