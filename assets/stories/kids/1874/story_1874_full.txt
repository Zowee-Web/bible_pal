Esther Becomes Queen

Once there was a girl named Esther.

She did not live in a grand palace. She did not wear fine clothes or a golden crown. Esther was an ordinary girl, who lived in an ordinary house, far from any throne. But Esther had something far more beautiful than fine clothes — she had a kind and gentle heart.

Esther had no mother or father of her own to care for her. But she was not alone. She had a cousin, a good and faithful man named Mordecai, who took her in and raised her like his very own daughter. He loved her dearly, and Esther loved him.

Now, in the land where Esther lived, there was a great and powerful king. And one day, the king decided it was time to choose a new queen. So he sent his helpers all across the kingdom, into every town and along every street, searching for the kindest and loveliest young woman in all the land, to come and live at the palace.

And in their searching, the king's helpers came upon Esther. "Surely," they said, "this one must come to the palace."

So Esther was taken to the king's palace. And oh — what a place it was! Esther had never seen anything like it in all her life. There were enormous doors, taller than three men standing one upon another. There were floors of shining marble, smooth and cool. There were golden lamps that filled every room with a soft, warm glow. And there were robes — beautiful robes, made of the finest, softest cloth, in colors like jewels — and they were given to Esther to wear.

But here is the truth: it was not Esther's beautiful robes that made her special. It was her heart. She was humble, and gentle, and kind to everyone she met. And everyone who met her could not help but love her.

At last, the day came when Esther was brought before the king himself. And the very moment the king saw her — her kind face, her gentle ways — he loved her more than any other. And so the king took a golden crown, and he placed it gently upon Esther's head, and he made her his queen.

Just think of it! Only a little while before, Esther had been an ordinary girl, in an ordinary house, with no palace and no crown at all. And now, here she stood — Queen Esther.

But there is something even more wonderful in this story. Something nobody knew yet — not the king, not even Mordecai, and not even Esther herself.

God had placed Esther in that palace for a reason.

One day soon, her people would be in great danger. And brave Queen Esther — kind, gentle Esther — would be the very one God would use to help save them all. God had a plan for her, a brave and beautiful plan, that was waiting just up ahead.

But that, little one, is a story for another day.
