Esther Becomes Queen

Once there was a girl named Esther.

She did not live in a palace. She did not wear fine robes. Esther was an ordinary girl, with a kind and gentle heart, who was cared for by her cousin, a good man named Mordecai. He loved her like his very own daughter.

Now, in that land, there lived a king. And the king was looking for a new queen. So all across the kingdom, his helpers searched, looking for the loveliest and kindest young woman in all the land. And one day — they found Esther.

They brought her to the palace. Oh, what a place it was! There were great tall doors, and shining marble floors, and golden lamps that glowed. Esther was given beautiful robes to wear, soft and fine, more lovely than anything she had ever owned.

But it was not only Esther's pretty face that shone. It was her kind heart. Everyone who met her loved her at once. She was gentle, and good, and lovely all the way through.

And when at last the king met Esther, he loved her more than anyone else of all. So he placed a golden crown upon her head, and he made her his queen.

Just imagine it! An ordinary girl, who only yesterday had no palace and no crown — and now she was Queen Esther.

Nobody knew it yet. Not even Esther herself. But God had placed her in that palace for a special reason. God had a plan for Esther — a brave and wonderful plan — that was still waiting, just up ahead.

But that, little one, is a story for another day.
