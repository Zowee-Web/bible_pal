Mary Says Yes

In a little town called Nazareth, there lived a young woman named Mary. She loved God with a quiet and gentle heart.

One day, while Mary was busy with her chores at home, God sent an angel named Gabriel to visit her. The angel appeared right there in her small, plain room, and its walls shone bright with his light. Mary had never seen anything like it, and she did not know what to think.

"Greetings, Mary!" the angel said. "You are dear to God, and the Lord is with you." Mary did not understand, and she wondered what such a greeting could mean.

"Do not be afraid, Mary," the angel said kindly. "God is very pleased with you. I have wonderful news. You are going to have a baby — a very special baby. He will be God's own Son. He will be very great, and his kingdom of love will never, ever end. And you will name him Jesus."

Mary's eyes grew wide. "But how can this be?" she wondered softly.

"With God," the angel said, "nothing is impossible at all."

Mary was quiet for a long moment, thinking it over in her heart. It was a very big thing the angel was asking. But Mary trusted God with all her heart. And so she gave her answer.

"I am God's servant," she said. "Let it happen just as you have said." And she smiled.

The angel left her then. And Mary stood quietly in the little room, holding the most wonderful secret in all the world — that God's own Son was coming, and that she had said yes.
