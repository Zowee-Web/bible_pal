The Friends at Emmaus

On the very same day that the tomb was found empty, two of Jesus' friends set out walking along a road that led to a village called Emmaus. It was a long walk, and as they went, their heads were down and their hearts were heavy and sad. For Jesus, whom they had loved with all their hearts, had died. They had heard a strange and wonderful rumor that morning — that his tomb was empty — but they did not understand it at all, and so they walked along feeling lost, and confused, and very sorrowful.

As they walked, a stranger came up the road and began to walk along beside them. "What are you talking about as you walk?" he asked them gently. "And why do you look so sad?"

The two friends stopped, surprised. "Have you not heard?" they said. And then they poured out the whole story — all about Jesus, the wonderful things he had done, how he had died, and how they had hoped he was the one who would save them. They told him about the empty tomb, too, and how they did not know what to think.

The stranger listened to it all. And then he began to speak. Starting from long, long ago, he gently explained all the promises God had made — all the clues, scattered through the old, old stories, about a Savior who would come. On and on he talked as they walked together, and the more he spoke, the more something warm and glowing stirred inside the two friends' hearts — though they could not have told you why.

At last they came near to the village of Emmaus. The stranger looked as though he meant to keep on walking, further down the road. But the friends did not want him to go. "Please, stay with us," they begged him. "It is nearly evening, and the day is almost over. Come and have supper." So the stranger agreed, and he came inside with them.

And then, at the supper table, something happened that they would never, ever forget. The stranger reached out and picked up the loaf of bread. He held it, and gave thanks to God, and broke it — and handed a piece to each of them.

And in that instant — their eyes were opened. They knew him! This was no stranger at all. It was Jesus! Their own dear friend, alive again, sitting right there at their own table!

But the very moment they recognized him — he was gone from their sight.

The two friends turned and stared at each other, their hearts pounding. "It was Jesus!" they said. "It was Jesus, all along! Didn't our hearts feel warm inside while he talked with us on the road? He was walking right beside us the whole time, and we didn't even know it!"

They were far too full of joy to stay and finish their supper. They leaped up from the table, and even though it was getting dark, they hurried straight back down the long road to the city — running, almost flying — to find the others and tell them the most wonderful news of all: Jesus is alive! We have seen him! He walked with us, all the way home.
