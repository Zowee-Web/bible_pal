The Friends at Emmaus

Two of Jesus' friends were walking along a road toward a village called Emmaus. Their heads were down, and their hearts were heavy and sad. For Jesus, whom they loved so much, had died — and even though they had heard a strange and wonderful rumor that his tomb was empty, they did not understand it, and they were full of sorrow.

As they walked, a stranger came and began to walk along beside them. "Why are you so sad?" he asked gently.

So the two friends told him everything — all about Jesus, and all that had happened. And then the stranger began to talk. Starting long, long ago, he explained all the things God had promised about a Savior. And as he spoke, something warm began to glow inside the friends' hearts, though they could not say why.

When they reached the village, the stranger looked as if he would keep walking on. "Please, stay with us," the friends said. "It is getting late." So he came inside to share supper with them.

And at the table, the stranger picked up the bread. He gave thanks to God, and he broke it, and he handed it to them — and all at once, their eyes were opened. They knew him! It was Jesus! Their dear friend, alive again, right there at their table!

And in that very moment, he was gone from their sight.

"It was Jesus, all along!" they cried. "He was walking with us the whole way!" And they were so full of joy that they jumped right up and ran, all the way back, to tell everyone the wonderful news.
