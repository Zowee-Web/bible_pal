Esther's Brave Choice

In a faraway land, in a palace full of marble floors and golden lamps and curtains the color of the sky, there lived a young queen named Esther.

She wore beautiful robes, and servants hurried to help her, and everyone bowed low when she walked by. But Esther carried a secret in her heart — one that not even the king knew. Esther was one of God's people, who had come from a faraway home to live in this land.

One day, dreadful news came to the palace. A proud and powerful man named Haman had made a cruel plan. He did not like God's people, and he wanted to harm all of them — every family, in every town, all across the kingdom. And that meant Esther's own people, and her own family too.

Esther's cousin Mordecai sent her an urgent message. "Esther," it said, "you must go to the king and beg him to save our people. Who knows? Maybe God made you queen for a moment exactly like this one."

But Esther was afraid, and with good reason. There was a strict rule in the palace: no one — not even the queen — was allowed to walk into the king's throne room unless the king had called for them first. To go without being called could mean great danger.

Esther sat very still and thought for a long time. Her heart felt small and frightened. But slowly, a quiet courage began to grow inside her.

"I will go to the king," she said at last. "Even though I am scared. And if it goes badly — well, I will go anyway."

But first, Esther did the most important thing. She prayed. And she asked all of her people to pray along with her, for three whole days.

Then, on the third day, Esther stood up tall. She put on her finest royal robes. She took a deep breath. And she walked, step by step, into the great throne room, where the king sat high upon his throne.

Her heart was pounding like a drum.

The king looked up — and when he saw his queen standing there, he smiled. He reached out and held his golden scepter toward her. In the palace, that meant the most welcome thing of all: Come close. I am glad you are here.

So Esther came near, and she told the king everything — about the cruel plan, and about her people in danger. And the king listened. He stopped the cruel plan completely, and he kept Esther's people safe and sound.

One young queen — frightened, but brave enough to go anyway — had helped to save them all. And God had placed her in that palace, all along, for just such a time as this.
