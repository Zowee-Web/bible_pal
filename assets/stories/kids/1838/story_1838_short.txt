Esther's Brave Choice

In a grand palace, full of marble floors and golden lamps, there lived a young queen named Esther. She wore beautiful robes, and everyone bowed when she passed. But Esther had a secret that even the king did not know: she was one of God's people.

One day, terrible news reached her. A proud man in the palace had made a cruel plan. He wanted to harm all of God's people, everywhere in the land — and Esther's own family too.

Esther's cousin Mordecai sent her a message. "You must go to the king and ask him to help," he said. "Perhaps this is the very reason you became queen — for a time just like this."

But Esther was afraid. There was a rule in the palace: no one was allowed to come to the king unless he called for them — not even the queen. To go without being called could be very dangerous.

Esther thought and thought. Then she made up her mind. "I will go to the king," she said. "And if it is frightening, I will go anyway." First she prayed, and asked all her people to pray with her.

Then, on the third day, Esther put on her royal robes and walked into the great throne room. Her heart was pounding. The king looked up — and he smiled. He held out his golden scepter to her, which meant: welcome, come close.

So Esther told the king everything. And the king stopped the cruel plan, and kept her people safe.

One brave queen, who was afraid but went anyway, had saved them all. And God had placed her there, all along, for just that moment.
