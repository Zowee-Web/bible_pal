The Army That Sang

One day, frightening news came rushing to King Jehoshaphat. Messengers hurried in, out of breath: "A huge army is marching toward us! A great crowd of soldiers, far too many to count — and they are almost here!"

The king was afraid. And all his people were afraid too. What could they possibly do against an army so big?

But King Jehoshaphat knew exactly what to do with his fear. He called for all the people to come together — every single one of them, from every town in the land. Mothers and fathers came. Grandparents came. Little children came, and tiny babies in their parents' arms. And there they all stood together before God, and they prayed.

"O God," prayed the king, and he was honest about it, "we do not know what to do. This army is far too big for us. But our eyes are on you. We are looking to you to help us."

And God answered them. The Spirit of God came upon a man right there in the crowd, and he spoke God's own words out loud, so all could hear. "Listen, all of you! Do not be afraid. Do not be frightened of that great big army. For this battle is not yours. It belongs to God. You will not even need to fight. Just go out tomorrow, and stand still, and watch what God will do for you."

So the very next morning, the people did something no army had ever done before. King Jehoshaphat did not put his strongest soldiers out in front. He put the singers out in front! And as all the people walked out toward that huge and frightening army, they did not tremble, and they did not turn and run. They opened their mouths, and they sang. They sang out loud and clear to God: "Give thanks to the Lord, for his love goes on forever and ever!"

And here is the wonderful thing. At the very moment they began to sing, God set to work. He took care of that whole enormous army, all by himself, in his own way. And by the time God's people came over the last hill, the danger was gone. Completely gone. They had not had to lift a single finger. They had only had to trust God — and sing.

There was so much left behind by that scattered army that it took God's people three whole days just to gather up all the good things. And on the fourth day, they gathered in a valley and praised God together until the hills rang.

Then they marched all the way back home, singing the whole way, their happy voices ringing out, thanking God with all their hearts. And every country round about heard how God himself had fought for his people.

When something feels too big, and too scary to face, you do not have to face it on your own. You can do what they did. You can look to God, and trust him — and let him fight for you.
