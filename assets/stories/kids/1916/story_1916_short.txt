The Army That Sang

Frightening news reached King Jehoshaphat one day: a huge army was marching toward his land. Far too big to fight. The king was afraid, and so were all his people.

But King Jehoshaphat knew exactly what to do with his fear. He gathered everyone together — mothers and fathers, little children, tiny babies — and they all stood before God and prayed. "We don't know what to do," the king said honestly. "But our eyes are on you, God. We are looking to you."

And God answered them. "Do not be afraid," God said. "Do not be frightened of that great big army. For this battle is not yours. It is mine. Stand still, and watch what I will do."

So the next morning, the people did something no one had ever done before. King Jehoshaphat put the singers right out in front. And as the people walked out toward that huge army, they did not tremble, and they did not run away. They sang. They sang out loud to God: "Give thanks to the Lord, for his love goes on forever and ever!"

And here is the wonderful thing. The very moment they began to sing, God went to work. He took care of that whole enormous army, all by himself. By the time God's people got there, the danger was gone. Completely gone. They had not had to lift a single finger. They had only had to trust him, and sing.

So they sang even louder, all the way home, their happy voices ringing out across the hills, thanking God with all their hearts.

When something feels too big, and too scary to face, you do not have to face it on your own. You can do what they did. You can look to God, and trust him — and let him fight for you.
