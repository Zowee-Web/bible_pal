David the Shepherd Boy

Out in the green hills near Bethlehem, where the grass grew soft and the wind smelled of flowers, a boy named David watched over his father's sheep.

David was the youngest of eight brothers — the smallest, the last, the one who was usually left to mind the lambs while the older boys did the big and important jobs. But David did not mind at all. He loved the wide-open fields. He loved the sheep, every woolly one of them, and he knew each by name. When a little lamb wandered too far and got stuck, David climbed after it and carried it home safe in his arms. And on the long, quiet afternoons, he played his harp and sang soft songs to God, just the two of them under the great blue sky.

Now one day, something special happened. A wise old man named Samuel came walking all the way to David's house. God had whispered a secret to Samuel. "I have chosen a new king for my people," God had said. "You will find him in this very family."

So Samuel asked to meet David's brothers. One by one, they came and stood before him. The very first was tall and strong and handsome. Surely, thought Samuel, this must be the king God means! But God said quietly, "No, Samuel. People look at the outside — how tall, how strong, how grand a person seems. But I do not look there. I look at the heart."

So another brother came. And another. Seven big brothers in all. And each time, God gently said, "No. Not this one."

Samuel was puzzled. "Are these all your sons?" he asked.

"There is still the youngest," said the father. "But he is only a boy. He is out in the field, watching the sheep."

"Send for him," said Samuel.

So they called David in from the hills. He came with grass on his clothes and sunshine on his cheeks, his harp still in his hand. And the moment he stepped inside, God spoke softly to Samuel: "Stand up. This is the one. This is him."

Samuel lifted a horn full of sweet-smelling oil, and poured it gently over David's head. It ran warm and shining down his hair.

God had chosen the shepherd boy — the smallest one, the one nobody else had even thought to call inside. Because long before this day, in all those quiet hours out in the field, God had been watching the love inside David's heart.
