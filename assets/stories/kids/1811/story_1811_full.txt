The Burning Bush

Out in the wild desert, far from any town, a shepherd named Moses was leading his sheep. He had been a shepherd for many years, and he knew these quiet hills well. On this day, he led his flock all the way to the foot of a tall mountain.

It was an ordinary day — until Moses saw something that made him stop and stare.

A bush, off among the rocks, was on fire. Bright orange flames leaped and danced all over it. But the strangest thing was this: the bush did not burn up. The flames burned and crackled, and yet the little bush stayed green, its leaves whole and bright.

Moses rubbed his eyes. "How wonderful," he said softly. "I have never seen anything like it. I must go closer and see why this bush does not burn up."

But as he stepped toward it, a voice called out from the very middle of the fire.

"Moses! Moses!"

Moses froze where he stood. "Here I am," he answered.

"Do not come any closer," said the voice, gently but clearly. "Take off your sandals, for the ground where you are standing is holy ground." And in that moment, Moses understood: this was God himself, speaking to him from the fire. Moses quickly covered his face, for he was afraid to look.

"I have seen my people, far away in Egypt," God said. "I have seen that they are sad and hurting, and I have heard them cry. I am going to rescue them. And Moses — I am sending you to help set them free."

Moses could hardly believe it. "But who am I?" he whispered. "I am only a shepherd. How could I do such a big thing?"

"Do not be afraid," said God, kindly. "You will not go alone. I will be with you — I will be with you the whole way."

Then Moses asked one more question. "When your people ask me your name, what shall I tell them?"

And God gave Moses something very special — his own name. "My name is Yahweh," God said. "Tell them I have sent you." It was God's holy name.

So Moses slipped off his sandals and stood quietly on the holy ground. The road ahead would be long, and Moses still felt small. But he was not alone — for God had promised to go with him, every single step of the way.
