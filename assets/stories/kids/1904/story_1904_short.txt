The Jar That Never Ran Empty

In a time when no rain had fallen for a long, long while, food had become very hard to find. And in a little town, there lived a poor woman, a widow, with her young son. They had almost nothing left to eat.

One day, the prophet Elijah came walking into her town, tired and hungry from his journey. He saw the woman gathering sticks, and he asked her, kindly, "Could you please make me a little bread to eat?"

The woman's eyes filled with tears. "Oh, sir," she said, "I would gladly — but I have almost nothing. Just a tiny bit of flour in the bottom of my jar, and a little drop of oil. I was gathering these few sticks to make one last small meal for my son and me. After that, there is nothing left at all."

Elijah looked at her gently. "Do not be afraid," he said. "Go and make your little meal. But first, make a small loaf for me, and bring it out. For God has made a promise: your jar of flour will not run empty, and your jug of oil will not run dry, until the rains come again."

It was a big thing to ask. But the woman trusted God. So she went, and she made a little loaf, and she shared it with Elijah first.

And do you know what happened? When she went back to her jar — there was still flour inside! And still oil in the jug! However much she used, there was always enough for the next day. The flour never ran out. The oil never ran dry. Day after day, there was food for the woman, and her son, and Elijah too.

She had shared the very last of what she had — and God made sure she always had enough.

So when you share, little one, even when you do not have very much, you can trust God. He sees what you give. And he loves to fill an empty jar.
