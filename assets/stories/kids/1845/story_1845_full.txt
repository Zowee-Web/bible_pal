The Holy Spirit Comes

After Jesus rose from the dead and went back up to heaven, his friends were left wondering what would happen next. But Jesus had given them a wonderful promise before he went. "Wait here in the city," he had told them. "I am going to send you a special helper — the Holy Spirit — who will be with you always, and give you courage."

So Jesus' friends stayed together in Jerusalem. They waited, and they prayed, and they wondered when the helper would come.

Then one morning, it happened. All at once, a sound came from heaven — a sound like a great rushing wind — and it filled the whole house where they were sitting, from floor to ceiling. And then they saw something wonderful: little flames of fire, soft and bright and warm, came and rested gently on each one of them. One small flame for every single friend.

The Holy Spirit filled them up, right down to their toes, with joy and courage. And suddenly, they found they could speak in languages they had never even learned!

Now, Jerusalem was crowded that day with visitors from many faraway lands, each speaking a different language of their own. When they heard the strange and marvelous sound, a great crowd came hurrying to see. And every person there — no matter where they were from — heard Jesus' friends telling about God's wonderful love in his very own language!

"How can this be?" the people asked, amazed. "How do we each hear them in the language we grew up speaking?"

Then a friend named Peter stood up tall. Once, Peter had been too afraid to even say that he knew Jesus. But now, filled with the Holy Spirit, he spoke to the whole enormous crowd with joy and courage, telling them the good news: that Jesus loved them, and was alive forever. He spoke so beautifully that day that thousands and thousands of people believed, and became friends of Jesus too.

It was the day the very first church was born. Not a building made of stone — but a great, big, happy family of people who love God and belong to each other.

And the best part of all? God's own Spirit had come to live with them, just as Jesus promised — to comfort them, and help them, and give them courage. He would never, ever leave them on their own again.
