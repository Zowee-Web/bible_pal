The Holy Spirit Comes

After Jesus went back to heaven, he gave his friends a wonderful promise. "Wait in the city," he told them. "I will send you a special helper — the Holy Spirit — to be with you always."

So Jesus' friends waited together, praying, wondering what would happen.

Then one morning, something amazing happened. A sound came from heaven, like a great rushing wind, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting. And then they saw little flames of fire, soft and bright, resting gently on each one of them — one flame for every friend.

They were filled with the Holy Spirit, right down to their toes. And suddenly they could speak in languages they had never learned before!

Now the city was full of visitors from faraway lands, all speaking different languages. When they heard the sound, a great crowd gathered. And every single person heard Jesus' friends telling about God's wonderful love — each one in his very own language! "How can this be?" they asked in amazement.

Then a friend named Peter stood up and told the whole crowd the good news about Jesus. He spoke with such joy that day that thousands of people believed and became friends of Jesus too.

It was the day the very first church was born — not a building, but a great big family of people who love God. And God's own Spirit had come to live with them, just as Jesus promised. He would never, ever leave them alone.
