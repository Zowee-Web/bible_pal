The Boy in the Temple

When Jesus was a boy — about twelve years old — he set off on a journey with his family. They were traveling to the great city of Jerusalem, for it was time for a special festival that came once every year. From all over the land, families were going up to the city to celebrate together. Jesus went along with his mother, Mary, and his father, Joseph, and they all had a wonderful time.

When the festival days were over, it was time to go home. Now, in those days, families traveled in big groups, all walking together along the road for safety — grandparents and parents, cousins and friends, and children running here and there among them. So when Mary and Joseph set out for home, they were not worried. They felt sure that Jesus was somewhere in the great crowd, walking with his cousins and his friends.

But that evening, when they stopped to rest, they went to find him — and Jesus was nowhere to be seen. They asked their relatives. They asked their friends. They looked all up and down the line of travelers. But Jesus simply was not there!

Oh, how their hearts filled with worry. So Mary and Joseph turned right around and hurried all the way back to Jerusalem, searching for their boy every step of the way.

For three whole days they looked. They searched the busy streets and the crowded markets. And then, at last, they came to the temple — the great and holy house of God. And there, inside, they found him.

There sat young Jesus, calm and happy, right in the middle of the wise old teachers. He was listening to them, and asking them deep questions, and answering their questions too. And all the teachers were simply amazed — for they had never met a boy who understood so much about God. They leaned in close, listening carefully to every single thing he said.

When Mary saw him, she rushed over, full of relief. "Oh, Jesus!" she said. "Your father and I have been searching everywhere! We were so worried about you."

But Jesus answered her gently, with a little wonder of his own. "Why were you searching for me?" he asked. "Didn't you know that I would be right here — in my Father's house?"

For even as a boy, Jesus loved God, his Father, more than anything in all the world. And there was nowhere he felt more at home than close to him.

Then Jesus went back home to Nazareth with Mary and Joseph, and he was a good and loving son to them. And he kept on growing — taller, and wiser, and closer to God, year after year.

So remember, little one: it is a good and beautiful thing to ask your questions about God, and to love being near him. Just like the boy in the temple, you are never too young, and never too small, to come close to the One who loves you.
