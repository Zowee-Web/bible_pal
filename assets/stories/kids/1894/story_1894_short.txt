The Boy in the Temple

When Jesus was a boy, about twelve years old, he traveled with his family to the big city of Jerusalem. It was time for a special festival, and crowds and crowds of people had come to celebrate. Jesus went with his mother, Mary, and his father, Joseph, and they had a wonderful time.

When the festival was over, everyone packed up to travel home. It was a great big group, walking together along the road — whole families, all mixed in together. Mary and Joseph thought Jesus was somewhere in the crowd, with his cousins and friends.

But when evening came, they looked for him — and Jesus was not there! They searched all through the group of travelers, but they could not find him anywhere. Oh, how worried they were! So they hurried all the way back to Jerusalem to look for their boy.

For three whole days they searched the city. And then, at last, they found him — right inside the temple, God's house. There sat young Jesus, calm and happy, among the wise teachers. He was listening to them, and asking them questions, and answering their questions too. And everyone was amazed at how much this boy understood about God.

"Oh, Jesus!" said Mary. "We have been so worried! We looked everywhere for you!"

But Jesus answered gently. "Why were you searching for me?" he said. "Didn't you know that I would be here, in my Father's house?"

For Jesus loved God, his Father, more than anything — and he loved being close to him.

So it is good to ask questions about God, little one, and good to love being near him. Just like the boy in the temple, you are never too young to come close to the One who loves you.
