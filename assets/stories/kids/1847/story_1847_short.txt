Elijah Fed by Ravens

A long, dry time had come to the land. The rain had stopped falling, and the streams grew small, and food became hard to find for everyone.

But God had not forgotten his helper Elijah. "Go and hide by the little brook out in the hills," God told him. "You can drink the cool water there. And do not worry about food — I have a surprise for you. I have told the ravens to bring it."

Now, ravens are big black birds. And what God said came true. Every morning, just as the sun came up, the ravens came flying down to Elijah — and in their beaks they carried bread and good food! They set it down gently beside him, and away they flew.

And every evening, as the sun went down, the ravens came again, carrying more bread and more food. Morning and evening, day after day, the birds brought Elijah just what he needed. And he drank the cool, clear water from the little brook.

So Elijah was never hungry, and he was never thirsty, even in that dry and difficult time. He sat by his brook in the quiet hills, and he watched the sky for his feathered friends, and his heart was thankful.

For God knew exactly where Elijah was. God knew exactly what he needed. And God took care of him in a way no one would ever have guessed — with a brook, and a little bread, and a flock of faithful black birds.
