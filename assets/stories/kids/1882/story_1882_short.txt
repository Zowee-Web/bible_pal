The Wish of a King

Solomon was a young man when he became king. He had a very big job ahead of him — to lead a whole nation of God's people. And sometimes he felt small, and unsure. "How can I do this?" he wondered. "I am so young."

One night, as Solomon slept, God came to him in a dream. And God said something amazing. "Solomon, ask me for anything you want, and I will give it to you. Anything at all!"

Imagine that! Solomon could have asked for anything. He could have asked to be rich, with mountains of gold. He could have asked to live for a hundred years. He could have asked to be the most powerful king in all the world.

But Solomon did not ask for any of those things.

Instead, he said, "O God, I am young, and this is such a big job. So please — give me a wise and understanding heart. Help me to know what is right and what is wrong, so that I can lead your people well."

God was so pleased! "Because you asked for this," God said, "and not for riches, or a long life, or power — I will give you a wise heart, wiser than anyone's. And I will give you the other things too — riches, and honor — even though you did not ask."

And God did just as he said. Solomon grew up to be the wisest king who ever lived. People came from faraway lands just to hear his wise words.

So you see, little one, of all the things a person could ever wish for, a wise and loving heart is one of the most precious of all. And God loves it dearly when we ask him for one.
