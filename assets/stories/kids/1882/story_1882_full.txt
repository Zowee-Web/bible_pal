The Wish of a King

Long ago, there was a young man named Solomon, and he had just become king. His father, David, had been king before him — but now David was gone, and the whole kingdom belonged to Solomon. It was an enormous job. Solomon would have to lead a great nation of God's people: to guide them, and care for them, and help them know right from wrong. And sometimes, when he thought about it all, Solomon felt very small. "I am so young," he worried. "How will I ever know how to do this?" He wanted so much to do it well, and to make God glad.

Then one night, while Solomon was fast asleep, God came to him in a dream. And God said something truly amazing. "Solomon," said God, "ask me for anything you wish — anything in all the world — and I will give it to you."

Just think of that! Anything at all! Solomon could have asked for so many things. He could have asked to be tremendously rich, with palaces full of gold and treasure. He could have asked to live a long, long life. He could have asked to be the strongest, most powerful king the world had ever known.

But Solomon did not ask for any of those things.

Instead, he thought about the great job God had given him. And he said, humbly, "O God, I am only young, and you have made me king over all these people — more people than anyone could ever count. So this is what I ask of you: please give me a wise and understanding heart. Help me to listen well, and to know what is right and what is wrong, so that I can lead your people the way you would want."

It was not a wish for himself at all — not for his own comfort, or riches, or glory. It was a wish to be good, and fair, and kind to all the people he had been given to care for.

And God was so very pleased with Solomon's answer.

"Because you asked for this," God said, "and did not ask for riches, or a long life, or power over others — but asked instead for a wise heart, to do what is right — I am going to give you exactly that. I will make you wiser than anyone who has ever lived. And what is more, I will also give you the things you did not even ask for: riches, and honor, all your life long."

And God did just as he had promised. Solomon grew up to be the wisest king the world had ever seen. People traveled from faraway lands just to hear his wise and gentle words, and to marvel at how God had blessed him.

So you see, little one, of all the things a person could ever wish for, a wise and loving heart is one of the most precious of all. And God loves it dearly when we come and ask him for one.
