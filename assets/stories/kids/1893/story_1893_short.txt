Deborah, the Wise and Brave

Long ago, God's people had a leader named Deborah. Now, Deborah was very wise — so wise that people came from far and wide to ask her what to do. She would sit beneath a tall palm tree, and listen, and help them solve their troubles. God had given Deborah a wise and understanding heart.

But in those days, God's people were in trouble. A powerful enemy, with a great army and many iron chariots, was making their lives hard and frightening. The people did not know what to do.

So God gave Deborah a brave and clever plan. She called for a man named Barak. "God has a job for us," she told him. "Gather our people together on the mountain. And do not be afraid — for God himself will go ahead of us, and he will keep us safe."

But Barak was nervous. "I will only go," he said, "if you come with me, Deborah."

"Then I will come," said brave Deborah. And she did.

So Deborah and Barak led God's people out together. And just as God had promised, he went ahead of them. A great storm came, and rain poured down, and the enemy's heavy chariots got stuck fast in the mud! The mighty enemy was beaten, and ran far away, and God's people were safe at last.

Then Deborah sang a happy song of thanks to God, for he had rescued them.

So you see, little one — God gives wisdom, and courage, to all kinds of people. Boys and girls, men and women. When God calls you to be brave, he will go ahead of you, every step of the way.
