Deborah, the Wise and Brave

Long, long ago, God's people had a wise and wonderful leader named Deborah. She was one of the judges — the special leaders God gave his people to help them. And Deborah was so very wise that people came from all over the land just to ask her advice. Every day she would sit beneath a tall palm tree, and people would come to her with their worries and their quarrels, and she would listen carefully, and help them know the right thing to do. God had given Deborah a wise and understanding heart, and she loved him with all of it.

But in those days, God's people were in great trouble. A powerful king nearby had a huge army, with hundreds of strong iron chariots, and he was making life hard and frightening for God's people. They felt small, and scared, and they did not know what to do. Year after year, they had hoped and prayed for someone to lead them to safety.

Then God gave Deborah a brave and clever plan. She sent for a man named Barak. "Listen," she told him. "God has spoken, and he has a job for us. Go and gather our people together up on the mountain. And do not be afraid — for God himself will go out ahead of us, and he will keep us safe."

But Barak felt nervous and unsure. "I will go," he said slowly, "but only if you come along with me, Deborah. I am braver when you are there."

Deborah smiled. "Then I will surely come with you," she said. For Deborah was brave, with the courage God had given her.

So together, Deborah and Barak gathered God's people, and led them out. And just as God had promised, he went ahead of them. Down from the sky came a great storm! The rain poured and poured, and the rivers rose, and the ground turned to thick, sticky mud. And all those mighty iron chariots — the very ones the enemy was so proud of — got stuck fast in the mud and could not move at all! The proud soldiers slipped and slid and tumbled about, and not one of their great chariots could help them now.

And so the great enemy army was beaten, and they turned and ran far, far away. God's people were safe again, all because God had gone out ahead of them, just as he said he would.

When it was all over, Deborah was so thankful that she sang a beautiful song to God — a song of thanks, and of joy, because he had rescued his people. "The Lord is our strength!" she sang. "He is the one who kept us safe!"

So remember this, little one: God gives his wisdom, and his courage, to all kinds of people — to boys and girls, to men and women, to the quiet and the small. And whenever God calls you to be brave, you will never have to go alone. He will go out ahead of you, every single step of the way.
