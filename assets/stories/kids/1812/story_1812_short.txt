The Empty Tomb

Very early on Sunday morning, while the sky was still gray and soft, some women walked quietly along a path toward a garden. Their hearts were heavy and sad. Their dear friend Jesus had died, and they were going to the place where he had been laid to rest, to care for him with sweet-smelling spices.

A large, heavy stone had been rolled across the door of the tomb. "Who will move the stone for us?" the women wondered. It was far too big for them to move alone.

But when they reached the garden, they stopped in surprise. The great stone had already been rolled away! And the tomb was open — and empty.

As they stood there wondering, a shining angel appeared beside them, bright as lightning. The women were afraid, but the angel spoke kindly. "Do not be afraid," the angel said. "I know you are looking for Jesus. But he is not here. He is alive! He has risen, just as he said he would. Come and see the place where he lay."

The women could hardly believe it. Their sadness melted away, and a great joy rose up inside them. Jesus was alive!

"Go quickly," said the angel, "and tell his friends the wonderful news."

So the women ran — they ran as fast as they could, their feet flying along the path, their hearts bursting with the most wonderful news the world had ever heard: Jesus is alive! Jesus is alive!

And it was true. It is still true today. That is why, every year, people everywhere celebrate the morning the tomb was empty — the happiest morning there has ever been.
