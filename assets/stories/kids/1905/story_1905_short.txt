The Woman Who Kept Asking

Jesus once told a little story, to help his friends learn never to give up praying.

"In a certain town," Jesus said, "there lived a judge — a man whose job was to help people when something was unfair. But this judge was grumpy, and he did not really care about anyone.

"Now, in that same town lived a woman, a widow, who needed his help. Someone had treated her unfairly, and she needed the judge to set it right. So she came to him and asked. 'Please,' she said. 'Help me.'

"But the grumpy judge said no, and he sent her away.

"Did the woman give up? She did not! She came back the very next day, and asked again. 'Please, help me.' And the next day. And the next. Again, and again, and again she came, never giving up, always asking.

"At last, the grumpy judge threw up his hands. 'All right, all right!' he said. 'I will help her — for she will simply never stop coming back!' And so the woman got the help she needed, because she had not given up."

Then Jesus explained why he had told the story. "Now think," he said gently. "If even a grumpy judge, who did not care, would help in the end — how much more will God help you? For God is not grumpy at all. God loves you. God listens, gladly, every single time you pray. So do not ever give up talking to him."

So keep on praying, little one. Keep on talking to God, about everything, big and small. He is never too busy, and never too tired. He loves to hear from you — always.
