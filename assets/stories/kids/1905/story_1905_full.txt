The Woman Who Kept Asking

One day, Jesus wanted to teach his friends something important: that they should always keep praying, and never give up, and never lose heart. So, as he often did, he told them a little story.

"Once," Jesus began, "in a certain town, there lived a judge. Now, a judge is someone whose job is to help people when something is unfair, and to make things right again. But this particular judge was a grumpy, grumbly sort of man. He did not really care about other people, and he did not much like being bothered.

"Now, in that same town, there also lived a woman — a poor widow. And something unfair had happened to her, something that badly needed putting right. So she went to the judge to ask for his help. 'Please, sir,' she said. 'Someone has treated me unfairly. Will you help me?'

"But the grumpy judge just frowned, and shook his head, and sent her away.

"Well — did the woman give up, and go home for good? Oh, no. Not at all! The very next day, back she came. 'Please,' she said again. 'Will you help me?' And the judge said no again. But the day after that, there she was once more. And the next day. And the day after that. Again, and again, and again, that woman came back — patient, and hopeful, and never, ever giving up. She came in the morning, and she came again in the evening. She would not be sent away, and she would not lose hope.

"At long last, the grumpy judge let out a great big sigh. 'Oh, all right!' he grumbled. 'I will help this woman, and set things right for her — because she is simply never going to stop coming to ask me!' And so, in the end, the woman got the help she needed — all because she had kept on asking, and had not lost hope."

Then Jesus looked at his friends, and he explained what the story really meant. "Now, think about that," he said gently. "If even a grumpy old judge, who did not really care at all, would help in the end — then how much more will your loving Father in heaven help you? For God is nothing like that grumpy judge. God is never too busy, and he is never annoyed when you come to him. God loves you with his whole heart. And he is always glad to listen — every single time you pray. He never, ever grows tired of hearing your voice."

So that is why we keep on praying, little one. Not to wear God down, and not because he does not want to hear us — but because he loves us, and he truly loves to listen. So keep on talking to God, about everything — the big things, and the little things, and everything in between. He is never too tired, and never too busy. He is always, always glad to hear from you.
