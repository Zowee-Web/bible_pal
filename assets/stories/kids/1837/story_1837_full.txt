Ruth and Naomi

The road was long and dusty, and two women walked along it side by side. One was old, and her name was Naomi. The other was young, and her name was Ruth.

Naomi had not always been sad. Once, long ago, she had a husband and two grown sons, and her house was full of laughing and the smell of supper cooking. But a very sad thing had happened — her husband and her sons had grown sick, and they had died. Now Naomi had no one left in all the world but Ruth, who had married one of her sons. And Naomi was far, far from the land where she was born.

"You should go home, dear one," Naomi said to Ruth, with tears in her eyes. "Go back to your own mother and father. You are young. You should stay among your own people, where life is easier."

But Ruth took hold of Naomi's hand, and she would not let it go.

"Please do not send me away," Ruth said. "Wherever you go, I will go. Wherever you live, I will live. Your people will be my people. And your God will be my God. I will stay beside you, always."

So the two of them walked all the long way together, until they came at last to Naomi's old hometown — a little place called Bethlehem.

They were very poor, and had almost nothing to eat. So every single morning, Ruth got up early and went out to the barley fields. Behind the workers who cut the grain, she gathered up the bits and pieces they left behind, so that she and Naomi would have enough bread for the day. It was hard work under a hot sun, but Ruth never once complained.

Now the field belonged to a good and gentle man named Boaz. He noticed Ruth working there, day after day. And he heard the story of how this kind young woman had left her own home just to care for old Naomi.

"Let her gather all she wants," Boaz told his workers. "And drop some extra barley on purpose, just for her." He made sure Ruth always had cool water to drink and a safe place to rest.

As the days went by, Boaz came to love Ruth, and Ruth came to love him. And so they were married. And before long, God gave them a wonderful gift — a baby boy.

When the baby was born, they placed him in old Naomi's arms. And as she held that tiny child close, Naomi's heart, which had been so heavy for so long, filled all the way up with joy.

She was not alone anymore. Kindness had walked her all the way home.
