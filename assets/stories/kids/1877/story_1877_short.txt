The Coin in the Fish

One day, somebody came to Peter with a question. "Does your teacher, Jesus, pay the temple tax?" they asked. The temple tax was a little bit of money that everyone was supposed to pay.

"Oh — yes," said Peter. But then he stopped. Because he suddenly remembered something. He and Jesus did not have any money to pay it with! Not a single coin.

Peter went off to find Jesus, wondering what in the world they would do. But before he could even say a word, Jesus already knew exactly what Peter was worried about. Jesus always did.

And do you know what Jesus told him to do?

"Peter," Jesus said, with a smile, "go down to the lake. Take your fishing line, and throw it into the water. And the very first fish you catch — open up its mouth, and look inside."

Now, that was a strange thing to say! But Peter trusted Jesus. So down to the lake he went. He threw in his line, and he waited. And soon — a tug! Peter pulled up a wriggling, splashing fish.

He opened its mouth and peeked inside. And there — can you believe it? — was a shiny coin! A coin, right inside a fish's mouth! And it was exactly enough to pay the tax — for Jesus, and for Peter too.

Peter laughed out loud. What a surprise! God had given them exactly what they needed, in the silliest, most wonderful way.

So whenever you have a worry, even a little one, you can remember Peter's fish. God knows just what you need. And sometimes he gives it in a way that makes you laugh.
