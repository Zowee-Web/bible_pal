The Voice from Heaven

By the edge of a river called the Jordan, there lived a man named John. John had an important job: he was helping people get ready, because Jesus was coming. Many people came to John at the river, and he baptized them in the water — dipping them gently under, as a sign that they wanted to follow God.

One day, someone very special came walking down to the river. It was Jesus himself.

"Will you baptize me?" Jesus asked.

John could hardly believe it. "But you should baptize me!" he said. Yet Jesus gently asked him to, and so John did. He led Jesus into the cool river water, and baptized him, just as he had the others.

And the moment Jesus came up out of the water, something wonderful happened. The sky opened up above him. And the Spirit of God came down, gently, softly, like a beautiful white dove, and rested upon him.

Then a voice spoke — the voice of God, from heaven itself. And do you know what it said? It said:

"This is my Son, whom I love. With him I am very pleased."

What a moment that was! Heaven open, a dove drifting down, and God's own voice, full of love, telling the whole world how much he loved his Son.

And here is something just as wonderful, little one. God feels that very same love for you. You are his dear child, and he is always, always glad to call you his very own.
