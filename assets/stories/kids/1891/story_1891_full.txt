The Voice from Heaven

By the banks of a wide river called the Jordan, there lived a man named John. John wore simple clothes, and he ate simple food, and he had one very important job to do. He was helping everyone get ready, because Jesus was coming soon.

Crowds of people walked out to the river to hear John speak. And many of them, when they heard him, wanted to turn their hearts toward God. So John baptized them in the river — gently dipping them down under the cool water, and lifting them back up again, as a sign of a fresh, clean start with God. Day after day, John pointed people toward the One who was coming. "Someone far greater than I am is on his way," he would tell them. "Get your hearts ready!"

Then one day, someone very special came walking down the path to the river. The crowds may not have known him yet. But John did. It was Jesus himself.

Jesus came to the water's edge. "Will you baptize me, John?" he asked.

John was astonished. "But it should be the other way around!" he said. "You should baptize me!" For John knew just how special Jesus was. But Jesus gently told him that this was the right thing to do, to please God. And so John agreed.

He led Jesus out into the river, into the cool, clear water. And there, just as he had done for so many others, John baptized Jesus, dipping him gently beneath the water.

And the very moment Jesus rose back up, shining and dripping in the sunlight — something wonderful happened. High above, the sky itself seemed to open wide. And out of the bright sky came the Spirit of God, floating down softly, gently, like a beautiful white dove. Down, down it came, and it rested right upon Jesus. It was the gentlest, most beautiful thing the people had ever seen — as if heaven itself had reached down to touch him.

And then a voice spoke. It was the voice of God, the Father, ringing out from heaven itself. The whole crowd grew still. And this is what the voice said, warm and full of love:

"This is my Son, the one I love. With him I am very pleased."

Oh, what a moment it was! The sky thrown open, the dove drifting down, and God's own voice telling the whole wide world how dearly he loved his Son. Some of the people standing by felt tears come to their eyes. Somehow they knew they had seen something they would never, ever forget.

And here is the most wonderful thing of all, little one — a secret to keep close to your heart. The same God who spoke those words feels that very same love for you. You are his dear child. And there is nothing in all the world that could ever make him stop loving you. He is always, always glad to call you his very own.
