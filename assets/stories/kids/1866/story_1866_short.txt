Come to Me and Rest

It is the end of the day now, and maybe you feel a little tired. Maybe today was happy. Or maybe today was hard. Either way, there is something gentle that Jesus once said — something just for you.

Jesus said:

"Come to me, all you who are tired, and all you who are carrying heavy things. Come to me, and I will give you rest."

He did not say, "Come only if you have been good." He did not say, "Come only when you are happy." He simply said: come, just as you are.

So if your heart feels heavy tonight, you do not have to carry it all by yourself. You can bring it to Jesus. You can bring him anything at all — the happy things and the hard things, your worries and your tears and your tired self. He will hold it gently, and he will give you rest.

So close your eyes now. Let your shoulders go soft. Take a slow, deep breath.

Rest now, little one. Jesus is gentle and kind. You can bring him anything.
