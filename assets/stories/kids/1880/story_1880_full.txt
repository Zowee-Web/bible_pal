The Chariot of Fire

Elijah was one of God's greatest prophets — a man who loved God with all his heart, and spoke God's words to the people. And wherever Elijah went, a young helper went with him. His name was Elisha. Elisha had followed Elijah for a long, long time, learning from him, and he loved the old prophet dearly — the way a son loves his father.

Now, one day, Elijah knew that God was going to take him home to heaven. It was almost time. So the two friends set out to walk together, one last time. Again and again, Elijah said to Elisha, "You may stay here." But Elisha would not. "As surely as God lives," he said, "I will not leave you. I will stay right by your side, all the way to the very end." And so, side by side, the two of them walked on together, talking as they went. They had shared so many adventures, the two of them — so many long years of serving God, side by side.

Then God did something wonderful — something no one had ever seen before in all the world.

As Elijah and Elisha were walking along, deep in talk, all at once the air began to shine. And there before them appeared a chariot of fire, pulled by horses of fire — blazing bright and beautiful, and not frightening at all! The fiery chariot came right between the two friends, gently parting them. And then a great whirlwind, a swirling wind straight from heaven, swept Elijah up — up, and up, and up — carrying him all the way home to God. Up through the clouds Elijah rose, in that blaze of glorious light, until at last he was gone from sight.

Elisha watched it all with wide, wondering eyes. "My father! My father!" he called out. For he was sad, of course, to see his dear old friend go. But deep down, he knew that Elijah was happy now — safe and home with the God he had loved so well. Still, it was a strange and quiet thing, to stand on that road all alone, where only a moment before his dear friend had been.

And then Elisha noticed something on the ground. It was Elijah's cloak — his very own coat — left behind, just for him. Elisha bent down and picked it up, and he wrapped it around his own shoulders. For this was God's gift: the very same Spirit that had been with Elijah would now be with Elisha. Now it was Elisha's turn — his turn to love God, and to speak God's words, and to care for the people, just as Elijah had done. And though his heart still missed his old friend, Elisha was not afraid, for he knew that God was with him now.

So remember this, little one. Even when someone we love has to go away, God never leaves us all alone. He stays close beside us. And, just as he did for Elisha, he gives us everything we need to carry on, brave and strong.
