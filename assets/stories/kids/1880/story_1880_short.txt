The Chariot of Fire

Elijah was one of God's greatest prophets — a man who loved God and spoke for him. And Elijah had a young helper named Elisha, who followed him everywhere and loved him dearly, the way a son loves his father.

One day, Elijah knew that God was going to take him home to heaven. So he and Elisha walked along together, talking, for one last time. Elisha would not leave his side. "I will stay with you," he said, "right to the very end."

Then God did something wonderful — something no one had ever seen before.

As the two friends were walking and talking, all at once there appeared a chariot of fire, pulled by horses of fire, shining bright and beautiful! It came right between them. And a great whirlwind, like a swirling wind from heaven, lifted Elijah up — up, and up, and up — all the way home to God.

Elisha watched with wide, wondering eyes. "My father! My father!" he cried. He was sad to see his dear friend go. But he knew that Elijah was safe now, home with God.

And Elijah had left something behind for Elisha — his cloak, his very own coat, lying there on the ground. Elisha picked it up and wrapped it around himself. For God was giving Elisha the same Spirit that had been with Elijah. Now it was Elisha's turn to love God, and to speak for him, and to help the people.

So even when someone we love goes away, little one, God does not leave us on our own. He stays close beside us. And he gives us all that we need to carry on.
