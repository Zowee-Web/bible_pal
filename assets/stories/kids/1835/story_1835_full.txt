God Made You

Long, long ago, a man named David — the same David who once was a shepherd boy — sat down and wrote a beautiful song to God. He had so many songs, but this one was very special. It was all about something wonderful: how very, very well God knows you, and how very much God loves you.

Here is what David wanted you to know.

God made you. Yes, you! God made every single part of you, carefully and on purpose. God made your fingers and your toes, and your eyes that see the world, and your ears that hear the birds, and the special sound of your laugh, and your very own smile that is like nobody else's. You did not just happen. You were made, like a wonderful gift, with great love.

And here is something even more amazing: God knew you before you were ever born. Before anyone else in the whole world had even seen your face, God already knew you, and was already loving you, and was waiting with joy for the day you would come.

God knows you better than anyone, anywhere. God knows when you wake up in the morning, all sleepy, and when you lie down in your bed at night. God knows when you feel happy and want to dance, and when you feel sad and want to hide. God even knows the thoughts inside your head, before you say a single word out loud. There is nothing about you that God does not know — and God loves you completely, just as you are.

And there is one more thing David wanted you to remember, the very best part of all. No matter where you ever go, God is always, always with you. If you could climb up, up, up, all the way past the moon and the highest twinkling star, God would already be there. And if you could dive down, down, down, to the very bottom of the deepest, darkest sea, God would be right there too. There is nowhere in all the world, and nowhere in all the sky, that you could ever go where God is not right beside you, holding you close and loving you.

So whenever you feel small, or forgotten, or like nobody notices you, you can remember David's song. You are not an accident. You are not a mistake. You were made, on purpose, with love — and you are wonderful to God.

You are wonderfully made. And the God who made you will be with you, and will love you, always.
