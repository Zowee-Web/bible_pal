God Made You

Long ago, a man named David wrote a beautiful song to God. And this is what the song was all about: how very, very well God knows you, and how much God loves you.

You see, God made you. God made every single part of you — your fingers and your toes, your eyes that see, and your ears that hear, and your smile, and your laugh. Before you were even born, before anyone else had ever seen you, God already knew you, and was already loving you.

God knows you better than anyone in the whole world. God knows when you wake up in the morning, and when you lie down to sleep at night. God knows when you are happy, and when you are sad. God even knows what you are thinking, before you say a single word.

And no matter where you ever go, God is always, always with you. If you climbed up, up, up to the very highest star, God would be there. If you dived down, down, down to the bottom of the deepest sea, God would be there too. There is nowhere you could ever go where God is not right beside you, holding you close.

That is what David's song says. You are not an accident. You are not a mistake. You were made, on purpose, with love, by a God who knows you and is always near.

You are wonderfully made. And the God who made you will be with you, and love you, always.
