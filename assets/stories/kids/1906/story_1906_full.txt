Joseph Far From Home

Joseph was a long, long way from home. He had grown up safe among his big family, the favorite of his father. But his brothers had grown jealous, and one terrible day they sent him away — all the way to Egypt, a strange land where the people spoke words he did not know, and no one had ever heard his name.

How Joseph must have missed his father. He must have missed the smell of home and the sound of his own language. But even in that faraway place, far from everyone who loved him, Joseph was not alone. God was with him.

Joseph was brought to work in the grand house of a man named Potiphar. He could have given up. Instead, Joseph worked hard and did what was right, day after day. And God was with him in it — everything Joseph put his hands to seemed to turn out well. Potiphar noticed. Before long he trusted Joseph with his whole house, because he could see that God made Joseph wise and good.

But then something unfair happened. Joseph was blamed for something he had not done. He had done nothing wrong at all — and still, he was taken away and locked in a prison.

It was dark in that prison, and quiet, and lonely. Anyone might have wondered if God had forgotten them in a place like that. But God had not forgotten Joseph. God was right there beside him in the dark, just as he had always been. And little by little, even in prison, the things Joseph did began to go well again. The prison keeper came to trust him, and put Joseph in charge of all the rest. Day after day, Joseph did his work well and kindly, and the long days passed a little easier, because he was not facing them by himself.

One night, two of the king's own servants, locked up there too, each had a strange dream. In the morning their faces were troubled. "Why do you look so sad?" Joseph asked. "We had dreams," they said, "and no one can tell us what they mean."

"God knows what dreams mean," Joseph said gently. "Tell them to me." And God helped Joseph understand. He told each man what his dream meant — and just as Joseph said, it all came true. One of the men went free again, back to the king. "Please remember me," Joseph had asked him. But the man went off and forgot all about Joseph.

So Joseph waited. The days went by, and then the months, and still he sat in that prison, remembered by no one at all.

But he was never truly alone. God was working quietly the whole time, in ways Joseph could not see — getting everything ready to lift him up one day, higher than he could possibly imagine.

Through every part of it — the long road to Egypt, the years of hard work, the unfair prison, the waiting that would not end — God stayed right beside Joseph, all the way through.
