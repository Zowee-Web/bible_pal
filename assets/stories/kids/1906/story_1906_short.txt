Joseph Far From Home

Joseph was a long, long way from home. His brothers had sent him away, all the way to Egypt, where the language was strange and no one knew his name. He must have missed his father so much. But even there, in that faraway place, Joseph was not alone. God was with him.

Joseph went to work in the house of a man named Potiphar. He worked hard and did what was right, and everything he touched seemed to go well — because God was helping him.

But then something unfair happened. Joseph was blamed for something he did not do, and he was put in prison. It was dark there, and quiet, and Joseph had done nothing wrong.

Yet even in that prison, God had not left him. God was right there beside Joseph in the dark. And once again, everything Joseph did began to go well, until even the prison keeper trusted him.

One night, two of the king's servants had strange dreams that no one could understand. "God can tell us what dreams mean," Joseph said. And God helped him understand them, and everything came true, just as he said.

Joseph still had to wait a long while. But God was working quietly the whole time — getting ready to lift Joseph up in a way no one could imagine yet.

Through all of it — the long road, the hard work, the dark prison, the waiting — God stayed right beside Joseph, all the way through.
