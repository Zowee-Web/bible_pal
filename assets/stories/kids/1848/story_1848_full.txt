Jesus Heals a Blind Man

Beside a dusty road, at the edge of a busy town, sat a man named Bartimaeus. Bartimaeus could not see. His eyes did not work, and they never had. So every single day, he sat in the very same spot beside the road, listening to the whole world go by — the shuffling footsteps, the chattering voices, the carts creaking past — and wishing, more than anything in the world, that he could see it all for himself.

One day, Bartimaeus heard something different. A great crowd was coming down the road. There were many, many feet, and many, many voices, all at once, and the sound grew louder and louder as it came closer. "What is happening?" Bartimaeus asked. "Who is coming?"

"It's Jesus!" someone told him. "Jesus, the one who heals the sick, is passing by, right here!"

Bartimaeus's heart leaped up inside him. Jesus! He had heard the wonderful stories — how Jesus made sick people well, and gave hurting people hope. This was his chance, and he was not going to let it slip away. So he opened his mouth and called out, as loud as he possibly could, "Jesus! Jesus, please help me!"

Some of the people nearby frowned at him. "Shh! Be quiet," they said. "Stop making such a fuss."

But Bartimaeus would not be quiet. This was far too important to keep silent about. So he called out even louder than before: "Jesus! Please — help me!"

And Jesus stopped.

In all that big, busy, noisy crowd, Jesus had heard the one man who was calling out to him. He stood still right there on the road, and he said, "Bring him to me."

Now the people turned to Bartimaeus, and this time their voices were kind and excited. "Get up!" they said. "Cheer up! He is calling for you!" Bartimaeus threw off his old coat, jumped right up to his feet, and hurried toward the sound of Jesus' voice.

"What do you want me to do for you?" Jesus asked him, ever so gently.

Bartimaeus did not even have to think about it. "Teacher," he said, "I want to see."

Jesus smiled at him, warm and kind. "You believed that I could help you," he said. "Now — open your eyes."

And all at once, Bartimaeus could see!

Light came flooding in. Color came rushing in — the deep blue of the sky, the green of the far-off hills, the faces of all the people crowded around. But the very first thing Bartimaeus saw, with his brand-new eyes, was the kind and smiling face of Jesus, looking right back at him.

Bartimaeus was so happy he could hardly believe it. He stared at his own two hands. He looked up at the wide blue sky. He looked, and looked, and looked! And then he did not go back to his old spot beside the road. Instead, with his shining new eyes, he followed Jesus right down the road — thanking God with every single step he took.
