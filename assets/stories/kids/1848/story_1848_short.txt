Jesus Heals a Blind Man

Beside a dusty road sat a man named Bartimaeus. Bartimaeus could not see. His eyes did not work, and so every day he sat by the road, listening to the world go by.

One day, he heard a great crowd of footsteps and voices coming down the road. "What is happening?" he asked. "Jesus is coming!" someone told him. "Jesus, the one who heals the sick!"

Bartimaeus's heart leaped. This was his chance. So he called out, as loud as he could, "Jesus! Jesus, please help me!"

"Shh! Be quiet," some of the people said. But Bartimaeus would not be quiet. He called out even louder: "Jesus, please help me!"

And Jesus stopped. In all that big, noisy crowd, Jesus heard one man calling. "Bring him to me," Jesus said.

"Get up!" the people told Bartimaeus. "He is calling for you!" Bartimaeus jumped to his feet and hurried to Jesus.

"What do you want me to do for you?" Jesus asked, kindly.

"Teacher," said Bartimaeus, "I want to see."

Jesus smiled. "You believed that I could help you," he said. "Now — open your eyes."

And all at once, Bartimaeus could see! Light and color came rushing in. And the very first thing he saw, with his brand-new eyes, was the kind and smiling face of Jesus.

Bartimaeus was so happy he could hardly believe it. And he did not go back to his old place by the road. Instead, with his shining new eyes, he followed Jesus, thanking God with every step.
