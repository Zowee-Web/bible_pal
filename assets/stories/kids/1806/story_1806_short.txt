Jairus' Little Girl

In a town beside the sea, there lived a father named Jairus, and he loved his little girl with all his heart. But she had become very, very sick. She lay in her bed, too weak to play, and Jairus did not know what to do.

Then Jairus heard that Jesus was nearby. He hurried through the crowd and knelt down before him. "Please," he said. "My little girl is so sick. Please come and help her."

Jesus looked at him kindly. "Take me to her," he said.

But before they reached the house, some people came with sad faces. "It is too late," they said softly. "Your little girl is gone."

Jairus's heart sank. But Jesus turned to him and said, gently, "Don't be afraid. Only believe."

When they came to the house, everyone was weeping. Jesus stepped inside. "Do not cry," he said. "The little girl is not gone. She is only sleeping." Some people did not understand. But Jesus walked quietly into her room.

He sat down beside her and took her small hand in his. "Little girl," he said, "it's time to get up."

And the little girl opened her eyes. She sat up and looked around — and there was Jesus, smiling at her. She was well again, head to toe!

Her mother and father could hardly believe it. Their little girl was up and laughing and running to them. Jesus smiled. "Now," he said, "give her something to eat."

And there was more joy in that house than there had ever been before.
