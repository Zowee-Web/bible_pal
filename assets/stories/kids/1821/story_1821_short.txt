The Cross

One day, Jesus was led up a hill outside the city. His friends watched from a little way off — and his mother, Mary, was there too, standing close among them. Their hearts were heavy and sad, because they loved him so.

Jesus had never done anything wrong. He had healed the sick, and blessed the children, and loved every person he met. But there were some who did not understand him, and they put Jesus on a wooden cross.

And even then — even there — Jesus did not stop loving. He looked down at the people below him, and he prayed for them. "Father," he said, "forgive them. They do not know what they are doing." That is how much love was in his heart.

Then the sky grew dark, all across the land, as if the whole world had gone quiet and still. And Jesus looked up to God, his Father. "Into your hands," he said softly, "I give myself." And he closed his eyes, and his breath grew still.

His friends wept. It was the saddest day they had ever known. They did not understand why this had happened, and they did not know yet what God was doing.

But God had not left. God was there, even in the dark — close, and holding everything, even now. And this was not the end of the story.

For Jesus had walked this path because he loved the whole world. He loved every single person. He loved you. And a love as strong as that could never, ever stay in the dark.

Sunday was coming.
