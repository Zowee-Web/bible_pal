The Shepherd Boy Becomes King

When David was still a shepherd boy, God had already chosen him for something big. One day, God said, David would be king.

But it did not happen all at once. David had to wait — for years and years. He kept his sheep on the quiet hills. He played his harp, and he made good, faithful friends. He was brave when he needed to be. And through happy days and hard ones alike, he kept trusting God, even when the waiting felt very, very long.

Through all those years, God stayed close to David, just as God had stayed close to him out in the fields. God was getting everything ready, in his own good time.

Then at last, the day came. From every corner of the land, the people came together — more than David could count. They remembered how God had been with him. They remembered how he cared for them, the way a shepherd cares for his sheep. And all together they said, "You will be our king."

So they placed the crown on David's head. The shepherd boy, who once carried lambs in his arms and sang to God under the stars, was now the king of all God's people. Imagine the cheering and the music that day.

And David did not forget where he came from, or who had brought him there. The same God who was with him among the sheep was with him now. God had made that promise long ago. And God always keeps his promises.
