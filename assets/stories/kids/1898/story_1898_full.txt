Just Say the Word

In a town called Capernaum, there lived a Roman soldier — a captain who was in charge of a hundred other soldiers. He was a strong and important man, and when he gave an order, his soldiers obeyed at once. But this captain was also a kind and gentle man. He was good to the people of the town, and he cared deeply for those who served in his house.

Now, the captain had a servant whom he loved very much, almost like family. And one day, this servant fell terribly ill. He lay in his bed, hardly able to move, and growing worse and worse. Every day the captain checked on him, hoping for some small sign that he was getting better. But none ever came. The captain was very worried. He would have done anything to help him — but no matter what he tried, nothing made the poor servant any better.

Then the captain heard some wonderful news: Jesus was coming to Capernaum — Jesus, who could heal the sick and make hurting people well again. The moment he heard it, the captain hurried out to find him.

"Lord," he said, when he reached Jesus, "my dear servant is lying at home, terribly sick. He is in so much pain. Please — will you help him?"

"Yes," said Jesus kindly. "I will come to your house, and I will make him well."

But then the captain said something that surprised everyone. "Oh, Lord," he said, "you do not need to come all the way to my house. I am not important enough to have you under my roof. But listen — I understand how this works. I am a captain. When I say to one of my soldiers, 'Go,' he goes. When I say, 'Come,' he comes. And so I know that you have that kind of power over sickness itself. You do not even need to be there. You have only to say the word — just say it, right here, right now — and I know, deep in my heart, that my servant will be healed."

When Jesus heard this, he was amazed. He turned to all the people following him. "I tell you the truth," he said, "I have not found faith as great as this in anyone, in all the land!"

Then Jesus turned back to the captain, and smiled. "Go on home now," he said. "It will happen just as you believed it would."

So the captain went home. And when he got there — there was his servant, up out of bed, smiling, completely well again! The captain laughed out loud with relief and joy. His servant had been healed at the very same moment Jesus spoke, even though Jesus was far away across the town.

So you can trust Jesus too, little one, even when you cannot see what he is doing, and even when he feels far away. For his love, and his power, reach everywhere — into every house, and every heart, no matter the distance.
