Just Say the Word

In a town called Capernaum, there lived a soldier — a Roman captain, who was the leader of a hundred men. He was a strong and important man. But he was also a kind one. And he had a servant whom he cared about very much.

One day, the servant became terribly sick. He lay in bed, hardly able to move, and the captain was worried about him. Then the captain heard that Jesus was coming to town — Jesus, who could make sick people well. So he hurried out to find him.

"Lord," said the captain, "my poor servant is very, very sick at home. Will you help him?"

"I will come," said Jesus, "and make him well."

But the captain said something surprising. "Oh, Lord — you do not need to come all the way to my house. I am not important enough for that. But I understand how things work. I am a captain; when I tell my soldiers to go, they go. So I know that you have power over sickness, too. You only have to say the word — just say it, right here — and I know my servant will be healed."

When Jesus heard this, he was amazed. He turned to the crowd. "I tell you," he said, "I have never seen such great faith in anyone, anywhere!"

Then Jesus said to the captain, "Go on home. It will be just as you believed." And at that very moment — far away, back at the house — the servant was healed, and got up, completely well!

So you can trust Jesus, little one, even when you cannot see what he is doing. His love and his power reach everywhere — to every house, and every heart, no matter how far away.
