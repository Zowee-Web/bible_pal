The Man Who Leaped for Joy

The man at the temple gate could not run, the way the other children did. He had never been able to walk — not once, not since the day he was born. Every morning, kind friends lifted him up and carried him to the beautiful gate of the temple, and there he sat. People hurried past him all day long, going in to pray. And the man held out his hands and asked them, very softly, for a coin or two, so he would have something to eat.

He had sat by that gate his whole life.

One day, two men came walking up to the temple. Their names were Peter and John. The man looked up and held out his hands, the way he always did. "Please — a coin or two?" he said.

But Peter stopped. He looked right at the man — really looked, straight into his eyes. "I have no silver," Peter said. "I have no gold. But what I do have, I will give you." He leaned down close. "In the name of Jesus — get up and walk!"

And Peter reached out his hand and took hold of the man's hand.

Right away — that very moment — something happened deep inside the man. His feet grew strong. His ankles grew strong. He felt it rush all the way up his legs, warm and sure and brand new.

So he pushed himself up. He stood! He had never once stood before in all his life — and now he was standing on his own two feet. He took one step. Then another. And then he could not help what happened next.

He jumped.

He leaped straight up into the air, and he came down laughing, and he leaped again. Walking and leaping and singing his thank-you to God, he went right through the gate and into the temple — holding on to Peter, holding on to John, jumping the whole way in.

And all the people who saw him stopped and stared, their mouths open wide. Was that the man from the gate? The one who had never once walked? They could hardly believe their eyes. And their hearts filled up to the very brim with wonder.
