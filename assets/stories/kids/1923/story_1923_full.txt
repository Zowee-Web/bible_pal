The Man Who Leaped for Joy

There was a man who could not feel his feet move. He had never been able to walk — not once in his whole life — and every single morning, kind friends lifted him gently and carried him to the gate of the temple. It was a beautiful gate, all gold and shining, and that is where they set him down to sit. People came up the steps all day long, going in to pray to God. And the man held out his hands to them and asked, very softly, for a coin or two, so that he would have a little something to eat.

That was his day. The same as yesterday. The same as the day before. He had sat by that golden gate his whole long life, watching everybody else go walking by.

Then one afternoon, two men came up the temple steps. Their names were Peter and John, and they had been friends of Jesus. The man saw them coming and held out his hands, the way he always did to everyone. "Please — a coin or two?" he said.

But Peter did something nobody else ever did.

He stopped. He stood very still, right in front of the man. And then Peter looked at him — really looked, straight into his eyes, the way you look at someone who matters. "Look at us," Peter said gently. So the man looked up, hoping, sure they were about to drop a coin into his hands.

"I have no silver," said Peter. "I have no gold." The man's heart began to sink. No money today, then.

But Peter was not finished. "What I do have," he said, "I will give you." He leaned down close. And in a strong, sure voice he said, "In the name of Jesus — get up and walk!"

And Peter reached out his own hand, and he took the man firmly by the hand, and he began to lift him up.

Right then — that very second — something wonderful rushed all through the man. He felt it start deep down in his feet. His feet grew strong! His ankles grew strong! Warm new strength went pouring up his legs, all the way up, sure and steady, like nothing he had ever felt in all his life.

So the man pushed himself up off the ground. And he stood. He stood! He had never once stood up before — not ever — and now here he was, balanced on his own two feet, tall as anyone.

He lifted one foot. He took a step. Then he took another. His legs held him! They truly held him! And right there, with the whole busy crowd around him, the man found he simply could not help what happened next.

He jumped.

He leaped straight up into the air — high — and came down laughing, and bent his knees, and leaped up again. Oh, how he leaped! Walking and leaping and singing his thank-you up to God, he went right through the beautiful gate and into the temple, jumping every step of the way. And he held on tight to Peter, and he held on tight to John, the two who had stood still for him, and the three of them went in together while the man praised God at the top of his happy voice.

All around them, the people stopped where they stood.

They knew this man. They had walked past him a thousand times. Was that really him — that man leaping in the air? The one who had never, ever walked? They stared and they stared, and they could hardly believe their own eyes. Some of them rubbed their eyes and looked again. It was him! It truly was!

And a great wave of wonder swept through the whole crowd, brighter and brighter, as they watched him spin and leap and shout for joy — the man who had sat so still by the gate for so long, now jumping and singing and holding on to his two friends, too full of gladness to ever sit still again, all the way up to the very brim with wonder.
