Daniel's Healthy Food

Daniel was a young man who loved God with all his heart. One day, Daniel and three of his good friends were taken far, far away from home, to live and learn in the king's grand palace, in a strange new land. It was a long, long way from everything they knew — their families, their homes, the streets where they had played. They must have missed it all very much. But Daniel decided something deep in his heart: wherever he was, even here, he would keep on loving and honoring God.

The king of that land wanted these young men to grow big and strong and clever, so that one day they could help him. So he gave an order: every day, they were to be given his very own royal food — rich, fancy food, brought straight from the king's table.

But Daniel knew that some of that food was not right for him to eat — not if he truly wanted to honor God in everything he did. Even here, in the middle of the king's palace, so very far from home, Daniel wanted to do what was good and true. He did not make a fuss, and he was not rude about it. But quietly, in his heart, he made up his mind.

So Daniel went to the guard who looked after them, and he asked, very kindly and politely, "Please, would you let my three friends and me eat only simple food — just vegetables and fresh water — for ten days? And then you can see for yourself how we are doing."

The guard was a little worried. "But what if you grow thin and weak?" he said. "Then the king will be cross with me!" Still, Daniel asked so kindly, and so respectfully, that the guard agreed to give it a try — just for ten days.

So for ten days, Daniel and his three friends ate their simple food and drank their cool, fresh water, while all the other young men ate the king's rich meals. And when the ten days were over, the guard came to see how they were looking.

And what a surprise he had! Daniel and his friends looked healthier, and brighter, and stronger than any of the other young men in the whole palace.

So the guard happily let them go on eating just the way they had asked. And God was very pleased with Daniel and his friends, because they had chosen to honor him, even in a faraway land where it would have been so easy not to. God gave them strong, healthy bodies — and something even better: wise and understanding hearts. And in time, those four faithful friends grew up to serve the king well, and to do a great deal of good — all because they had trusted God and stayed true to him. And Daniel never forgot: God had been right there with him, even in that faraway land, every single day.
