Daniel's Healthy Food

Daniel was a young man who loved God with all his heart. One day, Daniel and three of his friends were taken far away from home, to live and learn in the king's grand palace, in a strange new land.

The king wanted the young men to grow strong, so he ordered that they be given his very own royal food — rich, fancy food from the king's own table. But Daniel knew that some of that food was not right for him to eat, not if he wanted to honor God in everything he did. Even here, so far from home, Daniel wanted to do what was good and true.

So Daniel went to the guard who looked after them, and he asked, very kindly, "Please, would you let my friends and me eat only simple food — just vegetables and fresh water — for ten days? Then you can see for yourself how we are doing."

The guard was a little worried. "What if you grow thin and weak?" he said. "Then the king will be cross with me." But he agreed to try it, just for ten days.

So for ten days, Daniel and his friends ate their simple food and drank their water. And when the ten days were over — why, they looked healthier and brighter and stronger than all the other young men!

So they were allowed to keep eating just the way they wished. And God was pleased with Daniel and his friends. He gave them strong, healthy bodies — and something even better: wise and understanding hearts, because they had chosen to honor him.
