David and Jonathan, Best Friends

When David was a young man, he came to live at the king's house. And there he met the king's son, a kind young man named Jonathan.

From the very first day, David and Jonathan became the dearest of friends. They liked the same things and laughed at the same jokes. They told each other their secrets. It was as if their two hearts were tied together with a strong, warm thread. Each one loved the other as much as he loved himself.

One day, to show how much he cared, Jonathan did something wonderful. He was a prince, with fine and beautiful things — but he took off his own royal robe and gave it to David. He gave him his belt, and his bow, and his sword too. It was the gift of a true friend, who keeps nothing back.

And the two friends made a promise to each other. "We will be friends always," they said, "no matter what happens. You and me, forever."

Now, a hard time came, and David had to go far away for a while. The two friends were sad to part. But before David left, they held on tight to their promise. "God is between us," said Jonathan, "watching over you and me, always."

And that promise never broke — not ever, not even when they were far apart.

For that is what a true friend is. Someone who loves you just as you are. Someone who shares what they have. Someone who stays your friend, near or far, on happy days and hard ones.

And as long as he lived, David never forgot his dear friend Jonathan.
