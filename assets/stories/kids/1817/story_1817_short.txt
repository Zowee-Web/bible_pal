The Loving Father

Once, Jesus told a story about a father who had two sons, and who loved them both very much.

One day, the younger son came to his father. "Father," he said, "I would like to go far away and see the world." It made the father sad, but he let his son go. And he stood and watched him walk down the long road, until he could not see him anymore.

The young man traveled to a faraway land. At first it was exciting. But little by little he spent all his money, every last coin, until he had nothing left at all. He grew hungry, and tired, and lonely. He had to take a job feeding pigs, and there among the pigs, he thought about home.

"I will go back to my father," he said, "and tell him I am sorry."

So he began the long, long walk home.

Now, every single day that his son was gone, the father had watched that road, hoping. And while the son was still far, far away, the father saw him coming. His heart leaped. He did not wait one moment longer — he ran. He ran all the way down the road, threw his arms around his son, and held him close.

"Father, I am sorry," the son began.

But the father was already calling out, with tears of joy, "My son is home! Let us have a feast! My boy was gone, and now he is here again!"

And there was such great joy in that home, it lasted all the evening long.
