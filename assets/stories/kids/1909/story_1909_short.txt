When Moses' Arms Grew Tired

It was a hard day for God's people. They had to be brave down in the valley, while their leader Moses climbed up a high hill to pray.

Moses lifted his hands up toward God, holding his tall wooden staff high in the air. And as long as Moses' hands were lifted up, God helped his people, and all went well for them down below.

But after a while, Moses' arms began to get tired. Have you ever held your arms up high for a long, long time? Slowly, slowly, they started to droop. And whenever Moses' tired arms came down, things grew hard for his people again.

Moses tried his best. But his arms were simply too tired to hold up all by himself.

Now, Moses had two good friends with him on the hill — Aaron and Hur. And they had an idea. First they found a big smooth stone and rolled it over, so Moses could sit down and rest. Then Aaron stood on one side of Moses, and Hur stood on the other side. And gently, each one took hold of one of Moses' tired arms — and they lifted it up for him.

One friend on the left. One friend on the right. And Moses in the middle, his hands held high against the sky.

They stayed just like that, all three together, holding steady as the sun moved slowly across the sky. Aaron and Hur did not let go, not even when their own arms grew tired. They held Moses' hands up until the very end of the day — and God brought his people safely through.

Moses did not have to do it alone. When he was too tired to hold his own arms up, God gave him friends to hold them up for him.

And God still does that. When we are weary, and the day feels too hard to face by ourselves, God gives us people who will stand beside us — one on each side — and help us hold on.
