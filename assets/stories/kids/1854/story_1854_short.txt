David's Kindness to Mephibosheth

King David sat in his palace one quiet day, thinking about his dearest friend, Jonathan. Jonathan was gone now, but long ago the two of them had made a promise to always be kind to each other's families.

So David asked his servants a question. "Is there anyone left from Jonathan's family? If there is, I want to show them kindness, for my dear friend's sake."

"There is one," a servant said. "Jonathan has a son named Mephibosheth. But he lives far away. And his feet were hurt when he was little, so he cannot walk."

"Bring him to me," said David.

Now, when Mephibosheth came before the king, he was frightened. He did not know why the king had sent for him, and he bowed down low.

But David spoke to him gently and warmly. "Don't be afraid," David said. "I loved your father Jonathan with all my heart, and I am going to be kind to you for his sake. I am giving back to you all the land that belonged to your grandfather. And from now on, you will always eat here, at my own table, like one of my very own sons."

Mephibosheth could hardly believe it. He had thought he was forgotten — and here was the king, welcoming him like family.

And so it was. Every single day after that, Mephibosheth came and sat at the king's table, with a place set just for him — loved, and welcomed, and home, for the rest of his life.
