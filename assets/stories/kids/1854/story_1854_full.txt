David's Kindness to Mephibosheth

By now, David was the king. He lived in a fine palace, the long wars were over, and the whole land was at peace. But one quiet day, as he sat upon his throne, David found himself thinking about his very dearest friend, Jonathan. Jonathan was gone now. And long ago, when the two of them were young, they had made a special promise to each other — to always be kind to each other's families, no matter what happened.

David had never forgotten that promise. So he called his servants to him and asked them a question. "Is there anyone left from Jonathan's family?" he said. "If there is, I would dearly love to show them kindness, for the sake of my dear friend."

"There is one," a servant answered. "Jonathan has a son. His name is Mephibosheth. But he lives far away, in a small, out-of-the-way place. And his feet were hurt when he was a little boy, so he is not able to walk."

"Go," said David, "and bring him to me."

So they went and brought Mephibosheth all the long way to the palace. And when he was carried in before the king, he was frightened. He did not know why the king had sent for him. In those days, a new king was not always kind to the family of the king who came before him — and so Mephibosheth bowed down low to the ground, his heart pounding, wondering what would become of him.

But David spoke to him gently and warmly, with a kind smile. "Don't be afraid," David said. "I loved your father Jonathan with all my heart, and I am going to be kind to you for his sake. I am giving back to you all the land that once belonged to your grandfather. And from this very day on, you will always eat here, at my own table — like one of my very own sons."

Mephibosheth could hardly believe his ears. He had thought he was forgotten, tucked away in that faraway little place, with no one left to remember him. And here was the king himself, welcoming him as if he were his own family!

And so it was. From that day on, every single day, Mephibosheth came and took his place at the king's table. The king's own sons sat there together, and Mephibosheth sat right among them, with a place set just for him.

And here is the most beautiful part of all. Every day, when King David looked across the table and saw Jonathan's son sitting there, safe and happy, he remembered his dear friend, and he was so glad he had kept his promise. And every day, Mephibosheth looked around that table and knew, deep in his heart, that he was not forgotten, and he was not alone. Whatever else might come, he had a family now, and a place that was truly his own. He was loved. He was welcomed. He was home — for the rest of his life.
