Joseph's Colorful Coat

Jacob had twelve sons, but the one he loved most of all was Joseph. To show how much he loved him, Jacob gave Joseph a wonderful gift: a beautiful coat, woven in every color you can imagine — red and gold, blue and green, bright as a garden full of flowers.

Joseph loved his coat. He wore it everywhere. But when his older brothers saw it, they did not feel happy. They felt jealous. "Father loves Joseph more than us," they grumbled.

Joseph also had two special dreams. In them, it seemed his whole family was bowing down to him, as if one day he might be a leader. When he told his brothers, they grew more jealous still.

One day, when Joseph came out to the fields where his brothers were working, they decided to send him far away. Some traders were traveling down to the land of Egypt, and the brothers sent Joseph along with them, far from home.

It was a long, lonely road to Egypt. Joseph must have felt so afraid, and so far from everyone he loved.

But here is the most important thing of all: God was with Joseph. On that long road. In that strange new land where he knew no one. God never left his side, not for one moment.

And though Joseph could not see it yet, God had a good plan — one day, God would use Joseph to help save his whole family. The boy in the colorful coat was not alone. God was watching over him, all along.
