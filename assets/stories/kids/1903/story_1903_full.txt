Breakfast on the Shore

It was the early, early morning, and out on the wide lake, the sky was just beginning to turn soft shades of pink and gold. The water lay calm and still.

In a little fishing boat, some of Jesus' friends were tired and discouraged. They had been out fishing the whole night long — casting their nets again and again, hour after hour, in the dark. But they had caught nothing at all. Not one single fish. Now the sun was beginning to rise, and they were cold, and weary, and hungry, and ready to give up and go home.

Then, through the soft morning light, they noticed someone standing on the shore, a little way off. They could not quite make out who it was.

"Friends!" the man called out across the water, in a warm and friendly voice. "Have you caught any fish?"

"No," they called back. "Nothing all night."

"Then throw your net over the right side of the boat," he called. "Try there."

Well — it seemed a simple thing. But they did it. They tossed the net over the right side, and let it sink down. And all at once, the net grew heavy — so full of wriggling, shining fish that they could hardly haul it in!

And in that very moment, one of the friends — the one who loved Jesus dearly — knew. He caught his breath. "It is the Lord!" he cried. "It's Jesus!"

When Peter heard those words, he could not wait one second longer. He was so full of joy that he jumped straight into the water, and splashed and swam his way to the shore as fast as ever he could, just to get to Jesus.

And when they all came ashore at last, dragging their net bursting with fish — there was Jesus, waiting for them. And he had been busy. He had built a little fire of glowing red coals there on the sand. Fresh bread was warming beside it, and fish were sizzling gently on top. And the wonderful smell of breakfast cooking drifted out across the cool morning air.

"Bring some of the fish you've just caught," Jesus said, with a smile. "And come — come and have some breakfast."

So the tired, happy friends gathered around the warm little fire, there on the quiet beach. And Jesus took the bread, and handed it to them, and gave them fish as well. And they sat together and ate, in the gentle golden light of the sunrise, with the lake lapping softly nearby.

There was no great crowd that morning. There was no long speech, and no big miracle for everyone to see. There was just a warm fire, and good food, and friends together — and Jesus, their dear friend, alive again, cooking breakfast for the people he loved.

And it was one of the coziest, happiest, most peaceful mornings of their whole lives. For their dear friend Jesus was alive — and he loved them more than they could ever say.
