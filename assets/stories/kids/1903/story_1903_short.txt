Breakfast on the Shore

It was very early in the morning, and the sky over the lake was just beginning to turn pink and gold. Some of Jesus' friends had been out fishing all night long in their boat. But they had not caught a single fish — not one. They were tired, and cold, and hungry.

Then, as the sun came up, they saw someone standing on the shore. They could not quite tell who it was. "Friends!" the stranger called across the water. "Have you caught anything?"

"No," they called back. "Nothing at all."

"Try the other side of the boat," the man called. "Put your net down there."

So they did. And all at once, the net was so full of fish they could hardly pull it in!

Then one of the friends gasped. "It is the Lord!" he said. "It's Jesus!" And Peter was so happy that he jumped right into the water and splashed his way to shore as fast as he could go.

And there, waiting for them on the beach, was Jesus. He had made a little fire of glowing coals. Bread was warming beside it, and fish were cooking on top, and the good smell drifted out over the water.

"Come," said Jesus warmly. "Come and have some breakfast."

So they all sat down together by the fire, in the soft morning light, and Jesus gave them warm bread and fish to eat. There was no crowd. There was no big speech. Just Jesus, and his friends, and breakfast by the lake at sunrise.

And it was one of the coziest, happiest mornings they had ever known. For their dear friend Jesus was alive — and he loved them as much as ever.
