The Lost Coin

One day, Jesus wanted to show people just how much God loves them. So, as he so often did, he told them a little story.

"Once," Jesus said, "there was a woman who had ten silver coins. They were very special to her — perhaps the most precious things she owned. She kept them carefully, and now and then she would count them, just to see them all together. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. All ten, safe and shining.

"But one day, she counted them — and something was wrong. One, two, three… six, seven, eight, nine… and then she stopped. She counted again. Nine. There were only nine! One of her ten coins was missing. It was lost!

"Now, the woman could have shrugged her shoulders and said, 'Oh well. Nine coins is still a lot. I do not really need that last one.' But she did not say that. Not at all. That one lost coin was precious to her, and she simply could not leave it lost. So she made up her mind: she was going to find it.

"First, she lit a lamp, and held it up high, so its warm light could reach into every dark and shadowy corner. Then she picked up her broom, and she began to search.

"She looked under the table. She looked in all the corners. She swept behind the door, and under the chairs, and beneath the rug. She moved the baskets, and peeked into every dusty little crack. She shook out the blankets. She felt along the cool floor with her fingers. She searched the places she had already searched, and then she searched them all over again. She swept, and she swept, and she swept some more. She would not stop. She would not give up — not for anything — until that little coin was found.

"And then — there! Something glinted in the lamplight, way over in the corner. She bent down. And there it was: her lost coin!

" 'I found it!' she cried, holding it up high. 'I found my little coin!' "

Jesus smiled as he told it. And then he said that the woman was so full of joy she simply could not keep it to herself. She ran outside and called to all her friends and neighbors. "Come, come and be happy with me!" she said. "I had lost one of my coins, and now — now I have found it again!"

And they all celebrated together. What a happy sound filled that little house — laughing and clapping, and the pure joy of something lost being found again.

Then Jesus told them what the story really meant. "That," he said, "is exactly how God feels about you. When even one lost thing is found, the angels in heaven throw a party for joy!"

For that is how precious you are to God, little one. If you were ever lost, God would light his lamp, and search every corner, and never, ever give up — until he found you, and gathered you up, and held you close, with the greatest joy of all.
