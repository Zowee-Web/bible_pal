The Lost Coin

Jesus loved to tell stories, and here is one he told.

Once there was a woman who had ten special silver coins. She kept them safe, and she loved them, and she liked to count them: one, two, three… all the way up to ten.

But one day, when she counted, something was wrong. Eight… nine… and then — oh no! One coin was missing! She only had nine. One of her ten coins was lost!

Now, she could have said, "Well, I still have nine. That is plenty." But she did not. That one little lost coin mattered to her. So she made up her mind to find it.

She lit a bright lamp, so she could see into every shadow. She got her broom, and she began to sweep. She looked under the table. She looked in the corners. She looked behind the door, and beneath the rug, and in every dusty little place. She swept, and she swept, and she swept. She would not give up — not until she found it.

And then — there! A little shine in the corner. Her lost coin!

"I found it!" she cried. "I found my coin!"

She was so happy that she ran to her friends. "Come and celebrate with me!" she said. "I lost my little coin, and now I have found it again!" And they were all glad together.

Then Jesus told them something wonderful. "That is just how God feels," he said. "When even one lost thing is found, all of heaven fills up with joy."

For you are precious to God, little one. So precious that he would search, and search, and never, ever give up — until he found you.
