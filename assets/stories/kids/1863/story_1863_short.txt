Mary and Martha

Martha and Mary were two sisters who loved Jesus dearly. So when their dear friend came to visit their home one day, they were overjoyed — there was nothing they loved more than having Jesus come to stay.

Martha wanted everything to be just right for Jesus. So she got busy — very, very busy. She swept the floor and cooked the food and set the table. She bustled here and bustled there, working as hard as she could to make everything perfect.

But Mary did something different. Mary came and sat down quietly at Jesus' feet. And she listened. She listened to every word Jesus said, soaking it all in, just happy to be near him.

After a while, Martha grew tired and a little flustered. There was so much to do, and she was doing it all by herself! "Jesus," she said, "don't you mind that Mary is just sitting there? Please tell her to come and help me!"

But Jesus answered her gently and kindly. "Martha, dear Martha," he said. "You are working so hard, and worrying about so many things. But really, only one thing matters most of all. Mary has chosen it — she has chosen to be here with me. And that is a good thing, and it will not be taken away from her."

It was Jesus' gentle way of saying: the cooking and the cleaning can wait a little while. Sometimes the very best thing of all is simply to stop, and rest, and be close to the ones we love.
