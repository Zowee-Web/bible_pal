The Beatitudes

One day, Jesus sat down on a soft, grassy hillside, and the people gathered all around to listen. And Jesus began to tell them about the people God holds especially close to his heart. He calls them blessed — which means they are loved, and cared for, and safe with God.

Blessed are those who are sad, Jesus said, for God will comfort them.

Blessed are the gentle and the quiet.

Blessed are the kind and merciful, for kindness will come back to them.

Blessed are those whose hearts are pure and good.

Blessed are the peacemakers, the ones who help others get along, for they will be called the children of God.

And blessed are those who know they need God, for heaven belongs to them.

The people listened, there on the hillside in the sunshine, and Jesus' gentle words settled softly over them like evening light. For Jesus wanted every single person to know: God sees you. God loves you, just as you are. And whether you are sad, or gentle, or kind, or small — you are precious to him, and you are blessed.

So rest now, little one, close in the love of God. You are seen. You are loved. You are blessed.
