Jesus Calms the Storm

One evening, when the sky was growing dark, Jesus and his friends climbed into a little boat to sail across the lake. It had been a long day, and Jesus was tired. So he went to the back of the boat, rested his head on a soft cushion, and fell fast asleep.

While he slept, the wind began to blow. It blew harder and harder. Big waves rose up and splashed over the sides, and water began to fill the little boat. Jesus' friends held on tight. They were fishermen, and they knew this lake well — but they had never seen a storm like this one. They were afraid.

And through it all, Jesus stayed fast asleep on his cushion, as peaceful as could be.

"Teacher, wake up!" his friends cried. "Don't you care that we are sinking?"

Jesus opened his eyes. He stood up in the rocking boat, and he was not afraid, not even a little. He looked at the wild wind and the crashing waves, and he said, in a calm, strong voice, "Peace. Be still."

And all at once, the wind stopped. The waves lay down. The lake grew smooth and quiet, still as glass.

Jesus turned to his friends. "Why were you so afraid?" he asked them, gently.

They looked at the calm water, and then at Jesus, and they were filled with wonder. "Who is this," they whispered, "that even the wind and the waves do what he says?"

And the little boat sailed softly on, steady and safe, beneath the quiet evening stars.
