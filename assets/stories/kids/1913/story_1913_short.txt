The Shepherd Who Knows My Name

There was once a shepherd, Jesus said — a good shepherd, who took care of his sheep.

Now, a good shepherd doesn't just see a big woolly crowd of sheep that all look the same. Oh no. He knows every single one. He knows the little lamb with the wobbly legs, and the old sheep with the gentle eyes, and the bouncy one who is always wandering off. He has a name for each and every one. And he calls them by their names.

In the morning, when it is time to go out to the green fields, the shepherd opens the gate and calls. And all the sheep lift up their heads — because they know that voice. It is the voice that has fed them, and cared for them, and kept them safe, every single day. So they follow him, out to the cool grass and the still water, walking close behind the shepherd they trust.

And if even one little lamb were ever in trouble, the good shepherd would do anything at all to keep it safe. He would face the wolf. He would face the storm. There is nothing he would not do for his sheep, because he loves them so.

And do you know what Jesus said next? Jesus said, "I am the good shepherd."

He meant: that is how much I love you. I don't see you as just part of a big crowd. I see you — you, all by yourself. I know your name. I know your voice. You belong to me, and I will keep you close, and safe, and near.

So when the world feels very big, and you feel very small, you can remember the voice of the Good Shepherd — the One who knows your name, and calls you his own.
