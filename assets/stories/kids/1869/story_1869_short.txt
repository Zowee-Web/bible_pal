The Fruit of the Spirit

Have you ever seen an apple tree? An apple tree does not have to try hard to grow its apples. When the tree is healthy, and rooted down deep, and warmed by the sun, the apples simply grow.

The Bible says that God grows good things in us, too. When we stay close to God, his Spirit grows beautiful things inside our hearts, the way fruit grows on a tree. These good things are called the fruit of the Spirit.

God grows love in us — and joy — and peace.
He grows patience, so we can wait without fussing.
He grows kindness and goodness, so we can be good to the people around us.
He grows gentleness, so we are soft and careful with others.
He grows faithfulness, so we can be trusted.
And he grows self-control, so we can stop, and choose what is right.

You do not have to grow all these things by yourself, or all at once. Like fruit on a tree, they grow slowly, a little at a time, as you stay close to God.

So rest now, little one. God is growing good and beautiful things in you. And he is so very glad to call you his own.
