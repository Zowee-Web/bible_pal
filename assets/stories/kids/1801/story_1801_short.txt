David and the Giant

A long time ago, there was a shepherd boy named David. He watched his father's sheep on the green hills, and he sang songs to God under the wide blue sky. David was the youngest of all his brothers, but his heart was brave, because he knew God was always with him.

One day, David's father sent him to bring bread to his big brothers. They were soldiers in the king's army. But when David reached the army, he found the soldiers hiding behind the rocks. Their faces looked afraid.

"Why is everyone so worried?" David asked.

Then he heard it — heavy footsteps, thud, thud, thud. A giant named Goliath came stomping into the valley. He was taller than any man David had ever seen. "Send out a man to fight me!" he shouted. Every soldier stepped back. But David did not.

"He is too big," the soldiers whispered. "No one can stand against him."

David remembered something. Once a lion had come for his sheep, and once a bear, and God had kept him safe both times. "God helped me before," David said softly. "God will help me now."

The king gave David a heavy sword and armor, but they were far too big. So David set them down. Instead, he walked to a little stream and chose five smooth stones, round and cool in his hand. He slipped one stone into his sling.

Goliath laughed when he saw the shepherd boy. But David looked up at the giant, and his voice did not shake. "You come with a sword," David said. "But I come in the name of God, who is with me."

David swung his sling round and round — and the stone flew straight and true through the air. The giant swayed. And then the giant fell down to the ground, and the whole valley grew still.

The soldiers stood up from behind their rocks. The fear that had held them was gone. They had seen that God is greater than the greatest giant.

And David went home to his hills and his sheep and his songs, the same boy he had always been, with the same God beside him.
