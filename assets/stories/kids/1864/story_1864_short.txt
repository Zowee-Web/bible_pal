The Ascension

After Jesus rose from the dead, he did not leave his friends right away. He stayed with them for a while longer — talking with them, eating with them, and showing them again and again that he was truly, wonderfully alive. How happy they were to have him back!

But the time came when Jesus had something to tell them. He led his friends out to a hillside, and he gathered them close. He lifted up his hands and blessed them, the way you might bless someone you love very much before a journey.

"It is time for me to go back to my Father in heaven," Jesus told them. "But do not be sad. I will not leave you alone. I will be with you always — every single day, even to the very end. And soon I will send you a special helper, the Holy Spirit, to be with you too."

And then, as his friends watched, Jesus began to rise — gently up, and up, into the sky — higher and higher, until a soft cloud covered him, and they could not see him anymore.

The friends stood there, gazing up at the sky in wonder. And suddenly, two angels in white were standing beside them. "Why are you looking up at the sky?" the angels asked kindly. "Jesus has gone to heaven. And one day, he will come back again, just the same way."

So the friends went home — not crying, but smiling, their hearts full of joy and hope. For Jesus had not really left them at all. He was with them still, just as he had promised — and he always would be.
