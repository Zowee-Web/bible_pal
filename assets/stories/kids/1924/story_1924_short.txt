The Good News Is for Everyone

There was a soldier named Cornelius, and he was good and kind. He loved God with all his heart, and he prayed every single day. But Cornelius came from a faraway people. He was not one of God's own people the way the others were. Deep down, he must have wondered: Does God hear a prayer like mine? Am I really one God loves too?

One day, God sent a shining messenger to Cornelius. "God has heard your prayers," the messenger said. "He has not missed even one. Now send for a man named Peter."

Far away, God was teaching Peter something too. He was showing Peter that his love is not just for one kind of person. It is for everyone — every people, every place, every single one.

So Peter came to the house of Cornelius. And when he stepped through the door, his eyes grew wide. The house was full! Cornelius had gathered all his family, and all his friends, and all his neighbors. A whole roomful of people, waiting and hoping. People who had always thought the good news was for somebody else.

Peter looked around at them all, and a wonderful smile spread across his face. "Now I truly understand," he said. "God has no favorites! He loves people from every nation on earth. And the good news about Jesus — it is for you too."

And then something happened that nobody could ever forget.

As Peter told them all about Jesus, God's own Holy Spirit came down on every person in that room. On the soldier. On the children. On the family and the friends and the neighbors. Every single one. And the whole houseful of people who thought they were outsiders began to praise God out loud, glad all the way to the brim, their faces shining.

They could hardly believe it. The good news was for them. It had been for them all along.
