The Little Whisper

Elijah was one of God's helpers, and he was very, very tired. He had been brave for a long time, but now he felt afraid and worn out, and he ran far away, all the way to a tall mountain. There he found a cave, and crept inside to rest.

In the dark of the cave, God spoke to him kindly. "Elijah," God asked, "why are you here?"

"I have been trying so hard," said Elijah, "and now I am all alone, and I am tired."

"Come outside," said God. "Stand on the mountain. I am going to pass by."

So Elijah stood at the mouth of the cave. And then —

A great wind came roaring up the mountain, so strong it cracked the rocks. But God was not in the wind.

After the wind, the ground began to shake and rumble. But God was not in the shaking.

After that came a crackling fire. But God was not in the fire either.

And then, when all the loud and mighty things had passed, there came a sound so soft that Elijah had to hold his breath to hear it.

A little whisper.

Quiet as a feather. Soft as a breath on your cheek.

And Elijah knew, deep in his heart, that this was God. He wrapped his coat around his face and stepped closer, for God was very near.

God did not need the wind, or the shaking ground, or the fire. God had come close in a whisper — to tell his tired helper that he was loved, that he was not alone, and that he never had been.
