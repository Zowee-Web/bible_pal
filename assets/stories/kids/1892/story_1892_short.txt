Water from the Rock

God's people were traveling through a hot, dry desert. The sun beat down, and the sand stretched on and on, and there was no water anywhere. The people grew thirstier and thirstier. Their mouths were dry, and their throats were parched, and oh, how they longed for a cool drink!

Soon the people began to worry. "We are so thirsty!" they cried to Moses, their leader. "Where will we ever find water out here?"

So Moses did the very best thing he could do. He called out to God. "Please help us!" he prayed. "What shall I do?"

And God answered him. "Go on ahead," God said, "to that big rock over there. Take your special staff, and strike the rock — and you will see."

Now, that seemed a strange thing to do. Water, out of a hard, dry rock? But Moses trusted God. He walked up to the great rock, lifted his staff, and struck it.

And — splash! — out gushed water! Fresh, cool, sparkling water, pouring right out of the solid rock! It bubbled up and ran down in a clear, cool stream, more than enough for everyone.

The people cupped their hands and drank deeply, and laughed, and splashed the cool water on their faces. God had given them water in the middle of the desert — water from a rock!

So whenever you feel like you have run dry, little one, and you do not know where help will come from, remember the rock in the desert. God can bring good things out of the most surprising places. You only have to ask him.
