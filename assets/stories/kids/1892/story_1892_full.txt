Water from the Rock

God's people were on a long journey through a great, dry desert. Day after day, they walked beneath the burning sun, with hot sand all around them as far as they could see. And the longer they walked, the thirstier they became. There were no rivers out there, no wells, no streams — no water anywhere at all. Their water skins had run empty long ago, and even the strongest among them felt weak and weary.

Soon their mouths were dry, and their throats were parched. The little children whimpered for a drink. And the people grew worried, and then frightened. "We are so thirsty!" they cried out to Moses, their leader. "Where will we ever find water in a place like this?"

Moses understood. He was thirsty too. But he knew exactly where to turn. So he called out to God. "O Lord," he prayed, "please help us! These people are so thirsty. What should I do?"

And God answered him, kindly and clearly. "Go on ahead of the people," God said. "Take some of the leaders with you, and bring along the special staff you always carry. Walk to the great rock over there. And when you reach it — strike the rock with your staff. Then watch what I will do."

Now, this was a very strange thing to be told. Water? Out of a hard, dry rock? Whoever heard of such a thing! But Moses trusted God completely. So he walked up to the great rock, with all the people watching. He lifted his staff up high. And he struck the rock.

For one heartbeat, nothing happened at all. The people held their breath.

And then all at once — splash! — out came water! Cool, clear, sparkling water, gushing right out of the solid rock! It bubbled and poured and ran down over the stones in a fresh, cool stream, more than enough for every last person there. It was the most welcome sound in all the world — the splash and trickle of water, in a dry, dry land.

How the people cheered! They rushed to the stream and cupped the water in their hands and drank, and drank, and drank. They splashed it on their hot faces, and gave it to their thirsty children, and let their animals drink their fill. Mothers filled cups for sleepy little children, and tired old men smiled as the cool water ran down. And no one was thirsty anymore.

God had done it. He had brought cool, fresh water right out of a dry desert rock — enough for thousands and thousands of people. He had not forgotten them. He had given them exactly what they needed, just when they needed it most.

So whenever you feel like you have run all the way dry, little one, and you cannot see where any help could possibly come from, remember the rock in the desert. God can bring good and wonderful things out of the most surprising places. All you have to do is ask him.
