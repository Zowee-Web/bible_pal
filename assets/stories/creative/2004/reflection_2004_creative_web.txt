Sometimes we find something beautiful and want to hold it close forever. A tiny bird feels so soft and warm in our hands. We want to keep it safe right there with us.

But love isn't always about holding on tight. Sometimes love means opening our hands wide. It means watching something we care about spread its wings and soar into the big blue sky.

That can feel a little sad at first. And that's okay. Sad feelings and happy feelings can live together, like sunshine and rain on the same day.

The bird still remembers those gentle hands. And those gentle hands helped it fly.
