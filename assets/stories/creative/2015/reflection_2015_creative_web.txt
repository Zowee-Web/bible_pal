Sometimes the smallest things hold the biggest surprises inside. A little seed sits quietly in the dark dirt, wondering if anything will ever happen. Then the rain comes, soft and steady. Drop by drop, something begins to change. A tiny green shoot pushes up toward the sunlight. That small seed was never too small at all. It just needed time and a little help from the rain. Growing happens like that sometimes — slowly, quietly, when nobody is even watching. Every flower in every garden started out just as tiny. And every kid was once very, very small too.
