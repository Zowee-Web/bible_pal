There are places in the world that seem to know what people need before the people do. A bench in a garden, a chair by a window, a porch step facing the evening sky. They don't offer advice or solutions. They simply hold still and wait.

Perhaps peace is less a feeling we manufacture and more a thing we uncover — already present beneath the noise, the way the ground is always there beneath the snow. The work, if it can be called work, is mostly the willingness to sit down and let the stillness gather. Not to force calm, but to stop running from it long enough for it to arrive on its own terms.
