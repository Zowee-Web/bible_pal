The big truck backed into the driveway on a Tuesday morning. Its engine rumbled low and deep, and the ramp came down with a clang that echoed across the whole street.

Nora sat on her front porch steps and watched.

Across the road, two men in gray shirts carried a couch through Becca's front door. Becca's couch. The brown one with the soft cushions where they'd eaten popcorn and watched movies and built blanket forts so tall they touched the ceiling fan.

The couch disappeared into the dark inside of the truck.

Nora's mom came out and sat beside her. She didn't say anything. She just sat.

More things came through the door. Becca's bike. The kitchen table with the wobbly leg. A stack of boxes marked FRAGILE in big red letters.

Then Becca came outside.

She walked across the street slowly, holding something in both hands. It was a shoebox with a rubber band around it.

"These are for you," Becca said.

Nora took the box. Inside were all the friendship bracelets they had made together since first grade. Blue and purple and yellow and green, tangled up like a nest.

"I kept the ones you made me," Becca said. "They're in my suitcase already."

Nora's chin wobbled. Her eyes stung.

"I don't want you to go," she whispered.

Becca sat down next to her on the step. Their shoulders touched.

They didn't talk for a while. They just listened to the men loading boxes and the birds in the oak tree and a dog barking somewhere far away.

Becca's dad called her name from across the street.

Becca stood up. She squeezed Nora's hand once, hard, and then she ran back to the truck.

Nora watched the truck doors close. She watched it pull away, slow and heavy, until it turned the corner and was gone.

She looked down at the shoebox in her lap. She pulled out a purple bracelet and tied it around her wrist. Then she pulled out a blue one and tied that one too.

The oak tree rustled. A warm breeze came through, smelling like cut grass and summer.

Nora held her wrist up to the light and turned it slowly, watching the colors catch the sun.
