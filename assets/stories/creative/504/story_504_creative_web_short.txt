Kai looked up from his chair by the hearth, glasses perched on his nose. He took the parchment, smoothing it out. **"It's a story about a farmer,"** he began, **"one who planted his fields with faith, not just seeds."**

Years ago, Kai remembered when they'd first moved to this farm. The land was barren, but he had hope. He'd planted as if God would make it grow, despite the drought that year.

**"Read on, Pa,"** Aila urged, her eyes wide with curiosity.

Kai cleared his throat and continued, **"The farmer worked tirelessly, watering his fields by faith alone..."** As Kai read, he felt a warmth spread through him. The story was their story. The farmer faced ridicule, but he persevered, trusting in something unseen.

Aila watched him, her gaze softening as she listened to the familiar tale. **"It's you,"** she whispered when he finished.

Kai nodded, smiling. **"Yes, it is."** He'd planted his fields with faith all those years ago, and though droughts came, the harvest had been plentiful. Their life was simple but rich, just like the story they'd read together.

That night, as Kai lay in bed, he looked out at the stars twinkling over their farm. He whispered a prayer of thanksgiving, feeling the joyful peace that comes from knowing one's purpose. And so, under the watchful gaze of the night sky, Kai drifted off to sleep, ready for another day of planting seeds of faith.
