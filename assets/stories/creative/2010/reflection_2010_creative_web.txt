Sometimes after a long day, your legs feel so heavy. Your eyes want to close. The little fox felt just like that. He walked and walked until he could barely take one more step. And then — a warm den, right there waiting. Soft and cozy inside. He did not have to build it. He did not have to dig it. It was already ready for him. That feels so good, doesn't it? Like climbing into a bed with warm blankets after a really big day. Like someone already knew what you needed before you even asked. Rest is such a gentle gift.
