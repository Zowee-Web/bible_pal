Sometimes the best things happen when we open our hands. That little puppy loved his stick so much. It was his favorite one in the whole park. But when he shared it, something wonderful happened. His friends wanted to share too. Soon there were sticks everywhere! Sharing can feel a little tricky at first. It might feel like letting go of something special. But kindness has a funny way of coming back around. Like a ball that bounces right back to you. Like a smile that makes someone else smile too. The puppy's heart grew even bigger than his pile of sticks.
