## The Seedling's Prayer

The sun, a plump orange, hung low in the sky, casting long shadows over the dusty paths of Whispering Valley. In the heart of the village, beside a bubbling well, sat a weary girl named Lily. Her brown braids drooped over sun-kissed cheeks, and her dusty hands clutched a limp sunflower, its petals drooping downwards like a weary traveler.

Lily sighed, a sigh that carried the whispers of the wind through dry leaves. Her heart felt like a dusty jar, too heavy to lift. She yearned for something to ease the burden, something to bring joy back to her life.

Suddenly, a soft voice startled her. "Why the long face, little one?"

Lily looked up to see an old woman with eyes as deep and sparkly as the well's reflections. Her smile was as warm as the afternoon sun.

"My sunflower is sad," Lily mumbled, tears welling up in her eyes. "It's lost its color and its scent."

The old woman chuckled. "Even the strongest plants need a little help sometimes. But guess what? Even the smallest seed, planted in the right place and with a kind touch, can grow into something truly magnificent."

She gently took the sunflower from Lily's hands and planted it in the soft earth beside the well. "Now, we just need a little prayer," she whispered.

The old woman closed her eyes and spoke in a language filled with love and hope. "Dear Heavenly Gardener, please fill this flower with life anew. Give it strength and sunshine, and let its fragrance fill the air with joy once more. Amen."

A warm feeling spread through Lily's heart like sunlight warming the earth. Her tears flowed freely, not of sadness, but of hope. She watched as the sunflower's wilted head slowly lifted, a tiny green shoot pushing through the soil.

The old woman smiled and placed her hand on Lily's shoulder. "Remember, little one, even the smallest seed can grow into something truly beautiful. Just believe in yourself and your power to make things grow."
