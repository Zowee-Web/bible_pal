Little pebbles are easy to find. They fit right in your hand. But when someone leaves one at your door, it feels like a tiny surprise. It says, "I was thinking about you." A pebble does not cost anything. It does not need wrapping paper or a bow. Still, it can make a whole morning feel brighter. Sometimes the smallest gifts carry the biggest feelings inside them. A kind thought can be like that too — small and quiet, but warm. People notice when someone remembers them. Even a little rock on a doorstep can feel like a hug.
