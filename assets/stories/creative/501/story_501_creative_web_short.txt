The morning sun slipped through the window, warming Lily's cozy bed. She stretched and smiled, still half asleep.

Something soft tapped her nose.

She blinked.

A tiny brown seed bounced gently into her hand.

"Hello!" it chirped.

Lily sat up quickly. "You can talk?"

The seed wiggled happily. "I'm a grateful seed," it said. "I grow best when I remember all the good things around me."

Lily tilted her head. "How do you do that?"

The seed gave a tiny bounce. "I notice them. The sunshine. The breeze. The little things most people forget."

Lily looked toward the window. The sunlight felt warm on her face.

She smiled.

"I think I can try that," she said.

⸻

That day, Lily paid closer attention.

When the baker handed her a warm piece of bread, she paused. "Thank you," she said, holding it carefully in her hands.

At school, when her friend shared a bright blue crayon, Lily smiled. "Thanks," she said again.

Even the wind felt different as it brushed past her, cool and gentle.

Each time she noticed something good, her chest felt a little warmer.

⸻

That night, Lily lay in bed, the quiet of the room wrapping around her.

The seed rested beside her on the pillow.

"You grew today," it said softly.

Lily looked at it. "I didn't get bigger."

The seed laughed. "Not like that. Your heart did."

Lily thought about the bread, the crayon, the breeze.

She smiled and pulled her blanket close.

"Thank you," she whispered into the quiet.

The room felt warm and peaceful.

And this time—

she noticed.
