Lily walked through the field, hand in hand with Mama. The warm sunshine rested gently on her shoulders. They picked bright red apples and sat together in the grass, sharing them.

Lily smiled and leaned close. "Thank you, Mama," she said softly.

Mama squeezed her hand.

For a moment, everything felt still and good.

Lily looked at the apples, the field, and the sky above her.

She noticed how much she had.

And quietly, in her heart—

she was thankful.
