Joy has a way of hiding in the unhurried moments — the ones we're most tempted to rush past on our way to somewhere else. A conversation on a porch, the scent of something peeled slowly, the sound of a laugh that comes from the whole chest and doesn't apologize for itself.

It's curious how delight often lives not in the grand occasions but in the ordinary ones we give enough time to. The orange is the same orange whether you peel it in eight seconds or five minutes. But something happens in the longer version — something that has to do with presence, and attention, and the willingness to let sweetness unfold at its own pace.
