Waves have a way of changing things at the beach. One moment a sandcastle stands tall, and the next moment it melts back into wet sand. That can feel really sad inside. But something special happened when his sister sat down beside him. She didn't fix the sadness right away. She just started building. Sometimes the best thing in the whole world is someone who stays close when something breaks. A new castle might look different from the first one. It might even have more towers. And the warm feeling of building it together — that part the waves can never wash away.
