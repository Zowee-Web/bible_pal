Callum pressed his hands into the wet sand. He shaped a tower with careful fingers. Then he smoothed the walls around it. His sandcastle had a little doorway and a bridge made from a flat shell.

The beach smelled like salt and seaweed. Warm afternoon air brushed his cheeks. Small waves curled and uncurled far from his feet. He had been working for a long time.

"Look, Petra," he called to his sister. She sat on a towel nearby, reading a book with bent corners. "It's the best one I ever made."

Petra looked up and smiled. "That's really good, Callum."

He sat back on his heels and admired every part. The tower stood straight. The shell bridge held firm. He felt proud and happy.

Then the tide came in a little closer. A wide, foamy wave slid across the sand. It reached his castle and melted the walls flat. The tower crumbled. The shell bridge floated sideways and disappeared into the foam.

Callum stared at the smooth, wet place where his castle had been. His chest felt tight. His eyes burned. He pulled his knees up and rested his chin on them.

Petra set her book down and walked over. She sat beside him in the damp sand. She didn't say anything for a while. She just stayed close, her shoulder touching his.

"It was a really good castle," she said quietly.

Callum nodded. A tear rolled down his cheek. "I worked so hard on it."

"I know," Petra said. She picked up a handful of sand and let it fall slowly through her fingers. "Want to build another one? Together this time. We could make it higher up the beach, where the waves can't reach."

Callum wiped his face with the back of his hand. He looked at the dry sand further from the water. It was pale and soft up there, near the dune grass.

"Okay," he said.

They walked together to the higher ground. Petra carried a bucket. Callum brought the little shovel. They knelt side by side and started digging.

This time, Callum made the tower while Petra shaped a wide wall around it. She found two perfect white shells for windows. He pressed a piece of driftwood into the top like a flag.

"This one has windows," Callum said. A small smile came back to his face.

"And a flag," Petra added.

The new castle stood sturdy on the dry sand. A breeze carried the smell of the ocean toward them. Somewhere behind the dunes, a bird sang three clear notes.

Callum leaned against his sister's arm. "Thanks for helping me, Petra."

She bumped her shoulder gently against his. "Always."

They sat together, looking at what they had made, while the afternoon stretched out easy and warm around them.
