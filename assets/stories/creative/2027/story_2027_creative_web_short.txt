Marcus noticed the new girl at recess. She sat on the brick wall near the basketball court, watching everyone play. Her lunch bag sat unopened on her lap.

Nobody had talked to her all day. Marcus knew what that felt like. Last year, he had been the new kid.

He dribbled his basketball twice, thinking. His friends were already picking teams for a game. He could join them. He almost did.

Instead, he walked over to the wall.

"Hey," he said. "I'm Marcus."

The girl looked up. "I'm Juliet." Her voice was small.

"Did you just move here?"

She nodded. "From far away. Everything is different."

Marcus sat beside her on the wall. The bricks were warm from the afternoon sun. "Yeah," he said. "I remember that feeling. It gets better, though."

"When?"

He thought about it honestly. "A couple weeks, maybe. But some parts get better faster."

She looked at the basketball court. "I'm not very good at sports."

"That's okay. Neither is Danny, and he plays every day." He pointed at a short boy who tripped over his own feet and laughed about it.

Juliet almost smiled.

"You want to know a secret?" Marcus said. "The first week is the hardest. After that, you start knowing where things are. You find the good water fountain. You figure out which lunch lady gives extra crackers."

"Extra crackers?" Now she did smile.

"Mrs. Benitez. Third one from the left. Just say please."

The bell rang. They slid off the wall together.

"Marcus?" Juliet said as they walked toward the doors.

"Yeah?"

"Thanks for sitting with me."

He shrugged like it was nothing. But it wasn't nothing. He knew that.

He held the door open, and she walked through.
