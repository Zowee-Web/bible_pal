"Nova, why are you tilling this old, hard ground?" Nova's neighbor, Eli, wiped sweat from his brow as he watched Nova work.

Years ago, Nova had planted seeds here during a drought. No rain came, and the seeds withered. But Nova believed in second chances. He remembered when God told him, "I will make rivers flow on barren heights, and springs will arise." So, he kept tilling.

Eli scratched his head. "You've been at this for weeks now. Nothing's growing."

Nova leaned on his hoe. "Patience, Eli. Remember the mustard seed? Small but powerful." He pointed to a tiny sprout poking through the soil. "Look! Life is pushing its way up."

Eli squinted. "I see it. But what if it dies too?"

"Then I'll plant again," Nova said simply.

Days turned into weeks, and Eli became an unwilling witness to Nova's perseverance. He saw Nova wake before dawn, till the soil, sing to his plants like they were children, and pray for rain.

One afternoon, as Eli watched from his porch, he noticed something unusual. Nova was standing still, hands raised. Then, he looked up at the sky with a smile. Eli followed Nova's gaze. Dark clouds gathered, and the first drop of rain fell on Nova's outstretched hand.

Eli rushed over as thunder rumbled. "Nova! You'd better get inside!"

But Nova just laughed, turning his face up to the sky. "It's finally happening, Eli! Just like I believed it would."

The rain poured down, drenching them both. When it finally stopped, Eli looked at the now-soggy ground and shook his head. "You're either very brave or very foolish, Nova."

Nova just smiled, holding up a green leaf from one of his plants. "I choose brave," he said.
