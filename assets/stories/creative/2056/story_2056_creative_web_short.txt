Mabel stood outside the door of Room 14 and counted the tiles on the hallway floor. Twenty-two from the water fountain to the door. She had counted them three times.

Inside, she could hear chairs scraping and kids talking. It was her first day at a new school. Her old school was two states away. Her old friends were two states away. Everything familiar was two states away.

Her mother had said, "You'll make friends fast. You always do."

Mabel wasn't so sure about that.

She pushed the door open. The room went quiet for half a second — the way rooms do when someone new walks in — and then the noise started again. A teacher with curly hair and glasses smiled at her from the front.

"You must be Mabel. There's a seat by the window."

Mabel walked to the desk by the window. She sat down. She put her backpack on the floor and folded her hands on the desk and looked straight ahead.

The girl next to her had red sneakers and a ponytail. She was drawing something on the corner of her notebook — a cat with enormous eyes.

"That's a good cat," Mabel said. She hadn't planned to say it. The words just came out.

The girl looked up. "Thanks. His name is Beans." She turned the notebook so Mabel could see better. "I draw him on everything. My mom says it's a problem."

Mabel almost smiled. "I had a cat named Soup."

The girl's eyes got wide. "Soup?"

"He was orange."

The girl laughed — a short, surprised laugh, like a hiccup. "I'm Vera."

"I'm Mabel."

The teacher started talking about fractions. Mabel opened her notebook. On the corner of the first page, Vera had already drawn a tiny orange cat labeled SOUP.
