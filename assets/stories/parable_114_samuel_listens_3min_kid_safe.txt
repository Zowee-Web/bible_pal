## Young Samuel Learning to Listen

The quiet hum of the temple lamplight bathed the holy space in a warm glow. Gentle whispers danced on the night breeze, carrying the scent of spices and ancient prayers. Young Samuel, nestled in his small bed near the altar, felt a peaceful slumber approaching.

Then, a soft whisper seemed to brush against his ear. "Samuel?" It was a gentle voice, like the rustle of falling leaves. He blinked, confusion flickering in his eyes.

"Yes?" he whispered back, his heart pounding ever so slightly.

"It is I, the Lord," the voice spoke again, a touch of kindness in its tone. "Can you come closer?"

Samuel cautiously approached Eli, the wise old priest who had become his mentor. A gentle smile rested on Eli's face. "The Lord wishes to speak with you, Samuel," he explained, his voice soothing as the night wind.

Days earlier, Samuel had longed to hear God's voice for himself. He had diligently practiced stillness, learning to quiet his heart and listen. Tonight, the opportunity had finally arrived.

Eli placed a hand on Samuel's shoulder and whispered, "Close your eyes and listen. Let the Lord's voice fill your heart with love and guidance."

Slowly, Samuel closed his eyes. He surrendered to the peaceful surroundings, silencing every thought. In the quiet of his soul, he heard it - a gentle whisper, like the whisper of the wind. It seemed to say, "I love you, Samuel. You are special and chosen."

A warm feeling of love enveloped him like the comforting embrace of a loving father. He felt safe and cherished, knowing that God was always near, watching over him with love.

When morning arrived, Samuel shared his experience with Eli. The wise priest smiled, his eyes filled with warmth. "You have learned a valuable lesson, Samuel," he said. "The Lord's voice is always gentle and loving."

As Samuel lay back in his bed, the stars twinkled brightly outside his window. The night was quiet, peaceful. He closed his eyes, feeling the gentle love of God surrounding him. "Goodnight," he whispered, "may sweet dreams fill your night."
