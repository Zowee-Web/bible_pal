Two of them were walking the road to Emmaus.

And they were sad.

A stranger came near, and went with them. They did not know him.

They told him everything. How they had hoped. And how the hope had ended.

And as he opened the Scriptures to them — their hearts began to burn within them.

When they came to the village, they urged him: “Stay with us. It is almost evening. The day is almost over.”

So he went in.

And when he sat down at the table — he took the bread. He gave thanks. He broke it.

And their eyes were opened.

And they knew him.

Then he vanished out of their sight.

They said one to another:

“Weren’t our hearts burning within us, while he spoke to us along the way?”
