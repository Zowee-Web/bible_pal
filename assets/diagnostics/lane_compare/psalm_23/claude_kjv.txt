Upon the hill stood the shepherd, and the flock was gathered round about him. The grass beneath their hooves was thick and yet untrodden, and a small wind moved among the blades. The sheep cropped the green pasture, and not one was wanting.

He led them down by a slow path, and the path bent toward water. The brook lay still between low banks, neither rushing nor troubled, and the surface of it caught the noon light. The sheep stooped, and they drank, and the shepherd stood beside them with his staff resting in the crook of his arm.

When they had drunk their fill, he turned them upon the right way. The way wound between hills, and the hills cast long shadows across the path, for the sun was bending toward the west. A narrow place opened before them, and the walls of stone rose up on either hand, and the shadow within was deep.

The shepherd went first into the gorge. The sheep followed close at his heel. From somewhere above, a stone fell and clattered upon the path, and the sheep startled, and the shepherd spake softly, and they came on. In his right hand was the rod, and in his left the staff, and he held them both before him as he walked. The shadow lay heavy in that place, yet not one of the flock turned back.

They came forth into a wide field at the going down of the sun. There a table had been set upon the grass — a board laid plain upon the earth, with bread upon it, and a cup, and oil in a small flask of horn. Round about the field, upon the ridges, there stood other men with spears, who watched and came not down. The shepherd took the oil and poured it upon the head of his servant, and the oil ran down, and the cup was filled until the wine brimmed over the lip and wetted the wood.

He sat to meat in the sight of them that hated him, and he did eat, and he did drink. The watchers upon the hills moved not.

When the meal was ended, the lamps were lit within a house beyond the field. The door stood open, and the light fell out upon the threshold, and the shepherd rose and walked toward it. Behind him followed goodness, and behind him followed mercy, all the days appointed unto him. And he came unto the house, and he went in, and the door remained open behind him.
