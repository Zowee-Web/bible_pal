The morning sun climbed over the hills near Capernaum, and a wide crowd gathered along the slope above the lake. Fishermen with salt still on their hands, mothers carrying small children, old men leaning on staves, and travelers from Decapolis pressed close together on the grass. The Sea of Galilee spread below them, blue and quiet, with a few boats drawn up on the shore.

Jesus walked up the hillside. When he saw the crowd, he sat down on a flat stone, and his disciples gathered around him. The people stilled. A breeze moved through the grass and lifted the edges of their cloaks.

He opened his mouth and began to teach them.

"Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven.

"Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.

"Blessed are the gentle, for they shall inherit the earth.

"Blessed are those who hunger and thirst after righteousness, for they shall be filled.

"Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy.

"Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.

"Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God.

"Blessed are those who have been persecuted for righteousness' sake, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven."

His voice carried clearly over the hillside. A child shifted on her mother's lap. An old man leaned forward, cupping a hand to his ear. The disciples sat near his feet, watching his face as he spoke.

Jesus looked out over the crowd and continued.

"Blessed are you when people reproach you, persecute you, and say all kinds of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceedingly glad, for great is your reward in heaven. For that is how they persecuted the prophets who were before you."

He paused. A gull cried out over the water below. The breeze rolled across the grass again, bending the small wildflowers between the stones.

The crowd did not move. A fisherman wiped his brow with the back of his hand. Two women glanced at each other. Near the front, Peter sat with his hands resting on his knees, his eyes fixed on Jesus.

The sound of the lake lapping the shore reached them faintly from below. Somewhere down the slope, a donkey brayed, and a man hushed it. The sun rose higher, warming the stones, and Jesus drew breath to speak again to the gathered people on the mountain.
