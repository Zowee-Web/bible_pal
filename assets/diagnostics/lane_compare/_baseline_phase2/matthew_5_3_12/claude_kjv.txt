Upon the slope above the sea of Galilee the multitudes gathered, drawn from the towns and the fishing shores, from the dusty roads of Judea and the country beyond Jordan. The grass lay green beneath their feet, for the rains had passed, and small flowers stirred where sandals trod. A wind came off the water, cool upon the brow, carrying the cry of gulls and the far creak of moored boats.

Jesus, beholding the press of them, went up into the mountain. He sat down, as the rabbis sit when they would teach. His disciples drew near unto him, and the people leaned forward upon the hillside, that they might catch the sound of his voice.

He opened his mouth, and taught them, saying:

Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

A hush came upon the slope. A child shifted in his mother's arms. Jesus spake on.

Blessed are they that mourn, for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.

Somewhere among the throng a man bowed his head. An old woman gripped her staff the tighter. The wind moved through the grass, and the words went forth upon it.

Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness, said he, for they shall be filled.

He paused. The brown hills lay folded in the distance, and the lake glimmered far below.

Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God. Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called the children of God.

His voice was even, and it carried. Men who had come up from the road set down their bundles. Fishermen who had left their nets stood with arms folded, listening.

Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake, said Jesus, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

He looked upon them, upon the faces lifted toward him, upon the weathered hands and the patched garments. Then he stretched forth his words unto them as a man speaketh unto his own.

Blessed are ye, said he, when men shall revile you, and persecute you, and shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceeding glad, for great is your reward in heaven. For so persecuted they the prophets which were before you.

The wind passed over the mountain. The multitude stood still upon the grass, and Jesus sat among his disciples, and spake on.
