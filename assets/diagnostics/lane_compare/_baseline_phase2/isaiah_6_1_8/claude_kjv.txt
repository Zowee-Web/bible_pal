In the year that king Uzziah died, Isaiah came into the house of the Lord. The lamps burned low along the walls. The smoke of the morning offering yet lingered in the courts, curling slow against the cedar beams.

And on a sudden, the place was changed.

Isaiah lifted up his eyes, and beheld the Lord upon a throne, high and lifted up. His train filled the temple. The hem of his robe poured down the steps and spread upon the stone floor, and spread further still, until every corner of the great hall was covered with the trailing of it.

Above the throne stood the seraphim. Each one had six wings. With twain he covered his face, and with twain he covered his feet, and with twain he did fly. They hovered there, the air stirred beneath their wings.

And one cried unto another. The voice of the first rang out, calling the holiness of the Lord of hosts, declaring that the whole earth was full of his glory. Then answered the second, and then the third, each taking up the cry. The sound of it broke against the pillars. The posts of the door shook upon their hinges. The thresholds trembled beneath Isaiah's feet. And the house was filled with smoke, thick and white, rolling between him and the throne.

Isaiah fell.

He cried out, his voice small beneath the singing of the seraphim. Woe was him, for he was undone. He was a man of unclean lips, and he dwelt in the midst of a people of unclean lips, and his eyes had seen the King, the Lord of hosts.

Then flew one of the seraphim unto him. In his hand was a live coal, taken with the tongs from off the altar. The coal glowed red, and the seraph came swiftly through the smoke, and laid it upon Isaiah's mouth.

The seraph spake. This had touched his lips; his iniquity was taken away, and his sin purged.

Isaiah lay upon the stone. The coal was gone. The smoke yet rolled.

Then came the voice of the Lord. It went forth from the throne, and it asked whom he should send, and who would go for them.

Isaiah lifted himself from the floor. He raised his face toward the throne, and he raised his hand also, and he spake.

Here am I. Send me.

The seraphim hovered yet above the throne. The hem still filled the temple. The smoke moved among the pillars, and the thresholds were still.
