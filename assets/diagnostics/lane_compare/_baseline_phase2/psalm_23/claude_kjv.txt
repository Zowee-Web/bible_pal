The hills lay quiet at the breaking of the day, and a thin mist rose from the low places. Down the slope came the flock, white against the grey grass, and before them walked the shepherd, his staff in his hand and his rod at his side.

He spake unto the sheep as he went, calling them by their voices, and they followed after him. The grass beneath their feet was green and thick with dew. He led them down to where the land grew level, and there he stayed his steps, and the flock spread out and bowed their heads to feed.

By and by he turned aside, and the sound of running water came up out of a hollow. The stream moved softly between its stones. He led the sheep beside it, where the waters were still and the banks were low, and they drank without fear.

The sun climbed. He walked among them, and where one was weary he laid his hand upon its back. Where one had wandered, he went after it and brought it again. The path bent at last between two high walls of stone, and the shadow within was long and cold. The flock entered, and the shepherd entered with them. His rod was lifted, and his staff went before. No sheep was lost in the dark place; the sound of his voice went on before them, and they came up out of the valley into the open land.

When the day was far spent, he brought them unto a table of pasture set upon the high ground. Round about, in the further hills, were the shapes of them that watch and wait — wolves upon the ridges, lean and still — yet the sheep did eat in plain sight of them, and were not troubled. The shepherd took oil from a horn at his belt, and he anointed the head of one whose brow was torn, and another whose ear was rent, until each that had need was tended. He set down a vessel of water, and it ran over upon the stones.

The evening came. He led them in unto the fold, and stood himself in the door of it. One by one they passed beneath his hand, and he numbered them as they went in. The last of the light went out of the west. Within the fold the sheep lay down together; without, the shepherd remained, his staff across his knees, and the stars came forth above the hills.
