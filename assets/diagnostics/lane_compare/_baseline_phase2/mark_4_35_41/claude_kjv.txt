When evening was come, Jesus spake unto his disciples, saying, Let us pass over unto the other side. So they left the multitude behind upon the shore, and took him even as he was into the ship. And there were also with him other little ships.

The sun went low upon the water. The sail was loosed, and the prow turned toward the farther coast. The voices upon the beach grew faint. The oars dipped, and the lake lay open before them.

Then arose a great storm of wind. The clouds gathered black above the hills. The waters of Galilee were stirred, and the waves began to beat upon the ship, and to break over the sides thereof. The vessel was filled, and the timbers groaned beneath the weight of the sea.

The disciples laid hold upon the ropes. Some bailed with their hands and with vessels. The wind tare at the sail; the rain came down upon them. They cried one to another above the roaring of the storm, and their feet slipped upon the wet boards of the ship.

But Jesus was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep upon a pillow. The waves smote the wood beside his head, and yet he slept on.

They came unto him, and they awoke him. Master, said they, carest thou not that we perish?

He arose. He stood up in the midst of the tossing ship, and he looked upon the sea. He rebuked the wind, and he said unto the sea, Peace, be still.

And the wind ceased. The waves sank down. The water grew smooth round about the ship, and there was a great calm. The clouds parted, and the night was quiet upon the lake.

He turned then unto his disciples. Why are ye so fearful? said he. How is it that ye have no faith?

They stood upon the wet planks. The ropes hung loose; the sail moved gently. They looked one upon another, and they looked upon him. The water lapped against the sides of the ship with a low sound.

And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?

The little ships came near upon the still water. The oars were taken up again. The prow of the ship turned once more toward the farther shore, and they went forward across the quiet sea, and the stars came out above them.
