On a hillside above the lake, a crowd gathered around Jesus. The grass was bent under their sandals, and a warm breeze came off the water below. Fishermen had left their boats pulled up on the shore. Mothers held children on their hips. Men leaned on walking sticks. Some sat. Some stood at the back, craning to see.

Jesus went up the slope and sat down. His disciples came close around him. He looked out across the faces in front of him, and he began to speak.

"Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven.

Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.

Blessed are the gentle, for they shall inherit the earth.

Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.

Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy.

Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.

Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God.

Blessed are those who have been persecuted for righteousness' sake, for theirs is the Kingdom of Heaven."

A hawk circled high above the slope. The lake glittered behind him. No one spoke. A child shifted in her mother's arms. Somewhere down the hill, a goat bell rang once and went quiet.

Jesus looked at the men nearest him, the ones who had left their nets to follow.

"Blessed are you when people reproach you, persecute you, and say all kinds of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceedingly glad, for great is your reward in heaven. For that is how they persecuted the prophets who were before you."

His voice carried clearly down the slope. A man at the edge of the crowd lowered his head. An older woman pressed her hand against her mouth. Two of the disciples glanced at each other.

The wind moved through the grass. A few thin clouds drifted across the hills on the far side of the water. Jesus rested his hands on his knees and waited, and the crowd waited with him.

A small boy slipped through the legs of the adults and sat down at the front, close to where Jesus sat. He pulled his knees up to his chest. Jesus looked at him, then lifted his eyes again to the gathered people on the slope.

The lake lapped against the stones below. The hawk turned once more and drifted south. Jesus drew a breath to continue.
