Upon the slope above the sea of Galilee the multitude pressed close, men of the villages and women with children at their hems, fishermen with salt yet upon their hands, and beggars who had walked far upon the dust of the road. The grass was green from the early rains, and a wind moved across it, bending the stalks toward the water below.

Jesus went up into the mountain. When he was set, his disciples came unto him, and the people gathered round about, sitting upon the earth in great companies. He opened his mouth, and the murmur of the crowd fell away. Even the gulls upon the shore grew still.

Blessed, he said, are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

A man near the front leaned forward upon his staff. Beside him a woman drew her shawl closer about her shoulders.

Blessed are they that mourn, said Jesus, for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.

The wind stirred the hem of his garment. He looked upon the faces nearest him, then upon those farther off, where the slope ran down toward the sea.

Blessed, he said, are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled.

A child shifted in her mother's lap. Somewhere among the company a man wept quietly into his sleeve.

Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God. Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.

He paused. A flock of small birds rose from the grass below and turned together upon the wind. His voice came again, and it carried far upon the hillside.

Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

Then he stretched forth his hand toward those gathered closest unto him, and his words came slower, and weightier.

Blessed are ye, said he, when men shall revile you, and persecute you, and shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceeding glad: for great is your reward in heaven. For so persecuted they the prophets which were before you.

The wind passed again across the grass. The disciples sat near unto his feet, and the multitude beyond them sat unmoving upon the slope. Far below, the waters of Galilee shone, and a single fishing boat moved slowly upon the surface, its sail bent toward the shore.
