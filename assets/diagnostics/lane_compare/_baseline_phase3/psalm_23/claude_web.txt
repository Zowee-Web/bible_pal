The shepherd walked ahead of the flock with a long staff in his hand. The sun rose over the hills, and the sheep followed the sound of his voice along a narrow path. The grass below the ridge was thick and green, still wet from the night. He led them down into it, and they lowered their heads to feed.

A stream ran through the field, and the water moved slowly between smooth stones. The shepherd stood at its edge until each sheep came to drink. He counted them as they passed. None were missing.

When they had finished, he turned them toward a worn trail that climbed between two slopes. The path was old and marked by many feet. He walked it without hesitation, and the sheep walked behind him. He sang as he went, and his voice carried over the rocks.

The trail narrowed into a valley where the cliffs rose high on either side. Shadows fell across the ground, and the air grew cool. Somewhere above, a bird called once and went quiet. The sheep pressed closer together. The shepherd lifted his rod from his belt and held it ready in one hand, his staff steady in the other. He spoke low to the flock, and they kept moving. No animal came out from the rocks. He led them through, and the sun met them on the other side.

By evening they reached an open place where a long table had been set on the grass. Bread lay on the cloth, and oil stood in a small clay jar. Beyond the table, at the edge of the trees, dark shapes watched from a distance and did not come closer.

The shepherd took the jar and poured oil over the head of each sheep that came to him. The oil ran down their wool and caught the last light. He filled a cup from a skin of water and drank, and the cup overflowed onto the table and onto the ground.

The flock lay down around him. He walked among them slowly, touching each one, checking their feet and their sides. The watchers at the tree line turned and disappeared into the woods.

Night came, and the stars opened above the field. A small fire burned beside the table. The shepherd sat near it with his staff across his knees, and the sheep slept around him in a wide circle. He kept watch through the hours, and when the dawn came, he rose and led them on, and they followed him home.
