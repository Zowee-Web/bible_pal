In the year that king Uzziah died, Isaiah came into the house of the Lord. The lamps burned low along the walls, and the smoke of the morning incense yet hung in the air, slow and grey above the altar.

Then was the veil drawn back from his eyes.

He beheld the Lord, high and lifted up upon a throne. The hem of his robe poured down and filled the temple, fold upon fold, until the stones of the floor were hid beneath it. The pillars seemed to draw back. The chamber widened beyond its walls.

Above the throne stood the seraphim. Each had six wings: with two they shielded their faces, with two they covered their feet, and with two they held themselves aloft. They hovered without sound of feathers, only a steady burning, as of coals taken from the fire.

One called, and another answered him across the throne.

"Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts."

The voice was not as the voice of men. The doorposts shook upon their hinges. The threshold trembled beneath Isaiah's feet. A second seraph took up the cry, and a third, and the house began to fill with smoke, thick and rolling, until the throne itself was wreathed and the wings shone dim through the cloud.

Then Isaiah fell upon his face.

"Woe is me," he cried out, "for I am undone. I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell among a people of unclean lips. Mine eyes have seen the King, the Lord of hosts."

His hands were pressed against the stones. The smoke moved over him. The chanting did not cease.

Then one of the seraphim came down unto him. In its hand was a pair of tongs, and in the tongs a live coal, taken from off the altar. The coal burned red, and the air about it shimmered.

The seraph laid the coal upon his mouth.

"Behold," it said, "this hath touched thy lips. Thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged."

Isaiah lifted his face from the floor. His lips were marked. The smoke still rolled, and the seraphim still burned above the throne, and the doorposts trembled yet.

Then came the voice of the Lord himself, sounding through the chamber.

"Whom shall I send? And who will go for us?"

Isaiah rose up from the stones. He stood within the smoke, before the throne, beneath the wings.

And he answered, "Here am I. Send me."
