Upon a hillside above the sea of Galilee, the morning sun rose pale over the water, and the grass stood damp with the dew of night. A great multitude came up the slope — men with the dust of the road upon their sandals, women bearing children, and the sick borne upon mats by their kinsmen. They gathered in a wide half-circle where the land sloped gently downward, and the wind passed over them with the smell of the lake.

Jesus, seeing the press of the crowd, went up further into the hill and sat upon a stone. His disciples came near unto him. The murmur of voices fell away. Even the children grew still.

He lifted up his eyes, and he began to speak.

Blessed, he said, are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

A man with empty hands let them rest upon his knees. The wind stirred the hem of his garment.

Blessed are they that mourn, said Jesus, for they shall be comforted.

A woman in dark cloth bowed her head. Another laid a hand upon her shoulder.

Blessed are the meek, he said, for they shall inherit the earth. Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness, for they shall be filled.

He paused. Somewhere down the slope a lamb bleated, and the herdsman hushed it. Jesus spoke again, and his voice carried clear across the gathered company.

Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.

A boy upon his father's shoulder turned his face toward the sound. Men leaned forward where they sat upon the grass.

Blessed are the peacemakers, said Jesus, for they shall be called the children of God.

Then he looked upon them, and his words grew weightier still.

Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

The disciples drew nearer. Peter sat with his hands open upon his lap. John watched the master's face.

Blessed are ye, said Jesus, when men shall revile you, and persecute you, and shall speak all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceeding glad — for great is your reward in heaven. For so persecuted they the prophets which were before you.

The sun climbed higher above the hill. A breeze passed through the long grass, and bent it, and passed on. The multitude sat very still upon the slope, and Jesus spake yet further unto them.
