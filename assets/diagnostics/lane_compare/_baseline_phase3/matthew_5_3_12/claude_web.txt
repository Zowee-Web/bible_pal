On a hillside above the Sea of Galilee, the grass lay flat where many feet had walked. The morning sun was warm. A breeze came up from the water, smelling of fish and wet stone. Crowds had gathered from the towns along the shore, and they sat on the slope, some on cloaks spread over the ground, some leaning on staffs.

Jesus climbed a little way up the hill and sat down. His disciples came close around him. The people behind them quieted. A gull called out over the lake.

He opened his mouth and began to teach them.

"Blessed are the poor in spirit," he said, "for theirs is the kingdom of heaven."

His voice carried down the slope. A man with dust on his sandals leaned forward to listen.

"Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted."

A woman near the front lowered her face into her hands. The man beside her placed a hand on her shoulder.

"Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth."

A boy turned to look at his father. His father nodded once, slowly, and kept his eyes on Jesus.

"Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled."

The breeze moved through the grass. Somewhere down the hill a child laughed and was hushed.

"Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy."

"Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God."

"Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God."

He paused. The crowd waited. A fisherman seated near the disciples set down the net he had been mending and folded his hands in his lap.

"Blessed are those who have been persecuted for righteousness' sake," Jesus said, "for theirs is the kingdom of heaven."

He looked across the faces in front of him. Then he spoke again, and now his voice was steady and clear.

"Blessed are you when people insult you, persecute you, and say all kinds of evil against you falsely, for my sake. Rejoice, and be exceedingly glad, for great is your reward in heaven. For that is how they persecuted the prophets who were before you."

He stopped speaking. No one moved. The wind passed over the hill again, bending the grass toward the lake. Far below, the water shone, and a small boat rocked at anchor near the shore. The disciples sat with their hands on their knees. Jesus looked at them, and then he looked out at the crowd, and the morning held still around them.
