When evening was come, Jesus said unto his disciples, Let us pass over unto the other side. They left the multitude upon the shore, and took him into the ship even as he was. Other little ships followed after.

The light upon the lake was failing. The hills above Capernaum stood dark against the western glow, and the water lay grey beneath the keel. Peter set his hand to the tiller. Andrew and the sons of Zebedee loosed the sail. Soft was the wind at the first, and the bow cut a clean furrow toward the eastern shore.

Then came the storm down upon them.

It fell from the high places, as storms upon that sea are wont to fall. The wind smote the water and tore the wave-tops white. The sail bellied hard and cracked like a whip in the hands of the men. The little ships behind them were lost from sight in the black of it. Rain drave sideways across the deck. The boards groaned. The mast shuddered in its block.

A wave came over the prow. Then another. The water gathered cold about the ankles of the men who laboured at the ropes. They bailed with their hands and with the wooden bowl, and still the sea came in.

And Jesus was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep upon a pillow.

They saw him there as the lightning showed the deck — his head laid upon the cushion, his body at rest, the water washing about him. They cried out unto him above the noise of the wind. They shook him by the shoulder. Master, said they, carest thou not? The sea taketh us. We perish.

He arose.

He stood up in the stern of the ship, and the wind tore at his garment, and he looked upon the sea. He rebuked the wind. He spake unto the water. Peace, said he. Be still.

The wind ceased. The waves fell back into themselves and lay flat. The rain was gone. There was a great calm upon the face of the deep, and the only sound was the dripping of water from the rigging and the breathing of the men.

Then turned he unto them. Why are ye so fearful? said he. How is it that ye have no faith?

They spake not one to another at the first. They looked upon him, and upon the still water, and upon him again. And they said among themselves in low voices, What manner of man is this? Even the wind and the sea obey him.

The ship moved on toward the eastern shore.
