Evening came over the Sea of Galilee. The crowds along the shore began to thin, and Jesus turned to his disciples and said, "Let us go across to the other side."

They left the people on the beach and stepped into the boat where Jesus had been teaching. Other small boats followed, their sails catching the last light off the water. The lake was calm at first. Oars dipped and pulled. Wood creaked under the men's feet. Jesus walked to the stern, lay down on a cushion, and closed his eyes.

The wind shifted. It came down hard from the hills, sudden and cold, and the water answered. Waves rose along the sides of the boat and slapped over the rim. Spray hit the faces of the disciples. One wave broke across the bow, then another, and the boat began to fill. Peter and Andrew leaned into the oars. James shouted over the noise of the wind. Water sloshed at their ankles, then their shins.

Still Jesus slept.

The men called to one another, hauling at the sail, bailing with their hands and with anything they could grab. The boat tilted hard. A gust drove rain across the deck. Thomas stumbled and caught himself against the mast. The horizon pitched up and down, gray and black, and the far shore was lost in the storm.

They went to the stern. Jesus lay there with his head on the cushion, his cloak soaked through. They shook him awake. "Teacher," they cried, "do you not care that we are perishing?"

Jesus stood. The wind pulled at his hair and clothes. He looked out across the heaving water, and he spoke to the wind and to the sea.

"Peace. Be still."

The wind dropped. The waves flattened. Within a breath, the lake lay quiet, and the only sound was water trickling along the inside of the boat and the slow drip from the sail. The clouds began to break apart above them.

He turned to his disciples. "Why are you so afraid? Have you still no faith?"

No one answered. They looked at the calm water, then at one another, then at him. The boat rocked gently. Somewhere a rope tapped against wood. One of the men spoke at last, his voice low.

"Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

Jesus said nothing more. He sat down again at the stern, and the boat moved on across the dark water toward the other side.
