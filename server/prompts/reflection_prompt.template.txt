# Reflection Generation Prompt Template
# ADR-024: LLM-generated reflections grounded in specific story content
#
# VARIABLES:
# - {{MOOD}}: Target mood (joyful, weary, anxious, etc.)
# - {{BIBLE_SOURCE_REF}}: Scripture reference (e.g., "1 Samuel 17:1-54")
# - {{AUDIENCE}}: "adult" or "child"
# - {{STORY_TEXT}}: The full story text to reflect on

You are writing a brief spoken reflection to follow a Bible story that was just read aloud.

## THE STORY
Scripture: {{BIBLE_SOURCE_REF}}
Mood: {{MOOD}}
Audience: {{AUDIENCE}}

{{STORY_TEXT}}

## YOUR TASK
Write a 2-3 sentence spoken reflection that a listener hears after the story ends. It should feel like a quiet exhale — a grounding moment, not a lesson.

## RULES (STRICT)

REQUIRED:
- 2-3 sentences only (20-50 words total)
- Ground the reflection in something specific from THIS story (a moment, image, or action)
- End with a gentle closing line about "enough for today" or "one small step"
- Write for spoken audio — natural rhythm, short sentences
- Calm, direct, personal tone

FORBIDDEN:
- No theology or doctrine ("God's plan", "His grace", "the Lord works in...")
- No direct references to God, Jesus, Yahweh, or the Holy Spirit
- No preaching or "we/you" moral instruction ("We should...", "You can learn...")
- No "This story teaches..." or "This story shows..." openers
- No questions to the listener ("Have you ever...?", "What would you...?")
- No exclamation marks
- No titles, headers, or metadata
- No quotation marks around the reflection

## AUDIENCE STYLE
- Adult: Reflective, slightly literary, uses em-dashes and natural pauses
- Child: Simple words, short sentences, warm and clear

## GOOD EXAMPLES (adult)
"Courage doesn't always feel strong. Sometimes it looks like picking up a stone when everyone else stands still. Even that small step can be enough for today."

"The well was just a well — until a conversation changed everything. Sometimes the most ordinary places hold what we need most. Even one honest moment can be enough for today."

## GOOD EXAMPLES (child)
"Sometimes being brave means doing something even when you feel small. Even one step forward counts."

"Resting does not mean giving up. Even a short pause can help you feel ready again."

## OUTPUT
Write ONLY the reflection text. No preamble, no labels, no quotation marks.
