## CONSTRAINTS
- Mode: Creative (original story, NOT Bible retelling)
- Mood: {{MOOD}}
- Length: {{LENGTH_BUCKET}} bucket
  - short: 250-600 words
  - full: 601-1200 words
  - long: 1201-2000 words
- Language Style: {{LANGUAGE_STYLE}}
  - WEB: Modern, contemporary English
  - KJV: Classical, poetic diction (thee/thou/hath) - POETIC ONLY, NOT SCRIPTURE

## NARRATIVE RULES (CREATIVE MODE CONTRACT)

REQUIRED:
- Original characters and settings (not from Bible)
- Themes of faith, hope, love, kindness, perseverance
- Descriptive, not prescriptive language
- Third-person narrative or gentle omniscient

ALLOWED:
- Biblical themes and values woven naturally into the story
- Contemporary or timeless settings
- Original parables that illustrate spiritual truths
- Gentle, pastoral tone

FORBIDDEN - SCRIPTURE CLAIMS:
- DO NOT retell specific Bible stories (even loosely)
- DO NOT claim scriptural authority ("as the Bible says", "scripture tells us")
- DO NOT teach doctrine as fact
- DO NOT imply this story IS scripture

FORBIDDEN - MoDC VIOLATIONS:
- NO commands or prescriptions ("you should", "you must")
- NO advice ("try to", "remember to", "don't forget to")
- NO dependency language ("you need this", "come back tomorrow")
- NO therapeutic promises ("this will help you heal")

{{#if LANGUAGE_STYLE == KJV}}
## EXTRA: KJV STYLE IN CREATIVE MODE
When using KJV/classical language style in Creative mode:
- Treat as POETIC DICTION ONLY
- DO NOT use scripture-claim markers:
  - NO "Thus saith"
  - NO verse numbering (3:16, chapter 5, verse 12)
  - NO "This is the Word" or "Hear the Word"
  - NO "Saith the Lord"
- The classical language is for atmosphere, NOT authority
{{/if}}
