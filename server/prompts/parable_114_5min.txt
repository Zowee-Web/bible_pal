Write a 5-minute bedtime story (~600 words) based on the Bible story of young Samuel hearing God's voice (1 Samuel 3).

REQUIRED STRUCTURE (follow these EXACT word budgets):

SECTION 1 - GENTLE OPENING (80-100 words):
- Set the scene at the temple at night
- Describe warm lamplight, quiet atmosphere
- Introduce young Samuel and elderly Eli
- Use calm, soothing sensory details

SECTION 2 - BIBLICAL MOMENT (120-150 words):
- Samuel lies down to rest
- He hears a gentle voice calling his name
- He goes to Eli, thinking Eli called
- Eli says he didn't call, tells Samuel to go back to rest
- This happens again (keep it peaceful, no urgency)

SECTION 3 - GENTLE LESSON (120-150 words):
- Eli realizes it must be the Lord calling
- He gently instructs Samuel what to say next time
- Samuel returns to his place and waits peacefully
- The voice comes again, soft and kind

SECTION 4 - CHILD REFLECTION (120-150 words):
- Samuel responds with willingness to listen
- God's presence feels warm and loving
- Samuel feels safe and cherished
- Eli is pleased that Samuel learned to listen
- Samuel understands God speaks gently to those who listen

SECTION 5 - BEDTIME CLOSING (80-100 words):
- Evening settles into peaceful night
- Stars twinkle softly outside
- Samuel rests, feeling loved and protected
- Use sleep-invitation language
- End with drowsy, calming imagery

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Exactly 5 sections as described above
- Total word count: 540-720 words (aim for ~600)
- NO forbidden words (violence, peril, fear, power imagery)
- Short sentences (average 12 words or fewer)
- End with clear bedtime/sleep signals
- Scripture-faithful but gentle retelling
- Non-denominational (no specific church traditions)
- Warm, nurturing tone like a loving grandparent
