Write a gentle, calming bedtime story for children ages 5-9 about Young Samuel Learning to Listen (based on 1 Samuel 3).

THIS STORY MUST BE 600-650 WORDS. NOT SHORTER. Count every word.

SECTION 1: PEACEFUL EVENING OPENING (100 words minimum)
Write about the quiet temple at evening time. Describe the soft golden lamplight. Describe the gentle sounds. Describe young Samuel preparing for sleep in his small bed near the temple. Use many sensory details. Make the reader feel the peace and warmth.

SECTION 2: THE GENTLE VOICE CALLS (100 words minimum)
Write about Samuel hearing his name called softly three times. Each time he goes to Eli, confused but not afraid. Eli is patient and kind. Describe Samuel's curiosity. Describe the gentle, loving quality of the mysterious voice.

SECTION 3: LEARNING TO LISTEN (150 words minimum)
Write about Eli teaching Samuel how to listen for God's voice. Eli explains with warmth and wisdom. Samuel learns to be still and quiet his heart. Describe Samuel's growing trust. Describe the feeling of being loved and guided.

SECTION 4: GOD'S LOVING PRESENCE (100 words minimum)
Write about Samuel feeling God's love wash over him like warm sunlight. He feels safe, cherished, and special. God's voice is gentle and kind. Samuel learns that God is always near, watching over him with love.

SECTION 5: PEACEFUL BEDTIME CLOSING (100 words minimum)
Write the bedtime ending. Samuel lies back down, feeling peaceful. The stars twinkle outside. The night is quiet and safe. Samuel closes his eyes. He feels warm and loved. Include "Goodnight" and references to rest, peace, sleep, and dreams.

RULES:
- Short, simple sentences (under 15 words each)
- No violence, fear, danger, punishment
- No crowns, thrones, kings, battles, or power
- Calm, gentle, reassuring tone throughout
- Child may fall asleep listening - be soothing

TOTAL WORD COUNT: 600-650 words (THIS IS MANDATORY - DO NOT WRITE LESS)
